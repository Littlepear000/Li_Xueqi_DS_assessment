(for example: both build a farmer registry used to target subsidies; both use satellite imagery to monitor crops;
both deploy computer-assisted personal interviewing for surveys). Wording, country, sector, scale and vendor may differ.

"information system") but serve different purposes, or when one document only mentions the approach as background,
a future plan or another programme instead of applying it.
