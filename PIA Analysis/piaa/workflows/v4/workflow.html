Workflow v4 - digital / AI innovation approaches by category × sector × theme

Definition. An approach is a digital-, data- or AI-related innovation adopted by a project that falls into one of the four categories of the working paper Innovation Patterns in the World Bank Group Portfolio (Table 3.4): Operational / Institutional, Technological, Financial, Collaborative. Within a project, one approach is identified by the triple (category, sector, theme): all passages that share the triple are evidence of the same approach, however many times and in whatever wording the document returns to it.

Steps

  1. Segment every selected document (PAD, Project Paper, PID, Program Document, ICR, newest 2 ISRs) into sentences with character offsets and context; boilerplate is flagged.
  2. Candidates: sentences carrying a digital / data / AI cue (the DDAT keyword lists for all 21 digital and data themes, plus the enabling-technology terms of the innovation dictionary). This enforces the digital precondition cheaply before any model call.
  3. Classify (LLM, batches of 20, taxonomy cached): for each candidate, zero or more matches with category, adoption and a short label, plus a reason. Rejected candidates go to the exclusion log.
  4. Codes (LLM, batches of 30): each coded passage gets ONE sector and ONE theme, chosen from the project's own lists in projects.csv (names copied exactly; answers are snapped to the list).
  5. Aggregate: passages with the same (category, sector, theme) collapse into one approach. number_of_evidence = number of passages; the approach's adoption is apply if any passage is apply, otherwise prospect if any is prospect, otherwise discuss; the approach label is the most frequent passage label; the strongest passage (apply, PAD before ISR) is Evidence 1.
  6. Replication: unchanged (explicit cross-references + similarity against the portfolio store; reviewer verification in the web app).

Adoption

ValueMeaningCounts in headline?
applyThis project finances, procures, builds, deploys, pilots, scales, requires or has implemented the approach (component activity, cost line, indicator / DLI, covenant, progress reported)Yes
prospectEnvisaged for the future - later phase, follow-on operation, "will be considered", government vision, expected benefits - without a commitment by this projectNo
discussAdvantages, disadvantages, risks, lessons from elsewhere, other programmes, options weighed, background - without adoptionNo
