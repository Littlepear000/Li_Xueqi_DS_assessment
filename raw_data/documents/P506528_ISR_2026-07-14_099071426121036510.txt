KEY ISSUES & STATUS 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions The World Bank Implementation Status & Results Report Human Capital Protection Project (P506528) Jul 14, 2026 Page 2 of 8 Official Use Only The Human Capital Protection Project was approved on September 19, 2024, with initial IDA financing of US$100.4 million. Additional Financing of US$100.4 million was approved on October 31, 2025, and became effective on January 22, 2026, bringing total IDA financing to US$200.8 million. The project closing date is December 31, 2026. Project performance remains Satisfactory. Progress toward achievement of the PDO and Implementation Progress are both rated Satisfactory, reflecting continued delivery of essential health and education services, effective fiduciary arrangements, and capable management by the Project Coordination Unit. As of July 7, 2026, overall disbursement stood at approximately 84.65 percent. The original financing is nearly fully executed, at 99.43 percent, while the Additional Financing has reached about 68.64 percent execution. Timely completion of IGF audits remains critical to sustaining disbursement performance. Health sector implementation is progressing, with 3.68 million free consultations provided to women and children under five, 253,960 children vaccinated, pentavalent vaccination coverage at 28 percent by end-Q1 2026, 20,114 dialysis kits used, and intermittent preventive treatment for pregnant women estimated at 82 percent. Results are expected to improve as implementation intensifies during the remainder of 2026. Education sector results for 2026 are not yet fully available due to the school calendar. However, 2025 results remained strong, with teacher certification at 99.54 percent and annual examination coverage at 97.46 percent, both above targets 5.
