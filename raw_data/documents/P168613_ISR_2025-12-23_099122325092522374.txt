KEY ISSUES & STATUS 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions Dec 23, 2025 Page 1 of 15 The World Bank Implementation Status & Results Report Guinea Natural Resources, Mining and Environmental Management Project (P168613) The Guinea Natural Resources, Mining and Environmental Management Project continues to play a central role in supporting Guinea’s mining-sector transformation and environmental protection agenda, particularly in the context of the Simandou Iron-Ore Project and the Government’s ambitious reform program. The key reforms supported under the Project include: (i) the updating of the mining cadastre following a comprehensive compliance review of existing mining operations against signed agreements; (ii) the professionalization of technical, environmental, and social monitoring of investment projects, including mining operations; and (iii) the professionalization of the National Agency for National Parks and Reserves (OGPNRF), along with the implementation of a roadmap to strengthen the country’s network of protected areas. Significant progress is being made in achieving the Project Development Objective (PDO) and this achievement is expected to be accelerated further with the implementation of upcoming, key activities. The additional financing formally requested by the Government (US$17.5 million in grants from the PROGREEN, SCALE, and GEF/GBFF trust funds) will enable the scaling-up of high-priority interventions in the forest economy and green jobs, and support carbon-market readiness, operationalization of the National Conservation Trust Fund, and international recognition of the Fouta-Djallon Highlands as a globally significant ecological and ecosystem-services asset. 5.
