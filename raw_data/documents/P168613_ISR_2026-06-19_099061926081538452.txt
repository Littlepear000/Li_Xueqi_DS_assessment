KEY ISSUES & STATUS Jun 19, 2026 Official Use Only Page 1 of 16 The World Bank Implementation Status & Results Report Guinea Natural Resources, Mining and Environmental Management Project (P168613) 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions The project is performing well. Both the progress towards the PDO and overall implementation progress are rated Satisfactory, upgraded from Moderately Satisfactory in the previous ISR. This improvement reflects accelerated implementation across all components, resolution of earlier governance and fiduciary issues, and the achievement or near-achievement of several key results targets. Cumulative disbursement has reached approximately 49%, a significant increase from 37.5% recorded in November 2025. This reflects the activation of major contracts and an improved pace of expenditure processing across all project components. On results, forest areas under management plans stand at 1,277,290 hectares, exceeding the closing target. The local content platform has recorded US$304.47 million in goods and services procured by mining companies, well above the project's US$60 million target. The national airborne geophysical campaign has been completed, and the Guinea Biodiversity Conservation Foundation (FCG) has been formally constituted, with its inaugural Board of Directors meeting held in April 2026. The project closing date remains September 30, 2027. 5.
