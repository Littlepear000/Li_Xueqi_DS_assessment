KEY ISSUES & STATUS 3.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions As the Program nears completion, it has exceeded key targets. The Government of Punjab has largely achieved its results framework, including all PDO indicators. Major pension reforms under DLI 1 are expected to lower future pension liabilities. Budget credibility has improved (DLI 2), and e-procurement has expanded government-wide, with over PKR 1 trillion in contracts processed (DLI 3). E-office is being used across all departments, improving government efficiency. Over 7 million beneficiaries have accessed services online, far surpassing the 1.5 million target (DLI 4). Recent tax collection efforts are set to enhance revenue for Punjab, with overall collection expected to exceed Program targets by the end of June 2025. 4.
