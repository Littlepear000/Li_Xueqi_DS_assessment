KEY ISSUES & STATUS May 22, 2026 Official Use Only Page 1 of 10 The World Bank Implementation Status & Results Report Governance Modernization to Enable Efficient Service Delivery Project (P178808) 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions The Governance Modernization to Enable Efficient Service Delivery Project (P178808) has been effective since February 2025. Implementing agencies include the Ministry of Finance, the Federal Civil Service Commission, and the Ministry of Revenue. Over the past year, the project made progress across all three components by laying key institutional, technological, and policy foundations for long-term public sector modernization in Ethiopia. Under public administration, the project advanced evidence-based human resource management through preparations for a national civil service census, rollout and integration of digital HR systems, and development of national competency frameworks and training modules to support competency-based HRM reforms. In domestic revenue mobilization, the project focused on strengthening the technological and institutional foundations of tax administration through progress toward refreshing the IT equipment of the data center, upgrading other IT infrastructure, and initiating reforms related to e-invoicing, and riskbased tax administration. In public financial management, the project supported actions for the modernization of core PFM systems such as IFMIS and IBEX, strengthened procurement and audit systems, expanded e-government procurement and payroll systems, and advanced professionalization of accounting and auditing functions. The project’s achievements over the reporting period primarily focused on building the digital infrastructure, institutional frameworks, analytical foundations, and capacity development systems necessary for deeper civil service, revenue administration, and PFM reforms in the coming years. Overall project implementation remains satisfactory. 5.
