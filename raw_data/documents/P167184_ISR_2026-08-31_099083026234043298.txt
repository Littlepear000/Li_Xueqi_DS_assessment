KEY ISSUES & STATUS 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions The Project continues to support Papua New Guinea’s efforts to increase utilization of quality essential health services in four target provinces. Since becoming effective in December 2020, implementation has progressed, and service delivery interventions are being scaled up across all project provinces. As of August 13, 2026, cumulative disbursements reached US$21.4 million, equivalent to 71 percent of the original US$30 million financing. Year 5 results indicate improved access to selected essential health services in project provinces. Fourth antenatal care visits increased by 23 percent from the baseline, while Pentavalent 3 immunizations increased by 28 percent. Outreach clinics also increased by 84 percent from the baseline. Quality improvements were recorded in Year 4, with 153 of 156 assessed health facilities improving their quality scores from baseline and 85 percent meeting the minimum quality threshold. Continued attention will be needed to sustain service delivery and quality gains through project closing. Performance-based funding. Performance-based financing has supported outreach services, training, equipment purchases, and facility rehabilitation in project provinces. Competitive disbursement-linked indicators have also contributed to broader engagement by Provincial Health Authorities (PHAs), with 21 of the 22 PHAs signing Memoranda of Understanding and several showing improvements from baseline in priority service delivery indicators. Project restructuring and extension. The Project was restructured in January 2026 to help address implementation delays, reallocate financing to priority activities, and update targets and indicators to better support delivery of essential health services in selected provinces. The restructuring included a no-cost extension. The Project closing date was extended from June 30, 2026 to May 31, 2028 to allow additional time to complete priority activities, including health facility assessments, equipment delivery, facility rehabilitation, and health worker upskilling. The next technical mission is planned for October 2026 and will continue to support implementation progress, monitor results, and help ensure timely completion of priority activities. 5.
