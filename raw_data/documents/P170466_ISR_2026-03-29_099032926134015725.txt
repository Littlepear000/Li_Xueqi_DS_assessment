KEY ISSUES & STATUS 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions This implementation status report follows second project restructuring, approved in November 2025 and reports on the progress in project implementation since September 2025. Activities in and around Central Forest Reserves (CFR), previously implemented by the National Forestry Authority, have been largely paused since NFA was dissolved in August 2025 (as part of the overall government process of agencies' rationalization) and is in the process of being merged into the Ministry of Water and Environment (MWE). Good progress has been achieved under the agroforestry support, implemented by a techical service provider to MWE in 19 districts in the refugee hosting areas, with over 37,000 household members (of which 28% women) reached through various community engagement, training and extension activities, and over 5,000 ha developed under agroforestry. Provision of firewood to the most vulnerable refugees (persons with specific needs, as defined by the UNHCR and Office of the Prime Minister) has also continued, reaching a total of 70,000 beneficiaries by the end of 2025. Led by Uganda Wildlife Authority, construction of five visitor information centers, as well as other small-scale tourism infrastructure, is ongoing, in order to improve tourism infrastructure in the project protected areas and enhance visitor numbers and their length of stay. Activities related to removal of invasive vegetation in some parks have also advanced (with additional 80 hectares cleared). Activities related to prevention of human wildlife conflict were also implemented, including extension of the electric fence in Queen Elizabeth and Murchison Falls National Parks and excavation of an elephant trench in Kibale National Park. MWE has led the revision (including two rounds of consultations) of the Vulnerable and Marginalized Groups Plans for the Batwa communities living close to four project protected areas (Echuya CFR and Bwindi Impenetrable, Mgahinga Gorilla and Semuliki National Parks) and is initiating their implementation. The project closes on June 30, 2026. 5.
