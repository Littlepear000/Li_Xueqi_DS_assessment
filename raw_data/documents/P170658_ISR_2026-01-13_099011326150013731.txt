KEY ISSUES & STATUS 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions The Project is under implementation with full support from the Authorities. Since the last implementation support mission, the Project has achieved several important milestones. The contract for the construction of the 911 Center (Component 1) was awarded in May 2025, with construction planned to be finalized in December 2026. In parallel, the Emergency Management and Response System (SAGE), which will serve as the technological platform for handling emergency calls and dispatching services, is under implementation and its pilot testing started in January 2026. Additionally, all institutional agreements with key First Respondents Units (i.e., National Police of Peru, SAMU and Firemen) and Gender Based Violence hotline (Line 100) have been signed, and protocols for emergency response have been developed. These include specific measures for addressing gender-based violence and supporting vulnerable populations. These efforts reflect a growing alignment between infrastructure, technology, and institutional readiness to deliver a unified and effective emergency response system. 5.
