KEY ISSUES & STATUS Mar 19, 2026 Page 1 of 12 The World Bank Implementation Status & Results Report Girls Empowerment and Quality Education for All Project (P169222) 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions Project implementation continues to advance, with results achieved in key areas, including improved monitoring of attendance and expanded participation in girls’ and boys’ clubs, completion of WASH rehabilitation works in an initial group of schools, and participation in the 2025 PASEC assessment. These activities support improved learning environments, girls’ empowerment, and stronger national learning measurement systems. Implementation has nevertheless faced delays, particularly related to procurement processes affecting core learning and system-strengthening activities—such as structured lesson plans, inclusive education technical assistance, and education management information system activities. In addition, quality and safeguards challenges affecting WASH works in the Príncipe Autonomous Region have required corrective actions and closer supervision. The additional financing and extension of the project closing date provides an important opportunity to consolidate results. Key decisions focus on accelerating implementation during the remaining period, including timely operationalization of the additional financing, signature and implementation of output agreements with UNFPA and UNICEF, fast-tracking priority procurement processes with streamlined scope and realistic timelines, and completing remaining WASH works with strengthened technical and environmental and social oversight. 5.
