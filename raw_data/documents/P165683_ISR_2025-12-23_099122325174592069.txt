KEY ISSUES & STATUS 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions Dec 23, 2025 Page 1 of 10 The World Bank Implementation Status & Results Report Paraiba Improving Water Resources Management and Services Provision (P165683) This ISR is based on the Implementation Support Mission of November 24-28, 2025. Project status as of December 2025. The Project underwent a Restructuring which was approved by the World Bank Country Director on December 16, 2025. This first Restructuring comprised of: (a) an 18-month extension of the Closing Date until December 31, 2027; (b) excluding activities related to the rehabilitation and expansion of the largest wastewater treatment plant (WWTP) in the Metropolitan Region of Joao Pessoa city (JPMR), non-revenue water (NRW) control contracts in three areas of the JPMR water supply system, and pre-feasibility studies of proposed dams in the Piranhas-Açu River Basin; (c) adjusting the results framework; and (d) updating the disbursement schedule. The addendum to Loan Agreement No. 8931/BR is expected to be signed in the next few days. The pace of implementation has gained momentum with all major contracts for works underway and disbursements that have increased substantially. By December 2025, the Bank has disbursed US$88.3 million — 70% of the loan's proceeds. Satisfactory progress has also been reported on activities financed with counterpart funds including the Curimataú pipeline, CAGEPA's commercial non-revenue water (NRW) control programme, and institutional development activities at CAGEPA and AESA. Progress towards the Project Development Objective (PDO). The progress towards achievement of PDO is maintained as Moderately Satisfactory (MS). Both, the Bank team and the client agree that the PDO is achievable within the new proposed timeline. 5.
