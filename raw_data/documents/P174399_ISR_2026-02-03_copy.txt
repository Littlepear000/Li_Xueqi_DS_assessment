KEY ISSUES & STATUS 3.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions The Program is on track to achieve its development objective by June 2029. During the first part of FY 2026, the Program has supported the upskilling of more than half the current early childhood care and development (ECCD) workforce, with 495 out of 684 government ECCD facilitators (72.36%) having completed or undergoing the Diploma in ECCD, and 487 out of 850 ECCD focal monitors and program managers (57.29%) having completed a revised training program. In parallel, the construction and/or renovation of 120 ECCD centers across the country has enabled coverage to 41.3% of the population eligible for ECCD services. To further address foundational learning needs, the Ministry of Feb 03, 2026 Page 1 of 21 The World Bank Implementation Status & Results Report Human Capital Recovery & Resilience Program (P174399) Education & Skills Development (MoESD) has issued a policy directive mandating the integration of climate-resilient features into school infrastructure. This measure, to be implemented by 22 schools across 9 districts in the coming months, would support the learning environment and safeguard students from climate change impacts. These achievements have been supported under disbursement-linked indicators (DLIs) 1, 2 and 4. The Program's high-level results, spanning the lifecycle, aim to ensure improved (i) coverage of children in ECCD services where the workforce is adequately qualified, (ii) access to the online learning management system (LMS) by schools, (iii) female graduation from technical and vocational education and training (TVET) and Skills Development Program (SDP) courses, (iv) employment among TVET/SDP graduates, and (v) treatment of patients in outpatient facilities where the workforce is adequately skilled. Among these five, the rates of female graduation from TVET/SDP and employment among such graduates, have already been consistently above the Program end-target. 4.
