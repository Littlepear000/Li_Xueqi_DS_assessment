KEY ISSUES & STATUS 3.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions The Improving Education Quality, Results, and Access for All Operation (IQRAA) is financed by an International Development Association (IDA) credit/grant of US$40.0 million and a Global Partnership for Education (GPE) grant of US$16.75 million. The Government of Mauritania (GoM) is contributing US$13.25 million in counterpart funding. IQRAA comprises two parts: a Program-for-Results (PforR) component of US$51.75 million and an Investment Project Financing (IPF) technical assistance component of US$5.0 million. IQRAA aims to: (i) improve the quality of basic education and access to secondary education, with a particular focus on adolescent girls; and (ii) strengthen education service delivery in refugee-hosting areas. The World Bank’s Board of Executive Directors approved IQRAA on June 17, 2026. The IDA Financing Agreement and the GPE Grant Agreement were signed on August 25, 2026. The Operation is not yet effective. The National Assembly is expected to ratify the Financing Agreement during its October 2026 ordinary session. Some critical early years conditions The World Bank Implementation Status & Results Report Mauritania Improving Education Quality, Results, and Access for All Operation (IQRAA) (P509243) Oct 25, 2026 Page 2 of 15 Official Use Only remain: the legal opinion, adoption of the Program Operations Manual, and recruitment of key Project Implementation Unit (PIU) staff. The GoM, with WBG support, is working to meet these effectiveness conditions by end-October 2026. This is the first PforR operation in Mauritania's education sector, so the GoM and the WBG are prioritizing early preparation to meet the first-year disbursement-linked indicators (DLIs) on schedule. These DLIs include early childhood education quality standards and frameworks; design studies on the use of g education technology and artificial intelligence in literacy and numeracy teaching, play-based learning frameworks and learning measurement tools; and a learning assessment methodology aligned with the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs). Arrangements for the Independent Verificaiton Agency (IVA) to verify DLI results are also being set up. The GoM is also preparing to record all Program expenditures, including its counterpart contribution, in the 2027 program-based budgeting. Implementation Readiness. No funds have been disbursed under the IDA Credit or GPE Grant because the Operation is not yet effective. Implementation readiness is progressing well: the Procurement Plan for the IPF component was approved on June 25, 2026, and preparatory consultancies are planned for the pre-primary cycle to develop a play-based learning pedagogical guide and tools for measuring early childhood education outcomes. The existing Project Implementation Unit (Direction des Projets Éducation-Formation, DPEF/PASEB) will lead implementation, drawing on its management of the Project Preparation Grant (E6050) and its experience under the Mauritania Basic Education Sector Support Project (P163143). Next Steps : • Effectiveness: The National Assembly is expected to ratify the IDA Financing Agreement during its October 2026 session. The GoM will also work to meet the others remaining conditions by end-October 2026, namely: the legal opinion, adoption of the Program Operations Manual, and recruitment of key PIU staff. • First-year DLI preparation: The GoM, with WBG support, will prepare the terms of reference (ToRs) and launch the procurement process for the technical studies linked to the achievement of the first-year DLIs. These include the early childhood education quality standards, the education technology and artificial intelligence design studies, and the learning assessment method. • Independent verification: The independent verification arrangements and protocols for DLI results, agreed during preparation, will be duly established by the GoM with WBG support. • 2027 budget: The GoM will confirm that Program spending and part of the counterpart funding are included in the 2027 budget under program-based budgeting. • First official implementation support mission in early November 2026 : The mission will review progress on these actions, agree on a detailed first-year work plan and timeline for achieving the first set of DLIs, and confirm that the implementation unit and the fiduciary, environmental, and social arrangements are in place. The first official implementation support mission has been discussed with client and is planned for early November 2026. Discussions are undergoing with the Government counterpart on that considering the INSPIRE Academy and GPE in-country visit. 4.
