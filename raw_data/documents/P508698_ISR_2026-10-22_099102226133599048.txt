PROJECT DEVELOPMENT OBJECTIVE 1.1 Development Objective Original Development Objective (Approved as part of Approval package on 01-Oct-2025) The program development objective is to increase and sustain access to at least basic water supply, sanitation and hygiene services in all rural districts.

KEY ISSUES & STATUS 3.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions The Sustainable Rural Water Supply and Sanitation Program Phase II (SRWSSP II) became effective on February 19, 2026, following an inception mission held on February 16–19, 2026 to confirm implementation readiness and agree on initial priorities.
Since then, steady progress has been made through regular coordination meetings Advance financing has been partially disbursed, with remaining funds expected shortly.
Overall, implementation is progressing well, reflecting strong readiness and commitment from implementing agencies.
