KEY ISSUES & STATUS 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions Implementation remains behind schedule, and progress toward the Project Development Objective is not yet visible in results indicators. However, the project has moved beyond start-up and is now positioned to begin generating results, with implementation arrangements functioning across the Council for Development and Reconstruction, Green Plan, Kafalat, and the Ministry of Agriculture, and priority activities defined for farmer grants, small and medium enterprise financing, rural infrastructure, greenhouse rehabilitation, food safety, agricultural data, and digital services. The mission agreed on a set of time-bound actions to make progress more visible over the coming months, including launching priority recovery activities, advancing contract awards for rural infrastructure and emergency rehabilitation, initiating outreach for matching grants and small and medium enterprise financing, and beginning consolidated monthly results reporting across implementing agencies. The next support mission, planned for January 2027, will assess progress against these agreed milestones, including whether priority activities have moved into implementation, reporting is providing credible evidence of results, and early benefits are beginning to reach farmers, cooperatives, and agri-food enterprises. In the meantime, recurrent technical missions will continue between now and then, as has been the practice, to support implementation follow-up, troubleshoot bottlenecks, and monitor progress against the agreed actions. 5.
