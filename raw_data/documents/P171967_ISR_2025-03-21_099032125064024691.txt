KEY ISSUES & STATUS Mar 21, 2025 Page 1 of 9 The World Bank Implementation Status & Results Report Regional Infrastructure Finance Facility (RIFF) Project (P171967) 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions The project became effective on October 5, 2020, in during covid-19. As such the project was off to a slow start as many Energy Utilities’ ability to enter into power purchasing agreements with private investors was severely weakened. As a result, several infrastructure sub-projects in the pipeline did not materialized. Since then, TDB has managed to add several new deals to the pipeline as evidence of its strong business development efforts. Component #2 which finances off grid solar energy projects with a total amount of $75M, is now fully disbursed. The funding under this component has since been increased by another $50M. TDB with the support of the World Bank is implementing several actions to increase the pace of disbursement. COMESA Secretariat deliverables. The COMESA implementation progress is rated as satisfactory. Overall progress in the delivery of the workplan has improved with several deliverables in advance stages of completion. Two key deliverables have been approved by the Ministers of Infrastructure: (i) the development of COMESA model solar standards and (ii) the updating of the 2008 COMESA Model Energy Policy. Having received ministerial approval, COMESA is now providing technical assistance to various member states on the domestication and implementation of the policies. COMESA continues to advance the work on four other deliverables namely (i) the Mini grid tariff setting methodology tool (ii) the PPP framework (iii) the solar products registration system and (iv) the COMESA model solar common customs tariff framework. Additionally, COMESA is supporting the establishment and capacitation of four energy regulators. 5.
