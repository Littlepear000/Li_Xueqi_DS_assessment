KEY ISSUES & STATUS 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions Although the project is on track to achieve its main objectives, significant challenges remain and must be addressed as soon as possible, including: (a) accelerating the implementation of several activities that are still experiencing significant delays, particularly those related to direct business support programs (component 3); (b) significant gaps in project management, particularly in financial management. As a result, the project's rating has been revised to “moderately unsatisfactory” in these two areas. Better planning of activities is also required to ensure the optimal use of available resources and to achieve the project's objectives, particularly after the effectiveness of the additional financing. 5.
