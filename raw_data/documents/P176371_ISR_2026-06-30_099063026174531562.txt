KEY ISSUES & STATUS 3.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions Overall, progress towards the PDO remains strong even though implementation progress continues to lag for several components. Reflecting agreements reached during the MTR (May 2025), the program was restructured to scale up the ambition for regional statistical harmonization (with a doubling of the PDO indicator target on "number of statistical dimensions harmonized" from 5 to 10). The most recent missions showed continued strong progress towards the regional harmonization agenda, and the new end target of 10 is likely to be attained by the closing date of June 30, 2027. Jun 30, 2026 Page 1 of 44 The World Bank Implementation Status & Results Report Eastern Africa Regional Statistics Program-for-Results (P176371) Implementation progress remains slower, witnessed by a disbursement rate of 49% with approximately 13 months of implementation remaining. Kenya has made progress since the Mid Term Review but implementation remains moderately unsatisfactory due to funding delays to the Kenya National Bureau of Statistics (KNBS), as a result of which the Kenya component was scaled down in the restructuring. Tanzania shows satisfactory progress with a disbursement rate of 68%. Rwanda is on track with good progress and satisfactory ratings, while Burundi's progress remains moderately satisfactory, requiring technical assistance for delayed activities. Progress on the EAC's statistical harmonization is highly satisfactory, but capacity building activities need acceleration. AUC's implementation has accelerated markedly over the past year though remains behind schedule due to the initial delays. 4.
