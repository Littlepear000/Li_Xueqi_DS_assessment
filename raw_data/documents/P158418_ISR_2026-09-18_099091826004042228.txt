KEY ISSUES & STATUS The World Bank Implementation Status & Results Report Türkiye Irrigation Modernization (P158418) Sep 18, 2026 Page 2 of 8 Official Use Only 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions Under Component 1, the Project finances investments to modernize DSI’s irrigation systems in Atabey, Eregli-Ivriz, Alasehir, and Uzunlu schemes, with a combined area of 50,764 hectares benefiting 17,312 people. Under this Component, the construction contract ended in Eregli Ivriz. The contracts are in progress in Uzunlu, Atabey and Alasehir. DSI has completed all the activities under Component 2 which is financed through a Global Environment Facility (GEF) grant. DSI has (i) designed and piloted a national program for Water Users Association (WUA) capacity building , and (ii) piloted groundwater stewardship through US$2.0 million GEF grant. Component 3 is for Project Management and included organization of a Farmers Training Program. 5.
