KEY ISSUES & STATUS 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions Mar 20, 2025 Page 1 of 16 The World Bank Implementation Status & Results Report Strengthening Institutions for Refugee Administration Project (P165542) The Mission noted that progress has been made, and some important milestones have been reached. However, most results remain unachieved and disbursements remain low. The Project has now been restructured to accommodate the shorter Project duration, however, concerted efforts will be necessary to reach the development objective in the remaining year. Issues have been discussed with the Government of Pakistan and the OSU. Actions on procurement, hiring, and M&E have now been agreed. Progress towards achieving the Development Objective therefore continues to be rated as Moderately Satisfactory. Progress will be reassessed during the next implementation support mission. 5.
