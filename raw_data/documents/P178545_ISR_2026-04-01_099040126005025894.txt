KEY ISSUES & STATUS Apr 01, 2026 Page 1 of 13 The World Bank Implementation Status & Results Report Community Livelihood Enhancement And Resilience (P178545) 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions Under component 1 on Local Economic Development and Community Capacity Strengthening, the village development planning process was completed in all Batch 1 (230 villages) and a pipeline of 306 community infrastructure investments currently under implementation and due to be completed by the end of May 2026. Under Component 2 on livelihood enhancement, 851 Self-Help Groups (SHGs) have been formed in Batch 1 villages with US$1.6 million in seed grants disbursed doubling group capital through savings and loan rotation while 789 new SHGs have been formed in Batch 2 villages, with strong inclusion of women and non-Lao-Tai households throughout. Under Component 3, nutrition activities have reached scale in Batch 1 villages with nearly 10,000 members across 321 community nutrition groups supporting 5,983 home gardens, 15.5 tons of processed foods produced, and extensive behavior change activities carried out. Overall, progress toward improved food diversity appears positive, but remains to be conclusively confirmed with more robust data in the second half of implementation. 5.
