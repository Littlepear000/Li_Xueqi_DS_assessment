OBJECTIVE 1.1 Development Objective Original Development Objective (Approved as part of Approval package on 13-Dec-2019) The project’s development objective is to improve (i) the relevance and excellence of scientific research, and (ii) innovative entrepreneurship and access to finance for enterprise growth, as a way of contributing to Serbia’s growth and competitiveness. Relevance of research refers to the potential of research results to be commercialized on the market, thus contributing to the economy, while excellence refers to international recognition of the quality of the research. KEY ISSUES & STATUS 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions The project has advanced research commercialization, institutional reform, startup acceleration, and the preparation of new AI-support instruments. However, implementation remains delayed by administrative approvals and the release of funds. The Government and the World Bank are working to address these constraints, protect the continuity of ongoing activities, and align remaining implementation with the project's closing date of September 30, 2027. Progress by Component Component 1.1. Science Fund: The Science Fund continued implementing research-support programs and launched the “From Lab to Market” program to foster partnerships between scientific organizations and industry to develop market-oriented AI solutions. The call closed on June 15, 2026, and received 214 proposals. The evaluation process is ongoing, with full proposals expected from long-listed applicants. Component 1.2. Research and Development Institutes (RDIs) Transformation: Support for participating RDIs continued through institutional diagnostics, business development and technology transfer advice, commercialization support, startup and spin-off mentoring, and assistance with preparing competitive research proposals. Component 1.3. Serbian Diaspora Facility. Implementation of the Serbian Diaspora Facility continued, though a significant share of supported projects requested extensions. The project team is reviewing implementation schedules and outstanding activities to facilitate completion of agreed outputs and accelerate disbursement. Component 2. Innovation Fund (IF) programs: • Katapult enterprise acceleration scheme. The fifth Katapult cohort completed its acceleration phase on June 30, 2026, following a structured mentoring program and Demo Day activities. The participating companies have entered the post-acceleration stage and may apply for co-investment support in accordance with program requirements. The IF and the World Bank advanced preparations for an AI Voucher scheme to help eligible firms access specialized AI-related services. Program design includes a proportionate risk- management mechanism, with targeted review and monitoring for higher-risk use cases and streamlined treatment for lower-risk applications. • AI Technology Adoption scheme. Preparation continued for an AI Technology Adoption program intended to support eligible micro, small, and medium-sized enterprises in integrating AI technologies into their business processes, products, and services. 5.
