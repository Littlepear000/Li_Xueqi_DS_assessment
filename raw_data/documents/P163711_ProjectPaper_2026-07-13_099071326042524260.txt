PROJECT STATUS AND RATIONALE FOR RESTRUCTURING A. Project Status 1. The Tax Administration and Statistical System Modernization Project (TASSMP) was approved on February 28, 2020, for a total of US$35 million, comprising an IDA Credit (IDA-65460) of US$17.5 million and an IDA Grant (IDA- D5660) of US$17.5 million. The project became effective on December 9, 2020. The Project Development Objective (PDO) is to improve effectiveness of tax collection and the national statistical system in the Kyrgyz Republic. The project is structured across five components: (1) STS Institutional Development; (2) STS Operational Development; (3) NSC Institutional Development; (4) NSC Data Development; and (5) Project Management. 2. The project was previously restructured in June 2024 and in March 2025. The first restructuring extended the project closing date from September 30, 2025 to March 31, 2027, the second, processed partial cancellation of the full amount of IDA credit (SDR 6,350,000) allocated to Category 1, i.e., Components 1 and 2, implemented by STS. The 18- month closing date extension was processed to allow sufficient time to complete IT systems under both STS- and NSC- implemented components, which suffered from initial delays including effectiveness delays, COVD-19 disruptions and delays in NSC engagement with the Twinning Partner. Following extension, implementation improved, however, from mid-June 2024 implementation significantly slowed with serious delays on key procurement packages for the STS and NSC due to frequent staff turnovers in the Project Management Units (PMU) and STS and NSC. These led to delays and disruptions in decision-making on ongoing contracts, new procurement processing, and project management. As a result, the Progress toward achievement of the PDO and Overall Implementation Progress (IP) ratings were again downgraded to Moderately Unsatisfactory in October 2024,1 and the project remained in the problem status until September 2025. Following March 2025 restructuring and partial cancellation of credit funds, the implementation has significantly accelerated, notably, key procurement packages including TA to ISNAK-2 and NSC data center equipment, advanced considerably, thus, both PDO and IP ratings were upgraded to Moderately Satisfactory. To date, the implementation progress remains uneven: NSC has advanced elements of institutional reform, while STS activities related to ISNAK-2 have shifted to state budget financing and selected policy reform activities have been adjusted. Status of STS-implemented Components 1 and 2 3. The State Tax Service (STS) components, to-date, achieved the following results: 1,101 STS staff were trained, nearly three times the end-of-project target of 400; the number of electronic services available to taxpayers has reached 35, surpassing the target of 27; and the e-filing rate for payroll (PAYE) withholding tax has reached 100% to date, reflecting a significant shift toward digital compliance. A new STS institutional strategy has been developed and approved. 4. Implementation of STS-implemented Components 1 and 2 has been significantly affected by changes in government priorities. The ISNAK-2 tax information system modernization activities — originally envisaged under Component 2 — were shifted to state budget financing; the IDA funding was cancelled in March 2025. In addition, technical assistance activities related to the development of a new Tax Code were suspended following a government decision to allow businesses to adapt to the 2022 Tax Code and preserve legislative stability. This led to the second partial cancellation request, which is being processed through this Restructuring Paper. 5. PDO-level indicators. The new methodology is more robust, internationally consistent, and sustainable beyond project closing. The final approval of revised business processes is expected in October 2026, following delivery of the final version of the business processes by GOPA on September 30, 2026. The final version will align with the law on de-bureaucratization, and NSC is expected to formally approve it upon receipt. 9. However, some deliverables under the GOPA twinning partnership are still pending, and NSC and GOPA are working closely together to complete all outstanding deliverables by November 2026. Other TA activities are progressing and expected to be completed on time. 10. The achievement of two PDO indicators related to Components 3 and 4 is on track. These are: (i) reduction of time between completion of data collection and release of key statistical indicators to the public, which measures efficiency gains in NSC's statistical production cycle. The current value remains at the baseline of 90 days, with a significant reduction expected following full implementation of the e-reporting system and business process improvements by late 2026; and (ii) openness of official statistics, measured across 21 data categories. This indicator has already been achieved, with the Kyrgyz Republic's ODIN score reaching 59 as of January 2025, exceeding the end-of-project target of 56. The proposed restructuring will not affect the likelihood of achieving these indicators. B. Rationale for Restructuring 11. In the letter dated February 11, 2026 (received by the World Bank on February 12), the Ministry of Finance (MoF) requested a restructuring of the TASSMP, following a decision by the State Tax Service (STS) to partially cancel the remaining IDA grant funds allocated to Component 1 (STS Institutional Development) and Component 2 (STS Operational Development). The MoF letter refers to an estimated available grant balance of US$3,180,567.19 under IDA Grant (IDA-D5660). The amount subject to cancellation in this Restructuring Paper is US$3,080,328 and reflects the USD equivalent of XDR 2,232,313 and is the operative figure for the purposes of this restructuring. It is approximately US$100,239 less than the amount provided in the MoF letter due to the SDR/USD exchange rate fluctuation. In the same letter, the MoF further proposed that the grant funds cancelled from the STS-implemented components 1 and 2 (Category 1) be reallocated to finance activities under the NSC-implemented components 3 and 4 (Category 2), where financing needs remain high and implementation capacity is stronger. This reallocation is accompanied by a simultaneous and equivalent partial cancellation of the IDA Credit (IDA-65460) originally allocated to the NSC-implemented components (Category 2). This approach ensures a more efficient use of limited IDA grant resources and avoids underutilization of grant funds. The proposal has the support of both implementing agencies. 13. The retained balance under IDA Grant D5660 of US$291,116 is earmarked for completing the following essential remaining activities under STS-implemented components: (i) two surveys on taxpayer compliance and service satisfaction; (ii) the final project audit; and (iii) preparation of the Project Implementation Completion Report (PICR). 14. This restructuring also revises the methodology for the PDO-level indicator measuring VAT gap reduction (Component 1). In the letter dated November 25, 2025, the Ministry of Finance requested the replacement of the appraisal-stage consumption-based methodology (M-2021) with a production-based ("top-down") methodology developed by the Bank and aligned with IMF approaches. The previous methodology faced data availability constraints and was unable to capture the significant growth in imports and re-exports in the Kyrgyz Republic’s economy since 2022. The new methodology addresses these shortcomings by decomposing the VAT gap into domestic production and import components, distinguishing between policy-driven and compliance-driven gaps, and remaining operable by STS specialists beyond project closing. The end target for this indicator has been revised accordingly from 12.00 percent to 3.5 percent to reflect the updated measurement approach, while remaining consistent with the overall PDO. 15. Having reviewed the restructuring request and the proposed reallocation and cancellation arrangements, the Bank team confirms that the revised project scope remains feasible within the current closing date of March 31, 2027, and that the project remains on track to achieve its PDO. The Bank will continue to provide active implementation support and coordination with both implementing agencies to facilitate timely completion of the remaining activities. II.
