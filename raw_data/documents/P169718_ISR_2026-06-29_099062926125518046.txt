KEY ISSUES & STATUS 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions Implementation of Component 1 (Climate Resilient Infrastructure and Urban Upgrading) is progressing satisfactorily. The feasibility and detailed design consultancy is ongoing, with deliverables submitted to date reflecting adequate progress and quality. Several new investment contracts, including household water connections, solar streetlights, stormwater drainage cleaning equipment, and pothole patching, have commenced and are expected to be completed by the end of August 2026. These investments are projected to directly benefit 94,905 people. Implementation of Component 2 (Strengthening Integrated Resilient Urban Development Capacity) has experienced delays following the termination of the main contract for the preparation of spatial development plans. The re-procurement process is currently at the expression-of-interest stage, with completion anticipated by the end of October 2026. Component 3 (Project Management) continues to encounter implementation challenges, particularly with respect to strengthening the project’s M&E system. 5.
