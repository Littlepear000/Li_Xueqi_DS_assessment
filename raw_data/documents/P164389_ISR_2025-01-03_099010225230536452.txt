KEY ISSUES & STATUS Jan 03, 2025 Page 1 of 9 The World Bank Implementation Status & Results Report Water Security and Resilience for the Valley of Mexico (PROSEGHIR) (P164389) 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions The Project was signed on February 28, 2022 and was declared effective as of June 1, 2022. In February 2024, the National Water Commission (CONAGUA) and the Valley of Mexico's Water Basin Agency (OCAVM) formally requested the World Bank to restructure the Project, which included: (i) a partial cancellation of US$90 million from the total loan proceeds; and (ii) shortening the project implementation period by one year, targeting completion by December 31, 2024. Five maintenance works contracts were successfully launched in 2024 and are set to be completed by project closing, contributing to improving the Cutzamala System's reliability in the face of climate change. 5. These have been detailed in a document sent to the Bank and approved in December 2022. The grievance redress mechanism (GRM) is accessible to project-affected people and can receive grievances through the following channels: By email addressed to the Head of the Internal Control Body (OIC), • Through the Sistema Integral de Denuncias Ciudadanas (SIDEC) under the Ministry of Public Administration, • In writing through the OCAVM Reports Office, • In person through the General Residence for the Construction of the Cutzamala System and/or the General Residence of Operation of the Cutzamala System, • Through institutional email, • Through official letters addressed to the Director General of CONAGUA.
