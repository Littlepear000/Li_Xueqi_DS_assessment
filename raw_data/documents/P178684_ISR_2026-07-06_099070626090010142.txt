KEY ISSUES & STATUS 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions This ISR is informed by the findings of the Mid-Term Review and supervision mission conducted from May 11–22, 2026. Overall, the project has achieved important implementation progress despite a challenging operating environment and significant contextual constraints. Procurement processes for school construction and WASH facilities have been launched and remain on track, with works expected to commence in September 2026. Given the security situation in Ituri Province, the project will leverage UNICEF’s implementation capacity to support the delivery of school construction and WASH activities in the affected areas. Procurement of textbooks is underway, and distribution to beneficiary schools is scheduled to begin in January 2027. While the Girls’ Scholarship Program experienced delays related to the verification of the pilot phase and the first year of the generalization phase, the corrective measures have been identified to accelerate implementation in preparation for the 2026–2027 school year. Some activities continue to face implementation delays, notably the procurement of IT equipment and the recruitment of 5,000 female teachers. Overall, project implementation remains on a positive trajectory, with a strong focus on accelerating execution and achieving planned results. 5.
