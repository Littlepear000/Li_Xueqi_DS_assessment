KEY ISSUES & STATUS 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions Dec 23, 2025 Page 1 of 10 The World Bank Implementation Status & Results Report Liberia Urban Resilience Project (P169718) Component 1 (Climate Resilient Infrastructure and Urban Upgrading) project activities are underway. The re-procurement process for the feasibility and detailed design consultancy has been finalized, and the selected firm is now onbaord of so far progress and quality of deliverables are good. Several new investment contracts—including household water connections, solar streetlights, stormwater drainage cleaning equipment, and pothole patching—have commenced and are currently at different stages of completion. These investments will serve 94,905 direct beneficiaries when completed in the next six months. Component 2 (Strengthening Integrated Resilient Urban Development Capacity) has experienced delays. The primary contract for preparing spatial development plans is facing significant setbacks. Within Component 2.2, support for Solid Waste Management (SWM) will be reduced, with a stronger focus on urban flood issues. Component 3 (Project Management) continues to encounter implementation challenges, particularly in strengthening the M&E system’s capacity. 5.
