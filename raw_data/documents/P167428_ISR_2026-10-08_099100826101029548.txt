KEY ISSUES & STATUS 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions The project continues to make progress across its five components, with an overall implementation rating of Moderately Satisfactory. Component 1 (Regional Economic Planning and Regulatory Functions) is advancing well. The Osh Region and City of Osh Development Strategies (2025–2030), prepared with project support, are being actively implemented through coordinated investments across industry, infrastructure, agriculture, tourism, and social services. The Digital Online Platform for Regional Development (aidstat.kg), now covering 230 projects from 95 units, was handed over to the Ministry of Finance in February 2026, strengthening transparency and efficiency in public investment management. Component 2 (Agri-Food Supply Chains and SMEs) has established 27 productive partnerships covering nearly 5,000 beneficiaries across key agricultural value chains, with additional contracts worth US$6 million expected by end-2026, bringing projected commitments to 92 percent. More than 8,200 farmers have benefited from training supporting capacity building and value chain participation. Component 3 (Catalyzing Investments for Tourism and Urban Development) remains a central focus of implementation. The flagship Suleiman-Too Complex and Uzgen Historical and Archaeological Complex are progressing, with the first civil works tender for Suleiman-Too launched in August 2026 and the Uzgen tender expected by end-October 2026. Public Private Cooperation initiative has helped mobilize over US$23 million in private investment and supported the creation of nearly 300 jobs, significantly exceeding the project's end target of US$4 million. Component 4 (Small Grants Program) continues to demonstrate strong results. To date, 232 businesses have received support across three phases, with US$2.79 million in total investment mobilized. Over 85 percent of Phase 1 projects are operational and generating revenue, with women accounting for 56 percent of beneficiaries in Phase 1 and 74 percent in the additional phase, contributing to more than 1,200 jobs created. Component 5 (Project Management) with fiduciary performance remains on track. The task team continues to provide hands-on implementation support, with coordination meetings and monthly reporting in place across all components. 5.
