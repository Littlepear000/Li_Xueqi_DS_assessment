KEY ISSUES & STATUS May 11, 2026 Official Use Only Page 1 of 9 The World Bank Implementation Status & Results Report Guinea – Mali Interconnection Project (P166042) 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions The implementation of the PIEGM is progressing, with an overall advancement rate of 85% for World Bank–financed infrastructure as of February 2026. This includes the construction of three new 225/30 kV substations at Siguiri (85% completed), Kankan (85% completed), and Kérouané (85% completed), along with the 225 kV transmission line (L2) from Siguiri to the Guinea–Mali border, which is 85% completed. In Mali, growing insecurity has driven the contractor to evacuate foreign experts, leading to contract management challenges. A meeting was held between the WB and EDM to address the issue, and it was agreed that a dedicated meeting with Tata will be organized to find an amicable resolution and ensure smooth continuation of the works. The AfDB-financed L4 line (Fomi–Kankan) is at 95% completion, and the Fomi substation (P5) is at 89%. The EIB-financed L5 line (Kankan–Kérouané–Beyla–Nzérékoré) is 86% complete, with supply almost completed and varying progress in foundations, tower assembly, and cable stringing. The Beyla and Nzérékoré substations (P6 & P7) are at 90%. The EBID- financed L3 line (Fomi–Siguiri) is at 61% due to delays in compensation and contract amendments. The joint AfDB/WADB- financed transmission line in Mali is 69% completed. Promptly addressing port congestion to accelerate equipment delivery, updating and consolidating integrated schedules for all lots, finalizing compensation for individuals affected by the project, strengthening contractor teams—particularly in delayed lots—and ensuring comprehensive implementation of all contractual and environmental/social safeguard requirements are essential. Finally, there is a need to accelerate the finalization of the energy purchase contract between EDG and EDM and to prepare for the operation and maintenance phase. 5.
