KEY ISSUES & STATUS Jun 01, 2026 Official Use Only Page 1 of 11 The World Bank Implementation Status & Results Report Log-In Georgia (P169698) 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions Project implementation is progressing well with Achievement of PDO and Implementation Progress continuing to be rated as 'Moderately Satisfactory.' All pending passive infrastructure construction lots are under implementation or procurement - see opennet.ge for a detailed infrastructure map. Procurement documents for active equipment are under review by the Government and expected to be launched by June 2026. The Digital Adoption and Inclusion Program has delivered over 900 training sessions in 300 villages, covering over 7,500 individuals. New training modules on the use of AI, online job search, and on the government portal for digital public services (mygov.ge) have been launched in March 2026. 5.
