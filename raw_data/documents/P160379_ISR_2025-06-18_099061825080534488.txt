OBJECTIVE 1.1 Development Objective Original Development Objective (Approved as part of Approval package on 29-Mar-2019) The Project Development Objectives (PDO) are to demonstrate the operational and economic feasibility of utility-scale innovative renewable energy technologies and battery energy storage solutions, and to strengthen institutional capacity to facilitate scale-up of such technologies on a commercial basis in India. KEY ISSUES & STATUS 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions Jun 18, 2025 Page 1 of 8 The World Bank Implementation Status & Results Report Innovation in Solar Power and Hybrid Technologies (P160379) SECI is implementing two sub-projects under this Project: 1. 100 Megawatt (MW) Solar Photovoltaic (PV) with a 40 MW/120 megawatt-hour (MWh) BESS in Rajnandgaon, Chhattisgarh: This sub-project was commissioned on February 1, 2024, and was formally inaugurated by the Honorable Prime Minister of India on February 24, 2024. The project is in operations since its commissioning. 2. 100 MW Floating Solar PV in Getalsud, Jharkhand: The contract was awarded in May 2024. The survey works have advanced well after a slow start. SECI and the contractor are working towards completing the sub-project as per the original contractual timelines (i.e., to commission the sub-project within 18 months from the effective date of the contract agreement). 5.
