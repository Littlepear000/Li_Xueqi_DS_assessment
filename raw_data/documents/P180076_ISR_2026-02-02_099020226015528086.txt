KEY ISSUES & STATUS 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions This interim progress update provides an overview of the project. According to the official progress reports and discussions made at federal and regional levels, the project implementation is progressing well. Two important planning documents—Woreda Disaster Risk Profiles (WDRPs) and Disaster Preparedness and Resilience Strategic Investment Plans (DPRSIPs)—are still in development. These are necessary for advancing climate-smart investments in the project and the project team has been asked to complete it soonest. 5.
