In collaboration with IGAD, member states established regional platform for improving coordination of desert locust (DL) interventions among IGAD member states and between IGAD and non-IGAD member states. The project supported member states to build capacity to monitor and respond to DL and other transboundary pests by streamlining locust monitoring, control, early warning, and preparedness policies and systems and by strengthening requisite capacity in the Horn of Africa to foster responsible pest management practices. The project helped member states to develop policies and regulatory frameworks for transboundary pest management, and adoption of biopesticide policies and guidelines. It was agreed to expand collaboration and training with member states on areas of expired pesticide management and disposal and to engage the Bank' E&S team in the forthcoming training programs for plant protection officers, focusing on the Proper Use of Biopesticides and Insect Growth Regulators. No I. PROJECT STATUS AND RATIONALE FOR RESTRUCTURING Summary 1. The Government of South Sudan requested a restructuring of International Development Association (IDA) grant D829-3E on July 22, 2022, to reallocate funds among categories and to slightly change the implementation arrangement. It was followed by another request letter on February 21, 20241, to extend the closing date. Intergovernmental Authority on Development (IGAD) has also submitted a restructuring request for IDA grant D839-3E on April 10, 2024, to extend the closing date as well. This restructuring paper seeks to: (i) Reallocate Special Drawing Rights (SDR) 5.7 million from Category 3 to Category 2 under IDA grant D829-3E. (ii) Extend the closing date from August 30, 2024, to June 30, 2025, for IDA grants D829-3E and D839-3E. 2. This is a level 2 restructuring for approval by the country director. Project Status 3. The Multiphase Programmatic Approach (MPA) Phase 3 of the South Sudan Emergency Locust Response Project (ELRP, P174546) was approved by the World Bank Board of Directors on June 8, 2021, with a closing date of August 30, 2024. The overall performance towards the achievement of Project Development Objective (PDO) is advancing well. Both, the progress towards PDO and Implementation Progress, are rated Satisfactory. Procurement, Monitoring and Evaluation, and Environment and Social Safeguards are rated Satisfactory. Project Management and Financial Management are rated Moderately Satisfactory requiring improvements in the internal control system and timely submission of quarterly interim financial reports. The project has disbursed 74 percent (US$38.34 million) and 55 percent (US$1.64 million) of the IDA grant to the government and the regional IDA grant to IGAD, respectively. All project components are being implemented satisfactorily. 4. The project has supported the government of South Sudan for improving the desert locust (DL) monitoring and control system, protecting and rehabilitating livelihoods of locust affected communities, food production and building the natural resource base for crops and livestock, and strengthening national preparedness system against desert locust and other migratory pests. The project has so far restored: (i) 305 hectares (ha) of DL affected pasture and rangeland against the end project target of 185 ha; (ii) 29,300 ha of DL affected agriculture land against the end project target of 35,000 ha; and (iii) surveyed 26,000 ha of DL affected land against the end project target of 45,000 ha target at the end of the project. The project has provided social safety nets to 45,929 DL affected households against the end target of 48,300 households. 5. IGAD component is working towards enhancing national and regional preparedness to respond to the threats posed by DL outbreaks. The project established regional platform for improving coordination of DL interventions among IGAD member states and between IGAD and non-IGAD member states; and building capacity to monitor and respond to DL and other transboundary pests is performing very well. The platform membership has expanded beyond the IGAD member states, to include Yemen, the International Center for Insect Physiology and Ecology (ICIPE), Center for Agriculture & Biosciences International, FAO Sub Regional Office for Eastern Africa. The Commission for Controlling the Desert Locust in the Central Region, International institute of Tropical Agriculture, European Space Agency Global Development Assistance program, the National Agricultural Research Organization of Uganda, and Association for Strengthening Agricultural Research in Eastern and Central Africa. 6. Guidelines to harmonize registration of biopesticides and policy briefs on DL control and biopesticide adoption have been prepared for endorsement of the next ministerial meeting. Guidelines for use of drones in agriculture are under preparation. Based on a capacity needs assessment for IGAD and non-IGAD member states, a capacity strengthening strategy was prepared. Accordingly, a comprehensive capacity building program is under implementation. Two working groups i.e., early warning systems group and capacity building working group are performing well. A review of the policies and regulations to support effective response at national and regional (cross-border) levels to facilitate synchronized management of transboundary pests, including DL is underway. 7. The Task Team confirms that: (i) the PDO is achievable; (ii) the Borrowers’ performance is satisfactory; (iii) the revised work plans have been prepared by the Government and IGAD to complete the project within the revised closing date; (iv) there are no overdue financial audit reports, or interim financial reports; and (v) the project complies with all the legal covenants. 8. The proposed project restructuring would enable the project to extend the closing date to allow the full achievement of the PDO and contribute to the sustainability of project outcomes. II. DESCRIPTION OF PROPOSED CHANGES 9. The proposed changes are as follows: (i) reallocate funds among withdrawal categories from category 3 to category 2 and (ii) revise the closing dates of two grants thus resulting in an extension of the implementation schedule. 10. In the project design, United Nations Office for Project Services (UNOPS) is responsible to implement sub- component 2.1 whereas United Nations Food and Agriculture Organization (FAO) is responsible for the implementation of sub-component 2.2 including identifying, registering and employing the beneficiaries through labor intensive public The World Bank Emergency Locust Response Project (P174546) works. Activities under component 2.1 were for 11 months and have been completed. Under sub-component 2.2, UNOPS role is limited to cash transfers i.e. making the final payment to the same beneficiaries recruited and supervised by FAO. In the government’s experience, UNOPS’ involvement in implementing activities under sub component 2.2 has added complications and significantly increased the administrative costs with no value added for services that FAO is well placed to complete including making the final payment to the beneficiaries. The proposed change of scope to shift the implementation of all the activities under sub-components 2.2 to FAO will simplify the implementation arrangements and significantly reduce the administrative cost. The Government of South Sudan has therefore requested a reallocation of funds between categories as a result of adjustments in the scope of work of the two agencies during implementation to have one implementing partner execute all the activities under sub-component 2.2. The withdrawal schedule is, thus, proposed to be revised to reallocate part of the proceeds from category 3 to 2 to have one single implementing partner execute all sub-component 2.2 activities. 11. In addition, the proposed change responds to the recipient request to extend the closing date of IDA grants D829-3E and D839-3E from August 31, 2024, to June 30, 2025. To ensure full completion of all the outstanding works especially completion of the ongoing labor-intensive public works and the finalization of the pesticide storage facilities within the revised closing date, the Bank task team has endorsed the revised timelines and work plans prepared by the Government of South Sudan for IDA Grant D8230 and IGAD for IDA Grant D839-3E.
