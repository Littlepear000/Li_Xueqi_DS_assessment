KEY ISSUES & STATUS 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions The Contingency Emergency Response Component (CERC) activities to support the Governments of Grenada, Saint-Lucia and Saint Vincent and the Grenadines in response to the food insecurity crisis were completed on March 31, 2025, with the financial closure in July 2025. The project across the three participating countries has since commenced small procurement of the agreed Blue Economy (BE) activities in the fisheries, tourism and waste sectors. These need to accelerate to move infrastructure and rehabilitation works forward and, to deliver tangible results. This is being enabled by strong and functional project implementation units in SLU, Grenada and OECS Commission, while SVG still needs to secure a full complement of personnel. The tourism and waste management sector activities advanced the most in this reporting period particularly in Grenada and Saint Lucia with design and supervision consultancy procurements underway for selected intervention sites, and procurement for key equipments advancing. Overall, the regional activities under the OECS Commission are progressing well, particularly under the Matching Grants Program targeting Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs) in the three countries with 28 blue MSMEs (focusing on fisheries, tourism and solid waste sectors) accessing financing against a final target of 75, and 119 firms have benefited from business development services against a target of 120. Women-owned or managed enterprises represent 36% of grantees. Significantly, the project has established in January 2026, a functional and joint Regional Project Steering Committee with representation from all three participating countries, led by the OECS Commission. 5.
