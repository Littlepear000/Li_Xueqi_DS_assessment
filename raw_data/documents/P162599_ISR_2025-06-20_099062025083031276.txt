KEY ISSUES & STATUS 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions As the Benin e-Agriculture Project (P162599) approaches its closing date of June 30, 2025, implementation is progressing satisfactorily, with all critical activities either completed or nearing completion. The project has achieved or surpassed most of its development objectives, including significant improvements in rural broadband access and the adoption of digital solutions in agriculture and financial inclusion. As of May 31, 2025, the disbursement rate stands at 80 percent and is expected to reach approximately 95 percent by project closure. Key achievements include the deployment of 119 mobile sites providing broadband coverage to over 400,000 people, the training of over 29,000 individuals in digital financial literacy, and the enrollment of nearly 200,000 farmers on digital platforms. The final Implementation Completion and Results Report (ICR), due by December 31, 2025, will provide a comprehensive assessment of results, lessons learned, and sustainability considerations. 5.
