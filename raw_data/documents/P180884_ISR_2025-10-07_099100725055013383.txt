KEY ISSUES & STATUS 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions Oct 07, 2025 Page 1 of 9 The World Bank Implementation Status & Results Report Sustainable Cities and Land Project (P180884) The project is on track of a good start of implementation. The Decree of Rehabilitation was signed by the President of the Republic on September 11, 2025, and the Financing Agreement was countersigned on October 1, 2025. Progress towards meeting the effectiveness conditions is at an advanced stage, including recruiting the project Coordinator and Procurement Specialist, establishing the Project Steering Committee, and finalizing the Project Procedures Manual and Operations Manual. Significant progress has been made among all the effectiveness conditions and the legal covenants that are due one or two months after project effectiveness. Substantive progress has been achieved across all the components, relative to the current stage of the project. Progress in fiduciary, E&S, and project management aspects are satisfactory. 5.
