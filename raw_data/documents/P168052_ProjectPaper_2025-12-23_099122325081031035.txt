PROJECT STATUS AND RATIONALE FOR RESTRUCTURING Project Status: 1. Background. The Socio-Economic Resilience Strengthening Project (SERSP) was approved on June 20, 2019 for an IDA grant of US$37 million and became effective on July 8, 2020. The Project is implemented by the National Social Investment Fund of Tajikistan (NSIFT) and aims to strengthen socio-economic resilience in targeted communities through community-based investments, building capacity of local government, improved local inclusion, and access to opportunities. The Parent Project comes to an end on December 31, 2025. The Additional Financing (AF), in the amount of US$25 million, was approved on May 19, 2025 and became effective on October 10, 2025. 2. Focus of the AF. The AF uses its proven community-driven-development and livelihoods approach to extend SERSP’s coverage to 7 new districts and 46 jamoats. It builds on the Government’s successful experience with community- driven development (CDD) to complement Rogun HPP livelihood restoration activities and pilot approaches that can inform the Rogun HPP benefit sharing program (which will have limited revenues in the early years of Rogun construction). It also aligns with Government of Tajikistan’s objectives to improve the role of local institutions in economic development, alleviation of social and gender imbalances, and local-led climate change adaptation. It also aligned with the World Bank Tajikistan’s Country Partnership Framework (2019-2024) and focusses on addressing regressive gender norms, promote livelihood opportunities for women in rural settings, enhance local government capacity, and ensure affordable, climate resilient energy. 3. Geographic coverage of the AF. The AF will expand SERSP coverage to include Rogun-affected jamoats (70% of AF community investments) and selected jamoats with higher shares of disadvantaged population (the remaining 30%) in other parts of the country. In the Rogun-affected area, the AF will cover jamoats in the three districts and two cities (Faizobad, Nurobad, and Danghara districts, Rogun and Tursunzoda cities) that include resettlement villages from the Rogun HPP (5 jamoats, Darband small town with 8 resettlement villages and an additional 27 jamoats across the five districts. In addition, it will also cover all the jamoats in Qubodiyon district in Khatlon and Roshtqal’a district in GBAO selected based on the lowest per capita income. 4. PDO and implementation progress ratings. The Parent Project (PP) is on track to meet targets by the end of the PP (December 31, 2025). By December 31, 2025, under Component 1, 225 subprojects will be completed across target districts of Khatlon and GBAO. Under Component 2, 22,398 youth have benefited from training on mind masters, upshift, digital literacy, healthy lifestyles and psychosocial support. 6,162 beneficiaries have received entrepreneurship training and 2,400 have benefited from grants to start micro businesses. Since becoming effective, the AF has also initiated staffing changes, where required, and all procurements to support implementation of components 1&2 and to conduct the baseline study in the new districts. NSIFT has also completed the initial outreach meetings at the district level in all new districts and has plans in place to begin mobilization in the 46 target jamoats. 5. Rationale for Restructuring. Based on a formal request from the Government of Tajikistan, the World Bank team recommends restructuring the Additional Financing of the Socio-Economic Resilience Strengthening Project (SERSP). The reason for restructuring early in the AF implementation period is to cover an unexpected financing gap occurred in the PP due to the exchange rate fluctuations between April-October 2025. This led to a financing gap of $520,000. Around half of this gap in the Parent Project is being covered through the reallocation of eligible parent activities to the AF ($120,000 of PIU staff salaries incurred since AF signing and $120,000 of parent project sanitary facilities). However, this still leaves a financing gap of around $280,000 in SERSP parent project commitments for existing subprojects in one district. These expenses cannot be financed by the AF without restructuring as the AF financing agreement (FA) specifies the” Project Official Use Only Page 1 The World Bank Tajikistan Socio-Economic Resilience Strengthening Project (P168052) Area” to include only the new districts under the AF and does not include districts covered under the SERSP Parent Project. To accommodate the request from the client, the World Bank team proposes restructuring the AF FA to include Shamsidiin Shohin district in the project area. Restructuring the AF now is critical to enable financing the remaining construction for one SERSP subproject still under construction and to cover the 5% defect liability period for nine sub-projects completed late in the Parent Project period for this district. This will include reallocation of $280,000 from Category (1) to Category (2) of the AF. Due to the budget constraints, the AF is also covering specific monitoring and evaluation costs for the parent project (endline survey) that is being conducted together with the AF baseline. The Project Development Objective (PDO) remains unchanged. There are no overdue audits. This is the first restructuring of the Additional Financing. II. DESCRIPTION OF PROPOSED CHANGES 6. The proposed restructuring will make the following changes to the SERSP AF: (i) Shamsidiin Shohiin district will be added to the definition of “Additional Project Areas” in the AF financing agreement and (ii) the withdrawal table in Section III.A. of Schedule 2 to the financing agreement will be updated to reflect the reallocation of $280,000 from Category (1) to Category (2) of the AF. This will enable SERSP to use $180,000 of AF funds to complete the rehabilitation of a 6- classroom classroom in Shamsidiin Shohiin district, and around $100,000 of AF funds for 5% retention payments for completed SPs in Shamsidiin Shohiin to be paid to contractors one year after construction was completed/certified. The SERSP and SERSP AF PDO, intermediate results indicators, project targets, timeline will remain unchanged.
