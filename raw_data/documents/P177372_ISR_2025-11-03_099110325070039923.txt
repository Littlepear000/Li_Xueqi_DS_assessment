KEY ISSUES & STATUS 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions Nov 03, 2025 Page 1 of 8 The World Bank Implementation Status & Results Report Gabon Urban Development Project (P177372) The project, approved on June 11, 2025, aims to improve access to climate-resilient urban infrastructure and reduce flood risk in selected cities. Effectiveness steps are advancing, with the Project Operations Manual cleared by the Bank and legal opinions accepted. The Government and the World Bank are working to accelerate readiness—finalizing technical and environmental studies for priority investments, establishing interministerial steering and technical committees, and staffing the PIU. Procurement preparation is underway, and the grievance mechanism will be operational shortly after effectiveness. The project’s risk profile is being actively managed, with a strong focus on institutional capacity, fiduciary processes, and environmental and social safeguards. 5.
