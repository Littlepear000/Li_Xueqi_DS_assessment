KEY ISSUES & STATUS 3.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions There has been reasonable progress recorded under the project, including: (a) agreement signed with a payment provider for the hybrid social protection savings scheme, and the enrolment module made functional in all tehsils of the 15 pilot districts; (b) State Bank of Pakistan issued regulations for opening of “Social Protection Wallets” by the current six banks engaged by BISP for beneficiary payments, and task commenced to ensure POS level interoperability for these banks; (c) development of the cloud based NSER data sharing model on track; and (d) the Integrated Grievance Management System being rolled out to all field offices in a phased manner.
