KEY ISSUES & STATUS Apr 16, 2026 Official Use Only Page 1 of 8 The World Bank Implementation Status & Results Report Strengthening Lebanon’s Covid-19 Response (P178587) 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions The Project was approved by the World Bank on May 15, 2022, for US$25 million (composed of an IBRD loan and concessional financing) and a US$4 million grant. The project is being implemented by the MoPH. The Project falls under the COVID-19 Strategic Preparedness and Response Program (SPRP), using a Multiphase Programmatic Approach (MPA) that was approved by the World Bank’s Board of Executive Directors in April 2020, as well as additional financing approved by the World Bank in October 2020. The Project has been declared effective on December 20, 2022. A Project restructuring was approved on September 24, 2024, to reallocate project funds for priority activities including the procurement of medical equipment and supplies for public hospitals and extend the project closing date by 15 months, from September 30, 2024, to December 30, 2025. The Project enabled the large-scale procurement and deployment of essential medical equipment, significantly expanding service delivery capacity and strengthening the resilience of the health system across the entire public hospital network. 4.2 MPA Program Development Objective Original MPA Program Development Objective (Approved as part of (Seq. 0) package on ) The Program Development Objective is to prevent, detect and respond to the threat posed by COVID-19 and strengthen national systems for public health preparedness Current MPA Program Development Objective (Approved as part of (Seq. ) package on ) 5.
