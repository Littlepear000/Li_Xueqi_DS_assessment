KEY ISSUES & STATUS May 05, 2026 Official Use Only Page 1 of 9 The World Bank Implementation Status & Results Report Sustainable Cities and Land Project (P180884) 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions Overall, the project is satisfactory in its progress of implementation and towards achievement of PDO. The project was signed on October 1, 2025, and became effective on January 21, 2026 after meeting all the three effectiveness conditions. The legal covenants have been complied with or are nearly complied with. Most of the PIU key staff have been onboard through competitive recruitment, and the UTLs have been established. The first large works and control contracts are at the final stage ready for signing. Priority goods and consulting services contracts under Component 1 and 2 have started the procurement process or with TORs at the final stage. However, there have been delays in comparison with the work plan, procurement plan, and disbursement projection. It was agreed that the action plan will be diligently followed up by the PIU to accelerate progress. The Bank will assess the progress and performance at the next mission. 5.
