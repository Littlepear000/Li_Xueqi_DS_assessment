The focus in the coming months is to put the main implementation arrangements in place and begin the priority actions under the program. A key priority is advancing preparation for the CREMA road improvement contracts, which are central to improving road conditions, safety, and resilience. Work is also moving forward on the PPP agenda, with coordination among the main state institutions and partners.At the same time, the program is advancing actions in road safety, mobility, climate resilience, and institutional strengthening, including preparations with municipalities and partner agencies.
