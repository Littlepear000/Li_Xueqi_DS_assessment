OBJECTIVE 1.1 Development Objective Original Development Objective (Approved as part of Approval package on 02-Mar-2021) The proposed Project Development Objective (PDO) is (i) to enhance connectivity and road safety along the Zinder - Agadez road section in Northeastern Niger through the rehabilitation of the Tanout – Tiguidit road segment and maintenance of the full Zinder - Agadez section, and (ii) to improve access to basic socio-economic infrastructure for selected communities, along the Zinder - Agadez road section. KEY ISSUES & STATUS 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions The project is on track to achieve its objectives by the revised closing date of September 2031. However, it is currently facing safeguards and security risks, including delays caused by a recent security incident. While some progress has been made on the maintenance front, rehabilitation work has not yet resumed after the security incident. The World Bank has recently approved a $90 million additional financing to reconstruct the Tiguidit-Agadez (62 km) section). Road safety remains a concern, with limited risk mitigation measures completed so far. Continued attention to safety, security, contract management, and timely effectiveness of the Additional Financing is necessary to address these challenges and keep the project on track for completion by September 2031. 5.
