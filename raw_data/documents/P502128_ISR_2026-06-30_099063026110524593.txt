KEY ISSUES & STATUS 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions After approximately 18 months of implementation, the project has made significant progress across several components, although overall performance remains below initial expectations. Key achievements include the following sub-components: Construction and rehabilitation of school infrastructure: Of the 741 infrastructure works planned under the first phase of the program, progress indicates that 610 classrooms, 111 latrine blocks with three cabins each, and 20 boreholes are expected to be completed by the end of August 2026. Construction of the five Regional Pedagogical Centers (CPRs): The construction work of the 5 CPRs in Sibut, Bouali, Mobaye, Batangafo and Nola is progressing normally. The work on the Sibut CPR has been completed and provisionally accepted. The work of the other CPRs is more than 80% advanced. Current progress indicates that all five centers are expected to be completed by August 2026. Design and printing of textbooks: Thirty titles of language (including Sango at the experimental level) and mathematics textbooks and teachers’ guides, have been developed by national authors in line with international quality standards. More than 2,738,000 copies are currently being printed and will be distributed to all primary school students in public and private schools at the start of the September/October 2026 school year. A “track and trace” system has also been developed to strengthen textbook distribution. Improving the number of qualified teachers in the school system: A total of 3,250 primary school teachers across 10 Academy Inspectorates, 29 percent of whom are women, have been trained in the use of the harmonized curriculum and explicit pedagogy. In addition, 5,133 qualified contract teachers have been recruited and deployed nationwide for the 2025–2026 academic year, and their contracts are being renewed for the next school year. Implementation of the accelerated education and remediation programs: Implementation is proceeding as agreed, with 22,235 students enrolled in the accelerated education program, including 11,235 girls, and 23,341 students enrolled in the remediation program, including 10,949 girls, during the current school year. Key decision The Government will submit a formal request to GPE to extend the October 2026 deadline for integrating 3000 contract teachers into the civil service. 5.
