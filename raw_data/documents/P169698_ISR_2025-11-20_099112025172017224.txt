KEY ISSUES & STATUS 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions The Project's Additional Financing was approved on July 25, 2025 and is expected to be Effective by November 25, 2025. Nov 20, 2025 Page 1 of 11 The World Bank Implementation Status & Results Report Log-In Georgia (P169698) The Project’s Implementation Progress (IP) and Achievement of PDO ratings have been retained as ‘Moderately Satisfactory’ due to delays in implementation of key passive infrastructure development contracts under Component 1 and in launch of the active infrastructure procurement. Mitigation steps to avoid such delays in the future include launching parallel construction of multiple lots as part of the Additional Financing and enhancing procurement capacity. The Project has disbursed US$25.4 million (32% of committed amount). Co-financing with European Investment Bank (EIB) is effective and contracts under implementation are co-financed. Procurement of active infrastructure under Subcomponent 1.1 is to be launched by December 2025 and expected to drive disbursement rate higher. 5.
