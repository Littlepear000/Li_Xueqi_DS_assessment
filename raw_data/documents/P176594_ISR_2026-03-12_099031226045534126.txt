KEY ISSUES & STATUS 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions Mar 12, 2026 Page 1 of 21 The World Bank Implementation Status & Results Report Getting Results: Access and Delivery of Quality Education Services in Punjab Project (P176594) Following some initial delays, the project has gained implementation momentum after the appointment of a new Project Director. Progress has accelerated across recruitment, procurement, and other implementation arrangements. Key implementation enablers, including the costed Annual Workplan, POM, near-complete staffing, and ongoing procurement for IVA and TA firms are now largely in place. Early results have been achieved across several PBCs, reflected in increased disbursements. Accountability arrangements have also been strengthened, supporting improved overall implementation performance. The project PC-1 is being revised to accommodate the government's proposed implementation arrangements for infrastructure works. Expeditious approval of the PC-1 is critical to keep the project on track. 5.
