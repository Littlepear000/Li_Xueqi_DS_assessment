KEY ISSUES & STATUS 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions The development objectives of the project are to (i) increase the coverage of Egypt’s UHIS in Phase I Governorates, (ii) strengthen UHIS-related governance and institutions, and (iii) provide temporary financial protection against high out of pocket health expenditures for vulnerable populations outside Phase I Governorates.
This ISR will be updated once the Government provides a new progress report that includes key developments from July to December 2025, such as:
(i) the launch of UHIS in Aswan in July 2025;
(ii) the issuance of the Prime Ministers memo on automatic enrollment of the Takaful and Karama beneficiaries into UHIS; and (iii) progress with verification of achieved PBCs.
