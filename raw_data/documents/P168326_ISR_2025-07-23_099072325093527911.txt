KEY ISSUES & STATUS 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions The project is in its last calendar year of implementation. The only remaining activity is the rehabilitation of the Yamchun Fortress located in GBAO. The works are well underway with the Fortress walls reinforced to avoid the threat of the Fortress's collapsing. The remaining activities (25%) include the completion of the construction of the sightseeing deck, restrooms, and the bridge connecting the main road and the Fortress. The works at the Yamchun Fortress are scheduled to be completed by the end of the calendar year 2025. The current works are in line with the agreed time-bound action plan. The three cultural heritage sites in Khatlon have been completed. The Castle Khulbuk has been inscribed on the UNESCO World Heritage List by the decision adopted on July 12, 2025, during the 47th session of the World Heritage Committee in Paris, as one of the most significant parts of the cultural heritage monuments of Ancient Khuttal. The project's community-based and matching grants program has been successfully completed, achieving the targets of all the results indicators agreed upon for the program. As the project has been focused on improving the sources of income for the population in GBAO and Khatlon, particular attention was paid to women-led MSMEs, assisting them, through facilitators, to prepare applications for matching grants, develop relevant business plans, and implement their projects. 605 MSMEs, out of which 50% are women-led, upgraded their tourism-related facilities and agribusiness equipment. Close to 3500 documented jobs, out of which 1150 are for women, have been generated under the project. The project's funds have been fully disbursed. 5.
