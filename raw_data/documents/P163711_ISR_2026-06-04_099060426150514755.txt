KEY ISSUES & STATUS 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions The Tax Administration and Statistical System Modernization Project is under implementation in the Kyrgyz Republic. For the State Tax Service (STS), to-date, the project has delivered the following results: a total of 1,101 STS staff has been trained under the project, nearly three times the end-of-project target of 400; the number of electronic services available to taxpayers has reached, to date, 35, surpassing the target of 27, and the e-filing rate for payroll (PAYE) withholding tax has reached 100% to date, reflecting a significant shift toward digital compliance. A new STS institutional strategy has been developed and approved. For the National Statistical Committee (NSC), the project is delivering tangible results across institutional, digital, and capacity dimensions. The NSC has completed its transition to a two-tier organizational structure, strengthening institutional capacity for coordinated statistical production. Eight electronic reporting forms were launched in December 2025 with approximately 10,000 respondents registered, and an additional 30 forms are in advanced testing for rollout in early 2026. The project has supported the establishment of 10 courses in Official Statistics and Data Science in local universities, building a pipeline of future statistical professionals. 5.
