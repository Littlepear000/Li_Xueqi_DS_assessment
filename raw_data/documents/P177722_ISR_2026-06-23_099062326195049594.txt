KEY ISSUES & STATUS 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions The primary implementation challenge has been the delayed operationalization of the Partial Risk Sharing Facility (PRSF) instrument, pending the issuance of the Presidential Decree establishing the National Guarantee Fund. Decree #364 was issued on June 9, 2026, resolving this pending issue. While awaiting the Decree, the Ministry of Finance PIU advanced preparatory activities, including: (i) procurement and installation of the Management Information System (MIS) required for the PRSF's launch and operation; (ii) adoption of the PRSF Procedural Manual, satisfactory to the World Bank; and (iii) preparation of Terms of Reference for the Fund Manager and relevant technical experts. The Environmental and Social Management System (ESMS), developed as part of the PRSF Procedural Manual, has received World Bank approval. The PRSF is expected to be launched by fall 2026. A second key issue concerns the reallocation of US$5 million from the unallocated funds category to other project components. Under current project arrangements, such reallocation requires a formal project restructuring. The Ministry of Finance submitted a restructuring request, with a proposed allocation across eligible expenditure categories, in its letter dated April 10, 2026. The proposed allocation was discussed and agreed upon with the World Bank project team during the June 2026 technical mission. The proposed reallocation includes: (i) activities related to capital market development; and (ii) introduction of "Works" as an eligible expenditure category, consistent with the feasibility study for the rehabilitation of the building designated by the Government for the PRSF Development Center. 5.
