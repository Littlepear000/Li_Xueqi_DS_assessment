KEY ISSUES & STATUS 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions • • • • • • • • Research Projects: 304 research projects have been initiated (exceeding the target of 209). The target of establishing eight institutional alliances has been achieved, with seven focusing on climate research. Ph.D. Scholarships: 303 students have been selected, including 266 Ph.D. students with project-funded scholarships in climate-related programs. Among them,138 are female, making up 45% of the group. Lab Strengthening Grants: 35 research labs have been equipped and strengthened. Lab Accreditation Grants: Nine labs have been chosen and are in the implementation phase. Services provided by accredited labs: The laboratories have provided over 8,932 services for six value chains. Academia dialogues facilitated: A total of 24 public meetings were conducted with firms, universities, and support institutions across selected value chains (avocado, mango, blueberry, grapes, ethanol, and cotton).Seven meetings took place outside Lima and eight were related to climate. Loan Resource Execution: 171% execution of resources allocated under Components 2 and 3 (US$78.8 million) towards activities in the climate strategic area. Surveys Implementation: Two surveys completed (National Social Perception; Scientific Equipment), one ongoing (R&D survey to research centers), and three pending (R&D Survey to Firms – ENIDE; Science and Technology Activities – ENACT; Technology Adoption in Firms – ENATE). 5.
