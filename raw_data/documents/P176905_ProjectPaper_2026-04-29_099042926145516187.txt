PROJECT STATUS AND RATIONALE FOR RESTRUCTURING A. Introduction and Purpose 1. This Restructuring Paper seeks the approval of the Country Director for a Level-2 restructuring of the Climate Intelligent and Inclusive Agrifood Systems Project (CIAF/AGRO XXI, P176905) in Argentina, financed by IBRD Loan No. 9313AR, to reflect the cancellation requested by the Government of Argentina. The proposed restructuring would (i) advance the Project Closing Date to March 31st, 2026 and (ii) cancel US$243.38 million from the unwithdrawn balance, with associated reallocations to align remaining proceeds to the reduced scope through closure. No changes are proposed to the Project Development Objective or the environmental and social risk classification. The rationale for the restructuring is to align the operation with the Government’s revised priorities and fiscal envelope while safeguarding benefits from activities completed or near completion. 2. The Climate Intelligent and Inclusive Agri-food Systems Project was approved on December 9, 2021, and became effective on March 21, 2022. The IBRD Loan 9313-AR has a net commitment of US$326.09 million (original loan amount US$400 million), with an original and current closing date of June 15, 2027. As of March 31st, 2026, cumulative disbursements amount to approximately US$82.71 million (about 25 percent of net commitments), with an undisbursed balance of around US$243.38 million. Implementation progress has been severely constrained since mid-December 2023 due to the new Government’s redefinition of priorities, institutional realignments, and tight budget allocations, resulting in a de facto standstill in activities throughout 2024–2025. B. Project Status 3. The PDO is to: (i) support economic recovery and promote climate-smart practices among project beneficiaries in Argentina’s agri-food system; and (ii) respond effectively in case of an eligible crisis or emergency. Progress toward the PDO is rated Unsatisfactory, and overall implementation progress is rated Moderately Unsatisfactory. No physical investments were executed under Component 1 (Public Infrastructure for Agro-industrial Development). Component 2 (Agro-industrial Development and Strengthening of Rural Livelihoods) registered partial progress, most notably the construction of 2,620 slab cisterns for rainwater harvesting (95 percent of the 2,772 tendered), with payments pending for a subset and a planned contract addendum to rescind approximately 152 units not executed. Under the livelihoods and agro-industrial entrepreneurship windows, 165 agreements were signed; many are partially executed. Component 3 (Innovation for a Green and Inclusive Agri- food System Transformation) advanced institutional strengthening procurements in 2023–2024; no planned tasks were executed in 2025, and several processes require orderly closure. Component 4 (Project Management and Evaluation) requires consolidation of monitoring, fiduciary, and environmental and social (E&S) closure documentation to support project close- out. Given the proposed cancellation of the loan at the Borrower’s request, the PDO will no longer be achievable within the life of the operation. 4. Overall Fiduciary performance is mixed. Financial management performance is rated Moderately Satisfactory (MS), with moderate shortcomings that affect timeliness and completeness of information, including: (i) uncertainty in budget allocation; (ii) overdue audit deliverables, and (iii) pending justifications and designated account reconciliation. Procurement performance is rated as Moderately Unsatisfactory (MU), reflecting limited implementation progress, capacity constraints at both central and decentralized levels, and the complexity of arrangements involving multiple stakeholders. Procurement implementation has not progressed during the last period, and required updates in STEP remain outstanding. The Procurement Plan continues to include 91 activities, of which 15 are canceled, 10 are signed, 32 are pending implementation, and 34 are under implementation, all with delays, indicating no recent improvement. The Bank has requested that Official Use Only Page 1 The World Bank Climate Intelligent and Inclusive Agri-food Systems Project (P176905) all contract modifications, including rescissions, amendments, and cancellations, shall be fully documented in the corresponding process files and accurately reflected in STEP; for contracts subject to prior review, the Bank’s No Objection through STEP is required prior to formalization. Expenditures associated with the implementation of subprojects in Santa Victoria del Este (Province of Salta) are currently under review due to inconsistencies in the centralized procurement processes, non-compliance with applicable Procurement Regulations, and the omission of the Bank’s Fraud and Corruption provisions in both the procurement processes and the resulting contracts. 5. ESF performance is rated Moderately Satisfactory overall. While there were delays earlier in the project in the submission of required reporting and documentation, the PIU has since made progress in addressing key Environmental and Social (E&S) requirements. In particular, the E&S closure report for the cisterns constructed under Component 2 has been submitted, allowing the Bank to complete the formal E&S oversight for these activities. The PIU has also informed on the status of the signed agreements under the rural livelihoods subcomponent in Component 2. There are no outstanding items posing material E&S risks at this point. C. Rationale for restructuring 6. The Government of Argentina has formally requested an advance of the Project Closing Date and cancellation of the remaining undisbursed balance of the Loan for the Climate Intelligent and Inclusive Agri-food Systems Project (CIAF/AGRO XXI, P176905). As part of the Government of Argentina’s second-round review of the World Bank portfolio initiated in December 2024, aimed at aligning Bank financing with the country’s strategic priorities, the activities financed under this Project were determined not to be immediate priorities. Consequently, following completion of all required Environmental and Social Framework (ESF) and fiduciary due diligence, the Project will be closed, and the uncommitted US$243.38 million from the IBRD Loan AR-9313 will be canceled. Unused funds already disbursed to the Designated Account (around US$31.7 million) will be reimbursed to the World Bank. II. DESCRIPTION OF PROPOSED CHANGES A. Scope of restructuring 7. The proposed restructuring entails: (i) cancellation of the undisbursed balance of the IBRD Loan; and (ii) associated updates to the Legal Agreement (including the Disbursement Table and the new Closing Date), Results Framework, and relevant Implementation and monitoring documentation to reflect early termination and loan cancellation. No change in the PDO, components, or implementation arrangements is proposed beyond actions required to execute the close-out. 8. Cancellation and advancing the closing date: a cancellation of loan proceeds in the amount of US$243.38 Million of the loan and the advance of the closing date from June 15th, 2027, to March 31st, 2026, as requested by the Government of Argentina. This cancellation of the uncommitted portion of Loan 9313-AR will be upon completion of the close-out actions described below. As of March 31st, 2026, US$ 51,585,096 remained in the Designated Account (DA) as an undocumented advance. In accordance with World Bank disbursement policies, the Borrower will be required to submit withdrawal applications documenting eligible expenditures incurred prior to the closing date and/or refund any undocumented balance to the Bank, During the grace period, the PIU will reconciliate its designated accounts, document and justify expenditures incurred prior to the closing date, update and complete implementation and contract registries in STEP and obtain the Bank’s no objection to prior review activities, and resolve pending audit observations on internal controls. 9. Disbursements: No new disbursements are anticipated prior to cancellation. Official Use Only Page 2 The World Bank Climate Intelligent and Inclusive Agri-food Systems Project (P176905) B.
