KEY ISSUES & STATUS 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions Nov 24, 2025 Page 1 of 8 The World Bank Implementation Status & Results Report Cambodia Basic Education Improvement Project (P508313) The project was approved by the Board on August 29, 2025, and the Financing Agreement and Grant Agreement were counter-signed on October 3, 2025. The project is expected to become effective by December 31, 2025. Key achievements include: (i) the establishment of the Project Management Unit (PMU) to supervise project implementation, (ii) the initiation of procurement activities following the approval of the procurement plan in the Systematic Tracking of Exchanges in Procurement (STEP) system, and (iii) the completion of necessary preparatory activities. Additionally, the project launching is scheduled on December 9, 2025 and the first implementation support mission is expected in April 2026. 5.
