KEY ISSUES & STATUS 3.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions Progress towards achievement of the project development objectives and Overall implementation progress ratings are maintained as Moderately Unsatisfactory (MU) due to the persistent lack of progress under Result Area #2 on the development of the four wholesale markets (WMs). Under Results Area 1 (RA1), which focuses on improving employment and income-generating opportunities for rural youth, Disbursement-Linked Indicator (DLI) 1.6 on the preparation of business plans eligible for financing by the Regional Youth Entrepreneurship Committees (CREJ) has already achieved 87 percent of its end-of- program target, with 8,736 validated business plans against a target of 10,000. DLI 3.4, which measures the number of young people who have accessed public financing to implement their projects, has exceeded its final target of 16,000 beneficiaries, reaching 105 percent completion. Similarly, PDO Indicator 1 has already reached The World Bank Implementation Status & Results Report Morocco Green Generation Program-for-Results (P170419) Sep 30, 2026 Page 2 of 26 Official Use Only 22,028 young people who have their agricultural, para-agricultural, and/or digital projects operational and/or under implementation, representing 169 percent achievement against the program’s end target of 13,000 young people, demonstrating the strong momentum of public support initiatives. With regard to Results Area 3 (RA3), which aims to strengthen agricultural digitalization and the adoption of climate-smart practices, steady progress has also been recorded. Development of digital agricultural advisory tools continues, notably through the operationalization of the ARDNA virtual platform for communication and agricultural advisory support, which has 8,496 registered users against a target of 7,500. As of the end of June, PDO Indicator 3 had reached 63 percent of the 2026 target, with 3,798 producers adopting climate-smart agricultural practices compared to a target of 6,000 for 2026. The current disbursement rate stands at 76 percent for the World Bank and 75 percent for AFD, both including and excluding advances. It should be noted that the full recovery of advances has been completed for both financing institutions. The lack of progress on the four wholesale markets (WMs) makes it urgently necessary to proceed with the Program's restructuring, including cancellation of the four wholesale market projects and reallocation of the funds thereby released, in order to ensure the Program's effective continuation and maximize results in other areas, particularly those related to Result Area 1 on rural youth entrepreneurship. 4.
