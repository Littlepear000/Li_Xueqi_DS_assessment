KEY ISSUES & STATUS 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions Progress toward the Project Development Outcome (PDO) remains satisfactory. By April 2026, cumulative disbursements are US$30.92 million, representing around 52% of total financing. The Somali Integrated Business Establishment Survey is complete except for Puntland, and analysis is ongoing. Listing for the Multi-Indicator Cluster Survey is scheduled to begin June/July, and effectiveness conditions for the Somaliland component are expected to be completed by the end of June, as confirmed to the WB team during its recent support mission to Hargeisa (April 27–29, 2026). Preparation for the Somalia Integrated Household Budget Survey (SIHBS) is underway, with data collection anticipated in the second half of 2026. Regional CPI continues to be published. The project is showing strong outcomes: The World Bank's Statistical Performance Indicators (SPI) have increased from 25.9 in 2019 to 51.7 in 2024; the number of UNSD core gender indicators reported by Somalia nearly doubled from 18 in 2022 to 34 in 2023; and user satisfaction rose from 55 percent in 2021 to 78 percent in 2025. 5.
