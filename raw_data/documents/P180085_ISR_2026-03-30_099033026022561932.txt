KEY ISSUES & STATUS 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions This Implementation Status and Results Report is based on face-to-face implementation support missions that were conducted in the respective project participating countries and regional organization between november 2025 and February 2026. Progress has been made in the implementation of all project components . In addition, since the last ISR, the project has made solid progress toward its development outcomes. Cameroon: Since the last ISR, the project has transitioned from effectiveness to early implementation, with all effectiveness conditions met and a fully staffed, operational PIU. Preparatory work has advanced on key activities, including preparations for the Sixth Cameroon Household Survey and the seventh round of MICS, as well as early steps toward rehabilitating the national statistics office headquarters, including consultant recruitment for design and environmental assessments. The team has deliberately sequenced activities to prioritize survey readiness and institutional strengthening, addressing start-up challenges and positioning the project to move into full implementation in the next phase to deliver improved data and stronger institutional capacity. Central African Republic: Data collection for the General Population Census was completed on January 31, 2026, marking a major milestone toward updated population data for planning and service delivery. Education sector routine data systems have been strengthened, enabling production of the 2025/26 statistical yearbook and, for the first time since 1960, statistical yearbooks and dashboards for higher education. Institutional strengthening has advanced through updated legal and regulatory frameworks for ICASEES, while preparations for upcoming surveys including the household living conditions survey, business census, and General Agricultural Census are progressing. Adaptive implementation decisions, such as incorporating newly identified mining sites into the census framework, reflect a strong focus on data quality and relevance, with earlier bottlenecks being addressed. Chad: Since the last ISR, progress toward the project’s development objectives has strengthened, with several indicators improving as implementation gains momentum. Completion of population census cartography marks a key milestone, providing a strong foundation for comprehensive and accurate population data to support national planning and service delivery. Implementation capacity has improved significantly, with all specialists now in place and supporting execution. Recent missions emphasized sequencing activities to prioritize census readiness and institutional strengthening, addressing earlier staffing and coordination constraints. Strengthened arrangements and coordination have helped resolve start-up challenges, enabling a transition from preparation to implementation and steady progress toward achieving the project’s objectives. Gabonese Republic: Regular production of the Harmonized Consumer Price Index continued from January to May 2025, with the June bulletin nearing completion, strengthening the availability of timely inflation data. Technical work on rebasing the national accounts is advancing, alongside preparations for the next Gabonese poverty survey. Capacity of the national statistical Mar 30, 2026 Page 2 of 18 The World Bank Implementation Status & Results Report Harmonizing and Improving Statistics in West and Central Africa - Series of Projects Two (HISWACA - SOP 2) (P180085) office has been reinforced through regional training workshops and the award of 19 scholarships to students in African statistical training institutions. Recent implementation decisions focused on maintaining continuity of core economic indicators while sequencing more complex reforms to address capacity constraints and ensure quality and institutional readiness. Republic of Congo: Regular production of the Consumer Price Index has continued, supporting timely inflation monitoring, while gaps in foreign trade statistics are being addressed and preparatory work for national accounts rebasing is advancing. A key achievement is the publication of a long historical series of quarterly national accounts (2005–Q2 2025), enhancing transparency and data usability. Recent implementation decisions emphasized strengthening dissemination and user access, with improvements to the national statistics office website and the open data portal to ensure wider availability and effective use of statistical outputs. Central African Economic and Monetary Community (Communauté Économique et Monétaire de l'Afrique Centrale, CEMAC) : Implementation of harmonized surveys has advanced, improving the availability and comparability of data on household living conditions, beneficiary satisfaction, and cross-border trade. Technical foundations for harmonization have been strengthened through the development and validation of methodological tools and guides, alongside intensified technical assistance via regional workshops and missions. Recent implementation decisions focused on sequencing efforts to prioritize core statistical domains while accommodating country readiness, with phased validation underway for methodologies covering the HICP, business statistics, public finance statistics, and the quality assurance framework addressing earlier coordination challenges and strengthening the regional statistical system. 5.
