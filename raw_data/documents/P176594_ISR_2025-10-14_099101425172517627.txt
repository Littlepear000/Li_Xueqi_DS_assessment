KEY ISSUES & STATUS 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions Oct 14, 2025 Page 1 of 20 The World Bank Implementation Status & Results Report Getting Results: Access and Delivery of Quality Education Services in Punjab Project (P176594) The project, approved by the World Bank’s Board in June 2024, became effective in April 2025 after delays in administrative approvals. The project’s implementation rating has been downgraded to Moderately Unsatisfactory due to delays in effectiveness, followed by slow progress after effectiveness. However, steady progress has been made recently, including appointment of a Project Director, notification of a PSC, recruitment of essential staff, and development of a workplan and budget. PMU staffing is advancing, with full staffing expected before the end of November 2025. A POM will be submitted for approval soon and key procurements are gradually progressing. A follow-up review is planned for January 2026, with the potential to upgrade the project rating if further milestones are met. 5.
