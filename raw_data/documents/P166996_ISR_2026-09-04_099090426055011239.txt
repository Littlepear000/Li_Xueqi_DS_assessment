KEY ISSUES & STATUS 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions The mission noted that the Project Development Objective (PDO) to support skills development and job creation in the Recipient’s territory continues to be highly relevant in light of the government’s priority of creating jobs for the population and the economy’s challenges in generating jobs. Although the project is on track to achieve all PDO and intermediate results indicators, the project team is retaining the PDO and IP ratings of Moderately Satisfactory (MS), given that disbursements have remained minimal since August 2025. The mission processed a cancellation of US$55 million from the project, as indicated in the request letter from the Ministry of Finance, dated April 1, 2026 (received by the World Bank on April 10, 2026). Based on the mission findings, the task team cancelled US$44 million from Components 1 and 2.2 and US$11 million from Component 3. The team would also like to highlight one critical actions to be undertaken by the government in light of the upcoming project closure on June 30, 2026: • Under Component 3, the MLJE will complete stakeholder consultations and make payment of compensation for the Bolgatanga PEC. 5.
