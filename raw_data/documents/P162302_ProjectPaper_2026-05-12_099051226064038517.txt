PROJECT STATUS 1. The Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Revenue Mobilization and Public Resource Management Program (KPRMP) is a Bank-financed five-year Program for Results (PforR) of US$ 118 million, comprising a Program component of US$ 100 million and an Investment Project Financing (IPF) component of US$ 18 million. The Program (Part 1) disburses against the achievement of Disbursement Linked Indicator (DLI) targets across two result areas: (i) efficient revenue mobilization, and (ii) effective public resource management. The IPF component (Part 2) supports technical assistance, ICT investments, and capacity building to enhance e-government functionality, complementing key areas under Part 1 and providing operational support. 2. The Program is currently rated as Moderately Satisfactory on progress towards its development objective. A restructuring is being requested, which is expected to improve overall performance. To date, the Program has disbursed US$ 97.78 million, representing approximately 83 percent of the total financing (US$ 118 million). The Mid- Term Review (May 2023) and subsequent implementation support missions noted significant achievements under the Program component across several DLIs. However, as discussed in recent progress review meetings, to address implementation challenges, a PDO indicator and DLIs 1, 3, and 4 require updating to provide greater flexibility for and align with the KP government’s approved cash management policy and plan1, adjust returns filing targets as per the availability of final tax filing data for timely verification, and incentivize the survey and digitization of property tax records in additional cities -- in addressing implementation challenges. 3. Overall progress is summarized below. a) Part 1: Program for Results i. Result Area 1 - Efficient Revenue Mobilization. Khyber Pakhtunkhwa (KP)'s tax revenue has increased from PKR 14.3 billion (US$ 51.4 million) in FY19 to PKR 70 billion (US$ 248 million, revised estimates) in FY25 – surpassing the Program target of US$ 212 million (PDO indicator). The improved revenue performance is mainly supported by General Sales Tax on Services (GSTS), which accounts for 60 percent of the tax revenue of the province. The GSTS base has expanded from 3,938 registered taxpayers in 2018 to 25,039 in June 2025, and filing compliance for GSTS has improved from an average of 51 percent of registered taxpayers in 2018 to 72 percent in FY24 (DLI 1). The tax data integration plan of the Government of KP (GovKP) is being implemented, and KP Revenue Authority (KPRA) has established third-party data linkages, which will be helpful for tax intelligence and audit (DLI 2). Another important source of KP's tax revenue is Urban Immoveable Property Tax (UIPT). The UIPT records of 10 cities in Abbottabad, Nowshera and Mardan districts have been updated (based on survey), digitized, and linked with the MIS and GIS map (DLI 3). As a result, the UIPT base in these cities has substantially expanded, which will support increased collections. ii. Result Area 2: Effective Public Resource Management. The improvement in the management of public investment is supported by an increased share of Annual Development Programme (ADP) funds being spent on capital investment (from 47 percent in FY17 to 80 percent in FY25; PDO indicator). Furthermore, only 17 percent of ADP funds were allocated to unapproved projects in FY25, compared to the 49 percent baseline in FY18 (DLI 5). In addition, more than 128 Tehsil Municipal Authorities (TMAs) are using the Centralized Financial Management System (CFMS) to record their transactions according to the object code of the Chart of Accounts (DLI 6). 1 The KP Government recently updated its cash management policy and prepared an implementation plan. This plan facilitates a phased consolidation of departmental commercial bank balances into the Treasury through an automated account-sweeping of accounts balance approach. In the first phase, the plan supports cash consolidation in at least four departments which have balances of more than PKRs. 20 million. Page 1 Official Use Only The World Bank Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Revenue Mobilization and Public Resource Management (P162302) Some progress has also been noted in improving cash management (PDO indicator and DLI 4). For example, the KP Public Financial Management Act has been approved, and an updated Cash Management Policy has been adopted. Procedures for the reconciliation of Government accounts have been approved. As per the updated cash management policy, the GovKP has piloted a "sweeping of bank balances" approach to account for daily cash balances held in commercial banks by the Finance Department and KP Procurement Regulatory Authority (KPPRA). Drawing lessons from the pilot, this approach is being implemented in other selected departments. In addition, a cash mangement unit within the Finance Department has been established, and a cash management and forecasting module is being developed as part of the e-Governance initiative, which will support implementation of the new approach. Cash planning and forecasting training is being provided to the line departments. b) Part 2: IPF component i. The e-Governance (e-Gov, also referred to as paperless governance or Workflow & Document Management System) initiative aims to facilitate GovKP's administrative processes in selected departments. With recent renewed efforts of the GovKP in implementing the e-Gov initiative, significant progress has been noted. The e- Gov implementation and sustainability strategy has been approved. The Additional Chief Secretary is regularly monitoring and guiding the implementation of e-Gov initiatives. Business process mapping (BPM) has been completed for selected Business Processes (BPs) relevant to all government departments. For the reengineering of BPs, functional requirements have been designed, and digital modules have been developed and rolled out for prioritized BPs. Module development is in progress for the remaining BPs. In addition, the required hardware, software, and networking facilities have been procured. To support the change management process, training and communication activities were conducted. Overall, the Government has expanded the scope of rollout of digitized BPs from 7 pilot departments to cover the secretariat of all 34 departments. ii. Operations implementation support: The Shared Services Unit (SSU), headed by a Director, is providing support for both KPRMP and the KP Spending Effectively for Enhanced Development (SPEED) Program on fiduciary, safeguards, and M&E functions. The Project Director of KPRMP is being assisted by the SSU and other staff to implement the Program. II. DESCRIPTION OF PROPOSED CHANGES AND RATIONALE FOR RESTRUCTURING 4. As per the request of the Ministry of Economic Affairs, Government of Pakistan (letter dated March 31, 2026), this restructuring paper proposes to revise selected PDO and DLI indicators and their verification protocols to: (i) provide flexibility for, and alignment with, the government’s approved plan for cash management; (ii) adjust final target in line with the availability of tax filing data for timely verification, and (iii) incentivize the survey and digitization of property tax records in additional cities. Accordingly, funds will be reallocated between DLIs 1 and 3 within P4R component, and relevant changes will be made in the Financing Agreement. The changes to the results matrix are summarized in Table 1, while detailed revisions are available in the Annex 1. These revisions are intended to improve the performance of the Program. 5. No changes are made to the implementation arrangements, fiduciary management, and environmental and social arrangements of the Program. There are no outstanding or overdue audit reports, and no ineligible expenditure identified under the Program or its IPF component.
