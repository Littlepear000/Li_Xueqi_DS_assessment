KEY ISSUES & STATUS Apr 28, 2026 Official Use Only Page 1 of 10 The World Bank Implementation Status & Results Report Tunisia Health System Strengthening Project (P507209) 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions The Tunisia Health System Strengthening Project is financed through an IBRD loan to the Republic of Tunisia in the amount of EUR 99.9 million (US$108 million equivalent), and a US$17.16 million grant from the Pandemic Fund approved by the Pandemic Fund Governing Board. The project was approved on May 28, 2025, and signed by the Government on November 3, 2025 but is not yet effective. The Project aims to improve access to resilient, quality, and responsive health services. Aligned with national health reform priorities, this Project includes three interlinked components aiming to (i) strengthen pandemic prevention, preparedness, and response, including through the Pandemic Fund grant; (ii) support reorganization of primary health care services to improve provision of cost-effective preventive health services; and (iii) strengthen management of emergency medical services. Activities supported by the Project include infrastructure rehabilitation and modernization, procurement of equipment, and capacity building, with a focus on climate readiness of the health system. Key expected outcomes by 2030 include: 2 million people receiving outpatient services of which 1 million women (contributing to the World Bank's ambitious goal of providing quality, affordable health services to 1.5 billion people by 2030); reaching 100 percent of the population that can be reached by emergency services within 30 minutes from 54 percent in 2025; and 6 million people benefiting from climate-resilient planning, preparation, surveillance, and/or response of which 3 million women. 5.
