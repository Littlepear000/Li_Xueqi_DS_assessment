KEY ISSUES & STATUS 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions The Project became effective on January 11, 2022, and adopts a landscape approach to promote sustainable agriculture and integrated water resources management in the Yaque del Norte and Ozama–Isabela river basins. Its main activities include: (i) support for agricultural productive subprojects (Component 1); (ii) rehabilitation of dams and large-scale irrigation infrastructure (Component 2); (iii) improvements in sanitation plants (Component 3); and (iv) water basin strengthening measures (Component 4). The World Bank has carried out a mission in October 2026 to assess viability and support sustained implementation under the current timeframe. 5.
