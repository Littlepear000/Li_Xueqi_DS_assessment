KEY ISSUES & STATUS 3.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions The Program continues to deliver strong results and is on track to meet its objectives ahead of its current closing date (December 31, 2026). The Program has significantly overachieved its main PDO indicator on safety net coverage, with the productive cash transfers program (PTMP) reaching 1,747,991 additional social safety nets beneficiaries since 2023 (of which 960,299 women). The PTMP has established itself as the government's flagship social and economic inclusion program, anchored in the Plan National de Développement (PND) 2026–2030 with a dedicated budget line through 2030. Progress towards the system strengthening objective is also on track, thanks to a restructuring (February 2026) that better aligned results to implementation realities and priorities. The Unique Social Registry (RSU) now covers approximately 1.69 million households and is pursuing interoperability with other government programs. The PTMP has fostered synergies with other social and economic programs, including with the Caisse Nationale d’Assurance Maladie (CNAM), resulting in Jun 22, 2026 Page 1 of 21 The World Bank Implementation Status & Results Report Côte d'Ivoire Productive Social Safety Nets System Strengthening Program (P175594) over 55,000 PTMP beneficiaries transitioned to the non-contributory mechanism of the universal health coverage system, and more upcoming. Preparations for the proposed next phase, to cover 2027-2030, are advancing at pace. 4.
