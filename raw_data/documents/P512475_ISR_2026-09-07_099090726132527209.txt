KEY ISSUES & STATUS The World Bank Implementation Status & Results Report Modernizing Public Institutions and Systems in Yemen (P512475) Sep 07, 2026 Page 2 of 9 Official Use Only 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions The project became effective on July 9, 2026, and implementation is at an early stage. Initial progress has been made in establishing the project management, fiduciary, safeguards, monitoring and evaluation, and governance arrangements required to launch implementation. Implementation status as satisfactory both at the PDO level and for each component. Component 1: National Statistics System. The implementation of this component is progressing satisfactorily, in particular: (i) advertisement of engineering and IT assessments to design hybrid power system and IT data system solutions for the Central Statistics Organization (CSO), (ii) refinement of the list of IT equipment, and (iii) detailed discussion for two priority activities for 2026: the Statistical Foundations Training Course and Twinning Partnership and the Technical Assistance for National Accounts. Following the discussion, the Terms of Reference (ToR) for these two activities will be improved. Next steps include: (i) CSO to share technical feedback on ToRs; (ii) UNDP and CSO to finalize the list of IT equipment, (iii) UNDP to complete the procurement for IT and engineering assessments; (iv) UNDP national coordinator to engage with OECD and METAC to foster synergies and collaboration; (v) meetings with other statistical offices and University of Aden to plan for activities implementation. Component 2: Public Finance and Civil Service: Implementation of this component is progressing satisfactorily. The first meeting of the ministerial committee on institutional reform chaired by Prime Minister held on Aug. 23, 2026 gave clear momentum and high level commitment to public finance and civil service reform. Progress is also being made toward finalizing the action plan for implementing Treasury Single Account (TSA) and the training modules for rolling out cash management and commitment controls. There is a need to frontload technical assistance and the procurement package for the Financial Management Information System and Human Resource Management Information System (FMIS/HRMIS) in the coming months, including undertaking related market research. This includes(i) drafting the ToRs for the Technical Assistance to support MoF and MoCSI in elaborating a detailed and comprehensive action plan for reforms, and in revising and simplifying the processes of the core functions prioritized for Public Finance Management and Civil Service Management in synergies with support provided by International Monetary Fund and Arab Monetary Fund; (ii) preparing the procurement packages for the FMIS/HRMIS and plan for the additional studies (data readiness assessment, site readiness assessment) to be completed before a FMIS/HRMIS vendor is recruited. Data center. The approach for hosting and back-up data and statistics was defined as follow: (i) data and statistics will be hosted locally in a primary data processing and storage solution within CSO, MoCSI, MoF, supported by a cloud service for backup and redundancies; (ii) there will be a fully active secondary data center shared across institutions, as an additional backup; (iii) the Project Steering Committee will discuss the options for the Disaster Recovery back-up solution and location according to cost and risks, and clarify the preferred options. Component 3: Project Management & Localization. The implementation of this component was evaluated satisfactory. Most PMU team members are recruited or already onboard (fiduciary, safeguards, M&E, national coordinators). The international recruitment of the Project Manager is on-going. The Project Operational Manual (POM) has been drafted and is being revised to integrate comments by World Bank. Once the ITF is formally established, the planning of formal trainings and on-the-job learning on IDA Project Management to foster localization will be prepared. M&E. The contracting of the Third-Party Monitoring Agent (TPMA) is expected to be finalized shortly. The PMU M&E specialist is recruited and presented the Project M&E approach.
