OBJECTIVE 1.1 Development Objective Original Development Objective (Approved as part of Approval package on 15-Oct-2024) The Development Objective of the Series of Projects is to strengthen the shared management of selected Pacific Island oceanic and coastal fisheries, and the critical habitats upon which they depend. The Development Objective of the objective is to strengthen stakeholder capacity for regional collaboration and enhanced sector performance in fisheries, and for improved habitat preservation of the Federated States of Micronesia. KEY ISSUES & STATUS 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions Implementation progress during the reporting period has been constrained by delays in technical terms of reference development, procurement evaluations, and mobilization of key specialists. To address these challenges, the project has agreed on corrective actions including short-term technical support, streamlined procurement processes, and monthly progress monitoring meetings between the PIU and the World Bank. Site confirmation for the NORMA office building at Palikir enables negotiations with the selected design firm to proceed. 5.
