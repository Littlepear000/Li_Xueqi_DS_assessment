KEY ISSUES & STATUS 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions The project remains highly relevant to Cabo Verde’s transport, connectivity, and urban resilience priorities, and the PDO remains achievable. Implementation experienced some slowdown during the electoral period and the subsequent transition to a new Government. The task team is engaging with the newly appointed authorities and will work closely with the incoming teams to confirm priorities, sustain implementation progress, and ensure that the project continues to support the Government’s evolving agenda ahead of the planned Mid-Term Review in November 2026. 5.
