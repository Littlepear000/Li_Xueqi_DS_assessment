KEY ISSUES & STATUS 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions Jun 03, 2025 Page 1 of 9 The World Bank Implementation Status & Results Report Support to the Bogota Metro Line 1 Section 1 Project - (Series 1) (P165300) The project progress indicators are positive, for which implementation progress across all components is satisfactory. As of May 27, 2025, EMB continues to demonstrate strong leadership across technical and fiduciary fronts. Implementation surpassed the 50 percent milestone in March 2025, and is currently at 52.65%, slightly below the scheduled optimum 53.11%. The project shows positive momentum, with overall project implementation performance (actual vs scheduled progress, of a maximum of 100%) at 98.3%, and the overall performance of major civil works progressing above 95% of the current implementation plan targets (v13), which is exemplary for a megaproject. The PPP concessionaire is working actively on all fronts, including railyards, viaduct, stations and rolling stock, and has incorporated more than 500 local subcontractor engineering and construction firms. Several workfronts are ahead of schedule, particularly the railyards and production of prefabricated slabs for the viaduct. Environmental performance continues to perform well and is improving. EMB fulfilled most recommendations from the prior mission, including updated execution planning, increased field presence, and environmental and social safeguards compliance. EMB, the concessionaire, and the supervisor have strengthened their institutional capacity and maintained zero tolerance for major ESHS incidents. The implementation of the corrective SST (occupational health and safety) action plan is on track. The concessionaire is making efforts to minimize disruptions and implement mitigation measures for noise, vibration and traffic management. Financial Management and Disbursements: All financial and audit requirements have been met. Disbursements under Series 1 are proceeding on schedule, with revised flows aligned to the current implementation plan (V13). EMB confirmed its plans to fully disburse Series 1 by October 2025, and announced its plans to disburse resources from Series 2 as early as November 2026, requiring the World Bank to prepare the Series 2 Loan ($530 million) during FY24 and seek board approval by November 2025. This timeline is contingent of National Government's sign off, and in particular, agreeing to the proposed negotiations schedule and processing of the sovereign guarantees and counterguarantees that the MDB financing scheme requires. 5.
