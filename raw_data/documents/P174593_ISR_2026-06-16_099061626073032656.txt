KEY ISSUES & STATUS 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions The overall status of the project is considered satisfactory. The project has achieved 95% physical progress of no regret river works for Beki and Buridehing. Development of flood early warning system is in progress and will be operated during the 2026 monsoon season.
