KEY ISSUES & STATUS 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions Dec 23, 2025 Page 1 of 11 The World Bank Implementation Status & Results Report Strengthening the Statistical System of Uzbekistan (P173450) Good progress has been made since the June 2025 when the project was restructured to align with the government’s new priorities for modernizing the Uzbekistan statistical system and the revised census approach. A major milestone was the approval of Presidential Resolution No. 173 in September 2025, which legally authorized the census, set its dates for January–February 2026, and formalized implementation structures at the national and local levels. These developments put the project on track to achieve its objectives. Successful census implementation will meet a critical outcome indicator, mitigate the main project risk, and further improve Uzbekistan’s Statistics Performance Indicator, which is already above target. 5.
