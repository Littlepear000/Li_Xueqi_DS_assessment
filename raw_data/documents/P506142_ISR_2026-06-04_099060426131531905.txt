KEY ISSUES & STATUS 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions Jun 04, 2026 Official Use Only Page 1 of 10 The World Bank Implementation Status & Results Report Santa Catarina Rural Development Project for Sustainability and Innovation (P506142) The Santa Catarina Rural Development Project for Sustainability and Innovation was approved by the World Bank on March 14, 2025, and declared effective on April 24, 2026. Key actions to be completed within the first 90 days after effectiveness include: (i) appointment of all Project Management Unit staff; (ii) designation of staff across the implementing agencies (SAR, EPAGRI, CIDASC, IMA, SAQ, SIE); and (iii) establishment of the Project Steering Committee. The implementing institutions are progressively advancing these steps and are expected to initiate procurement processes shortly.
