KEY ISSUES & STATUS Apr 02, 2026 Page 1 of 9 The World Bank Implementation Status & Results Report DR Resilient Agriculture and Integrated Water Resources Management (P163260) 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions The Project became effective on January 11, 2022, and adopts a landscape approach to promote sustainable agriculture and integrated water resources management in the Yaque del Norte and Ozama–Isabela river basins. Its main activities include: (i) support for agricultural productive subprojects (Component 1); (ii) rehabilitation of dams and large-scale irrigation infrastructure (Component 2); (iii) improvements in sanitation plants (Component 3); and (iv) water basin strengthening measures (Component 4). The World Bank approved the extension of the Project Closing Date to January 2, 2028. Implementation has progressed across components, with productive subprojects under execution, irrigation rehabilitation contracts advancing toward award, dam safety studies underway, and sanitation designs nearing completion. Continued coordination with relevant authorities will support sustained implementation under the extended timeframe. 5.
