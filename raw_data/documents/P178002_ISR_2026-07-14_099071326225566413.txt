KEY ISSUES & STATUS 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions Progress toward the Project Development Objectives continues to be mixed, with significant delays in Component 1.1 and Component 2. The project faces on-going obstacles related to low technical capacity of implementing agencies, weak coordination among the Project Implementation and Management Team (PIMT) and Project Implementation Units (PIUs), and limited resources, both human and in some cases, ICT equipment. Two new challenges have also emerged since the Mid-term Review. One, the fuel crisis associated with the conflicts in the Middle East delayed several data collection activities and affected budgeting of project activities. Two, new approval processes at Ministry of Finance for procurement and project activities require extra preparation time and have caused significant delays in key procurement packages. Various actions have been agreed at the end of the 5th ISM between the PIUs and PIMT to accelerate implementation progress. A Project Restructuring is being prepared to streamline project activities, improve efficiency and strengthen project monitoring and evaluation. 5.
