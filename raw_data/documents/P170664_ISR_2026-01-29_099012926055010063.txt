KEY ISSUES & STATUS 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions The Project is making steady progress toward its development objectives, with implementation advancing across most components and disbursement at US$430.2 million (36% of the total envelope). Two of five Year 5 PDO indicators have met or exceeded targets (girls’ enrollment and digital literacy) with strong momentum in girls completion, life skills training, and reaching the overall Project beneficiaries. Original Financing states have completed multiple school improvement grant cycles and delivered extensive rehabilitation and new facilities, while several states have concluded the first phase of new construction. Most Additional Financing states have finalized workplans for 2025–2026 and are positioned to accelerate activities in the coming months. Financial management performance has improved in select states following targeted remedial actions. Environmental and social risk management has strengthened, with ongoing training, improved reporting, and commitments to complete security planning instruments in line with ESF requirements. A Mid-Term Review is planned for March 2026 to assess achievements and consider any necessary adjustments. 5.
