KEY ISSUES & STATUS 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions Aug 08, 2025 Page 1 of 11 The World Bank Implementation Status & Results Report Lowlands Livelihood Resilience Project (P164336) This ISR is issued following the 9th ISM held on June 16-20, 2025, and reflects the progress made and results attained since the 8th ISM in January 2025. The project is on track to achieve its development objectives, with both progress towards attaining the Project Development Objectives (PDO) and Implementation Progress (IP) rated satisfactory. The project closing date is October 10, 2025. The following actions have been agreed to be followed up by the project team: • To ensure smooth and timely closure, the project will finalize key reports—including the independent end-line study and the Borrower Completion Report—by September 2025. • Budget utilization is being closely monitored, and any unutilized funds will be identified for cancellation. • The project is assessing the sustainability of productive alliances and rural financial institutions to inform future programming. • Efforts are underway to strengthen the grievance redress system, including enhancing the functionality and publicity of the new digital GRM (6867) and further strengthening the paper-based GRM across the project regions. • Procurement and financial management actions are being taken to resolve outstanding issues and ensure compliance with audit recommendations. • Environmental and social safeguards are being addressed, with corrective actions planned to ensure full compliance and sustainability. 5.
