KEY ISSUES & STATUS 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions The PDO and implementation progress are rated Moderately Satisfactory. Disbursement is 61%. From a technical standpoint, the project continues to add value to private sector development in the regions and sectors concerned, and most performance indicators are on track to meet their targets. However, the project’s results could be more significant if project management were improved—particularly with regard to the planning and implementation of activities. Given the planned completion date in January 2028, project management shortcomings have become more significant and pose major risks to the achievement of the project’s objectives and results. Due to delays in procurement and the failure to effectively implement agreed-upon recommendations and measures, the project was rated “moderately unsatisfactory” in the areas of financial management, environmental and social management, and project management. The PIU should adopt a more proactive approach to accelerate the implementation of activities and improve project planning, monitoring, and evaluation. The list below outlines the key activities for which proactive measures must be taken and which are critical to the project’s outcomes. Further detailed recommendations regarding all activities are provided in the Aide Memoire. 1. Enterprise Support: MBIF (Madagascar Business Investment Facility). Given the delay in signing the MBIF Agribusiness agreements, it is imperative to proactively ensure that subprojects are implemented in accordance with the agreed timelines. All initial disbursements must be made by May 2026. To ensure that the MBIF Tourism initiative can be implemented according to the project timeline, agreements with the selected companies should be signed by September 2026. 2. Enterprise Support: Acceleration. To ensure that businesses receive support within the project timeline, the acceleration program should be launched by June 2026, and contracts with the accelerators should be signed by September 2026. 3. Financial Inclusion: SWITCH National. Finalize the contract with the new service provider by mid-May 2026 at the latest. 4. Infrastructure: Payment of compensation to project-affected people (PAPs). The Government must ensure the payment of compensation to PAPs related to the rehabilitation of the Manakara and Farafangana urban roads, and the Project Implementation Unit (PIU) must submit the report on the implementation of resettlement plans (RPs) by the end of April 2026. The PMU must also submit the implementation report for the ESMP (Environmental and Social Management Plan), in accordance with the financing agreement. 5. Infrastructure: Rehabilitation of the DDP (Public Debt Directorate) building. Sign the construction contract by mid-May 2026 and the project management contract by mid-April 2026. 6. Infrastructure: Rehabilitation of the Ambila/Manambato roads. The mission highlights the high risks associated with this activity. Several amendments to the initial contract have altered the scope of the work and increased the price per kilometer. The work must be completed by June 2026. An audit will be conducted thereafter.
