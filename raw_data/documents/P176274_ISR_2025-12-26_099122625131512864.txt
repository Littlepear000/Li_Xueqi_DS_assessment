KEY ISSUES & STATUS 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions Implementation and disbursement have accelerated since the last ISR, with notable progress across all four technical components. The Mid-Term Review confirmed that the project is now in a phase of scaled implementation, supported by strengthened coordination mechanisms, fully contracted implementation partners, and improved fiduciary and safeguards systems. Disbursement reached 33.99%, and projections suggest surpassing 40% by the first quarter of 2026. Under Component 1, the investment-climate reform agenda continues to advance. The Public-Private Dialogue (PPD) supported by the project through CMCAA leadership is operational, delivering sectoral workshops and steering key regulatory reforms, including SEZ legislation, PPP implementation, and revisions to core business laws. The GUFE finalized its institutional reform package and progressed on digitalization (in coordination with PNUD support). The CNI issued 13 new investment approvals totaling over XAF 50 billion and 1,140 projected jobs, signaling renewed investor interest. Under Component 2, the National Guarantee and Investment Fund (FNGI) is nearing operational readiness. All technical instruments (operations manual, business plan, investment and E&S strategies) were finalized and cleared by the Bank. Due-diligence discussions with partner banks are well advanced. The formal appointment of the Board of Directors by Presidential Decree required prior to disbursement of the first capitalization tranche (USD 4 million) was done on December 19, 2025. COBAC licensing is expected in the first half of 2026. Under Component 3, support to SMEs and skills development has accelerated. Approximately 2,100 entrepreneurs have been supported through CAPMEA, CCIMA, CPMEC, PLAMFE, UNPC, and GICA. A unified beneficiary-tracking system is being deployed with SCI Capital to harmonize data across implementation partners. The ACFPE launched nationwide mobilization for apprenticeships, finalized partnerships with 14 training centers, and initiated PREPA-PRO implementation for a first wave of 2,000 youth, with a focus on female participation (40%). FORMA-PRO will launch in early 2026 in partnership with MSME apprenticeship hosts. Under Component 4, the PIU is fully staffed with fiduciary, procurement, safeguards, M&E, communication, and security functions. Internal audit recruitment is completed. Coordination has improved, though the mission encouraged more frequent technical committee meetings and strengthened communication efforts. The PIU was authorized to enter the new premises on December 11, 2025 by the Ministry of Finance and Budget. Key decisions agreed during the Mid Term Review include: • Disbursing the first capitalization tranche of the FNGI. • Launching the “I-COMPETE Formation” platform to harmonize SME support programs. • Finalizing the preparation of the AWPB 2026 and strengthening interdepartmental planning to improve execution. • Operationalizing the grievance mechanism and strengthening E&S capacity-support plans. • Increasing the frequency of technical committee meetings to support agile decision-making 5.
