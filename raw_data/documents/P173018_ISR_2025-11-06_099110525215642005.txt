KEY ISSUES & STATUS 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions Nov 06, 2025 Page 1 of 11 The World Bank Implementation Status & Results Report Solomon Islands Sustainable Mining Development Technical Assistance Project (P173018) The Solomon Islands Sustainable Mining Development Technical Assistance Project (SMDTAP) continues to make steady progress toward its development objectives. During the reporting period, the project advanced the regulatory framework through the finalization of the Mineral Resources Bill 2025, which is currently pending parliamentary approval. Legal advisory support has facilitated the drafting of implementing regulations, including provisions for licensing, mine safety, environmental management, and fiscal administration. Institutional capacity within the Ministry of Mines, Energy and Rural Electrification (MMERE) has been strengthened through the full operationalization of the Mining Cadastre Administration System (MCAS). MCAS has enhanced transparency and efficiency in mineral rights administration, with the average processing time for exploration license applications reduced to one month. The system is now fully integrated and supporting improved sector governance. Stakeholder engagement has been bolstered by the establishment of a Community Liaison Section within MMERE, with staff recruitment completed and training activities underway. The Gender Equality and Social Inclusion (GESI) Strategy has been developed and is pending Cabinet endorsement, reflecting the project’s commitment to inclusive and sustainable development in the mining sector. In May 2025, the Government of Solomon Islands formally announced its intention to re-engage with the Extractive Industries Transparency Initiative (EITI). With technical assistance from the project, a Multi-Stakeholder Group has been established, and preparatory documentation—including a work plan, budget, and Memorandum of Understanding—has been completed to support the country’s application for candidacy. These developments represent significant progress toward enhancing transparency and accountability in the extractives sector. 5.
