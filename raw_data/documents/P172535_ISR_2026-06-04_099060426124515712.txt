KEY ISSUES & STATUS Jun 04, 2026 Official Use Only Page 1 of 9 The World Bank Implementation Status & Results Report Colombia: Resilient and Inclusive Housing Project (P172535) 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions The Project implementation continues to show progress. To date, 18,265 people have benefited from improved urban living conditions, including 4,342 women and 5,680 Venezuelan migrants, of whom 1,986 have received rental subsidies. Procurement and financial management have strengthened considerably, with over 90% of processes contracted or under execution, and the Grievance Redress Mechanism is resolving 97.8% of complaints within 15 working days. Two Integrated Neighbourhood Improvement sub-projects and 9 public space works are currently under execution, expected to reach approximately 30,800 additional beneficiaries. Total disbursements stand at US$43.67 million (approximately 32% of total funds). To ensure the orderly completion of ongoing activities — including public space works, rural housing contracts, and rental subsidy payments for vulnerable households — an 11-month extension of the Project closing date to June 24, 2027, is being processed. 5.
