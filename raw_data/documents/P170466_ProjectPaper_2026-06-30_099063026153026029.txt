PROJECT STATUS AND RATIONALE FOR RESTRUCTURING 1. The Investing in Forests and Protected Areas for Climate-Smart Development (IFPA-CD) Project was prepared to support the forest, conservation, and tourism sectors in the country. Its Project Development Objective (PDO) is to (i) improve sustainable management of forests and protected areas (PA) in target landscapes and (ii) increase benefits to communities from forests in target landscapes. The project aims to improve management and economic productivity of forest ecosystems. It combines investments in forest management in both state- and community-managed lands and focuses on improving the management of forests, increasing revenues for sustaining forests, and supporting resilient livelihoods. The project has a strong job-creation element, both through direct activity support and indirectly through planned investments in wood processing and tourism facilities. The project was approved by the Board of the International Development Association (IDA) on April 23, 2020 and became effective on August 18, 2021. The project was formally launched in March 2022, as the restrictions related to the novel coronavirus (COVID-19) were starting to ease. The project is implemented by the Ministry of Water and Environment (MWE) and Uganda Wildlife Authority (UWA), working in close coordination with the Ministry of Tourism, Wildlife and Antiquities and the Office of the Prime Minister - the latter two agencies having general government mandates on tourism and refugee hosting, respectively. Uganda Timber Growers’ Association (UTGA) is an implementing agency for a small set of activities related to production forestry. 2. The project was restructured three times. The first restructuring was approved in December 2024 to refine the PDO and better define the project’s geographic scope.1 The second restructuring was approved in November 2025 to (i) remove the National Forestry Authority (NFA) as a project implementation agency following its dissolution as part of the government process of agencies’ rationalization, transferring ex-NFA activities to MWE; (ii) adjust the scope of activities to enable more efficient use of project resources within the remaining project period (including related revisions of the results framework and disbursement categories); (iii) further refine the definition of the project target landscapes; and (iv) mitigate risks associated with project support for infrastructure and equipment for PA management. Four disbursement conditions were introduced as part of the November 2025 restructuring – of these, three have been complied with to date: related to the signing of an implementation agreement between MWE and UTGA,2 related to the adoption of standard operating procedures for law enforcement by UWA,3 and related to MWE having in place satisfactory fiduciary and staff provisions for implementation of activities previously under NFA.4 The third restructuring, approved by the Bank on June 29, 2026, accompanied by a cancellation notice dated June 24, 2026, cancelled funds in the total amount of SDR30.7 million,5 or US$42.1 million6 equivalent (cancellation is effective as of May 6, 2026 and is currently being processed in the Bank system) and made related revisions to the scope of project activities, expected results as per the project results framework, and the project’s theory of change. After the cancellation, total current revised IDA commitment is SDR77,287,877, including SDR43,908,824 under the IDA credit and 33,379,053 SDR under the IDA grant. This is equivalent to US$106.1 million of IDA financing. 3. The project has four components. The project’s structure responds to the need to invest in development of the forestry and tourism sectors (with Component 2 supporting economic investments in these sectors). Implementation progress (IP). Commencement of implementation of project activities was slow due to the delayed effectiveness, related to lengthy internal approval processes by the Government of Uganda (GoU) and COVID-19related restrictions. Effective implementation started with the project launch in March 2022. Project performance had been in a satisfactory range until early 2025, as reported in the Implementation Status and Results Reports. In view of continued implementation delays, both the PDO and IP ratings were downgraded to Moderately Unsatisfactory in May 2025. The ratings were further downgraded to Unsatisfactory for PDO and IP by the most recent mission in May-June 2026. Activities implemented by UWA and NFA have made substantial progress. However, the progress achieved by the NFA was interrupted by its dissolution and the ongoing gradual merger into MWE. In the recent supervision period, activities by UWA and ongoing activities by MWE have progressed well, but this progress has not been sufficient to fully compensate for the initial delays; activities of ex-NFA have been limited to maintenance of restored forest areas, timber plantations, and support to community groups in the current fiscal year, due to the slow-paced merger of NFA into MWE. There are currently no outstanding audits or legal covenants. II. DESCRIPTION OF PROPOSED CHANGES 5.
