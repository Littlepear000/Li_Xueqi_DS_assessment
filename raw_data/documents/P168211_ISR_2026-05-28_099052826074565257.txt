KEY ISSUES & STATUS 3.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions Implementation of the Program continues to progress, supported by significant reforms in Tajikistan’s electricity sector. Since 2025, the Government has implemented further electricity tariff increases, strengthening the trajectory toward cost-recovery tariffs by 2027 and contributing to improved sector revenues. Tariff reform is essential for the long-term financial health of the sector and for reducing reliance on government subsidies. The program is supporting the government in managing this transition, including measures to protect vulnerable households. Barqi Tojik (BT) has demonstrated improved financial discipline, including regular payments for electricity purchases from independent power producers, while sector cash-flow transparency and predictability have improved through the introduction of May 28, 2026 Page 1 of 20 The World Bank Implementation Status & Results Report Program for the Recovery of the Electricity Sector in Tajikistan (P168211) tariff-based escrow account rules. However, important challenges remain. The utility's financial position continues to require close attention. In parallel, the three utilities - BT, Shabakahoi Intiqoli Barq (SIB), and Shabakahoi Taqsimoti Barq (STB), now regularly publish key operational, commercial, and financial data, enhancing transparency. 4.
