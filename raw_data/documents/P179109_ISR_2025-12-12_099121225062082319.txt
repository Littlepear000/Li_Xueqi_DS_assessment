KEY ISSUES & STATUS 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions Dec 12, 2025 Page 1 of 8 The World Bank Implementation Status & Results Report Comoros Interisland Connectivity Project SOP2 (P179109) 1. The project implementation has been delayed. Especially for the Port Boingoma rehabilitation works (SOP1), the project has lost nine months to decide a borrow pit to be used. For the secondary ports (SOP2), the detailed design (APD) has been delivered but indicates significant cost overruns. The mission focused on addressing these issues, in collaboration with other cofinanciers, such as AfDB, IsDB, AFD, and EIB. 2. Component 1. Port Boingoma. Implementation remains slow despite recent acceleration. Port rehabilitation formally started in July 2024 but was delayed due to the resuscitated debate on the selection of borrow pits between the contractor and the Government. After the Government’s final decision to keep using the original Domoni site in May 2025, works resumed, including establishing a rock crusher site, base camps and constructing access roads to Domoni and Hoani beach. Physical execution is 13%, up from 6% in September 2025, but remains low. C-ESMP and traffic management plan were revised. To accelerate progress, it was agreed to revise the works timeline and to address key constraints, such as fuel shortage, authorization for importing explosives, finalization of detailed design, and timely delivery of barges. 3. Component 2. Secondary Ports and Passenger Boat Program (only SOP2). The finalization of detailed design has been delayed. Draft detailed design (APD) delivered in September 2025 indicates significant cost overruns for developing secondary ports at Ouroveni and Vassy. To contain the costs, it was agreed to simplify some design features, such as parking lot and buildings, and balance port security and operability. While APD, ESIA and RAP are expected to be finalized, the procurement process for Ouroveni Port will be advanced and funded solely by IsDB. For Vassy, WB and IsDB will jointly finance it, subject to remaining resources and potential resource reallocation. 4. A new passenger boat company started operating among the three islands in September 2025, confirming existing significant demand as well as the need for developing secondary port infrastructure. The Government is vigilant to supervise all boat departures and arrivals, but further technical assistance may be needed to facilitate coordination among different agencies, led by the National Agency of Maritime Affaires (ANAM). 5. Component 3. Implementation support/Technical assistance. The technical assistance (TA) to support the PPP operations for Port Boingoma and the new passenger boat program has been advanced. The recruitment of a transaction adviser is expected by early 2026. To support a national maritime transport academy at the national fishery and maritime school (ENPMM), including a GBV/SEA/SH training module, the Government identified a strategic partner. Following the need assessment, the first training program is expected to start in 2026. On the other hand, the TA for ANAM to enforce maritime safety was completed. 5.
