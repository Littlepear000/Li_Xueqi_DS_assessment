KEY ISSUES & STATUS 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions Mar 06, 2026 Page 1 of 8 The World Bank Implementation Status & Results Report Türkiye Irrigation Modernization (P158418) Under Component 1, the Project finances investments to modernize DSI’s irrigation systems in Atabey, Eregli-Ivriz, Alasehir, and Uzunlu schemes, with a combined area of 50,764 hectares benefiting 17,312 people. Component 2 covers (i) designing and piloting a national program for Water Users Association (WUA) capacity building (US$1.1 million), and (ii) groundwater stewardship pilot (US$0.9 million) through US$2.0 million GEF grant. Component 3 is for Project Management. Under Component 1 and Component 3 all the contracts have been signed and the works are on-track. Under Component 2, all the activities have been completed as of December 2025. 5.
