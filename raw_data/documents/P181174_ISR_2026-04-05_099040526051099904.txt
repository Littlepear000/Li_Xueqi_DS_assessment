KEY ISSUES & STATUS 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions Apr 05, 2026 Page 1 of 11 The World Bank Implementation Status & Results Report Costa Rica Results in Education (CORE) (P181174) The Loan Agreement was signed on December 2, 2026 and is pending Effectiveness, as well as ratification by the National Assembly of Costa Rica. The Ministry of Public Education (MEP) is advancing in meeting the Additional Conditions of Effectiveness: (i) establishment of a Project Implementation Unit and (ii) finalization of the Operational Manual. The Bank is working closely with MEP to ensure readiness for implementation, including through support in the preparation of the Operational Manual and Terms of Reference for key contracts that would be launched in the first 12 months of implementation. Given that the Project is not yet effective, indicator data presented in the Results section remains at baseline values. 5.
