PROJECT STATUS AND RATIONALE FOR RESTRUCTURING PROJECT STATUS AND RATIONALE Summary 1. This Restructuring Paper seeks the approval of the Regional Vice president for a Level Two restructuring of the Peru Centralized Emergency Response Project, following the Government of Peru’s written request dated November 20, 2024. The restructuring includes a 12-month extension of the loan closing date, from January 7, 2026, to December 31, 2026. The extension aims to provide sufficient time for the Project to achieve its Project Development Objective (PDO), align with the Government of Peru’s budget cycle and enable the completion of key signed contracts, including the construction of the 911 Center. The restructuring will also update disbursement estimates, the Results Framework target dates and the implementation schedule, to align them with the new closing date. This would be the third restructuring and extension of closing date, for a cumulative extension of three years since the original closing date. 2. The Project was approved by the World Bank (WB) Board of Executive Directors on April 7, 2020, with a Loan in the amount of US$36.32 million (Ln. 9039-PE). The PDO is to provide a more efficient response to emergencies, urgencies, and associated helpline requests from the population of Lima Metropolitan area and Callao. It has 6 components: 1) Physical infrastructure; 2) Integrated platform; 3) 911 Central response protocols; 4) Interconnection of the video cameras of National Police to the 911 Platform; 5) Use and appropriation of 911 platform; and 6) Project supervision and management, and technical studies. The Project is implemented by the National Telecommunications Program (PRONATEL), under the Ministry of Transport and Communications (MTC). 3. Previous restructurings. In November 2023, the WB approved a first extension of the loan closing date by 12 months (from January 10, 2024, to January 10, 2025), to allow the Project to demonstrate progress towards achieving the PDO. A second 12-month (until January 7, 2026) was approved in November 2024, as part of a two-phase extension, with the second phase conditioned on the satisfactory achievement of key project milestones. Project Status 4. The Project is currently rated moderately satisfactory for overall progress towards achievement of the PDO and satisfactory for Implementation Progress, with procurement, financial management and safeguards rated satisfactory in the last Implementation Status and Results Report archived on June 14, 2025. As of June 30, 2025, the Project has disbursed US$20.39 million (56.2 percent) and has around 90 percent committed in ongoing procurement processes. All legal covenants have been met, including the approval of the necessary adjustments to the legal framework regarding geolocation requirements that include the obligation of mobile operators to share real time data and transfer the geolocation data to the 911 Emergency Response System.1 The Project has no outstanding audit reports and all audit reports have been considered satisfactory to the WB. 5. Shortcomings in achieving the PDO are due mainly to implementation challenges faced between 2020 and mid-2023, which are reflected in the past limited disbursement performance.
