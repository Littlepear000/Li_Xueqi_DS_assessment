KEY ISSUES & STATUS Mar 23, 2026 Page 1 of 9 The World Bank Implementation Status & Results Report Third Phase of the Central Asia Regional Links Program (CARs-3) (P159220) 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions The project has seen significant progress since the last reporting period, in large part due to the effective organization and dedication of the Ministry of Transport and Communications (MOTC) representatives and the PIU. Civil works on the road component are completed, and the road was opened in October 2025 in the presence of the Europe and Central Asia Transport Practice Manager, the World Bank Country Manager, and the President of the Kyrgyz Republic. Component 1: Road construction works are fully complete. The new road has already shown significant increases in traffic levels between eastern Issyk-Kul and Kazakhstan as well as local tourism development along the road. Procurement of road maintenance equipment is underway, which will allow for maintaining year-round road connectivity. Component 2 focuses on Aviation Safety and Service Provision. Based on the ICAO Safety Audit results, a Corrective Action Plan was developed. The procurement process for State Civil Aviation Authority operational/safety software and operations equipment is set to launch by December 2025. Meanwhile, the identification of needs for the training program for inspectors is complete. Training is expected to be implemented in 2026. Procurement for safety auditor equipment is complete, and equipment has been delivered. Contracts for procurement of Kyrgyz Aviation Institute (KAI) laboratory equipment is partially complete. Several equipment pieces needed to be re-tendered due to breach of contract by the supplier. Delivery of re-tendered pieces is expected by end of Q1 2026. Component 3 on sustainable Tourism Development in Issyk-Kul Oblast is now fully completed. Five Tourist Service Centers, now handed over to the Tourism Department. The "Program for sustainable development of tourism in the Issyk-Kul region for 2024-2028" has been adopted by the Plenipotentiary Representative of the President of Kyrgyzstan. Consulting services for Tourist Satellite Accounts development have been completed. This component is positively contributing to tourism development in the region, with increasing tourism numbers. A tourism center user satisfaction survey is planned to be completed before the end of Q2 2026. 5.
