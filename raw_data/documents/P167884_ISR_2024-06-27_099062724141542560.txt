KEY ISSUES & STATUS Jun 27, 2024 Page 1 of 10 The World Bank Implementation Status & Results Report Guinea Support to Local Governance Project (P167884) 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions The project is on track to close on 31 October 2024 with satisfactory results and having achieved its development objective; however, the timeline for completing some outstanding deliverables needs to be monitored closely. Outstanding deliverables include (i) the execution by communes of at least 60% of their 2023 FNDL allocation; (ii) the transfer to the ANAFIC of at least 70% of the planned 2023 FNDL allocation; (iii) the completion of the 2024 training program delivered by the National Training Center for Local Cadres and Elected Officials (CNFPCE). A virtual review of progress against the 2024 work plan (AWPB) was held on 7 May 2024. The review concluded that these results areachievable by closing date. Project ratings and disbursements. The project's rating for PDO and IP remain respectively Satisfactory and Moderately Satisfactory. The IP rating will be upgraded to Satisfactory once the two last DLIs are met and paid. Cumulated disbursements amount to 32 million USD or 77.7%. Project funds will be fully disbursed by closing. Next steps. A closing mission is planned for September 2024. 5.
