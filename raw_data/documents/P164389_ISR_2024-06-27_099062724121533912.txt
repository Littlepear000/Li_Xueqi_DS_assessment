KEY ISSUES & STATUS Jun 27, 2024 Page 1 of 8 The World Bank Implementation Status & Results Report Water Security and Resilience for the Valley of Mexico (PROSEGHIR) (P164389) 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions The Project was signed on February 28, 2022 and was declared effective as of June 1, 2022. The Project is not on track to achieve the Project Development Objective within the current implementation period. In February 2024, the National Water Commission (CONAGUA) and the Valley of Mexico's Water Basin Agency (OCAVM) formally requested the World Bank to restructure the Project. The key aspects of the proposed restructuring are (i) partially cancel US$90 million from the total loan proceeds; and (ii) shorten the project implementation period by one year, targeting completion by December 31, 2024. Following this request, the World Bank is preparing a Project Restructuring, which is expected to be processed in July 2024. 5. CONAGUA has implemented several mechanisms for beneficiaries and stakeholders to provide feedback and to cpture petitions and complaints on the impacts caused by the hydraulic infrastructure of the Cutzamala System. These have been detailed in a document sent to the Bank and approved in December 2022. The grievance redress mechanism (GRM) is accessible to project-affected people and can receive grievances through the following channels: Grievances resolved related to delivery of project benefits addressed (Percentage) By email addressed to the Head of the Internal Control Body (OIC), • Through the Sistema Integral de Denuncias Ciudadanas (SIDEC) under the Ministry of Public Administration, • In writing through the OCAVM Reports Office, • In person through the General Residence for the Construction of the Cutzamala System and/or the General Residence of Operation of the Cutzamala System, Jun 27, 2024 Page 5 of 8 The World Bank Implementation Status & Results Report Water Security and Resilience for the Valley of Mexico (PROSEGHIR) (P164389) • Through institutional email, • Through official letters addressed to the Director General of CONAGUA. In addition, and as part of the institutional strengthening component, the Project will continue to support CONAGUA in boosting awareness of the existence of these grievance-handling procedures among the public and to enhance the mechanisms with which grievances are redressed as they relate to the Project. CONAGUA has provided evidence that the GRM is receiving and processing complaints for past activities. Nevertheless, as project activities have yet to start implementation, this evidence does not yet concern the PROSEGHIR, and future evidence will be documented as these activities are rolled out – this could happen as early as the first quarter of CY 2024, namely afer the Project's first 4 procurement packages are successfully launched and awarded.
