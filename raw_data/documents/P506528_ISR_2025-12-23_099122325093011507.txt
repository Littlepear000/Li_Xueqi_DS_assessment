KEY ISSUES & STATUS 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions The Human Capital Protection Project was approved by the World Bank Board on September 19, 2024, with initial International Development Association (IDA) financing of US$100.4 million. Additional Financing (AF) of US$100.4 million Dec 23, 2025 Page 1 of 9 The World Bank Implementation Status & Results Report Human Capital Protection Project (P506528) was approved on October 31, 2025, bringing the total financing envelope to US$200.8 million with a new project closing date of December 31, 2026. As of December 2025, cumulative disbursements under the original financing have reached US$100.4 million, representing 100 percent of the original IDA financing. By mid-December 2025, both Progress toward the Project Development Objective (PDO) and Implementation Progress (IP) were rated Satisfactory, supported by strong results in the delivery of both health and education services, effective fiduciary arrangements, and capable management by the Project Coordination Unit (PCU). Key achievements Ensuring the provision of essential health services • 1,885,146 children have been immunized since beginning of the project. • 12,805,489 free healthcare consultations have been provided to women and children under five years in 2025 (data as of November 15) • Children’s vaccination coverage on Pentavalent has improved from 91 percent in 2023 to 96.7 percent as of November 15, 2025. Ensuring the provision of essential education services • 668,140 students have been supported with better education in primary school, including 368,092 female students. • The share of teachers certified by national training institutions has increased from 94 percent in 2023 to 99.5 percent as of November 15, 2025. • The share of students benefiting from annual exams has increased from a 94 percent in 2023 to 97.5 percent as of November 15, 2025. Forward-Looking Priorities • The HCPP Phase 2 supported by an additional financing of US$100.4 million provides an opportunity to consolidate high-impact activities, maintain delivery of essential health and education services, and strengthen systems for sustainability. The immediate priority is to finalize the ratification of the additional financing and declare its effectiveness, thereby unlocking a first disbursement in early 2026. 5.
