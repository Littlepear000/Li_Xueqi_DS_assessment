KEY ISSUES & STATUS 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions Nov 22, 2025 Page 1 of 8 The World Bank Implementation Status & Results Report Second Tanzania Intermodal and Rail Development Project (P176682) The Second Tanzania Intermodal and Rail Development Project (TIRP-2) aims at improving safety, climate resilience and operational efficiency of railway operation along the Dar es Salaam to Isaka section of the existing meter gauge railway.The project has four following components: (i) strengthening of railway infrastructure and support to transport studies; (ii) strengthening of climate resilience of Kilosa-Gulwe-Igandu railway section; (iii) operational and institutional support; and (iv) Contingent Emergency Response component (CERC). TIRP-2 was approved by the Board of Executive Directors of the World Bank on March 28, 2024 with total allocation of US$200 million from IDA. The financing agreements were signed on March 28, 2025 and the Project implementation has started after the project became effective on August 8, 2025. 5.
