KEY ISSUES & STATUS 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions Sep 22, 2025 Page 1 of 9 The World Bank Implementation Status & Results Report Governance Modernization to Enable Efficient Service Delivery Project (P178808) The Governance Modernization to Enable Efficient Service Delivery Project (P178808) has been effective for six months since February 2025. Implementing agencies include the Ministry of Finance, The Federal Civil Service Commission and the Ministry of Revenue. The last six months have been focused on defining and implementing project management systems and consultancy inputs. Hardware and software for digitizing aspects of Financial and Revenue Management and Human Resource Information Management Systems for creating greater efficiency are in the final stages of being procured and consultancy support for reforming various business process across all three implementing agencies is ongoing. Overall project implementation remains satisfactory at this time. 5.
