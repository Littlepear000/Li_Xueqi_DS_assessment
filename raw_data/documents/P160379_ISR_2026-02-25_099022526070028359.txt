OBJECTIVE 1.1 Development Objective Original Development Objective (Approved as part of Approval package on 29-Mar-2019) The Project Development Objectives (PDO) are to demonstrate the operational and economic feasibility of utility-scale innovative renewable energy technologies and battery energy storage solutions, and to strengthen institutional capacity to facilitate scale-up of such technologies on a commercial basis in India. KEY ISSUES & STATUS 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions Feb 25, 2026 Page 1 of 8 The World Bank Implementation Status & Results Report Innovation in Solar Power and Hybrid Technologies (P160379) SECI is implementing two sub-projects under this Project: 1. 100 Megawatt (MW) Solar Photovoltaic (PV) with a 40 MW/120 megawatt-hour (MWh) BESS in Rajnandgaon, Chhattisgarh: This sub-project was commissioned on February 1, 2024, and was formally inaugurated by the Honorable Prime Minister of India on February 24, 2024. The project is in operations since its commissioning. 2. 100 MW Floating Solar PV in Getalsud, Jharkhand: The contract was awarded in May 2024. The construction works have picked up pace after slowing down due to prolonged rainy season. SECI and the contractor are putting in all efforts to complete the sub-project by March 31, 2026. 5.
