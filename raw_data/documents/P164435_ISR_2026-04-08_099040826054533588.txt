KEY ISSUES & STATUS 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions Apr 08, 2026 Page 1 of 8 The World Bank Implementation Status & Results Report Solar Energy in Local Communities (P164435) The Burundi Solar Energy in Rural Communities Project (SOLEIL NYAKIRIZA) was approved by the World Bank (WB) Board of Directors on February 28, 2020 and was declared effective on September 3, 2020. As of March 31, 2026, the project has enabled : As of March 31, 2026, the project has enabled: ( i) the commissioning of 361 health centers (out of 362) and 45 schools (out of 353), (ii) the construction of improved stoves in 374 schools (out of 400), (iii) 240 000 people to access electricity, and (iv) 1 308 000 people to access clean cooking solutions for the first time. The project is on track for the achievement of the project development objective. 5.
