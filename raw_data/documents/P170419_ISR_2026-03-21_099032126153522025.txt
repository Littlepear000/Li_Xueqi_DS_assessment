KEY ISSUES & STATUS 3.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions Progress towards achievement of the project development objectives and Overall implementation progress are rated Moderately Unsatisfactory (MU) due to the persistent lack of progress under Result Area #2 on the development of the four wholesale markets (WMs). The two projects in Marrakech and Agadir are currently at a standstill, with no certainty regarding the timeline for commitment and growing concerns among stakeholders about the modernization objectives. In addition, the launch of construction works for the WMs in Berkane and Meknes, which were the most advanced, still remains contingent on the signing of project-specific agreements. This situation puts the achievement of PDO#2 "Volume of local products marketed through modernized wholesale markets" at risk. Meanwhile satisfactory progress is observed on RA#1 "Increased job opportunities and income generation for rural youth" with full achievement of the target number of young people with their agricultural projects and agricultural, para-agricultural and digital services operational and/or in the process of being implemented and an increasing number of young entrepreneurs who have received support ranging from entrepreneurship awareness raising to business plans preparation. However, it should be noted that access to commercial financing remains a critical constraint in the implementation of these projects. Progress on RA#3 on "Adoption of Climate Smart Practices" is also being observed in the development and improvement of digital agricultural advisory tools. While the capacity for measuring the impact of the extension services provided by ONCA has improved, more efforts should be made on the operationalization of the dedicated digital platform and methodology to be applied by extension agents.
