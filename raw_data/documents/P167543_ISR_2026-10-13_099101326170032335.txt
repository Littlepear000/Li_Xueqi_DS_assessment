KEY ISSUES & STATUS 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions Overall implementation progress and progress toward achievement of the Project Development Objective remain “Satisfactory”, despite security, logistical, and implementation constraints in some rural areas. Mobile broadband had reached 1,071 of 2,175 targeted previously unserved rural localities, while connectivity was also extended to schools, health centers, and municipalities. The Project has further advanced digital and financial inclusion through 125 multiservice digital centers, support to 12 fintech companies, electronic payment terminals, and enrollment of more than 63,000 agricultural producers in the digital registry. Disbursements reached 81 percent as of Septemebr 2026. Rural connectivity remains the main implementation priority. The eight-month extension of the closing date to June 30, 2027 will support completion of connectivity investments, priority digital infrastructure, and project-closing activities. During the extension, the Government and Project team will finalize sustainability arrangements for digital centers, connectivity services, and other project activities. 5.
