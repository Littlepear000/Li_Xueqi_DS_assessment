KEY ISSUES & STATUS The World Bank Implementation Status & Results Report FSM and RMI: Implementation Capacity for Transformative Results (IMPACT) (P506422) Jul 30, 2026 Page 2 of 7 Official Use Only 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions The project is progressing well in both FSM and RMI. The Central Implementation Units (CIUs) in both countries are fully operational and providing support to Project Implementation Units (PIUs) to improve project delivery. Standard Operating Procedures have been developed to streamline the functions of the CIUs and the support to the PIUs. Project Steering Committees are active and providing overall governance. Both RMI and FSM are undertaking an institutional review to examine ways to improve the effectiveness of project implementation and delivery for the public. 5.
