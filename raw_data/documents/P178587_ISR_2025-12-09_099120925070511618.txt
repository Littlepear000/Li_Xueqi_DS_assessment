KEY ISSUES & STATUS 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions Dec 09, 2025 Page 1 of 8 The World Bank Implementation Status & Results Report Strengthening Lebanon’s Covid-19 Response (P178587) The Project was approved by the World Bank on May 15, 2022, for US$25 million (composed of an IBRD loan and concessional financing) and a US$4 million grant. The project is being implemented by the MoPH. The Project falls under the COVID-19 Strategic Preparedness and Response Program (SPRP), using a Multiphase Programmatic Approach (MPA) that was approved by the World Bank’s Board of Executive Directors in April 2020, as well as additional financing approved by the World Bank in October 2020. The Project has been declared effective on December 20, 2022. A Project restructuring was approved on September 24, 2024, to reallocate project funds for priority activities including the procurement of medical equipment and supplies for public hospitals and extend the project closing date by 15 months, from September 30, 2024, to December 30, 2025. As of November 2025, the delivery of several packages of medical equipment has been initiated. 4.2 MPA Program Development Objective Original MPA Program Development Objective (Approved as part of (Seq. 0) package on ) The Program Development Objective is to prevent, detect and respond to the threat posed by COVID-19 and strengthen national systems for public health preparedness Current MPA Program Development Objective (Approved as part of (Seq. ) package on ) 5.
