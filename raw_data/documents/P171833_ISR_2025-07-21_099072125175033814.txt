KEY ISSUES & STATUS 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions In May 2023 the Contingency Emergency Response Component (CERC) of the UBEC project was activated in response to the food insecurity crisis including the supply chain disruptions and high food prices resulting post COVID-19 pandemic, to support the Governments of Grenada, Saint-Lucia and Saint Vincent and the Grenadines with an additional US$10 million each. The CERC funded activities were prioritized by the three recipient countries to provide immediate and urgent support to priority areas including: (i) crop production; (ii) enhanced livestock production and, (iii) enhanced safe fishery production and reduction in food loss and waste. Following an extension from December 2024, the CERC activities were completed and closed on March 31, 2025. The primary focus of the project so far has been on implementation of the CERC activities, resulting in limited progress on the main Blue Economy activities planned under the other components of the project. However since February 2025, the participating countries have commenced small procurement of the agreed Blue Economy (BE) activities in the fisheries, tourism and waste sectors. Regional BE activities are progressing, with the launch of the Matching Grants Program targeting Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises in the three countries and the development of regional policies for tourism and fisheries. There is now a needed focus to speed up the implementation pace in the remaining months of 2025 particularly on planning and sequencing the infrastructure support activities and key policy documents. The Mid Term Review of the project was successfully conducted across the month of May 2025 in all three countries and with the OECS Commission to review progress, implementation bottlenecks and agree on needed corrective measures and adjustments to enhance efficiency. Key findings from the MTR emphasized the notable delivery of critical and time-bound food security activities and agreement that the project design and scope remain overall adequate and relevant at mid term. An additional financing to support tourism activities in SVG (completion of rehabilitation of Fort Charlotte) was successfully processed and will begin implementation by end July 2025. 5.
