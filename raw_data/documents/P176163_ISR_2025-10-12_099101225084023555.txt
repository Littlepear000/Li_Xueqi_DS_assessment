KEY ISSUES & STATUS Oct 12, 2025 Page 1 of 10 The World Bank Implementation Status & Results Report Clean and Resilient Environment for Blue Sea Project (P176163) 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions The Project became effective on March 31, 2025, following the successful completion of all effectiveness conditions, including loan ratification, recruitment of the Project Management Team (PMT) and Project Coordination Unit (PCU), and finalization of the Project Operations and Environmental Performance- Based Investment (EPBI) Manuals. Since effectiveness, efforts have focused on advancing implementation, particularly through the preparation of several Terms of References (TORs). The current status is as follows: • Component 1: TORs for (i) TA activities on enhanced monitoring and transition to circular economy, (ii) Verification of municipalities’ performance under EPBI, (iii) Awareness-raising and communications activities, and (iv) Workshop with municipalities on EPBI have been completed. The PMT is proceeding with the procurement processes and activities will begin as soon as contracts are awarded. In parallel, the technical specifications for upfront investments of equipment for municipalities as well as recycling equipment and compaction bins are under preparation. The procurement processes for these goods are expected in early 2026. • Component 2: TORs for (i) Feasibility study, Preliminary design, Detailed design, Assistance in Tendering and Supervision of works for Gjirokastra, (ii) Feasibility study, Preliminary design, Detailed design, Assistance in Tendering and Supervision of works in Permet, Tepelene and Memaliaj, (iii) Technical Assistance for preparation of the ESIA, Environmental and Social Framework for Gjirokaster, Permet, Tepelene and Memaliaj related to Sewers/Wastewater/WWTP/ and on site sanitatiton solution, (iv) Technical assistance to carry out studies to develop a strategy for sanitation services and regional water and sanitation plans, (v) Assistance for regional/municipal water/sanitation plans and technical assistance for regional utilities, (vi) Unified GIS-based information system to monitor sanitation service provision as well as environmental protection; and (vii) Project Audit costs are all under preparation. After review and finalization of TORs, these procurement processes will be launched as soon as possible, with the objective of having all the above contracts in place by Q4 of FY26. • As of sub-component 2.3, the TORs for a technical assistance consultancy to help identifying hotspots and prioritize an investment list for afforestation/reforestation, pasture improvement and/or other erosion control measures are under preparation and are expected to be finalized and related procurement processes launched in November 2025. 5.
