KEY ISSUES & STATUS Jun 16, 2026 Official Use Only Page 1 of 8 The World Bank Implementation Status & Results Report Solar Energy in Local Communities (P164435) 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions The Burundi Solar Energy in Rural Communities Project (SOLEIL NYAKIRIZA) was approved by the World Bank Board of Directors on February 28, 2020 and declared effective on September 3, 2020. As of May 31, 2026, the project has: (i) commissioned 362 health centers (100 percent of target) and 83 schools (23 percent of a target of 354) ; (ii) constructed improved cookstoves in 400 schools (100 percent of target); (iii) provided 390,000 people (97 percent of a target of 402,000) with access to electricity; and (iv) provided 2,160,000 people with access to clean cooking solutions for the first time, exceeding the original target of 1,800,000 by 20 percent. Progress has been supported by efforts to strengthen market development and private sector participation. In particular, results-based financing mechanisms and dedicated support to distributed energy providers under the project have helped expand access to solar home systems and clean cooking solutions in underserved rural areas, contributing to the commissioning of 65,000 off-grid SHS connections and 360,000 cookstove installations to date. The project remains on track to achieve its Project Development Objective by the closing date of March 1, 2028. 5.
