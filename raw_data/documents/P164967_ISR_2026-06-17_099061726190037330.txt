KEY ISSUES & STATUS 3.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions Significant progress has been noted in achieving the Program’s Development Objectives (PrDO), with most of the final indicator targets reached or about to be reached: 1. maize yields of 3.2 tons/ha in 2025/2026 overreaching the final target of 3 tons/ha; 2. millet yields of 1.1 tons/ha in 2025/2026 against a final target of 1.5 tons/ha; 3. groundnut yields standing at 1.2 tons/ha in 2025/2026 and closed to the final target of 1.5 tons/ha. Increased uptake of certified seeds and favorable rainfall led to this yield performance. 4. number of farmers reached with agricultural assets or services estimated at 350,263 (including 158,862 women 45 percent of the beneficiaries) in 2024 compared to a final target of 300,000; and much more is expected in 2026 with the updated data; Jun 17, 2026 Page 1 of 18 The World Bank Implementation Status & Results Report AGRICULTURE AND LIVESTOCK COMPETITIVENESS PROGRAM FOR RESULTS (P164967) 5. a decrease in small ruminant mortality estimated at 13 percent in 2024, and close to the final target of 15 percent. The update of this indicator was planned as part of phase 2 of the National Livestock Census (RNE), scheduled for November 2025. An update will be done during the upcoming mission. The Program made good progress in meeting the disbursement-linked indicators (DLIs), with 13 out of 15 DLIs whose targets have been met or nearly met with a disbursement rate standing at 81 percent. Regarding the overdue institutional reforms, first, the ministerial order for the removal of the uncertified/skimmed seeds was issue making effective this important reform aiming to improve productivity and the efficiency of the subsidy program. Second, the digital M&E platform is finalized and being deployed for an effective use. Third, the National Agro-Pastoral Development Fund (FNDAPS) have enabled significant progress in the restructuring of the Interprofessional Committee of Groundnut (CNIA), among other actions: the implementation of a restructuring action plan, the establishment of a standardization committee responsible for reviewing CNIA’s statutory documents; the submission of the CNIA-State Framework Agreement by the inter-ministerial drafting committee. It is urge to renew the CNIA executing committee and board to get the Framework Agreement CNI-Government signed to make this decade overdue-reform effective. On the financial side, the Program’s expenditure framework for 2025 is largely covered, with 49 billion FCFA budgeted and a satisfactory execution rate of 72.74% as of October 9, 2025. An update will be done during the upcoming mission. Key decisions Considering achievements made, to ensure a satisfactory Program closure, two following key decisions were agreed with the MASAE: 1. Organization of an implementation support mission in early July 2026 to update progress assessment; 2. Acceleration of Disbursements: Conduct the review by the Independent Verification Agent to confirm disbursements for the achieved remaining DLIs by the end of July 2026. 4.
