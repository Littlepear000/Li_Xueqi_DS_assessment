KEY ISSUES & STATUS 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions Dec 11, 2025 Page 1 of 9 The World Bank Implementation Status & Results Report Public Service Capability Enhancement Project (P174067) There has been steady progress towards achieving project results. Under Component 1, Capacity Building Commission has developed Annual Capacity Building Plans (ACBPs) for all ministries, departments and agencies (MDAs) of the Government of India. MDAs have established Capacity Building Units within their respective institutions to support the implementation of ACBPs and training interventions. Under Component 2, as of September 2025, more than 13 million users and 3636 online courses have been onboarded onto the iGOT Karmayogi platform. The World Bank has shared 18 courses from its Open Learning Campus repository which are now integrated into iGOT covering diverse topics such climate change, PPP, urban development, sustainable cities, health inequalities and open data. Under Component 3, a Project Management Consultancy (PMC) has been in place in the Department of Personnel & Training and Karmayogi Bharat. 5.
