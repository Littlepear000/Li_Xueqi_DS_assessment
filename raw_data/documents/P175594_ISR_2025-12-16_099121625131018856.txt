KEY ISSUES & STATUS 3.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions The Program continues to demonstrate strong performance and is on track to meet its objectives. It has already significantly overachieved its main Program Development Objective's (PDO) indicator on the safety net coverage, thanks to the expansion of the productive cash transfers program (PTMP) to a new cohort of 70,000 beneficiary households in mid-2025. The PTMP has reached 527,000 beneficiary households since 2017 and has established itself as a flagship government program expected to continue under the Plan National de Développement 2026-2030. The Program is also on track to achieve its systems strengthening objective, thanks to the successful roll-out of the Unique Social Registry (RSU), now covering over 1.5 million households. The Program is also exploring innovation opportunities aimed at digital financial inclusion and women and girls' empowerment. Restructuring is underway, in order to better align results with priorities and implementation realities to date, while maintaining the program's objectives.
