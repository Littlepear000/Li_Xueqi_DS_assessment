KEY ISSUES & STATUS 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions Jan 28, 2026 Page 1 of 16 The World Bank Implementation Status & Results Report Ghana Economic Transformation Project (P166539) Progress toward achieving the development objective remains Moderately Satisfactory, as three of four PDO indicators are on or above target: 1. Construction permits: average days reduced from 83 to 53 (target 50). 2. Private investment mobilized: US$890m committed; US$83.5m realized (targets: US$250m and US$100m). 3. SME sales: weighted real gross sales up 16.8% (target 15%); women owned firms 12.2% (target 15%). 4. Commercial dispute resolution indicator to be dropped following activity cancellation. However, the project’s overall trajectory is now affected by the substantial loss of implementation time during 2025, which slowed or halted several activities critical to sustaining results, including the modernization of investor-servicing systems and the delivery of the new business-registration platform. The largest constraint to achieving the development objective is the stalled resettlement for the Greater Kumasi Industrial City, where unresolved compensation, limited financing, and a court case have made it unlikely that the zone can advance to implementation before closing and have increased safeguard and reputational risks. The expiration of key Project Coordination Unit staff contracts has further reduced the project’s ability to manage safeguards and close-out activities. A government decision on the future of the Greater Kumasi zone, together with restoration of minimum implementation capacity, will be essential to protect the results already achieved and to guide the final phase of the project toward an orderly and credible completion. 5.
