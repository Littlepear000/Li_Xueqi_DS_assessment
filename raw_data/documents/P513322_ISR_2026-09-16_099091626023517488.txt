KEY ISSUES & STATUS 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions The World Bank Implementation Status & Results Report Social Protection Project for Inclusion, Resilience, Innovation, and Transformation (SPIRIT) (P513322) Sep 16, 2026 Page 2 of 14 Official Use Only The SPIRIT project, approved on May 28, 2026, has made significant progress toward achieving its development objectives during the first months of implementation. Progress toward the Project Development Objective and overall Implementation Progress are both rated Highly Satisfactory, reflecting the strong momentum in advancing the social protection reforms supported by the project and the significant early results achieved. SPIRIT benefits from multiple sources of financing, including two IBRD loans: a US$360 million loan backed by a credit enhancement from the Advancing Needed Credit Enhancement for Ukraine (ADVANCE Ukraine) Trust Fund, supported by the Government of Japan, and a US$500 million loan supported by a bilateral guarantee from the Government of the United Kingdom. Grant funding from the Ukraine Relief, Recovery, Reconstruction, and Reform Trust Fund will be incorporated through the forthcoming Additional Financing to support activities under Component 2. Under Component 1, the project disbursed US$598.75 million to the State Budget within one month of approval, equivalent to 70 percent of the total IBRD commitment. This included financing linked to the achievement of three Performance-Based Conditions supporting: (i) the introduction of new and enhanced child benefit programs covering the period from pregnancy through school entry; (ii) an administrative reform transferring the functions of awarding and paying benefits to the Pension Fund of Ukraine, establishing it as a single payment agency for social assistance; and (iii) the launch of a pilot program to expand social services for children with disabilities. The Ministry of Social Policy, Family, and Unity continues to make steady progress in implementing reforms related to Basic Social Assistance; assessing individual functioning in line with the International Classification of Functioning, Disability and Health (ICF); and employment support services for persons with disabilities. 5.
