KEY ISSUES & STATUS 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions Dec 17, 2025 Page 1 of 10 The World Bank Implementation Status & Results Report SENEGAL HIGHER EDUCATION PROJECT - ESPOIR-JEUNES (P178750) The Senegal Higher Education Project – ESPOIR-Jeunes was approved by the World Bank’s Board (WB) of Executive Directors on June 29, 2023, signed on October 6, 2023, and became effective on December 21, 2023. The project development objective (PDO) is to increase equitable access to market-relevant short-term vocational tertiary education and strengthen governance and research in higher education and the project is expected to benefit over 400,000 young people in Senegal. It consists of a credit in the amount of US$150 million from the International Development Association (IDA), a US$1.9 million grant from the Early Learning Partnership multi-donor Trust Fund, and co-financing from the Agence Française de Développement (AFD) in the amount of US$55 million, for a total project financing envelope of US$206.9 million. The project consists of three components: Component 1: Diversifying and increasing access to tertiary technical and vocational education for improved employability of Youth. Component 1 seeks to improve equitable access to higher education programs by building, equipping, and supporting eight additional ISEPs throughout Senegal which offer market-relevant programs, as well as improving pedagogical approaches in each of the centers. The sites to host the ISEPs in each of the planned 9 locations are now confirmed; however, they are pending final security measures and completion of the remaining administrative steps required before AMO mobilization. In addition, field verification missions are planned to reassess the exact positioning of the sites and to ensure that they are not located too far from population centers or excessively remote from surrounding communities. The ISEP training needs survey is now finalized and will inform the design of the ISEPs. Regional consultations and validation workshops will be held in end of December 2025 with validation expected by end of January 2026. Component 1 also supports key reforms and capacity building of the 3FPT to ensure an adaptive model based on ISEP needs, as well as facilitation of 3FPT support to the labor market insertion of ISEP students. To date, 1,450 youth have received training under the project through DISEP, CCP/attestation, and specialized certification programs. Component 2: Improving the Management and Governance of the Higher Education System. Component 2 seeks to promote tertiary education, entrepreneurship and research that respond to development challenges and improve the governance and efficiency of the higher education system through performance-based contracts with public universities. All PBCs have been signed, and the initial funds have been disbursed. The public institutions are now working on budgeted action plans, and procurement plans, and refocusing efforts not only on governance, but also on improved employability practices. Furthermore, the National Strategic Plan for Research and Innovation (2023- 2032) has been validated and will be reflected into the principles of the competitive fund, as well as the associated research indicator in the PBCs. Significant progress has been made in strengthening the national research ecosystem. The orientation law on research and innovation (LORI) is advancing well, with a second validation workshop scheduled for November 2025. In parallel, the competitive research fund is progressing: the revised sub-manual is under MESRI review and will be submitted to the World Bank for ANO by December 30, 2025, ahead of the early-2026 call for proposals. System modernization and digital transformation have also advanced. The higher education data system (SIGESR) has completed its scoping, diagnostic and interconnection phases, with several contracts signed, and the student portal and sector dashboard now in testing. ANAQ-Sup has similarly progressed on its AI-driven quality assurance strategy and has begun redesigning the e-ANAQ platform to fully digitalize accreditation and habilitation procedures. Component 3: Strengthening Project Management and Monitoring and Evaluation. Component 3 supports the necessary project management and monitoring and evaluation costs, as well as technical assistance.
