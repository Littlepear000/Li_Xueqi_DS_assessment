KEY ISSUES & STATUS Jun 26, 2026 Official Use Only Page 1 of 7 The World Bank Implementation Status & Results Report El Salvador Geothermal Energy for Sustainable and Inclusive Development (P506109) 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions Project implementation is underway. Progress on the procurement package for the geothermal drilling contract has been slower than expected, but the procurement documents are now being finalized and the bidding process is expected to be launched during the second half of calendar year 2026. Preparation of the terms of reference for the owners engineer, geothermal direct uses market assessment, and environmental and social impact assessment are also being prepared.
