PROJECT STATUS AND RATIONALE FOR RESTRUCTURING The Somali Integrated Statistics and Economic Planning Capacity Building Project was approved on June 30, 2020, and became effective on September 29, 2020. The original grant IDA-D6660 in the amount of SDR18.3 million (US25 million equivalent) is 96% disbursed, and the Additional Financing (AF) grant in the amount of SDR26.7 million (US$35 million equivalent) was approved by the Board on February 25, 2025 and became effective on March 7, 2025. The Project Development Objective (PDO) is to strengthen the national statistical system in the collection, processing, and dissemination of fundamental macroeconomic, socioeconomic, and climate data to inform development policy and poverty reduction activities. The AF is financing an extension of the project closing date of August 31, 2025, to August 31, 2028, for the completion of the following activities: (i) scaling-up of Component 1 to support data collection to strengthen fundamental data needs and fill sex-disaggregated data gaps on climate change, human capital, and private sector development; (ii) scaling-up support under Component 2 to expand the investment in Information and Communications Technology (ICT) Development and Equipment to support statistical activities in Somaliland and the Federal Member States (FMS); and (iii) scaling-up support under Component 3 to support the national development plan and strategies, and build the capacity of the public sector to use data to address policy challenges, including on climate, human capital, and private sector development. Progress towards achieving the PDO and implementation progress are rated Satisfactory. The cumulative disbursement is US$23.50 million, about 39% of the combined financing of US$60 million. The purpose of this Restructuring Paper is to reallocate SDR1.3 million from Category 2 – Refund of Preparation Advance to Category 1 - Goods, non-consulting services, consulting services, workshops and training, and incremental operating costs. Advance Agreement for the Project Preparation Advance (PPA) No. V3390-SO in the amount of US 1,766,000 dollars was signed on June 14, 2020. The Federal Government of Somalia, in the letter dated April 21, 2025, requested the reallocation of the remaining funds from Category 2: Refund of Preparation Advance to Category 1 in the Financing Agreement (FA) for the Grant # D6660-SO.
