No The World Bank DRC - Gender Based Violence Prevention and Response Project (P166763) I. PROJECT STATUS AND RATIONALE FOR RESTRUCTURING A. Project status 1. The Gender-based Violence Prevention and Response Project (hereafter “PRVBG project”) was approved in 2018 with an aim to increase participation in gender-based violence (GBV) prevention programs, increase the utilization of multi-sectoral response services by GBV survivors, and provide immediate and effective response in the event of a crisis or emergency in the Democratic Republic of the Congo (DRC). The project implementation area covers four provinces in Eastern DRC – North Kivu, South Kivu, Maniema and Tanganyika – and has the following components: (a) GBV prevention and integrated support for survivors at community level which supports comprehensive GBV prevention interventions by promoting behavior change, strengthening referral mechanisms and providing immediate support for survivors at community level; (b) GBV response to improve responsiveness of front-line service providers through integrated Centers of Excellence (CoEs), mobile clinics and de-centralized One Stop Centers, and by improving the health sector's capacity to respond to GBV; (c) support for policy development, project management and monitoring and evaluation; and, (d) a Contingency Emergency Response component. 2. The project achieved the Project Development Objective (PDO). While initially targeting 785,000 beneficiaries over the life of the project, the project has far exceeded this goal, reaching 8.6 million beneficiaries through awareness raising, GBV prevention and mental health activities at the community level. A total of 79,000 survivors have received care through the two CoEs, community-based organizations and health centers across the four provinces. Other project achievements include 53 percent of survivors who have received services provided by the project's partners have received at least two response services; 100 percent of eligible reported GBV cases received Post-Exposure Prophylaxis treatment within 72 hours of the incident. The mental health program included among the project activities has yielded promising results, including reductions in post-traumatic stress disorder, anxiety, and depression, as well as significant increases in self-esteem and local functioning among the people given this training. As part of its livelihoods activities, the project supported the development of 1,228 Village Savings and Loan Associations (VSLA) and organized over 100 VSLA exchanges to support social inclusion of Indigenous Peoples to take part in the economic support services and livelihoods activities. The project includes partnerships with the Ministry of Public Health to ensure coordination with the Health Sector, as well as with the Ministry of Gender, Family and Children and the Office of the Presidential Representative for GBV to build capacity for policy coordination and GBV data collection and management. Through these partnerships, 433 health personnel were trained to provide care and services to GBV survivors, the national sexual and gender-based violence policy was disseminated through outreach with stakeholders in the project area, and the number of local women's organizations providing GBV services increased from 84 to 400. The PDO will remain the same during this restructuring. 3. Despite implementation delays due to the coronavirus disease (COVID) restrictions and the worsening security situation in the East, project disbursements increased during the final years of project implementation. The project had an US$100 million equivalent International Development Association (IDA) grant (IDA-D3330-ZR) grant which was reduced to US$89.5 million following a cancellation request by the Government of the DRC in June 2023. To date, 91 percent of its financing (US$76.68 million equivalent) have been disbursed. There are no outstanding audits or financial reports. B. Rationale for restructuring The World Bank DRC - Gender Based Violence Prevention and Response Project (P166763) 4. The restructuring of the PRVBG project in in response to a cancellation request from the Government of the DRC. A total of US$3 million (2,281,403.52 in Special Drawing Rights) will be cancelled. The cancellation of funds will be reflected in a revised budget for component 1 and reallocation of funds in the project’s disbursement categories. II. DESCRIPTION OF PROPOSED CHANGES Cancellation of funds and changes in disbursement categories 5. Cancellation of US$3 million and reallocation. The restructuring will allow for the project to go ahead with a cancellation of US$3 million that was requested by the government. The cancellation will result in changes in the final budgets for component 1 as well as in the associated disbursement categories. III.
