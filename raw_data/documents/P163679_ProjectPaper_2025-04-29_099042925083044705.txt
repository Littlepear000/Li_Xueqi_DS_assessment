PROJECT STATUS AND RATIONALE FOR RESTRUCTURING 1. This restructuring paper seeks approval for a Level 2 restructuring of the China: Renewable Energy and Battery Storage Promotion Project (P163679, “Project”). This is the first restructuring of the Project. The proposed restructuring responds to the request from the Ministry of Finance (MOF), People’s Republic of China dated on March 24, 2025 to process the following changes: a. A 23-month extension of the Project closing date from June 30, 2025 to May 31, 2027. b. Adjustments to the results framework to better capture the results of the Project’s implementation and reflect changes in the policy environment. 2. Project Background. The Project, financed by an IBRD loan in the amount of US$300 million (EURO€267.3 million), was approved by the World Bank Board of Executive Directors on June 11, 2019 and became effective on March 18, 2020. The Project Development Objective (PDO) is to promote the integration and use of Renewable Energy (RE) through the deployment of battery storage systems and innovative applications for renewable energy. The Project comprises an investment component, with an IBRD loan of US$300 million and co-financing from Huaxia Bank (HXB) of about US$450 million, to support the expansion of financing for battery storage systems and innovative RE applications. The investments to be financed by sub-loans from HXB, acting as the Financial Intermediary (FI), to sub- borrowers would include: (a) battery storage systems to be installed: at the generation level in existing wind farms and solar PV plants; at the grid level in existing substations; and, at the consumer level such as in industrial and commercial zones; (b) installation of Distributed Renewable Energy (DRE) capacity with battery storage; and (c) scale- up of innovative RE applications such as RE for heating (geothermal and biomass) and for hydrogen production. A. Project Status 3. Progress towards achievement of PDO. The progress towards achievement of PDO is rated Moderately Satisfactory. The PDO remains highly relevant and achievable with the proposed closing date extension under this restructuring. The Project continues to demonstrate strong advancements in energy storage innovation and deployment. By January 2025, the Project has enabled 443GWh renewable power to be integrated into grids, exceeding the end target of 300 GWh for one of the PDO indicators - Increase in RE absorption by the grids (Gigawatt-hour (GWh)). 4. Overall Implementation Progress (IP). The Project is on track to achieve its PDO and all the results indicators with the proposed closing date extension. The current rating for IP is Moderately Satisfactory. After some initial delays, partly due to COVID-related disruptions, overall implementation progress has significantly improved, evidenced by a stronger project pipeline of the FI and a robust disbursement outlook for the full IBRD loan. The current IBRD loan disbursement stands at approximately 46 percent (up from 31 percent in December 2024), amounting to US$ 134.72 million. The overall disbursement is expected to reach 65 percent by end of FY25. Notably, the Hubei Yingcheng Compressed Air Energy Storage (CAES) project achieved full grid integration in January 2025, making it the first operational 300MW/1500MWh CAES project globally. Another key milestone is the approval of an innovative CAES sub-project in Tongchuan, Shaanxi, featuring two 350MW/1400MWh units. This sub-project has secured a US$260 million sub-loan from HXB. Construction preparations are underway, with full disbursement expected over a two- year period from early 2025 to May 2027. Additionally, the remaining US$70 million sub-loan of the Hubei Yingcheng 300MW/1500MWh CAES project is scheduled for full disbursement in Q4 FY25. Moreover, five additional battery storage sub-projects, amounting to US$100 million in sub-loans, have a firm disbursement plan, with US$64 million to be fully disbursed by Q4 FY26. Looking ahead, five new pipeline projects worth US$86 million, currently in various stages of appraisal, are expected to be approved by HXB. These sub-projects include both behind-the-meter battery storage, geothermal heating and hydrogen utilization. Furthermore, two category subprojects are under Page 1 The World Bank China Renewable Energy and Battery Storage Promotion Project (P163679) development, in the areas of green hydrogen/ammonia/methanol and industrial battery storage demand aggregation projects. 5. Commitment from Government of China (GoC) and Project Implementing Entity (PIE). GoC and the PIE remain committed to the full achievement of the PDO, demonstrated by the completion of agreed actions in recent Implementation Status and Results report (ISR) on Feb 8, 2025, including meeting disbursement targets, conditions for restructuring and timeline, accelerated pipeline development and implementation. HXB’s Vice President and senior management have reaffirmed their full commitment to full utilization of the IBRD loan and HXB’s co- financing to fully achieve the PDO. Despite the higher interest rate of the IBRD loan compared to domestic Chinese Yuan (CNY) financing—and the added currency risk—HXB has expressed strong willingness to deepen collaboration in advancing green finance. GoC emphasized the importance of ensuring the Project delivers its full transformative impact. 6. Project Compliance. The Project is in compliance with all legal covenants, procurement, fiduciary and environmental and social (E&S) systems requirements. The procuremen is rated satisfactory. Both the fiduciary and E&S systems are rated satisfactory. The proposed adjustments will not lead to the change of E&S risk rating and project design. Audit reports and financial statements have been submitted to the World Bank on time with unqualified opinions from auditors. No audit report or financial statement is outstanding. B. Rationale for Restructuring and Risk Assessment 7. The Project remains relevant to the World Bank-China strategic engagement, specifically Objective 2.1 in the Engagement Area Two: Promoting Greener Development: Facilitating the Transition to a Lower Carbon Energy Path presented in the FY20-25 Country Partnership Framework (CPF) (Report No. 117875-CN). The project is also highly aligned with the government policy priorities. China’s 14th Five Year Plan (FYP, 2021-2025) identifies energy storage as a key element of the decarbonisation of the energy sector and its security. The Chinese government promotes the high-quality and large-scale development of new energy storage in order to accelerate the construction of a clean, low-carbon, safe and efficient energy system, and seeks to advance knowledge and capacity in a range of different storage technologies. The Project interventions strongly align with the 14th FYP, supporting: (i) the development of an enabling environment to support power sector reforms; (ii) the design of new policies and regulations and financing of pilot projects to promote emerging innovative technologies; and (iii) the design and piloting of innovative financing mechanisms to leverage commercial banking and crowd in private investors and developers. In addition, the Project will help further disseminate China’s successful experiences in scaling up and mainstreaming clean energy with other developing countries. 8. Changes to the Project context created new opportunities to achieve the PDO. China has witnessed remarkable advancements in its technical conditions, particularly in the energy sector. The rapid cost reduction of battery storage has been a significant driver, making energy storage solutions more affordable and accessible. Battery storage costs will decrease from US$100−US$150 per MWh in 2025 to a projected US$80−US$120 per MWh by 2030. This cost reduction is complemented by the emergence of new technologies such as CAES, green hydrogen, and advanced lithium-ion batteries, which have revolutionized the energy storage landscape. Additionally, innovative business models have been developed, including energy storage service providers participating in power trading as independent entities and the integration of renewable energy with energy storage systems. These fast-paced technological developments have greatly improved market conditions in China for scaling up energy storage, which has a significant impact on promoting renewable energy. By providing the necessary flexibility to match supply and demand, large scale energy storage application enables the integration of higher shares of renewable energy into the Page 2 The World Bank China Renewable Energy and Battery Storage Promotion Project (P163679) power grid and ensures a stable and uninterrupted supply of electricity, fostering a more efficient and sustainable energy system. 9. Despite initial delays, the project is now on track for accelerated implementation, having successfully overcome the initial challenges. a. The unprecedented shock of the COVID-19 pandemic caused initial delays. The pandemic emerged shortly after the inception of the Project implementation. The strict mobility control measures during the 2020-2022 period resulted in suspension of construction for all sub-projects. This led to a delay of more than two years despite the WB’s and HXB’s best efforts to support construction progress. With the lifting of mobility restrictions in Dec. 2022, the construction of sub-projects pace has accelerated. b. The high interest rate of the IBRD loan in comparison to domestic Chinese yuan financing in addition to currency risk. A strong pipeline has been developed to ensure full utilization of the IBRD loan and to further leverage HXB’s co-financing. Recognizing the significant impact of the Project on accelerating China’s energy transition, the HXB management is committed to strengthen the cooperation in green finance with the WB and subsidize the financial loss from implementing sub-projects. 10. Restructuring and extension of the Project Closing Date is required to complete and enhance the sub-projects that are crucial for PDO achievement. Given the changes and initial delays in the Project described above, the WB team and the client conducted technical analysis and due diligence during the Mid-Term Review. It concluded that although the theory of change is still sound, the three PDO indicators and some sub-projects cannot be completed without an extension. The proposed extension is essential to achieve the PDO by completing all the sub-projects approved in the pipeline, including the second CAES sub-project in Shaanxi, which is scheduled for completion in May 2027 (proposed project extension date). 11. Revisions to the Results Framework. The proposed changes for evaluating some PDO indicators which would need to be introduced formally into the project through a restructuring to better capture the results of the Project’s implementation, reflect the changes in Project Context and the transition to new WBG corporate scorecard. 12. Risk Assessment. The Project’s overall risk rating was lowered to "Substantial" due to moderately improved sectoral risk. The proposed restructuring does not change the PDO and project design. The Project continues to demonstrate strong advancements in energy storage innovation and deployment. With the Technical Assistance provided by WB to GoC and PIE, the project implementation capacity has been strengthened to scale up and implement regulatory reforms that covers for industrial user side Battery Energy Storage System (BESS) projects. The WB team has been providing timely technical support to help the HXB team to achieve the PDO and intended results. II. DESCRIPTION OF PROPOSED CHANGES 13. Closing Date Extension. The restructuring is proposing a 23-month extension of the closing date, from June 30, 2025, to May 31, 2027. This will be the first extension of the Project closing date. HXB has developed a work plan to complete all the sub-projects and enable the achievement of the PDO and intermediate results within the extended period. This work plan has been discussed with the WB team and deemed actionable. Meanwhile, there is no audit issue to preclude the extension. The GoC has endorsed the work plan and the WB team confirms that HXB’s Page 3 The World Bank China Renewable Energy and Battery Storage Promotion Project (P163679) performance remains satisfactory, as per the last two ISRs reporting periods. The WB team will also closely monitor the work plan. 14. The implementation schedule and disbursement estimates. The project’s implementation schedule and disbursement estimates will be adjusted accordingly. See Annex 1 for the updated implementation plan and Section IV for disbursement estimates. 15. Revision to the Results Framework. The revision to the Results Framework has been agreed with HXB. The revised results framework will be incorporated into the Project Operations Manual (POM), upon approval of this restructuring. In addition to adjusting the end target date to reflect the new closing date for all indicators, the main changes and respective explanations are summarized below: • Merging the PDO indicator 1 - “Increase in RE absorption by the grids (Gigawatt hour)” and PDO indicator 2 - “Increase in RE use from innovative applications (Gigawatt-hour) into one indicator “Increase in RE absorption and use“ while the total target value remains the same. • Adding one PDO indicator for “RE Capacity (Giga watt) enabled by the Project” to align with new WBG corporate scorecard. • Dropping the intermediate indicator “Installed capacity of DRE (Megawatt)” as it is merged with PDO indicator 1.
