KEY ISSUES & STATUS 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions The effectiveness deadline was extended to December 29, 2026, following a request from the Ministry of Finance. The project remains pending ratification by the National Assembly of Costa Rica, with the Legislative Commission currently reviewing the operation. The Ministry of Public Education (MEP) has continued advancing actions to meet the Additional Conditions of Effectiveness. On September 22, 2026, the Bank approved the Project Operational Manual (POM) and the Terms of Reference for key Project Implementation Unit positions. The Bank and MEP continue to work closely to ensure implementation readiness and advance preparatory activities ahead of effectiveness. Given that the Project is not yet effective, indicator data presented in the Results section remains at baseline values. 5.
