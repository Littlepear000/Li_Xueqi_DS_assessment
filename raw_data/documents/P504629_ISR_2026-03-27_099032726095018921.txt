KEY ISSUES & STATUS 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions Mar 27, 2026 Page 1 of 8 The World Bank Implementation Status & Results Report Sudan Health Assistance and Response to Emergencies (P504629) The Sudan Health Assistance and Response to Emergencies (SHARE) project aims to restore access to essential health and nutrition services and preserve core public health functions for vulnerable populations across Sudan. As of March 2026, over 7.3 million health, nutrition, and population services have been delivered — reaching 93% of the end-of- project target — across 420 primary health care facilities and 10 referral hospitals in 22 districts. More than 2.8 million children have been immunized and disease surveillance has strengthened. Key achievements since the last Implementation Status Report include the expansion of project coverage from 18 to 22 districts, the onboarding of over 6,800 health workers onto digital health platforms across approximately 290 facilities, and the completion of assessments for 92 primary health care facilities to inform climate-resilient rehabilitation. The project has continued to adapt facility targeting and monitoring approaches in response to access constraints and the evolving security situation. While aggregate service delivery remains on track, progress on maternal and child health indicators is below the expected target. Efforts addressing the underlying causes are underway to accelerate the results over the remaining implementation period. 5.
