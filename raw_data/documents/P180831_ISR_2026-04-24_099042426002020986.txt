KEY ISSUES & STATUS 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions The Project was declared effective in October 2025 and is moving from the initial phase to early implementation in Phase 1 countries and regionally. Implementation teams are being brought onboard, national and regional coordination is underway, and work plans are being prepared for countries and the regional entity. Saint Lucia and Grenada have advanced on technical discussions with their respective power utilities for grid modernization. Progress in Saint Vincent and the Grenadines has been slow with the reshuffling of ministry portfolios after elections, but steps are being taken actively by the relevant agencies to advance the project implementation. Delays in operationalizing the RCU within ECPCGC have had an adverse impact on the hiring and decision-making for Components 1 and 2. The next phase will focus on speeding up RCU setup, finalizing institutional details and workplans, launching technical procurements, and enabling disbursements. 5.
