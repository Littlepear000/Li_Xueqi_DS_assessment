PROJECT STATUS AND RATIONALE FOR RESTRUCTURING 1. The Raising and Accelerating MSME Performance (RAMP) Program is a US$500 million Program-for-Results (PforR) operation approved by the World Bank Board on June 4, 2021, and declared effective on September 23, 2022. In June 2026, the first Restructuring led to a cancellation of US$63.6 million in exchange rate savings from the depreciation of the Indian Rupee. The revised program size is US$436.40 million. The program is implemented by the Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MoMSME), Government of India, with participating State Nodal Agencies (SNAs) at the state level. The Project Development Objective (PDO) is to strengthen MSME performance through sustainable and innovative market-based services at the central level and in selected states. This is the second restructuring of the Program. 2. The program is structured around two Results Areas. Results Area 1 — Strengthening Institutions and Governance — focuses on strengthening institutional capacity at MoMSME and participating states, including coordination mechanisms, planning, monitoring and evaluation, and evidence-based policy design. Results Area 2 — Support for Firm Capabilities and Access to Markets and Finance — focuses on expanding and improving firm capability interventions, developing capacity for market-based provision of Business Development Services (BDS), and supporting platforms for factoring of MSME receivables and online dispute resolution (ODR). Results Area 2 also includes three new schemes: the Green Investment and Financing for Transformation (GIFT), the Scheme for Promotion and Investment in Circular Economy (SPICE), and the Trade Enablement and Marketing (TEAM) scheme. 3. As of June 30, 2026, the program has disbursed US$353.53 million out of a net commitment of US$436.40 million, representing approximately 81 percent of revised program financing. An outstanding balance of approximately US$84.12 million remains to be disbursed. Both the PDO and overall Implementation Progress ratings are Satisfactory, with an overall risk rating of Substantial. The current closing date is September 30, 2026. A. Rationale for Restructuring 4. The proposed restructuring is limited to a six-month extension of the program closing date, from September 30, 2026, to March 31, 2027, in response to the Government of India’s request (initiated by MoMSME and conveyed by DEA on May 27, 2026). The extension is required to complete activities introduced under the First Restructuring, ensure full achievement of the PDO, disbursement of committed financing and alignment of expenditure against disbursement. 5. The First Restructuring significantly expanded the scope, ambition, and complexity of the Program. It increased coverage from 5 to 25 States/UTs, introduced three new national schemes (GIFT, SPICE, and TEAM) aligned with Government priorities, and revised the DLI/DLR framework, including increasing MSME outreach targets from 555,000 to 729,000 and raising DLI 2.3 allocations from US$98.75 million to US$125.15 million. These enhancements broadened the implementation agenda and required additional time for results to materialize. 6. Program implementation remains satisfactory, with expenditures progressing in line with implementation and supporting the achievement of DLI-linked results. However, some results under the expanded program design require additional time to be fully realized, validated, and translated into corresponding disbursements. The extension will ensure that eligible expenditures and achieved results remain aligned with the program’s disbursement framework. All the audit reports under the Program have been received for the period till FY 2024-25 and currently there are no overdue audit reports under the Program. 7. The proposed extension is consistent with the implementation trajectory envisaged during the First Restructuring and is formally supported by the Government of India. The First Restructuring Paper explicitly noted that a decision on The World Bank Raising and Accelerating MSME Performance (P172226) Page 2 Official Use Only closing date extension would be taken closer to program completion, and revised disbursement projections. The extension therefore represents a planned measure to complete the expanded program agenda, fully utilize available financing, and align expenditures, results, and disbursements. II. DESCRIPTION OF PROPOSED CHANGES 8. The restructuring proposes a single, targeted change: extending the program closing date by six months, from September 30, 2026, to March 31, 2027. This extension will align the implementation schedule with the expanded scope introduced under the First Restructuring and provide sufficient time to complete remaining activities, verify results, and process associated disbursements. The closing period for indicators and DLIs which have not been achieved, will be revised to align with the proposed new closing date of the program. No changes are proposed to the PDO, financing allocations, legal covenants (including the Program Action Plan), implementation arrangements, or Environmental and Social instruments. All other terms and conditions of the Loan Agreement, as amended through the First Restructuring, remain unchanged. The additional implementation period is expected to be critical for completing and verifying the results that underpin the remaining DLR-linked disbursements.
