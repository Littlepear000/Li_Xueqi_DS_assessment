KEY ISSUES & STATUS 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions The Senegal Natural Resource Management (SNRM) Project continues to make steady implementation progress, with an increasing number of activities now underway across target regions. Field implementation has accelerated, and early results are becoming visible, particularly in fisheries, aquaculture, and ecosystem management. Key achievements to date include: (i) finalizing and publishing Senegal’s Environmental Code; (ii) strengthening the national environmental monitoring system, including expanding air quality montioring; (iii) strengthening fisheries governance and monitoring systems, including fisheries data systems; (iv) establishing local and regional fisheries management structures to support sustainable resource use; (v) construction of the first aquaculture production and training center; (vi) implementing community-based livelihood activities to reduce pressure on forest resources; and (vii) strengthening management of protected areas, including the Niokolo-Koba National Park. 5.
