KEY ISSUES & STATUS 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions Jun 30, 2025 Page 1 of 8 The World Bank Implementation Status & Results Report Guinea – Mali Interconnection Project (P166042) The World Bank-financed Guinea-Mali Interconnection project is part of a multi-donor power transmission infrastructure operation. The World Bank-financed project (US$84 million) consists of two components. Component 1 (US$71.8 million) finances (i) the construction of a 53.7 km transmission line from the Northeastern Guinean city of Siguiri to the Guinea-Mali border; (ii) the construction of three new substations in Siguiri, Kankan, and Kerouane, all within Guinea; and (iii) the extension of the Sanankoroba substation in Mali. Component 2 (US$12.2 million) finances (i) the supply of operation and maintenance equipment for the Guinean and Malian power utilities, (ii) technical studies and assistance; and (iii) project management costs. Although the project implementation experienced significant delays the first four years (from May 2019 to April 2024), some issues (funding for compensation of project affected people, contract price adjustments requested by contractors, etc) have been addressed, and the pace of implementation improved significantly since April 2024. The construction completion rate of the Siguiri, Kankan, and Kerouane Substations (in Guinea) is estimated to be about 70 percent. The construction of the Siguiri-Guinea/Mali border transmission line (In Guinea) is about 75 percent completed. In Mali, the extension of the Sanankoroba Substation stands at about 75 percent completed. About 60 percent of the World Bank financing has been disbursed. 5.
