KEY ISSUES & STATUS 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions Dec 13, 2025 Page 1 of 9 The World Bank Implementation Status & Results Report National Urban Cadaster and Municipal Support Project (P162278) The Project Development Objective (PDO) and Implementation Progress are rated Moderately Satisfactory, reflecting steady progress toward targets. To date, 647,993 cadastral units have been surveyed, with over 113,000 units passing all quality checks in Area 1. Due to unresolved quality issues, the contract for Area 2 expired in May 2025, and a new bidding process will be launched to complete the survey in that area. Implementation has advanced in other areas: all contracts for Areas 3–5 are in place, and a cooperation agreement was signed with the Cadastral Institute of Lima for Area 6. Development of the National Urban Cadaster System (SICUN) is on track, with two stages completed and the third expected by March 2026. Disbursements have increased to US$19.44 million (39% of the loan), and additional disbursements are planned to reach 71% by mid-2026. The project continues under COFOPRI’s leadership with strong coordination among key agencies and maintains satisfactory compliance with safeguards and project management standards. 5.
