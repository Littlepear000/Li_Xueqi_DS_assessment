PROJECT STATUS AND RATIONALE FOR RESTRUCTURING A. Summary of Project Status The project’s progress toward achieving its Project Development Objective (PDO) is rated Moderately Satisfactory. This progress is reflected in the PDO results indicators: time to obtain a construction permit (51/50 days), private sector investment facilitated through project activities (US$890m/250m) - of which actual investment (US$89.8m/100m), increase in average value of gross sales by firms supported by the project (18%/15%) - by women-owned firms (13%/15%). As of June 20, 2026, the project has disbursed US$138.91 million, which represents 70% of the equivalent credit allocation. Overall implementation progress is rated Moderately Unsatisfactory. Procurement is rated Satisfactory, with Project Management and Financial Management rated Moderately Unsatisfactory. B. Rationale for Restructuring This restructuring is in response to the request from the government through a letter dated April 10, 2026. The letter requested a partial cancellation of US$56 million of the credit amount, which will occur prior to project closing. II. DESCRIPTION OF PROPOSED CHANGES Partial Cancellation and Reallocation The partial cancellation will involve reducing the total project finance supported by the IDA credit from $200 million to US$144 million equivalent. This will result in an equivalent reduction of expenditure categories 1, 3, 4, 5, and 6. The detailed reductions are included in the Reallocation Between Disbursement Categories of Section IV. Results Framework The proposed partial cancellation does not undermine progress toward the project’s development objective (PDO) to promote private investments and firm growth in non-resource-based sectors. As part of the restructuring, the PDO Indicator on “Time to resolve commercial disputes at district courts (Days)” will be dropped. As noted in the project’s February 2025 Aide Memoire, this change reflects the decision not to proceed with the court case management system due to unexpected high costs and an implementation timeline beyond the project’s closure date.
