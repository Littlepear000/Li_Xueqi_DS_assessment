KEY ISSUES & STATUS 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions The project was declared effective on January 13, 2026, and the Bank released a first disbursement advance of FCFA 6,199,184,950 (ca. USD 10.9 million) on April 7, 2026, following its non-objection to the 2026 Annual Work Plan and Budget (AWPB) on March 6, 2026. Total disbursements stand at USD 13 million as of April 7, 2026. The project is on track toward achieving its PDO. Good progress has been made on implementation readiness and legal covenant compliance since effectiveness. However, accumulated delays in investment readiness - specifically in the hiring of construction firms and finalization of Resettlement Action Plans (RAPs) - have deferred the start of civil works, currently scheduled no earlier than September 2026 for the four priority investments (roads in Franceville, Oyem, and Lambaréné, and a school in Koulamoutou). To prevent bottlenecks and safeguard the project timeline, the March 2026 Implementation Support Mission recommended: (i) finalizing the preparation of the four priority investments to enable works to begin by September 2026; (ii) accelerating preparation of the remaining investments in the first four cities by mobilizing larger qualified firms and consultants to complete technical and safeguard studies on time and to agreed standards; (iii) finalizing the recruitment of the PIU team and the supervising engineering company; and (iv) meeting all dated covenants within the required timeframes. The government teams are commended for having processed the first advance disbursement as agreed during the mission andsubmitted the financing request for resettlement-related costs to the World Bankon May 28, 2026. It is also recommended to finalize competitive recruitment of the remaining core PIU positions by June 30 2026 latest to ensure the unit is equipped with the necessary operational resources. 5.
