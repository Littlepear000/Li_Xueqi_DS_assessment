KEY ISSUES & STATUS Jun 24, 2024 Page 1 of 7 The World Bank Implementation Status & Results Report Institutional Strengthening for Improved Village Service Delivery (P165543) 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions Following the first wave of successful completion of the face-to face-training for 33,458 village governments and institutions, which were evaluated by participants as highly relevant. An evaluation will be conducted for the effectiveness and impact of the first wave of trainings. The project is on track to achieve its Project Development Objective with the implementation of a second wave of national face-to-face training for 33.038 village governments and institutions. A project exit strategy is being developed and the operationalization of the Learning Management System for national rollout will be essential for the sustainability of the investment. The sustainability of the SISKEUDES system is vital to the success of the project and is expected to be used by all villages. 5.
