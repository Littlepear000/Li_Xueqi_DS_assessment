KEY ISSUES & STATUS Jan 20, 2025 Page 1 of 8 The World Bank Implementation Status & Results Report Institutional Strengthening for Improved Village Service Delivery (P165543) 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions Progress against the Project Development Objective (PDO) indicators has surpassed targets by December 2024. “Increase in village governments submitting village budget realization report (APBDes)” has reached 73 % (versus a 70% target); and “Increase in villages with increase in “village development status” (Indeks Desa Membangun/IDM)” reached 37% (versus 35% target). Moreover, most key activities are now completed. Key achievements under Component 1 including the Learning Management System for Village Apparatus (LMS Pamong Desa), launched in October 2024, along with its integration into the National Village Apparatus Strengthening Strategy; and submission of village budget realization reports via SISKEUDES that exceed the project’s target of 70% (current achievement 73%). However, several actions remain outstanding, and it is critical that the project completes the uploading into STEP of all 127 procurement activities in the agreed timeline. Overall, based on progress towards the component’s intermediate indicators, the rating of Component 1 has increased from Moderately Unsatisfactory to Moderately Satisfactory. Key achievements under Component 2, Strengthening Community Capacity for Village Development, include: (i) completion of face-to-face training for 10,633 village facilitators, who assist 80%of the total villages; (ii) development and integration of the required training modules into the Learning Management System (LMS) platform for village facilitators; (iii) improvements to the digital marketplace platform (P2KTD) are underway, and (iv) the implementation of the inclusion and social accountability scorecard is set to be completed by the end of 2024. As part of the exit strategy, village facilitators and cadres will also be trained in the utilization of digital technology, including the Online Video Platform, under the smart village and village academy (Akademi desa) activities. Based on progress towards the achievement of the component’s intermediate indicators, the rating of Component 2 is increased from Moderately Satisfactory to Satisfactory. Satisfactory (rather than Highly Satisfactory) is proposed, given that several activities still need special attention to ensure full operationalization and sustainability beyond the project lifetime. 5.
