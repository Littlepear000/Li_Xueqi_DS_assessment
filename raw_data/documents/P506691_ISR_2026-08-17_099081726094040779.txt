KEY ISSUES & STATUS 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions Component 1 (Statistical Capacity Enhancement and Modernization for Evidence-based Policy and Public Investment): Since becoming effective in January 2026, the project has made satisfactory implementation progress, with the May 2026 Implementation Support Mission confirming that the PIU is established and fiduciary systems are in place. Continued progress will depend on timely delivery of ICT systems, progress on results-linked activities, and strengthened quality assurance for Household Income and Expenditure Survey (HIES) 2026-27 and the poverty line update. Component 2 (Domestic Revenue Mobilization for Sustainable Public Sector): Implementation has advanced with NBR assigning PIU staff from the Income Tax, VAT, and Customs cadres and initiating recruitment of key consultants, including onboarding the procurement and financial management consultants. As the first NBR-wide operation, the project is building coordination across wings and offices while the PIU familiarizes itself with implementation requirements and procurement processes. Component 3 (Improvement of Public Investment Management System ): Implementation of the Planning Division component is gaining momentum following the recruitment of key fiduciary staff for financial management and procurement. Progress is now focused on preparing major IT activities for smooth procurement and packaging the planned training program. Component 4 (Public Procurement Modernization to Improve Service Delivery): Implementation by BPPA is progressing satisfactorily, with 15 contracts awarded and 74 packages pending or under implementation. Two major procurements— for e-GP and e-PMIS enhancements, operations and maintenance, and helpdesk support—are under negotiation. BPPA is also expected to launch the Business Process Reengineering for e-GP 2.0 in August 2026. Component 5 (Strengthening Public Audit through Digital Transformation and Capacity Enhancement ): Following the first disbursement in June 2026, staffing and procurement are advancing, with key personnel recruitment nearing contract award and procurement packages rationalized to support timely implementation. Training needs are also being realigned with IFAC-accredited professional accounting bodies, strengthening the quality of planned capacity-building activities. 5.
