KEY ISSUES & STATUS 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions This Implementation Status and Results Report is based on face-to-face implementation support missions that were conducted in the respective project participating countries and regional organization during June and July 2025. Progress has been made in the implementation of in all component project in some participant countries Cameroon: The project officially became effective on April 15, 2025, and the first disbursement was made on June 25, 2025. Progress toward achieving the Project Development Objective (PDO) is currently rated as moderately unsatisfactory, primarily due to cumulative implementation delays due to late fulfillment of effectiveness conditions. Central African Republic: Overall Implementation is satisfactory over the last sixth months with significant progress in preparatory activities of the population census, data production, institutional reforms, human capital development, and data dissemination. Under the population census, this includes the recruitment of key technical assistance, the completion of technical preparation including a successful test of the overall census protocol, and delivery of data collection equipment. Progress continues on the modernization of the Consumer Price Index (CPI), including preparations for a transition to Classification of Individual Consumption According to Purpose (COICOP) 2018 and the anticipated deployment of an updated PHOENIX system to enable expanded coverage. Work on national accounts is also advancing, with the 2020 accounts now completed and ongoing efforts focused on finalizing data for 2021 and 2022. Over the past six months, twenty-one scholarships have been awarded to students attending African statistics schools, and staff have received targeted training in survey implementation and project management. Significant upgrades have been made to the data dissemination infrastructure, including enhancements to the national statistics office (ICASEES) website, which contributed to a favorable assessment of data standards by the International Monetary Fund (IMF) in May 2025 Chad: Some project development indicators have improved, all project specialists have now been recruited and are in a position, and the implementation of activities is progressing well. For example under component 3, construction of the NSO and the statistics school, activities have jumpstarted with the recent hiring of the infrastructure specialist that was hired as part of the PIU. The procurement process to hire the architecture firm and the EDGE green building certification provider have already started and architectural studies are expected to start in January 2026. Due to slow progress in the implementation of construction activities, progress towards the project's development objectives is moderately satisfactory. Gabonese Republic: Significant progress has been made in the implementation key statistical activities. Progress toward the PDO is satisfactory. The Harmonized Consumer Price Index (HCPI) has been produce for the period of January to May 2025, with the June bulletin currently being finalized. Technical work for national accounts rebasing are ongoing. The statistical office is preparing the new Gabonese Survey for Poverty Assessment (EGEP III - EHCVM) with capacity building of DGS through regional workshops held in Dakar, Lomé, Douala, and Cotonou, organized jointly by CEMAC and WAEMU. 19 scholarships have been awarded to young students studying in African statistical schools. Republic of Congo: Implementation of critical statistical activities is underway, including the production of the CPI, efforts to address gaps in the compilation of foreign trade statistics, and preparatory work for the rebasing of national accounts. Aug 20, 2025 Page 2 of 16 The World Bank Implementation Status & Results Report Harmonizing and Improving Statistics in West and Central Africa - Series of Projects Two (HISWACA - SOP 2) (P180085) Central African Economic and Monetary Community (Communauté Économique et Monétaire de l'Afrique Centrale, CEMAC) : Significant strides have been made in advancing regional coordination and harmonizing data quality standards among West and Central African economic communities. A notable recent development is the signing of a cooperation agreement between the WAEMU (West African Economic and Monetary Union) and the CEMAC Commission. Over the past six months, two joint capacity-building workshops were organized in Douala (Cameroon) and Cotonou (Benin). Collaboration with key regional and international partners remains robust. Ongoing partnerships with the Economic and Statistical Observatory of Sub-Saharan Africa (AFRISTAT) and the United Nations Population Fund (UNFPA), the 50x30 initiative, and the Africa Regional Technical Assistance Center established by the International Monetary Fund (IMF) (IMF AFRITAC Center) continue to support a broad range of statistical activities, including technical assistance, capacity building, and methodological harmonization. Support to the statistics school (Institut Sous-régional de Statistique et d’Économie Appliquée (ISSEA)) has also been strengthened. 5.
