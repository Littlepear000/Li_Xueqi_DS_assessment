KEY ISSUES & STATUS 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions Project execution remains robust, with strong performance across works, safeguards, and fiduciary fronts. As of November 30, 2025, the Bogotá Metro Line 1 project reached 69.1% overall progress. The executing agency, EMB, Dec 22, 2025 Page 1 of 9 The World Bank Implementation Status & Results Report Support to the Bogota Metro Line 1 Section 1 Project - (Series 1) (P165300) continues to demonstrate strong technical and fiduciary leadership. Construction of the viaduct, railyard, and stations remains on schedule, with key physical fronts advancing above 95% of planned targets. Two trains have arrived in Colombia, ahead of schedule. This reflects positive coordination across the PPP concessionaire, EMB, and the supervision team. Operational readiness is improving steadily, with preparations underway for dynamic tests along the initial 5.7 km segment. Series 1 disbursements are complete; Series 2 was approved by the World Bank Board on December 12, 2025. Disbursements under the $600 million Series 1 loan reached 100% in September 2025. Financial management performance continues to be rated satisfactory, with timely and clean audit reports, and use of automated reporting tools. The $530 million Series 2 operation (P510687) was approved by the Bank’s Board of Executive Directors on December 12, 2025. EMB and the Ministry of Finance are finalizing the legal and financial arrangements to allow disbursement of Series 2 resources during the first semester of 2026, once the sovereign guarantees and local counterpart procedures are in place. EMB has also secured co-financing agreements with IDB and EIB, and a private placement process is being prepared with IFC. System integration and public space works now require sharper attention. While civil works and rolling stock manufacturing remain on track, the next implementation phase will require more intense coordination between civil and electromechanical systems, interface management, and commissioning. EMB is reinforcing its integration capacity with a dedicated team and external technical assistance. Similarly, utilities relocation and public space urban design, still incipient, must be accelerated to avoid downstream delays. EMB has committed to fast-tracking permits and final designs by mid-2026, aligned with the 2028 opening target. Environmental and social performance remains strong. EMB and the concessionaire continue to meet ESF requirements. Implementation of the ESMP and RAP remains satisfactory, including mitigation for affected economic activities and strengthened gender-based violence prevention measures. No major ESHS incidents have been reported. EMB maintains ongoing dialogue with communities and public institutions and has scaled up communication during construction near sensitive areas. Occupational health and safety compliance is strong, supported by active supervision and reporting. 5.
