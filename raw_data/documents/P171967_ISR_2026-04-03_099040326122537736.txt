KEY ISSUES & STATUS 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions Regional Infrastructure Finance Facility (RIFF) Project Overview Apr 03, 2026 Page 1 of 11 The World Bank Implementation Status & Results Report Regional Infrastructure Finance Facility (RIFF) Project (P171967) The Regional Infrastructure Finance Facility (RIFF) project continues to make solid implementation progress, supported by accelerated disbursements and the advancement of priority infrastructure investments across Eastern and Southern Africa. Disbursements have increased significantly over the past year, reflecting improved project implementation and a strengthened pipeline of transactions under both project Components One and Two. Component Three utilization continues to advance reflecting continued demand for capacity-building, project preparation, and implementation of Environment and Social (E&S) standards. Continued engagement with participating financial institutions (PFIs) and targeted technical assistance (TA) have helped expand the pipeline of eligible investments and reinforce progress toward achieving the Project Development Objective (PDO). PDO indicator results have also improved, with both private capital mobilization (PCM) and average maturity of the TDB project finance and infrastructure portfolio indicators exceeding their targets ahead of project closing, and several intermediate results (IR) indicators already meeting or surpassing their targets. Recent implementation support efforts have focused on strengthening pipeline development, enhancing environmental and social systems (ESS), and supporting timely advancement of priority investments. Continued close collaboration with the implementing partners will be important to sustaining implementation momentum and achieving the remaining project objectives before the scheduled closing date. 5.
