KEY ISSUES & STATUS 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions The project is effective since February 29, 2024, and EDM-PIU, AMADER-PIU and the Central Coordination team at the Ministry of Energy are all in place. However, the implementation is still delayed due mainly to the government willingness to revise the initial project design, while the planned restructuring to help address the sector crisis is abandoned. From the PIUs perspective, additional staff recruitment is still underway, but the process is slowed down because of the lack of visibility. 5.
