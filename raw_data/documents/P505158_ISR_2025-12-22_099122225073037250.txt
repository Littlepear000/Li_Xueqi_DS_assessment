KEY ISSUES & STATUS 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions Dec 22, 2025 Page 1 of 12 The World Bank Implementation Status & Results Report Angola Youth Employment Opportunities Project (P505158) The Angola Youth Employment Opportunities Project (P505158) was approved by the World Bank’s Board of Executive Directors on March 20, 2025. Following the signature of the loan agreement on July 4, 2025 most of the preparatory activities for the start of active implementation have been completed, namely the adoption of the Project Operational Manual (POM), designation of the key staff to support project implementation, and the issuance of legal opinion. The remaining procedural steps necessary for the declaration of effectiveness (e.g. signing of subsidiary agreement) are currently underway, and expected to be finalized by the beginning of next calendar year. 5.
