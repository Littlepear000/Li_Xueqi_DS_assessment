KEY ISSUES & STATUS 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions The Second Tanzania Intermodal and Rail Development Project (TIRP-2) aims at improving safety, climate resilience and operational efficiency of railway operation along the Dar es Salaam to Isaka section of the existing meter gauge railway. The project has four following components: (i) strengthening of railway infrastructure and support to transport studies; (ii) strengthening of climate resilience of Kilosa-Gulwe-Igandu railway section; (iii) operational and institutional support; and (iv) Contingent Emergency Response component (CERC). TIRP-2 was approved by the Board of Executive Directors of the World Bank on March 28, 2024 with total allocation of US$200 million from IDA. The financing agreements were signed on March 28, 2025 and the Project implementation has started after the project became effective on August 8, 2025. The tendering process for the two railway track works packages under component A - 'safety strengthening of the Dar es Salaam to Tabora' and 'rehabilitation of Tabora - Isaka railway section (130km)' - is planned to be launched by June 30, 2026. Whereas, the contract for feasibility studies, flood modelling, detailed engineering designs, and Environmental and Social Impact Assessments for rehabilitation of the six dams under component B is under implementation. 5.
