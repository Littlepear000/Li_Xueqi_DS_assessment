OBJECTIVE 1.1 Development Objective Original Development Objective (Approved as part of Approval package on 16-Dec-2022) The PDO is to improve the capacity of the Lao PDR Statistical System to produce and disseminate good quality statistics in a timely manner and to enhance use of key statistics, and in case of an Eligible Crisis or Emergency, respond promptly and effectively to it. KEY ISSUES & STATUS Feb 13, 2026 Page 1 of 8 The World Bank Implementation Status & Results Report Lao PDR Strengthening the National Statistical System (P178002) 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions The project has made important achievements since its effectiveness in May 2023, including the regular publication of quarterly GDP and GDP by expenditure (current and structure), the completion of the 2024/25 Laos Expenditure and Consumption Survey, the Gender Factbook, and the 2025 Population and Housing Census (PHC). However, activities related to price and sectoral statistics, National Account rebasing, data integration, and the development of a centralized data portal have experienced significant delays, which threatens full achievement of the Project Development Objectives. 5.
