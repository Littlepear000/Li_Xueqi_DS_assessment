KEY ISSUES & STATUS 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions 1. Project overview and strategic alignment The TransFORM project, approved on May 16, 2023, aims to strengthen regional, evidence-based fisheries management in the South-West Indian Ocean Region and enhance competitiveness in the fisheries sector in the Maldives. Regional activities are implemented by the Indian Ocean Commission, while national activities are led by the Maldives Ministry of Fisheries and Ocean Resources. The project’s objectives and design remain highly relevant and aligned with the strategic priorities of the new Maldives government, including modernizing fisheries management, promoting value-added processing to reduce raw material exports, decarbonizing the sector through green practices, investing in critical infrastructure, and leveraging private sector financing. The World Bank and implementing agencies have been working on adjustments to the project design and targets to further align with these priorities. 2. Implementation progress and key achievements Although implementation has been slower than planned, notable progress has been achieved. In the Maldives, historical catch reports have been digitized, stakeholder consultations conducted, and Fisheries Management Plans redrafted or updated. A digital registry of fishers has been created, accompanied by awareness activities for the Fisheries Information System. Quarantine and laboratory facilities have been refurbished, and capacity-building initiatives are underway, including the initiation of a degree program and preparations for mariculture training. 3. Advancing sustainability and energy efficiency Efforts to decarbonize the fisheries sector have progressed with the installation of echo sounders and tracking devices on anchored Fish Aggregating Devices (FADs), improving efficiency and sustainability through enhanced data and monitoring capabilities. Additionally, a consultant has been mobilized to conduct energy audits of fish processing facilities and fishing vessels, with the goal of developing bankable business plans under the project’s matching grants program. These initiatives aim to improve energy efficiency, reduce consumption, and deliver cost savings alongside environmental benefits. 5.
