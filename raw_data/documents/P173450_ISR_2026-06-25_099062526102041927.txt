KEY ISSUES & STATUS Jun 25, 2026 Official Use Only Page 1 of 11 The World Bank Implementation Status & Results Report Strengthening the Statistical System of Uzbekistan (P173450) 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions The project was restructured in June 2025 to align with the government’s new priorities for modernizing Uzbekistan’s statistical system, as outlined in Presidential Decree No. 27, and to incorporate the revised integrated census approach covering the Population, Housing, and Agriculture Census. A second restructuring was completed in December 2025 to add eligible expenditure categories, including one-time incentive payments for civil servants involved in the 2026 census enumeration, supervision, and coordination. The integrated census has since been conducted, with preliminary results due for publication in June 2026. The agreed next steps are to: (i) strengthen procurement planning and ensure timely implementation of the approved procurement plan; (ii) secure formal adoption of the NSDS 2026–2030 roadmap. 5.
