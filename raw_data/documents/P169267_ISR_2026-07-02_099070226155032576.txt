KEY ISSUES & STATUS 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions The project has delivered important results for both land and mining in strengthening public administration, including upgrades to technological infrastructure, construction and rehabilitation of facilities, and targeted capacity building for relevant directorates. • Two project development indicators under the mining component have been achieved. Geoscientific field mapping is ongoing, and support for gender, income generation, EITI, and local community development activities is progressing. • The project has expanded the operational presence of land administration units through the construction of dedicated facilities, including 22 rural land service buildings and offices in urban areas. • The establishment of municipal boundary markers in 12 municipalities, together with the deployment of 11 GNSS/CORS stations, is improving the georeferencing of topographic surveys and creating opportunities for largescale land tenure security operations. • The Land Information System is expected to significantly reduce processing times for land-related procedures once fully operational. However, implementation constraints remain, including delays in systematic land rights registration, analysis of geoscientific survey samples, and procurement of equipment for artisanal and small-scale mining cooperatives. These issues are under discussion with Government officials. 5.
