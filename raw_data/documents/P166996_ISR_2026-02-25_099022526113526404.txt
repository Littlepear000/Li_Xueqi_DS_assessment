KEY ISSUES & STATUS 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions According to a report submitted by the independent firm engaged to collect survey-based data on PDO-level indicators, the project is on track to deliver on all PDO indicators. Component 1: Under this component, CTVET is offering apprenticeship training at National Proficiency 1 (NP 1) and National Certificate 1 (NC 1) levels, which entail delivering basic-level training in various trade areas across the country over a period of six months to a year. The project targets to train 25,000 apprentices (PBC 2). Presently, 10,608 beneficiary apprentices who completed their apprenticeship training at the National Proficiency Level 1 (NPL 1), 9,303 have been formally certified. An additional 10,520 apprentices are currently undergoing the National Competency 1 (NC1) training in eight CBT trades. Component 2.2: CTVET has awarded 528 competitive grants under GSDF through two calls for proposals. It has disbursed 60 percent of funds (US$15.5 million) to grantees and is awaiting disbursement of the remaining 40 percent (US$9.7 million). CTVET also opened the third round of calls for proposals, under which it has received 414 applications, from which it needs to award at least 172 grants to fulfill the conditions for achieving DLI 5 (awarding 700 competitive grants under GSDF). Component 3: The Ghana Labour Management Information System (GLMIS) is ready to be launched, however, only 5 of 16 PECs currently operate it. Training of PEC staff in using the GLMIS and increasing public awareness through regional and community sensitization efforts are pending. 5.
