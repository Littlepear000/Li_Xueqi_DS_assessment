PROJECT STATUS AND RATIONALE FOR RESTRUCTURING Project status 1. The Third Phase of the Central Asia Regional Links Program (CARs-3) provides SDR 39.4 million1 in lending to the Kyrgyz Republic through an IDA Credit (IDA-63280) of SDR 19.7 million and an IDA Grant (IDA-D3850) of SDR 19.7 million approved on October 5, 2018. The Project Development Objective (PDO) is to increase regional connectivity and support sustainable tourism development in Issyk-Kul Oblast. The PDO is expected to be achieved through: the rehabilitation and upgrade of approximately 37 km of road between the city of Tyup and the Karkyra border crossing with Kazakhstan, and the rehabilitation of 13 km of road between Karkyra and Sary-Jaz; improvements to aviation safety infrastructure; and the construction of five Tourist Service Centers. The financing for the Project became effective on January 30, 2020, and its original closing date of November 30, 2024, was extended to October 31, 2026 following a Level 2 restructuring approved on June 10, 2024.2 2. At the end of September 2026, cumulative disbursements stand at approximately 79 percent (US$42.08 million equivalent out of US$53.55 million) and works under each of the Project’s three components (road, aviation, and tourism) have been finalized. Under Component 1, civil works on the Tyup-Kegen road, covering approximately 53.2 km in total, have been fully completed, exceeding the Results Framework target of 51 km, with the road formally open to traffic since September 28, 2025. The high-quality road has already demonstrated a significant increase in traffic at the Karkyra border crossing point, with 40,155 vehicles recorded in 2025 well above the revised end target of 21,000 vehicles. In 2024, the indicator was revised to reflect actual historical traffic data and updated traffic projections, with the baseline increased from 7,000 to 8,131 vehicles and the end target from 8,500 to 21,000 vehicles per year. Travel time along the rehabilitated section has been reduced from 94 to 42 minutes. The all-weather road led to the development of a tourism cluster at its eastern end in Karkyra. Hangars for road maintenance equipment were installed in August 2026. 3. Under Component 2 (Aviation), implementation has progressed across all planned activities. Aviation safety audit and accident investigation equipment was procured and delivered to the State Civil Aviation Agency (SCAA) in 2025, inspector training was completed in August 2026, and safety management software was successfully installed in September 2026. The Kyrgyz Aviation Institute completed facility renovations and received the requisite training equipment. Kyrgyzstan's Universal Safety Oversight Audit Programme (USOAP) compliance rate reached 59.82 percent, exceeding the end-target of 55 percent; results of the ICAO audit conducted in September 2026 are expected in October 2026. In June 2026, the European Union removed all Kyrgyz-certified carriers from its Air Safety List, a development that partly reflects the strengthened safety oversight capacity supported under this component. With respect to hardware procurement, the originally planned SCAA procurement was cancelled due to protracted security approval processes; SCAA has since procured the hardware through its own resources and will operationalize it in conjunction with an existing government cloud storage solution. Remaining disbursements for training and software are expected to be finalized by the end of October 2026. 4. Under Component 3 (Tourism), implementation of all planned activities has been completed. All five Tourist Service Centers have been constructed and formally handed over to the Tourism Department. The “Program for Sustainable Development of Tourism in the Issyk-Kul Region for 2024–2028” has been adopted by the Plenipotentiary Representative of the President of the Kyrgyz Republic in Issyk-Kul Oblast, providing a policy framework for the sustainable 1 US$55 million equivalent. 2 The restructuring paper can be found at https://documents.worldbank.org/en/publication/documents- reports/documentdetail/099061124091024320 The World Bank Third Phase of the Central Asia Regional Links Program (CARs-3) (P159220) Page 2 Official Use Only management of tourism in the region. Consulting services for the development and pilot application of Tourist Satellite Accounts have been completed and the outputs formally commissioned by the National Statistics Committee, strengthening the institutional capacity for tourism data collection and analysis. A resident satisfaction survey conducted in August 2026 recorded a satisfaction rate of 98 percent, surpassing the Project target. 5. The Project's progress towards achievement of the PDO is currently rated Moderately Satisfactory, as is the Overall Implementation Progress rating.3 All PDO targets are on track to be achieved by the Project closure. The Government of Kyrgyz Republic (GoKG) is undertaking a large road renovation program around Lake Issyk-Kul. In this context, the World Bank’s support towards high-quality standards on design, environmental and social practices, and procurement, best industry practices as well as hands-on support to the Project Implementation Unit (PIU) increase project quality and provide a high standard for other road projects to follow. The aviation component addresses the key ICAO findings in the 2019 audit, aiming to correct record-keeping and training deficiencies at the SCAA. At the same time the Bank’s convening power brought together the SCAA, ICAO, and brought several high-caliber aviation safety consultants to assist the SCAA in building its capacity. 6. The Project is in compliance with the legal covenants under the Project. The Project meets the financial management reporting requirements including timely submission of interim unaudited financial reports and annual audited financial statements. Environmental Safeguards compliance was rated as Moderately Satisfactory due to several issues along the road construction sites, which have since been largely resolved. Social Safeguards are rated as Satisfactory. Rationale for Restructuring 7. The remaining activity under the Project pertains to the delivery, acceptance, and payment for road maintenance and snow removal equipment under Component 1. This equipment is critical to the long-term sustainability of the Project's outcomes, as it will enable the implementing agency to maintain the rehabilitated Tyup-Kegen road to the required standard, preserve the infrastructure investment, and sustain the improvements in road safety and travel time already achieved. Without this equipment, the durability of the civil works completed under Component 1 and the continued operationalization of the road maintenance hangars installed in August 2026 cannot be fully assured. 8. The contract for the supply of road maintenance and snow removal equipment (CARS3/ICB/G/01) was originally signed on April 30, 2026, and subsequently amended to clarify payment terms. Due to procedural delays on the part of PIU and the equipment supplier, there is a risk that delivery, acceptance, and payment under the contract may not be completed prior to the current Project closing date. A two-month extension to December 31, 2026, was requested by the Ministry of Finance on August 20, 2026, to provide the minimum time necessary to complete this critical procurement, ensure the sustainability of infrastructure outcomes, and allow the PIU to finalize all outstanding project reporting. Following assessment by the Word Bank task team and discussion with the government, it is recommended to extend the closing date through January 29, 2027, to allow sufficient time to complete the Project. Separately, on August 5, 2026, the Ministry of Finance submitted a request to reallocate SDR 1,105,000.00 between Component 1 and Component 2 of IDA Credit (IDA-63280). On September 3, 2026, an additional request was received to cancel uncommitted project funds under IDA Credit (IDA-63280) in the amount of SDR 3,701,428.12 to be potentially used in the projects supporting portfolio in the Kyrgyz Republic. 9. The proposed extension of the Project closing date from October 31, 2026, to January 29, 2027, is consistent with the Bank's IPF Policy and Directive on closing date extension. Under IDA Credit 63280, SDR 1,105,000 will be reallocated from Category 1 to Category 2 (which previously had no allocation under the Credit).4 The proposed allocation to Category 2 will enable the Credit to finance eligible expenditures under Parts B and C of the Project, which have until now been financed under the IDA Grant (which is currently 82 percent disbursed and fully committed). This reallocation will optimize the use of the remaining Credit and Grant resources and align the allocations with the Project’s remaining eligible expenditures. The proposed financing percentage for Category 2 under the Credit will be 100 percent. No change is proposed to the allocations under IDA Grant D3850. II. DESCRIPTION OF PROPOSED CHANGES 11. The changes to the Project include: (a) extension of the closing date; (b) cancellation; (c) reallocation across disbursement categories; (d) revision of disbursement estimates; (e) adjustments to the Results Framework. 12. Extension of the Closing Date. Closing date of IDA Credit (IDA-63280) and an IDA Grant (IDA-D3850) will be extended from October 31, 2026, to January 29, 2027. 13. Cancellation. SDR 3,701,428.12 will be cancelled under IDA Credit (IDA-63280) as of September 3, 2026.5 14. Reallocation across disbursement categories. Reallocation is proposed to be processed as follows: (a) IDA Credit (IDA-63280): SDR 1,105,000.00 will be reallocated to Category 2; (b) IDA Grant (IDA-D3850): changes in line with the Category 1 overdraft6. 15. Change to the Results Framework: Following cancellation of the SCAA hardware contract procurement (CARS3/NCB/G/01), the indicator “Software and hardware in use by SCAA for regulatory oversight management (Yes/No)” is proposed to be adjusted to “Aviation Safety Software is in use by SCAA for regulatory oversight management (Yes/No)” to reflect that the Project does not contribute to SCAA hardware acquisition. 16. Revision of disbursement estimates.
