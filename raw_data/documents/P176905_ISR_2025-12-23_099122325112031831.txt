KEY ISSUES & STATUS Dec 23, 2025 Page 1 of 13 The World Bank Implementation Status & Results Report Climate Intelligent and Inclusive Agri-food Systems Project (P176905) 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions Project implementation has remained on hold since mid-December 2023 as the authorities review priorities, institutional arrangements, and budget allocations. Progress toward the PDO has been negligible during the period, and implementation across major components has not advanced. Component status (summary): • Component 1: Public infrastructure investments have not progressed. Subsidiary arrangements signed with selected provinces exist, and one public works contract has been awarded but construction has not started. • Component 2: Execution is mixed. Livelihood and enterprise calls include agreements with partial disbursements. • Component 3 (INTA): No new activities were implemented during the period; previously planned acquisitions and activities did not advance. • Component 4 (Project management and evaluation): The grievance mechanism remains operational and accessible, with a small number of recent cases. Monitoring and fiduciary reporting is being strengthened to support ongoing portfolio management. The Bank team continues to work with counterparts to maintain technical, fiduciary, and environmental and social compliance and to support implementation readiness, while engaging with authorities on formal next steps. PDO-level results remain limited overall, but the results framework shows some measurable progress on selected indicators during this reporting cycle: (i) jobs created/maintained reached1,696 positions, including 70 formal and 1,626 informal jobs (with 692 women and 533 youth reported), and (ii) under the climate-smart practices PDO outcome, 13,100 beneficiaries are reported as having access to new or improved rural public infrastructure for agricultural risk mitigation/natural resource management. Ratings: • PDO: Unsatisfactory • Implementation Progress: Moderately Unsatisfactory • Overall Risk: Substantial 5.
