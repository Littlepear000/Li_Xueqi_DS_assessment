KEY ISSUES & STATUS 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions The Project has made significant progress in enhancing digital connectivity and financial inclusion in rural areas. As of November 30, 2024, the project has successfully deployed mobile connectivity in 178 target localities, resulting in over 240,532 mobile subscribers, including 212,161 mobile broadband subscribers. The roll-out of digital platforms and services for farmers has also seen substantial progress, with 103,476 farmers enrolled and 219,391 users of digital financial services through mobile money. The project is on track to meet its targets for broadband internet usage and mobile broadband penetration by the project closing date. Additionally, the number of people using digitally enabled services is under review, with a study being launched to assess the usage of the developed platforms. The target for farmers reached with non-financial services is expected to be met if the contracts for platform maintenance are signed by mid-January 2025. The overall implementation progress rating has been upgraded to Satisfactory (S), reflecting the full deployment of access to broadband services and the scaling-up of digital platforms for farmers. The disbursement rate has increased from 43% in June 2024 to 66% as of November 30, 2024, and the project remains on track to disburse 90% by its closing date on June 30, 2025. Key activities include additional connectivity under Component 1, such as connecting 46 decentralized structures of the Ministry of Agriculture and deploying the metropolitan fiber optic network in Parakou city, and scaling-up access to digital platforms for farmers under Component 2. However, delays in the connectivity of local administrations and the construction of the Parakou Metropolitan Network, as well as the finalization of the contracts for the maintenance of digital platforms, require close monitoring to ensure timely completion. 5.
