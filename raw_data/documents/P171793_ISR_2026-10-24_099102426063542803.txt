OBJECTIVE 1.1 Development Objective Original Development Objective (Approved as part of Approval package on 02-Mar-2021) The proposed Project Development Objective (PDO) is (i) to enhance connectivity and road safety along the Zinder - Agadez road section in Northeastern Niger through the rehabilitation of the Tanout – Tiguidit road segment and maintenance of the full Zinder - Agadez section, and (ii) to improve access to basic socio-economic infrastructure for selected communities, along the Zinder - Agadez road section. KEY ISSUES & STATUS 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions The PDO is rated Satisfactory, and the mid-term review confirmed that the project remains on track to achieve its objectives by its closing date of September 2031. However, security-related work stoppages and portfolio disbursement suspensions had a lasting negative impact on the project’s disbursement performance. Despite these security issues, progress toward the PDO remains strong. The three travel-time indicators have reached their end targets, while the corrected fatality indicator stands at 0.74 deaths per million vehicle-kilometers, below the 2026 intermediate target. The new direct-user indicator introduced under the Additional Financing has not yet been measured for 2026 and will be updated at year-end. Disbursement stands at 35.69 percent and execution of the 2026 work plan stands at 84.28 percent. Overall implementation is Moderately Satisfactory, supported by efforts deployed by the project team which reached the completion of about 173.40 km of which lot 1 (116.7 km) are completed before completion date. Lot 2 is under rehabilitation with a completion rate of 52% as of end of July 2026. The Progress includes implementation of 60 km of rural roads, procurement for about 204 km, completion of the Tiguidit–Agadez technical study, preparation of the women’s empowerment action plan, recruitment for community infrastructure, and stronger contract management through STEP Thanks to a training organized as a recommendation of the MTR. Priorities are to strengthen procurement and contract management and monitoring, accelerating road-safety measures, and install weigh stations and toll facilities to protect the RN11 corridor. 5.
