KEY ISSUES & STATUS 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions There have been substantial improvements in project implementation thanks to the effective organization and dedication of the Ministry of Transport and Communications (MOTC) representatives and PIU. Civil works have progressed well during the 2024 construction season, with many tasks substantially completed. Approximately 80% of the loan amount has been committed. Component 1: Significant progress has been made during the second construction season, with Lot 2 substantially completed and only 9 km of asphalt remaining on Lot 1 before winter closure. Draft bidding documents for road maintenance equipment have been developed, with procurement set to commence in May 2025. Component 2: The State Civil Aviation Agency is developing a Corrective Action Plan based on the ICAO Safety Audit. The procurement process for safety software and equipment is set to launch in April 2025, with an individual consultant coordinating the development of bidding documents and the bidding process. The training program for inspectors is in the identification phase, and the PIU has finalized procurement procedures for safety auditor equipment and is monitoring the supply schedule. Component 3: Civil works for five Tourist Service Centers (TSCs) have been completed and handed over to the Tourism Department. The "Program for sustainable development of tourism in the Issyk-Kul region for 2024-2028" has been adopted. Consulting services for the development and pilot application of Tourist Satellite Accounts (TSAs) have been completed and commissioned by the National Statistics Committee. The Team is confident that the project's PDO will be achieved within the remaining timeframe. With the smooth progress of various procurement activities, the project is on track to meet its goals and ensure a successful closure. 5.
