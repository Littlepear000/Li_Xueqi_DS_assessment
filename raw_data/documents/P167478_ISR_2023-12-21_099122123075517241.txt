It became effective on July 15, 2020. The military coup of September 5, 2021, led to the suspension of the constitution and public institutions. As a result, the World Bank’s Operation Policy OP. 7.30 “Dealing with de Facto government” was triggered on September 9, 2021, and all the World Bank’s operations in Guinea were put on hold. A new Government was fully formed in November 2021, with a new Ministry of Pre-University Education and Literacy (Ministère de l'Enseignement Pre-Universitaire et de l'Alphabétisation - MEPU-A). The World Bank approved the resumption of the disbursement on March 9, 2022. The Project Coordination Unit (PCU) is fully staffed and operational, i.e., the project coordinator and all key specialists are in place. The Mid-Term-Review took place on June 1-7, 2023 and the project was restructured on September 17, 2023. 12/21/2023 Page 1 of 9 The World Bank Implementation Status & Results Report Guinea Project for Results in Early Childhood and Basic Education (P167478) To date, most Project key activities are under implementation. Component 1: Improving equitable access to pre-school education. The construction of the first group of 361 pre-schools was launched in June 2023, to be achieved by the end of January/February 2024. A second group of 204 pre-schools was launched in November 2023, to be achieved by April/May 2024. A firm supervises the work at each site. Four NGOs trained the beneficiary communities and they continue providing support to the communities for the supervision of all the activities at the local level. The bids for pre-school furniture, learning, and teaching materials are launched. To teach in the new pre-primary classes, 784 pre-primary teachers completed their training. The National Pre-Primary Policy and its implementation plan have also been approved and launched in October 2023. The Curriculum for Pre-Primary along with the Curriculum for pre-primary teachers are developed and validated by the MEPU-A. Component 2: Improving pre-school and basic education quality using technological innovation. The development of the policy for in- service teachers' training using new technologies is underway with the support of a consulting firm. A Guinean delegation participated in a study tour in Benin to develop a plan to improve foundational learning and to focus the project's in-service training activity on foundational learning. For in- service teacher training, the Project purchases 19,620 tablets and 4,000 chargers. Component 3: Strengthening education sector management capacity. The decentralized teacher recruitment process for the prefectural has been established and about 18,000 teachers have been recruited and assigned through the mechanism. The disbursement for the DLI 3 related to the decentralized recruitment of teachers has been completed. The model for the operationalization of a non-salary recurrent budget for public primary schools has been developed. The allocation of the budget to selected primary schools is planned for the 2023/2024 school year. The first phase to enhance the MPEU-A capacity on student learning assessment is completed, with the training of the education staff and the piloting of the learning assessment for pre-school (MELQO) and for early grades (EGRA and EGMA). Albeit with some delay, the first phase of the contract with UNESCO on the assessment of the MEPU-A information system management is being implemented. The Independent Verification Agency (IVA) for the verification of the Disbursement-Linked Indicators (DLIs) has been recruited and the IVA has verified the achievement of the DLI 3 on "Recruitment of teachers is decentralized at prefectural and regional levels" and the associated four DLRs.
