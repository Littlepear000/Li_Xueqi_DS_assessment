KEY ISSUES & STATUS Jun 12, 2026 Official Use Only Page 1 of 9 The World Bank Implementation Status & Results Report Syria Public Financial Management Capacity Strengthening Project (P512516) 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions The project was approved by the World Bank Board of Directors on March 5, 2026 and the Financing Agreement was signed on May 19, 2026 with project effectiveness deadline of August 17, 2026. Current activities focus on activities focused on establishing the governance structures and institutional arrangements needed for implementation to commence. Therefore, the progress made so far towards achieving the project development objectives is regarded as moderately satisfactory. Implementation progress is also considered moderately satisfactory. The Ministry of Finance has established the Public Financial Management Reform Oversight Streeting Committee with the first meeting planned in June 2026. The Project Operations Manual and the Annual Work Plan and Budget are ready for approval at the planned steering committee meeting. Recruitment processes for the positions of Project Coordinator, Procurement Specialist, and Financial Management Specialist are ongoing (advertisements published on May 11, 2026). Also, the Reform Implementation Teams for the various reform activities are being set up to ensure implementation readiness. The grant from the State and Peace-Building Fund is providing technical assistance for operationalizing the Syria External Assistance Fiduciary Section which will handle fiduciary responsibilities for the project and the World Bank-funded portfolio. The SPF grant is also setting the foundations for reporting and digitalization of PFM functions and wage bill management. Improvements in the budget classification and chart of accounts, as well as functional requirements for the Syria Integrated Financial Management Information System are well advanced. 5.
