KEY ISSUES & STATUS 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions 1. African Union (AU): The AU component of the project successfully closed on June 30, 2026. Through STATAFRIC and other relevant institutions, the project contributed to regional statistical harmonization, capacity building, knowledge sharing, and the development and dissemination of harmonized statistical methodologies and standards. Current efforts are focused on documenting project achievements, assessing outcomes, and capturing lessons learned through the Results and Learn process. 2. ECOWAS: Implementation remains satisfactory, with all planned activities completed and substantial progress achieved in advancing the regional statistical harmonization agenda. The project supported the implementation of the ECOWAS Regional Statistical Program and strengthened the adoption of harmonized methodologies and standards across member states. ECOWAS also finalized and validatied several regional methodological guides covering key statistical domains, contributing to the production of regional public goods. 3. Burkina Faso: Implementation remains satisfactory, with all PDO indicators achieved and project disbursement reaching 99 percent. Significant progress has been recorded in the EHCVM-3 survey, employment survey, MICS, artisanal gold-mining census, national accounts, and digital modernization initiatives. The focus during the remaining implementation period is on disseminating statistical outputs, finalizing project closure activities, and ensuring sustainability of project-supported investments. 4. Cabo Verde: Project implementation remains moderately satisfactory. Several important statistical activities have been completed, while the V-IDRF 2026 household poverty and living standards survey remains critical for achieving the PDO. Given that survey implementation extends beyond the current closing date, a 12-month extension to complete the survey and other priority activities is under review. 5. Côte d’Ivoire: Implementation is progressing satisfactorily across statistical production, economic forecasting, data dissemination, and the use of innovative data sources. Key achievements include completion of EHCVM-3 fieldwork, progress in agricultural statistics and enterprise census analysis, development of multidimensional poverty indicators, and initiatives to expand the use of big data in official statistics. Authorities have expressed strong interest in participating in the follow-on operation to consolidate project achievements. 6. Ghana: Implementation remains satisfactory, with six of the seven PDO indicators achieved. Progress has been made in completing the Ghana Living Standards Survey (GLSS 8), the Integrated Business Establishment Survey (IBES II), GDP rebasing, CPI rebasing, and the production of key statistical products. However, implementation has been affected by funding constraints since 2025, resulting in delays to some activities. A 12 month extension of the project closing date is under consideration to allow completion of activities financed under the Additional Financings and achieve the intended development outcomes. Liberia: Implementation remains satisfactory, with continued progress in strengthening the national statistical system. To ensure successful completion of Liberia’s first Labor Force Survey since 2016, a six-month no-cost extension of the project closing date through June 2027 is under review. The extension will allow completion of data collection, processing, analysis, and dissemination activities while maximizing the project's development impact. 8. Sierra Leone: Implementation remains satisfactory, with all PDO indicators achieved and approximately 99 percent of project funds disbursed. Major achievements include substantial completion of the Statistics Sierra Leone headquarters and completion of key survey data collection activities. Remaining efforts will focus on finalizing survey analyses, disseminating results, completing construction handover, and documenting project results and lessons learned. 9. Togo: Implementation remains satisfactory, and the project has already achieved its development objective. Significant progress has been made in the EHCVM-3, Demographic and Health Survey (DHS-T), GDP rebasing, and modernization of statistical and IT infrastructure. To complete ongoing statistical operations and consolidate project achievements, the Government has requested a six-month extension of the project closing date through June 2027 is under consideration. 5.
