The GRID DPC has specifically anchored the on- going preparation of the GRID Strategic Action Plan which was presented by the MOF at a high level GRID Roundtable in June 2023 with two World Bank vice presidents, 16 Development Partners, and the Chief Secretary of the Neal as well as leaders from line ministries. This is the first Bank-financed development policy operation devoted to Green, Resilient, and Inclusive Development (GRID) worldwide. Many sectors and institutions have come together around a strategic reform program led by Nepal's Ministry of Finance, and other country teams are replicating it to help address the multiple crises (climate and other environmental risks, economic disparities among social groups) that complicate the achievement of durable development on a livable planet. No policy reversals have been observed since approval of the operation in July 2022. This progress has taken place through an election and two consecutive governments. Focus is now placed on advancing the reform program by identifying the detailed program to be supported by the second operation. Bank and Government teams are mobilized and working on the actions identified for DPC-2 as presented in the DPC-1 Program Document.
