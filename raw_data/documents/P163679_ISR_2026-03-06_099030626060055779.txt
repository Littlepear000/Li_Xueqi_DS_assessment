KEY ISSUES & STATUS 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions Implementation continues to drive energy storage innovation and scaled deployment in China. Subprojects span cutting-edge renewable energy applications—user-side battery storage, biogas-based heating, hydrogen fuel cell trucks, and vanadium redox flow batteries—demonstrating a strong, diversified pipeline. The scale and technological breadth of approvals underscore effective investment mobilization and clear alignment with the project’s innovation mandate. Given the strong disbursement performance to date and the robust, diversified pipeline of subprojects, the Mar 06, 2026 Page 1 of 6 The World Bank Implementation Status & Results Report China Renewable Energy and Battery Storage Promotion Project (P163679) Project Development Objective and Implementation Progress ratings are being upgraded to Highly Satisfactory (HS).
