KEY ISSUES & STATUS 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions Feb 24, 2026 Page 1 of 10 The World Bank Implementation Status & Results Report Strengthening Social Assistance and Labor Market Programs Project (P179024) Project implementation is ongoing and has delivered important results contributing to achievement of the Project Development Objective.
Emergency support under the UBK program was successfully completed, providing timely assistance to poor households.
A total of 346,334 children received UBK top up payments;
105,626 families received increased UBK benefits on time.
The Social Contract program has demonstrated strong performance, supporting 3,000 beneficiaries under the project and benefiting from parallel national scale up with government financing, which demonstrates sustainability and government ownership.
Youth Employment activities have generated positive employability outcomes.
A total of 394 youths (of whom 271 are female) completed in class training and received life skills mentoring, indicating effective labor market insertion and contribution to improved employment prospects among young people.
