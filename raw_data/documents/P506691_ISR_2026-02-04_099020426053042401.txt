KEY ISSUES & STATUS 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions The Strengthening Institutions for Transparency nd Accountability (SITA) Project ($250 million) was approved by the Board on June 12, 2025, and approved by the Executive Committee of National Economic Council (ECNEC) during its June 25, 2025, meeting. The project will provide technical and financial support to 5 critical agencies (Bangladesh Bureau of Statistics (BBS), National Board of Revenue (NBR), Planning Division, Bangladesh Public Procurement Authority (BPPA), and the Office of Comptroller and Auditor General (OCAG)) to implement and institutionalize urgent transparency and accountability reforms undertaken by the government. The Financing Agreement was signed on January 7th, 2026, and the project declared effective on January 15th, 2026. 5.
