PROJECT STATUS AND RATIONALE FOR RESTRUCTURING A. Project Implementation Status 1. The Government of Ghana (GoG) submitted a restructuring request by letter dated October 31, 2025. The letter requested an extension of the closing date of the IDA Credit No. 6482-GH and the Global Partnership for Education (GPE) Grant TF0B0846 by 18 months to June 30, 2027. Accordingly, this restructuring paper seeks management decision for a level 2 restructuring to extend the closing date of the IDA Credit by 12 months. After assessment, the 12-month extension of the IDA Credit is deemed sufficient to accelerate the pace of implementation of outstanding activities. The extension will allow the government to fulfill its commitments to project beneficiaries and enhance achievement of the Project Development Objectives (PDO). This will be the second extension of the closing date of the IDA Credit, with a cumulative extension of 24 months from the original closing date of December 31, 2024. However, the GPE Grant will not be extended, as the Parent Trust Fund is scheduled to close. There are no proposed changes to the PDO with this restructuring request. There are no outstanding audits. 2. The Ghana Accountability for Learning Outcomes project (GALOP), with total financing of US$173.9 million, comprising an IDA credit of US$150 million equivalent, and a GPE grant of US$23.9 million, was approved on October 24, 2019. The first Additional Financing (AF1) of US$44.8 million, approved on July 21, 2020, extended the original closing date to December 31, 2025. The AF1 was financed through two Trust Funds: (i) US$14.8 million from the GPE COVID-19 Accelerated Fund; and (ii) US$25.5 million from the Global Partnerships for Results-Based Approaches (GPRBA) funded by the Foreign, Commonwealth and Development Office (FCDO), which were complemented by US$4.5 million in counterpart financing from the GoG. The AF2 of US$117.13 million was approved on December 31, 2024, with a closing date of December 31, 2028. AF2 was financed through four sources: (i) US$31.3 million from the GPE System Transformation Grant; (ii) US$40 million from the GPE Multiplier Grant; (iii) US$40 million from System Change Architecture for Learning Excellence (SCALE) Funders -- including Jacob’s Foundation; and (iv) US$7.4 million from the Early Learning Partnership (ELP) Multi-Donor Trust Fund. The GoG requested extension of the IDA credit closing date to allow for completion and verification of outstanding PBC under the parent GALOP project. Partially achieved and unverified PBCs represent a total outstanding commitment of US$16.60 million under the IDA Credit and US$1.64 million under the GPE Grant. The outstanding commitment under the IDA Credit will be canceled without extension of the closing date, while the outstanding GPE Grant funds will be canceled if associated PBCs are not completed and verified by December 31, 2025. The delay in achieving the remaining PBCs is primarily due to external factors that have limited the ability of MoE to sustain implementation progress during 2025. Beginning January 2025, the MoF has capped disbursements across the entire World Bank portfolio, as part of the GoG’s International Monetary Fund program to support fiscal consolidation. This has limited MoE’s ability to access critical resources to implement project activities and achieve outstanding PBCs. Further, a recent amendment of the procurement act (Act 1139), requires the Minister of Finance’s commencement authorization for all procurements regardless of ceiling, and requires the use of government systems. Specifically: (i) all funds must be processed through the Ghana Integrated Financial Management Information System (GIFMIS); and (ii) all procurement must be processed through the Ghana Electronic Procurement Systems (GHANEPS). While discussion of a waiver for GALOP – in accordance with the Financing Agreement that stipulates the use of World Bank procurement systems – has advanced, the delay has significantly impacted implementation progress and thus the extension allows for additional time to obtain the waiver, complete project activities and verify outstanding PBCs.
