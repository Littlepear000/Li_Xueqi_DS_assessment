KEY ISSUES & STATUS 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions Jul 08, 2025 Page 1 of 16 The World Bank Implementation Status & Results Report Ghana Economic Transformation Project (P166539) The project is progressing towards achieving its development objectives as follows: the time required to obtain a construction permit is 50 days out of the targeted 50 days; private sector investment facilitated through project activities stands at $239 million out of the targeted $250 million, with actual investment at $48.5 million out of the targeted $100 million; the average value of gross sales by firms supported by the project has increased by 18% compared to the target of 15%, with women-owned firms seeing an increase of 12.7% compared to the target of 15%. The indicator related to the time required to resolve commercial disputes at district courts will need to be dropped because the court case management system, which supports this indicator, will not be implemented due to unexpectedly high costs and an implementation timeline that extends beyond the project’s closure date. Project disbursement currently stands at 71.16%. However, a few activities will not be completed due to time and funding constraints. These include: • The planned court system upgrade, will be dropped. • Construction of Heroes Park. • Certain works in the Greater Kumasi Economic Zone, which are delayed due to resettlement challenges. 5.
