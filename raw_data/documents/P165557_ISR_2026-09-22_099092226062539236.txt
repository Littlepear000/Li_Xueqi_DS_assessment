KEY ISSUES & STATUS 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions Overall, implementation across Components under the Ghana Accountability for Learning Outcomes Project (GALOP) shows meaningful progress toward strengthening foundational learning and achieving project development objectives. Key progress under components 1 and 2 include progress towards implementing the differentiated learning refresher trainings, kindergarten (KG) play-based trainings, and procurement of teaching and learning resources for all public KG and Primary schools. To date, over 72,000 teachers have been trained in the implementation of Differentiated Learning to improve the quality of teaching in classrooms. Differentiated Learning teaching and learning resources have been distributed to over 10,000 public schools to support learning. The Ghana Education Outcomes Project component has supported about 52,000 out of school children through an Accelerated Learning Program to transition them back to school, and supported over 100,000 students in public schools through the Mainstream School Improvement Program, with encouraging learning gains and strong placement results. The Ghana Education Service (GES) is using teacher deployment assessment findings to inform recruitment and deployment. Notwithstanding this progress, implementation progress is downgraded to Moderately Unsatisfactory due to slower-than-planned rollout of some activities, which is largely attributed to delayed releases of project funding and actions required on financial management, environmental and social aspects. Priority actions have been agreed to accelerate progress ensuring the project remains on track to deliver its foundational learning outcomes by project closure. 5.
