KEY ISSUES & STATUS Dec 09, 2025 Page 1 of 8 The World Bank Implementation Status & Results Report Financial and Private Sector Development Project (P177722) 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions The project continues to be rated Satisfactory for both the Project Development Objective (PDO) and the Implementation Progress ratings. The Deposit Insurance Fund and the Portfolio Risk Sharing Facility (PRSF) Components were declared effective and are well into the implementation. The main deliverables under these two components include the development of the automated payments under the DIF Component and the establishment of the State Guarantee Fund under the PRSF Component. The Supplier and Export Development Program (SEDP) Component will be declared effective and launched by the end of December 2025, as the disbursement conditions for this Component have been lifted. All three main activities under the project's components are expected to be completed by June 2026 (the end of the World Bank fiscal year). 5.
