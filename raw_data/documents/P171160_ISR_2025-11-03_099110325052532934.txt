KEY ISSUES & STATUS 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions Progress toward achieving the Project Development Outcome (PDO) remains Satisfactory. By October 2025, the cumulative disbursement is US$27.74 million, about 45% of the combined financing of US$60 million. The Somali Integrated Business Establishment Survey is underway and is expected to be completed in November 2025. The listing for the Multi-Indicator Cluster Survey is expected to take place before the end of the year, and the second user satisfaction report is close to completion. The project has achieved strong outcomes, with the World Bank's Statistical Performance Indicators (SPI) rising from 19.6 in 2019 to 49.7 in 2023. The number of United Nations Statistics Division (UNSD) core gender indicators reported by Somalia has nearly doubled, from 18 in 2022 to 34 in 2023. In March 2025, the Federal Government of Somalia completed and launched the National Transformation Plan for 2025-2029, and in June 2025, the Centennial Vision 2060 was launched. 5.
