PROJECT STATUS AND RATIONALE FOR RESTRUCTURING 1. In response to the request from the Government of Benin (GoB) dated March 21, 2025 and received by the World Bank on March 25, 2025, this Restructuring Paper seeks a partial cancelation of IDA Credit 6453-BJ (the “Credit”), granted to the Republic of Benin under the Digital Rural Transformation Project (the Project, P162599). The proposed partial cancellation in the amount of EUR 4,573,471 (equivalent of XOF 3.000 billion, around US$ 5 million at the current exchange rate) of the total current Credit amount of EUR 38,330,228. This mainly results from economic gains where costs were lower than predicted and the cancellation of non-essential activities (including the cancellation of the shared platform for decentralized financial services, as noted in the Aide- mémoire of the supervision mission that took place during 7-11 October 2024). 2. This would be the fifth Level-2 Restructuring of the Project (US$ equivalent are provided based on the exchange rate at the time of the approval): i. The First restructuring (May 18, 2020, CD Decision) was to: (i) cancel EUR 46,296,300 (equivalent to US$ 49.95 million, or 52 percent of the original financing amount of EUR 89,200,000) from Loan No. 6453-BJ; (ii) adjust the disbursement categories as a result of the cancellation. ii. The Second restructuring (January 12, 2021, RVP Decision due to change in PDO) was to: (i) adjust, reduce the scope and streamline the existing components of the project following the cancellation of 52 percent of the original IDA funding from the First restructuring; (ii) adjust the Project Development Objective (PDO) following the restructuring of the components; and (iii) adjust the Project Results Framework (RF) as a result of the reduced scope of the existing components. iii. The Third restructuring (April 19, 2024, CD Decision) was to: (i) extend the closing date by eleven months (from July 31, 2024, to June 30, 2025); and (ii) adjust the Project RF to reflect current and expected level of achievement by the new Project's closing date, and retrofit it by including the two new digital-related indicators from the Corporate Scorecard. iv. The Fourth restructuring (June 27, 2024, CD Decision) was to: (i) cancel EUR 4,573,472 (equivalent to around US$ 5 million); and (ii) adjust the disbursement categories as a result of the cancellation. Project status 3. The rating of progress towards achieving the PDO remains Satisfactory (S). Significant recent progress has been achieved by the Project. On Component 1, work to extend connectivity in rural areas has been completed and all 119 mobile sites have been commissioned. Mobile Network Operators registered more than 240,532 mobile subscribers, including 212,161 mobile broadband subscribers in the 178 target localities as of January 2025 – the Project Implementation Unit (PIU) is in the process of updating the data (target at closing date is 400,00 and is expected to be achieved). On Component 2, the three digital e-Agriculture platforms are launched (platform to provide agriculture advisory services, agriculture equipment management platform, farm management platform) and are currently being scaled-up. Significant progress has been made in rolling out digital platforms and services for farmers, with over 68,614 enrolled and 219,391 users of digital financial services through mobile money. Regarding the digital financial inclusion sub-component, 1,200 Electronic Payment Terminals are expected to be distributed to merchants (which would exceed the target of 800). 4. Based on the progress recorded, there is a high likelihood that the PDO will be achieved by the closing date of the project. In the latest Implementation Status & Results Report (ISR) of Dec 19, 2025 (Seq. II. DESCRIPTION OF PROPOSED CHANGES 5. The proposed change includes the partial cancellation of EUR 4,573,472 (equivalent to XOF 3.000 billion – US$ 5 million at the current exchange rate). To reflect the new amount of the Credit, the allocation between disbursement categories is adjusted, and disbursement estimates and allocations per Components are also adjusted. In addition, EUR 1,900,000 million (equivalent to around US$ 2.1 million) will be reallocated from Disbursement Category 1 to Disbursement Category 2 as stronger needs were identified for connectivity activities linked with Disbursement Category 2. 6. The proposed restructuring does not include any other changes. The PDO, the Results Framework, the project components and activities, the implementation arrangements, and project’s safeguards remain unchanged. The restructuring will not trigger any new safeguards policies. The overall risk rating remains unchanged. 7.
