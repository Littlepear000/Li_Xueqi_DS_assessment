KEY ISSUES & STATUS Apr 19, 2026 Official Use Only Page 1 of 9 The World Bank Implementation Status & Results Report Assam: School Education and Adolescent Wellbeing Project (ASAP) (P507865) 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions ASAP was approved by the World Bank on January 13, 2026, and signed on February 18, 2026. Effectiveness is expected in early May, 2026. A PMU has been set up with the Mission Director, Samagra Shiksha, as the Project Director, and supported by designated staff and nodal officers from the three implementing agencies, viz, Department of School Education (DoSE), Department of Women and Child Development (DWCD) and Public Works Department (PWD). The PMU is supported by individual consultants on social and environmental safeguards. Several activities to initiate reforms related to strengthening student’s foundational learning; and upgradation of schools to secondary and senior secondary and data management systems to build school capabilities have begun, marking a clear transition to implementation. 5.
