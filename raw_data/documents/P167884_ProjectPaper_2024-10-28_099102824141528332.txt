PROJECT STATUS AND RATIONALE FOR RESTRUCTURING 1. The financing for the Support to Local Governance Project (Projet d’Appui a la Gouvernance Locale - PAGL – P167884) was approved by the World Bank’s Board of Executive Directors on March 22, 2019. The project became effective on September 9, 2019. The current closing date is October 31, 2024. The project’s budget amounts to US$40 million financed in full by the World Bank through an IDA grant (IDA- D4450). The Project Development Objective (PDO) is to improve local government capacity in managing their public financial resources in a transparent and participatory manner, and in mitigating local conflicts. 2. The project was restructured on 27 April 2023, to adjust Disbursement Linked Indicators (DLI) and the results framework. DLI adjustments included stronger incentives for a fluid and timely replenishment of the National Fund for Local Development (Fond National pour le Développement Local – FNDL). 3. The project has four components: a. Component 1: Supporting the operationalization of the FNDL (US$20 million). The component incentivizes the operationalization of the National Fund for Local Development (Fonds National de Développement Local– FNDL). A key instrument of decentralization reform in Guinea, the FNDL is replenished by mining revenues and provides all 356 municipalities outside of Conakry with resources to finance development infrastructure (schools, health centers, socio-economic infrastructure etc.). This component consists of four Disbursement- Linked Indicators (DLIs) that condition disbursement on the achievement of targets related to the operationalization of the FNDL and the execution of the FNDL funds by local government: i. DLI 1 - Regulatory texts to operationalize FNDL are signed and the FNDL account has effectively been opened and funds transferred by December 2019 – US$2 M (met) ii. DLI 2 - FNDL real annual disbursement > 70% by December 2020 – US$4 M (met) iii. DLI 3 - 200 LGs have disbursed for appropriately authorized purposes more than 50% of their FNDL transfers by December 2021 – US$7 M (met) iv. DLI 4.a - 300 LGs have disbursed for appropriately authorized purposes more than 60% of their 2023 FNDL transfers – US$4 M (not met) v. DLI 4b - FNDL real annual disbursement > 70% by December 2023 - US$3 M (met) b. Component 2: Building institutions and capacity for inclusive and accountable local governance (US$15 million). This component consists of technical assistance and capacity building to (i) strengthen and digitalize local-level Public Financial Management (PFM); (ii) support local government capacity to implement decentralization reform and local development planning; and (iii) improve Citizen Engagement (CE) in local governance and establish an Early Response Warning Systems (EWRS). c. Component 3: Project management support (US$5 million). Finances the costs of the Project Implementation Unit’s (PIU) core management functions. d. Component 4: Contingent Emergency Response Component – CERC (US$0 million). This zero-budget subcomponent establishes a disaster contingency fund that could be triggered in the event of a natural disaster through formal declaration of a national emergency, or upon a formal request from the Government of Guinea (GoG). These ratings are likely to be maintained following the ongoing project closing mission (September 30 – October 11, 2024). To date, nearly all planned activities have been completed and most final PDO and intermediary results indicator targets have been met or are expected to be met by the 31st of October 2024. Project disbursement stands at US$ 35.8 million (89.5%) as of October 18, 2024 (Client Connection). 5. One deliverable remains outstanding, namely the payment of DLI 4a “300 communes have executed at least 60% of their 2023 FNDL transfers”. The payment of this DLI (US$ 4 million equivalent) corresponds to the undisbursed project balance of US$ 4 million. The attainment of the DLI is delayed by contextual factors outside of the project’s control including (i) the 2022 year-long freeze of Local Government (LG) accounts which continues to have ripple effects on the timeline for the planning and execution of yearly investments plans; (ii) the dissolution of the government in February 2024, which introduced bureaucratic delays that impacted the replenishment of the FNDL; (iii) the dissolution of municipal councils in March 2024; and (iv) the suspension of a local public service reform initiated in 2023. Despite these delays, the execution of the FNDL funds by LGs is underway. To date, LGs have executed 35% of their FNDL 2023 allocation (of the 387 billion GNF (44 million USD) allocated to LGs in the amended finance law) and have committed an additional 252 billion GNF (29 million USD) in contracts for local investments. 6. In light of the above circumstances, the Ministry for Territorial Administration and Decentralization (Ministère de l’Administration du Territoire et de la Décentralisation – MATD) has formally requested a 3-month extension of the project’s closing date to the 31st of January 2025. The extension will provide sufficient time to achieve the target for DLI 4a and ensure the verification of the DLI by the independent verification agent. II. DESCRIPTION OF PROPOSED CHANGES The only proposed change to the project consists of an extension of the project and loan closing date from the 31st of October 2024 to the 31st of January 2025. This extension carries no budgetary implications as the follow-on operation, the Support to Local Governance Project 2 (Project d’Appui a la Gouvernance Locale 2 - PAGL 2), effective since October 2023, will cover the full cost of PIU salaries and operating costs.
