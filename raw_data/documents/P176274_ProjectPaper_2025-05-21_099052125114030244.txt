PROJECT STATUS AND RATIONALE FOR RESTRUCTURING Project Status 1. The project was approved by the World Bank Board on May 5, 2022, and the financing agreement was signed by the Government of the Central African Republic on May 19, 2022. A first restructuring took place on December 22, 2022. 2. All components are under implementation, although progress has been relatively slow due to capacity constraints within participating institutions. Nevertheless, activities are advancing. For Component 2, which focuses on the Partial Credit Guarantee Fund for small and medium enterprises, the project has reached the stage where capitalization can begin, a critical step for supporting private sector access to finance. Rationale for Restructuring 3. Under Subcomponent 2.2, a total of USD 5 million was allocated for the capitalization of the Partial Credit Guarantee Fund, to be disbursed in two equal tranches of USD 2.5 million. While conditions for disbursing the first tranche have been met, a regulatory requirement now necessitates a revision to this allocation. 4. According to applicable regulations of the Central African Banking Commission (Commission Bancaire de l’Afrique Centrale, COBAC): (a) Article 2 of COBAC Regulation R 2009/01 requires that financial institutions maintain a minimum capital of CFA francs 2 billion, approximately USD 3.5 million at current exchange rates (b) Article 5 of COBAC Regulation R 2016/01 mandates that proof of actual payment of this capital into the institution’s account must be provided before the license can be approved by COBAC 5. To enable the Guarantee Fund to obtain its license as a financial institution, at least USD 3.5 million must be deposited into its account at the Bank of Central African States (BEAC). A reallocation of funds is therefore necessary to comply with this requirement. II. DESCRIPTION OF PROPOSED CHANGES 6.
