KEY ISSUES & STATUS May 04, 2026 Official Use Only Page 1 of 8 The World Bank Implementation Status & Results Report Catalyzing Entrepreneurship for Job Creation (P162835) 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions The Egypt Catalyzing Entrepreneurship for Job Creation project (US$ 200 million; ongoing; closing date extended till December 31, 2026) is a nation-wide, sector-agnostic program that offers a comprehensive package of financial and non- financial support to the broad spectrum of entrepreneurs and business owners in Egypt (micro firms, traditional SMEs, innovative start-ups and high-growth firms). The Project Development Objective (PDO) is to foster job creation and improve other economic opportunities for targeted beneficiaries. Project comprises the following components: (i) Component 1: Financial Support to MSMEs (US$146 million). This component entails the provision of debt financing in the form of financial intermediary loans to participating financial intermediaries (banks and NBFIs) for the purpose of on-lending as sub-loans to eligible MSMEs. The objective of this component is to address access to finance gaps among MSMEs in Egypt and encourage greater private sector participation in the MSME market through a robust market demonstration effect; (ii) Component 2: Risk Capital for Innovative Startups and High Growth SMEs (US$50 million). This component entails the provision of equity investments to eligible risk capital intermediaries (e.g. venture capital and private equity firms) that target innovative startups and high growth SMEs. The objective of this component is to increase the early-stage finance offer in the market funding innovation and high growth entrepreneurship. This component brought to life the country’s most recent fund of funds; (iii) Component 3: Business and Capacity Development (US$3.5 million). This component entails the provision of non- financial support to the participating intermediaries and beneficiaries; and entrepreneurship ecosystem development activities; as well as provision of technical assistance to enhance MSMEDA’s capabilities; and project management costs. A EUR 5 million Bank-executed TF grant from the Italian Agency for Development Cooperation (AICS) is associated with this component. Discussions are underway for another grant from Qatar. The operation has yielded positive impacts across all components. Key highlights: (i) more than 200,000 entrepreneurs and business owners have been supported through this project, almost half are women and youth; (ii) more than 400,000 jobs have been supported; (iii) in the traditional MSME space: 50% of beneficiaries are first-time borrowers; (iv) in the investment space (fund of funds metrics): the US$ 50 million supported 15 funds, 150 innovative start-ups and high- growth firms, mobilized more than US$ 170 million in private capital, and leveraged US$450 million in FDI; 45,000 high- value jobs have been created—35% women. Main sectors benefiting from the investments: fintech (27.69%), e-commerce (17.98%), AI (3.82%), agritech (4.11%), pharmaceuticals (4.48%), transport and logistics (4.72%), infrastructure (8.72%), consumer goods and retail (4.23%), other (24.25%). 5.
