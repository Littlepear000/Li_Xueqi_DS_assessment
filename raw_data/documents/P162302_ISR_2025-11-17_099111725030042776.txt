KEY ISSUES & STATUS 3.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions The program implementation is on track to achieve its targets. The province has surpassed the Program’s target for total own-source tax revenue in FY25, collecting PKR 70 billion against the target of PKR 59 billion. The KP Revenue Authority has also improved Sales Tax on Services filing compliance to 72% in FY24, with the FY25 target to be reviewed in December 2025. Targets for tax administration database integration and updating urban property valuations are progressing well, while cash management targets for FY25 have been partially achieved, including the approval of updated cash management policy, establishment of cash management unit, and submission of cash plans by selected departments. Various modules under e-Governance component have been rolled out and being used by the government departments, which will contribute to improved government efficiency.
