KEY ISSUES & STATUS 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions The project continues to support the Ministry of Health (MoH) in responding to the COVID-19 pandemic, strengthening the health systems for emergency preparedness, and responding to the Cholera outbreak. The progress towards achievement of the Project Development Objective (PDO) remains Satisfactory and overall implementation progress is Moderately Satisfactory. The project has strengthened the MoH’s capacity to confirm disease outbreaks such as Cholera and MPOX, alongside improvements in the genomic surveillance workplan and laboratory quality assurance through proficiency testing. Procurement and installation of various medical equipment have progressed significantly, enhancing access to diagnostic services across the country. 4.2 MPA Program Development Objective Original MPA Program Development Objective (Approved as part of (Seq. ) package on ) Current MPA Program Development Objective (Approved as part of (Seq. ) package on ) 4.3 Multiphase Programmatic Approach (MPA) Status (Public Disclosure) As we approach the end of FY25, steady progress continues to be made under the SPRP Program, including under the MPA. Disbursements have been impacted by: (i) the processing of refunds to the project accounts, which in the past several months have increased at this late stage of project implementation, and (ii) fluctuations between the SDRs and Euros against the US dollar for operations denominated in SDRs and Euros. The following is a breakdown of closing dates: (i) fifty-eight (58) operations closed by June 30, 2024, (ii) ten (10) operations closed by December 31, 2024, and (iii) six operations closed by May, 2025 (Angola, Kenya, Mongolia, PNG, Samoa and Ukraine). With 74 operations (76%) out of 97 country operations closed by May 30, 2025, we will have an additional seven (7) closing by June 30, 2025. The remaining 16 will close between July and December 2025. As with the Mid Term Review (MTR) issued on March 25, 2023, no individual ICR is required for COVID-19 MPA operations. Only one Global ICR will be prepared upon completion of the SPRP COVID-19 MPA Program, which closes on December 31, 2025. The Global ICR will be informed by final ISRs packages prepared by country operations’ teams. A template for the final ISR package was issued by OPCS on March 21, 2023. Final ISR packages include a Summary Project Assessment – Annex 1, Borrower’s Evaluation – Annex 2, which is optional, and the final ISR. So far, sixty-nine (69) final ISR packages have been prepared (93% of operations that have closed). The 69 include three full-fledged ICRs for COVID-19 projects which were not financed under the MPA -- Sudan, Iran, and West Bank and Gaza and therefore required an ICR rather than Summary Assessments. As of June 16, 2025, overall disbursements under the MPA are $4,051 million or 99 (one percent higher than a month ago) of commitments of $4.11 billion. This amount does not include disbursements under MPA-V operations which are reported together with AF-V operations or disbursements under PEF grants as commitments do not include PEF Grants. Sixty-seven (67 operations or 78% of projects approved are fully disbursed (two percent higher than over a month ago). In addition, five have reached 98-99%, and nine have reached 92-97%. All MPA projects in AFW, ECA, and MNA are fully disbursed. Disbursements for the other four regions are: (i) EAP and LAC 98%, (ii) SAR 96%, and (iii) AFE 95% (one percentage point higher than two months ago). The SAR disbursements are impacted by the high undisbursed balance under the Afghanistan operation while the disbursements in AFE are a result of undisbursed balances under the Kenya operation. Retroactive Financing (RF) continues to represent 19 percent of overall commitments. Disbursements to UN agencies represent 13 percent of overall disbursements, while Direct Payment (DP) represents 23 percent of disbursements. EAP has the highest level of DP disbursements at 79 percent, followed by AFW at 39 percent. IDA continues to represent 40 percent while IBRD represents 60 percent of disbursements. The projects are benefitting about 4.01 billion people or 50 percent of the global population. When countries with AF- V operations are counted, the projects are benefitting about 4.26 billion or 54 percent of the global population. This is because some countries that do not have a parent project have AF-V operations. Streamlined procedures, delegated approvals, coupled with flexible project design and intensified efforts by counterparts and Bank teams have contributed to the progress. Implementation is being guided by Bank teams working in parallel with other health related projects, including Additional Financing operations which have supported vaccine procurement and deployment efforts. 5.
