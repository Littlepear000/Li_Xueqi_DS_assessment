KEY ISSUES & STATUS 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions The SERSP Parent Project closed successfully in December 2025, achieving all project targets and benefiting more than 338,000 people. All 208 community infrastructure subprojects were completed. Activities under Component 2 exceeded targets, reaching 22,562 youth through skills development and provided livelihood and entrepreneurship support to 2,500 youth. Implementation of the Additional Financing (AF) is also progressing satisfactorily. Community mobilization has been completed in the eight Rogun HPP-affected resettlement villages, with over 160 meetings conducted and nearly 5,000 people participating. Implementation partners have been mobilized, training materials updated, and entrepreneurship and livelihood activities initiated. Mobilization has also commenced in the 46 participating jamoats to identify and prioritize local social and economic investments. Communities are being informed that project-financed investments are being implemented as a pilot benefit-sharing mechanism associated with the Rogun Hydropower Project. 5.
