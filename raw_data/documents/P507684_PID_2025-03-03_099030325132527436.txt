Introduction and Context Country Context 1. Jordan has made notable strides in fostering its entrepreneurship and innovation ecosystem, with initiatives to support startups, promote digital transformation, and integrate technology into key sectors, although challenges remain in scaling these efforts and ensuring widespread impact. Access to finance remains an important hurdle. This undermines the growth of high-potential sectors and startups, which are vital for diversifying the economy, increasing exports, and creating jobs. Against this backdrop, it is essential to expand funding opportunities and stimulate additional sources of financing beyond the banking sector, particularly for innovative early-stage companies. Strengthening early-stage financing aligns with the Economic Modernization Vision (EMV), which prioritizes entrepreneurship and innovation as key drivers of sustainable economic growth and job creation. By addressing financing gaps and improving access to risk capital, the project will support EMV’s Page 2 The World Bank Jordan Innovative Startups and SMEs Fund 2.0 (P507684) objective of fostering a competitive and investment-friendly business environment that enables high-growth firms to scale and contribute to Jordan’s economic transformation. Sectoral and Institutional Context 2 Despite progress achieved in recent years, access to finance remains a significant constraint for MSMEs in Jordan. According to the 2023 diagnostic study conducted by the Central Bank of Jordan for the new National Financial Inclusion Strategy, only 28 percent of surveyed micro-, small- and medium-sized enterprises (MSMEs) borrowed from financial institutions in 2022, and just 50 percent had an account at financial institutions, underscoring significant gaps in financial access and inclusion for MSMEs. 3 Reflective of Jordan government’s support in facilitating entrepreneurial ecosystem, and an overall increased dynamism in regional venture capital (VC) market, Jordan has been among MENA’s fastest growing VC ecosystems. In 2018-2022, Jordan was strategically positioned in MENA as the 4th most funded country in the VC market, with $246M of total funding value, after UAE ($4.4 billion), Saudi Arabia ($1.9 billion), and Egypt ($1.4 billion). Top industries by funding volume in Jordan were e-commerce/retail (20%), EdTech (16%), and Health Tech (15%). Amongst countries that are yet to close a MEGA deal ($100M+), Jordan is the most funded. 4 In Jordan, despite the substantial impact made by the Innovative Startups and SMEs Fund (ISSF) 1.0, the Jordanian venture capital market has significant potential for further deepening. Launched in 2017, ISSF 1.0 was designed to catalyze early-stage equity financing for innovative startups and SMEs by mobilizing private capital and strengthening the venture capital ecosystem. The fund made strategic investments in local and regional VC funds, supported direct investments in high-potential startups, and provided capacity-building initiatives to enhance the entrepreneurship ecosystem. Limited long-term funding options often lead promising Jordanian startups to relocate their core operations to other countries, with much deeper private capital markets, in search for growth capital. This points to the importance of sustained Jordan government support to the venture capital ecosystem, including for the development of strong pipeline of investable startups. Relationship to CPF 5 The project will directly contribute to the Jordan Country Partnership Framework (CPF) 2024-2029 Objective 1 (Improved competitiveness and investment in high-potential sectors) and Objective 2 (Expanded MSME growth, entrepreneurship, and access to finance) by enhancing startup finance accessibility and boosting entrepreneurship skills and innovation capacity. This will complement the IFC programs to improve the quality and quantity of venture capital, with a specific emphasis on empowering female entrepreneurs, including the Start-Mashreq program. C. Project Description 6 The proposed ISSF 2.0 would be a follow-on project aiming to scale up early-stage equity funding for entrepreneurs in Jordan, building on the results and lessons of the ongoing ISSF 1.0 project. It will capitalize on ISSF's established position as a respected ecosystem leader both regionally and internationally. By leveraging ISSF's strong brand recognition and its strategic partnerships with venture capital funds, there is a significant opportunity to elevate Jordan's profile as an attractive investment destination. 7 The proposed new project would build on the lessons and experience of ISSF 1.0 and will potentially focus on continued Fund of Funds investments, co-investment in startups, and project management support.
