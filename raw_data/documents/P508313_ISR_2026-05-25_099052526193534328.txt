KEY ISSUES & STATUS May 25, 2026 Official Use Only Page 1 of 9 The World Bank Implementation Status & Results Report Cambodia Basic Education Improvement Project (P508313) 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions The project has effectively moved from the preparation phase to active implementation. Key areas, including technical operations, fiduciary management, procurement, safeguards, and monitoring and evaluation (M&E), are fully functioning, with the project management unit (PMU) adequately staffed. The 2026 Annual Work Plan and Budget has been approved and initial disbursement was made to the project’s designated account. Implementation is advancing across various components: Early Grade Learning is being rolled out in several provinces, preparations for the targeted instruction (PTOM) pilot are underway, childcare and preschool initiatives are progressing, and infrastructure works are being planned. The PMU conducts regular monthly meetings to monitor progress and implement timely corrective measures, and the development of an M&E dashboard is nearing completion. 5.
