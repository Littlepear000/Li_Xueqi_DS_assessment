KEY ISSUES & STATUS 3.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions After the program’s restructuring in 2025, new agreements allowed SIB and STB to join Barqi Tojik (BT) as implementing agencies, which resulted in the release of 20% of program funds and supported reforms in Tajikistan’s electricity sector. Recent progress includes achievement of some critical reforms related to tariff increases (with further adjustments for low-income households anticipated), restructuring of BT’s debt to the Ministry of Finance, and the formation of supervisory boards for each of the three companies. Operational and financial data are now published on the company’s websites to enhance transparency. As a result, the Nov 12, 2025 Page 1 of 21 The World Bank Implementation Status & Results Report Program for the Recovery of the Electricity Sector in Tajikistan (P168211) program’s implementation rating has been revised to “Moderately Satisfactory.” Continued actions are required to support the energy sector financial sustainability, electricity supply reliability and corporate governance. 4.
