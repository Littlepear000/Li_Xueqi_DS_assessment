KEY ISSUES & STATUS Mar 17, 2026 Page 1 of 12 The World Bank Implementation Status & Results Report Niger: Smart Villages for rural growth and digital inclusion (P167543) 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions Implementation Status The Niger: Smart Villages for Rural Growth and Digital Inclusion Project continues to demonstrate satisfactory progress toward achieving its development objectives. As of March 3, 2026, the project has reached a disbursement rate of 64.19 percent, underpinned by strong advances in the rollout of digital connectivity infrastructure, the operationalization of digital service centers, and the expansion of mobile financial services. While overall implementation performance remains positive, the pace of execution of certain activities has been affected by persistent security constraints in selected regions and delays in the delivery of telecommunications equipment. Key Decisions The Government of Niger is encouraged to: • Adopt a clear government roadmap for the expansion of digital public payments, including student grants, public sector salaries, health service fees, and municipal revenues. • Accelerate operationalization of the electronic signature decree, supported under a previous World Bank DPO, by: • Piloting e-signature use cases within selected ministries and municipalities, • Integrating electronic signature into public financial management and service delivery workflows. • Promote active usage of mobile financial services, beyond account ownership, through targeted awareness campaigns in rural areas, especially among women and underserved populations. • Formally adopt and operationalize studies and policy outputs completed under Component 1 (data protection, digital talent development) to anchor reforms institutionally. 5.
