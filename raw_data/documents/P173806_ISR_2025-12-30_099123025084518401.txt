KEY ISSUES & STATUS 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions The Malawi COVID-19 Emergency Response and Health Systems Preparedness project financed by an International Development Association (IDA) grant in the amount of US$7.0 million, prepared under the Multiphase Programmatic Approach (MPA) Fast Track COVID-19 Facility, was approved by the World Bank's Executive Board on April 14, 2020, and became effective on April 30, 2020. An additional financing of US$30 million IDA grant was approved on June 18, 2021, and declared effective on August 23, 2021 This provided financing to procure vaccines to strengthen delivery systems, and support vaccine rollout. The second additional financing consisting of US$49.9 million from IDA (a credit in the amount of US$25.4 million equivalent and a grant in the amount of US$24.5 million equivalent) and a US$10.0 million grant from the Global Financing Facility for Women, Children and Adolescents was approved on June 21, 2022, and became effective on September 13, 2022. The purpose of the second additional financing was to further scale up affordable and equitable access to COVID-19 vaccines, sustain support to ensure effective vaccine deployment through vaccination system strengthening, and enhance preparedness and response activities. The project closes on December 31, 2025. The project met its development objectives through a comprehensive set of COVID-19 response and health system– strengthening actions that: i) ensured sustained vaccine availability by supporting the full vaccination cycle, procurement and deployment of doses, vaccinator training, strengthened distribution, and targeted risk communication and community engagement; ii) expanded nationwide diagnostic capacity by procuring and installing key laboratory and radiology equipment, including GeneXpert platforms, high-throughput genomic sequencers, digital X-ray units, and COVID-19 test kits, improving timely detection and surveillance; iii) strengthened case management by equipping COVID- 19 emergency treatment units and training healthcare providers across the country, enhancing readiness and the quality of clinical care; and iv) protected essential health services during the pandemic by supplying core medical commodities and supporting outreach clinics, helping maintain routine service delivery and reducing secondary health impacts. 4.2 MPA Program Development Objective Original MPA Program Development Objective (Approved as part of (Seq. 0) package on ) Current MPA Program Development Objective (Approved as part of (Seq. ) package on ) 5.
