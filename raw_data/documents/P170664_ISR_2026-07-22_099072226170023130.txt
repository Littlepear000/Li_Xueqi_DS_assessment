KEY ISSUES & STATUS 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions Implementation of the Adolescent Girls Initiative for Learning and Empowerment (AGILE) Project continues to make progress towards achieving the PDO, with overall performance remaining Moderately Satisfactory. The Project has achieved important results, particularly in expanding girls’ enrollment and advancing digital literacy interventions across participating states. Core systems and implementation frameworks are now largely in place, providing a strong foundation for the next phase of delivery. At the same time, implementation progress has varied across states, reflecting differences in delivery readiness, technical capacity, and sequencing of activities. In Additional Financing states, the transition from preparatory stages to full-scale execution (particularly for civil works) has taken longer than anticipated. Addressing these constraints will be important to ensure timely and complete delivery of project investments within the remaining implementation period. Against this backdrop, the project's Mid-Term Review provided an opportunity to consolidate progress and strengthen the project’s implementation approach to ensure that resources are optimally aligned with delivery readiness and results. Moving forward, the project will emphasize a unified “One AGILE” approach to strengthen coherence between state-level implementation and the PDO, moving away from a fragmented perception of separate state-level operations. It will introduce clearer milestones and performance-based resource allocation. In addition, selected implementation approaches and targets will be refined to better align with operational realities and ensure their continued relevance and achievability. 5.
