KEY ISSUES & STATUS The World Bank Implementation Status & Results Report Sudan Health Assistance and Response to Emergencies (P504629) Oct 11, 2026 Page 2 of 9 Official Use Only 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions The Sudan Health Assistance and Response to Emergencies (SHARE) project continues to restore access to essential health and nutrition services and preserve core public health functions for vulnerable populations across Sudan. As of June 2026, the project had delivered more than 11.5 million health, nutrition, and population services, maintaining service continuity, including in conflict-affected areas. This value exceeds the end-of-project target and includes about 5.9 million services for women and 2.1 million for internally displaced people. Third-party monitoring across all 18 states found that 96 percent of 406 assessed primary health care facilities remained operational, with wider availability of integrated management of essential health and nutrition services than in the previous monitoring round. 3.5 million children have been immunized, and more than 47,000 women have received antenatal care at project-supported facilities to date. Rehabilitation works at supported hospitals reached approximately 90 percent completion, while rehabilitation and solar energy system installations continued at prioritized primary health care facilities. 5.
