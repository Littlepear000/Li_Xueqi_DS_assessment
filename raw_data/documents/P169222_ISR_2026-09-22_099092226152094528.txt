KEY ISSUES & STATUS The World Bank Implementation Status & Results Report Girls Empowerment and Quality Education for All Project (P169222) Sep 22, 2026 Page 2 of 12 Official Use Only 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions Since the last reporting period, the project expanded its life-skills and sexual and reproductive health program to 22 schools, reaching more than 2,600 students. Pilot educational projects are being implemented in seven schools, supporting activities such as science, technology, reading, arts, and violence prevention. School rehabilitation activities have continued across participating schools, improving water, sanitation and hygiene facilities and creating safer and more inclusive learning environments. The project has also continued to strengthen education quality through support to early childhood education, large-scale learning assessments, and teacher development initiatives. National learning assessments for primary and secondary education were successfully implemented in 2026, while activities supporting quality standards for pre-primary education and inclusive education continue to advance. These efforts contribute to strengthening education quality, improving learning conditions, and expanding opportunities for children and youth across São Tomé and Príncipe. 5.
