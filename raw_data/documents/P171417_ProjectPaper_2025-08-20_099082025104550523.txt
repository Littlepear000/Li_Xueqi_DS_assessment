PROJECT STATUS AND RATIONALE FOR RESTRUCTURING 1. This is a Level 2 restructuring of the Punjab Resource Improvement and Digital Effectiveness (PRIDE) program (Project ID: P171417), supported by a US $304 million International Bank for Reconstruction and Development (IBRD) loan. This is the second restructuring of the PRIDE project, undertaken to extend its closing deadline and cancel a portion of the allocated funds. The Economic Affairs Division (EAD) submitted a request July 22, 2025, to extend the project’s closing date by ten months, to the new date of June 30, 2026. In response, the World Bank approved a program extension, enabling the Finance Department to complete the outstanding activities. The Program remains fully aligned with the objectives of the Country Partnership Framework (CPF), particularly Outcome 5, which emphasizes expanding fiscal space and enhancing the efficiency and progressiveness of public spending for development A. Project Status: 2. The PRIDE Program, approved in December 2020 and effective from January 2021, aims to increase Punjab’s revenue, improve fiscal management, and enhance digital public services. The program supports actions under the Public Financial Management Reform Strategy (PFMRS) 2020-25 and Responsive Investment for Social Protection and Economic Stimulus (RISE) Punjab, targeting three result areas: i) budget formulation and fiscal risk management; ii) use of digital technology for public services delivery, and; iii) improved own-source revenue collection. The program’s main activities include implementing pension reforms, enhancing tax collection, introducing a government-wide e- procurement system, deploying integrated financial management systems, and broadening digital service delivery platforms. The Program is progressing satisfactorily towards achieving its development objectives and has reached a disbursement rate of 88%. The achievement of PDO and Program implementation are rated satisfactory in the latest Implementation Status Report (ISR). All legal covenants are being complied with, and there are no overdue audits. The Program has three Results Areas (RAs), and the progress against implementation is as follows: RA 1. Budget formulation and fiscal risk management 3. This area focuses on improving the credibility and transparency of Punjab’s budget process, including the adoption of single budget ceilings (combining recurrent and capital expenditures) and enhanced fiscal risk management tools. Key reforms include the publication of fiscal risk statements and the enactment of public financial management laws. These measures aim to ensure more predictable and efficient resource allocation across government departments. Key achievements include: 4. The Fiscal Risk statement, first presented to the Punjab Provincial Assembly in June 2022 and subsequently published in the budget, outlines a proactive fiscal risk management policy. This policy includes the Pension Reform Action Plan, commodity financing, and Fund Management Rules. Its aim is to identify, analyze, and address short, medium, and long-term fiscal risks, ensuring debt sustainability. Proactive fiscal risk management has reduced uncollateralized commodity debt from PKRs 760 billion (US$2.721 billion) to zero, enhancing fiscal space for critical infrastructure and human capital investments. 5. The Finance Department has established the Public Financial Management Unit (PFMU) to oversee the PRIDE Program and coordinate with various agencies in implementing fiscal reforms. The cabinet approved the PFMU’s roles and functions to enable effective execution of complex, multi-sectoral program activities. A Medium-Term Fiscal Framework (MTFF), consistent with the Federal MTFF, has been made operational. A joint priority committee was notified and is functioning to review recurrent and development expenditures simultaneously, enabling the GoPb to Page 1 Official Use Only The World Bank Punjab Resource Improvement and Digital Effectiveness (P171417) adopt unified budget ceilings. Furthermore, a Public Financial Management Law was enacted in December 2022 along with corresponding budget rules. These measures have strengthened budget credibility, and in fiscal year 2024, Punjab recorded a budget surplus of PKR 600 billion (US$2.142 billion). 6. The Finance Department has taken significant steps to implement a comprehensive pension reform plan aimed at reducing the fiscal burden of pension payments, which are the second-largest component of Punjab's recurrent expenditure after salaries. Punjab's pension reform plan includes setting a minimum retirement age of 55, introducing a discount factor for early retirement, applying lower commutation rates, indexing pension increases, linking pensions to the average pay of the last three years, and certain provisions for family pensions. According to the latest actuarial analysis conducted by the Finance Department, these parametric reforms could potentially generate savings of PKR 2.4 trillion (US$8.571 billion) by 2047. RA 2. Use of technology for Public Service Delivery 7. This result area expands digital platforms for public service delivery, such as e-procurement and e-office systems, to improve accessibility and efficiency. Millions of citizens now access government services online, far surpassing the original Program targets. This digital transformation has resulted in streamlined processes, reduced paperwork, and enhanced transparency in service provision. 8. The GoPb has implemented an e-Procurement system across all government entities including district and tehsil entities in the province. During the fiscal year 2024, contracts totaling more than PKRs 2 trillion were processed through the e-Procurement system. The operational budget expenditure processed via e-Procurement has facilitated access to information and helped address existing barriers to citizen engagement, especially in remote and marginalized areas. By adopting the federal government’s e-Procurement platform, the GoPb has embraced a nationwide approach to system implementation. 9. The Local Government Financial Management Information System has been rolled out in all 229 urban local areas. Accordingly, the finance department has published the disaggregated budget execution data for these 229 urban local areas for the bi-annual period, July – December 2024 on its official website. This also supports enhanced transparency in LG revenues and expenditure. 10. As of June 30, 2024, seven million citizens—19.65% of whom were women—had accessed government services through digital platforms. The final year target for the program was 1.8 million users. The Punjab Information Technology Board has digitized over 150 services, promoting usage through e-Pay Punjab, which offers discounts for the digital payment of taxes. This digitization benefits women by providing online access to services and overcoming mobility challenges. Citizen Facilitation and Service Centers (e-Khidmat Marakaz) offer citizens multiple services at a single point. The GO Punjab mobile app further aggregates various citizen services on one platform. A feedback mechanism, including the Citizen Feedback Monitoring Program (CFMP), is in place to gather user experiences and improve service quality. RA 3. Improved own-source revenue collection. 11. This area supports reforms to broaden the tax base, increase compliance, and modernize tax administration, particularly for sales tax on services. Initiatives include the implementation of new tax data integration plans and revenue mobilization strategies. As a result, Punjab’s own-source revenue collection has increased and is on track to Page 2 Official Use Only The World Bank Punjab Resource Improvement and Digital Effectiveness (P171417) exceed the Program’s target. At the start of the program, Punjab's own source revenue collection was PKR 191 billion. In the last fiscal year, the GoPb successfully collected over PKR 340 billion. This is an overall increase in own source revenue of 77%. 12. The COVID-19 pandemic had a significant impact on Punjab's tax revenues, causing a loss of approximately PKR 103 billion during FY20 and FY21. The most affected taxes were Stamp Duty with estimated revenue losses of PKR 59 billion (US$ 210 million) and the Sales Tax on Services (PSTS) with estimated losses of PKR 38 billion (US$135 million). A report on the impact of COVID-19 on tax revenue was finalized in January 2022 and approved by the Minister for Finance in March 2022, and was followed by steps to improve the collection of Own Source Revenue (OSR) in Punjab. Key activities included coordinating revenue policy, assessing the impact of COVID-19 and climate events, and expanding the service sales tax base. A Revenue Mobilization Strategy was approved with the aim of increasing the OSR to GDP ratio, minimizing taxpayer-tax collector interaction through automation, facilitating taxpayer compliance, and focusing on high-potential taxes like Property Tax, Motor Vehicle Tax, Sales Tax on Services, and Stamp Duty. 13. Operational linkages have been set up between Punjab tax authorities (PRA) and ten third-party datasets, enabling secure data sharing and enhanced compliance monitoring. The integrated citizen and enterprise profiler accessible through taxpayer identifiers (CNIC for individuals/NTN for firms) facilitates this process, ensuring secure and efficient data-sharing across the tax administration ecosystem. The integration of various institutional datasets, hosted both within and outside the Punjab Information Technology Board (PITB) Data Center, has been a critical measure for tax administration. Datasets from departments such as the Excise, Taxation & Narcotics Control Department (ET&NC- MVRS), Punjab Employees Social Security Institution (PESSI), Agriculture Department, and others are hosted within the PITB Data Center, allowing for efficient data retrieval. For externally hosted systems, secure API endpoints and encrypted file transfers have been established. 14. These measures are aimed at achieving high growth in Own-Source Revenue (OSR) and improving tax administration efficiency in Punjab. The introduction of digital services and the integration of various datasets are expected to enhance tax compliance and overall revenue collection. B. Rationale for restructuring 15. This restructuring constitutes a Level 2 modification of the Punjab Resource Improvement and Digital Effectiveness (PRIDE) Program (P171417), which is financed through a US $304 million loan from the International Bank for Reconstruction and Development (IBRD). It represents the first restructuring of the Program and has been initiated to extend the program’s closing date and cancel a portion of the allocated funds. 16. On February 7, 2025, the GoPb requested the cancellation of US $18 million under the Investment Project Financing (IPF) component. The Program’s Steering Committee approved this request along with a proposed one-year extension of the closing date to allow for the completion of remaining activities. However, the formal submission of the extension requests to the World Bank—via the Economic Affairs Division (EAD)—was delayed. Eventually, on July 22, 2025, the Government of Pakistan requested a 10-month extension, moving the closing date from August 31, 2025, to June 30, 2026. In response, the proposed restructuring will accommodate this extension to ensure completion of pending activities. 17. The extension aims to consolidate the results already achieved and provide sufficient time for the efficient completion of remaining activities, thereby ensuring a smooth Program closure. This adjustment will also enable optimal utilization of the remaining funds and safeguard the sustainability of Program outcomes. Page 3 Official Use Only The World Bank Punjab Resource Improvement and Digital Effectiveness (P171417) The restructuring is underpinned by several key considerations: • Economic Adjustments: The depreciation of the Pakistani Rupee has significantly affected financial projections, requiring a reassessment of budget allocations to preserve fiscal balance and program effectiveness. • Redundancy and Optimization: Certain activities expected to be financed under the Program have become redundant due to overlapping provincial initiatives and the rollout of federal systems. For example, the GoPb saved substantial sums by adopting the federal government e-procurement system instead of developing a new one. This has created opportunities to reallocate resources to areas with greater impact. • Extended Timelines for High-Impact Activities: Critical initiatives such as Business Process Reengineering and the development of a Disaster Risk Financing Strategy require additional time for effective completion, justifying the extension. II. DESCRIPTION OF PROPOSED CHANGES 18. The proposed changes to the PRIDE Program include: i. Loan Funds Cancellation: Cancellation of US$ 18 million under the IPF component due to redundancy and coverage by Punjab's own programs. ii. Extension of Loan Closing Date: The closing date for the loan is proposed to be extended from August 25, 2025, to June 30, 2026, to allow for the completion of critical activities. iii. Implementation Schedule Adjustments: Changes in the implementation schedule to accommodate the extended timelines required to complete certain activities including the development of a risk financing framework and the completion of business process re-engineering for revenue departments and district treasuries. 19. The cancellation of funds under the IPF component will not affect any Program activities related to the achievement of the Disbursement-Linked Indicators (DLIs). The primary areas impacted by the cancellations are consultancy contracts (37%) and procurement of goods (35%). All necessary consultancy services have already been secured; however, significant savings have been realized due to the depreciation of the rupee and elimination of certain TA activities due to shifting priorities. For example, the GoPb included consultancy services for the replication of the IFMIS system to Urban Local Councils in its Annual Development Plan and adopted the federal government's e-procurement system instead of developing their own. A similar pattern has emerged with the procurement of goods, resulting in further cost savings.
