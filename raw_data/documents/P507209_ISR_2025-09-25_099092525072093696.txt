KEY ISSUES & STATUS 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions Sep 25, 2025 Page 1 of 8 The World Bank Implementation Status & Results Report Tunisia Health System Strengthening Project (P507209) The Tunisia Health System Strengthening Project aims to improve access to resilient, quality, and responsive health services. Aligned with national health reform priorities, this Project includes three interlinked components aiming to (i) strengthen pandemic prevention, preparedness, and response, including through the Pandemic Fund grant; (ii) support reorganization of primary health care services to improve provision of cost-effective preventive health services; and (iii) strengthen management of emergency medical services. Activities supported by the Project include infrastructure rehabilitation and modernization, procurement of equipment, and capacity building, with a focus on climate readiness of the health system. Key expected outcomes by 2030 include: 2 million people receiving outpatient services of which 1 million women (contributing to the World Bank's ambitious goal of providing quality, affordable health services to 1.5 billion people by 2030); reaching 100 percent of the population that can be reached by emergency services within 30 minutes from 54 percent in 2025; and 6 million people benefiting from climate-resilient planning, preparation, surveillance, and/or response of which 3 million women. 5.
