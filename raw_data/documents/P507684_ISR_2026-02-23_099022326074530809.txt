All effectiveness conditions have been fully met, including the execution of the Subsidiary Agreement, finalization of the Project Operations Manual, and updates to the ISSF’s corporate governance framework. Overall implementation progress remains satisfactory, with no major issues affecting achievement of the Project Development Objective at this stage. 5.
