KEY ISSUES & STATUS Jun 24, 2026 Official Use Only Page 1 of 10 The World Bank Implementation Status & Results Report Paraiba Improving Water Resources Management and Services Provision (P165683) 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions This ISR is based on the Implementation Support Mission of May 18-22, 2026. Project status as of May 2026. The Project underwent a Level 2 Restructuring which was approved by the Country Director on December 16, 2025. The pace of implementation has gained momentum with all major contracts of works underway and disbursements that have increased substantially. By June 2026, the Bank has disbursed US$88.3 million — 70% of the loan's proceeds. Satisfactory progress is reported on activities financed with counterpart funds including the Curimataú pipeline and CAGEPA's commercial non-revenue water (NRW) control program. The pumping station 1 of the RMJP sewerage system is operational. Phase 1 of the Curimataú pipeline (out of three phases) is also operational. To date US$99.7 million have been disbursed, exceeding the initial commitment of US$80.2 million as adjustments to works contracts were made along this period. The Implementation Progress (IP) rating remains at MS to reflect the progress made in major works, the management of the FIDIC Yellow Book contract (Cariri pipeline) requires continued Bank support. Progress towards PDO. Both, the Bank team and the client agree that the PDO is achievable within the Project timeline. The PDO indicators "Annual operational water management plan issued for the Paraiba River" and "Hydromet stations operational and feeding into the water information system" have been achieved. The indicator “Reduction of NRW in the RMJP” has shown notable progress through counterpart financing. Two other indicators have also begun to be measured, as sections of the Curimataú pipeline are already operational: “Number of days with piped water in beneficiary cities in the Agreste and Borborema regions” and “Number of beneficiaries with access to reliable water services,” which already reports 29,780 direct users in the cities of Boa Vista, Boqueirão, and Soledade. 5.
