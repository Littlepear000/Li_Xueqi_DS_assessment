KEY ISSUES & STATUS 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions The Project has been making progress in implementation and is on track to achieve the PDO. The economic shock-responsive cash transfers has reached 9.2 million poor and vulnerable households selected from the Social Mar 03, 2026 Page 1 of 10 The World Bank Implementation Status & Results Report National Social Safety Net Program-Scale Up (P176935) Registry. Over 6.5 million households have received all three tranches of support following their biometric verification through the National Identification Number (NIN) or the Bank Verification Number. The progress on the rollout of the cash transfer support has been supported by implementation of the NIN Drive, including the harvesting and verification of existing NINs. In collaboration with the National Identity Management Commission, over 9 million households have been visited, and more than 11 million NINs from 7.1 million households have been verified and integrated into the Social Registry. Preparations are underway to commence NIN enrollment for remaining households. 5.
