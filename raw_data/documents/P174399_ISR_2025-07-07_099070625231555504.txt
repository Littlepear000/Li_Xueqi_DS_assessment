KEY ISSUES & STATUS 3.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions The Program is on track to achieving its development objective, both in the short term until June 2026 when its original financing and first additional financing (AF) will end; as well as in the longer term until June 2029, as a second AF has was signed and made effective in FY 2025. During the latter part of FY 2025, the Program has supported the Skills Development Program (SDP) in ensuring that more trainees have found employment within six months of graduation. The Ministry of Education & Skills Development (MoESD) has also issued an operational strategy for increasing climate-responsiveness of skills training which is expected Jul 07, 2025 Page 1 of 21 The World Bank Implementation Status & Results Report Human Capital Recovery & Resilience Program (P174399) to support climate change adaptation and help create green job opportunities, stimulate innovation, and contribute to a low carbon economy. These actions attest to the continued relevance of the SDP and technical and vocational education and training (TVET) for the labor market, and the uptake of these programs will continue to be supported by the Program. In enhancing coordination across key agencies on labor market issues, an Employment Responsibility System (ERS) Concept Note has been approved by the Ministry of Industry, Commerce & Employment (MoICE) (DLI 8). The ERS is part of the national strategy to tackle youth unemployment. These achievements have been supported under disbursement- linked indicators (DLIs) 6, 8 and 9. 4.
