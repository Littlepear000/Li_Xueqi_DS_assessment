KEY ISSUES & STATUS Mar 19, 2025 Page 1 of 9 The World Bank Implementation Status & Results Report Solomon Islands Sustainable Mining Development Technical Assistance Project (P173018) 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions The Solomon Islands Sustainable Mining Development Technical Assistance Project (SMDTAP) is making significant progress towards achieving its development objectives. The project has focused on improving the regulatory framework, enhancing transparency, and increasing stakeholder engagement in the mining sector. Key achievements include the drafting of the Mineral Resources Bill , the establishment of a more transparent regulatory environment, and the implementation of the Mineral Cadaster Administration System (MCAS). These accomplishments reflect early prioritization of activities, establishing a strong foundation for improved institutional governance in the mining sector. The government has announced its commitment to re-establish the implementation of the Extractive Industries Transparency Initiative (EITI) to enhance transparency in the mining sector. The project is providing technical assistance to the government in re-establishing EITI implementation, thereby promoting transparency and accountability within the sector. 5.
