KEY ISSUES & STATUS 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions The SERSP approved in June 2019 with an IDA grant of US$37 million, supports the Government of Tajikistan’s efforts to build resilience in communities in two regions (Khatlon and GBAO) that share borders with Afghanistan. It targets five districts in the Khatlon region and four districts in the GBAO region, including 55 sub-districts, relatively large populations, a combination of rural and urban areas, and limited coverage by other donor interventions. The parent project has achieved and in some cases exceeded expected outputs and results indicators. Based on the Government's request, additional financing project US$25mln (IDA grant) was approved by the WB management on May 19, 2025 to extend SERSP activities to jamoats and villages in new districts near the Rogun HPP and in two other districts. The Additional Financing became effective on October 10, 2025 and NSIFT is currently in process of procuring the training facilitation partners for component 1 and implementation partner for component 2, initiating mobilization at the district and jamoat levels as well as for 8 resettlement villages. 5.
