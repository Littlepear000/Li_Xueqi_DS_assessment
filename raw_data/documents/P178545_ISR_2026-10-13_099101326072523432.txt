KEY ISSUES & STATUS 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions The project continues to make strong progress toward achieving its development objectives. Under component 1, 271 climate-resilient infrastructure subprojects have been completed and are providing benefits to rural communities. Under component 2, 1,656 Self-Help Groups have been established, reaching over 23,000 poor and ultra-poor households and exceeding key targets for outreach, savings mobilization, and seed grant disbursement. Under component 3, 602 Community Nutrition Groups are active across project areas, with several nutrition-related indicators already exceeding Year 3 targets. Field visits confirmed that completed infrastructure and community institutions established under Batch 1 remain functional and continue to deliver benefits. Going forward, the focus will be on maintaining the quality, sustainability, and long-term impact of Batch 2 investments. Priorities include strengthening the climate resilience of infrastructure investments, enhancing market and financial linkages for livelihoods activities, and supporting the dialogue and preparatory work for the Government's National Agenda on Rural Development and Poverty Reduction. 5.
