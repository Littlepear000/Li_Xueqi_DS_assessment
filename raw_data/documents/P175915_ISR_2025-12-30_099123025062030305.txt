KEY ISSUES & STATUS Dec 30, 2025 Page 1 of 12 The World Bank Implementation Status & Results Report Senegal: Natural Resources Management Project (P175915) 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions A mid-term review of the Senegal Natural Resources Management Project (SENRM) was carried out in October 2025, which confirmed the progress made towards achieving the Project Development Objective (ODP), in particular, relating to the improvement of the management of fisheries and forestry resources and access to related economic opportunities. These achievement include: (i) in the fisheries sector, an increase in the number of jobs created in the aquaculture sector (from 1,854 jobs in 2022, to 2,612 in 2024), as well as an increase in national aquaculture production (from 1,374 to 2,374 tons/year between 2022 and 2024, due to the strengthening of the investment framework) and the establishment of conditions allowing the landing and processing of fishery products according to best practices; and (ii) in the forestry sector, the development of sustainable income-generating activities in targeted forest areas and the strengthening of the management efficiency of targeted forest areas. However, even though it has increased since the last mission in May 2025, the disbursement rate is still low at 18%, in particular due to a strict framework for the disbursement of projects in Senegal in line with national debt control objectives. The mission identified critical adjustments to be made to the Project to align its activities, its results framework and the modalities of its management so that it best responds to the new strategic context and the priorities for the implementation of the Senegal Vision 20250. 5.
