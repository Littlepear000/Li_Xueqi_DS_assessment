KEY ISSUES & STATUS 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions All countries (Comoros, Kenya, Malawi and Somalia plus the Africa Union Commission - AUC) are effective and under implementation. Comoros is effective since September 2023, Kenya since January 2024, Malawi since November 2023, Somalia since June 2023 and the AUC since November 2023.
