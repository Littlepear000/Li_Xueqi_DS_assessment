No I. PROJECT STATUS AND RATIONALE FOR RESTRUCTURING 1. The Fourth Phase of the Central Asia Regional Links Program (CARS-4, “the Project”) was approved on July 30, 2020 in the amount of USD 132 million (131 million USD of IDA funds and 1 million USD of PACT TF funds) and became effective on November 12, 2021. The Project Development Objectives (PDO) are to enhance the efficiency of cross-border trade for participants of the regional economy and to improve the resilience and safety of regional connectivity infrastructure in Sughd region and Gorno-Badakhshan Autonomous region. 2. The CARs-4 Project is structured around five components. The Component 1 involves activities to improve regional road connections in Sughd and GBAO regions, including (i) rehabilitation of about 50 km of roads in Sughd; (ii) construction of tunnels/galleries and bridges in GBAO; (iii) construction supervision services; and (iv) preparation of feasibility studies for improvement of regional road connectivity in Sughd, Khatlon and GBAO regions. The latter serves as a basis for preparation of the CARs-5 pipeline lending operation and stands in the core of the proposed restructuring. 3. Other operational components of the project aim at improving road asset preservation and road safety (Component 2), facilitating cross-border movement of goods through strengthening of institutional capacity of the Customs Service (Component 3), and supporting project implementation, coordination and management (Component 4).. The remaining component on the contingent emergency response (Component 5) is a zero-cost component designed to provide swift response in the event of an eligible crisis or emergency. 4. The Project’s progress towards achievement of the PDO and implementation progress are currently rated Moderately Satisfactory, mostly due to the previous slight delays with the fulfillment of one of the project effectiveness conditions. The declaration of the Project effectiveness in November 2021 enabled commencement and active implementation of the project activities since then. As of mid-May 2022, the project disbursements stand at about US$3 million (2.3% of the available funds). The PIGs in both implementing agencies (the Ministry of Transport and Customs Service) have been established and are fully operational. 5. The procurement of the key project contracts is advancing well. The prequalification for road construction works packages for rehabilitation of regional roads in Sughd and construction of tunnels/galleries and bridges in GBAO has been completed and bidding documents are being finalized. Procurement of the road construction supervision services has been completed. The terms of reference for the road asset management sub-component have been drafted. Custom The World Bank Fourth Phase of the Central Asia Regional Links Program (P166820) Service (CS) has made progress in the formation of the Steering Committee in an appropriate and acceptable manner to the WB and has finalized procurement on the provision of Technical Assistance for Migration to ASYCUDA World and the Implementation of Customs Reform. Lastly, implementation of preparatory studies for the new road sector investments to improve regional connectivity in Sughd and GBAO regions, to be partially financed by PACT Trust Fund, has been completed, but requires more funds than the originally estimated. 6. The Financial Management, Procurement, Project Management, and Monitoring and Evaluation aspects of the Project are rated Satisfactory. Based on the latest financial management review by the Bank, the Borrower is in compliance with the financial management requirements and the Project does not have overdue financial audit reports. 7. The Recipient-executed Program for Asia Connectivity and Trade TF (PACT TF0B3028) in the amount of USD $1 million has been allocated to provide financing for some of the feasibility studies for rehabilitation of roads in the Sughd and GBAO regions. The results of this activity are supposed to inform future operations of the Bank’s transport program in Tajikistan. The PACT TF became effective on November 13, 2021 and the recently extended closing date of this TF is September 30, 2022. The award of the Contract for the execution of the above-mentioned preparatory studies is completed. The cost of the required preparatory studies for the priority roads in Sughd region (57 km) and GBAO (200 km) exceed the available funds, as detailed below. 8. The proposed Project restructuring was prepared in response to the letter from the Government of Tajikistan dated March 18, 2022 requesting (i) allocation of additional PACT TF funds in the amount of $1million and (ii) adjusting the CARs-4 Project to allow the use of the IDA funds, both to enable implementation of preparatory studies for reconstruction of priority road sections in GBAO and Sughd regions of Tajikistan so that they could properly inform preparation of the CARs-5 project. 9. The Bank team seeks a proposed Project restructuring to accommodate the request from the client to allocate USD $ 1 million additional financing under the PACT Recipient Executed TF (TF0B3028) and allow utilization of the project’s IDA funds for preparation of the feasibility, detailed design, environmental, and social studies related to reconstruction of the regional roads in Sughd Oblast and GBAO. The restructuring will contribute to achievement of the project's development objective ‘to improve the resilience and safety of regional connectivity infrastructure in Sughd region and Gorno-Badakhshan Autonomous region” and will ensure timely development of the road investment preparation studies required for successful delivery of the pipeline CARs-5 project scheduled for approval in FY23. This is the first restructuring of the project. The restructuring does not require any change in the original PDO. The key Project stakeholders are fully committed to the objectives of the restructuring. The PACT TF has confirmed the commitment to allocation of the requested 1mln USD additional financing. The team has agreed with PIG the revised project implementation plan. The implementing agency has made very good progress in procurement of the consultancy services to be financed with the additional funding sought by the restructuring and the associated contract has been awarded. Despite the significant operational constraints imposed by COVID-19, the progress recently made in project implementation, especially under the Component 1, demonstrates strong commitment of the client and continued high strategic relevance of the Project. The World Bank Fourth Phase of the Central Asia Regional Links Program (P166820) II. DESCRIPTION OF PROPOSED CHANGES 10. The proposed restructuring envisages allocation of additional PACT TF funds for implementation of the project activities, introduction of changes in the expenditure categories reflected in the Financing Agreement, and slight adjustment of the Project scope. These changes are specified below in more detail. 11. As mentioned above, the Program for Asia Connectivity and Trade Trust Fund (PACT TF) granted the Recipient with US$ 1 million to develop feasibility studies for improvement of the road connectivity in Sughd, Khatlon and GBAO regions within the Component 1(iv). To bridge the financing gap for implementation of studies the Recipient has requested allocation of additional US$1 million PACT funds. Based on this request the Bank team applied to PACT TF for allocation of these additional funds and on December 1, 2021 was informed about approval of this request by PACT donors. The only change in the project cost table includes increase of the Trust Fund financing for the project from US$1 million to US$2 million and the associated increase of the total project cost from 132 million USD to 133 million USD. The design of the expenditure categories in the Financing Agreement of the Project currently precludes the use of IDA funds for financing of preparatory studies for reconstruction of roads in Sughd and GBAO regions and foresees financing of this activity only from PACT TF. The Government of Tajikistan in the letter to the Bank has requested to remove this restriction and allow to use IDA funds for financing the above activity so that the remaining financial gap (approximately 0.29 mln USD) for the activity could be bridged with IDA funds. To ensure this flexibility, the restructuring involves opening a new expenditure Category (4) of about 0.221 mln SDR related to Part I. iv (b) of the Project through the Amendment to the Financing Agreement. In addition, given the closing date of the PACT TF in September 2022, the restructuring is designed to allow disbursement of IDA funds for the subject activity only after depletion of TF funds, so that TF funds could be utilized first. To achieve this goal, a new provision is included in the Financing Agreement that no withdrawal shall be made for payments under the new expenditure Category (4) unless and until the grant resources under the Trust Fund Grant Agreement are fully disbursed . The Project Sub-component 1(iv) scope is proposed to be adjusted to make sure that the required range of preparatory studies for timely delivery of the pipeline CARs-5 project, including the key feasibility, detailed design, environmental and social studies, is properly covered by the project. To this end, the current description of the project’s Component 1 (iv) in the Financing and Grant Agreements is proposed to be slightly adjusted to more explicitly mention the detailed design, environmental, and social studies for the project roads and read as follows: “preparation of feasibility, detailed design, environmental and social studies for improvement of regional road connectivity, including, inter alia, through survey of initial road conditions, identification of natural disaster risks and mitigation measures, identification and assessment of measures to maximize wider economic benefits of corridor development, and assessment of technical, financial, social and environmental feasibility for (a) Khatlon region, and (b) Sughd and GBAO regions”. Based on the priorities of the client, the above preparatory studies will be undertaken for reconstruction of the Kulab-Guliston road section in Khatlon, selected sections of Khujand-Kanibadam road section in Sughd, and Rushon-Khorog-Shtam road section in GBAO.
