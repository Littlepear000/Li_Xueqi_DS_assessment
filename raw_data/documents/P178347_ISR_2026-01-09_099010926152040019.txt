KEY ISSUES & STATUS 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions Jan 09, 2026 Page 1 of 8 The World Bank Implementation Status & Results Report Yemen Emergency Electricity Access Project-Phase II (P178347) The implementation of the project is continuing steadily across its various components. Under subcomponent 1.1, contracts have been signed with microfinance institutions, and and the sale of solar systems to households has commenced. As of October 15, 2025, approximately 4.7 million people have benefited from the services provided through the project. Infrastructure improvements include energizing 497 critical service facilities—comprising 317 health centers, 76 schools, and 104 water pumping systems—toward a target of 700 facilities. The cumulative installed solar capacity has reached 10 MWp, leading to annual energy savings of 21,062 MWh and a reduction of 13,616 tons of CO₂ emissions per year. 5.
