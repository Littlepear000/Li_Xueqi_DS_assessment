KEY ISSUES & STATUS May 25, 2026 Official Use Only Page 1 of 9 The World Bank Implementation Status & Results Report Yemen Emergency Electricity Access Project-Phase II (P178347) 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions The implementation of the project is continuing across its various components, despite shipment delays and the difficult security and economic context. Under Sub-component 1.1, sales of subsidized solar home systems through microfinance institutions reached 137,182 units by April 2026. As of April 15, 2026, approximately 4.9 million people had benefited from new or improved electricity services under the project. Infrastructure improvements include energizing 552 critical service facilities, comprising 321 health facilities, 82 schools, 144 pumping systems, and 5 vaccine facilities. The revised target is to energize a total of 755 facilities by the end of the project. 5.
