KEY ISSUES & STATUS 3.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions The program is nearing completion and has surpassed most of its objectives. The Government of Punjab has successfully met the majority of its result framework indicators, with particular success in achieving the Project Development Objective (PDO) indicators. The disbursement rate currently stands at an impressive 98.78%. Notably, major reforms have been implemented, including the expansion of the e-procurement system across government departments, improved budget credibility, and increased access to digital services—over 7 million beneficiaries have accessed services online, far exceeding the original target.Additionally, a firm has been engaged to assist the Government of Punjab in tracking climate-related expenditures, and the inception report has already been submitted. The Disbursement Linked Result (DLR) for climate expenditure tracking is expected to be finalized by the end of March 2026. Meanwhile, the program impact assessment is in its final stages, and a first draft of the assessment report has been prepared. This impact assessment will supply Nov 26, 2025 Page 1 of 16 The World Bank Implementation Status & Results Report Punjab Resource Improvement and Digital Effectiveness (P171417) essential data for the forthcoming Implementation Completion Report, ensuring that comprehensive insights and findings are available to inform future decision-making and program evaluations. 4.
