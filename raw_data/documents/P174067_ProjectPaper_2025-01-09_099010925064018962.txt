PROJECT STATUS AND RATIONALE FOR RESTRUCTURING 1. The Public Service Capability Enhancement Project was approved by the World Bank’s Board of Executive Directors on April 27, 2022, and became effective on August 25, 2022. The Project Development Objective (PDO) is ‘To enhance functional, domain and behavioral competencies of civil servants in select departments and agencies of the Borrower.’ The progress against the PDO is being assessed by four PDO indicators: (1) civil servants in target departments and agencies who complete training on domain and functional competencies on the Integrated Government Online Training (iGOT) Karmayogi platform ; (2) civil servants in target departments and agencies who complete training on behavioral competencies on the iGOT Karmayogi platform; (3) civil servants onboarded on to iGOT Karmayogi platform with testing competency score available; and (4) civil servants expressing satisfaction with training undertaken on the iGOT Karmayogi platform. 2. The Project supports Government of India’s flagship civil service reform program called Mission Karmayogi. This innovative program envisages a comprehensive reform of the framework and incentives for capacity building both at the institutional and individual levels for efficient public service delivery. Under this program, a Capacity Building Commission (CBC) has been created and tasked with harmonizing training standards and creating shared faculty and resources and has a supervisory role over all central training Institutes. Government of India’s Department of Personnel and Training (DoPT), together with the CBC and a Special Purpose Vehicle - Karmayogi Bharat (KB), implement this learning transformation and shift to competency-driven capacity building for Government of India’s civil servants and are also the project implementing agencies. 3. The Project is financed by an International Bank for Reconstruction and Development (IBRD) Loan of US$47 million and comprises three components: • Component 1: Development and Implementation of Competency Frameworks (US$25 million) • Component 2: Development of an Integrated Digital Learning Platform including Infrastructure and Content Creation (US$12 million) • Component 3: Program Monitoring, Evaluation and Management (US$10 million). 4. Component 1 supports the development and implementation of competency frameworks at the organizational levels that enable the alignment of officials’ knowledge, skills, and attitudes with the government’s priorities, resulting in performance improvement and operational efficiencies. Competencies are broadly divided into those that are core skills which all civil servants would need to possess according to their role, behavioural skills such as leadership, management, and communication as well as to professional or specialized skills, referred to as ‘domain’ and ‘functional’ skills. At an operational level, the competency frameworks drive the design of courses and learning paths. Tailored content is developed and curated through identification of competency gaps and cross-cutting competencies that require high-impact courses. 5. Component 2 supports the scaling up of the technology platform - iGOT Karmayogi, as the backbone of the entire learning management system. The iGOT Karmayogi platform allows many other layers and solutions to be built on top, including personnel management functions, paving the way for larger Human Resource transformation. This component supports technological upgradation of iGOT Karmayogi, the development of appropriate infrastructure as needed, and the creation and curation of competency building products (CBPs) on the platform which include curated modules and courses for building targeted competencies. The component also supports targeted skill building in chosen functionalities/domains and promote sectoral expertise of civil servants in select areas such as gender budgeting and planning, gender-informed management and information systems, and sector-specific policies affecting female labour force participation. Component 3 provides consultancy support for design of the assessments to be onboarded on to the platform and for the broader Mission Karmayogi program, as needed. Interventions will focus on tracking achievements towards organizational goals and improvements in organizational performance. These assessments include the identification of suitable performance indicators and the development of monitoring dashboards with real-time data, as well as organizational scorecards for participating institutions. The scorecards in turn, will provide data to assess organizational performance over time. The findings from these reviews will be periodically reported through the ‘State of the Civil Services Reports’/Annual Report of the Capacity Building Commission and presented in Global Annual Human Resource Summits. This component also supports: (a) the Project Management Unit for the day-to-day management of Project implementation, (b) other institutions involved in the implementation of Mission Karmayogi, including the CBC with specialist staff as needed, (c) overseeing compliance with fiduciary obligations, and (d) monitoring and evaluation mechanisms for Mission Karmayogi. 7. As on November 26, 2024, the project is rated Moderately Satisfactory on progress towards achievement of the PDO. However, the implementation progress rating for the project has been downgraded to Moderately Unsatisfactory because of implementation delays and slow disbursement. There is a need to accelerate project implementation in all its components and improve disbursements (at 6 percent as on November 26, 2024), so that the project investments justifiably contribute to the PDO achievement. 8. While disbursement under the project has been relatively slow, there has been significant progress in towards achievement of targeted project results. The CBC has developed Annual Capacity Building Plans (ACBPs) and completed the competency gap assessments on functional and behavioural competencies, for all 93 Ministries/Departments/Agencies (MDAs) covered under this project of which 87 have been approved and are being rolled out. All MDAs have constituted Capacity Building Units (CBUs) through for supporting the roll out of capacity building plans in the respective institutions. CBC has hired 20 program coordinators who will be attached to these CBUs and will serve as the link between the Commission and the MDAs and plans to scale-up the number to 100. The number of users on the iGOT platform has surpassed 4.5 million with 1427 online courses available on the platform with 440 MDAs, including from state governments onboarded to the platform. The first National Training Conclave 2023 was organized and conducted by CBC in 2023 followed by a National Learning Week in October 2024. 9. The objective of the proposed restructuring is to: (i) cancel and reduce the project Loan by US$4 million due to exchange rate gains. Upon approval of the proposed restructuring, the IBRD Loan will be reduced from the original US$ 47 million to US$ 43 million. This request for the cancellation has been received by the IBRD vide Government of India’s letter (F. No. 19/01/ 2020-FB-II, dated November 11, 2024). Further reviews will be undertaken during the mid-term appraisal to identify additional potential savings for cancellation, after assessing the status of project implementation. II. DESCRIPTION OF PROPOSED CHANGES 10. The project has identified exchange rate gains of US$4 million that are being surrendered. The total loan cancellation proposed under this restructuring is US$4 million (from Component 1 of the project). The proposed cancellation does not impact project implementation, as all contracts and commitments are in Indian Rupees. The Government approved project cost of INR 510.86 crore with a IBRD loan component of INR 350 crore remains unaffected by the cancellation.
