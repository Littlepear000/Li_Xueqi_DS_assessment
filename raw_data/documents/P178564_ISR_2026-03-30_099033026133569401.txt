KEY ISSUES & STATUS 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions Mar 30, 2026 Page 1 of 12 The World Bank Implementation Status & Results Report Strengthening the National Statistical System in Ecuador Project (P178564) Project implementation continues to advance satisfactorily, with strong technical progress across components and high levels of disbursement. As of the mission, approximately 84 percent of the loan amount had been disbursed, reflecting continued execution of planned activities. Under Component 1, the Population and Housing Census has reached its final implementation stage, with core products completed and remaining activities focused on operational closure and integration into regular institutional production. Population census data is widely use by private and public sectors. Under Component 2, major household surveys and price statistics operations are progressing. ENIGHUR has completed fieldwork by November 2025 and entered the final validation and analysis phase, providing the basis for updating poverty lines, consumption baskets, and Consumer Price Index (CPI) weights. ENCIET is in the process of operational closure, with advances in methodological transition, incorporation of new labor modules based on ICLS resolutions 20, 21, and preparation of series linking. The change of the base year of the Producer Price Index (INPP) has been successfully implemented and institutionalized, with continuous monthly publication underway, while the CPI rebasing is advancing according to plan. Agricultural Survey (ESPAC) modules and the newly incorporated National Survey on Family Relationships and GenderBased Violence (ENVIGMU) survey are progressing as scheduled, with fieldwork for ENVIGMU ongoing and strong safeguards and ethical protocols in place. Under Component 3, the project continues to strengthen the use of administrative records through the development of base registers (population, firms, households and activities) and visualizers, positioning Ecuador as a regional reference in this area. All targets of this component have been achieved; extra targets were planned for 2026, as follow: 2 new improvement plans of key administrative records of the Ministries of Education and Labor; and 4 administrative records improved in same ministers related to school enrollment and public employment. Fiduciary, procurement, and environmental and social management arrangements remain in compliance with Bank requirements. Key decisions agreed during the mission focused on maintaining momentum in analytical deliverables, strengthening documentation and sustainability of core statistical operations, and ensuring continuity of project management and fiduciary capacity through the final stages of implementation. 5.
