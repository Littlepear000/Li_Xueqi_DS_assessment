KEY ISSUES & STATUS 3.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions The RAMP project continues to make steady progress toward strengthening MSME competitiveness and resilience across India. The program implementation remains broadly on track. The cumulative disbursements is expected to reach US$352.8 million (80% of the revised program size) upon submission of the June 2026 disbursement request Some activities, including rollout of the Udyami Bharat (INMDP) Portal, MSME reports (Annual, Policy, and Impact Assessment), scaling up of ZED/SPICE interventions, operationalization of green guarantees under GIFT, and implementation of the ODR platform, are being closely monitored and supported. The Government has also proposed extending the program closing date to March 2027 to facilitate completion of remaining activities /DLIs and expenditure associated with it.
