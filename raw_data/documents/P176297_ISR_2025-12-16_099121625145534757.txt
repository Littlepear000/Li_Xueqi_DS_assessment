KEY ISSUES & STATUS Dec 16, 2025 Page 1 of 10 The World Bank Implementation Status & Results Report STRENGTHENING PERU'S NATIONAL SCIENCE, TECHNOLOGY AND INNOVATION SYSTEM (P176297) 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions Key progress includes: Ph.D. Program Grants: Grants have been awarded, and eight alliances are being developed, with seven focusing on climate research. Ph.D. Scholarships: 304 students have been selected, including 266 Ph.D. students with project-funded scholarships in climate-related programs. Among them, 138 are female, making up 45% of the group. Lab Strengthening Grants: 36 research labs have been equipped and strengthened. Research Projects: 271 of 290 research projects have been initiated, which are linked to approved Ph.D. scholarships. Lab Accreditation Grants: Nine labs have been chosen and are in the implementation phase. Tests chains certified by labs: The laboratories have provided over 5,600 services, representing nearly two-thirds of the overall target of 9,000. IVAIs’ Industry-Academia dialogues facilitated: A total of 24 public meetings and 25 working group sessions were conducted with firms, universities, and support institutions across selected value chains (avocado, mango, blueberry, grapes, ethanol, and cotton). Academic Entrepreneurship Grants: The launch of Phase 2 for the " Academic Entrepreneurship " contest is pending the signature of the contract addendum. Industry-Academia Contract Research Grants: In the first round, nine grants were awarded toward the total target of 90. A second round was subsequently launched, with current projections indicating nearly 60 additional grants will be awarded. Consequently, a third round may be required to meet the overall target, with an 18-month execution period. Technology Transfer Capacity Building: The selection of the 5 TTOs is underway, with results anticipated by late November or early December. The hiring process for the firm to strengthen the Technology Transfer Offices (TTOs) is currently under review and is set to start in January 2026. 5.
