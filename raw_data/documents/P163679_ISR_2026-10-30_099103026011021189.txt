KEY ISSUES & STATUS 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions The World Bank Implementation Status & Results Report China Renewable Energy and Battery Storage Promotion Project (P163679) Oct 30, 2026 Page 2 of 7 Official Use Only The China Renewable Energy and Battery Storage Promotion Project (the Project) was approved by the World Bank’s Board of Executive Directors on June 12, 2019. The Loan Agreement and Project Agreement were signed on December 20, 2019, followed by the On-Lending Agreement on March 16, 2020. The Project became effective on March 18, 2020. Implementation progress as of August 2026 is summarized below. The project’s disbursement has reached 100% disbursement rate of IBRD funding. All PDO indicators have exceeded the targets by 2 to 4 times. The project has supported 23 subprojects. The subprojects cover a wide range of operations, including behind the meter, front of the meter, generation load, and distributed renewable plus storage, as well as heat storage. Huaxia Bank has developed a robust pipeline supported by its counterpart financing. Seven pipeline subprojects have been appraised and are either undergoing internal approval or have already been approved by HXB management. By the Project’s closing date in May 2027, the Project is expected to exceed its co-financing target of US$150 million. 5.
