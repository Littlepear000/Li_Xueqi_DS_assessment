 Project Development Objectives

  Project Development Objective (from Project Appraisal Document)
  The project’s development objective is to improve (i) the relevance and excellence of scientific research, and (ii) innovative entrepreneurship and
  access to finance for enterprise growth, as a way of contributing to Serbia’s growth and competitiveness.

  Relevance of research refers to the potential of research results to be commercialized on the market, thus contributing to the economy, while
  excellence refers to international recognition of the quality of the research.

  Has the Project Development Objective been changed since Board Approval of the Project Objective?
  No

 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  Overall Project implementation is advancing well, with no disruption caused by the transition to the newly formed Ministry of Science, Innovation and
  Technological Development (NITRA).
  The Mid-Term Review confirmed the continued relevance of the PDO– to improve (i) relevance and excellence of scientific research, and (ii)
  innovative entrepreneurship and access to finance for enterprise growth, as a way of contributing to Serbia’s growth and competitiveness. PDO
  level indicators, measuring progress toward achieving the PDO, as well as most of the intermediate indicators, are on track or exceeded. The team
  recommends that some of the targets, especially those on track to be exceeded significantly, be revised upwards.
  Implementation of specific activities under Project components is progressing as follows:

  SAIGE Component 1: R&D Sector Reforms
  Sub-component 1.1: Science Fund
  The Bank team reviewed progress of ongoing Science Fund programs (PROMIS, Artificial Intelligence (AI), Special COVID-19 Program, IDEAS,
  IDENTITIES, PRISMA and GREEN). All programs are progressing well.
  Sub-component 1.2: RDI Reforms
  RDI reforms have progressed very well. The original target of four (4) RDIs undergoing transformation has been surpassed with 18 RDIs taking part
  in the process.
  SAIGE Component 2: Enterprise Acceleration
  The Enterprise Acceleration program (Katapult), implemented in collaboration between the Innovation Fund (IF) and the World Bank, is advancing
  well. The Enterprise Acceleration program for the first cohort was completed in June, and all companies are currently in fund-raising stage, with
  several that have already raised investments. The evaluation for the second cohort is currently underway.
  SAIGE Component 3: Project Implementation and Monitoring
  The Project Implementation Unit continues to successfully coordinate all activities, while also directly managing the RDI Reforms component.
