 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The project was approved by the World Bank Board of Directors in July 2019. The Financing Agreement was signed in January 2020, but the
  project is not yet effective. Two of the four effectiveness conditions have been met: signing of the Project Agreement between VCTF and IDA, and
  the signing of the Subsidiary Agreement between the Ministry of Finance and VCTF. The conditions not yet met are the establishment of a PCU in
  accordance to agreed requirements with the World Bank and the drafting of a Project Implementation Manual. These delays have been inpart
  caused by the onset of the COVID19 pandemic, especially the hiring of key personnel for the PCU.

  At this point, the team expects that the conditions of effectiveness will be met by mid-May 2020. The Ministry of Finance, with the help of a hired
  consultant, has drafted a Project Implementation Manual that has gone through 2 iterations of comments from the World Bank project team; a final
  revision is underway, and No Objection to the PIM is expected for the end of April.

  The Project Coordination Unit is not yet in place: Terms of Reference for key staff have been cleared by the World Bank and uploaded onto STEP.
  The recruitment of key personnel (Project Coordinator, additional Procurement specialist and Safeguards specialist) has been impacted by the
  onset of COVID19. Advertisements for the 3 positions had to be put on hold due to the lockdown situation that the government implemented in April
  2020. With recent lifting of lockdown, MoF expects to move quickly to advertisement, interviews (virtual, if needed) and selection of
  candidates. This is expected to happen by mid-May 2020.
