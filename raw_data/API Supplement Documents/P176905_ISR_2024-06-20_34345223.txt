 Project Development Objectives

  Project Development Objective (from Project Appraisal Document)
  The objectives of the project are to (i) support economic recovery and promote climate smart practices among Project beneficiaries in Argentina's
  agri-food system; and (ii) respond effectively in case of an eligible crisis or emergency.

 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The Project was approved by the World Bank Board on December 9, 2021 and declared effective on March 21, 2022. The Project's implementing
  unit is the Dirección de Programas y Proyectos Sectoriales y Especiales (DIPROSE). Since mid-December 2023 to the present, Project
  implementation has been delayed as the new Administration continues to define its strategic priorities vis-a-vis the Project, its respective
  administrative lines of authority and the associated budget allocations.
  Up to December 2023, 22 provinces have joined the Project, 17 have updated and approved Provincial Strategies for the Agri-Food Sector (EPSA).
  Under Component 1 (Public infrastructure for agro-industrial development) 13 rural infrastructure subprojects covering irrigation, rural electrification,
  rural roads and rural connectivity have been prepared to date. Under Component 2 (Agro industrial development and strengthening of rural
