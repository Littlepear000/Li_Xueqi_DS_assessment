 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The Unleashing the Blue Economy of the Caribbean (UBEC) Project was approved by the World Bank Group's Board of Executive Directors on May
  2, 2022. Grenada, Saint Lucia, Saint Vincent and the Grenadines and the Organization of Eastern Caribbean States Commission have all
  successfully established their individual Project Implementation Units and are on track to commence procurement of agreed project activities. In
  addition, this year has afforded the three participating countries with additional financial support to address national food insecurity challenges
  (agriculture and fisheries) brought on by deteriorating global circumstances and events.
  .
