I. PROGRAM STATUS AND RATIONALE FOR RESTRUCTURING

1. Implementation progress. The rating towards achievement of the Project Development Objective (PDO) is rated as
Moderately Satisfactory given the overall impact of COVID-19 pandemic on financial standing of Barqi Tojik (BT) that may
not allow achievement the PDO level results indicators by the original Project closing date of August 30, 2026. Thus, the
results framework, indicators, and Disbursement Linked Result (DLR) targets of the Program will need to be revised
through restructuring once the Government completes the update of the Government Program for Financial Recovery of
BT for 2019-2025. The update of the Government Program is expected to be completed by April 30, 2021.
2. The DLRs due by June 30, October 30 and December 31, 2020 were achieved as planned, except for the DLR
1.1 related to tariff increase in 2020, achievement of which was not realistic due to the current economic and social
conditions of the country. The detailed progress towards achievement of DLRs is presented below.
       DLR 1.1: By December 31, 2020: (a) at least twelve (12) percent increase of average end-user tariff for BT has
        been adopted by the Recipient; (b) approval by the Recipient’s government of the Electricity Tariff Paper
        satisfactory to the Association, with estimated full cost-recovery tariff and tariff adjustment plan to reach full
        cost-recovery by the end of 2025; and (c) establishment of an adequately staffed Tariff Unit at the Anti-Monopoly
        Committee (AMC).
        Status: Under implementation, but achievement by December 31, 2020 was not possible. The slow
        implementation was caused by economic impacts and other constraints caused by COVID-19. The Government,
        with support from the Bank/Energy Sector Management Assistance Program (ESMAP) financed Analytical
        Services and Advisory engagement on Power Tariff Design and Mitigation of Social Impacts of Tariff Increases, is
        also finalizing the conceptual design of potential increasing block/lifeline tariff. This is important aspect of social
        mitigation because adequate protection of socially vulnerable consumers against tariff increases may not be
        feasible through top-up to Targeted Social Assistance program considering the overall macroeconomic and fiscal
        situation in the country.
       DLR 2.1: By June 30, 2020, the Recipient converts into equity ownership of BT the outstanding principal amounts
        and interest payable by BT for ten (10) loans under Group 1 Subsidiary Agreements.
        Status: On track to be achieved. The Bank is expecting to receive the independent verification agent’s (IVA)
        report by January 26, 2020.
       DLR 2.2: By June 30, 2020, the terms of any new financing received by BT from the MOF are aligned with the
        terms in the respective legal agreements between the Recipient and the financiers.
        Status: On track to be achieved. The Bank is expecting to receive the IVA report by January 26, 2020.
       DLR 2.3: By December 31, 2020, the Recipient converts into equity ownership of BT the outstanding principal
        amounts and interest payable under additional four (4) Group 1 Subsidiary Agreements.
        Status: On track to be achieved. The Bank is expecting to receive the IVA report by January 26, 2020.
       DLR 2.4: By December 31, 2020, the BT Supervisory Board adopts a formal decision to prohibit BT from receiving
        new financing from the MOF, unless the terms of such financing are aligned with the terms reflected in the legal
        documents between the Recipient and the financiers.
        Status: Achieved.
       DLR 2.5: By December 31, 2020, the terms of any new financing received by BT from the MOF are aligned with
        the terms in the respective legal documents between the Recipient and the financiers.

3. Rationale for restructuring. The restructuring is required to revise the DLR 1.1 and extend the deadline for
achievement of the targets considering that: (i) the main building block of this DLR - increase of electricity tariffs in 2020
– was not feasible due to deterioration of economic situation in the country caused by COVID-19; and (ii) development
of Electricity Tariff Paper and appointment of key staff in the Tariff Unit of Anti-Monopoly Committee (AMC) were delayed
due to COVID-19 impacts. The restructuring was requested by the Ministry of Finance (MOF) in its letter №5/5-19/700,
dated December 11, 2020. The rationale for restructuring of DLR 1.1 are the following:
   (i)       To replace the DLR 1.1 with two new DLRs comprised of components of the original DLR 1.1; and
   (ii)      Specify new deadlines for achievement of new DLRs as detailed in the next section of the restructuring
             paper.

II. DESCRIPTION OF PROPOSED CHANGES

4. The following changes are proposed to address the request from the Recipient.
         Revised DLR 1.1: By September 30, 2021, at least twelve (12) percent increase of average end-user tariff for BT
          has been adopted by the Recipient. This new deadline is consistent with the Recipient’s request.
          Amount of financing allocated: SDR 5,480,000.
         New DLR. 1.7: By June 30, 2021, approval by the Recipient’s government of the Electricity Tariff Paper satisfactory
          to the Association, with estimated full cost-recovery tariff and tariff adjustment plan to reach full cost-recovery
          by the end of 2025; and (b) establishment of an adequately staffed Tariff Unit at the Anti-Monopoly Committee
          (AMC). The Recipient’s request was to specify a deadline of March 31, 2021. However, considering the complexity
          of the ongoing work, the team proposes to specify a deadline of June 30, 2021.
          Amount of financing allocated: SDR 2,500,000.
