 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  Implementation Status

  Overall, satisfactory progress is noted in achieving the Program Development Objectives (PDOs) with three out of six of the annual PDO indicator
  targets achieved and others in the data collection phase and on track, with:

     i.      a yield for peanuts of 1.22 tons/hectare in 2021-2022 and 1.38 tons/hectare (provisional) in 2022/2023, thus exceeding the annual target
             of 1.1 tons/hectare (125 percent) and approaching the final target of 1.5 tons/hectare;

     ii.     good progress in yields of diversification crops with: a yield for maize increasing from 2.84 tons/hectare in 2021/2022 to 3.36 tons/hectare
             in 2022/2023 (provisional), exceeding the annual target of 2.8 tons/hectare; and a yield for millet increasing from 1.13 tons/hectare in
             2021/2022 to 1.34 tons/hectare in 2022/2023;

    iii.     an increase in groundnut exports with 319,786 tons exported in 2021/2022 (139 percent achievement) against a target of 230,000 tons
             and 105,409 tons exported in 2022/2023 against a target of 255,000 tones (41 percentage of achievement), and a final target of 350,000
             tones. In 2022/2023, the target is not reached due to vagaries of rainfall which led to a fall in yields and production combined with the
             effects of the quota imposed on groundnut exporters. As the campaign is just starting, data for 2023/2024 is not yet available.

  Regarding the Disbursement Link Indicators (DLIs), progress is noted with the annual targets of 2022 and/or 2023 achieved for four out of the ten
  DLIs in the Program. Particularly, notable progress has been recorded in the production of diversification crop seeds (DLI2); in the access of
  producers and herders to agricultural insurance (DLI7); in the production of certified forage seeds (DLI5) and in supporting milk collection centers to
  make them operational (DLI 9). With a little more effort, the DLIs relating to groundnut seeds (DLI1) and the digital platform of the MEPA
  information, monitoring and evaluation (M&E) system (DLI10) can be quickly met.

  However, from the point of view of institutional reforms, the restructuring of the Interprofessional Groundnut Committee (CNIA - DLI 6), the
  establishment of an M&E system for agricultural performance (DLI 10) are experiencing delays , especially at the level of MAERSA while it is a
  necessary element to ensure good coordination, synergy of the different interventions in the sector and their contribution to achieving the strategic
  objectives of the sector.
