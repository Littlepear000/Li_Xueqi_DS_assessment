 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The project was approved by the World Bank Board on July 1, 2019, and became effective on December 3, 2019. The implementation of the project
  has encountered significant delays mainly due to project implementation arrangements not yet in place and the Covid-19 crisis. On Component 1,
  significant progress has been achieved with: (i) the successful launch of the Request for Bids (RFB) for the extension of digital coverage in the
  targeted areas (the deployment of the rural digital connectivity sites is expected to start before the end of the calendar year 2022); and (ii) the

  contract for the extension of the connectivity of decentralized agencies of the Ministry of Agriculture is expected to be signed by May 2022. On
  Component 2, for digital financial inclusion, the RFB to provide sub-grants to eligible merchants for the acquisition of digital payment equipment is
  expected in June 2022. Still on Component 2, for the digital solutions for rural development, the digital platform for agricultural e-Advisory services is
  now "live" and the platform for the management of agricultural materials and equipment is being migrated to the national datacenter and is expected
  to go "live" by the end of May 2022. The project closing date remains July 31, 2024.
