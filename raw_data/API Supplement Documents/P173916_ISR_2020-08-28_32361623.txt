 MPA Table Body
 Implementation is proceeding well, albeit with challenges aggravated by the lockdown. Seventy three percent (73%) of countries with MPA projects
 were reported still in lockdown at the end of May. As of June 17, 2020, 70 projects have been approved (including WBG funded under a TF - West
 Bank) with a total commitment of US$4.3 billion. The approved projects benefit a population of 3.25 billion people or 43% of the global
 population. Of the 70 projects approved: (i) 27 are in Africa; (ii) 9 in East Asia; (iii) 11 in Europe and Central Asia; (iv) 8 in Latin America and the
 Caribbean; (v) 7 in Middle East and North Africa; and (vi) 8 in South Asia.

 Fifty four (54) Financing Agreements have become effective or 87% of signed financing agreements (77% of approved projects). On average, it has
 taken only 13 days for projects to move from approval to effectiveness (a Bank record). Forty eight (48) projects are disbursing or 89% with financing
 agreements that are effective. Total disbursements as of June 17, 2020 under these projects amount to US$1,114.7 billion or 26 of overall
 commitment. In addition, US$234 million have been committed under signed contracts with UNICEF and Bank Facilitated Procurement (BFP),
 bringing to 32% actual disbursements and committed amounts. This level of disbursement attest to the rapid implementation of the Bank
 response. Bank capacity is being stretched to meet streamlined delivery demands and provide enhanced implementation to clients.
