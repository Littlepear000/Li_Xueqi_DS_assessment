 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  Overall progress toward achieving the Project Development Objective (PDO) and the overall implementation progress were assessed as
  Moderately Satisfactory. The Government has requested an extension of the project to catch up on the delays caused by restrictions associated
  with the COVID-19 pandemic and to also engage in new activities that will enhance the sustainability of the reforms supported by the project.
  Component 1: PFM IT Systems Roll-Out, has progressed well. Implementation of the IFMIS technical assessment recommendations is on track.
  There was a setback in the implementation of Component 2: Accounting and Financial Reporting, due to a delay in hiring a consulting firm for expert
  advice on the implementation of the International Public Sector Accounting Standards (IPSAS). The firm is now on board and Minecofin is working
  towards a timeline of FY 2024 for the adoption of IPSAS accrual-based standards.

  Under Component 3: Performance-Based Budgeting (PBB) and Medium-Term Budgeting, the National Budget Department has successfully
  undertaken a full rollout of PBB. A monitoring and evaluation framework is being implemented to closely assess the effectiveness of PBB. Lessons
  from PBB implementation have informed the FY 22/23 budget preparation.
  Component 4: Professionalization of PFM Staff - PFM Capacity Development, was the most affected by the COVID-19-related restrictions. While
  targeted results have largely been achieved in terms of the number of public finance staff with a level of professional qualification and the number of
  public finance officials who received training or are in training under the project, the achievement of future results is at risk. The mission team noted
  the downward trajectory in the number of qualifications achieved in recent years. A conducive environment that provides the right incentives to
  pursue professional studies is essential for the sustainability of these reforms and the Government is exploring ways of achieving it.
