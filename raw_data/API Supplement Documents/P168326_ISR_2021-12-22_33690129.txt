 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  Several large procurement packages have been completed including the hiring of the Grant Management Company to administer the grant and
  matching grant program. The company to design the landscape beautification and general improvement for the three selected Khatlon sites has
  developed the design for cultural heritage sites in Khatlon ready for adaptation by the local relevant agency to start the construction. The company
  was hired to hold a diagnostic study for the Yamchun Fortress rehabilitation. Several agribusiness experts have been hired to support rehabilitation
  of food safety labs and market infrastructure, including an agribusiness coordinator who serves as a liaison between the Project Implementation
  Unit and the Ministry of Agriculture.
