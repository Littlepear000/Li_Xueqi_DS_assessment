 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The Financing Agreement was signed on July 3, 2019, and the project was declared effective on January 30, 2020 and formally launched in a
  workshop in Bishkek Mar 3-4, 2020. The Project Implementation Unit (WB-IPIG) under the Ministry of Transport and Communication (MOTC) is fully
  functional with all staff in place as per the Financing Agreement (FA) requirements. Despite the challenges of implementing the project in the post
  COVID-19 pandemic as well as the ongoing governance transformations at all levels the project has consistently advanced preparatory activities in
  collaboration with project working groups, government counterpart agencies, and through appropriate consultations at municipality and community
  level. Next six months will be critical as several of the preparatory activities shall be entering into implementation phase in line with the project
  development objectives.

   Support sustainable tourism development in Issyk-Kul Oblast

    IN00993495

                                       Baseline                    Actual (Previous)             Actual (Current)          End Target

    Value                              0.00                        0.00                          0.00                      10.00

    Date                               30-Mar-2018                 31-Oct-2020                   31-May-2021               29-Nov-2024
                                       Data will be identified during the survey to be conducted by the selected consultant. The survey used to
                                       measure the community satisfaction change with sustainable development will be a part of the consulting
                                       services procured for the development and piloting of TSA and tourism sector statistics. The technical
                                       assistance service is currently in procurement process and will commence with the development of a
                                       survey identified as a top priority. The sampling size is expected to be 1,000 residents randomly selected.

   Comments:                           IPIG along with the representatives from the Tourism Department conducted a preliminary survey of 80
                                       people during April 26 – 27, 2021 in a visit to Issyk-Kul. The results of the preliminary survey show that
                                       90% of respondents believe that Issyk-Kul is an attractive tourist destination, which can be sustainably
                                       developed thanks to its natural resources. Eighty percent of the respondents to this survey believe that the
                                       quality of services, including tourism infrastructure and poor air connectivity, is one of the major
                                       constraints to tourism development.
