  Program Development Objective (from Program Appraisal Document)
  The PDO is to increase the economic inclusion of youth in rural areas and the marketing efficiency and environmental sustainability of agri-food
  value chains in the Program area.

 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  Following approval by the Board on December 15, 2020 and signing of the Loan Agreement on December 22, 2020, the loan became effective on
  February 25, 2021.

  Program Management Unit (PMU) and Steering Committee. The PMU was formally created on November 10, 2021 as well as the joint Steering
  Committee (with the TREEA project financed by the French Agency for Development). The first Steering Committee was organized on February 14,
  2022. Four meetings of the extended PIU (including all the implementing agencies) have taken place since beginning of implementation.

  Implementation progress. Three DLI have already been fully achieved: (i) Food export improved with 100% of export inspection certifications
  issued in digital forms in 2021 representing around 200,000 certifications (DLI#6); (ii) Improving Food safety with 1,000 agri-food enterprises
  authorized by ONSSA (DLI#7); and (iii) The national Strategic E&S study have been completed and published (DLI#5.1).

  Two DLIs have been also partially achieved: (i) Digital ecosystem for Climate-smart agriculture established, with 634 users (8.4% of the final
  target) using the digital advisory support platform "ARDNA" (DLI #8.4). The disbursement related to this partial achievement has been carried out;
  and (ii) 955 business plans prepared by young entrepreneurs in rural areas eligible for a commercial loan, representing 47% of the
  2022 target (DLI#1).

  In addition, activities of Result Area 1: "Increased employment and income generation opportunities for rural youth" have progressed
  satisfactorily, including: (i) the creation of regional rural youth entrepreneurship centers CRJEAA and the establishment of a dedicated staff and
  institutional mechanism for this youth entrepreneurship program (approved and published) and (ii) the regulatory texts to incentivize young
  entrepreneurs to start their rural investments have been prepared and are in the process of being approved (DLI#2).

  For Result Area 2 “Improved efficiency of agri-food marketing systems” there are progress on activities related to the Construction of
  modernized wholesale markets in four major cities with the Strategic E&S Assessment already completed and disclosed (DLI#5.1), on-going
  feasibility studies and creation of dedicated entities for their management (DLI#4).

  Progress of Result Area 3’s activities “Enhanced digitalization of agriculture and adoption of climate-smart practices” are variable, while
  some tools included in DLI#8 (Digital ecosystem for climate-smart agriculture established) are making progresses, the activity included in DLI#9
  (Adoption of climate-smart agriculture practices for the efficient use of water) is negatively affected by the current drought (interruption of Irrigation
  services), which lowers the priority of developing services for more efficient use of water (including advisory services).
