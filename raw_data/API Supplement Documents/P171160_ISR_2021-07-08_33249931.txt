            Somali Integrated Statistics and Economic Planning Capacity Building (P171160)



  The project is implemented by the Somalia National Bureau of Statistics (SNBS). The project became effective on September 29, 2020. The last
  Implementation Status & Results Report (ISR) was conducted in December 2020 flagging a satisfactory progress towards the project development
  objectives given that it was the first ISR. Progress by component since the last ISR is summarized below. The current ISR assesses the progress of
  the project towards its development objectives as satisfactory.
  The main actions to sustain the momentum and mitigate risks were identified and mitigation actions put in place as summarized in the action matrix
  of the Aide Memoire of the Implementation Support Mission conducted in June 2021. The next implementation status review is planned for
  November 2021 and will follow up on those actions. The current integrated workplan for 2021 was reviewed and approved by the Project Steering
  Committee in March 2021.
  Component 1: Strengthening coordination, statistical infrastructure and data development.
       1. SNBS has started the selection process for the institution development consultant to review SNBS organization structure and assist in
            reforms for SNBS. The selection process is underway.
       2. The FGS has established Statistics Sector Working Groups, which are expected to provide forums where technical and other issues
            common to statistics in that sector will be discussed and where specific solutions to problems and issues will be identified.
       3. SNBS has completed a number of steps for SIHBS preparation including started the procurement process for the SIHBS consulting firm,
            drafted the questionnaire and organized a consultation meeting with statistics departments from FMS.
  Component 2: Information and Communication Technology (ICT) Development. The SNBS is conducting a selection for the consultant to assess
  ICT needs at the SNBS.
  Component 3. Data Utilization for M&E and Planning
       1. The NEC has produced a research study “Health and Economic Impact of Covid-19 in Somalia
       2. A virtual policy forum took place on June 20, 2021
       3. The M&E Department has developed TORs and started the selection process for consultants to develop a National Integrated M&E
            Framework.
  Component 4: Project Management. The Project Implementation Unit is fully functional.
