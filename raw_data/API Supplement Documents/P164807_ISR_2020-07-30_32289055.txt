 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  Overall progress towards achieving the project development objectives (PDO) and the overall implementation progress were assessed as
  satisfactory despite the temporary set-back due to the COVID-19 pandemic. However, the implementation progress will be closely assessed in the
  next few months and a decision will be made at mid-term if the Program will need to be restructured, including if an extension of the closing date will
  be required.
  Some of the key achievements of the project thus far include:
  - Completion of a technical quality assurance review of the Integrated Financial Management Information System's (IFMIS) infrastructure.
  - Performance improvements of the IFMIS and the School Database Management System (SDMS)
  - Creation of an additional data centre for the IFMIS and SDMS for improved performance and security
  - Introduction of a new IPSAS -inspired reporting template
