4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  The project was approved by the World Bank Board of Directors on November 14, 2023, and became effective on May 23,
  2024. A steering committee has been established, comprising representatives from the Ministry of Economy and Finance
  (MEF), the Comptroller General's Office (CGR), and the National Customs Administration (ANA). An implementation
  kick-off mission took place in early September 2024, during which the project objectives and key activities were presented
  to the new authorities at MEF.
