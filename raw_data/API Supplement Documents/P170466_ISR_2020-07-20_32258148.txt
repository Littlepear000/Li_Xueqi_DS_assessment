 Grievance Redress Mechanism (GRM)

 Does the project have a GRM in place?
 No

 Explanation
 The project is not yet effective. The Ministry of Water and Environment (MWE) through the designated environmental and social (E&S) focal point has
 initiated E&S readiness work to ensure timely establishment and maintenance of an organizational structure with qualified staff and resources to
 support management of E&S risks. It will establish a functional grievance mechanism for project implementation. For the interim, the regular GRM of the
 MWE is available for project use.

 Is the GRM accessible to project-affected people?
 Yes

 Explanation
 The regular GRM of MWE is available to the general public, including all people in the project area. As per above, the project-specific GRM has not yet
 been established as the project is still awaiting approval by the Parliament of Uganda and effectiveness.

 Is there evidence that the GRM is receiving and processing complaints?
 No

 Explanation
 No grievances have been lodged in relation to this project with the MWE GRM.

1.2 KEY ISSUES & PROJECT STATUS

Key Issues and Actions for Management Attention
 Issues
   This is the first ISR following project Board approval on April 23, 2020. The project is awaiting approval by the Cabinet and Parliament of Republic
   of Uganda before the Government / Ministry of Finance, Planning and Economic Development can sign the Financing Agreement. The project has
   moved through some of the internal Government clearance processes, including approval by the Prime Minister, and is awaiting review by the
   Cabinet. The Government teams at the Ministry of Water and Environment and the Ministry of Tourism, Wildlife and Antiquities have made a
   significant effort at engaging with the parliamentarians regarding this project, to expedite reviews by the Parliament. The teams are doing their
   best, including with support from the CMU, to achieve GoU approval before the next elections, currently scheduled for January-February 2021
   (exact dates to the determined) - this hinges largely on approval by HE President.

   Lengthy Government approval processes are common in Uganda, with projects not being able to achieve effectiveness, on average, for over a
   year. A lot of the high level engagement has been undertaken during project preparation by the counterparts to try and shorten this period but
   COVID and the upcoming elections have both added to the pressures.

   There are four effectiveness conditions, of which the team assesses that two have been fully met. The remaining two conditions relate to
   preparation of the Project Implementation Manual (in process) and assessment of adequacy of the Government's refugee protection framework
   (which will be undertaken once the financing agreement has been signed).
