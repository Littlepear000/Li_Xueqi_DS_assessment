 Project Development Objectives
  Project Development Objective (from Project Appraisal Document)
  The development objective of this project is to support the production of harmonized and quality statistics in the EAC, in support of the regional
  integration in the region, through capacity building of the National Statistical Offices of the East African Community (EAC) Partner States and the
  EAC Secretariat.

  Has the Project Development Objective been changed since Board Approval of the Project Objective?
  No

 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  The project was approved on July 27, 2018, signed on October 10, 2018, and declared effective on December 20, 2018. The first disbursement of
  the project took time to happen due to an outstanding balance of a previous World Bank project. The pace of project 's activities implementation is
  also low due to long time it takes to complete the recruitment process of the PIU and technical staff. Fortunately, this process is now almost
  completed.
