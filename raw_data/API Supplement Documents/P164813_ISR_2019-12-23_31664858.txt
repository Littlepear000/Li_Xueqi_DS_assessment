 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  The second implementation support mission (October 7-11) found that implementation progress continues to be strong with all major procurement
  packages completed and consultancies under way. The Business Assistance Facility is up-and-running, and the first seventeen matching grants
  have been approved. Five Challenge Facility (CF) proposals have been endorsed by the Minister of MOIC, supporting a broad range of Ministries.
  With technical support teams in place, government's focus will now have to shift towards effectively supervising technical consultants and their
  outputs to ensure recommendations align to government priorities, implementing such technical recommendations, and obtaining results. This will
  require Senior MOIC management leadership and supervision as well as involvement by the PMO and MPI to garner political support to work
  across all Ministries and levels of administration (including in provinces and particularly in Vientiane where data for DB indicators is collected).
