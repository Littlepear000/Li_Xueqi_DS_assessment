1. PROJECT DEVELOPMENT OBJECTIVE


 1.1 Development Objective
   Original Development Objective (Approved as part of Approval package on 22-Oct-2020)
   To increase own source revenue, and improve reliability of resource allocation and access to digital services for people
   and firms in the province of Punjab.

 3. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


 3.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
        The Government of Punjab (GoPb) is maintaining a steady pace of accomplishments in the Program
implementation. Single Budget Ceilings were communicated to all departments of Punjab and the progress on roll out of e-
procurement system is highly commendable. In one day and as result of the use of the e-procurement system, 4,000 bids
for communication and works department were uploaded to the system with total bid price of PKRs 68 billion and a saving
of PKRs 13 billion. Local Government Integrated Financial Management Information System (IFMIS) is replicated across
all Urban Local Councils. Punjab’s tax revenue collection is on track for achieving its year-end target (June 2024), while
the expansion in services sales tax base and filing compliance are also expected to achieve annual targets. Total Program
disbursements currently stand at US$240 million or 78.95 percent of the net commitment amount.
