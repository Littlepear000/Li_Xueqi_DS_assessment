 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The World Bank' s Board of Executive Directors approved the project on March 3, 2020 and the project became effective on March 31, 2021. This
  year long delay in signing of the project Financing Agreement, has led to delayed implementation which is only getting underway. The Government
  is making an effort to finalize approval of the PC1. In parallel, the Government has initiated the procurement process for staffing the project team.
  The Implementation Committee, Procurement Committee and Inter Provincial Technical Committee have been notified. The Government and World
  Bank are working closely to finalize key project structures and will continue working with all stakeholders to successfully initiate implementation.
