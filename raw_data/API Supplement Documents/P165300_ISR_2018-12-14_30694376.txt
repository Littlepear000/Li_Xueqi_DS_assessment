 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  The Project was approved and signed in August 2018. Most project implementation efforts at this early stage are focused on advancing key
  procurement processes (consulting for Project Management Office, DFBOMT concession, and supervision contract) and having these contracts
  awarded by end 2019. There is also significant efforts underway by EMB to advance the transfer of utility networks, and land acquisition and
  resettlement processes.
