4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  There has been substantial progress in toward achieving project results. Under Component 1, CBC has developed
  Annual Capacity Building Plans (ACBPs) for all ministries, departments and agencies (MDAs) of the Government of
  India. Under Component 2, as on October 25, 2024, 45 lakhs users and 1427 online courses have been onboarded
  onto the iGOT Karmayogi platform. The World Bank has shared 18 courses from its Open Learning Campus
  repository which are now integrated onto the iGOT platform covering diverse topics such climate change, PPP,
  urban development, sustainable cities, health inequalities, open data, etc.
