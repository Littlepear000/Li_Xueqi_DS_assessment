           Somali Integrated Statistics and Economic Planning Capacity Building (P171160)



  The project is implemented by the Somalia National Bureau of Statistics (SNBS). The project became effective on September 29, 2020. The last
  Implementation Status & Results Report (ISR) was conducted in July 2021. Progress by component since the last ISR is summarized below. The
  current ISR assesses the progress of the project towards its development objectives as satisfactory.
  The next implementation status review is planned for July 2022 and will follow up on those actions. The current integrated workplan for 2022 was
  reviewed and approved by the Project Steering Committee meeting on 28th November 2021.
  Component 1: Strengthening coordination, statistical infrastructure and data development.
        1. SNBS has contracted a firm to undertake the Consultancy Services for Institutional Development for SNBS with the objective of revising
            SNBS organizational structure and assist SNBS in the design and implementation of the Human Resource Development reforms as a
            means of strengthening and developing statistics in the country.
        2. Federal Government of Somalia (FGS) has established Statistics Sector Working Groups, which are expected to provide forums where
            technical and other issues common to statistics in that sector will be discussed and where specific solutions to problems and issues will be
            identified. The first meeting was held in Mogadishu on the 22 September 2021.
        3. The SNBS conducted the second Federal Member States (FMS) consultation meeting for the preparation and implementation of Somalia
            Integrated Household Budget Survey (SIHBS) on 10-13th July, 2021. The main objective was to strengthen the coordination and working
            relationship between SNBS and FMS planning Ministries during preparation and implementation of SIHBS. The SIHBS Technical
            Committee was also established.
        4. The SNBS has contracted an international survey company, IPSOS, to support field implementation of the survey in mid-November 2021.
            A kick-off meeting between Ipsos team and the SNBS took place on November 22nd, 20201. The team will collect data in 17 regions of
            Somalia from a determined sample of 7,140 households. The SIHBS will target all households living in all areas of the country covering all
            sampled enumeration areas in urban, rural and nomadic areas. The data will be representative at regional level. The questionnaire
            development has been finalized and the scripting of the questions for handheld device is underway. The field work is expected to start in
            March 2022 and preliminary data available by end of June 2022.
        5. To provide capacity building in business statistics area, the NBS is recruiting an advisor to help support the entire process of establishing
            a statistical registry and conducting the first Integrated business establishments economic survey. The support should work in parallel on
            improving quality and timeliness of results as well as on capacity building within NBS. The NBS is finalizing the recruitment.
        6. The Training Needs Assessment (TNA) aims at identifying key technical and functional capacity gaps within the SNBS and Statistical
            Units of the selected key Ministries, Departments and Agencies (MDAs) at federal level. The results of this assessment will be used to
            develop an integrated training plan based on the identified capacity gaps, and training needs to enhance staff capacities in the National
            Statistics System (NSS) to enable them to produce quality statistics. This task commenced on 1st June 2021. A revised draft of training
            assessment report and training plan was submitted to the NBS in December 2021.
        7. The first draft of statistics compendium report has been submitted and is reviewed by NBS and the Bank teams.
  Component 2: Information and Communication Technology (ICT) Development.
  The SNBS has recruited a firm to assess ICT needs of the Somali National Statistics System with the aim to identify gaps, recommend solutions
  and strategy to implement them. The consulting firm joined in October 2021 and has been carrying out the following activities: review the current
  ICT; conduct an assessment of ICT adequacy in terms of hardware, software, system, network security, related policies and skill-adequacy of IT
  staff; assess ICT capacity for data collection & processing, data exchange and data dissemination, including underlying networks and IT platforms;
  and review software currently in use. An initial draft of the assessment was submitted in December 2021 and the work is expected to finalize in the
  second quarter of 2022. The report is expected to determine the quality and quantity of ICT equipment and software, and the corresponding
  capacity building and other technical assistance needed to support the production and dissemination of official statistics. It will also provide detailed
  specifications for the data hardware, software, and other facility needed to enhance the SNBS website.
  Component 3. Data Utilization for Monitoring and Evaluation (M&E) and Planning
  Subcomponent 3.1: Strengthening MED capacity
  The Monitoring and Evaluation Directorate (MED) is developing a national integrated monitoring and evaluation (NIMEF) framework. The
  Development of the framework and the reporting mechanism is undertaken through consultative process of the stakeholders involved in monitoring
  implementation of the programs and projects that are being undertaken in the country’s economic and social sectors. Four M&E consultants have
  been hired to undertake the assignment; each consultant is working on one of the following four pillars of NDP-9.
  Stakeholder consultation was conducted on October 3, 2021; the inception report covering the proposed methodology for developing NIMEF and
  detailed workplan was also discussed and agreed on.
  Subcomponent 3.2: Supporting economic policy analysis and planning capacity
  On October 4, 2021, NEC organized an inauguration of “Growth and Economic Transformation Strategy (GETS)” comprising Inclusive Growth,
  Climate Change Adaptation, and Sustainable Blue Economy. The event covers topics under the GETS study:
           “Sustainable Economic Development” highlights the need to develop a broad-based growth model for Somalia that addresses the
            economic infrastructure and Trade constraints, limited social services, inadequate financial resources and attains macroeconomic stability.
           “Climate Change Adaptation, Building Resilience and food insecurity” addresses the impact of climate change in Somalia, with a focus on
            addressing the increasingly fragile and degraded natural environment, and adaptation and resilience building to frequent and extreme
            cycles of droughts.
           “Sustainable Blue Economy in Somalia” focuses on Sustainable development of the Blue Economy; highlighting the potential of Marine
            resources and of Oil and Gas; and delineating the Blue Economy Investment Strategy.

  Component 4: Project Management. The Project Implementation Unit is fully functional.
  As part of project M&E function, the NBS has conducted a user satisfaction survey. A mixed method approach was employed involving triangulation
  of both qualitative and quantitative approaches targeting different users of official statistics. A total of 224 structured interviews, 2 FGDs and 24 Key
  Informant Interviews were surveyed across the different users of official statistics. The report has been finalized and adopted by the NBS.
