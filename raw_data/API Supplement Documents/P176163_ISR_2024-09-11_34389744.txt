1. OBJECTIVE


 1.1 Development Objective
   Original Development Objective (Approved as part of Approval package on 30-Nov-2023)
   The Project Development Objective (PDO) is to reduce pollution from land-based sources into the aquatic environment in
   selected areas of the South-West Coastal Belt of Albania.



   Has the Development Objective been changed since Board Approval of the Project Objective?

   No

4. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  The Project implementation hasn’t started yet due to pending effectiveness. Following the expression of interest from
  the client to extend the effectiveness date deadline, the Bank has approved such decision and established November 13,
  2024 as a new effectiveness deadline. This additional time is expected to allow for successful achievement of all
  effectiveness conditions (including the Loan ratification, hiring of the PMT and PCU, and completion of the POM), after
  which the implementation will start.
