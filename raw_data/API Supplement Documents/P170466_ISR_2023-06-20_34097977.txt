 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

   Project Coordination Unit at the MWE has been fully operationalized, with recruitment to all planned support consultants. There has been good
  progress in the initial implementation of planned project activities and procurements by the NFA and UWA, although delays in the environmental
  and social (ES) screening delayed start of some activities. UWA had procured and deployed field vehicles and equipment for field operations;
  trained existing collaborative resource management (CRM) groups in 8 protected areas; and initiated development of designs and ES due diligence
  for planned park infrastructure. NFA had raised seedlings for restoration planting and initiated restoration in target CFRs. UWA and NFA also have
  a number of procurements ongoing, related to designs of tourism and park infrastructure, trainings for staff and communities, and provision of inputs
  for livelihoods activities. Activities planned for implementation by MWE are still under procurement.

                                       other benefits from tourism - such as increased employment opportunities, improved markets for local
                                       produce and handicrafts, increased tax revenue from tourism businesses that are more difficult to
                                       quantify.

 Overall Comments PDO

  The METT score is, by design, measured only twice during project implementation - the next measurement will be at MTR.

  The EOP target for the indicator related to visitor numbers has been exceeded - the EOP value was set after the COVID-related slump in
  visitor numbers; however, tourist numbers post-COVID have increased quite significantly, mostly due to domestic, student, and regional
  visitors. The EOP value will be adjusted at MTR, based on the analysis of current and past trends.

  Data for other indicators remains as per reports provided to the March 2023 mission.
