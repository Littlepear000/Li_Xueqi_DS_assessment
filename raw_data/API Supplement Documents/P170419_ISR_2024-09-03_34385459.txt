1. PROJECT DEVELOPMENT OBJECTIVE


 1.1 Development Objective
   Original Development Objective (Approved as part of Approval package on 15-Dec-2020)
   The PDO is to increase the economic inclusion of youth in rural areas and the marketing efficiency and environmental
   sustainability of agri-food value chains in the Program area.
