 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  Component 1: Program for Results (PforR)
     1. The Government of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa (GoKP) has made significant progress in project implementation. Own-source tax
        revenue collection has increased from Rs.20 billion in FY19 to Rs.34 billion in FY21 and Rs. 44 billion in FY22 (revised estimates) –
        surpassing the end-program target. GST filing compliance has substantially improved from the 51% baseline to 77% in FY22 (DLI-1); the
        tax database integration plan is being implemented -KP Excise and Taxation department and the Board of Revenue have confirmed their
        readiness to link their databases with the KP Revenue Authority (DLI-2); Urban Immoveable Property Tax records of 6 cities have been
        digitized and linked with the GIS map (DLI-3); allocation of the Annual Development Plan funds to unapproved projects (excl. foreign
        project assistance) has significantly reduced to 30 percent in FY22 from 49 percent in FY18 (DLI-5); and more than 20 Tehsil Municipal
        Authorities are using the National Financial Management Information System to record revenue and expenditures (DLI-6).
     2. The progress on DLI 4 (cash management based on regular consolidation of cash balances and regular cash plans) has been
        slow, and is being closely monitored. The Government of KP has taken necessary steps in FY22 to improve progress on this indicator
        – for example, the KP Public Financial Management Act has been approved in FY22; the Cash Management policy has been adopted;
        and procedures for reconciliation of government accounts have been approved. The progress on this indicator is linked with PDO
        indicator for reducing cash balances in commercial banks. The progress will again be reviewed in December 2022.

  Component 2: Technical Assistance and Capacity Building (Investment Project Financing/IPF)
     1. There is satisfactory progress in implementation of e-Government functionality. The Government of KP has established a Steering
        Committee to manage implementation of the paper-less government intervention under the leadership of the Minister for Science,
        Technology & Information Technology, and the KP Information and Technology Board. Key contracts have been signed and
        implementation commenced in FY22. The Government of KP has completed a Business Process Review of critical government
        processes to automate approvals, signatories, and authorization, and held several workshops with departments to prepare the
        implementation plan. The Work Plan for FY23 has been approved, supporting the implementation during the year.
     2. Program Coordination is satisfactory. The Coordination Unit is fully staffed, conducts regular meetings and has managed both
        components satisfactorily. The same team also provides support to other selected projects (KP SPEED and KP Governance and Policy
        Project) under the Shared Services Unit.
