1. OBJECTIVE


 1.1 Development Objective
   Original Development Objective (Approved as part of Approval package on 10-Jan-2020)
   The project development objective is to improve the coverage of urban cadaster services in selected municipalities to
   enhance local government capacities for revenue generation and urban management.
   Has the Development Objective been changed since Board Approval of the Project Objective?

   No

In this ISR, the Project Development Objective (PDO) is rated Moderately Satisfactory, with evidence-based progress in the PDO-Level
indicators and Intermediate Results Indicators as follows: (i) 647,993 cadastral units surveyed (from Areas 1 and 2), of which 71,630 have
passed the quality control stages. A cadastral surveying implementation strategy is being defined for Area 3. Cadastral surveying contracts of
Areas 4 and 5 were awarded in April 2025. Area 6: a specific cooperation agreement and work plan have been prepared and agreed upon
with the Cadastral Institute of Lima (Instituto Catastral de Lima, ICL); (ii) The cadastral supervision and quality control firms for Areas 1 and 2
are already contracted and achieving deliveries; (iii) The first stage of the production of the SICUN is fully completed; stage 2 and 3 are
expected to be concluded by September 2025 and March 2026, respectively, (iv) The Government's Final Evaluation Plan will include eight
different studies and is being implemented in close coordination with the Bank: the Terms of Reference are expected to be approved by the
end of July 2025; (v) All project municipalities are being provided with constant and direct technical assistance for institutional strengthening;
and (vi) Several activities that contribute to achieving each indicator target are underway. Implementation Progress is rated Moderately
Satisfactory, considering that implementation arrangements are working well, with the Project under the leadership of the Agency for the
Formalization of Informal Property (Órgano de Formalización de la Propiedad Informal, COFOPRI) and based on close coordination with
implementing agencies, including the Ministry of Economy and Finance (Ministerio de Economía y Finanzas, MEF), the Ministry of Housing,
Construction and Sanitation (Ministerio de Vivienda, Construcción y Saneamiento, MVCS), and the Metropolitan Municipality of Lima. In
addition, the Project is rated Moderately Satisfactory in compliance with Social and Environmental Safeguards, Moderately
Satisfactory in Financial Management, Satisfactory in Project Management, and Moderately Satisfactory in Procurement.

Disbursements have increased from US$ 5.48 million as of May 2024 to US$ 12.53 million as of May 2025, representing 25% of the
US$ 50 million Loan. As of April 2025, the Project's Actual Costs (paid) were US$ 11.36 million (23% of the US$ 50 million Loan). The
Government plans to request disbursements to reach US$ 15.53 million by June 2025, representing 31% of the US$ 50 million Loan.
