 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The Public Sector Capability Enhancement Project (PSCEP) was approved by the Board on April 27, 2022, and project loan was signed by
  Government of India on July 22, 2022. The declaration of loan effectiveness was issued on Aug 25, 2022.
  Under Component 1, the roll-out of Framework for Roles, Activities and Competencies (FRAC) in the ministries, departments and agencies of
  Government of India is now with the Capacity Building Commission (CBC) instead of Department of Personnel and Training (DoPT). CBC has
  initiated the process of developing FRACs through preparation of more simplified annual capacity building plans (ACBPs) for all 93 MDAs while in
  parallel piloting the full- fledged FRACs for six ministries/ departments.
  Under Component 2, as on September 30, 2023, 2.4 million users have been onboarded on to the iGOT Karmayogi platform with more than 1300
  hours of content. There has been an increasing trend of course completions per month on iGOT - 62000 course completions were recorded on the
  platform during the period July 2022 to April 2023. As on October 12, 2023, 743 courses have been uploaded on the platform which include 12
