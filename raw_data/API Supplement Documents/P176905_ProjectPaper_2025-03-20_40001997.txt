 I. PROJECT STATUS AND RATIONALE FOR RESTRUCTURING

1.      The Argentina Climate Intelligent and Inclusive Agri-food Systems Project - CIAF (P176905) was approved on
December 9, 2021, signed on December 21, 2021, and became effective on March 21, 2022. CIAF has four components:
(a) Component 1 - Public Infrastructure for Agro-industrial Development; (b) Component 2 - Agro-industrial Development
and Strengthening of Rural Livelihoods; (c) Component 3 - Innovation for a Green and Inclusive Agri-food System
Transformation; and (d) Component 4 - Project Management and Evaluation. IBRD 9313-AR finances CIAF in the amount
of US$ 400,000,000, with additional counterpart financing in the amount of US$150,000,000 from subnational
government and farmer beneficiaries. The Closing Date for IBRD 9313-AR is June 15, 2027.

2.       The Project Development Objective (PDO) of CIAF is to: (a) support economic recovery and promote climate smart
practices among Project beneficiaries in Argentina's agri-food system; and (b) respond effectively in case of an eligible
crisis or emergency. Per the December 2024 Implementation Status and Results Report, the PDO rating is Moderately
Satisfactory, as there are likely to be moderate shortcomings in the achievement of the PDO and in the Project's efficiency.
Following 39 months of implementation (48% of the total time between Board Approval (Dec. 9, 2021) and the Closing
Date of June 15, 2027), there has been negligible achievement of the six PDO indicators; as of December 2024, only one
PDO indicator has reported results. The Implementation Progress (IP) rating is Moderately Unsatisfactory due to: (a) high
undisbursed balance of Ln. 9313-AR of US$ 317,293,190.74 or 79% of Loan proceeds; and (b) negligible implementation
progress across Components 1-3 over the previous six months. Cumulative disbursements are US$ 82,706,809.26 and the
Designated Account has a current balance of US$ 56,923,687.01. The 2024 External Audit of IBRD 9313-AR was unqualified
and there are no outstanding Environmental or Social issues.

3.       The Government of Argentina has requested a revision of the World Bank portfolio to better align Bank funding
with its new strategic priorities for the country. As part of this revision, the Government requested a cancellation of US$1.7
billion corresponding to uncommitted balances in nine operations. This cancellation aims at: (a) closing operations and/or
activities that are no longer a priority; and (b) increasing the lending space for financing of new priorities. As a response
to this request, US$ 73,910,295 will be cancelled from IBRD 9313-AR and the Project will be restructured. The process will
take place in two phases – first, the partial cancellation and second, the restructuring – to: (a) ensure sufficient time to
adjust the activities and results of the operation with the remaining Loan proceeds, and (b) not delay the process of
increasing the lending space for the country.

 II. DESCRIPTION OF PROPOSED CHANGES

This Project Paper proposes: (a) a partial cancellation of US$ 73,910,295 from IBRD 9313-AR at the Borrower’s request;
and (b) an update of the IBRD 9313-AR disbursement estimates.

5.       Partial Cancellation: In correspondence dated February 14, 2025, the Ministry of Economy requested a partial
cancellation of US$ 73,910,295 (23 percent of the undisbursed Loan proceeds). It is therefore proposed to: (a) Increase
Category 1 (Goods, works, consulting services (including audits), non-consulting services, Training, and Operating Costs for Parts
1, 2 and 4 of the Project (other than categories 2. 3. 4. 5 and 6)) of IBRD 9313-AR by US$ 25,000,000 to a revised US$
42,000,000; and (b) decrease Category 3 (Matching Grants for Rural Livelihoods Subprojects and an Agro-industrial
Agroindustrial Entrepreneurship Subproject Entrepreneurship Subprojects, Agreement or Rural Livelihoods under Part 2 of the
Project) by US$ 98,910,295.00 to a revised US$ 32,089,705.00. After the proposed partial cancellation, the total Loan
proceeds will be US$ 326,089,705.
