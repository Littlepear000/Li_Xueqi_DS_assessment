 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The implementation of the project is gaining momentum across its various components, after experiencing a slow start in the first year. Under sub-
  component 1.1, contracts have been signed with microfinance institutions to initiate the sale of solar systems to households. Sales are expected to
  commence by December 2023. Under sub-component 1.2, work has commenced, and it is anticipated that over 170 critical service facilities will be
  electrified by January 2024. Additionally, several assessments and studies are being launched to provide support for the recovery of Yemen's power
  sector and support capacity building to government entities and microfinance institutions.
  The challenging conditions on the ground are being closely monitored, as they have the potential to impact the project's implementation. Currently,
  the project is progressing smoothly without encountering any significant obstacles.
