 I. PROJECT STATUS AND RATIONALE FOR RESTRUCTURING


A. Summary

      1. This restructuring paper seeks to: (i) cancel EUR46,296,300 (US$49.95 million equivalent, corresponding to 52 percent of the
      initial financing amount) from Credit No. 6453-BJ; (ii) adjust the disbursement categories as a consequence of the cancellation.
      The Government of Benin (GoB) has requested this cancellation with letter received on May 12, 2020 in order to urgently
      reallocate the funds to the Benin First Fiscal Reform and Structural Transformation Development Policy Operation: Supplemental
      Financing (P174008), currently in preparation as part of the response to the COVID19 crisis.

      2. Following this cancellation, a more comprehensive restructuring package for the project will be submitted for approval to
      adjust project components, the results framework and other aspects as may be needed as a consequence of the funds
      cancellation. Given the large cancellation amount, and while the Project Development Objective (PDO) continues to be relevant
      and mostly achievable, it may need to be adjusted in light of the changes in the approach. Any changes will be included in
      subsequent project revisions.

B. Project Status

      3. Project performance is currently rated Moderately Satisfactory for progress toward achievement of the PDO and Moderately
      Unsatisfactory for Implementation Progress. The project was approved by the IDA Board of Directors on July 1, 2019 and became
      effective on December 3, 2019. It is at early implementation stage as it has encountered delays mainly due to project
      implementation arrangements not yet being in place. As of May 14, 2020, disbursement stood at EUR1.03 million (or 1.2 percent
      of the total project financing).

      4. The Ministry in charge of Digital Economy, Ministère du Numérique et de la Digitalisation (MND), is in the process of
      establishing the project’s Steering Committee. Recruitment of the Project Implementation Unit (PIU) is currently in final stages
      and the PIU is expected to be set up over the next few weeks. The MND has prepared initial versions of the Project
      Implementation Manual and Operations Manual, and these are currently being finalized and expected to be submitted for Bank’s
      review in June 2020. First versions of the Annual Work and Budget Plan for 2020 have been prepared and are expected to be
      approved once the project’s Steering Committee will be in place.




C.   Status of Project Components

      5. Practical implementation of project activities has not yet started, but the main developments and next steps agreed during
      the last Implementation Support Mission conducted in February 2020 to initiate implementation are described below.

                a.   Component 1 (Extending Connectivity): An assessment of energy and telecom infrastructure needs in targeted
                     areas is ongoing. This study will help identify out-of-coverage/white zones. The immediate next step was to define
                     and agree the specifics of the PPP model to follow for this component.

                b.   Component 2 (Financial Inclusion): Activities have not yet started. The MND is launching preparation of the ‘Sub
                     Grant’ Manual. This manual will help define the process for offering grants for digital transformation and digital

                    payments in agriculture value chains. Following preparation of the Manual, the immediate next steps, was to
                    launch the call for proposals for the grants

               c.   Component 3 (Digital Agricultural Services): A 2020-2024 National Digital Agriculture Strategy has been
                    developed by the GoB, the Food and Agriculture Organization (FAO), and the International Telecommunications
                    Union (ITU). This document will serve as a reference for activities implementation within this component. The
                    immediate next step was to develop the modalities for digital enrollment of farmers.

               d.   Component 4 (Access to Markets): Activities under component 4 are progressing, the project has identified
                    824km of rural roads to rehabilitate, of which 600km are foreseen to be financed under this component. The next
                    step was to start safeguard screening.

               e.   Component 5 (Project management): covered above.


     6. Going forward, with the proposed cancellation, selected components and activities within components will need to be
     cancelled and next steps as described above adjusted accordingly.



D. Rationale for Restructuring

     7. In order to appropriately respond to the COVID19 crisis, and given that the full IDA18 envelope for Benin had been committed,
     the GoB has asked for the proposed cancellation in order to urgently reallocate the funds to the Benin First Fiscal Reform and
     Structural Transformation Development Policy Operation: Supplemental Financing (P174008), currently under preparation as
     part of the response to the COVID19 crisis. A reallocation of credit proceeds between categories is needed as a consequence of
     the cancellation.



 II. DESCRIPTION OF PROPOSED CHANGES


    1. Cancellation of Funds: Cancellation of EUR46,296,300 (US$49.95 million equivalent) from Credit No. 6453-BJ as requested by
    the GoB.

    2. Reallocation between Disbursement Categories: Disbursement categories are being adjusted as a result of the proposed
    cancellation. For now, adjustments are being made keeping the same proportions between categories, but adjusting to the new
    total financing amount, with one exception: the PPF refinancing category is being adjusted to match the disbursed amount to
    date (since there will not be any additional disbursements under this category).
