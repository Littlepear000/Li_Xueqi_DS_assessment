3. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


 3.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
   The Program is completing its second year of implementation.
   It achieved visible results in increasing access for poor and vulnerable households to safety net programs. The Productive
   Cash Transfers Program (Programme de Transferts Monétaires Productifs, PTMP) reached 432,000 households
   cumulatively, including 205,000 active beneficiaries across three cohorts (cohorts 5, 6 and 7). Women represent an
   increasing share of designated PTMP recipients, signaling progress towards Disbursement Linked Indicator (DLI) 6.
   Economic inclusion measures have been rolled out for cohort 5 and over 2,100 new community saving and credit
   associations (Associations de Valorisation de l'Entraide Communautaire, AVECs) have been established. This progress
   enabled meeting 2024 targets of PTMP coverage, advancing towards achievement of DLI 4, DLI 5 and DLI 7. Partnerships
   between the PTMP and other social programs are being strengthened (linked to DLI 8). The PTMP has established itself as
   a solid platform for economic inclusion and women’s empowerment. Progress towards DLI 9 (Shock-response pilot) has
   been remarkable, with a pilot response to floods about to be implemented in the Cavally region.
