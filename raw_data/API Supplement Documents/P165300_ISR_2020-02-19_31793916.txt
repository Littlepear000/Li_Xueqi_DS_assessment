 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The Project was approved and signed in August 2018. Most project implementation efforts at this early stage are focused on advancing key
  procurement processes (consulting for Project Management Office, DFBOMT concession, signed November 27, 2019 and supervision contract,
  tendering currently underway). There is also significant efforts underway by EMB to advance the transfer of utility networks, and land acquisition
  and resettlement processes.
