 Project Development Objectives

  Project Development Objective (from Project Appraisal Document)
  The Project will implement reforms to enable investment, improve access to credit, and support targeted SMEs and young workers.

 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The Project was approved by the Board on May 5, 2022 and signed on May 19, 2022. The project is not yet effective as conditions have not yet
  been met, namely (i) recruitment of key staff (ii) preparation of the project implementation manual and (iii) adoption of environmental and social
  action plans.
