 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  Due to the accomplishment of the milestones scheduled in the 2023 Action Plan, the team proposes upgrading the PDO and IP, to Moderately
  Satisfactory, based on the following results: (i) the signing of two contracts for the cadastral surveys in two out of 5 cadastral areas, which
  comprise 13 of 22 project municipalities (59% project coverage area), located in the provinces of Piura (four districts), Lambayeque (five districts),
  and Lima (four districts), with a target of 661,924 cadastral units for a total contract amount equaling 26% of the Loan; (ii) the procurement
  processes for the supervision and quality control of cadastral surveys of areas 1 and 2 are in their final stages; (iii) the production of the urban
  cadastral information system is ongoing and its first phase is expected to be completed in March 2024; (iv) the Government's MTR report, was sent
  to the Bank in January 2024; (v) loan disbursements increased to 11%; and (vi) all project municipalities included in cadastral areas 1 and 2 are
  being provided with direct technical assistance by the Project. In addition, several activities that contribute to achieving each indicator target have
