I. PROJECT STATUS AND RATIONALE FOR RESTRUCTURING


  A. Introduction

     1.      Somali Integrated Statistics and Economic Planning Capacity Building (P171160) seeks to
     modernize Somalia’s data ecosystems by addressing gaps in data production and use. The Project
     Development Objective (PDO) is to strengthen the national statistical system in the collection, processing
     and dissemination of poverty and selected macro-economic data to inform development policy and poverty
     reduction activities.

     2.     The Federal Government of Somalia (FGS) requested a restructuring of the project through a letter
     dated May 16, 2024. The proposed restructuring is described in this Restructuring Paper.

     3.        The project has four components as follows:

           Component 1: Strengthening coordination, statistical infrastructure, and data development of the
            national statistical system provides support to the client’s key official statistics-producing agencies in
            strengthening the capacity of the country’s National Statistical System (NSS) to collect data for poverty
            and macroeconomic monitoring (national accounts/gross domestic product/sector growth, inflation,
            key administrative data). Priority data gaps to fill include poverty statistics, revision of the Consumer
            Price Index (CPI) and national accounts. The project also supported conducting a Somali Integrated
            Business Establishment Survey as well a Somali integrated Household Budget Survey (SIHBS). The
            results of these two surveys are fundamental for the design and compilation of a national CPI basket
            and the weighting of the commodities in the basket.

           Component 2: ICT development infrastructure and equipment of the National Statistical System
            supports the information and communications technology (ICT) infrastructure in the Directorate of
            National Statistics (DNS, headquarters and field offices) and selected key federal level ministries,
            departments and public agencies mentioned in Component 1.

           Component 3: Data utilization for monitoring and evaluation (M&E) and planning. This component
            aims to build capacity to use of data in planning and economic policy analysis through support to the
            Monitoring and Evaluation Directorate (MED), the National Economic Council (NEC), and
            macroeconomic and fiscal programming and enhance capacity in fiscal and macroeconomic analysis,
            economic forecasts, research as well as in monitoring and evaluation.

           Component 4. Project Management provides support to the Recipient to strengthen its capacity for
            project planning and budgeting, management, coordination, monitoring and evaluation activities, all
            through the provision of technical advisory services, training, operating costs and the acquisition of
            goods.


  B. Current Status

     5.    Implementation progress is strong. The progress towards achieving PDO is rated Satisfactory, and
     implementation progress is rated Satisfactory. As of April 2024, cumulative disbursement stood at US$16.7

         million, representing approximately 67 percent of the total project budget of US$25 million. The project has
         completed most of the planned activities. These include the annual budget outlook document, data sets,
         reports, updated indicators from SIHBS, the Compendium of Statistical Concepts, Definitions, and
         Standards, and the National M&E Framework. Nonetheless, several activities remained unfinished, and the
         client and the task team agreed that these activities could be completed before August 2025. Project has
         achieved decent outcomes; the Statistical Performance Indicators (SPI) of the World Bank1 have indicated
         an upward trend in outcomes, with the SPI rising from 19.6 in 2019 to 48.4 in 2022. In terms of gender
         results, the number of United Nations Statistics Division (UNSD) core gender indicators that Somalia is
         reporting has almost doubled, from 18 indicators in 2022 to about 34 indicators after the release of the
         report.

    C. Rationale for Restructuring.

         6.       FGS is faced with urgent needs to prepare national strategies to guide Somalia in the post-HIPC
         environment. The current National development plan (NDP-9) is approaching its culmination in December
         2024. Despite the considerable progress made, the government requires urgent transformation to integrate
         Somalia back into the global economy. Following the conclusion of the current NDP-9, the Ministry of
         Planning, Investment, and Economic Development has initiated the formulation of Somalia’s five-year
         National Transformation Plan (NTP) covering the period 2025 to 2029. Thus, there is a need to complete
         and approve this plan for implementation starting from January 2025. Basic economic research related to
         Somalia’s development will have to be carried out to inform the plan preparation. The additional budget
         allocation for this activity is $2.2 million.

         7.      FGS has requested Additional Financing (AF) on November 6, 2023, in the amount of USD 35
         million. The task team is working on the preparation of the AF, which will cover new areas of data collection
         and support of data use for policies, including the preparation of NTP.

         8.      The reallocation of fund to support NTP preparation is proposed to be carried out in anticipation
         of the additional financing (AF), expected to be approved around September 2024. After the project
         receives new financial resources through AF, the $2 million amount will be reallocated back to the original
         component.

         9.       ICT component. Following project implementation over the last three years, it has become evident
         that the original budget estimates to achieve all of the results under the Component 2 ICT development
         infrastructure and equipment of the National Statistical System were too low. Therefore, it is proposed to
         reallocate $300,000 from Sub-component 1.4. Strengthening Human Capacity in Poverty, CPI and National
         Accounts Statistics to Component 2. This sub-component has savings from the activity to support production
         of a national Somali CPI. Other donors, particularly International Monetary Fund and African Development
         Bank, are already providing financial support to CPI modernization in Somalia. Thus, the reallocation of
         $300,000 from this sub-component will not affect its ability to achieve PDO.
