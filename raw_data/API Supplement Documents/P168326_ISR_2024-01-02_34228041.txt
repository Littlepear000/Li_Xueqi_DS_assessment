 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  Based on the assessment of the overall project progress, it was concluded that the Project is on track to achieve its Project Development Objective
  (PDO) as set in the Financing Agreement (to improve the sources of livelihood for local populations in GBAO and Khatlon through tourism and
  agribusiness). The project’s key outcome indicators are on track to being achieved. The potential Project risks were discussed and analyzed.
  Overall, the risks to the project and the achievement of its objectives are manageable, with the key risk being the project’s closing date of December
  2, 2024. Given the delay in the launch of key activities caused by the COVID-19 pandemic, there is a risk of not completing all the construction
  works related to the Yamchun Fortress Rehabilitation. The Government has requested to extend the project's closing date for 12 months till
