4. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
   The Human Capital Protection Project became effective on November 27, 2024. This 15-month project is financed by a
   US$100.4 million loan from International Development Association (IDA). The project’s objective is to support the Recipient's
   efforts to ensure the provision of essential social services. The project consists of two components: (a) ensuring the provision of

  essential health and education services (US$100 million) and (b) project management (US$400,000). The project finances
  essential services in the health and education sectors for 2024 and 2025.

  This project is being implemented in Burkina Faso's volatile security environment and challenging humanitarian situation,
  highlighting the urgent need to maintain essential social services and prevent short-and long-term declines in human capital.
  The operation aligns with the priority of the Western and Central Africa Region to strengthen core institutions for service
  delivery 2021-2025 (Report 159265); select priorities of the Regional Sahel Prevention and Resilience Allocation; and Country
  Partnership Framework FY18-24, which was adjusted in the Performance and Learning Review (Report 166080-BF).

  Progress towards achievement of the project’s development objective and overall Implementation Progress are currently rated
  as satisfactory. Significant developments to date include the following:

  Under Component 1, which focuses on ensuring the provision of essential selected services, key achievements in the education
  sector include organizing nine student exams with a 98.11 percent attendance rate, certifying 99.8 percent of teachers in
  national training institutions, and providing pedagogical kits to 69,000 teachers. In the health sector, the Project has facilitated
  16.2 million free healthcare consultations for women and children under five years old, immunized 1,13 million children, and
  improved the payment rate for free care expenses from 75 percent in 2023 to 82 percent as of the end of December 2024.

  Under Component 2, which focuses on project management, core governance arrangements designed in the project appraisal
  document are in place. These include an operational steering meeting, effective ex-ante audit arrangements under the
  leadership of IGF, a well-established fiduciary platform with qualified staff, a manual of procedures, an information
  management system, and a publicly available environment and social instruments.
