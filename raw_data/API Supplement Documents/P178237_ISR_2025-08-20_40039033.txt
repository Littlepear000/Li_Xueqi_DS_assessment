1. OBJECTIVE


 1.1 Development Objective
   Original Development Objective (Approved as part of Approval package on 15-Oct-2024)
   The Development Objective of the Series of Projects is to strengthen the shared management of selected Pacific Island
   oceanic and coastal fisheries, and the critical habitats upon which they depend.

   The Development Objective of the objective is to strengthen stakeholder capacity for regional collaboration and
   enhanced sector performance in fisheries, and for improved habitat preservation of the Federated States of Micronesia.
   Has the Development Objective been changed since Board Approval of the Project Objective?

   No

4. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  There has been progress made towards the Project Development Objective since effectiveness was achieved in February.
  The request for proposal process for the key infrastructure of Chuuk and Pohnpei marinas is underway, and the
  Environmental, Social and Impact Assessment (ESIA) request for expressions of interest (REOI) has been launched. The
  design terms of reference (TOR) for the Chuuk landfill and materials recovery facility (MRF) have been drafted. Key
  technical positions are under procurement including the Marine Engineer and Coastal Fisheries Specialist. The project has
  a Project Coordinator, Project Officer, Environmental and Social Officer and Communications Officer in place. The
  Monitoring and Evaluation Officer position and Project Assistant positions are under procurement.
