 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The Strengthening Afghanistan's Financial Intermediation (SAFI) project was approved by the World Bank Board on September 28, 2020. The
  Project’s Financing Agreement between the World Bank and the Ministry of Finance (MOF) and Project Agreement between the World Bank and Da
  Afghanistan Bank (DAB) were signed on October 15, 2020. The project was declared effective on January 06, 2021 following the signing of the
  Subsidiary Agreement between MOF and DAB and Implementing Partner Agreement (IPA) between MOF and the Afghan Credit Guarantee
  Foundation (ACGF). The disbursements under the project are expected to commence as soon as the project is included in the Government's next
  Fiscal Year (1400 lunar year) budget and approved by the lower house of the National Assembly. In the interim, the authorities and the ACGF are
