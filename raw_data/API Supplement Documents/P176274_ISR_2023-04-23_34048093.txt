 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

       1.    The project was restructured on December 22, 2023 and changes included 1) project hosting, from the Ministry of SMEs and Private
             Sector Development to the Ministry of Finance and Budget, and associated institutional arrangements, 2) The PPA refinancing and
             effectiveness deadline was extended to June 30, 2023, and 3) a small provision was added to enable enhanced capacity building to
             SMEs. A technical review meeting took place with the interim PIU on April 3-5, 2023. This is in lieu of a formal mission which will take
             place in May 2023. The technical review meeting findings were that there is very good progress in reaching the effectiveness conditions,
             which can be expected in May 2023.
       2.    Parallel technical assistance on PPPs: Technical assistance to the government of the Central African Republic for the operationalization of
             the government's PPP mechanism is in its final phase of implementation with the holding of a capacity building workshop for executives
             designated by the various government ministries, which took place from April 11 to 13, 2023. This workshop complemented several
