4. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  As of November 27, 2024, overall project progress (of a maximum of 100%) is at 97.8%, a very positive indicator.
  Implementation is at 42.45%, slightly below the scheduled optimum 43.31%.
  Construction is active on all fronts, including railyards, viaduct, stations and rolling stock.
  Implementation progress of key workfronts as of November 2024 are:
  +Viaduct, progress of 25.87%, above the 25.66% scheduled,
  +Railyards (patiotaller, progress as of November, 2024: 66.40%, nearly 1% above the scheduled progress to date),
  +Cl 68-Primero de Mayo interchange, currently at 60.99% (scheduled: 59.65%).
  +Stations and access buildings: current progress at 5.70% (scheduled: 6.16%).
  +Rolling Stock: progress: 25.72%, out of 26.20% scheduled.
  Several workfronts are ahead of schedule, and the only notable workfront that is significantly delayed is the Calle 72
  front, where all supervision efforts and requests to the concessionaire are being channeled. Progress as of November,
  2024: 88.34%, scheduled: 100%),
  The secondary utility network transfer should be at 37.31% and has advanced 31.69%.
  EMB, interventoria and ML1 are working to speed up recruitment to fill gaps in labor shortage, speed up permitting for
  works on heritage protected areas, and reduce issues with the implementation of traffic management plans.
  Changes in shareholders in DFBOMT concessionaire, Metro Linea 1, reported on 2023, have not been processed: EMB has
  reported to the MDBs that Metro Linea 1 intends to change the shareholder composition of the concessionaire. With the
  proposed changes, the lead firms, CHEC and Xi'an Metro would transfer their shares of the concessionaire to their
  second-level subsidiaries in Spain and Colombia, respectively, the former controlled by CHEC Singapore, the latter by
  Xi'an Metro International.This issue was expected to be decided over the first semester of CY 2024, but has not made
  additional progress since.
