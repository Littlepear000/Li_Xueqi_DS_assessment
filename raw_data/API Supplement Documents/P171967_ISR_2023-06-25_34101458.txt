 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The project became effective on October 5, 2020, in during covid-19. As such the project was off to a slow start as many Energy Utilities’ ability to
  enter into power purchasing agreements with private investors was severely weakened. As a result, several infrastructure sub-projects in the
  pipeline have not materialized. Since then, TDB has managed to add several new deals to the pipeline. In the past quarter, a few projects reached
  financial close. Therefore an additional $50M was disbursed which led to the full disbursement of component #2 of the project in which had a total
  amount of funds of $75M. Based on the review of the pipeline, this level of disbursement performance is expected to be sustained. COMESA
  continue to make progress in the implementation of its component. Both TDB and COMESA continue to demonstrate satisfactory performance in
  the E&S and Fiduciary areas.
  COMESA Secretariat deliverables. The FY23 workplan is being implemented. Overall progress in the delivery of the workplan has improved with
  several deliverables in advance stages of completion. Three main deliverables are underway: (i) the development of COMESA model solar
