4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  The CAR Accelerating Results in Education was approved by the World Bank’s (WB) Board of Executive Directors on June
  18, 2024 signed by the WB and the Government of the Central African Republic on September 17, 2024, and is expected
  to close on 31 Oct 2030. It is jointly financed by the International Development Association (IDA) and the Global
  Partnership for Education (GPE). The Project is expected to become effective by its deadline of December 17, 2024.

  The Project aims to address some of the most critical challenges to improve access to quality basic education and
  strengthen capacity for sector management in CAR. It focuses on activities that support and strengthen teacher-learner
  interaction in the classroom, and improve the overall teaching and learning environment in classrooms. Finally, in
  alignment with the Ministry of Education's priorities, the Project aims to be inclusive across several key dimensions,
  including in the design and construction of climate-resilient and environmentally sustainable school infrastructure, taking
  into account the needs of students with disabilities. It also focuses on promoting girl’s retention and completion of the
  basic education cycle. The Project is expected to achieve its development objective through four components:
  Component 1: Increase access to improved learning environments; Component 2: Improve teaching quality ; Component
  3: Strengthen data system and sector management; and Component 4: Contingency Emergency Response Component
  (CERC).
