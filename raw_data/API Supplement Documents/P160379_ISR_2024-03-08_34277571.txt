 Project Development Objectives

  Project Development Objective (from Project Appraisal Document)
  The Project Development Objectives (PDO) are to demonstrate the operational and economic feasibility of utility-scale innovative renewable energy
  technologies and battery energy storage solutions, and to strengthen institutional capacity to facilitate scale-up of such technologies on a
  commercial basis in India.

  Has the Project Development Objective been changed since Board Approval of the Project Objective?
  No

 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  First of the two subprojects supported by the Project - 100 MW(AC) Solar PV Project with 40 MW/120 MWh Battery Energy Storage System - at Rajnandg
  commissioned on February 1, 2024. This is India's largest Battery Energy Storage Project. The project was formally inaugurated by the Hon'ble Prime Min
  February 2024 (https://pmindiawebcast.nic.in/2024/24feb24.html ). Further details on this subproject are available at the Government of India’s Public Info
  (https://pib.gov.in/PressReleaseIframePage.aspx?PRID=2008681#:~:text=The%2040%20megawatts%20(MW)%20%2F,is%20located%20in%20Rajnand
  )

  Tendering process for the second subproject in Jharkhand (100 MW floating solar) is underway and will be concluded shortly.
