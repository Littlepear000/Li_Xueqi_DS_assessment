 Implementation Status and Key Decisions


  Introduction
  This ISR is based on the latest virtual GALOP implementation support mission from December 12 to 21, 2022. The parent GALOP is 34 percent
  disbursed and the GALOP Additional Financing (AF) is 37 percent disbursed. The AF includes the COVID-19 Accelerated Response Trust Fund
  (TF B3009) which is fully disbursed and closed on November 30, 2021. The rating of the Project’s development objective is rated “satisfactory” and
  the overall project implementation progress rating is upgraded to “satisfactory” from “moderately satisfactory.”
  The GALOP is on track to achieving its Project Development Objective (PDO) of improving the quality of education in low performing basic
  education schools and strengthening education sector equity and accountability in Ghana. Key results for achieving the PDO have been completed

  as follows: (i) 10,579 schools have received the first allocation of learning grants in August 2022; (ii) a follow-up assessment of P2 and P4 students
  is currently underway in the first half of 2023 after the maiden assessment for P4 successfully undertaken in December 2021; (iii) accountability
  dashboard has been developed and training is underway to make it functional in 2023; (iv) the accountability dashboard is being verified by IVA for
  functionality to be completed by mid-March, 2023 --following the development of an accountability framework approved by the MoE; (v) for the first
  time in at least 20 years, the MoE has digitized the collection of Education Management Information System (EMIS) data in September 2022; (vi)
  teacher deployment and transfers have been reviewed by the GES in December 2022, in an effort to align to norms of pupil-teacher ratio; and
  (vii) GALOP AF GPE COVID-19 Accelerated Financing grant closed on November 30, 2021 with “satisfactory” outcome rating on the ICR.
  The project has also made satisfactory progress on achieving the Performance Based Conditions agreed under the GALOP and AF. Out of
  28 PBCs, 14 (representing 50 percent) have been achieved, verified, confirmed, and disbursed for a total amount of US$69.99 million (34.60
  percent). Total disbursement under the GALOP IDA is US$47.55 million representing 34 percent of total proceed of US$150 million; and 37 percent
  under the GALOP AF -Trust Funds which has disbursed US$22.44 million out of total proceed of US$60 million. Four (4) additional PBCs under the
  GALOP have been achieved and awaiting verification (representing a total amount of US$24 million including US$2 million under the GPE). The
  IVA has initiated verification of these four PBCs, expected to be completed by March 15, 2023. This verification and notification of the achievement
  of PBCs will bring IDA disbursement to US$93.99 million (about 45 percent) of total proceeds in the first quarter of 2023.
  The rating on Implementation Progress (IP) is upgraded from "moderately satisfactory" to "satisfactory" because of the completion of key
  activities in the last six months, namely: (i) IVA has been procured for the Ghana EOF, and contracts for six of seven lots for the GALOP AF
  GPRBA Ghana EOP has been signed on November 15, 2022; (ii) disbursement conditions for the GALOP AF GPE TF0B0846 has been lifted on
  November 25, 2022 allowing for disbursements under TF; (iii) report for assessment data and analysis on maiden P4 assessment has been
  presented and report has been shared with Bank team on December 16, 2022; (iv) IVA has initiated the verification of four (4) PBCs achieved i.e.
  PBC1.2 (US$10M); PBC3.1 (US$6M); PBC4.2 (US$4M) and PBC5.2.1 (US$2M) on December 21, 2022 and is expected to complete notification
  report by March 15, 2023; (v) draft implementation plan and budget for 2023 has been prepared and submitted to the Bank for review on January
  24, 2023; and (v) funds for training of 73,000 teachers have been released and training has started in January 2023 for five additional regions.
