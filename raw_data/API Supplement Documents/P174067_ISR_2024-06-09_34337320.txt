4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  The Public Sector Capability Enhancement Project (PSCEP) loan was made effective on August 25, 2022. There has been
  good progress in implementation of activities by the CBC and KB.

  Under Component 1, there has been a shift in the approach towards undertaking competency assessments from
  developing individual level Framework for Roles, Activities and Competencies (FRACs) to developing organizational level
  Annual Capacity Building Plans (ACBPs) for all ministries, departments, and agencies (MDAs) of the Government of India.
  The responsibility for developing and finalizing these ACBPs has been given to CBC. CBC has supported preparation of the
  ACBPs for 89 MDAs and is in the process of creating a repository of online training courses for integration on the iGOT
  platform, based on the requirements identified through the exercise of developing these ACBPs.
  Under Component 2, the contract for hiring of the managed service provider (MSP) for development and maintenance of
  the iGOT Karmayogi portal for a period of five years was awarded in June 2023. The tender for procuring cloud space for
  the platform, launched by Karmayogi Bharat in August 2023, has been cleared for award and will be signed in early June
  2024. As on April 30, 2024, 3.2 million users have been onboarded with 1002 online courses available on the platform.
  There is an increasing trend of course completions on iGOT with about 74 lakh completions recorded by end of April
  2024.
  It has been decided that DoPT, in consultation with CBC and KB, will be submitting to the World Bank an activity-wise
  plan and funds required for existing activities in the coming years as well as for additional interventions which are at the
  planning stage. This is critical for pushing the project disbursements under the project which have been slow at 4.9% of
  the loan amount till end of March 2024. In the TPRM held on April 5, 2024, DoPT confirmed that disbursements will reach
  about 15% of the loan amount by December 31, 2024.
