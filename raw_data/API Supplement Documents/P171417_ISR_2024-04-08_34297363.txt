 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The Program implementation continues at a steady pace and several accomplishments have been achieved in the past few months. A summary
  regarding a set of parametric pension reforms has been prepared for Cabinet approval. The roll-out of the e-Procurement system is progressing well
  after a successful pilot phase. All summaries to the Chief Secretary are now submitted in a digital format, marking a significant shift from manual file
  movements. Punjab’s tax revenue collection is on track for achieving its year-end target (June 2024), while the expansion in services sales tax base
  and filing compliance are also expected to achieve annual targets.
