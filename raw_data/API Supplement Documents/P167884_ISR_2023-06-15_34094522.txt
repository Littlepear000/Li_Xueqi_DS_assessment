 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The lifting of a freeze of local governments’ bank accounts augurs well for an improved pace of implementation and disbursement in
  2023. The previous ISR dated 16 December 2022 noted delays in implementation resulting from measures taken by the Government of the
  Republic of Guinea to verify the use of public funds by local government. A year-long freeze of the accounts of all municipalities brought to a halt the
  execution of the National Fund for Local Development (FNDL), a key mechanism of decentralization reform which the project supports with
  technical advice and capacity-strengthening activities. However, the situation has since improved. A mission held from 5-14 December 2022 verified
  that the Ministry of Territorial Administration and Decentralization (MATD) ordered the lifting of the freeze. Most municipalities have resumed the
  execution of FNDL subprojects initiated in 2021, and the National Agency for the Financing of Communities (ANAFIC) was able to resume the
  implementation of project-funded TA and capacity-building activities from January 2023 onward.

  The project was restructured to alleviate the impact of delays experienced in 2022. The restructuration only includes changes to DLIs and the
  results framework. DLI adjustments include stronger incentives for a fluid and timely replenishment of FNDL.
  A follow-on project – SLGP2 (P177095) – was approved by the WBG Board on 23 March 2023 and signed on 13 April 2023. The new project
  (a US$ 81 million IDA credit) strengthens the capacity of national and local institutions to mobilize and manage development resources efficiently,
  accountably and with a greater emphasis on the quality of outcomes including (a) an adequate prioritization of climate adaptation measures in the
  selection of community infrastructure and services; and (b) a leading role for community members, particularly women, youth and the most
  vulnerable, in decisions pertaining to investments.
