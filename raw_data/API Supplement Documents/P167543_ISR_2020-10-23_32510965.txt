 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  This is the first ISR to be completed for this project. A more comprehensive ISR will be prepared after the declaration of effectiveness, and will
  incorporate outcomes from the first implementation support mission (October 5-7, 2020).
  Implementation is generally progressing well, though there have some delays in procurement and in the selection of candidates from among
  competitive bidding selections. With the declaration of effectiveness, the project implementation unit (PIU) will shift from the PCDS, which has been
  serving as a transitional PIU, to the newly recruited team within ANSI in the Projet Villages Intelligents (PVI) unit. An official launch of the program is
  expected in the next few weeks, following the Declaration of Effectiveness, to be followed by a stakeholder workshop.
