 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  This is an interim ISR. The team expects to undertake a virtual implementation support mission in May 2022 (exact date to be confirmed) at which
  time the ISR will be updated. The Project was declared effective on January 25, 2022 and is currently focused on advancing recruitment of the
  Project Management Unit staffing complement and preparing the background materials and terms of reference to enable full delivery of Year 1
  activities under all components. An detailed budget and workplan for the first year of project implementation is under preparation.
