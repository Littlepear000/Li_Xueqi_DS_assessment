1. OBJECTIVE


 1.1 Development Objective
   Original Development Objective (Approved as part of Approval package on 19-Jun-2019)
   Project Development Objective is to improve the sources of livelihood for local populations in GBAO and Khatlon through
   tourism and agribusiness.
   Has the Development Objective been changed since Board Approval of the Project Objective?

   No
