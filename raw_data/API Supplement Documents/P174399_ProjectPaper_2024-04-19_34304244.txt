I. PROGRAM STATUS AND RATIONALE FOR RESTRUCTURING

1.      This Restructuring Paper seeks Country Director (CD) approval to introduce the following changes to the Bhutan
Human Capital Recovery and Resilience Program (P174399): (i) results framework; (ii) extension of closing date; (iii)
reallocation between and/or changes in DLIs; (iv) disbursement arrangements; (v) implementation schedule; and (vi)
addition of two new World Bank scorecard indicators. These adjustments are considered adequate for the completion of
remaining activities which would ensure that the Program can successfully achieve its Project Development Objective
(PDO).

A. Program Status

2.       The World Bank (WB) is supporting the Royal Government of Bhutan (RGoB)’s investment into resilient human
capital accumulation and recovery post COVID-19 pandemic through the Human Capital Recovery and Resilience Program
for Results (HCRRP) (US $20 million from the International Development Association (IDA) and a US$ 3 million grant as an
additional financing from the Global Partnership for Education (GPE) Multiplier Fund). The PDO is to support building,
protecting, and utilizing human capital in Bhutan. HCRRP is linked to the RGoB’s National Key Result Areas (NKRAs) 7
“Improving Quality of Education and Skills” and 11 “Productive and Gainful Employment” of the 12th Five-Year Plan (FYP)
2018-23, including associated COVID-19 recovery and resilience programs. As such, the Program implementation is
overseen by both the Ministry of Education and Skills Development (MoESD) and the Ministry of Industry, Commerce, and
Employment (MoICE), which are lead implementing agencies for the Program. It is also the first Program for Results (PforR)
implemented in Bhutan.

3.      Following effectiveness, the Program has achieved significant progress. 52.82 percent of the original IDA amount
disbursed as well as an advance amounting to US$ 0.5 million from the GPE grant. Progress towards achievement of the
PDO and overall implementation progress are both rated Moderately Satisfactory.

4.      The Program has successfully achieved the following key results, outputs, and outcomes:
        (a)    One PDO indicator out of five has been achieved (digitization of education for improved student
               outcomes).
        (b)    All period-one Disbursement Linked Results (DLRs) have been achieved and disbursed against.
        (c)    Significant contributions in supporting the RGoB NKRA 7 and 11 as follows:
               (i)      Improving ECCD access and quality: The MoESD approved the National Early Childhood Care and
                        Development (ECCD) Action Plan to achieve its enrolment and quality goals. In addition, a total of
                        57 ECCD trainers completed training using revised training programs that aim to improve the
                        focus on age-appropriate pedagogy and to be more inclusive and climate sensitive (this
                        achievement is among period-one DLRs under Disbursement Linked Result (DLI) 1).
               (ii)     Improving quality and inclusiveness of the education system: The MoESD developed and
                        approved an Action Plan on curriculum digitization and over 5,000 teachers have been trained on
                        Information and Communication Technology (ICT)-based competencies. In addition, core subjects
                        have been digitized and contents have been made accessible to at least 80% of schools in urban
                        areas and 40% of schools in rural areas, along with instructional guides and structured session
                        plans (This achievement constitutes one of the PDOs, period-one DLR under DLI 3, and an
                        intermediary indicator).
               (iii)    Strengthening design of Skills Development Programs (SDP) and their linkages to the labor market:
                        The operational manual for SDP has been approved by MoESD, along with a National Strategy to
                        enhance inclusion of women in Technical and Vocational Education and Training (TVET) system
                        and improve their employment outcomes. Further, a tracer system for regular tracking of

                        employment outcomes for trainee graduates has been established (This achievement constitutes
                        a prior result, a period-one DLR under DLIs 5 & 6, and two intermediary indicators).
                (iv)    Promoting entrepreneurship and enabling easy access of employment services for jobseekers:
                        The incubation centers are operational at five colleges of Royal University of Bhutan with
                        dedicated incubation managers and approved business plans. In addition, MoICE and MoESD have
                        developed a single intake and profiling window (Bhutan Labor Market Information System) where
                        job seekers and trainees can access online applications for job vacancies and skilling programs

                        matching (This achievement constitutes a prior result, a period-one DLR under DLI 7, and a period-

        (d)     Several period-two DLRs are close to being achieved. These include results related to improving digital
                pedagogy competency of teachers and reduction in the digital divide (DLI 3), increasing the share of
                women SDP graduates and trainings delivered outside main cities (DLI 5), linking incubation center
                graduates with access to finance (DLI 7), and delivering trainings on climate-responsive occupations as
                part of the Skills Development Program curriculum (DLI 9).

B. Rationale for Restructuring

5.      The Project Mid-Term Review (MTR), which was conducted in September 2023, recommended a restructuring to
extend the closing date from January 2025 to June 30, 2026. A letter from the Ministry of Finance (MoF) formally
requesting the restructuring was received on January 29, 2024. The key rationale is that the extension would allow the
RGoB to fully utilize the HCRRP credit and grant resources to achieve the PDO. Currently, two key results areas on
expanding access and professionalization of ECCD and on improving employment and inclusion outcomes are experiencing
delays. The two results areas are linked to two PDOs, namely PDO indicator 1 (Percentage of children (3-5 years old)
enrolled in ECCD services led by a ECCD facilitator which complete the basic certificate (Percentage)) and PDO indicator 3
(Percentage increase from baseline (TBE) in percent of trainees employed (including full and part-time, wage and self-
employment) within 6 months of completing SDP (Percentage)). They are also linked to the following DLIs: DLI 1
(Strengthening the quality of ECCD services for improved child outcomes), DLI 2 (Expansion of ECCD in targeted
population), DLI 5 (Enhanced inclusion under SDP), and DLI 6 (Increased employment post SDP).

6.       Reasons for the delays include changes in government priorities and policies, or delays in execution due to
procurement, funding, or other administrative bottlenecks. In the case of DLI 1, ECCD facilitator training is delayed due to
a delay in recruitment of facilitators by the Royal Service Commission and capacity constraints in Paro College of Education.
Recruitment delays also happened due to elections that were just concluded. In the case of DLI 2 on the expansion of
ECCD, the government has prepared a list of ECCD centers to be constructed and renovated, but implementation could
not be initiated in a timely manner due to delays with release of funds by the MoF and other factors including the national
elections. In the case of DLIs 5 and 6, the MoESD plans to increase the SDP duration from six months to one year. This
means that measuring achievement on inclusion and post-training employment outcomes for trainees who enroll in later
years would extend beyond the current Project closure date.

7.      Program restructuring would also include a reformulation of two DLRs (namely, period-two DLR under DLI 4 and
period-two DLR under DLI 8) to align with recent government priorities. Consequently, changes are proposed to the DLI
Matrix, DLR verification protocol, and DLR financing formula to allow disbursements against achieved results under both
DLIs.

    II. DESCRIPTION OF PROPOSED CHANGES

   8.      Extension of closing date: The Program closing date will be extended from January 2025 to June 2026. The end
   target date for all indicators in the Results Framework (PDO indicators and intermediate results) will also be revised to
   account for the new Program closing date.

   9.      Revision in the definition of implementation periods: “Period 1” means the first period of implementation from
   effectiveness to June 30, 2023. “Period 2” means the second period of implementation during fiscal year 2023-24. “Period
   3” means the third period of implementation during fiscal year 2024-25. “Period 4” means the fourth period of
   implementation during fiscal year 2025-26.

   10.     Revision to DLRs: The period-two DLR under DLI 4 (building climate change resilience through education
   system) and period-two DLR under DLI 8 (strengthen systems and coordination to enhance employment support
   delivery under MoICE) will be reformulated as follows:

       (a) For both DLIs, changes to the DLI matrix on DLR definition for period two.
       (b) For both DLIs, changes in the Verification Protocol Table for the relevant DLR that include changes on the
           financing formula. Allocated amount will remain the same.
       (c) A summary of changes, with a brief justification, is described below.

11.      Prior Result under DLI 3: The revision to prior result under DLI 3 (and placing it under period-one DLR) was agreed
on during the 2022 Restructuring and it was made effective by the signed amendment letter. However, there appears to
be a glitch in the portal that is not reflecting this change, so this restructuring is reflecting it in the updated data sheet.

12.      PDO indicator revisions: Given that the definition for period two DLR under DLI 8 is changed, one of the PDO
indicators linked to this DLR (PDO 4: Enhanced resilience of job seekers) will be slightly amended by revising the text for
the end target (without any changes to the indicator itself). The proposed changes to the text for End Target are as follows:
“Linkages established through data sharing and system linkages protocols between MoICE and the three economic sectors
in line with the Employment Responsibility System (ERS) Standard Operating Procedure”.
