1. PROJECT DEVELOPMENT OBJECTIVE


 1.1 Development Objective
   Original Development Objective (Approved as part of Approval package on 15-Dec-2020)
   The PDO is to increase the economic inclusion of youth in rural areas and the marketing efficiency and environmental
   sustainability of agri-food value chains in the Program area.

3. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


 3.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
   The Regional Centers for Agri-Food and Agribusinesses Entrepreneurs (CRJEAA) have been fully operaionalized and coordination
   between involved institutional actors has been improbved. As a result of the progress made pn RA#1, an increasing number of young
   entrepreneurs have received support ranging from entrepreneurship awareness raising to preparing business plans for their projects.
   However, access to financing remains a critical constraint in the implementation of these projects, with the exception of projects
   receiving public funding through the agricultural development fund (FDA) and solidarity agriculture projects (in the context of DLI3 which
   has seen signficiant progress). Going forward, the Program will explore improving young entrepreneurs’ access to finance in rural areas
   through new interventions in collaboration with IFC. For RA#2, despite some progress in the preparation of technical and
   environmental/social studies, the achievement of objectives related to the mondernization of the four wholesale markets is uncertain if
   construction does not start before the end of 2025. To accelerate progress on the modernization of the wholesale markets, high-level
   intervention and close coordination between the Ministry of the Interior and the Ministry of Agriculture are needed. Despite a context of
   increasing water scarcity, RA#3 has made significant progress, for example in the development and improvement of digital agricultural
   advisory tools. In addition, the capacity for measuring the impact of the extension services provided by ONCA has improved significantly
   (through the development of a dedicated digital platform and methodology to be applied by extension agents).
