 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The objective of the project is to expand long-term finance to private firms in selected infrastructure sectors in Eastern and Southern Africa. The
  project seeks to address one of the main barriers to infrastructure development, i.e. access to suitable finance. The project became effective
  October 5, 2020, during Covid-19. The global economic downturn caused by the pandemic has weakened the appetite for private investment in
  infrastructure. As a result several planned transactions did not materialize and disbursements to approved sub-projects have been slower than
  projected. However, TDB has managed to add several new deals to the pipeline and are optimistic that investor confidence in the sector is being
  restored and the pent-up demand will result in increased disbursement to the sub-projects in the pipeline. Further, the demand for infrastructure
  development is likely to increase as infrastructure development is pursued as a path to post-pandemic recovery and green, resilient, inclusive
  development. Hence the relevance of the objectives and demand for this project will increase even more.
