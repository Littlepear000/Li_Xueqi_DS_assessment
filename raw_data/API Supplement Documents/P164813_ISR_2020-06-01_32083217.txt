 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The third implementation support mission (March 23-27) took place in Vientiane, Lao PDR between March 23 and 27, 2020.
  Overall implementation progress since the last mission has been moderate as technical work is advancing while mechanisms to
  effectively implement technical recommendations for results remain limited. Implementation of reforms remains slow, limiting the impact of
  the project on the real economy. Approved Challenge Facility (CF) proposals to support reform efforts of various concerned Ministries/Agencies are
  experiencing implementation delays and significant efforts will be required by Senior Management at MOIC but also MPI, other line Ministries, and
  the Prime Minister's Office to bring their implementation back on track to allow timely completion and avoid cancellation of CF proposal activities.
  Strengthening the supervision of technical consultants by government and project implementation unit staff will be critical to ensure
  recommendations can be swiftly and decisively implemented. The Business Assistance Facility continues to perform strongly with 44 matching
  grants approved and the first 14 fully disbursed.

  While the emerging COVID-19 crisis is starting to impact the economic environment, project implementation and especially the matching
  grant component, also offer opportunities to address the crisis. Fully and effectively implementing the reform package outlined in the LCTP
  could contribute to accelerating economic recovery by reducing the costs of firms to start and operate business, allowing firms to more flexibly
  adjust their operations under a more transparent and flexible environment of operating licenses, and reducing the costs for inputs and consumer
  goods to come in, while making it easier for firms to export. The matching grant component seems to offer an opportunity for government to support
  firms in responding to the crisis.
