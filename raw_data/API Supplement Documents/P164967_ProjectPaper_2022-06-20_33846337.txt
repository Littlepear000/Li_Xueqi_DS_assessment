I. PROGRAM STATUS AND RATIONALE FOR RESTRUCTURING

          A. PROGRAM STATUS
Summary.

1. This Project Paper seeks the approval of a restructuring of the Senegalese Agriculture and Livestock Competitiveness
   Program for Results (PCAE) (P164967, Cr.6616) to fix a corrigendum regarding the formulation of the DLI1 and DLI2
   pertaining to the quantity of certified seeds, by adding the thousands of metric tons.

2. The Program Development Objective is to enhance productivity and market access of priority commodity value chains and
   livestock, in the Extended Groundnut Basin and Agro-pastoral Areas. There will be no change to the design of the Program,
   nor to the PDO, nor to the implementation arrangements, nor to DLIs, except adding the mission thousands of metric tons
   in the formulation of DLI1 and DLI2. The result framework will also be revised to account for this change pertaining to the
   quantity of certified seeds.

Implementation status.

3. The Program became effective on September 2, 2020. The Government had been using its own resources to carry out
   the planned activities under the Program, even before the initial advance requested had been granted in April 2021,
   and finally mobilized in December 2021. Implementation is on track and significant progress has been made. A first
   request for payment of some achieved DLIs is pending approval from the Bank. Approval of this request will enhance
   disbursement rate.

          B. RATIONALE FOR RESTRUCTURING
4. There is a mistake in the formulation of the DLI 1 and DLI2 and the result framework (RF), regarding the quantity of
   certified seeds of certified groundnut seed acquired by producers in the Extended Groundnut Basin, and the
   cumulative certified seeds for diversification crops. There is a missing thousand metric tons in the formulation of the
   DLR1 and DLR2, as well as in the formula for disbursement and the RF. This is fixed by adding thousand (000) in the
   formulation.
