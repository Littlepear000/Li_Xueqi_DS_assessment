 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The Program has entered its second year of implementation having achieved initial results and continuing to progress towards the Program
  Development Objective (PDO). The first two Disbursement Linked Results (DLRs) were met. Four more DLRs are under verification by the
  Independent Verification Agent (IVA), who is expected to finalize the verification report by June 2024.
  Advances continued to be most visible towards the objective of increasing access for poor and vulnerable households to safety net programs,
  which surpassed most year 2 targets. The Productive Cash Transfers Program (Programme de Transferts Monétaires Productifs, PTMP) reached
  100,000 beneficiaries with their third and fourth digital cash transfers and initial economic inclusion activities in almost half of the localities. Two new
  cohorts of beneficiaries (75,000 supported by IDA and 30,000 by government resources) have been enrolled, signaling progress toward the
  achievement of DLI 5 and DLI 7. The share of women recipients within the new cohorts (DLI 6) is expected to increase thanks to improved
  sensitization efforts. Partnerships between the PTMP and other social programs are being strengthened (linked to DLI 8). Activities linked to DLI 9
  (Shock-response) have accelerated significantly, ahead of a pilot response to floods expected in the summer of 2024.
  Progress towards the objective of improving the performance of the delivery systems has picked up since the last ISR, despite remaining
  challenges. The operationalization of the Unique Social Registry (RSU) is starting. Preparations for the pilot data collection using the management
  information system deployed in December 2023 (DLI 1) and technical arrangements for integration of existing data (DLI 2) are ongoing, but delayed.
  The pilot aimed at strengthening Social Centers (linked to DLI 3) has not yet started, but a rapid acceleration is expected for the next semester,
  based on a revised concept and scope of activities agreed upon in April 2024.
  Implementation of commitments set in the Environmental and Social Commitment Plan (ESCP) has accelerated significantly, allowing it to meet all
  related legal covenants.
