 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The MPA was approved the World Bank in March 2023, and the Loan Agreement for Phase 1 was signed on June 11, 2023 by the Government of
  India. In early 2022 the Assam Water Resources Department has initiated anti-erosion and embankment upgrading works on the Beki and
  Buridehing Rivers but construction will cease during the monsoon season. It is anticipated that it will take two more construction seasons to
  complete the works. WRD, with assistance from the Flood and River Erosion Management Agency of Assam (FREMAA), has initiated procurement
  of consultancy to upgrade the Assam flood forecasting system and undertake Integrated Flood Risk Management Plans (IFRMPs) in the Beki and
  Buridehing basins.
  The Disaster Risk Management component is still in the start-up phase. The Assam State Disaster Management Authority (ASDMA) has initiated
  procurement for consultants to prepare flood shelter investments and village disaster mitigation plans. It will soon start procuring consultants and

 MPA Table Body
