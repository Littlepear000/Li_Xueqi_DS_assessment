 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The progress towards achievement of PDO is rated as Satisfactory given that there are no material risks observed currently that would undermine
  achievement of the PDO. Implementation progress is also rated as Satisfactory because the Project was declared effective on time, roll out of the
  pilot fiber optic network covering 49 settlements in Ozurgeti municipality (funded by Open Net NNLE) has been completed, and because the
  implementing agencies continue making progress in various activities launched under the Project.
