 Project Development Objectives

  Project Development Objective (from Project Appraisal Document)
  The development objectives of this project are to strengthen participatory local governance, improve the quality of local infrastructure, both in
  targeted communities, and increase extracurricular or livelihood opportunities for youth.

  Has the Project Development Objective been changed since Board Approval of the Project Objective?
  No

 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The implementing agency has launched project activities including: orientation workshops in Khatlon and Gorno-Badakhshan Autonomous Oblast
  (GBAO) and in the project's seven participating districts, the rehabilitation of youth facilities (subcomponent 1c), and the development of training
  materials for the youth inclusive services (subcomponent 2a). The implementing agency has established regional offices in Kulob and Horog,
  almost finished recruiting project staff, and advanced monitoring and evaluation activities. In July 2021, the implementing agency launched
  the baseline survey for the project. In August 2021, the implementing agency will launch the Management and Information System, which will
  include detailed information about the youth facilities that the project will be constructing and rehabilitating, the beneficiary feedback mechanism,
  and the first batch of jamoats that will be participating in the community mobilization for the subgrants for local development initiatives
  (subcomponent 1a).
