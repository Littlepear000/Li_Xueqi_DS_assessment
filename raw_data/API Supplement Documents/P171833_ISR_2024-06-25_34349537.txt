 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The Unleashing the Blue Economy of the Caribbean Series of Projects is under implementation in Grenada, Saint Lucia, Saint Vincent and the
  Grenadines and through the Organization of Eastern Caribbean States Commission which have all successfully established their Project
  Implementation Units and have commenced steady procurement of project activities. In addition, this past year, participating countries have
  transacted US$10 million each in additional financial to address national food insecurity challenges (i.e., in the agriculture and fisheries sectors
  ) brought on by deteriorating global circumstances. Priority activities selected by each country include specific interventions, such as enhanced
  crop, livestock, and safe fishery production, and reduction in food loss and waste to tackle the multidimensional impacts of food insecurity,
  necessitating significant support to build both short-term and long-term resilience.
  .
