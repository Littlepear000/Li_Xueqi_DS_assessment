 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The required preliminary studies for project implementation have been carried out. There is also significant progress under all Components of the
  Project and key activities such as the opening of digital centers (90 digital centers to date), deployment of mobile connectivity sites in 322 rural
  localities, as well as the design of customer centric digital financial services platform are underway.
  Leveraging on the digital centers, the Project enabled more than 30,607 persons to complete mobile financial transactions to this day, out of which
  22,010 female users (representing 71.91% of users of mobile financial transactions), and provided digital skills training to 103,219 persons,
  including 80,775 women (representing 78.25% of training beneficiaries).
