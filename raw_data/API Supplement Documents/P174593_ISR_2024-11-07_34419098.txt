4. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  The overall status of the project is considered satisfactory. The status of each component is as follows:
  Component 1: Institutional Development and Capacity Building for ASDMA and WRD. This component is proceeding
  well. Consultant procurements of key studies (State Water Resources Assessment, Post-Disaster Needs Assessment,
  Multi-Hazard Risk Financing and WRD Integrated Process Management) have substantially been completed.

  Component 2: WRD Water Resources Management. This component is proceeding well. The no-regret river works are
  on track with the expected completion before May 2025. The Flood Consultant is fully onboard and finalized the selection
  of modeling software. Most of the Real-Time Data Acquisition System (RTDAS) stations have been installed. The next
  major activity is the development of flood simulation models by the flood consultant. The consultant procurement of
  Water Resources Asset Management Consultant is in progress.

   Component 3: ASDMA Disaster Management. This component is proceeding well, with improved performance of
  ASDMA after filling the component lead positions. The consultant teams for Flood Shelters, Circle Quick Response Team
  (CQRT), Climate Resilient Village (CRV) and Communications are now onboard. The next major activities include: design
  finalization and tendering of pilot flood shelters and Disaster Resilient Network for Early Warning Dissemination System.

4.3 Multiphase Programmatic Approach (MPA) Status (Public Disclosure)
  This project is the first phase of the three-phase Multiphase Programmatic Approach (MPA). The Program Development
  Objective (PrDO) is to reduce the vulnerability of people to climate-related disasters and improve integrated water
  resources management in Assam. Each phase is designed to be four-year with one-year overlapping period. The
  preparation of the second phase has just been initiated to be approved by end of 2025.
