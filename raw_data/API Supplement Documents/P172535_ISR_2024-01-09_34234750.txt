 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The Loan and Grants for the Project were declared effective on October 5, 2021. The first disbursement was processed in March 2022, to finance
  home improvement interventions in selected municipalities. A second disbursement is being processed to date, for an amount of US $21.5 M with
  cash flow for 12 months. The transfer of resources is undergoing internal approval procedures by the WB.

  The minor restructuring process is in the last link of the GoC's internal process, with approval from APC, and awaiting approval from MHCP by
  December, 2023. The process must be completed and closed in the WB internal system by January, 2024, releasing the GPG Grant (US $ 15 M)
  from the pari passu restriction and reducing the risk of financial management by allowing the use of these resources in a more streamlined manner.

  The GoC and the Bank will continue holding follow-up meetings with the internal teams in the MVCT and the Project Implementing Unit (PIU), where
  the teams of the MHCP, DNP, APC Colombia, and other entities involved in the project also participate. In order to identify concrete actions to

  Component 1: (i) Indicator compliance review for households benefiting from home improvement interventions (ii) Detailed schedule with times for
  start of work, execution and delivery of home improvement works; (iii) Review of other possible execution schemes/models for home improvement
  interventions; (iv) Allocation of the 57 rental subsidies for migrants and initiation of payments; (v) Lessons learned by the MVCT to resume

  procedure with DNP to review the possibility of adjusting indicators associated with People with better urban living conditions.
  Component 2: (i) Concept to DNP for a possible recategorization of the amounts associated with the Project components in; (ii) Consolidation of
  the Credit democratization strategy (WB-IFC): Home improvement credit line for households (that can be articulated with the subsidies granted by
  Fonvivienda) and for the Generation of a line of credit and guarantee to finance the Social Organizations that participate as bidders for projects
  promoted by the GoC
  Component 3: (i) Hring of all specialists of the Project Implementation Unit; (ii) Renewal of the logistics operator's contract to begin site visits; (iii)
  Strategic agendas for visits with new mayors; (iv) Harmonization of the Project communications strategy with the Minister's office.
