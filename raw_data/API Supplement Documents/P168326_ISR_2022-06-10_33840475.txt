 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The PIU and the World Bank project team produced a timebound action plan with clear deliverables and the progress has been recognized on the
  big ticket items such as lifting of the disbursement conditions for the matching grants program, in particular the adoption of the matching grants
  program manual, satisfactory to the Bank. The progress was also recognized for the project in the following areas: (i) designs for public
  infrastructure investment including for Khatlon tourism sites; selection of ten agriculture markets for infrastructure improvement; and needs
  assessment and detailed list of proposed equipment for the food safety labs at markets, and (ii) the development and implementation of capacity
