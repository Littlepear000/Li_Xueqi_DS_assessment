 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  Introduction. With the objective to mitigate the short-term impact of the recent economic shocks on the poor and vulnerable population in both
  urban and rural areas, the government of Nigeria and the World Bank agreed to design a follow up NASSP Scale Up Project, through IDA financing
  of US$ 800 million. The Project Development Objective is to “Expand coverage of shock responsive safety net support among the poor and
  vulnerable and strengthen the national safety net delivery system”. NASSP-SU targets 10.2 million HHs (close to 50 million individuals) and will be
  implemented nationwide.
  Status of implementation. The project was approved by the World Bank Board of Directors on December 16, 2021, and by the Federal Executive
  Council (FEC) in March 2022. The Financing Agreement has been signed and the Legal Opinion was obtained from the Attorney General of the
  Federation confirming that the execution and delivery of the Financing Agreement for NASSP-SU has been ratified by all necessary governmental
  actions. However, the project is yet to become effective, although the necessary technical preparatory activities are in progress. These
  include putting in place the targeting mechanism, cash transfer payment mechanism, environmental and social requirements, and other cross
