 I. PROJECT STATUS AND RATIONALE FOR RESTRUCTURING
1.      The Mali Electricity System Reinforcement and Access Expansion Project (Yelen Sira) ("Project”) was approved by
the International Development Association (Association or IDA) Board on June 23, 2023. The Project has three sources of
financing and three corresponding legal agreements: (i) Financing Agreement between the Republic of Mali (Recipient)
and the Association - Credit No. 7376-ML, Credit No. 7377-ML (IDA Financing Agreement); (ii) Financing Agreement
between the Recipient and the Association acting as an Accredited Entity of the Green Climate Fund - Loan No. TF0C1876-
ML, Grant No. TF0C1681-ML (GCF Financing Agreement); and (iii) Grant Agreement between the Recipient and the
Association acting as an administrator of Energy Sector Management Assistance Program Multi-Donor Trust Fund - (Grant
No. TF0C1586-ML) (ESMAP Grant Agreement), each dated June 30, 2023. The Effective Date for the Project was
anticipated for October 28, 2023, but has been extended to December 29, 2023, at the request of the Recipient. All the
actions under the control of the Project Implementation Units, EDM-SA (Société Energie du Mali), AMADER (Agence
Malienne pour le Développement de l’Energie Domestique et de l’Electrification Rurale), and the National Energy
Directorate (DNE) are progressing well: (i) the Results-Based Financing Subsidiary Agreements have been signed between
AMADER and Participating Operators and are being transmitted to the Public Debt Department of the Ministry of Economy
and Finance (MEF); (ii) the memorandum establishing the EDM-SA PIU is at the level of EDM-SA Human Resources
Department for approval and signature; (iii) the memorandum establishing the AMADER PIU has been signed; (iv) the DNE
has appointed the staff of the central coordination unit awaiting the signature of the Minister of Energy; (vi) on the World
Bank side, the Project Operations Manual was reviewed and sent back to the client .

2.      As part of the Recipient’s internal approval process, ratification of the IDA Financing Agreement, ESMAP Grant
Agreement and the GCF Financing Agreement needs to be completed before the Project can be declared effective. To
move forward with the ratification, the Recipient has requested in its letter (Ref No.03953/MEF-SG) dated November 14,
2023, the deletion of the clause in each of the legal agreements relating to the use of military and security forces under
the project. IDA has agreed to process the deletion of the clause from the legal agreements, with the understanding that
there will be no possibility for the use of military or security forces to implement Project activities or any parts thereof. It
should be noted that the removal of this clause by no means constitutes a precedent for future operations.

 II. DESCRIPTION OF PROPOSED CHANGES

3.    In reference to the letter from the Ministry of Finance dated November 14, 2023, this paper requests specific
amendments to the following legal agreements: (i) IDA Financing Agreement; (ii) GCF Financing Agreement; and (iii)
ESMAP Grant Agreement.

4.     The proposal is to amend the Agreements as set out below.

            a. Deletion of paragraph J of Section I of Schedule 2 to the IDA Financing Agreement (Use of Military and
               Security Forces). The subsequent paragraph K becomes paragraph J.
            b. Deletion of paragraph H of Section I of Schedule 2 to the GCF Financing Agreement (Use of Military and
               Security Actors). The subsequent paragraph I becomes paragraph H.
            c. Deletion of paragraph I of Section I of Schedule 2 to the ESMAP Grant Agreement (Use of Military and
               Security Actors). The subsequent paragraph I (which was erroneously numbered I instead of J in the Grant
               Agreement) becomes paragraph I.

5.     The Project has not yet been declared effective and therefore no activities have started. The above detailed
changes will not affect the Project Development Objective or the activities of the Project.
