 Project Development Objectives

  Project Development Objective (from Project Appraisal Document)
  To (i) strengthen capacity for integrated water resources management in the State; (ii) improve reliability of water services in the Agreste and
  Borborema regions; and (iii) improve the operational efficiency of water and wastewater services in the Joao Pessoa Metropolitan Region.

 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  After 2.5 years of implementation the Borrower, through the Project Management Unit at the State Secretariat for Infrastructure and Water
  Resources (SEIRH), the Project Implementation Unit at the State Company of Water and Sanitation (Companhia de Água e Esgotos da Paraíba,
  CAGEPA), and a coordination team at the State Water Resources Management Executive Agency (AESA), is currently implementing 25 out of
  41 Project's activities. Twelve activities currently undergoing procurement represent the bulk of loan proceeds (85%); upon successful completion of
  procurement and signature of contracts by the second half of calendar year 2023 most of the Project resources should be committed.
  The client and the Bank team agree the PDO remains achievable within the closing date (June 30, 2026) as long as major contracts related to the
  Transparaíba System, the rehabilitation and expansion of the sewerage system in João Pessoa, and the NRW project to reduce physical losses for
  the metropolitan area start their implementation within the next four months. The Bank and the Borrower will be able to assess the need for

 Overall Comments PDO

  The indicator "Annual operation water management plan for the Paraiba river, issued for years 2020, 2021 and 2022" has been met.

  Regarding the indicator "Hydromet stations operational and feeding into the water information system", 31 hydromet stations had been
  installed by May 2023. The target will be fully attained by August 2023,

  The indicator "Non-revenue water reduced in the João Pessoa Metropolitan Region" is progressing pretty well with a total recovery by May
  2023 of 9,246,946 m3/year, which represents 54% of the target (through counterpart funded activities).

  The remaining three PDO indicators will be attained once the infrastructure is concluded and operational, as they relate to the completion of
  two major works (Transparaiba Cariri pipeline system and the construction of the wastewater treatment plant).

 Overall Comments IR

  The intermediary indicator 'Establishment and functioning of NRW water management program in CAGEPA' has been achieved. Another one
  is partially achieved, with execution and monitoring initiated (Grievances and claims addressed and resolved within the agreed time frame).
  Among the remaining 13 intermediary indicators, the ISM warned that 6 are at risk of missing their targets due to the delays in procurement of
  works, while another 7 have medium to high prospects for achievement. As signaled in the previous mission, the RMT confirmed the
  withdrawal of the intermediate indicator 'Number of dams prioritized for future design in the Piranhas-Açu basin'.

  Citizen engagement - Reporting on beneficiary feedback in FY23: the indicator "Level of beneficiary satisfaction with project activities in
  the Transparaíba Water Supply System" has not registered any results yet because the related WB-funded works have experienced delays
  and are still undergoing procurement (Cariri pipeline). However an initial mobilization work has been launched by engaging affected people,
  beneficiaries and stakeholders in the area of the Curimatau system (counterpart funded), which has initiated. A firm has been contracted out to
  accompany the works of the Curimatau system, through home visits, community meetings, school events, engagement with local authorities
  and relevant public servants, information campaigns using local networks, radio broadcasting, and socioeconomic surveys. The contractor´s
  team in the Curimatau area has been mobilized to apply the baseline survey that will assess results on this indicator in the future. The
  methodology for the survey has been approved and the initial launch of the baseline survey will happen in July 2023 (six months before the
  operation of the first sectors). A second round of interviews will take place with the same selected sample six months after the operations start.
