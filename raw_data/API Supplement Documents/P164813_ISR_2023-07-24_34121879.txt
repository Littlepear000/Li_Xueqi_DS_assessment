 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  This is an updated ISR based on Implementation Support Mission conducted May 8-18, 2023 and project progress are summarized below:
       1. Pillar A (Improving the Business Environment). Following progress was achieved (i) launch of the Inventory of Business Licenses (ii)
            The revised Enterprise Law was published, and an independent survey of businesses confirmed that the overall business registration
            process was shortened to 30 days on average, while receiving the Enterprise Registration Certificate (ERC) now takes 08 days on
            average; (ii) Three additional operating licenses were streamlined in e-commerce, industry and handicraft and legal consultancy, bringing
            the total to 10. The simplification consisted of reduction in steps, time to obtain the license as well as removal of minimum capital
            requirement; and (iii) Three new proposals were approved and commenced to support the work on Operating Licenses (Department of
            Industry and Handicraft, Department of Tourism, Department of Public Works and Transport)
       2. Pillar B (Facilitating Trade and Connecting to Markets). Following progress was achieved (i) National Trade Facilitation Committee
            (NTFC) and National Transport Committee (NTC) were merged into National Trade and Transport Facilitation Committee (NTTFC).

              Additional two issues out of fifty-five trade facilitation issues raised by private sector stakeholders at both the national and provincial level
              have now been resolved bringing the total to twenty-eight; (ii) The Time Release Study (TRS) was completed with a final report in Lao
              finalized end of April 2023. TRS results show a modest overall reduction in average clearance times since the last TRS in 2020 (5.12
              hours – 4.85 hours) and a significant reduction in the time taken to obtain licenses and permits. Notable reductions were recorded at
              Boten border checkpoint (-27%) in Luang Namtha province and Friendship Bridge 4 border checkpoint (32%) in Bokeo province; (iii)
              Progress was made by Food and Drug Department (FDD) and Department of Livestock and Fisheries (DOLF) who completed their initial
              product-based risk profiles and contributed to the development of a joint SOP for border inspections.
      3.      Pillar C (Improving Firm-level Competitiveness). Private-sector activity is showing signs of recovery since the shocks of the pandemic
              and then the more recent exchange rate and inflation shocks. Demand for the Second Business Assistance Facility (BAF-2) services has
              also picked up. The cumulative totals for end of April 2023, compared to agreed targets [in brackets] are encouraging: 431 grants
              approved [427]; $2.214m approved [$2.214m]; 325 grants disbursed [336]; $1.527m disbursed [$1.725m]. Provisional results from BAF2
              Impact Assessment shows that sales growth performance for BAF-2 supported firms in the most recent 12-month period was almost
              double that of comparable non-BAF-2 supported firms [60 percent compared to 33 percent] and levels of satisfaction with both the service
              level and the quality of the advice received are both high.
      4.      Pillar D (Supporting Better and More Inclusive Policies). The integrated project level Grievance Redress Mechanism (GRM) based on
              agreed structure and procedure has been operational since January 01, 2023 and no complaints received. Good progress made in the
              dialogue between the government and the private sector on trade and private sector development issues. The 14th Lao Business Forum
              (LBF), held on March 25th 2023, represented an important move forward, based on strong support from the national political leadership. A
              formal structure has now been established, with a small high-level Steering Committee meeting quarterly, under the chairmanship of the
              Deputy Prime Minister.
