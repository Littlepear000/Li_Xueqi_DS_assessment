 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  Progress towards the achievement the (Project Development Objective) PDO has been upgraded to Moderately Satisfactory, with a number of
  PDO and Intermediate Results Indicators met or on track to meet their end targets as well as improvement in the disbursement rate from 25 to
  49 percent since the last ISR (dated June 20, 2023). Underpinning this improved performance was a level two restructuring, leading to an amended
  and restated financing agreement, which was signed on July 4, 2023. The restructuring included: (i) an early closure of the project from June 30,
  2025 to June 30, 2024 and a partial financing cancellation of US$14,276,279; (ii) a revision of the project development objective and results
  framework to ensure realism and strengthen the link between the revised project activities and project indicators; and (iii) a revision of the project

  progress towards achievement of the restructured PDO indicators has improved and 8 out of 10 Intermediate Results Indicators have already
  reached their end targets.

  The overall project Implementation Progress is likewise rated as Moderately Satisfactory given recent improvements in progress, which have been
  recorded over the past year across all three components, including, among others: (i) the completion of the Gambia Revenue Authority (GRA)
  business re-engineering and ITAS tendering as well as the completion of the tax compliance and risk assessment policy; (ii) the passage of the
  GPPA Bill by the National Assembly and the training of 80 procurement officials; (iii) the passage of the SOE Bill by the National Assembly as well
  as the appointment of the SOE Oversight Authority Board of Directors; (iv) the acquisition of the Gambia Public Utilities Regulatory Authority
  (PURA) Quality of Service monitoring tool for 4G, Billing Verification System, the electromagnetic radiation measurement tool, as well as the
  completion of the Spectrum assessment report; (v) the development of an electronic government procurement (e-GP) readiness report; (vi) the
  organization of the market test for GAMTEL/GAMCEL; (vii) the public disclosure of three SOE performance audit reports by the National Audit
  Office; and (vii) the cabinet approval of the national cloud strategy and migration policy. Nevertheless, other project milestones have experienced
  some delays, including government decisions on GAMTEL/GAMCEL privatization options.
