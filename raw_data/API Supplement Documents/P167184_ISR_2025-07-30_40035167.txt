4. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The Project, effective since December 2020, reflects the Government of Papua New Guinea’s commitment to enhancing
  health service delivery, with the National Department of Health prioritizing alignment with the national health plan.
  Progress toward the Project Development Objective (PDO) of increasing quality essential health service utilization in East
  New Britain, Simbu, Central, and East Sepik remains Moderately Satisfactory. Disbursements have reached US$8.21
  million (28% of total funding) as of July 14, 2025, with an additional US$10.96 million projected for Financial Year 2026,
  raising the rate to 63%. In the four provinces, 94% of health facilities (149 of 158) were assessed, with marked
  improvements in the health facility quality scores over the baseline, and 86% are meeting the minimum quality threshold.
  East New Britain Provincial Health Authority, the early adopter province, effectively utilized performance-based funds for
  outreach services (health services delivered in the community), training, and equipment purchase. The Project also
  launched the use of Competitive Disbursement Linked Indicators, which was aimed at incentivizing improvements in
  number of services being provided for antenatal care 4 and pentavalent 3rd dose (21 of 22 of Provincial Health
  Authorities signed a Memorandum of Understanding on hand participating). Significant improvements in antenatal care
  and pentavalent 3rd dose coverage were observed, with 10–13 provinces improving by 1–38% over baselines. Two key
  activities (US$14.2 million) are advancing, including health facility audits, equipment delivery, rehabilitation of health
  facilities and the upskilling of health workers to boost capacity. The next technical mission is set for October 2026.
