 Project Development Objectives

  Project Development Objective (from Project Appraisal Document)
  The project’s development objective is to improve (i) the relevance and excellence of scientific research, and (ii) innovative entrepreneurship and
  access to finance for enterprise growth, as a way of contributing to Serbia’s growth and competitiveness.

  Relevance of research refers to the potential of research results to be commercialized on the market, thus contributing to the economy, while
  excellence refers to international recognition of the quality of the research.

 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

       1.    Overall Project implementation is advancing well, with all components progressing smoothly. Project disbursement stands at 62% overall
             for the SAIGE IPF (67% IBRD loan, 56% IPA grant). To ensure smooth continuation of Project implementation, including the operations of
             the PIU, an extension of twelve months is being considered. This extension will be processed in parallel with the processing of the second
             Additional Financing.
       2.    The Bank discussed extension of the original Project closing date with key stakeholders and NITRA. This 12-month extension will
             ensure uninterrupted implementation of activities whilst the project teams are preparing the Additional Financing.

      3.      Science Fund. Ongoing SF programs and activities are progressing well. The latest programs, PRISMA (30 projects) and Proof of Concept
              (52 projects) started with implementation. The Diaspora program call closed at the end of March, while the AI collaboration vouchers
              program is in preparation. During the mission, the SF celebrated its fifth anniversary, recognizing the pivotal role the institution has played
              in increasing the amount and competitive nature of research funding in Serbia. This event was an opportunity to recognize researchers
              who achieved significant scientific results and made substantial impact through their research projects, and present grant winners of
              PROMIS 2023, SF program for excellent projects of young researchers in Serbia.
      4.      Innovation Fund. The Katapult Acceleration program is advancing well. The level of private investments raised by early-stage firms in the
              first two cohorts of the Katapult acceleration program is well above expectations, currently at $24 million in total, leveraged by $7.7 million
              of public funds disbursed from SAIGE grants. The third cohort (K24) of 23 selected companies (out of 114 applications), participated in
              Residency Week, organized jointly by the Innovation Fund and the World Bank during the mission.
      5.      RDI Transformation. RDI transformation progress is going well. Three (3) RDI Networking Forums were organized by the firm engaged by
              the Bank to deliver R&D capacity building training. Topics covered included 1) Research Data Management and Open Access, 2) Sales,
              Branding, Business Development and Market, and 3) Basics for Horizon Europe Project Preparation. These were well attended and
              followed by networking among RDIs participating in the transformation process. The invitation and selection process for participation in
              these sessions needs to be further streamlined, to ensure best impact and value. RDIs continue to work with their national and
              international consultants and show excellent progress in achieving set milestones, with TT Offices capacity observed as the main
              challenge. Joint study tours are being planned for RDI representatives in the second half of the year.
