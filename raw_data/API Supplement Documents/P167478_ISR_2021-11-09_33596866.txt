 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The Project became effective on July 15, 2020. Important preparatory work has been done, including the submission of terms of reference (TORs)
  linked to activities that are critical to reach Disbursement-Linked Indicators (DLIs) and significant progress has been made on the evaluation and
  selection of consultants. However, the military coup of September 5, 2021 led to suspension of the constitution and public institutions. As a result,
  the Bank Operation Policy OP. 7.30 “Dealing with de Facto government” was triggered on September 9, 2021 and Bank operations were put on
  hold. The permanents secretary of Ministries has been designated to handle “current activities”. Now a Prime Minister and Permanent Secretary of
  Presidency have been nominated by the new President Colonel Mamady Doumbouya as well as 16 (out of 25) other keys Ministers including all
  Education Minister (Basic Education, TVET and Higher Education), health, security and defense. This is a promising political development,
  however, a new government is not yet been fully formed.

  The specific achievements to date are: (i) the PCU is fully staffed with the recent recruitment of an internal auditor and now fully operational; (ii) an
  independent verification agency for the verification of DLIs has been selected; (iii) the evaluation and selection of an external auditor has been
  completed (iv) the recruitment of an engineer has been completed; (v) the evaluation report of a consultant to support the government in finalizing
  the draft ECE policy is completed; (vi) an international consultant in charge of review and development of technical recommendations for the scholar
  canteens initiative has been recruited and a draft report issued; (vii) an evaluation report for the recruitment of a firm for f monitoring and supervision
  of 1,000 preschool classrooms has been completed; (viii) the recruitment of a consultant to revise the standards of construction is at evaluation
  stage; and (ix) implementation of school-related construction activities have started with support from a consultant architect that is working with the
  MEN-A to finalize the site list which is expected by November 2021.
