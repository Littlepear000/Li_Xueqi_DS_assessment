 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The last Implementation Support Mission (ISM) was carried out during September 4 - 8, 2023. Overall progress toward achieving the Project
  Development Objective (PDO) and the overall implementation progress were assessed as Moderately Satisfactory. All Project Development
  Objective (PDO) indicators are on track to be achieved by the end of the project life, although one indicator couldn't be measured awaiting the
  finalization of the financial statements for FY 22/23. Four out of six Intermediate Results Indicators (IRIs) have been achieved, one is in progress to
  be achieved for FY 23/24 and one, the IRI on implementation on improved disclosures in consolidated financial statements requires follow up
  although promising progress has been noted. Key status of the components is summarized below:

  • Component 1- PFM IT and Systems Roll-Out: IFMIS roll out activities continue to progress well. Efforts are underway to capture information at
  primary school level which extends the use of the system at service delivery level. The Government is enhancing IFMIS and reinforcing its capacity

  to support IPSAS implementation. Based on user requirements prepared by the IPSAS team, several modules in IFMIS have been configured
  (including the Debt, Financial Reporting and Consolidation and Asset Management modules). The internal audit function is being automated. The
  revised Standard Chart of Accounts (SCoA) once approved, will underpin revisions to the system in the future.

  • Component 2: Accounting and Financial Reporting: Progress under this component remains Moderately Unsatisfactory. However, promising
  initiatives have been introduced, which could potentially remedy the situation. These include the amendment of the contract agreement with the
  consulting firm; preparation of revised implementation roadmap and reinforcement of the capacity of MINECOFIN to fast track the implementation of
  the roadmap. Detailed remedial actions have been recommended and need to be urgently implemented.

  • Component 3: Performance-Based Budgeting (PBB) and Medium -Term expenditure Framework (MTEF): Satisfactory Progress continues
  to be made under this component. PBB has been rolled out to each sector in accordance with the PFM law and performance indicators are now
  included as Annex 10 of the budget estimates. Monitoring and evaluation reports (based on these defined performance indicators) are fully
  generated from IFMIS with further link required for non-financial indicators which is currently handled manually. The use of MTEF base line costing
  has started in FY 23/24. The citizen’s guide to the budget is developed each year and MINECOFIN carries out a comprehensive citizen
  engagement process during the budget preparation and dissemination.

  • Component 4-Professionalization of PFM Staff-PFM Capacity Development: Satisfactory progress continues to be made under this
  component as the target for FY 23 has already been achieved. Additional interventions to increase the uptake of professional opportunities are
  underway, including expanding the areas of professionalization and introducing support to high performing students in Universities through
  Memorandum of Understanding arrangements. However, with the leadership of MINECOFIN, dialogue should be initiated within government to
  develop a strategy on sustainably carrying out the professionalization agenda after the project closure.
