 4. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


  4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
The Project has made notable progress across all components:

Under Component 1 (Regional Development), the Regional Strategies for both the Osh Region and the City of Osh have
been officially adopted. These strategies serve as foundational documents, providing a coordinated and comprehensive
framework to guide the region’s future development efforts.

Under Component 2 (Productive Partnerships), all 25 targeted Productive Partnerships (PPs) have been identified and are
at various stages of implementation, across eight application rounds. Six PPs are already operational. Notably, two
partnerships focused on apples and poultry were formally launched on July 6, 2024, during an event attended by the Deputy
Chairman of the Cabinet of Ministers and the Minister of Agriculture—marking important milestones for agricultural
development.

In Component 3 (Catalyzing Investments for Tourism and Urban Development), construction activities are progressing
well. Several major works—such as the Ak-Bura River Embankment Enhancements, Central Square Revitalization, and
Summer Theater Rehabilitation—are nearing completion, while the renovation of the park surrounding the Drama Theater has
already been finalized. Additional works, including a new pedestrian bridge and the rehabilitation of a stadium and adjacent
park in Uzgen, are ongoing and expected to be completed in 2025.

Under Component 4 (Supporting Tourism and Rural Enterprise Development through Small Grants), the Small Grants
Program has engaged 181 participants to date, with grant agreements totaling US$1.7 million already signed. The delivery of
goods and equipment to recipients is currently underway, providing tangible support to rural enterprises and enhancing the
tourism potential of participating communities.
