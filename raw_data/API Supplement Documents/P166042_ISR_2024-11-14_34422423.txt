1. OBJECTIVE


 1.1 Development Objective
   Original Development Objective (Approved as part of Approval package on 12-Jul-2018)
   The Project Development Objectives are to: (i) increase electricity supply to the Eastern part of Guinea; (ii) enable
   electricity trade between Guinea and Mali; and (iii) increase Guinea’s electricity export capability towards other West
   African Power Pool countries.
   Has the Development Objective been changed since Board Approval of the Project Objective?

   No

4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  As of September 30, 2024, the cumulative disbursement stands at US$29.49 million, representing 39%, of which the Guinean
  side stands at 33% and that of Mali is 72%. The project was restructured in last June and the closing date was extended
  by eighteen (18) months, that will allow the full disbursement of the remaining amount by closure, December 31, 2025.
  Detailed implementation progress:
  Component 1: Power transmission infrastructure (US$71.8 million)
      • Construction of the section of the transmission line Siguiri-border Mali/Guinea (54 Km): The contract initial amount
          of EUR 7,299,714.00 was increased by EUR 2,232,604 representing a price adjustment of 30,58%.
  The overall progress rate of the works (Engineering and Design, Supply and Construction) stands at 73,63%.. However,
  progress has been made since the restructuring: 91 excavations out of 167 realized; installation of 71 bases and foundations
  out of 167; erection of 35 pylons out of 167. Because of floods due to heavy rains in july and August, works were suspended
  by almost the two months and could only resume in September. As result, the implementation schedule was revised and the
  completion of the section of the transmission line is now expected by April 30, 2025.
      • Construction of the Siguiri 225/30 kV substation: The initial contract value is EUR 6,299,169.60 and GNF
          18,756,169,819.18 followed by a price adjustment of EUR 1,600,552.82 (25.41%).
  The overall progress rate is 60.38% as of September 30, 2024. In addition to delays in delivery design studies and plans, the
  contractor was not able to timely renew the expired bank guarantee and therefore could not be paid by the project. Works
  have resumed allowing to reach the progress rate of 60.38%. The main electrical equipment including the transformer was
  manufactured and delivery in Conakry is expected by November 30, 2024. Due to floods resulting from heavy rains between
  July and August 2024, works were suspended during almost the two months. Consequently, the implementation schedule
  was revised and provides the completion of works by February28, 2025.
      • Construction of the 225/30 kV substation in Kankan: With an initial contract of 9,358,505.21 USD, a price
          adjustment of 3,808,560.16 USD, representing 40.69% of the initial contract was made.
  At the end of September 2024, the overall progress was 69.55%. During the last implementation mission, the team noted low
  pace of progress due to inadequacy in the mobilization of resources (material and human). Since then, the contractor has
  deployed adequqte staff and machine on sites that allowed to reach the progress rate of 69%. The main electrical equipment
  including the transformer have been shipped and are expected to be delivered in Conakry by November 30, 2024. The
  completion date of the work is scheduled for February 28, 2025.
      • Construction of the 225/30 kV substation in Kérouané: An adjustment of 4,658,983.59 has been made to the initial
          contract of USD 10,560,000, meaning an increase of 44.11%. As of September 30, 2024, the work progress stood
          at 65.39%, The main equipment of the substation, including the transformer, was already delivered on site Mars
          2023. The substation will be completed by February 28, 2025.
      •
      • Extension of the Sanankoroba substation in Mali: (i) Contract 1: Between the Mali Electricity Sector Improvement
          Project 3 (PASEM) and TATA for an amount of US$ 2,574,037.72 (design and installation services); and (ii)
          Contract 2: Between PIEGM and TATA for an amount of 5,498,779.5 USD (Materials and equipment of foreign
          origin). Since January 2024, the execution of work has accelerated and reached the current rate of 69%. The main
          equipment of the substation including the transformer were delivered in Mali early October 2024. The completion
          of the extension work is scheduled for June 2025.

  Component 2: Support for project implementation
     • Recruitment of a Consultant for the study of the connection of Guinean mining companies to the national
        interconnected grid in Guinea: The data collection report was submitted for validation. The final report is expected
        end March 2025.
