 Project Development Objectives

  Project Development Objective (from Project Appraisal Document)
  The Proposed Project Development Objective is to improve the national statistical capacity of Ecuador in the production and dissemination of timely
  and high-quality economic and sociodemographic statistics for evidence-based policymaking.

  Has the Project Development Objective been changed since Board Approval of the Project Objective?
  No

 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The Loan was approved by the World Bank’s Board on June 30, 2022 and the Loan Agreement is pending signature of the Republic of
  Ecuador. The counterpart is working on the conditions of effectiveness together with the World Bank team, in order to achieve the effectiveness of
  the project as soon as the LOA is signed.
  The effectiveness conditions status is as follow: (a) the Operational Manual has been finalized and approved by the Bank and is in adoption process
  by INEC; (b) the Labor Management Procedures have been prepared, consulted upon, approved by the Bank and in adoption process by INEC; (c)
  the PMU establishment is the effectiveness condition with less progress. INEC is still in adoption process of the ADMIN STEP system to upload the
  procurement plan, and send PMU’s ToRs for the WB non-Objection through the system, and start the specialists hiring process, and (d) the
  MIDENA Inter-Institutional Agreement condition has been entered into between INEC and MIDENA under terms and conditions acceptable to the
