 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  In the last 6 months, the GETP has made strong progress towards achieving its PDO of increasing private investment and firm growth in non-
  resource-based sectors following the successful Mid-Term Review which took place from December 5-8, 2022. The Progress towards
  achievement of the Project Development Objective and Implementation Progress remains Moderately Satisfactory. The project has made some
  progress towards two out of four PDO results indicators. The project implementation progress includes:
  Component 1: Enabling investments:(Cost $40.00 M):
  This sub-component has achieved modest results to date, mostly driven by regulatory reforms coordinated by the Business Regulatory Reform
  (BRR) unit of the Ministry of Trade and Industry (MOTI). For example, the digitization of the fire processing permit has reduced the time to get the
  required pre-construction approvals. Moreover, the BRR strengthened public-private dialogue launching the e-feedback platform. The Office of
  Registrar of Companies (ORR) was established and, the National Quality Policy and the Ghana Standards Act were approved in 2022.

  Component 2: Crowding-in investments: Promoting spatial and industrial planning and development:(Cost $50.00 M)
  The works at the Tema Export Processing Zone are completed, including the i) rehabilitation of the 1.6km access road from AMP Logistics – Cocoa
  Tuton; ii) rehabilitation of 1.75km existing main access road; iii) rehabilitation of 0.5km Niche Cocoa – Melcom road; and iv) construction of 295
  perimeter wall fence.
  Component 3: Accelerating entrepreneurship and MSME growth:(Cost $99.00 M):
  This sub-component achieved several key results, most notably the establishment of the Ghana Enterprises Agency (GEA) and the Ghana Venture
  Capital and Private Equity Association (GVCA). The GEA’s COVID-19 Recovery Grant (CRG) scheme awarded over 600 Small and Medium
  Enterprises (SMEs) that were negatively affected by the pandemic. The SME Growth Scheme was launched in January 2023 and will support 1,100
  SMEs through a firm performance assessment, technical assistance and/or grants. The Venture Capital Trust Fund established the Startup Catalyst
  Fund and Strategic Industries Fund and made initial investments in general partners.
