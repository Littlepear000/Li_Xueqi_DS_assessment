               SENEGAL HIGHER EDUCATION PROJECT - ESPOIR-JEUNES (P178750)



  The Senegal Higher Education Project – ESPOIR-Jeunes was approved by the World Bank’s Board (WB) of Executive
  Directors on June 29, 2023, signed on October 6, 2023, and became effective on December 21, 2023. The project
  development objective (PDO) is to increase equitable access to market-relevant short-term vocational tertiary education
  and strengthen governance and research in higher education and the project is expected to benefit over 400,000 young
  people in Senegal. It consists of a credit in the amount of US$150 million from the International Development Association
  (IDA), a US$1.9 million grant from the Early Learning Partnership multi-donor Trust Fund, and co-financing from the
  Agence Française de Développement (AFD) in the amount of US$55 million, for a total project financing envelope of
  US$206.9 million.

  The project consists of three components:

  Component 1: Diversifying and increasing access to tertiary technical and vocational education for improved
  employability of Youth. Component 1 seeks to improve equitable access to higher education programs by building,
  equipping, and supporting eight additional ISEPs throughout Senegal which offer market-relevant programs, as well as
  improving pedagogical approaches in each of the centers. The sites to host the ISEPs in Bignona, Richard Toll and
  Tambacounda are confirmed. For an additional site (Kedougou) the site selection was confirmed, and paperwork is
  pending. An additional two site proposals have been validated (Louga and Kaffrine) and topographic surveys, and
  documentation, are pending. Lastly, for Kolda and Sedhiou the proposed sites are being adjusted, as the original site
  proposals were not found to be suitable. The ISEP training needs survey is now finalized and will inform the design of the
  ISEPs. Component 1 supports key reforms and capacity building of the 3FPT to ensure an adaptive model based on ISEP
  needs, as well as facilitation of 3FPT support to the labor market insertion of ISEP students. To date, 1,208 youth have
  received training, including 100 in Higher Diploma of Professional Education - DISEP programs, 1,083 in CCP (Certificat de
  Compétences Professionnelles) and attestation programs, and 25 in specialized certification programs.

  Component 2: Improving the Management and Governance of the Higher Education System. Component 2 seeks to
  promote tertiary education, entrepreneurship and research that respond to development challenges and improve the
  governance and efficiency of the higher education system through performance-based contracts with public universities.
  All PBCs are expected to be signed in June 2025. Furthermore, the National Strategic Plan for Research and Innovation
  (2023-2032) has been validated and will be reflected into the principles of the competitive fund, as well as the associated
  research indicator in the PBCs.

  Component 3: Strengthening Project Management and Monitoring and Evaluation. Component 3 supports the
  necessary project management and monitoring and evaluation costs, as well as technical assistance. In addition, the
  childcare market survey funded under a WB-executed trust fund has been completed and the findings will inform the
  childcare center design.
