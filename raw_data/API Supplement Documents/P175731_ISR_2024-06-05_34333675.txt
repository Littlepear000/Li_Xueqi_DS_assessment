 Project Development Objectives

  Project Development Objective (from Project Appraisal Document)
  The Project Development Objective (PDO) is to strengthen the institutional capacity of SADC and participating countries to produce, disseminate
  and use quality statistics while increasing regional harmonization and collaboration.

 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  With all financing agreements now being effective, the project implementation has begun. SADC-organized SADC Regional Statistics project
  launch and training on results-based mechanisms and the National Statistical Development Strategy (NSDS) is taking place during April 22-26.
  The SADC Secretariat and INE-Mozambique hosted a four-day event for its 16 Member States’ National Statistical Offices and the Statistical
  Capacity Building Project’s beneficiaries (SADC, Comoros, Madagascar, Mozambique, Malawi, São Tomé and Príncipe, and Zambia).

  The main objectives of the Regional Project Launch workshop were to:
            Provide capacity building on the SADC results-based framework to ensure alignment of national policy frameworks to regional statistics;
            Take stock of the status of the development of National Strategy for Development of Statistics (NSDSs);
            Present the monitoring strategy for the SADC Protocol on Statistics;
            Present each country’s project activities and discuss their relevance to the project PDO;
            Present the risks identified for each country PIU during project preparation and their strategy to manage them;
            Present PIUs communications’ strategies including citizen engagement, outreach to data users, SEP, and digital communication.
            Set up a coordination mechanism to provide and receive information between the SADC PIU and the participating countries’ PIUs and
             agree on required tasks and timelines.
  Below is an update on each country and SADC.
  SADC (IDA-E1870)
  SADC financing agreement has been effective since August 29, 2023. The planned activities are underway, namely TA activities with Mauritius and
  Madagascar and 3 upcoming workshops: 1) National Statistical Development Plan; Statistics Protocol, and SADC project launch. 2) Poverty
  measurement. 3) Gender statistics. Disbursement is at 5.6%.
  Zambia (IDA-73360)
  The financing agreement for the project became effective on December 19, 2023. Project implementation has started with some delays. The Project
  Implementation Unit (PIU) setup is in progress but not yet finalized. Disbursement is at 6.7%.
  Madagascar (IDA-73250)
  The financing agreement was declared effective on October 18, 2023. The project is in the initial phase of implementing the 2024 Annual Work Plan
  and Budget (AWPB) activities. An interim GRM is being set up before the main GRM becomes operational. Disbursement rate is at 2.8%.
  São Tomé and Príncipe (IDA-E1890)
  The financing agreement became effective on February 14, 2024, and no activities have started yet, with the exception of the project launch and the
  annual work plan approval. Given that the INE is implementing the census during 2024, it is expected that the major activities involving data
  collection will not take place during this year. GRM is expected to be operational by May 30. Disbursement rate is at 5.7%.
  Comoros (IDA-E1860)
  Comoros Financing Agreement has been effective since August 29, 2023. At present, PIU is almost fully staffed. The GRM has not yet been
  finalized but is scheduled for the upcoming months. Disbursement rate is currently at 38%, and it is anticipated that the total disbursement rate will
  exceed 80% by the end of the calendar year, particularly following the commencement of the Household Survey, expected to take effect by end of
  April.
  Malawi (IDA-E1900):
  The financing agreement became effective on October 25th, 2023. The project used the project preparation advance (PPA) to initiate recruitment
  process of the PIU, procure equipment for the NSO staff, and start acquiring equipment for upcoming surveys in 2024, along with establishing
  steering committees for each survey. The NSO has prepared a GRM, which will be in place by the end of May once the team starts the
  fieldwork. Disbursement rate is at 41%.
  Mozambique (IDA-E2700)
  Additional Financing for Mozambique was approved on December 5, 2023 and became effective March 7, 2024. The project is currently in the midst
  of hiring Procurement, Financial Management, and Accounting Officers for the PIU.
