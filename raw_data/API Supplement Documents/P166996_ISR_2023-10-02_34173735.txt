 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The project has made significant implementation progress across all components and progress toward the achievement of the project development
  objectives.
  Component 1: As of August 31, 2023, CTVET, has successfully developed and approved ten Competency Based Training (CBT) packages and
  achieved the DLI 1.1 (a) of ten additional Competency-Based Training packages developed. CTVET is also finalizing the development of 23

  additional Competency-based training packages by December 2023 against a target of 20 CBT packages for achieving DLI 1.2 (a). Progress on the
  rollout of the formalized apprenticeship training for 25,000 individuals by the end of the project is, however, delayed. By the end of year two, the
  CTVET has not started the training for the target 5,500 individuals under a formalized, standardized, and quality-assured system.
  Component 2a: The progress includes successfully awarding 104 grants under the four windows of the Ghana Skills Development Fund (GSDF)
  and achieving the DLIs 5.1 (a), 5.1 (b), and 5.1 (c) related to the awarding of 100 grants. CTVET achieved DLI 4.2 (a) and 4.2 (b) related to
  managing and operating GSDF through a private sector firm in Year 2. Simultaneously, CTVET launched the 2nd Call for Proposals for Grants
  under GSDF on August 1, 2023. The call closed on September 7, 2023, and CTVET received a total of 1600 applications.
  Component 2b: As of August 02, Ghana Enterprise Agencies has provided basic training on entrepreneurship support program under component
  2a to 23,630 beneficiaries over 261 districts. 7,907 beneficiaries moved to intermediate training, while 4,345 undertook advanced training. Selected
  beneficiaries have been sent a portal link to start their applications for grants. GEA is currently reviewing the report submitted by the private firm on
  the beneficiary assessment conducted at the end of the training.
  Component 3: The Ghana Labour Market Information System (GLMIS) web-portal (www.glmis.gov.gh) is currently ‘live’ although backend
  interface work is still ongoing.
  Component 4: The component continues to facilitate administrative, procurement, financial management capacity building, and project oversight
  operations.
  A workshop is scheduled between Oct 2-6, 2023 in Accra, including the discussion on the planning of the Mid-Term Review , proposed to take place
  in early February 2024. A full ISR will be submitted by then.
