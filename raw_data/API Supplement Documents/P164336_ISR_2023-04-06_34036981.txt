 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  This ISR is issued following the Mid-Term Review Mission held on January 10 - 26, 2023 and reflects the progress made and results attained since
  the approval of the project in November, 2019 till to date.

  Progress towards the Project Development Objective (PDO): To date good progress has been registered against the PDO-level result
  indicators. The major achievements against three of the four PDO-level indicators include: a) Implementation of sustainable land management
  practices on 248,179 hectares of land, (130 percent of the MTR target), b) productivity of selected Crop and Livestock commodities increased by 14
  percent (exceeding the MTR target of 10 percent by 40 percent), and c) Reached 86,147 pastoralist and agro-pastoralists with agricultural assets
  and services. Data collection on the performances of the fourth PDO indicator on Biomass Index Corrected for Weather Variability (NDVI) was
  competed recently. Once the analysis is completed and ground truthing is done, the findings of the assessment and results achieved will be
  communicated along with the fourth quarterly progress report by end of June 2023

  In May 2020, upon the request of the Government of Ethiopia, US$125 million was cancelled from the original IDA allocations to cope up with
  macro-economic impacts of COVID-19 pandemic in the country. The intent was to replenish the cancelled amount through additional financing by
  FY24 or earlier. Therefore, the project has been operating under financing gap. However, all options of additional financing have not been
  successful. Thus, the possibility of fully achieving the PDO becomes unrealistic and hence the progress towards achieving the PDO is rated as
  Moderately Satisfactory. To address the financing gap, project restructuring is currently going on and will be completed well before June 2023.

  Implementation Progress (IP): The project encountered multiple challenges over the past three years, including COVID-19 pandemic, drought,
  flood, dessert locust, the spillover effect of the armed conflict in Tigray and localized conflicts in Benshangul and Oromiya regions, high inflation,
  and shortage of construction materials and inputs such as cement reinforcement bar and fuel. Nevertheless, good implementation progress has
  been made across most component activities . Most of the intermediate result targets are met, while few remain behind schedule. The mid-term
  assessment conducted in January 2023, concluded that the Implementation Progress (IP) has been Moderately Satisfactory.

  To date, the project has benefited more than 2 million Pastoralists and Agro-Pastoralists (PAPs), (82 percent of the End-of-Project target) of which
  47 percent and 15 percent are female and youth beneficiaries respectively.
