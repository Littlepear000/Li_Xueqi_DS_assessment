 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The project has been effective since October 5, 2020. Between approval and effectiveness, the longer-term impact of COVID-19 on infrastructure
  projects became clearer, and several infrastructure sub-projects in the pipeline did not seem realistic. COVID-19 severely weakened the ability of
  energy utilities to enter into power purchasing agreements with private investors, as a result several planned transactions were put on ice. While
  TDB has managed to include several new deals in the pipeline, COVID-19 has impacted the ability to undertake infrastructure investments.
  The off-grid credit line was officially launched last Spring, and one transaction was approved. TDB has developed the Renewable Energy Deal
  Room to facilitate access to renewable energy pipeline in the region. This deal room has been instrumental in driving awareness of component 2
  through targeted digital marketing. Several other transactions are in the pipeline.
  On the technical assistance managed by COMESA, the recruitment of consultants for major consultancies is ongoing. The consultancy for
  the regional off grid renewable energy market assessment; and quality assurance standards for off grid energy products and common tariff
  framework for COMESA region, with the expectation to have draft standards and draft tariff framework by early 2022.
