 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The Project was approved and Loan documents were signed in August 2018. Project implementation efforts at this early stage focus on preliminary
  works on the Calle 72 road interchange (intercambiador vial) and the Railyards (patiotaller). The preliminary works complete a 10.7 percent
  advance as of January 2022, in line with the implementation schedule. Resettlement activities are advancing well (84% of the 1,421 properties have
  received purchase offers), despite delays in EMB delivering properties to the concessionaire (of 34 percent properties demolished, only 6% have
  been delivered to the concessionaire). Anticipated utility transfer is advancing well, with 44 out of 123 utility transfer activities completed and 42 in
  progress (70 percent). EAAB, the water utility company, is yet to begin work on 10 utility transfer activities, which, if further delayed, might affect the
  project implementation critical path. The Project Supervision consultant (interventoria) is performing well and submitting the supervision reports to
  EMB.

 Overall Comments PDO

  Indicator 2 has been updated to track the overall progress of two preliminary worksites: The railyards (as tracked originally) and the Calle 72
