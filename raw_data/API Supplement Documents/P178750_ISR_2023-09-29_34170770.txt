 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The Senegal Higher Education Project – ESPOIR-Jeunes was approved by the World Bank’s Board of Executive Directors on June 29, 2023, and is
  expected to be signed by October 15, 2023. Effectiveness is expected by mid-late January, 2024.
  Recruitment of key staff is well underway, as are several key preparatory studies which will inform the design of the regional ISEPs, including both
  the soft (mainly pedagogical) elements, as well as the hard elements (infrastructure, equipment, etc).
  The government is also making progress on the development of the key implementation manuals which include the project implementation manual,
  performance-based contract manual, and competitive fund manual. Project activities are expected to begin just after effectiveness.
