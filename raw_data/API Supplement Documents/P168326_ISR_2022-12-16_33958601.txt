 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The PIU and the World Bank project team produced a time-bound action plan with clear deliverables and the progress has been recognized on the
  big ticket items such as the launch and implementation of the community-based and matching grants program with close to 150 applications
  approved. The construction works were launched for two cultural heritage sites in Khatlon and the contract was signed with a local construction
  company to launch the work on the last remaining cultural site under the project in Khatlon. The diagnostic and design for the Yamchun Fortress
  rehabilitation have been completed to proceed with the adoption of the design by the relevant governmental authorities. The latter is the final step
  before the hiring of a construction company to launch the rehabilitation works.
