 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
In this ISR, progress towards the achievement of the Project Development Objective (PDO) remains rated Moderately Satisfactory. The rating
is consistent with the following results: (i) as of December 2024, 69% progress of field surveyance for Area 1 and 83% for Area 2 has been
achieved; (iii) supervision and quality control is accompanying cadastral production in both areas; (iv) the first stage of the production of the
cadastral information platform (SICUN) is fully completed; stage 2 and 3 are expected to be concluded by September 2025 and March 2026,
respectively; (v) disbursements have increased, totaling US$ 8.33 million as of December 2024 (17% of the total US$ 50 million budget); (vi)
All project municipalities included in Cadastral Survey Areas 1 and 2 are being provided with direct technical assistance for institutional
strengthening by the Project according to the Cadaster Implementation Plan; (vii) the guidelines to ensure urban cadasters linked to hazard
data are being prepared (71% achieved, 5 out of 7 deliverables); and (viii) grievances and complaints have been resolved in three days
average per case (97% of resolution). Implementation Progress continues to be rated Moderately Satisfactory: Implementation
arrangements are working well, with the Project under the leadership of the Agency for the Formalization of Informal Property (Órgano de
Formalización de la Propiedad Informal, COFOPRI in Spanish). In addition, the Project is rated Satisfactory in compliance with social
and environmental safeguards, Moderately Satisfactory in financial management, Satisfactory in project management, and
Satisfactory in procurement.

Delays in the completion of cadastral activities in Areas 1 and 2, as well as the procurement process of an Area resulting in no bidders, have
required that the PIU introduces a series of changes to the implementation plan to ensure the completion of all activities within the Project
timeframe.
