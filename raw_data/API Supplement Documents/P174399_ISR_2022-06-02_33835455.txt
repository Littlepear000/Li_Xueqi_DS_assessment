 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The Program was approved on February 3rd, 2022, and became effective on March 30th, 2022. The Program has successfully disbursed USD 2
  million under the Ministry of Labor and Human Resources (MOLHR) pertaining to prior results under DLIs 5 and 8. The disbursement against prior
  results under the Ministry of Education (MOE) was not possible as the implementation of teacher training was impossible due to the COVID-19
  outbreak. The program will be restructured to allow for the disbursement against the relevant Disbursement Linked Result (DLR) once the teacher
  training is implemented in summer 2022.
  Both ministries have developed work plans to meet the first-year DLIs and PAP actions on time.
