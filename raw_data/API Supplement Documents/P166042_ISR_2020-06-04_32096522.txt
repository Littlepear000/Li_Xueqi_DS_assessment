 Project Development Objectives

  Project Development Objective (from Project Appraisal Document)
  The Project Development Objectives are to: (i) increase electricity supply to the Eastern part of Guinea; (ii) enable electricity trade between Guinea
  and Mali; and (iii) increase Guinea’s electricity export capability towards other West African Power Pool countries.

  Has the Project Development Objective been changed since Board Approval of the Project Objective?
  No

 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  As of May 31, 2020, the disbursement of the project stands at 6.8%. There has been steady progress until March 2020, when tender documents of
  major contracts for the construction of 715 kilometers of high voltage transmission lines and six substations were launched, but due to the COVID19
  situation, deadlines for the submission of bids have been extended twice to allow potential bidders undertake site visits, obtain quotations from
  equipment manufacturing industries that are locked down, to be able to submit credible offers.
  COVID-19 has affected the project implementation resulting slower pace of disbursement than originally planned. New project implementation
  schedule will be prepared as soon as the business returns to the normal to ensure that the overall project implementation period is not significantly
  affected.
