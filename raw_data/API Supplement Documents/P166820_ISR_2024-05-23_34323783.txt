 Project Development Objectives

  Project Development Objective (from Project Appraisal Document)
  The Project development objectives of the Fourth Phase of the Central Asia Regional Links Program are to enhance the efficiency of cross-border
  trade for participants of the regional economy and to improve the resilience and safety of regional connectivity infrastructure in Sughd region and
  Gorno-Badakhshan Autonomous region.

  The project contributes to the Central Asia Regional Links Programs higher-level development objectives of increasing cross-border connectivity
  and enhancing integrated regional development to revitalize historically active economic exchanges in Central Asia and beyond along the Silk
  Route.

  Has the Project Development Objective been changed since Board Approval of the Project Objective?
  No

 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The project is making steady progress with current disbursements at 30% of the project allocation.
  Component 1. Improve regional connections in Sughd and GBAO Regions: The contract for the construction of four (4) road sections in Sughd
  was signed and awarded on November 25, 2022. The civil works construction started on April 1, 2023. The contract for civil works for Khorog Bridge

  and Avalanche damaged road section in Barsem village of GBAO region was signed on March 27, 2023. Mobilization and initial construction
  activities have started from June 1, 2023. Supervision services are mobilized on both construction sites.
  Component 2. Improve road asset preservation and road safety: The critical Road Asset Management System (RAMS) development contract
  was signed in July 2023. RAMS is an integral part of the MOT efforts towards digitization of their systems and shall lead towards the ultimate vision
  of financial efficiency in the road sector maintenance. During the reporting period, the RAM Strategy was drafted and reviewed by MOT. The
  drafting of the organizational structure for the RAMS Unit was completed. A market sounding exercise was designed and launched in March 2024 to
  receive statement of interest from the leading RAMS providers and obtain preliminary information on RAMS functionality, magnitude of costs, and
  implementation timescale. Training sessions were organized on Road Data Collection and RAMS Database, Road Maintenance Planning and
  Programming, and Highway Economics and HDM-4. On the government vision of safer roads, the mission recognized that that there were
  additional opportunities that could be addressed under this sub-component. The project is focusing now on installation of safety barriers and to
  implement a road safety pilot section along the original identified location. This shall serve as future reference for the overall road safety
  enhancements in all MOT projects.
  Component 3. Facilitate cross-border movement of goods: This component's activities are advancing effectively, notably the UNCTAD
  Agreement, which was signed one year ago: "Provision of Technical Assistance for Migration to ASYCUDA World and the Implementation of
  Customs Reform". It has made significant progress since the previous update and top-level representatives of the CS and Government confirmed
  their strong commitment towards Customs reform and digitalization. Custom Services organized a high-level ceremony on July 26, 2023, to
  announce the start of ASYCUDA-Tajikistan piloting and to draw attention of a broad circle of stakeholders on the system’s advantages, regional
  connectivity, and next steps of the project. The task team acknowledges the progress made in the pilot implementation and the modules rolled out,
  recording improvements in processing. The features rolled out include assessment of duties and taxes, payment calculation, declaration
  processing, inspection act, border control modules etc. Some of the actions identified to improve the performance of the pilot include improving
  communication channels, upgrading equipment, and enhancing internet connectivity for all Customs posts and regional offices using fiber-optic
  connectivity. UNCTAD is responsible to implement their contract in a duration of 36 months from the date of signing and make ASYCUDA fully
  operational.
