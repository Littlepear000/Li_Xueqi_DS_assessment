 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The Strengthening Institutions for Refugee Administration Project was made effective on March 31, 2021. Component 1 - Performance-based
  Implementing Organizational and Institutional Reforms is assessed as Moderately Satisfactory with the progress reported against Disbursement
  Linked Indicators (DLIs). Conversely, Component 2 is rated Unsatisfactory due to delays in key activities, including the engagement of a Third-
  Party Validation (TPV) entity to validate achievements.
  Progress reported to date includes of results under: (a) DLI 1 - improved resolution of complaints of refugees and refugee hosting communities
  through operationalized complaints handling mechanism(s); (b) DLI 5 - improved knowledge and skills of professional staff of organizations
  responsible for refugee management; (c) DLI 6 - data on socio-economic characteristics of refugees and refugee hosting communities published
  regularly; and (d) DLI 7 - improved implementation arrangements for the visa policy.
