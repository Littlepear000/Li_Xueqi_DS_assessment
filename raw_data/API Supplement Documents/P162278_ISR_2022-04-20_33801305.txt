 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The project was declared effective on August 19, 2020. The Project Implementation Unit (PIU) has been created and was fully staffed until
  February 2022, when the General Coordinator and the Communication Specialist resigned. Contracts for the acquisition of ortho-imagery and
  cadastral cartography and the quality assurance of these goods have been awarded and execution is expected to start in April 2022. The PIU is in
  the process of completing the technical specifications for the rest of the project subcomponents and activities.
