 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The Board of Executive Directors of the World Bank approved financing for the Accountable Governance for Basic Service Delivery Project
  (P172492) on June 11, 2021. The Financing Agreement for the project was signed on July 29, 2021. The project became effective on January 25,
  2022. The project was formally launched during the first virtual implementation support mission which took place between February 15-23, 2022.
  The Annual Work Plan (AWP) has been prepared and discussed during the mission. Key actions have been agreed between the Bank and the
  Government of Sierra Leone (GoSL) in order to finalize the AWP by early April. The following action items have been identified and agreed upon
  during the mission:
           The AWP should be finalized and approved by the Project’s Steering Committee by early April. This will require the organization of several
            technical sessions and follow up meetings between the Bank team and the GOSL (as detailed in the Aide-Memoire) to ensure that we
            remain on track.

             The Project Steering Committee should be convened by the end of March to review and approve the AWP and budget.
             Finalization of the recruitment of the project’s fiduciary staff should be prioritized, following a competitive selection process.
             The Asset Review being conducted by the GoSL should help to inform the justification for items included in the project’s AWP, particularly
              regarding the procurement of vehicles and IT infrastructure.
             An advance should be requested by the GOSL to the World Bank to cover the costs of fiduciary staff to be recruited under the project, as
              well as the salary of Project Management Unit (PMU) staff for the month of February.
             Focal points from health and education ministries should be identified and invited to participate in forthcoming relevant project meetings
