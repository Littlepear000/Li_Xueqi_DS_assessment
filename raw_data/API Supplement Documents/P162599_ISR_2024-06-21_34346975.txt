4. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  Component 1 - Digital Infrastructure

  Expansion of digital connectivity in targeted rural areas is well underway. 112 mobile sites (out 119 sites) have been
  erected. The remaining 7 sites are expected to be deployed by end of July 2024..

  Connectivity for local administrations and decentralized structures of the Ministry of Agriculture (MAEP) is underway.
  and is expected to be finalized by September 2024.

  Construction of the metropolitan fiber optic network in the city of Parakou. The procurement process is underway, and
  the contractor has been identified. The contract signature is expected by June 15, 2024, at the latest. The activity is on
  track to be finalized before June 30, 2025.

  Component 2- Digital Financial Inclusion and Digital Solutions for Rural Development

  Three digital platforms on agricultural e-advice, management of agricultural equipment and equipment, and
  management of farms, are all deployed and being scaled-up. The agreement with the Federation of the Union of
  Producers (FUPRO), signed in January 2024, has made it possible to begin the extension phase of the platforms and to
  raise awareness.

  For activities related to digital financial inclusion:
      • DFS Shared Platform: The recruitment of the company to develop the DFS shared platform is ongoing and needs
          to be finalized by August 31, 2024, to ensure that the activity is completed before June 2025.
      • Electronic Payment Terminals: The Request for Bid was adjusted and relaunched on May 21, 2024, and the
          process needs to be finalized by August 15, 2024, to ensure that the activity is completed before June 2025.
