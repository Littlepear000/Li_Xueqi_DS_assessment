 Project Development Objectives
  Project Development Objective (from Project Appraisal Document)
  The Project Development Objectives (PDO) are to improve access to, and the quality of early childhood and basic education in the Project Areas
  and to strengthen the capacity of the education system to deliver better results.

  Has the Project Development Objective been changed since Board Approval of the Project Objective?

 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  The project was approved by the Board on July 9, 2019 and the grant agreement was signed on July 29, 2019.
  The MEN-A has started the work to fulfill the two conditions for project effectiveness (effectiveness deadline being January 25, 2020). These are: (i)
  the preparation and adoption of the Project Implementation Manual (PIM) satisfactory to the Bank; and (ii) the recruitment of a coordinator, a
  financial management specialist, a procurement specialist, and an M&E specialist for the PCU with terms of reference, qualifications and experience
  satisfactory to the Bank.
  Regarding the preparation and adoption of the PIM, the MEN-A has formed a team who is currently working with a Bank-recruited consultant to
  develop the PIM: the PIM is expected to be finalized by end of this calendar year.
