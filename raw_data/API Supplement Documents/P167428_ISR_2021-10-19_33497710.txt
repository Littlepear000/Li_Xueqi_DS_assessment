 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The project became effective on December 7, 2020. Since the previous mission in April 2021, the dated covenants were fulfilled, and some
  progress has been made across all components. While the project is on track towards achieving
  the PDO, the implementation progress still remains slow and therefore it is rated as moderately satisfactory. To enhance the project monitoring
  practice and ensure adequate implementation support, the Bank and PIE agreed to organize monthly progress review meetings to allow track
  progress, discuss challenges and provide additional support with timely implementation of planned activities.

                                    This indicator measures the number of farmers (of agricultural products) who have adopted an improved

                                    NB: "Agriculture" or "Agricultural" includes: crops, livestock, capture fisheries, aquaculture, agroforestry,

   Comments:                        Technology includes a change in practices compared to currently used practices or technologies (seed
                                    preparation, planting time, feeding schedule, feeding ingredients, postharvest storage/ processing, etc.). If

                                    counts as one technology.
