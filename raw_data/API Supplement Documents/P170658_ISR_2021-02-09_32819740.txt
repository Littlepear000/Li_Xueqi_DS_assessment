 Project Development Objectives

 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The Government of Peru approved the Supreme Decree (Decreto Supremo nº160-2020-EF) on June 27, 2020 (prerequisite for effectiveness for
  signing) and the Loan Agreement was signed on July 16, 2020. PRONATEL published on August 12, 2020 an Executive Resolution (Nº 097-2020-
  MTC/24) adopting the Operations Manual (acceptable and cleared by the World Bank on June 30, 2020). Given that the adoption of the Operations
  Manual was the only condition for effectiveness, the Project was declared effective on August 17, 2020.

  Under Component 6, (Project Supervision and Management, and Technical Studies), the process for hiring a consulting firm to prepare the
  technical specifications for the Integrated Platform (to be embedded in the Terms of Reference for the Integrated Platform) was launched through a
  Request for Expressions of Interest with a deadline of January 31, 2021.
  In order to advance project implementation, the Bank team and PRONATEL agreed that the technical study and the works for the 911 Center would
  be combined under one procurement. For that purpose and to analyze if there is enough demand and experience to carry out both of these
  processes together, a request for information was published on January 16, 2021 and January 20, 2021 in a local newspaper (“El Comercio”), and
  at the United Nations Development Business (UNDB) website, respectively.
  The Steering Committee with representatives from the Emergency Response Units (Firefighters, Police, and Mobile Emergency Care System
  (SAMU)), PRONATEL and the gender-based violence (GBV) hotline was formally established through Executive Director Resolution (Resolución de
  Dirección Ejecutiva nº 150-2020’MTC/24) on November 9, 2020 (Legal Covenant). As a next step, PRONATEL is working with the Emergency
  Response Units and the GBV hotline on the agreements to be signed with each of these entities describing responsibilities of each entity, in the
  Project.
  Finally, the process to hire key staff under the Project Implementation Unit (general coordinator, procurement specialist, and program assistant) was
  launched in November 2020. Recruitment for these three positions is planned to finalize on February 3, 2021.
