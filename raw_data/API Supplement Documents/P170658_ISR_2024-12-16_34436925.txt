1. OBJECTIVE


 1.1 Development Objective
   Original Development Objective (Approved as part of Approval package on 07-Apr-2020)
   The proposed project development objective is to provide a more efficient response to emergencies, urgencies, and
   associated help line requests from the population of Lima Metropolitan area and Callao.
   Has the Development Objective been changed since Board Approval of the Project Objective?

   No

4. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  The Project is under implementation with full support from the Authorities. Regarding Component 1, the design of the
  911 Center building was finalized and approved. The bidding process for the construction of the 911 Center building is
  under way.
  For Component 2, the contract for the Emergency Attention and Management System (SAGE) was awarded in September
  2024.
  Regarding Component 3, 911 Central Response Protocols have been finalized between PRONATEL and different First
  Response Units (i.e., National Police of Peru, SAMU and Firemen) and Gender Based Violence hotline (Line 100).
  The Council of Ministers approved the Legislative Decree 1590 on December 7, 2023 which is a critical step towards the
  operationalization of the 911 system.
