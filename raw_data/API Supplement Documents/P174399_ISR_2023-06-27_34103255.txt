 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  Implementation status can be summarized as follows (as agreed on during the last ISM between June 9 – June 16, 2023):
         As of June 19, 2023, the PforR disbursed around 25 percent of its original amount against results that map to DLI #5 (enhanced inclusion
          under Skills Development Program), DLI #8 (strengthen systems and coordination to enhance employment support delivery), and DLI# 3
          (ICT in education for learning recovery and improved access). An additional disbursement in the amount of 800,000 USD against the
          achievements of targets under DLI#7 (improved quality of entrepreneurship support under MoICE) and DLI#3 will be triggered shortly and
          reflected in the system.
         Progress towards meeting year-one DLRs can be summarized as follows:
      1. All year-one targets for DLI#3 and DLI#7 are complete and successfully verified.
      2. Year-one target for DLI#6 (establishing a system for regular tracking of employment up to 6 months post training completion) and year-one
          target for DLI#8 (developing an MIS portal for single intake and profiling window for employment support programs) are complete, and are
          pending verification by the CCA.
      3. Remaining year-one targets that have not been met are expected to be completed as follows, after which verification and disbursement
          can take place :(1) approval of ECCD national action plan under DLI#1 by end of August, 2023; (2) training ECCD trainers under
          DLI#1, which has been delayed and is expected to be completed by November, 2023; and (3) approval of TVET gender strategy under
          DLI#5 by end of July, 2023.
