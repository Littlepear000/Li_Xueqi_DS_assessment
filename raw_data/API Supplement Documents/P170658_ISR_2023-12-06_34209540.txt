 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  Building upon previous experience, the Project looks at increasing its implementation pace.
  Regarding Component 1 (Physical Infrastructure), on June 2022, the bidding process for the design and construction of the 911 Center building was
  declared unsuccessful. Based on applicants’ feedback, the Procurement Strategy was adjusted and a new bidding process for the design of the 911
  Center was completed in July 2023. At this stage, a separate bidding process will be launched in March 2024 for the construction of the 911 Center
  building.
  For Component 2 (911 Platform), the bidding process for the Emergency Attention and Management System (SAGE) started in June 2023.

  Regarding Component 3, 911 Central Response Protocols have been finalized between PRONATEL and different First Response Units (i.e.,
  National Police of Peru, SAMU and Firemen) and Gender Based Violence hotline (Line 100).
  The operation of 911 relies on the approval of calls geolocation supporting regulation. In this regard, the Government of Peru supports the Project
  (911 implementation) and in this regard, the Council of Ministers approved the related Legislative Decree on December 4, 2023.
  The Project closing date has been extended to January 10, 2025 to meet its development objectives.
