   Original Development Objective (Approved as part of Approval package on 18-Jun-2024)

  Although the project is at its early stage of implementation, a good progress has been made on some
  of the key activities. Bidding process for the rehabilitation and construction of about 569 classrooms in
  six inspections (Bangui, de l’Ouham-Fafa, de l’Ombella-Mpoko, de la Lobaye, de la Kémo et de Nana-
  Gribizi) has been advancing. Recruitment of more than 5000 contractual teachers is under way.
  Textbooks and teachers’ guide are planned to be deliverd to all the first to third grades’ students and
  teachers by November 2025.
