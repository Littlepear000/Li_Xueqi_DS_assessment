 1.1 Development Objective
   Original Development Objective (Approved as part of Approval package on 14-Jun-2024)
   To increase girls' and boys' participation rates in pre-primary and primary grades and enhance reading proficiency in
   primary grades.
   Has the Development Objective been changed since Board Approval of the Project Objective?

4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  The World Bank's Board of Executive Directors approved the project on 14 June, 2024. However, it is not yet effective,
  and was pending approval from the Punjab Cabinet of Ministers. While key preparatory steps have been completed,
  overall progress is delayed.
  The Punjab Cabinet granted approval on March 27, 2025, and project signing and effectiveness is expected by April 15,
  2025.
