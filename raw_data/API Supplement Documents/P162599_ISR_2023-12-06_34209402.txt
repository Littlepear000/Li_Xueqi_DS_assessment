 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The project was approved by the World Bank Board on July 1, 2019, and became effective on December 3, 2019. The implementation of the project
  has encountered significant delays partly due to the Covid-19 crisis.

  I. Mobile rural connectivity: On Rural Connectivity: The Request for Bids (RFB) for the extension of digital coverage in the targeted areas was
  successfully closed on June 16th, 2022. The contract was awarded to two mobile operators (notification of award was provided to the operators on
  March 2023). All parties involved signed the contracts on April 20,2023. This activity will expand mobile coverage in 178 locations using 119 mobile
  sites divided into four lots. Works have started for lots 2 and 4, with 55 sites in total; 30 sites have been deployed as of October 24, 2023, and works
  are expected to be completed by February 2024 for these two lots. Regarding the two other lots 1 and 3, equipment orders have been placed. The
  Environmental and Social screening reports have been validated by the WB team and the payment process of affected persons for land acquisition
  has been initiated by the mobile operator. Works for lots 1 and 3 are expected to be completed by March 2024 and all project stakeholders were
  advised to take necessary actions for finalize payments related to land acquisition prior to the launch of connectivity works.
  II. Parakou Metropolitan Network: The tender process has been launched and bids are scheduled to be opened in December 2023. Considering
  tight timeframe until the project closing date, the PIU was advised to accelerate the implementation of this activity.
  III. Connecting decentralized State agencies and administrations.
  A - Decentralized administrations (Tender #1): On-site works have been launched on October 16th, 2023 and are expected to be completed by
  February 2024.
  B - Decentralized state agencies (Tender #2.1): Environmental and social instruments (Management plan and Impact Notice) are finalized. Works
  are expected to start in December 2023 and should be completed by April 2024.
  C - IoT Labs (Tender #2.2): The competitive bidding process was declared unsuccessful twice. Technical specifications have been revised, split into
  separate lots and relaunched on October 16th, 2023. Considering the project closing date of July 31st, 2024, the cancellation of this activity has
  been recommended if the contract is not signed before January 31st, 2024.
  Component 2.
  I. Sub-Component 2.1 (“Digital financial inclusion”): The tender related the microfinance platform initially was launched on October 27th, 2023. The
  development of the platform is expected to start by March 2024 and be finalized by closing date. The request for bids for the acquisition of
  Electronic Payment Terminals (in French TPEs) has been published in November 2023.
  II. Sub-Component 2.2 (“Strengthening the data ecosystem”) and Sub-component 2.3 (“Mobilization of the digital ecosystem”) supporting digital
  solutions for rural development, three platforms have been developed so far: (A) the digital platform for agricultural e-Advisory services to foster
  access to information for producers; (B) the digital platform for the management of agricultural materials and equipment for the benefit of producers
  within the value chains targeted by the project; and (C) the digital platform on farm management and monitoring. The pilot phases of the three
  platforms have successfully been completed. The full scale-up of the platforms, in close collaboration with the Federation of Producers' Unions of
  Benin (FUPRO-BENIN), is expected to start in December 2023. All stakeholders, including the ICT and Agriculture Ministries, were encouraged to
  ensure the sustainability of these platforms and finalize the FUPRO agreement as soon as possible.
