4. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The implementation of the project is continuing steadily across its various components. Under subcomponent 1.1,
  contracts have been signed with microfinance institutions to initiate the sale of solar systems to households. To date,
  113,400 household solar units from the first CfP are in Yemen and MFIs are in the process of selling these to end
  beneficiaries. As of May 29, 2025, 91,825 units have been sold representing approximately 35% of the total. Under sub-
  component 1.2, as of May 2025, a total of 350 health facilities, 100 schools, and 200 pumping systems – totaling
  approximately 9.7MW. As of May 15, 2025, 444 facilities (62 schools, 294 health centers, and 88 water pumping systems)
  have been energized, representing a cumulative installed capacity of 9 MWp and delivering 17,023 MWh/year in energy
  savings and 11,065 tons CO₂/year in GHG reductions. Additionally, several assessments and studies are at various stages
  to provide support for the recovery of Yemen's power sector and support capacity building to government entities and
  microfinance institutions. The challenging conditions on the ground are being closely monitored, as they have caused
  delays in shipment of solar systems and equipment into Yemen. However, the project is progressing adequately overall
  without encountering any significant obstacles.
