 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  This is an interim update on Project Status. The Project became effective in December 2020. Despite challenges over the last few years, the
  Government of Papua New Guinea (GoPNG) remains committed to achieving the Project Development Objectives and the National Department of
  Health (NDoH) sees the Project as essential towards achieving their national health plan. The GoPNG has requested and the Bank has processed
  a request to restructure the Project to ease implementation. An amendment to the Financing Agreement was signed by the Bank on October 17,
  2023. Key changes included: reallocating about $4.2 million between Component 2 (which reimburses Government against the achievement of
  key targets) to Component 1 (providing direct investments in primary health care facilities); restructuring the disbursement-linked indicators (DLIs) to
  revise the timeframe and to accommodate the selection of a new project province to represent the highlands region; simplifying the plan for the
  Component 1 activities; and revising the Results Framework and targets accordingly. These changes will go into effect upon counter-signature of an
  amendment of the Financing Agreement by the Government. In the meantime, the DLIs for the Year 1 have been verified by an independent
