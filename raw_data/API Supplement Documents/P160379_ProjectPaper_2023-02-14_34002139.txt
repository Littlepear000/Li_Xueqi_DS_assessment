    I. PROJECT STATUS AND RATIONALE FOR RESTRUCTURING


    1.    Project Background: The Loan, CTF Loan, and CTF Grant Agreements were signed on December 14, 2022. The
    Project is yet to be effective. The project aims to support innovative technologies in renewable energy and hence
    contribute towards energy transitions journey of the country. The Project now proposes to support two subprojects and
    strengthening the institutional capacity of SECI:

           (a) 100 megawatt (MW) Solar with 40 MW/120 megawatt-hours (MWh) batteries subproject at Rajnandgaon,
               Chhattisgarh.
           (b) 100 MW floating solar in Jharkhand.

    2.     Current status of the project: The contract for the first subproject at Rajnandgaon, Chhattisgarh having 100
    megawatt (MW) Solar and 40 MW/120 megawatt-hours (MWh) batteries was signed and construction has begun at the
    site. The targeted commissioning date for this subproject is within this calendar year. The second subproject is 100 MW
    floating solar in Jharkhand. The procurement process for the same is planned to be initiated by May 2023.

    3.   This restructuring is for the partial loan cancellation of IBRD loan, CTF loan, and CTF grant (under P160379). The
    aforementioned cancellations necessitate certain changes to the Results Framework for the Project which will be agreed
    between SECI and the World Bank in due course.

    II. DESCRIPTION OF PROPOSED CHANGES

    4.    This Restructuring Paper is for partial cancelation of US$110.81 million, hence reducing the project size to US$89
    million. The request for the partial cancelation was received from Department of Economic Affairs (DEA), ministry of
    Finance, Government of India on February 10, 2023.

    5.     The original project was designed to support at least three subprojects, however, SECI has now decided to limit
    the financing to the two subprojects mentioned in paragraph 1 and accordingly requested for partial cancellation of USD
    110.81 million. Given the uncertainties around one of the identified subprojects (solar-wind-battery), delays in
    identifying another eligible subproject, and savings due to the exchange rate, a partial cancelation of US$110.81 million
    is proposed.
