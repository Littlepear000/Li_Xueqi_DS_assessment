 4. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
Since receiving funding in January 2023, PROCIENCIA and CONCYTEC have been actively engaged in preparing
and implementing various project activities. These efforts include the design and launch of grant instruments, as
well as the development of Terms of Reference and technical specifications for both consulting and non-consulting
services, most of which have already been awarded and are currently being implemented. Key advancements
include:

        •       Institutional Alliances for Ph.D. Program Grants: Grants have been awarded, and the development
                of alliances is underway.

        •       Ph.D. Scholarships: The second cohort of students has been selected.

        •       Lab Strengthening and Accreditation Grants: Grants have been awarded and are in the
                implementation phase.

        •       Academic Entrepreneurship Grants: 16 out of 23 positions were filled during the first phase. A second
                call is scheduled for the first half of 2025 to fill the remaining seven positions.

        •       Grants for Certification of Lab Tests: Grant proposals have been selected.

        •       Industry-Academia Contract Research Grants: Grants have been awarded for the development of
                technologies to support value chains.

        •       Technology Transfer Program: Instruments are currently being reviewed to incorporate WB technical
                advice.

        •       Access to Full-Text International Scientific Literature: As part of CONCYTEC’s digitization efforts,
                the contract is under implementation, enabling the Peruvian scientific community to access international
                scientific literature.

        •       National STI Evaluation System: The first national survey of public perception of Science, Technology,
                and Innovation (STI) has been completed, with results presented on December 12, 2024.
