 Project Development Objectives

  Project Development Objective (from Project Appraisal Document)
  The objectives of the Project are to mitigate the effect of COVID-19 on private sector investment and employment and to support new economic
  opportunities including in refugee and hosting communities.

  Has the Project Development Objective been changed since Board Approval of the Project Objective?
  No

 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The project will be effective once it receives parliamentary approval from the Ugandan Parliament. The project sum of USD 200 million, consists of
  IDA grants in the amount of USD104m (of which IDA grants for RHDs in the amount of USD50m) and an IDA financing in the amount of
  USD96m. The project will be supported by Development Partners, including the Netherlands, United Kingdom and Sweden through an addition
  Multi Donor Trust Fund in the amount of up to USD 18 million.
