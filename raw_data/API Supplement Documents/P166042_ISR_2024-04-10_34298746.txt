 Project Development Objectives

  Project Development Objective (from Project Appraisal Document)
  The Project Development Objectives are to: (i) increase electricity supply to the Eastern part of Guinea; (ii) enable electricity trade between Guinea
  and Mali; and (iii) increase Guinea’s electricity export capability towards other West African Power Pool countries.

  Has the Project Development Objective been changed since Board Approval of the Project Objective?
  No

 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  Project implementation
  The procurement process has been completed for all contracts relating to the construction of the transmission line and associated substations. All
  contracts have been signed and are currently being executed. Advance payments have been made and service orders issued.
  The disbursement rate stands at only 30% at less than six (6) months toward the closing date, that is representing a disbursement amount of US$
  23.54 million of which 19.18 M for Guinea and 4.36 M for Mali
  Component 1: Power transmission infrastructure (lines and substations)
  At the MTR mission, the team noted a significant delay in the execution of works on the different lots financed by the IDA and including the
  substations of Kankan, Siguiri, Kérouané and the portion of the line between Siguiri and the Malian border line in Guinea, as well as works to extend
  the Sanankoroba substation in Mali. With six (6) months to go before the closing of the project, 50% of the work has been completed in Guinea. In

  Mali, the contractor had still not started the civil engineering works and had not confirmed to its suppliers the orders for the manufacturing of
  electrical equipment. As a result, the execution of the contract of the extension of the Sanankoroba substation have been delayed by almost one
  year.
  Updated construction schedules were presented during the MTR mission. These schedules were revised and updated by the project owner's
  engineer during the mission, and indicate a completion date of the IDA financed components by December 31, 2024 in Guinea and in Mali.
  In light of the revised implementation schedule, it is obvious that the IDA-financed activities will not be completed by the project closing date on
  June 30, 2024. Therefore, an extension is necessary to enable the project to achieve its development objectives.
  The overal progress rates of works funded by other donors are the forllowing:
             Portion of the transmission line Fomi-Siguiri, funded by the Ecowas Bank for Development and Investment-EBID in Guinea is 15%. The
              revised completion schedule is June 30, 2025, provided that the Government mobilizes the amount of 2.4 million USD for the
              compensation process by March 31, 2024.
             Portion of the transmission line and substation funded by the European Investment Bak-EIB in Guinea is 46%. The revised completion
              date is Decembre 31, 2024.
             Portion of line and substation funded the African Development Bank-AFDB in Guinea is 40%. The revised completion date is December
              31, 2024
             Portion of the line funded by the AFDB and the West African Bank of Development-WABD in Mali is 35%. The revised completion date is
              June 30, 2025 due to the delay for the Governement of mali to secure the compensation funds of 4 million USD.
  Component 2: Support for project implementation
  Recruitment of a Consultant for the study of the connection of Guinean mining companies to the national interconnected grid in Guinea: . The
  consultant has started the execution of the services, with a survey sent to the mining companies, which feedbacks will trigger the mission on the
  sites. It has been proposed that a focal point for the study be appointed in the Strategy and Development Office of the Ministry of Mines, to facilitate
  interaction between the PMU, the consultant and the miners. A follow-up will be made at the monthly meeting with the PMU at the end of January
  2024.
  Power Purchase Agreement (PPA) between Guinea and Mali: . EDG and EDM will work and negotiate directly on the basis of the ECOWAS
  Regional Electricity Regulatory Authority (ARREC) model contract, with the assistance of WAPP.
  Acquisition of special equipment for the electricity companies EDG (Guinea) and EDM (Mali): In Guinea: two (2) maintenance trucks are planned to
  be acquired for EDG.. In Mali: Six (6) special vehicles for the maintenance of HV lines were procured and delivered to EDM July 10, 2023.

 Overall Comments PDO

  Achievement of the PDO is subject to the completion and commissioning of the the transmission line and the substations. Works are on track
  to be complete December 31, 2024 that is beyond the project closing date of June 30, 2024. It is therefore needed to extend closing date to
  allow the project achieve its PDO.

 Overall Comments IR

  Overall, the construction of the transmission lines and substations financed by the IDA is behind the schedule. At the MTR mission none of
  these infrastructures has been completed. The revised implementation schedule provides the completion of the IDA financed works December
  31, 2024. Therefore an extension is necessery to allow the completion of the project infrastructures and their commissioning

  Regarding the training indicators, the team is following up with the PIUs to ensure that the required training are included in the 2024 capacity
  building funded by the project.
