 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The Project was approved and Loan documents were signed in August 2018. Most project implementation efforts at this early stage focused on
  carrying out the Project's main procurement processes (consulting for Project Management Office, DFBOMT concession, and supervision contract)
  have been awarded without significant delays or any other issues. Significant efforts by EMB and the District to complete the transfer of utility
  networks are underway. EMB and IDU are advancing satisfactorily with scheduled land acquisition and resettlement processes.
