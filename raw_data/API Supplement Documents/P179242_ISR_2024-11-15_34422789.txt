4. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  The project was approved on May 16, 2023. Financing Agreements were signed with the Republic of Maldives on June 18,
  2023, and with the Indian Ocean Commission on June 22, 2023. Implementation activities are being initiated by the two
  implementing agencies - the Indian Ocean Commission (IOC) for Component 1 of the Project, and the Ministry of
  Fisheries and Ocean Resources for components 2 and 3 of the Project.
  The Project’s objectives and design remain highly relevant and aligns with the strategic direction and priorities of the new
  government of the Maldives for the fisheries sector, which include modernizing fisheries sector management, promoting
  processing to reduce raw material exports, decarbonizing the sector through the adoption of green practices, investing in
  critical infrastructure and leveraging private sector financing, among other objectives.
  Main progress includes:
  Key staff have been mobilized for project management, fiduciary, environmental and social risk management, and
  monitoring and evaluation, as well as several technical positions.
  · Component 1 (Enhance Evidence-based Management Advice to the Fisheries Sector in the South-West Indian Ocean
  Region) : A contract was signed with the Food and Agriculture Organization in July 2024 to provide overall technical
  assistance for the implementation of this component, and the first Steering Committee meeting is scheduled for
  December 2024 to approve the component’s annual budget.
  · Component 2 (Supporting Maldives as the Catalyst for Strengthened Regional Capacity for Fisheries Governance and
  Management): significant progress has been made in: (i) digitizing historical catch reports, conducting stakeholder
  consultations, redrafting Fisheries Management Plans, designing awareness materials, and enhancing regional
  engagements; (ii) carrying out activities that support digital registry of fishers and awareness activities for a Fisheries
  Information System; (iii) some refurbishing of quarantine and laboratory facilities; and (iv) capacity building and
  preparatory work for initiating a degree program and preparing a mariculture training program.
  · Component 3 (Enhanced Competitiveness and Private Sector Participation for improving Business Climate for Fisheries
  in Maldives and the South-West Indian Ocean Region): progress has been made with respect to (i) installing echo
  sounders and hiring of consultant for an energy audit of the two key areas of fisheries processing and fishing vessels; and
  (ii) finalizing the preparation of a matching grants manual for rolling out the activities under this component which aims
  at leveraging the private sector investments in decarbonization efforts.
