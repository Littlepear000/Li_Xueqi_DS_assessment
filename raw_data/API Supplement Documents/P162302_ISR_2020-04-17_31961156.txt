 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  There is satisfactory progress in the implementation of Results Area 1:Revenue Mobilization. Overall, there is good progress in the
  implementation of reforms related to improvement in revenue mobilization. Collection of Sales Tax on Services has improved significantly, with the
  project meeting and exceeding the target for Year 1. As of March 2020, STS collection stood at PKR 18.8 billion, exceeding the project target of
  PKR 15.7 billion for FY19/20. Similarly, as of March 2020, the registered STS payers who filed stood at 82 percent, substantially exceeding
  the FY19/20 project target of 55 percent. Work is underway--albeit with some delay--on database integration to facilitate registration and collection
  of taxes between the three revenue collection agencies. The proposed integration plan has been prepared, but has not been approved due to the
  COVID 19 outbreak. The targeted Urban Immovable Property Tax (UIPT) survey of two cities has not been met largely due to delay in selection of
  firm to undertake GIS mapping due to COVID 19. Department has completed its UIPT surveys in Abbottabad, Havelian and Nowshera. For
  Peshawar, authorities expect to leverage existing GIS surveys performed by the WSSP to complete the survey. The Excise, Taxation and Narcotics
  Control Department (ET&NCD) is assessing the existing database to integrate and align with evaluation tables. The ET&CD has also carried out
  another exercise to expand collection of UIPT in 15 districts, which could increase UIPT revenue by 25 percent.

  There is satisfactory progress in the implementation of Results Area 2: Public Resource Management. Overall the Government is making
  efforts to assess the volume of cash deposits in commercial banks, as a first step to reduce the amount of Cash deposits held by Government in
  commercial banks. The stock take of existing number of accounts and deposits has been initiated with the State Bank Pakistan (SBP), and is
  expected to present a clear picture on the level of cash in the commercial bank. At present, the Finance Department (FD) has received only
  accounts held in the Bank of Khyber, as they await feedback from the SBP. The Government is also making an effort to improve cash management.
  The Treasury Single Account (TSA) policy of Federal Government has been notified and FD has made satisfactory progress in preparing the
  roadmap for the implementation of Cash Management Policy. Similarly, satisfactory progress has been made in reducing the share of Annual
  Development Program (ADP) funds allocated to unapproved projects (excluding Foreign Project Assistance). The 2019/20 budget allocates 42
  percent of the budget to unapproved projects compared to the 49 percent for FY 18/19, the baseline year. The government has not made progress
  in revising the ADP guidelines to focus on capital expenditures. Similarly, there is no progress in preparation of sectoral plans. Only one sector has
  a sectoral plan. To improve the National Financial Management Information System (NFMIS) coverage, a draft Integrated Financial Management
  Information and System (IFMIS) implementation plan has been prepared. Testing and implementation of the plan has been delayed by the COVID
  19 outbreak. Finally, the output-based budgeting (OBB) departments have made progress in including measurable gender key performance
  indicators (KPIs) and by reporting on number of females reached by their services. At least 12 departments now have KPI disaggregated by
  gender. This exceeds the targeted 6 departments for Year 1. Satisfactory progress has also been made on strengthening government capacity
  for management of dedicated funds. Authorities have drafted a bill outlining proposed institutional framework for management of funds. The draft
  bill has been submitted to Cabinet for approval and recruitment is underway to complete an actuarial study.

  Technical Assistance: There is satisfactory progress in the implementation of activities under the Technical Assistance for automating
  government business processes. Several consultations have been held with the eight departments selected under the first phase of this process.

  RiskComments
  Comments
  The rating for Political and Governance risk is changed from "Substantial" to "Moderate". This is because there is less likelihood of any political and
  governance events that might affect achievement of the Project Development Objective. The activities supported by the project are mainly
  administrative requiring only minimal political consensus and are not likely to be affected in the event of any political changes at provincial or
  national levels.

  The rating for Institutional Capacity for Implementation and Sustainability risk is changed from "Substantial" to "Moderate". There is sufficient
  capacity to implement the activities in this project. The Finance Department (FD) , the main implementing agency, has demonstrated ownership of
  the project. Additional technical staff have been hired to support areas that require augmentation and the implementation also leverages existing

  capacity under the Governance Policy Project. The FD has also established several working groups drawn from agencies that are responsible for
  key project activities thereby strengthening inter- departmental collaboration for implementation.

  The rating for Overall project risk is similarly changed from "Substantial" to "Moderate" to reflect the more cooperative environment in which the
  project implementation is taking place, the overall consensus around the need for strengthening revenue and public financial management, which
  are the core activities under the project, and the continued close implementation support.

  The ratings for Stakeholder risk is similarly assessed to be "Moderate" at this time compared to the "Substantial" rating at project
  effectiveness. This is because the FD has rallied all the major key implementing partners, as well as stakeholders. There have been consultations
  with the business community regarding tax payment and consultations with various parts of Government on automation of administrative functions.
  Stakeholder consultation is embedded in project design and will be an ongoing activity during implementation to sustain the revised "Moderate"
  rating.
