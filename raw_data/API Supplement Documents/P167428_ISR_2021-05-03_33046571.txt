 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The Project became effective on December 7, 2020. To ensure coordination, the flow of information, and timely decision-making on strategic and
  programmatic aspects at a high level, the Project is coordinated by a Steering Committee (SC), represented by Vice Prime-Minister, Deputy minister
  of economy, Osh Deputy Governor, Mayor of Osh city, Head of the Uzgen district, representatives of the relevant ministries and agencies, ABCC
  and ARIS project team The SC composition has finalized, but due to the reorganization of the GoKR’s Ministerial functions, the new SC has yet to
  be amended through a new decree. The final SC composition is expected to be approved by a GoKR resolution by May 31, 2021, at the latest.
