4. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The Project is making notable progress toward its development objective of improving secondary education opportunities for girls in targeted

  areas, as evidenced by significant gains in enrollment and completion rates for girls in both junior and senior secondary schools, which have

  surpassed targets. The Project has also met or surpassed its targets on fostering digital literacy, upgrading school infrastructure with climate-

  resilient and disability-friendly classrooms and WASH facilities, and conducting community outreach campaigns to change behaviors and

  attitudes towards the education and empowerment of adolescent girls and boys. There remains room for improvement. Life skills sessions and

  scholarship programs have yet to reach their targets, and alternative education opportunities for out-of-school girls are lagging. The adoption

  of gender education policies at the state level is also slower than anticipated. Despite these challenges, the outlook remains positive, and the

  Project is implementing proactive measures to address these gaps. Actions include expanding the training of master and cascade trainers to

  increase the number and frequency of life skills sessions and alternative education opportunities, refining the collection and validation of

  beneficiary data for the scholarship program to ensure proper targeting of the most in need, and expediting the implementation of monitoring

  tools for infrastructure improvements. These focused interventions and the range of other mitigating measures being implemented mean that

  the Project remains on track to achieve its development objectives by the closing date.
