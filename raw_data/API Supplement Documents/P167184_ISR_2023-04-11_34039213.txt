 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The Project became effective in December 2020. Despite challenges over the last few years, the Government of Papua New Guinea remains
  committed to achieving the Project Development Objectives and the National Department of Health sees the Project as essential towards achieving
  their national health plan. Implementation performance of the Project has been hampered by slow processes in (i) contracting an Independent
  Verification Agent in accordance with the Financing Agreement; (ii) specific fragility challenges in Project provinces; (iii) proceeding with a detailed
  health facility needs assessment; and (iv) overall procurement and financial management. The Government and the Bank are currently discussing
  measures to restructure the Project taking into account these noted challenges.
