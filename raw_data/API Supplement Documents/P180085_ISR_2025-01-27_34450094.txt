1. OBJECTIVE


 1.1 Development Objective
   Original Development Objective (Approved as part of Approval package on 28-Sep-2023)
   The Project Development Objective is to improve country statistical performance, regional harmonization, data access
   and use, and to enhance modernization of the statistical system in participating countries.

   Has the Development Objective been changed since Board Approval of the Project Objective?

   No

4. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  This Implementation Status and Results Report is based on face-to-face implementation support missions that were
  conducted in the respective project participating countries and regional organization during December 2024.

  Central African Republic:
  Progress has been made in the implementation of the two main activities that were planned for 2024, these include the
  fourth population census , scheduled for in May 2025, and the constructing the Central African Institute for Statistics and
  Economic and Social Studies (Institut Centrafricain des Statistiques et des Etudes Economiques et Sociales, ICASEES) office
  building.

  Chad:
  Some of the project development indicators have improved including for example, number of staffs trained; number of
  scholarships provided, and the preparation of a gender note that includes many United Nations gender Statistics
  indicators. All Project Implementation Unit (PIU) staff are now recruited and in place. Thanks to a good monitoring
  system, the implementation of activities is underway. Thus, progress towards the project development objectives
  remains satisfactory.

  Central African Economic and Monetary Community (Communauté Économique et Monétaire de l'Afrique Centrale,
  CEMAC) :
  The project is effective for CEMAC and Key PIU staff are already hired with the remaining ones expected to be hired in the
  beginning of 2025. Activities under 2025 have already been planned and Regional harmonization activities have already
  started on the households living conditions harmonized Surveys (Enquête Harmonisée sur le Conditions de Vie des
  Ménages, EHCVM). The CEMAC commission has partnered with the West African Economic and Monetary Union
  (WAEMU) to organize two workshops with the participation of all the CEMAC countries including Equatorial Guinea.

  Cameroon: The project is not yet effective.

  Republic of Congo: The project is not yet effective.

  Gabonese Republic: The PIU team is in place and the recruitment of E&S specialists and M&E specialists are ongoing. The
  team is working closely with the statistical office (Direction Generale de la Statistique - DGS) to finalize the annual work
  plan for 2025.
