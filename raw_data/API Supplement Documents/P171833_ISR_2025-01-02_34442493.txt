4. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  In March 2023, the Governments of Grenada, Saint-Lucia and Saint Vincent and the Grenadines requested the triggering
  of the Contingency Emergency Response Component of the UBEC project as a response to address the food insecurity
  crisis in the three countries and strengthen resilience of the local food systems that was negatively impacted by the
  global polycrisis, including the supply chain disruptions and high food prices resulting post COVID-19 pandemic. The UBEC
  CERC was activated on May 5, 2023, with a maximum re-allocation amount of US$10 million per country and ativitities
  are currently under implementation. The CERC funded activities were prioritized by the three recipient countries to
  provide immediate and urgent support to priority areas including: (i) crop production; (ii) enhanced livestock production
  and, (iii) enhanced safe fishery production and reduction in food loss and waste.
  The three countries have made significant progress in the implementation of their respective CERC activities, with
  commitments within the three countries ranging from 72% in Grenada, 82% in Saint-Lucia and to more than 82% in Saint-
  Vincent-and-the Grenadines. While most activities have been completed, some tasks required additional time for delivery
  and processing of goods procured, and initiating selected remaining contracts which were critical to some of the
  procurements including installation and trainings. The CERC activities are now set to be completed by March 2025 for all
  three countries, following requests for extension from each. The participating countries have commenced small
  procurement of the agreed Blue Economy activities in the fisheries, tourism and waste sectors, but will speed up the
  implementation pace in 2025 to prioritize the blue economy activities.
  The Mid-term review of the Project will take place in the first semester of 2025.
