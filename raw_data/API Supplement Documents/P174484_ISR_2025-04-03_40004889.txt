1. PROJECT DEVELOPMENT OBJECTIVE


 1.1 Development Objective
   Original Development Objective (Approved as part of Approval package on 25-Mar-2021)
   To support the development of a more adaptive social protection system that will contribute to any future crisis-
   resilience among poor and vulnerable households

   Current Development Objective (Approved as part of Additional Financing package Seq. 1 on 20-Jun-2024)
   To Support the development of an adaptive and crisis-resilient social protection system

3. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


 3.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
   Most of the activities are progressing as planned, and the overall rating of the Program is retained as “Satisfactory”.
   DLIs 9, 16, 18 and 20 (value at USD 100 million) have been achieved and Benazir Income Support Programme is in
   the process of sending them to the IVA for its endorsement. The recently launched hybrid savings scheme has
   generated a lot of interest. There is a need to expedite work on the beneficiary centric payment model,
   recertification of beneficiaries, and removal of duplication with provincial CCTs programs.
