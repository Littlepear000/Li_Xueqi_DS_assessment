4. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  There has been continued progress on implementing activities under all Components of the Project. Road equipment has
  been procured by both UWA and NFA, to facilitate opening and rehabilitation of roads inside protected areas.
  Construction of an electric fence as a human-wildlife conflict prevention measure has progressed in Queen Elizabeth NP.
  Progress has also been made in the preparation of designs and E&S instruments for planned staff and tourism
  infrastructure within protected areas. UWA and NFA have continued to support, and work with, communities adjacent to
  the target PAs. UWA has so far provided training and supported installation of 633 rainwater harvesting systems and
  4,060 energy-saving cookstoves, with reported positive outcomes on community relations and reduction in resources
  extracted from the Protected Areas. Major activities under MWE have advanced, but remain in the procurement process.
