 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The Program implementation is on track. Most of the recommendations of the previous mission have been implemented, in particular, the
  recruitment of the IVA has been finalized, the start-up advance granted has now been effectively mobilized by the two concerned ministries (MAER
  and MEPA). Progress has been achieved in key performance indicators, including the achievement of some disbursement linked results (DLR) of
  the disbursement linked indicators (DLI), subject to confirmation by the (IVA) which is currently assessing them.
