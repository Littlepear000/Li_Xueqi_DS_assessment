 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The project was declared effective on August 19, 2020. The Project Implementation Unit (PIU) was established and was fully staffed until February
  2022, when the General Coordinator resigned and then in May 2022, the Procurement Specialist. Both hiring processes are ongoing. Contracts for
  the acquisition of ortho-imagery and cadastral cartography and the quality assurance of these goods have been awarded, and its implementation
  started in May 2022. The PIU is completing the technical specifications for the rest of the project activities.
