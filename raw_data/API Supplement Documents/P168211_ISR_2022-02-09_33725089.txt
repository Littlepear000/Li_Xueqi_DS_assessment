 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

   Despite the COVID-19 related challenges, implementation of the Project is progressing well. Several of the Disbursement Linked Results (DLRs)
  have been achieved by December 2021 and some actions of the Program Action Plans have been partially implemented. In particular, BT is doing
  good progress with establishment of Supervisory Boards and the specialized committees (audits and compensation) at BT and the newly created
  power transmission (Shabakahoi Intiqoli Barq) and distribution (Shabakahoi Taqsimoti Barq) companies, comprising of members of supervisory
  boards and chaired by independent supervisory board members. Considering the impact of COVID-19 pandemic on the financial and operational
  standing of BT, the Power Utility Financial Recovery Program need to be restructured to revise the results framework, indicators and the DLR
  targets of this Program.

  RiskComments
  Comments
  Due to the negative impact of COVID-19 on the financial standing of BT, SIB and STB, the utility companies have faced significant financial
  challenges caused by i) lower collections from sales of electricity including non-payments for exported electricity to Afghanistan and ii) depreciation
  of the Tajik Somoni against the US$ resulted in BT cash flow deficit given that most of BT's debt, liabilities and Power Purchase Agreements (PPAs)
  are US$ denominated. Moreover, given the COVID-19 impact on the socio-economic conditions of the country, the Government could not afford to
  increase the electricity tariff in 2020 and 2021 as planned under the DLI 1.1. Thus, the macroeconomic risk is rated as "High".
