4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  The Project is progressing well and will reach effectiveness by August 2025. The WB and the State of SC are working
  together to develop TORs for the hiring of project design consulting firms and recently conducted a workshop to
  familiarize the client with the WB's CREMA policy. The WB and states that are part of this MPA have also begun
  developing the CREMA policy and bidding documents for projects, works, and maintenance, which are key for each
  phase.



4.2 MPA Program Development Objective

  Current MPA Program Development Objective (Approved as part of (Seq. ) package on )




4.3 Multiphase Programmatic Approach (MPA) Status (Public Disclosure)
  The first four phases of the MPA have been approved, while the Tocantins Productive project, which participates in the
  learning agenda, is moving forward towards the Decision Meeting Review. Phases 5 and 6—in Piauí and Sergipe—are
  pending in the June Cofiex, and potential new phases are being prepared with Pernambuco and the Ministry of Transport
  for phases 7 and 8. Additional states might join later. Workshops that served as Early Market Engagement for the Basic
  Project Designs were conducted in the first four phases, and the CREMA guidelines, the climate-resilient manual, and the
  directives for urban crossings are all moving forward, being critical for the learning agenda.
