 Project Development Objectives
  Project Development Objective (from Project Appraisal Document)
  The project development objective of the proposed Third Phase of the Central Asia Regional Links Program (CARs-3 Project) is to increase regional
  connectivity and support sustainable tourism development in Issyk-Kul Oblast.
