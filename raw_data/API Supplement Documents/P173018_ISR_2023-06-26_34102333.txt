 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The Legal and Mining Sector advisors have reviewed the legal and regulatory framework for the mining bill and provided recommendations for
  improvement. The Minerals Resources Bill is expected to go to Cabinet in June 2023 and to Parliament by November 2023. A Mining Sector Master
  plan is being developed to guide the management of the Mineral Sector, and related mining sector inputs into the Solomon Island country
  macroeconomic model as well as an Action Plan and Strategy are being prepared to guide long-term Mining Sector Management initiatives,
  including the implementation of the new mining legislation. An online portal linked to the Mining Cadaster Administration System (MCAS) is now
  accessible at portal.mmere.gov.sb. The Project Steering Committee (PSC) held its first meeting on June 1, 2023, to support the implementation of
  the SMDTAP. The PSC reviewed the project's annual work plan, budget, and procurement activities. The activity of Strategic Environmental and
  Social Assessment is currently in progress.
