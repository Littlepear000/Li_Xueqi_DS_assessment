1. OBJECTIVE


 1.1 Development Objective
   Original Development Objective (Approved as part of Approval package on 13-Jun-2025)
   To strengthen Lao PDR's monitoring and enforcement of pollution management, improve municipal solid waste
   management in Targeted Areas, and provide immediate and effective response in case of an Eligible Crisis or Emergency.



   Has the Development Objective been changed since Board Approval of the Project Objective?

   No
