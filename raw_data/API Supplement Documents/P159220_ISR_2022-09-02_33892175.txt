 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  Despite delays in selecting a civil works contractor for the reconstruction of Tyup-Kegen road under Component 1, the Bank team recognizes the
  efforts made by the implementing agency and the WB PIU to maintain the overall progress of the project towards achieving its PDO. The contractor
  has been selected and contract was signed on July 28, 2022. It is important that mobilization will be completed by the end of September 2022, and
  construction works start in October of this year, taking into account the early start of the winter season at the project site.
  A key activity under Component 2 (Aviation) is the review of the State Safety Program. Consulting services for this activity are expected to be
  onboarded by August 31, 2022. Component 3 (Tourism) has made good progress since the last mission in November 2021. Design for 5 TSCs
  had been developed and approved by the Tourism Working Group. Consultant for Development of a Sustainable Tourism Strategy and Masterplan
  was selected and contract was signed on June 2, 2022. Selection of Consulting services for Development and Pilot Application of TSA for the Issyk-
  Kul oblast is being finalized and contract is expected to be signed by the end of September 2022.
