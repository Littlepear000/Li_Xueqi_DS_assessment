  Project Development Objective (from Project Appraisal Document)
  The project development objective is to improve the coverage of urban cadaster services in selected municipalities to enhance local government

 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The progress towards the achievement of the Project Development Objective (PDO) remains rated Moderately Unsatisfactory, considering the
  lack of PDO and results indicators achievement, by the closing date. However, several activities have been prepared to accomplish each indicator
  (See Section 5, Results Framework). In addition, the Implementation Progress (IP) remains rated Moderately Unsatisfactory considering the
  lack of progress on project components and activities. However, an Action Plan has been prepared and approved by the Government. It describes
  core project activities to be expected to achieve by the end of December 2023, such as the bidding procurement cadastral surveys processes for
  nine (out of 22) targeted municipalities.
  By December 2022, a new Project Coordinator, Financial Management Specialist and Procurement Specialists were hired and the key staff of the
  Project Implementation Unit (PIU) team is currently complete.
