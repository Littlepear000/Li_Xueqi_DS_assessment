 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The overall implementation progress (IP) since the mid-term review (MTR) is deemed satisfactory, whereas the progress towards achieving the
  Project Development Objective (PDO) remains moderately satisfactory. The disbursement rate now stands at 55 per cent, showing a significant rise
  from the 30 per cent recorded at MTR. Currently, various procurement activities are at different stages, with anticipation around the signing of high-
  value contracts before the end of 2023, including the e-Registration software for the ORC , and the Project Management and the Engineering,
  Procurement, and Construction (EPC) for the GKICP&SEZ.
  Except for component 1.4 (Trade Support), which became part of the project during the restructuring in June 2022, all components/subcomponents
  of the project are progressing well. Following the lack of progress, the Bank task team worked closely with the counterpart to refocus the
  subcomponent (now renamed as Trade Reform Support) to a few main areas, including i) trade policy support including trade facilitation; and ii)
