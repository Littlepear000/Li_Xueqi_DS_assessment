 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The dated covenants were fulfilled, and some progress has been made across all components.
  The Consultant for developing Regional Economic Development Strategy is onboard and it is expected that by September 2022 the draft strategy
  will be submitted for review.
  The initial feasibility study for 8 laboratories to finalize the detailed investment plan, develop technical specifications for goods/equipment, and
  provide input into the training plan is ongoing and it is expected that by August 15 the study will be finalized.
  The value chain study commissioned to guide the prioritization and development of productive partnership sub-projects was completed and 14
  value chains were identified. The study provided in-depth information on the economic potential of each chain, potential geographic areas where

                                      This indicator measures the number of farmers (of agricultural products) who have adopted an improved
                                      agricultural technology promoted by operations supported by the World Bank.

                                      NB: "Agriculture" or "Agricultural" includes: crops, livestock, capture fisheries, aquaculture, agroforestry,
                                      timber and non-timber forest products.
                                      Adoption refers to a change of practice or change in use of a technology that was introduced or promoted
                                      by the project.
   Comments:                          Technology includes a change in practices compared to currently used practices or technologies (seed
                                      preparation, planting time, feeding schedule, feeding ingredients, postharvest storage/ processing, etc.). If
                                      the project introduces or promotes a technology package in which the benefit depends on the application
                                      of the entire package (e.g., a combination of inputs such as a new variety and advice on agronomic
                                      practices such as soil preparation, changes in seeding time, fertilizer schedule, plant protection, etc.), this
                                      counts as one technology.
                                      Farmers are people engaged in farming of agricultural products or members of an agriculture related
                                      business (disaggregated by men and women) targeted by the project.
