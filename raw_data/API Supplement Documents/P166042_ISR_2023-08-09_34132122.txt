 Project Development Objectives

  Project Development Objective (from Project Appraisal Document)
  The Project Development Objectives are to: (i) increase electricity supply to the Eastern part of Guinea; (ii) enable electricity trade between Guinea
  and Mali; and (iii) increase Guinea’s electricity export capability towards other West African Power Pool countries.

  Has the Project Development Objective been changed since Board Approval of the Project Objective?
  No

 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  At one year from project closure, the disbursement rate is only 26.1%, representing a disbursement of $21.8 million. This low level of disbursement
  can be explained by the delay in the completion of work by contractors. From the last supervision mission in November to date, disbursements on
  the Guinean side have not really progressed (from 20.15% to 21%). In Mali, by contrast, the cumulative amount disbursed is 4.24 million USD, or
  46%. This improvement in disbursements is essentially due to the payment of the commissioning advance for the extension of the Sanankoroba
  substation and the payment of an invoice for special engines.
  Detailed implementation progress:
  Component 1: Power transmission infrastructure (US$71.8 million)
  Overall, there has been a considerable delay in the execution of work on the different lots, mainly due to delays in the engineering phase (design
  studies). The implementation state for the substations and the line is as follows: Construction of the 225 kV double-circuit line from the Siguiri

  substation to the Guineo-Malian border (53.7 km): 43.42%. Construction of the Siguiri 225/30 kV substation: 30.96%. Construction of the Kankan
  225/30 kV substation: 20.57%. Construction of the Kérouané 225/30 kV substation: 36.06%. This delay has had repercussions on procurement and
  the start of construction work, as well as on the completion date scheduled for December 2023.
  As for the extension of the Sanankoroba substation, the kick-off meeting with the contractor Tata took place on January 13, 2023. The advance
  payment was paid end June 2023. Works are scheduled to take 18 months to be completed. As such, works will not be finalized
  before December 2024, well beyond the project's actual closing date of June 30, 2024. The extension of the project's closing date is therefore
  required.
  The updated implementation schedule shows that works will be completed between July and October 2024 for Guinea and by end December 2024
  for Mali. Given that the closing date for the project is June 30, 2024, and in order to ensure that the works are properly and duly executed and
  completed, it has been agreed that the countries will request an extension of the project closing date by one year until June 30, 2025.

  Component 2: Implementation support and capacity building (US$12.2 million):
  Recruitment of a Consultant for the study of the connection of Guinean mining companies to the national interconnected grid: A contract of 290,000
  EUR has been signed with a consortium for a period of execution of four (4) months, on May 26, 2023, and is in the process of being registered. In
  addition, the mining companies Compagnie des Bauxites de Guinée (CBG) and Guinea Aluminium Company (GAC) have expressed an interest in
  being connected to the EDG grid to benefit from hydro electricity from the Kaleta and Souapiti hydro dams. The mining companies would be
  connected through the OMVG substation of Boké.
  As part of the acquisition of special equipment, 4 engines have been delivered and accepted, and the last 2 lorries are currently being delivered.
  Electricity trade between Guinea and Mali:
  The project is recruiting the consultant to support Guinea and Mali drafting and negotiating a PPA between EDM and EDG, in accordance with the
  ECOWAS Regional Electricity Regulatory Authority (ARREC) model. The Bank reviewed and approved the ToR for the consultant.
