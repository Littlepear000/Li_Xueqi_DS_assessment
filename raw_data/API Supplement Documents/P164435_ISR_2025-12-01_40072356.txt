  4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
Nearly half of the targeted health centers (191 out of 360) have been electrified with solar, school electrification (350) is expected to be
commissioned by March 2026. Significant progress has been made in constructing kitchen shelters and installing improved institutional
cookstoves, with half of them already commissioned (285 out of 400). The off-grid solar PV systems and clean cooking subsidy fund has now
reached a steady cruising speed, enabling the scale-up of the RBF and the expansion of the energy-as-a-service pilot, while also ensuring
product quality and after-sales monitoring. In addition, the Private Sector Technical Assistance Facility was created to provide technical
assistance to beneficiaries and other stakeholders, further strengthening the enabling environment. The project is on target to meet its
development objectives.
