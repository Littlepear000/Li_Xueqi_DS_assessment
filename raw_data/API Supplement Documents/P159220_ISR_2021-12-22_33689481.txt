 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  Despite the low disbursement rate, the project has made significant progress since the last mission of June 2021. Review and update of the final
  design documentation for the last leg of the Tyup-Kegen road section (50km) divided into 2-Lots was completed and the bids invitation for civil
  works was published on October 22, 2021. Environmental and Social Assessment (ESIA) report has been prepared and published on IPIG and WB
  websites. Key technical studies under the Aviation and Tourism components are in procurement processes and design for 5 tourist service centers
  and author supervision has commenced in November 2021.

 Overall Comments PDO

  PDO level Indicator 1 (number of vehicles passing through Karkyra border crossing point) - The WB PIU has been working closely with the
  Kyrgyz State Border Officials to obtain data on number of vehicles passing through Karkyra border crossing point. During the regular period of
  April – November 2021, a total of 4,466 vehicles crossed the border between Kazakhstan and the Kyrgyz Republic. The number of crossing
  vehicles is lower than expected due to the prolonged COVID-19 pandemic, which heavily affected the region and its traffic volume.

  Other PDO indicators have remained same since the last ISR.
