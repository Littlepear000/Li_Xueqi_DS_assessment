4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  The implementation of the Strengthening the National Statistical System in Ecuador project has faced challenges in
  budget allocation, data collection, and procurement. As of April 2024, the project has achieved a disbursement rate of
  54%, equivalent to $43.29 million. Delays in budget authorization from the Ministry of Economy and Finance have
  impacted operational activities, leading to adjustments in the original schedule for data collection. Challenges in
  procurement and storage of survey data have also been encountered, resulting in delays in the implementation of key
  surveys such as the National Survey of Income and Expenditure of Urban and Rural Households and the New Labor Force
  Survey. Efforts have been made to address these challenges, with progress in financial management and environmental
  and social aspects but continued monitoring and support are needed for effective implementation.
