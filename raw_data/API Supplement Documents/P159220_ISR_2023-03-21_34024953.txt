 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The Project remains highly relevant to the overall development agenda of the Government. Currently, Project is on track to achieve its Project
  Development Objective (PDO) as set in the Loan Agreement (to increase regional connectivity and support sustainable tourism development in
  Issyk-Oblast). By date, civil works contract is underway along with all other key activities. Moreover, during the last reporting period of second half of
  CY22 the Project Team made significant progress on procurement. As a result of this work, around 65% of the total loan amount has been
  contracted. With this, the pace of disbursement should significantly improve in 2023-2024. Finally, it should be noted that Ministry of Transport and
  Communications of Kyrgyz Republic is putting special effort to ensure that all required documents and procedures are in place.
