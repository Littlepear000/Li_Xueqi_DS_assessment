4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
REMIT is now fully effective and implementation is progressing across all implementing agencies, with first wave of procurement expected to be completed in the coming weeks.
Country progress:
• Uzbekistan: Works contracts for transmission lines and substations are in final stages of procurement, with one at
award stage and another at final evaluation stage.
• Kyrgyz Republic and Tajikistan: Procurement of the program management consultant is in final stages in both
countries, with contract award and signing expected imminently.
• CDC Energia: The REoI for the Regional Pilot Market Consultancy will be launched by October 30th.
