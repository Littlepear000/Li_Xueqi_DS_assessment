 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The objective of this ISR is to record project progress as of August 15, 2021.
  The World Bank’s Board of Executive Directors approved the Strengthening Afghanistan's Financial Intermediation (SAFI) project on September
  28, 2020. The World Bank signed the Project’s Financing Agreement with the Ministry of Finance (MOF) and the Project Agreement with Da
  Afghanistan Bank (DAB) on October 15, 2020. The MOF and DAB signed the Subsidiary Agreement. The MOF also signed an Implementing
  Partner Agreement (IPA) with the Afghan Credit Guarantee Foundation (ACGF). The project was subsequently declared effective on January 06,
  2021.

  The project has two implementing agencies: i) MOF - responsible for project Components 1 and 2 (i.e., Strengthening Credit Guarantee Fund and
  SME Matching Grants Program) with ACGF as the implementing partner; and ii) DAB - responsible for Components 3-6 (Strengthening Deposit
  Insurance, Enhancing DAB’s IT Infrastructure, Strengthening DAB’s Regulatory, and Supervisory Capacity and Project Management for
  Components 3-6).
  Implementation Progress by Implementing Agency
  Ministry of Finance/Afghan Credit Guarantee Foundation (Components 1 and 2)
  ACGF has recorded notable progress with active implementation of credit guarantee operations and related technical assistance. Since
  effectiveness, the project support facilitated ACGF’s onboarding of one new bank (increasing the total number of participating financial institutions
  (PFIs) to five) and the issuance of the first portfolio guarantee (for one of the existing PFIs). As a result, the volume of MSME loans facilitated by
  ACGF doubled compared to the project baseline. ACGF has also commenced with technical assistance activities to participating financial
  institutions to expand lending for the solar projects, with additional planned activities aimed at supporting expansion of outreach to women
  entrepreneurs and agribusinesses.
  The project has also completed all preparatory activities to launch the matching grants program (e.g., developing required manuals, establishing
  and staffing regional offices) finalizing preparation for public launch of the competitive matching grants program for small and medium enterprises.
  Da Afghanistan Bank (Components 3-6)
  DAB has completed all of the required steps towards project operationalization, including hiring of both the Project Director and the Deputy,
  preparation of the annual work plan, procurement plan and the Operations Manual for Components 3-6. DAB has commenced with implementation
  of planned activities with preparation of initial procurement packages.
