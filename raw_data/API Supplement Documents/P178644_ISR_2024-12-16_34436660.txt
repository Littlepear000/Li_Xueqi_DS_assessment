4. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  This is the second implementation status report since the project has been approved by the World Bank Board. The
  Financing Agreement was signed on November 29, 2023 and project's effectiveness was notified on February 26, 2024.
  The project has made progress with five road design-build civil works contracts signed, and technical studies underway
  regarding urban upgrading.
