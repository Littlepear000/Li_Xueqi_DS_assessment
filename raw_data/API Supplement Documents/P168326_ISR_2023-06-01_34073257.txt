 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The PIU and the World Bank project team produced a time-bound action plan with clear deliverables and the progress has been recognized on the
  big ticket items such as a successful implementation of the community-based and matching grants program with close to 300 applications already
  approved. The construction works on three cultural heritage sites in Khatlon are at 30-40% completion. The Government's adoption of the
  diagnostic and design for the Yamchun Fortress rehabilitation is close to completion. Once completed, the PIU will announce an international tender
  to hire an experienced construction company for the assignment.
