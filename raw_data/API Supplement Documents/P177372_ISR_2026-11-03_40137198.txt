4. KEY ISSUES & STATUS
4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
Construction works for the first four priority investments is commencing in October 2026. These investments include roads in Franceville (quartier Dialogue), Lambarene (quartier Simboue), and Oyem (quartier Akoakam, near Tougou-Tougou), as well as upgrading of a primary school located at Libongui in Koulamoutou. Project-
affected persons for the three roads-related investments have been compensated.
Preparation of other investments under Component 1 (urban roads, schools and other public facilities), as well as Component 2 investments (flood risk reduction, including nature-based solutions) is underway. Construction for these subsequent investments is expected to commence in 2027. These investments will be in the four cities already mentioned above, as well as in three more cities - Mouila, Ndende, and Lebamba.
