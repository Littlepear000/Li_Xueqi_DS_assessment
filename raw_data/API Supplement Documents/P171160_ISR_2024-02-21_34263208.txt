            Somali Integrated Statistics and Economic Planning Capacity Building (P171160)



  The Somali Integrated Statistics and Economic Planning Capacity Building Project (SISEPCBP) aims to strengthen the national statistical system in
  the collection, processing and dissemination of poverty and selected macro-economic data, strengthening of national M&E, public finance, and
  macroeconomic to inform development policy and poverty reduction activities. Total IDA funding of the project is US$ 25 million. The project
  became effective on September 29, 2020. Recent accomplishments of the project includes the completion of 5 training courses in collaboration with
  East Africa Statistical Training Center, the launch of National Integrated M&E framework (NIMEF), and 6th National Economic Policy Forum.
