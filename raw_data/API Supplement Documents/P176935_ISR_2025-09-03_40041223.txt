Progress towards achieving the Project Development Objective (PDO): The PDO is to expand coverage of
shock responsive safety net support among the poor and vulnerable and strengthen the national safety net delivery
system. The Project has been making positive progress in implementation and is on track to achieve the PDO. The
direct benefit transfer program has supported about 6 million poor and vulnerable households selected from the
Social Register with shock responsive cash transfers. Over 2.4 million households have received up to three
tranches of support following biometric verification of the adult member recipient from the eligible households.
Following the decision of government, made in March 2024, only households with members biometrically verified
will continue to receive cash transfer support under the direct benefit transfer program.
Strengthening the national social safety net delivery system. To strengthen the Social Register, a targeted NIN
validation and registration drive for households captured in the Social Register commenced on April 7, in
collaboration with the National Identity Management Commission (NIMC). So far over 6.7 million households have
been visited and NIMC is in the process of verifying over 9 million NINs. At the end of the NIN drive, all households
in the Social Register are expected to have at least one adult member with a validated NIN. The payments delivery
mechanism is fully digital, ensuring all payments go directly to verified and active beneficiary owned accounts;
ensuring the right beneficiary is the recipient of the support.
Survey of Direct Benefit Transfer Beneficiaries. The World Bank task team carried out a survey of beneficiaries
of the cash transfers, to complement monitoring and supervision of the delivery of the program. The survey as
designed to specifically shed light on the key issues and concerns surrounding the program, including those
relating to the Social Register, delivery of the program and if the program has generated the intended results. The
results, which largely corroborate the delivery system and program objectives, have been shared with the
implementing ministry and agencies, and the interministerial panel overseeing the social investment programs. One
key feedback from the survey is the need to carry out more outreach and sensitization activities targeted at the
general public, particularly to the intended beneficiaries of the program.
