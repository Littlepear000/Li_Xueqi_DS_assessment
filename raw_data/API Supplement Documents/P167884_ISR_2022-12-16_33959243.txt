 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The Guinea Support to Local Governance Project (SLGP) has achieved important results three years into implementation. The project has
  supported the Ministry of Territorial Administration and Decentralization, the National Agency for Financing Local Communities (ANAFIC) and local
  governments with the establishment and implementation of mechanisms that enabled the transfer of GNF 535 billion (US$ 62 million) in local
  development funding (FNDL) to implement a total of 1498 subprojects across Guinea’s 356 communes: 795 subprojects planned in 2019 and 2020
  were completed; 703 more planned in 2021 and 2022 are under implementation with an average execution rate of 52 percent. The PAGL supported
  community engagement in the planning and monitoring of these investments, which include education, healthcare and economic infrastructure. The
  project’s first two Disbursement-Linked Indicators (DLIs), whose release was conditional on the signing of regulatory texts to operationalize FNDL
  (DLI 1) and the FNDL disbursement rate from the national budget to the ANAFIC (DLI 2), were achieved.

  However, the pace of implementation has slowed down this year due to steps taken by the current administration to improve the
  accountability of public funds managed by local government (LG). The accounts of communes were frozen in December 2021 pending
  verification of their adequate use of public funds. It has halted or slowed down key project milestones that are predicated on the execution of local
  development funding by communes.
  Agreed actions and corrective measures. to resume and accelerate the implementation and disbursement. A World Bank mission planned
  for December 5-14, 2022, will outline a restructuration plan to mitigate the effects of the above issues and optimize the project’s contribution to the
  Government of Guinea’s efforts to improve accountability and transparency in public funds use. The restructuration will likely include a revision of
  the project timeline and planned activities. The mission will also seek to clarify the timeline for the completion of the verification of LG accounts and
  produce a revised timeline for SGLP 2 negotiations and board date.
