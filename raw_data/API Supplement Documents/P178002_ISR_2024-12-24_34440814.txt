1. OBJECTIVE


 1.1 Development Objective
   Original Development Objective (Approved as part of Approval package on 16-Dec-2022)
   The PDO is to improve the capacity of the Lao PDR Statistical System to produce and disseminate good quality statistics in
   a timely manner and to enhance use of key statistics, and in case of an Eligible Crisis or Emergency, respond promptly and
   effectively to it.

4. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  The third implementation support mission took place from October 14 to 25, 2024, with participation from officials of the
  Lao Statistics Bureau (LSB), the Ministry of Planning and Investment, and representatives from 11 relevant ministries and
  agencies. The mission noted progress in several areas, including the implementation of the Lao Expenditure and
  Consumption Survey, the data integration agenda—particularly regarding international merchandise trade statistics—the
  publication of three new economic aggregates, and preparatory activities for the development of an integrated
  information system and centralized portal. However, activities related to GDP rebasing and price statistics have
  experienced delays. LSB has committed to accelerating these activities ahead of the next implementation support mission
  to ensure the project remains on track to achieve its development objectives and maintains a satisfactory rating.
