 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The project is making good progress toward achieving the set objectives. Key activities for design services on Infrastructure and Tourism
  Development in Osh and Uzgen are ongoing. Key studies for agriculture development have also advanced.
  The Solid Waste Management assessment in the selected towns is expected to be finalized by November 30, 2022, to inform the next set of actions
  to address the identified shortcomings.
  The Small Grants Program implementation will start at the beginning of 2023.
