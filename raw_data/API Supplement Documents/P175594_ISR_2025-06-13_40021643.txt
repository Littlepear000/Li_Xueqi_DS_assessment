1. PROJECT DEVELOPMENT OBJECTIVE


 1.1 Development Objective
   Original Development Objective (Approved as part of Approval package on 19-May-2022)
   The Program for Results Development Objective (PDO) is to increase access of poor and vulnerable households to safety
   nets programs and improve the performance of delivery systems.

3. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


 3.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
   The Mid-term review (MTR), conducted in May 2025, confirmed the good performance of the Program that is on track to
   meet its objectives. It also confirmed its significant contribution to the World Bank Group’s (WBG) global targets and
   scorecard indicators on social safety net coverage, access to digital services and usage of financial services, including for
   women.

   The Program achieved visible results in increasing access for poor and vulnerable households to safety net programs. The
   Productive Cash Transfers Program (Programme de Transferts Monétaires Productifs, PTMP) reached 1,356,633 new
   beneficiaries since 2023, 761,950 of whom female, overachieving its target. Cumulatively since 2016, the PTMP reached
   457,000 households with regular quarterly digital transfers (Disbursement Linked Indicator, DLI 4). These include 230,000
   active beneficiaries across the country, with higher concentration in 14 prioritized regions, accompanied by a steady
   number of dedicated frontline social workers (DLI 5). Women represent about 70% of designated PTMP recipients,

  confirming progress towards DLI 6. Economic inclusion measures are being implemented according to plan, indicating rapid
  progress towards DLI 7. The full set of interventions has been offered to cohort 5 and measures are starting to be rolled-
  out for cohort 6. Close to 2,400 new community saving and credit associations (Associations de Valorisation de l'Entraide
  Communautaire, AVECs) have been established, strengthening not only financial literacy and access to credit for income
  generating activities of PTMP beneficiairies, but also community cohesion. This progress enabled meeting most targets
  linked to PTMP implementation, including the early achievement of the relevant program development objective indicator
  on safety net coverage. Partnerships between the PTMP and other social programs are being strengthened (linked to DLI
  8). A convention finalized between the Bureau de Coordination des Programmes des Filets Sociaux (BCPFS) and the Caisse
  National d’Assurance Maladie (CNAM) for the identification of poor and vulnerable households eligible for the non-
  contributory health insurance program (RAM-CMU) is an encouraging step towards greater synergies in the safety net
  system. The PTMP has established itself as a solid platform for economic inclusion and women’s empowerment. Progress
  towards DLI 9 (shock-response) has been steady in the last year, with a pilot response to floods implemented in the Cavally
  region in the last semester that should generate lessons to inform the strategic dialogue on the trajectory towards adaptive
  social protection.

  After two years of delays, the last six months witnessed a remarkable acceleration in the operationalization of the Unique
  Social Registry, which is intended to be used across sectors and ministries for targeting poor and vulnerable households for
  social programs. An expansion to approach nationwide coverage is ongoing (advancing towards DLI 1), as are initial
  measures to sustain its dynamism and to prepare for utilisation at scale (DLI 2). The Social Centers pilot (linked to DLI 3)
  has started implementation, based on a revised concept agreed in 2024, focusing on strengthening social centers’ capacity
  for sensitisation of vulnerable populations to improve health outcomes through access to the Couverture Maladie
  Universelle (CMU).
