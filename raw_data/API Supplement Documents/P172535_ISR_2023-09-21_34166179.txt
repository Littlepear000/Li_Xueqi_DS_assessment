 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The Loan and Grants for the Project were declared effective on October 5, 2021. The first disbursement was processed on March 2022, to finance
  home improvement interventions in selected municipalities. For the June 2023 mission, the disbursement level remains at 4.2% of the total
  Project’s resources. No additional disbursement has been requested (the next disbursement request is expected in Quarter 2 FY 2024).

  The GoC and WB have agreed on the importance of balancing the execution status of the Operation, for which it will request a second
  disbursement in November 2023. This disbursement must be supported in certain contracts according to the need for resources, expecting an
  approximate request of USD 15,9 M (Projection to June 2024). Likewise, from the progress in the execution of the project, the GoC has manifested
  additional requests in May and October 2024 to comply with the contracting defined for November 2023.

  The restructuring process, which has not yet been finalized, has not allowed the pari passu to be made more flexible for the resources of the GPG
  Grant (US 15 M). This process is expected to end in September 2023, releasing this Grant from the pari passu restriction and reducing the risk of
  financial management by allowing the use of these resources in a more streamlined manner.

  The GoC and the Bank will continue holding follow-up meetings with the internal teams in the MVCT and the Project Implementing Unit (PIU), where
  the teams of the MHCP, DNP, APC Colombia, and other entities involved in the project also participate. This, in order to identify concrete actions to
  bottlenecks that arise for the execution of the Project.
