 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The objective of the project is to expand long-term finance to private firms in selected infrastructure sectors in Eastern and Southern Africa. The
  project seeks to address one of the main barriers to infrastructure development, i.e. access to suitable finance. The project became effective
  October 5, 2020, during Covid-19. The global economic downturn caused by the pandemic has weakened the appetite for private investment in
  infrastructure. As a result several planned transactions did not materialize and disbursements to approved sub-projects have been slower than
  projected. However, Eastern and Southern Trade and Development Bank (TDB) has managed to add several new deals to the pipeline and are
  optimistic that investor confidence in the sector is being restored and the pent-up demand will result in increased disbursement to the sub-projects
  in the pipeline. Further, the demand for infrastructure development is likely to increase as infrastructure development is pursued as a path to post-
  pandemic recovery and green, resilient, inclusive development. Hence the relevance of the objectives and demand for this project will increase
  even more.

  On the technical assistance managed by COMESA, the FY22 workplan is being implemented and the FY23 workplan is being finalized. Overall
  progress in the delivery of the workplan has improved with several deliverables in advance stages of completion. The regional off grid renewable
  energy market assessment has been completed and stakeholders’ consultation conducted. Three main deliverables are underway: (i) the
  development of COMESA model solar standards (ii) the COMESA model solar common customs tariff framework and (iii) the updating of the 2008
  COMESA Model Energy Policy. These deliverables are in the final stages of consultation with a validation workshop being planned for December
  5-7; following which are expected to be submitted to the COMESA Ministers of Infrastructure for approval in Q1 2023.
