 Project Development Objectives

  Project Development Objective (from Project Appraisal Document)
  The proposed Project Development Objective (PDO) is (i) to enhance connectivity and road safety along the Zinder - Agadez road section in
  Northeastern Niger through the rehabilitation of the Tanout – Tiguidit road segment and maintenance of the full Zinder - Agadez section, and (ii) to
  improve access to basic socio-economic infrastructure for selected communities, along the Zinder - Agadez road section.

  Has the Project Development Objective been changed since Board Approval of the Project Objective?
  No

 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  This is the first ISR, submitted 3 months after project approval on March 2,2021. The financing agreements were just signed on June 1,2021, 3
  months after the project approval. This unusual long delay before signing FAs was due to the political transition in the country (following the
  December 2020 elections, and subsequent new government in place). The following is the implementation status of the project:
  1. Effectiveness conditions. Three conditions ( the legal opinion and two actions related to SEA/SH mitigation measures) are needed to be fulfilled
  so that the project can be declared effective. The Project Implementation Unit (PIU) has advanced on the 2 actions related to the SEA/SH mitigation
  measures : (i) the recruitment of the SEA/SH specialist is underway and the contract is expect to be signed by end of June, (ii) the GBV action plan

  2. Disbursement condition. The disbursement of the major category of spending - namely the civil works for the rehabilitation of the Tanout-
  Tiguidit section - is conditioned to the mobilizing by the government of 200,000 USD of counterpart funding for the implementation of the
  resettlement action plan. The counterpart funding was already included into the government Budget Act which expect to be approved by the
  Parliement before end of June 2021.
  3. Activities of the first year of implementation. Key procurement processes are on track for the Output Performance based Road Contracts
  (OPRC) under Component 1: Road reconstruction, maintenance and safety improvements along the Zinder – Agadez Road Section. The
  prequalification stage for the OPRC contractors has been finalized and the publication of the bidding documents for OPRC contracts is expected to
  be done by mid June 2021. The Bank gave the non-objection for the shortlist and the request for proposals for the recruitment of consultants in
  charge of the monitoring and verification of the OPRC contracts.
