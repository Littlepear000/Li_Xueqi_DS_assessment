1. OBJECTIVE


 1.1 Development Objective
   Original Development Objective (Approved as part of Approval package on 27-Feb-2025)
   To improve the employability of students and strengthen the quality and governance of higher education and scientific
   research institutions.

   Has the Development Objective been changed since Board Approval of the Project Objective?

   No
