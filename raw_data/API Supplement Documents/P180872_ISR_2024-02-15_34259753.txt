4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  The project is making steady progress toward effectiveness and the start of project implementation. The project was
  approved by the World Bank Board of Directors on November 14th, 2023. The loan agreement between the World Bank
  and the Ministry of Economy and Finance (MEF) was signed on January 26th, 2024. The project is expected to become
  effective in March or April of 2024, once the loan agreement has been endorsed (“refrendado”) by the Comptroller’s Office
  (Contraloria General de la República - CGR) and the conditions for project effectiveness are met.
