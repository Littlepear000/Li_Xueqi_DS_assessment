 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The reactivation of the World Bank portfolio in Guinea in the first quarter of 2022, following the triggering of OP7.30, enabled this project to reinitiate
  its prerequisite activities.
  Several prerequisites for the effective implementation of the project remain to be put in place, in particular legal covenants including (i) the
  replacement of the accountant who resigned and the recruitment of an internal auditor which are both at the contract negotiation stage, (ii) FM
  software to be installed soon, (iii) the establishment of effective coordination between the PIU and beneficiaries institutions with regards
  to procurement, M&E and ESF reporting. The assessment of the implementation of the mining reform SESA’s recommendations, which is key for
  the project ESF monitoring, has also been delayed.

  These bottlenecks slow the implementation of the project's core activities, in particular the acquisition of technical equipment and the mobilization of
  the technical assistance to lead the implementation of capacity support activities planned for key ministerial departments. Having the latter in place
  should facilitate the roll-out of implementation of many activities and the achievement of expected results.
  The project’s objectives of strengthening the governance and the management of renewable and non-renewable natural resources are key in this
  period of political transition in Guinea. The project must therefore initiate its activities as quickly as possible to support this pivotal period.
