1. PROJECT DEVELOPMENT OBJECTIVE


 1.1 Development Objective
   Original Development Objective (Approved as part of Approval package on 01-Oct-2025)
   The program development objective is to increase and sustain access to at least basic water supply, sanitation and
   hygiene services in all rural districts.

3. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


 3.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
   The Program was approved by the World Bank Board on September 30, 2025. The Financing Agreement was signed on
   November 21, 2025. The effectiveness deadline is February 19, 2026, and both Ministry of Water Mainland and Ministry
   of Water, Energy and Minerals – Zanzibar are making significant progress towards meeting this deadline.
