 Project Development Objectives

  Project Development Objective (from Project Appraisal Document)
  The objective of the Project is to improve the management of fish and forest resources and access to related economic opportunities in target
  areas.

  Has the Project Development Objective been changed since Board Approval of the Project Objective?
  No

 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The Project was approved on July 5, 2022 and its Financing Agreement signed on July 7,2002. It became effective on September 30, 2022.
  The two Project's Implementing Agencies (the Ministry of Environment, Sustainable Development, and Ecological Transition and the Ministry of
  Fisheries and Maritime Economy) are taking steps towards implementation readiness and procurement of first packages/lots should be initiated
  over the next two months.
