 MPA Table
 Multiphase Programmatic Approach (MPA) Status

 MPA Table Body
 The Emergency Locust Response Program (ELRP) MPA now has three active phases. Phases 1 and 2 of the MPA were approved in fiscal year
 2020, committing a total of US$200 million.

 Phase 1 (P173702) was approved for US$160 million and included Ethiopia, Kenya, Uganda and Djibouti. Only Uganda Financing Agreement is not
 yet signed, Phase one has disbursed US$74 million.

 Phase 2 (P174065) operates only in Somalia. and has a total commitment of US$115 million out of which 35% has been disbursed.

 Phase 3 (P174546) was approved in June 2021 and includes a US$50.7 million grant to South Sudan and a US$3 million grant to the
 Intergovernmental Authority on Development to facilitate information sharing, national/regional preparedness, and collaborations on areas such as
 research and development among its member states in response to the DL threat. It is completing the steps for grant effectiveness now.

 Phase 4 (P176950) will include a proposed US$65 million grant to Sudan and is currently under preparation with an anticipated Board date in
 November 2021. Once approved, this will raise the total amount program commitment through ERLP to US$393.7 million.
