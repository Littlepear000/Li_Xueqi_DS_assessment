 Project Development Objectives

  Project Development Objective (from Project Appraisal Document)
  The PDO is to improve the capacity of the Lao PDR Statistical System to produce and disseminate good quality statistics in a timely manner and to
  enhance use of key statistics, and in case of an Eligible Crisis or Emergency, respond promptly and effectively to it.

  Has the Project Development Objective been changed since Board Approval of the Project Objective?
  No

 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The project became effective on May 12, 2023. The implementation support mission was conducted from October 9-25, 2023, with participation
  from officials from the Lao Statistics Bureau, Ministry of Planning and Investment and 11 relevant ministries and agencies The mission noted good
  progress had been made in the implementation of activities under Subcomponent 1.2 Social Statistics and Project Management. The Lao Statistics
  Bureau agreed to speed up the preparation of activities under Subcomponent 1.1 Economic Statistics and Component 2 Enhancing Use of
  Statistics.
