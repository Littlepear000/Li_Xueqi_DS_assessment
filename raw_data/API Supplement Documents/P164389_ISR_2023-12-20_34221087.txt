 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The Project was signed on February 28, 2022 and was declared effective as of June 1, 2022. The Project is not on track to achieve the Project
  Development Objective within the current implementation period, and the Mid-Term Review mission which took place in November 2023 discussed
  the possibility of restructuring the Project (including a potential extension) to bring it onto a successful path to achieving its objectives. To accelerate
  project implementation, the National Water Commission (CONAGUA) and the Valley of Mexico's Water Basin Agency (OCAVM) are preparing a
  number of works, supervision and goods procurement packages to be financed under the Project.

                                      CONAGUA has implemented several mechanisms for beneficiaries and stakeholders to provide feedback
                                      and to capture petitions and complaints on the impacts caused by the hydraulic infrastructure of the
                                      Cutzamala System. These have been detailed in a document sent to the Bank and approved in December
                                      2022. The grievance redress mechanism (GRM) is accessible to project-affected people and can receive
                                      grievances through the following channels:
                                      By email addressed to the Head of the Internal Control Body (OIC),
                                               Through the Sistema Integral de Denuncias Ciudadanas (SIDEC) under the Ministry of Public
                                                Administration,
                                               In writing through the OCAVM Reports Office,
                                               In person through the General Residence for the Construction of the Cutzamala System and/or
                                                the General Residence of Operation of the Cutzamala System,
                                               Through institutional email,
                                               Through official letters addressed to the Director General of CONAGUA.
                                      In addition, and as part of the institutional strengthening component, the Project will continue to support
                                      CONAGUA in boosting awareness of the existence of these grievance-handling procedures among the
                                      public and to enhance the mechanisms with which grievances are redressed as they relate to the Project.
                                      CONAGUA has provided evidence that the GRM is receiving and processing complaints for past activities.
                                      Nevertheless, as project activities have yet to start implementation, this evidence does not yet concern the
                                      PROSEGHIR, and future evidence will be documented as these activities are rolled out – this could
                                      happen as early as the first quarter of CY 2024, namely after the Project's first 4 procurement packages
                                      are successfully launched and awarded.
