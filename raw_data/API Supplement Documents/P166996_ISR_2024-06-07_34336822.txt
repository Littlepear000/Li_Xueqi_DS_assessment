 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The project has made significant implementation progress across all components and progress toward the achievement of the project development
  objectives.
  Component 1: Commission For Technical And Vocational Educational Training (CTVET) has now enrolled 11,500 apprentices, with 4,517
  commencing training from four regions. Eighty-five percent of beneficiaries are female. These will account for Disbursement Linked Indicators (DLIs)

  DLI 2.1, 2.2 and 2.3 linked to provision of apprenticeship training for 20,000 individuals. Further, 8,000 facilitators across the 16 regions of Ghana
  have been trained in the delivery of the Competency Based Training (CBT) curriculum and CTVET has developed 73 CBT curricula and accredited
  144 institutions across the country to provide CBT linked to DLIs 1.2 and 1.3 related to development and approval of CBT packages and
  accreditation of training providers.
  Component 2.1: Ghana Enterprises Agency (GEA) initiated basic entrepreneurship training on April 25, 2023, and has reached out to around
  30,000 beneficiaries for the entrepreneurship training program, of which 76 percent are women and 1.7 percent are persons with disabilities (PWD).
  As of January 2024, 3,031 grants have been approved and are in the process of being awarded to beneficiaries. GEA achieved DLIs 3.1 and 3.2
  pertaining to provision of entrepreneurship training to 20,000 individuals and awarding of competitive business start-up capital grants to 2,000
  businesses. It is also on track to achieve DLI 3.3 by June 2024.
  Component 2.2: CTVET continues to operate the Ghana Skills Development Fund (GSDF) through a private sector firm and has competitively
  awarded 221 grants to private enterprises across Ghana. An estimated 13,300 individuals are expected to benefit from these enterprises. CTVET
  has successfully achieved DLIs 4.3 related to operationalizing GSDF through private sector firm and has also achieved DLIs 5.1 and 5.2 related to
  awarding 200 competitive grants from GSDF. CTVET is also on track to achieve DLI 5.3 by October 2024.
  Component 3: The implementation of activities under this component has progressed significantly, particularly the development of the Ghana
  Labour Market Information System (GLMIS) and the construction of the Labour Department (LD) head office and District Public Employment
  Centers (PECs). The GLMIS is live and can be accessed at www.glmis.gov.gh by jobseekers, employers, and public and private institutions. The
  Ministry of Labour and Employment (MELR) team has signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) with the Ghana Statistical Services (GSS) to
  support data sharing with the system and is actively working to partner with additional institutions of relevance to feed the system with the required
  labor market statistics. Procurement of assistive devices to provide enhancements for Persons with Disability (PWDs) is being initiated by the MELR
  to complete the system. Progress in works at the Labour Department (LD) head office and fourteen out of sixteen PECs was estimated at 60
  percent, verified by a series of field visits to twelve PEC sites. Procurement of Information and Communication Technology (ICT) equipment has
  been initiated, and a staffing plan and change management strategy have been proposed to support the operationalization of the PECs. Progress
  on independent reviews of Youth Employment and Skills Development Programs is still delayed.
  Component 4 - The Project Coordination Unit PCU continues actively interacting with and supporting the implementing and partnering agencies in
  executing their activities, participating in various support meetings, and organizing workshops and other events (virtual and in-person). The PCU
  continues to supervise the preparation of the government mid-term report, and it was agreed that the report, including the beneficiary assessment
  undertaken under the project, will be completed in April 2024. The PCU continues to facilitate the Ministry of Environment, Science, Technology and
  Innovation (MESTI), including its critical capacity development activities.
