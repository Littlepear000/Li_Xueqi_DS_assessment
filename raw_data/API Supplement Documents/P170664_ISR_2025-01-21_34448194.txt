4. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  The mission noted that the PDO achievement is on track and highlighted both the progress made and several areas where
  immediate action is needed to address challenges across each of the sub-components (further described in the annex).
  Overall implementation progress remains moderately satisfactory. While the AF was approved in September 2023, it only
  became effective in August 2024. As a result, activities are only getting started in the AF project states, and the task team
  expects rapid progress on project activities in subsequent missions.
  The PDO is “to improve secondary education opportunities among girls in targeted areas in participating states”, and
  progress towards achievement of the PDO is being measured by 5 PDO indicators and 17 intermediate results (IR)
  indicators. On PDO indicators, three of the five Year 3 targets defined in the results framework have been surpassed (girls
  currently enrolled in Junior Secondary School (JSS) and Senior Secondary School (SSS) in participating states, girls’
  completion rate in participating states, and girls trained who demonstrate digital literacy), while the two remaining
  indicators are on track. Based on project administrative data provided by the State Project Implementation Units and
  National Project Coordinating Unit, the project has reached 3,967,037 beneficiaries, of which 2,198,596 are girls in the 7
  parents states. Completed construction of 100 schools and additional 180 by January 2025. Project disbursement is at
  58% while additional states have started implementation and will be ready to disburse School Improvement Grant and
  Conditional Cash Transfer to schools and girls’ by March, 2025. Key decisions agreed during the mission include:
      1. All states to update the Prototype Design and BoQ of school construction by January 31, 2025.
      2. Completion of the recruitment of all necessary consultants for supervision, including a consultant to carry out
           environmental and social (E&S) assessment of all schools, and completion of SBMC trainings, validations of
           SBMCs and schools, and E&S screening in all schools in each state (Sub-component 1.2) by April 18, 2025.
      3. States are to finalize engagement of life skills implementing partner (IP) and conduct an inception
           meeting/workshop with IP (Subcomponent 2.2a) by January 31, 2025.
      4. Development of digital training manuals by AF states and procurement of digital devices (subcomponent 2.2b) by
           April 18, 2025.
      5. Engagement of a non-state actor/Local NGO to support implementation of second chance training activities and
           identification of non-formal centers to be included in the project (subcomponent 2.2c) by March 21, 2025.
      6. Massive sensitization campaign to enroll girls in school and educate stakeholders about financial incentives (FI)
           and all states are to complete registration and verification process for FI beneficiaries (P5/P6 and JSS2/JSS3) by
           February 28, 2025.
