 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The Directors Generals steering committee meeting was held in Cotonou from March 25 to 29, 2024 to conduct the regional level MTR for the
  seven project participating countries and the regional organizations. The steering committee, with the World Bank TTLs, reviewed the Mid-Term
  Reports prepared for each project participating countries and regional organizations and made recommendations.

  The MTR mission confirmed that the project design continues to be adequate to achieve the expected results and ensure their sustainability.

  The Mid-Term Review noted that significant progress has been made in the project implementation in the seven project-participating countries, the
  African Union, and ECOWAS.

  The Client's commitment to the project and willingness to work with the implementing entities, government authorities, and key stakeholders was
  good. However, some delays in procurement and low disbursement rates need to be improved to move implementation at a sustained pace to
  achieve the development objectives by the closing date. An action plan was agreed with the respective PIUs to address these implementation
  challenges.

  The timeliness of procurement of goods and services and the resultant disbursement have not been consistent in some countries for different
  reasons. However, most countries are picking up the pace of implementation. The respective World Bank teams will continue to organize follow-up
  technical missions to accelerate implementation and address challenges in implementation in all seven participating countries.
  Recommendations
            It is recommended that countries expedite user satisfaction survey implementation to establish the baseline or monitor progress as
            needed.

             To meet the end target for ICI, the calculation should consider the specific institutional reform each country undertakes to appreciate the
              differences between countries and enable accurate scoring.

             It is recommended that each county expedite the implementation of lagging activities.

             To fully implement and monitor the program activities and improve the performance of the project's financial management and
              procurement.

             Given COVID-19 pandemic-induced delays and project implementation challenges in the first year of the project, consider extending the
              project closing date if there is strong justification that more time is needed to fully meet the PDO.

  Actions the Bank and Government team will be working on:
       1. Improving the procurement processes, particularly contract management. This is essential for the quality and timely delivery of products
            and progress of the project.
       2. Updating implementation plans with quarterly disbursement projections for the remainder of the project period for close monitoring and
            follow-up.
       3. Providing the required technical assistance as needed.
