 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The Crisis Resilient Social Protection program became effective on March 29, 2021. It is a 4-year, $600 million Program for Results operation with a
  $ 5 million Technical Assistance component. The program has three (3) result areas focusing on; a) Building a crisis resilient delivery system; b)
  Establishing a Hybrid Social Protection (SP) system for resilience of poor against future crisis; c) Protecting human capital through demand-side
  education and health initiatives for children.
  The recent mid-term review (MTR) noted good progress towards achievement of most results ahead of the program’s closing date, as elaborated in
  the results section below. There are, however, delays in the design for the Hybrid Social Protection system and the new payment system which
  require attention.
