 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  Since the last mission in March 2022, the project has made good progress towards implementing community-level mobilization activities and
  capacity building activities for participatory development (subcomponent 1b).
  The implementing agency, National Social Investment Fund of Tajikistan (NSIFT), together with the Training and Facilitating Partners (TFPs),
  National Association of Dekhan Farmers and Agro-Services Consulting, completed the community mobilization activities in 22 jamoats in Khatlon
  and Gorno-Badakhshan Autonomous Region (GBAO). The community mobilization activities resulted in the prioritization of around 50 subprojects
  (SPs). The most commonly prioritized SPs are related to drinking water supply, irrigation water, the rehabilitation of connector roads, or the
  construction or expansion of schools. NSIFT will launch the construction works of a batch of SPs in October 2022. In June 2022, the TFPs launched
  the mobilization for the second batch of jamoats and will complete this process by the end of 2022.

  In preparation for the delivery of training for youth under subcomponent 2a, NSIFT completed the construction or renovation of 14 of 17 youth
  facilities (subcomponent 1c) in Khatlon and GBAO. The Implementing Partner for subcomponent 2a, BDO, concluded two rounds of Training for
  Trainers for the youth-inclusive services between January and June 2022, and delivered a round of pilot trainings to youth in June 2022 to test the
  training materials with the target beneficiaries.
  The project’s website includes a Beneficiary Feedback Mechanism module to collect grievances/queries and track the status of beneficiary
  feedback and grievances received (https://sersp.nsift.tj/bfm-grm).
