 Project Development Objectives

  Project Development Objective (from Project Appraisal Document)
  The proposed Project Development Objective (PDO) is (i) to enhance connectivity and road safety along the Zinder - Agadez road section in
  Northeastern Niger through the rehabilitation of the Tanout – Tiguidit road segment and maintenance of the full Zinder - Agadez section, and (ii) to
  improve access to basic socio-economic infrastructure for selected communities, along the Zinder - Agadez road section.

  Has the Project Development Objective been changed since Board Approval of the Project Objective?
  No

 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  This second ISR is based on the virtual implementation support mission of November 2022.
  The project became effective before the deadline on September 22, 2021.
  The Project development objective is satisfactory because the 7 years 10 months implementation is sufficient to implement both the component
  " road reconstruction, maintenance and safety improvement along the Zinder - Agadez Road Section" and the component " Improved accessibility
  and community infrastructure along Zinder - Agadez Section".
  The implementation progress is satisfactory and the components implementation are as follow:

  Component 1: Road reconstruction, maintenance and safety improvement along the Zinder - Agadez Road Section
  Procurement of Output Performance based Road Contracts.
  The recruitment of the contractors ( 2 contractors) in charge of the rehabilitation and maintenance of the Agadez-Zinder road sections is at the
  Bidding evaluation report and recommendation of contract award stage. The recruitment was delayed due to the time taken by the client to treat the
  complaints received after the bid opening. The recruitment of the monitoring and verification engineers is at the evaluation of technical proposal
  stage.
  Component 2: Improved accessibility and community infrastructure along Zinder - Agadez Section
  Prior to the implementation of this component a document which gives details on the criteria and procedures for identifying activities to be financed
  under this component is underway and is expected to be available before end of December 2021.
  Component 3: Project Management and implementation support
  The steering committee and the technical committee are now both in place before the stipulated deadline in the financing agreement. The budgeted
  annual work plan for the year 2022 was prepared and is under review at the bank.
  The recruitment of the NGO in charge of the implementation and management of gender based violence risk is at the evaluation of technical and
  financial proposals stage.
