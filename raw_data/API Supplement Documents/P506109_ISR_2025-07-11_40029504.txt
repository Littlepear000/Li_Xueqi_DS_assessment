  The Project has made significant progress in the past three months (March – June 2025). The loan and guarantee
  agreements for the project have been signed by the borrower CEL in May 2025 and by the Ministry of Finance in June
  2025. CEL and the project implementing entity LaGeo are working on meeting the remaining conditions for the project
  effectiveness including on the subsidiary agreement for the project and project operations manual. In addition, LaGeo
  has started preparing the procurement documents and plan for the initial contracts for the geothermal drilling services
  for the project.
