1. OBJECTIVE


 1.1 Development Objective
   Original Development Objective (Approved as part of Approval package on 16-Jun-2020)
   The development objectives of this project are to strengthen participatory local governance, improve the quality of local
   infrastructure, both in targeted communities, and increase extracurricular or livelihood opportunities for youth.
   Has the Development Objective been changed since Board Approval of the Project Objective?

   Yes


   Current Development Objective (Approved as part of Additional Financing package Seq. 1 on 19-May-2025)
   Strengthen participatory local governance, improve the quality of local infrastructure and increase livelihood
   opportunities in targeted jamoats.

4. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  The SERSP approved in June 2019 with an IDA grant of US$37 million, supports the Government of Tajikistan’s efforts to
  build resilience in communities in two regions (Khatlon and GBAO) that share borders with Afghanistan. It targets five
  districts in the Khatlon region and four districts in the GBAO region, including 55 sub-districts, relatively large populations,
  a combination of rural and urban areas, and limited coverage by other donor interventions. Based on the Government
  request, the project was later restructured in 2023, and project’s closing date was extended by 18 months from June 30,
  2024, to December 31, 2025. The project is on track to achieve most of its activities and results indicators.
  Implementation of the subprojects (SPs) has accelerated, with an additional 10 SPs from the original and additional
  districts completed (for a total of 163 completed SPs out of 208). The remaining 45 SPs are under implementation.
  Additional financing project for US$25mln. (IDA grant) was approved by the WB management on May 19, 2025.
