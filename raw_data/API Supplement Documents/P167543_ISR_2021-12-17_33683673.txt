 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The Niger Smart Village project which became effective on October 21, 2020 was officially launched in Niger by local authorities on December 3,
  2020. The project implementation unit (PIU) has migrated from the transitional PIU, Public Sector Capacity and Performance for Services (PCDS) to
  the newly recruited team within the National Information Society Agency (ANSI) since December 31, 2020.

  After a year of project effectiveness, the PIU has made stride establishing its key foundational elements especially (i) the recruitment of key
  personnel, except for the internal auditor whose recruitment process is at the final stage as of December 2021 and (ii) meeting key conditions of
  disbursement listed in the Project Financing Agreement. There is good progress in carrying out the required preliminary studies for project
