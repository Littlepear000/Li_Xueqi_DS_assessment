 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The Operation became fully effective in January 2023 and the first disbursement was made for the Rwanda component for a prior result. The
  Implementation Support Missions held in February 2023 for Rwanda, Tanzania, Kenya, and EAC components confirmed the implementation
  progress for year one Disbursement Linked Results (DLRs) and noted substantive delays in implementation of project activities among certain
  Implementing Agencies. Progress by Borrower is as follows:
          The Kenya component has experienced delayed implementation of several first year DLRs due to funding shortfalls from the Government
           of Kenya. However, the provision of sufficient funds would still support the completion of a number of DLRs within year 1, due to their brief
           implementation periods. Moreover, there have been delays in the release of completed reports by KNBS, including quarterly labor reports
           and a poverty report that makes up part of the Program DLRs. The release of the reports alongside two other soon-to-be complete survey
           reports will trigger the Kenya component’s first disbursement.
          The Rwanda component received its first disbursement for prior results, which include the digital cartography and pilot for the fifth Rwanda
           population and housing census. Implementation of several activities due to be achieved within year 2 of the Program is on course and all
           Year 1 DLRs are on track to be completed on time.
          While a prior result for the Tanzania component has been achieved, the corresponding disbursement is yet to be done (the Borrower has
           not requested disbursement). The Program has experienced delays in implementation, due to the implementation of the 2022 Population
           and Housing census. However, a work plan for achieving DLRS has been drafted and institutional arrangements for the Program are
           being initiated.
          Implementation of the EAC component is underway and the Annual Work Plan and Budget, and Procurement Plan have been shared with
           the Bank. Procurement delays due to the EAC's slow internal processes have slowed down the implementation of key activities, and the
           EAC is to consider how to streamline their internal procurement processes to better support the timely implementation of project activities.
          The AU component has experienced delays in key activities, which the team is actively attempting to address. The PIU is currently being
           set up.
