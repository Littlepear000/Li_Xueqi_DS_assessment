 Project Development Objectives

  Project Development Objective (from Project Appraisal Document)
  To (i) increase the coverage of Egypt’s Universal Health Insurance System in Phase I Governorates, (ii) strengthen UHIS-related governance and
  institutions, and (iii) provide temporary financial protection against high out of pocket health expenditures for vulnerable populations outside Phase I
  Governorates.

  Has the Project Development Objective been changed since Board Approval of the Project Objective?
  No

 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The Supporting Egypt’s Universal Health Insurance System (UHIS) project was approved by the Board of the World Bank on June 16, 2020, and
  the Loan agreement was signed on January 21, 2021.The Project became effective on August 29, 2021. The World Bank just completed the mid-
  term review of the project, which identified bottlenecks in implementation of the reform and a series of recommendations to put into place over the
  next six months. Out of five development objectives, the project is on track to meet two, at risk of not meeting two, and is missing data to track
