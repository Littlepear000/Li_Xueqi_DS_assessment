 Project Development Objectives

  Project Development Objective (from Project Appraisal Document)
  The development objectives of this project are to strengthen participatory local governance, improve the quality of local infrastructure, both in
  targeted communities, and increase extracurricular or livelihood opportunities for youth.

 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  Since the last mission in April-May 2023, SERSP has advanced the implementation of all subcomponents. Local communities have expressed high
  levels of satisfaction with the project’s decision-making processes and outcomes. Under Component 1, social mobilization process resulted in the
  prioritization of 193 sub-projects (SPs) in 44 jamoats, Of these 193 SPs, NSIFT has completed 53 SPs and will finalize procurements for the
  remaining SPs by December 29, 2023, and finalize renovation and construction works by June 28, 2024.

  Under Subcomponent 2a (youth-inclusive services), the project has delivered trainings on: (i) Upshift (social entrepreneurship) and Mind Masters
  (critical thinking) to 5,717 youth (target of 10,000); (ii) healthy lifestyles to 1,933 youth (target of 2,800); and (iii) digital literacy to 2,797 youth (target
  of 4,000, and has launched the delivery of psychosocial support services. For Subcomponent 2b (youth livelihoods), the project has delivered

  The PIU reported that the indicator "Percentage of subprojects that support climate change adaptation or mitigation" increased from 84%
  in May mission to 92% in November mission, based on reviewing each sub-project (completed or under implementation) against 4 climate
  criteria. There was also a large change in the denominator of this indicator between April/May (17 completed and 40 under implementation)
  and Oct/November (53 completed and 119 under implementation).
