 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  Overall project progress (of a maximum of 100%) is at 98.5%, which is a very positive indicator. Implementation is at 26.66%, slightly below the
  scheduled optimum 27.06%. Construction efforts focus on preliminary works on the Calle 72 road interchange (intercambiador vial, progress as of
  November, 2023: 42.32%, scheduled: 67.61%), the Railyards (patiotaller, progress as of September, 2023: 85.88%, nearly 10% above the
  scheduled progress to date), and the Primero de Mayo interchange, currently at 29.61% (scheduled: 31.17%). The only Workfront that is
  significantly delayed (below 80% of the scheduled progress) is the Calle 72 front, where all supervision efforts and requests to the concessionaire
  are being channeled. The secondary utility network transfer should be at 25.56% but has only advanced 3.74%. Works for the viaduct have
  commenced, with the November 2024 progress report registering a progress of 1.98% vs a scheduled 5.33%.
  Changes in shareholders in DFBOMT concessionaire, Metro Linea 1. EMB has reported to the MDBs that Metro Linea 1 intends to change the
  shareholder composition of the concessionaire. With the proposed changes, the lead firms, CHEC and Xi'an Metro would transfer their shares of the
  concessionaire to their second-level subsidiaries in Spain and Colombia, respectively, the former controlled by CHEC Singapore, the latter by Xi'an
