4. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  Overall, project implementation remains on track. However, disbursements rates in Zambia (28%) and Madagascar (21%)
  continue to lag behind targets. Mozambique’s slower disbursement reflects the planned activity cycle and is on track to
  reach 50% by June 2026, with current disbursement at 38%. São Tomé and Príncipe (STP) has doubled its disbursement
  since July, now reaching 63%. Malawi and SADC continue to progress as planned, while Comoros has fully disbursed its
  allocation.
  The most significant activities implemented in the second half of the year (since last ISR) include:
  In Mozambique, data collection for the Business Census (CEMPRE) started in September and will continue until January
  2026, with microdata expected to be available by March 2026. The Household Budget Survey (IOF 2025) began in August
  and is scheduled to conclude in July 2026. On national accounts, INE compiled the Supply and Use Table using NADABAS,
  collected and analyzed data to improve provincial GDP estimates, and gathered information on the production of cash
  crops and intermediate consumption in construction. Additionally, INE carried out household verification activities to
  ensure data consistency for the Energy Survey within the National Accounts framework. On the Consumer Price Index
  (CPI), INE supervised fieldwork activities, including expanding coverage to districts with greater economic significance;
  produced and disseminated territorial statistics; and provided training for CPI technicians.
  Comoros successfully finalized most of the planned statistical activities, which include the Household survey, Consumer
  Price Index (CPI) data collection and improvement, and National Accounts rebasing, as well as capacity building
  activities. Additional Financing for US7 million was approved by the World Bank Board of Directors on January 5, 2026. It
  will finance General Population and Housing Census 2027 (RGPH5), Economic Census, one round of annual agriculture
  surveys, as well as continued work on CPI, National Accounts, and strengthening institutional capacity.
  Malawi has finalized the data collection and analysis for the 6th Integrated Household Survey (IHS6). The country has
  disseminated the Labor Force Survey conducted in 2024 and has also completed data cleaning and report writing for the
  National Census of Agriculture. In addition, Malawi has begun data collection for a monitoring survey aimed at linking
  household survey data with mobile phone data from mobile network operators. Similarly they have finalized the analysis
  of the Demographic and Health Survey 2024 and have undertaken the validation with the data with stakeholders.
  In Madagascar political disruptions affected implementation; nevertheless, activities have resumed. The 2025 agricultural
  survey has been completed, and key activities—including the resumption of data collection for the Household Survey and
  preparations for the upcoming economic census—are progressing well. Preparatory work for the social registry and
  phone surveys has also advanced, with the first round expected to start in January 2026.
  Zambia implemented the Census of Economic Activities between September and November 2025 and has finalized both
  the analysis and report writing for the Integrated Agricultural Census. Similarly, they have concluded the analysis of the
  Demographic and Health Survey 2024.
  São Tomé and Príncipe (STP) started the preparation of the census thematic studies and is currently implementing the
  household budget survey.
