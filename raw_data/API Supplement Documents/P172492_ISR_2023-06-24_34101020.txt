 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  A recent Bank implementation support mission took place between April 17-May 18th, 2023 which provided the teams with an opportunity to assess
  the progress of key activities and discuss potential implementation challenges. The following action items have been identified and agreed upon
  during the mission:
           The Project Management Unit (PMU) will develop clear guidance and templates to support the Annual Work Plan (AWP) preparation and
            approval process to share with Implementing Entities well in advance of the launch of the AWP process following the guidance mentioned
            in this Aide-Memoire (AM). The Bank will support the conceptualization process of AWP activities in order to facilitate the eventual
            approval of activities.
           Guidance documents will be developed that lay out the lead times of various procurement and Bank No Objection processes help facilitate
            these processes and avoid implementation delays.

             The annual budgeting process will be reviewed with the aim of developing more realistic projections based on feasibility of activities and
              acknowledgement of related implementation challenges.
             A legal opinion will be obtained by the Attorney General in order to clarify potential inconsistencies between the Public Financial
              Management (PFM) Act 2016 and the Local Government Act 2022 related to internal audit committees for Local Councils.
             Team building exercises will be organized at the World Bank office for PMU staff in order to enhance team dynamics.
             Live trackers will be developed to enhance holistic monitoring of project activities. The trackers will include information on the status of
              AWP activities, including the submission of concept notes, obtention of the Bank’s No Objective, and procurement related steps.
             A separate live tracker will be developed to monitor Monitoring and Evaluation (M&E) updates under the project and training will be
              conducted for district-level M&E officers on the M&E manual.
             A series of coordination meetings will be organized between the implementing entities, the PMU, and the World Bank in order to facilitate
              discussions on implementing of project activities.
             The PMU will develop a comprehensive report documenting all grievances received from round one of the local development grants tobe
              shared with the Bank team. A knowledge and information sharing network will be created for Environmental and Social (E&S) and GRM
              related issues across Bank projects.
             In regards to the local development grants, the contractual framework between local councils and contractors will be revised. In addition,
              the Project Implementation Manual (PIM) and Grants Manual will be submitted to the Bank for review and approval, taking into account
              procedures for advance payments to Councils, per diem, and transport refunds to participants of project related activities.
