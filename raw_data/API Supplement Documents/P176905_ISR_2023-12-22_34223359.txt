 Project Development Objectives

  Project Development Objective (from Project Appraisal Document)
  The objectives of the project are to (i) support economic recovery and promote climate smart practices among Project beneficiaries in Argentina's
  agri-food system; and (ii) respond effectively in case of an eligible crisis or emergency.

 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The Project was approved by the World Bank Board on December 9th, 2021 and declared effective on March 21st, 2022. The Project's
  implementing unit is the Dirección de Programas y Proyectos Sectoriales y Especiales (DIPROSE). All components are already operational and
  implementing activities on the ground, although with some delays due to institutional and political changes, but an action plan to accelerate project
  activities is being implemented.
  Currently, 22 provinces have joined the Project, 17 have updated and approved Provincial Strategies for the Agri-Food Sector (EPSA). Under
  Component 1 (Public infrastructure for agro-industrial development) 26 rural infrastructure subprojects have been requested. Its formulations are in
  progress and its completion is planned between June and October 2023. Under Component 2 (Agro industrial development and strengthening of
