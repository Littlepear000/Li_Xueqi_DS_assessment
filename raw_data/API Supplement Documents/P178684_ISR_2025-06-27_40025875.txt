1. OBJECTIVE


 1.1 Development Objective
   Original Development Objective (Approved as part of Approval package on 28-Mar-2023)
   To improve safe and equitable access to education, particularly for girls, and teaching-learning conditions in secondary
   schools in selected provinces.
   Has the Development Objective been changed since Board Approval of the Project Objective?

  This is an interim Implemetation Status Report (ISR). An updated ISR will be submitted after the supervision mission
  scheduled for July 7-18, 2025. As of now, the provincial recruitment has been finalized. The PCT has started key activities
  related to civil works, textbooks, scholarships, signing of codes of conducts and girls clubs/safe schools. There is a need to
  accelerate the pace of project implementation to ensure that major project interventions will start with the beginning of
  the school year 2025-2026.
