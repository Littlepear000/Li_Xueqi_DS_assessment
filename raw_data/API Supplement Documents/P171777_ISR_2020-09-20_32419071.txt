 Project Development Objectives

  Project Development Objective (from Project Appraisal Document)
  To strengthen the capacity of the National Institute of Statistics of Djibouti (INSD) to produce and disseminate timely and reliable statistics in a
  sustainable manner and modernize the Recipient's selected economic and fiscal management tools and processes in Djibouti.

  Has the Project Development Objective been changed since Board Approval of the Project Objective?
  No
