4. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  The Project is progressing towards the achievement of its development objectives. The Regional Development Strategy
  for Osh Region is at the final stage and is expected to be adopted by the end of CY2024. 25 Productive Partnerships (PPs)
  have been identified and are in various stages of implementation, spread across eight application rounds. Of these, six
  PPs are already operational. Two partnerships, focused on Apples and Poultry, were formally opened by the Deputy
  Chairman of the Cabinet of Ministers and Minister of Agriculture on July 6, 2024, marking significant milestones in
  agricultural development. On several locations construction activities are nearing completion: Ak-Bura River Embankment
  Enhancements, Central Square Revitalization, and Summer Theater Rehabilitation projects are almost finished; the park
  around the Drama Theater has been successfully renovated; construction of a pedestrian bridge, along with the
  rehabilitation of the stadium and surrounding park in Uzgen, is ongoing and scheduled for completion in 2025. Under
  Small Grants Program, 181 participants have been covered with grant agreements totaling US$1.7 million now been
  signed. The process of delivering goods and equipment to grant recipients is underway, further supporting rural
  enterprises and enhancing local tourism potential.
