Multiphase Programmatic Approach (MPA) Status (Public Disclosure)

Status and Key Decisions (Public Disclosure)
As of January 17, 2022, 87 MPA-projects had been approved with a total commitment of US$4.2
billion. One operation (Guatemala) was cancelled in mid-September 2021, at the request of the
Government. Therefore, there are 86 MPA active operations. Fifty-eight (58) country projects or 67% of
projects approved have reached 70-100% disbursement (reasons for >100% disbursements relate to
fluctuations between the Euro and the SDR against the US$). Out of the 58 projects, 35 operations or 60%
have disbursed over 90% of commitments. The projects are benefitting around 3.7 billion people or 50% of
the global population. Of the 86 active projects: (i) 33 are in Africa – 12 in AFE and 21 in AFW; (ii) 12 in East
Asia; (iii) 14 in Europe and Central Asia; (iv) 11 in Latin America and the Caribbean; (v) 7 in Middle East
and North Africa; and (vi) 9 in South Asia. Eighty-five (85) or 99% of projects approved are disbursing. Total
disbursements as of January 17, 2022, amounted to US$3.30 billion or 78% of overall
commitments. Streamlined procedures, delegated approvals, coupled with flexible project design
and intensified efforts across the Bank have contributed to the progress. Six parent projects have been
restructured to include vaccine-related procurement. Implementation is being guided by Bank teams

 I. PROJECT STATUS AND RATIONALE FOR RESTRUCTURING

1.      This Project Paper seeks approval of the Country Director to restructure the Bangladesh COVID-19 Emergency
Response and Pandemic Preparedness Project (P173757) to update the definition of the “Project COVID-19 Vaccine” in
the legal agreement.

2.      This restructuring responds to a request made by the Government of Bangladesh (GoB) to pay for air shipping
costs related to transport of COVID-19 vaccines and safety boxes. The letter dated January 9, 2022, from the Economic
Relations Division of the Ministry of Finance, GoB, is attached.

3.       Project status: The project implementation progress slowed down considerably since September 2020 as noted
in the recently completed implementation support mission of held from November 28 to December 2, 2021. Since the
mission, the Project Implementation Unit (PIU) has accelerated implementation of project activities with several
procurement activities being finalized including for activities related to COVID-19 vaccination. Some of the procurement
activities include expansion of intensive care units, establishing isolation units, setting-up new testing laboratories,
establishing vaccine testing laboratory, and procurement of vaccines and accessories related to vaccination. Consequently,
disbursements under the project are expected to increase by March 2022.

4.       Environmental and social aspects: The government has disclosed the Environment Social Commitment Plan
(ESCP), the Stakeholder Engagement Plan (SEP), the Environmental and Social Management Framework (ESMF), and the
Human and Occupational Resource Management Procedures (HORMP) on the website of the Directorate General of
Health Services. In addition, environment and social consultants are onboard and working at the PIU. Procurement of
goods and services is underway. Renovation of selected health facilities is underway for intensive care units, isolation
units, and laboratories. The PIU is ensuring each of these sites is screened prior to initiation of any work to assess potential
environmental and social impacts.

 II. DESCRIPTION OF PROPOSED CHANGES

5.       The definition of Project COVID-19 Vaccine is being modified to read as follows:

     “Project COVID-19 Vaccine” means a vaccine for the prevention of COVID-19, authorized by the Recipient’s
     national regulatory authority for distribution, marketing and administration within the territory of the Recipient,
     and acquired or deployed under the Project; “Project COVID-19 Vaccines” means the plural thereof.

6.      No other changes are being proposed. This revision will be effective from July 1, 2021. This would enable the
project to settle air shipment related costs as requested by the government for flights operated between the period July
2, 2021 November 26, 2021, to transport vaccines and related accessories.

    EXPECTED MPA PROGRAM RESULTS

    Current Expected MPA Results and their Indicators for the MPA Program
    Progress towards the achievement of the PDO would be measured by outcome indicators. Individual country-specific
    projects (or phases) under the MPA Program will identify relevant indicators, including among others:
    •       Country has activated their public health Emergency Operations Centre or a coordination mechanism for
    COVID-19;
    •       Number of designated laboratories with COVID-19 diagnostic equipment, test kits, and reagents;
    •       Number of acute healthcare facilities with isolation capacity;
    •       Number of suspected cases of COVID-19 reported and investigated per approved protocol;
    •       Number of diagnosed cases treated per approved protocol;
    •       Personal and community non-pharmaceutical interventions adopted by the country (e.g., installation of
    handwashing facilities, provision of supplies and behavior change campaigns, continuity of water and sanitation service
    provision in public facilities and households, schools closures, telework and remote meetings, reduce/cancel mass
    gatherings);
    •       Policies, regulations, guidelines, or other relevant government strategic documents incorporating a multi-
    sectoral health approach developed/or revised and adopted;
    •       Multi-sectoral operational mechanism for coordinated response to outbreaks by human, animal and wildlife
    sectors in place;
    •       Coordinated surveillance systems in place in the animal health and public health sectors for zoonotic
    diseases/pathogens identified as joint priorities; and
    •       Mechanisms for responding to infectious and potential zoonotic diseases established and functional; and
    •       Outbreak/pandemic emergency risk communication plan and activities developed and tested
