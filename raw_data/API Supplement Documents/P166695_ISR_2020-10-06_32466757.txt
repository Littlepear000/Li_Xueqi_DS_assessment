 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The Gambia Fiscal Management Development Project became effective on July 28, 2020. The five-year project is financed through a US$35 million
  International Development Association (IDA) Grant.
  The Project Development Objective is to increase fiscal space and improve effectiveness of public resources management. This will be achieved
  by: (a) enhancing the Gambia Revenue Authority’s (GRA) compliance management, revenue risk analysis, and enforcement capacity, for
  broadening the tax base and improving voluntary compliance; (b) introducing electronic Government Procurement (e-GP); and (c)
  improving financial and operational performance of State-Owned Enterprises (SOEs) and reducing the telecom-related fiscal burden.
  The project responds to a context in which SOE's fiscal burden is unsustainable for public finance, and in which a significant tax potential gap (5-7
  percent of GDP) constrains revenue collection. The Fiscal Management Development Project (FMDP) is aligned with the strategic objectives of the
  Government’s National Development Plan (NDP) and the World Bank Group Country Engagement Note for the Republic of The Gambia for FY18-
  FY21 (CEN n° IDA/R 2018-0139).
