 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The Project is in its fourth year of implementation and is expected to last for an additional three years until the official closing date of February 27,
  2026. The Project aims to improve irrigation delivery through rehabilitation and modernization of selected irrigation schemes under Component 1
  and strengthening capacity of Water User Associations (WUAs) under Component 2. Component 1 includes rehabilitation and modernization of four
  irrigation schemes, namely: Alasehir irrigation scheme in Manisa, Atabey irrigation scheme in Isparta, Eregli-Ivriz left bank irrigation scheme in
  Konya, and Uzunlu irrigation scheme in Yozgat. The Project activities under Component 2 includes (i) piloting a solar energy-based pumping of
  groundwater for irrigation of the existing irrigation scheme in Bolvadin and (ii) supporting DSI for implementation of the nationwide program (i.e.
  management information system known as SUTEM) to improve WUAs’ capacity for water management.

   All of the five contracts were signed in 2020. However, work implementation has been delayed at Atabey, Alasehir, and Bolvadin irrigation
  schemes. The implementation challenges at these sites are expected to be resolved over the coming two months by deploying new capable
  contractors and closely following-up on works implementation progress. Overall the project has disbursed US$98.26 million so far, which is
  projected to reach US$116 million by June 30, 2024. There is about $100 million financing gap due to cost-overrun. Thus, the Additional Financing
  shall be initiated and processed to cover the cost overrun.
  The total irrigation service area planned to be improved was 50,764 hectares, of which 15,280 hectares are completed. Farmers report significant
  benefits due to improvements in irrigation services including shielding the farmers against the risks uncertain rainfall and unreliable irrigation
  schedule, reduction in costs of crop and livestock production, allowing at least two crop harvests per year, increases in crop yield due to adequate
  and reliable irrigation service, increased irrigated area and production, and allowing diversification towards high value crops. Overall, the emerging
  results show that the project contributed to sustaining the livelihoods of farmers, who were hitherto affected by water scarcity, which was
  exacerbated by climate change.
