4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  The authorization of spending for the project and government's decision on the census, which have been holding up the
  implementation of the project, were provided with the approval of the Presidential Resolution (No. 114) and the Cabinet
  of Ministers Resolution (No. 113) on March 4, 2024. Implementation of project activities started, with a focus on
  preparations for the establishment of the data center, Population and Housing Census and implementation review of the
  National Statistics Development Strategy.
  The next implementation phase will focus on advancing execution of other (non Population and Housing Census)
  activities in the project, having focused on operationalizing the PIU and setting key procurement activities for
  establishment of the data center and procurement of tablets for the Population and Housing Census on track in the first
  six months after decrees No. 113 and 114 were approved. Addressing higher level institutional and organization
  development gaps to strengthen the national statistics system including support to producers of other statistics is
  essential.
