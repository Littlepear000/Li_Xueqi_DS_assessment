 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The Project was approved on July 5, 2022 and its Financing Agreement signed on July 7,2002. It became effective on September 30, 2022.
  The Project’s governing bodies are now in place. The two Project Implementation Units are working on firming up procedures and accelerate
  implementation. A number of packages (coastal infrastructure, equipment) are read for tendering in early 2024.
