 Project Development Objectives

  Project Development Objective (from Project Appraisal Document)
  The objective of the project is to improve irrigation delivery, through rehabilitation and modernization of distribution infrastructure in selected
  schemes, and through strengthening of capacity of WUAs.

 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  Progress to-date is Satisfactory. The project is progressing well, although some initial delays have been encountered in procurement of the first
  works which were completed in April 2020. DSI has incorporated lessons learnt during the Eregli Ivriz bidding process for preparation of bidding
  documents for Yozgat Uzunlu, Atabey and Alasehir. Construction started in Eregli-Ivriz, work commencement letter has been sent recently in
  Uzunlu and procurement process is close to completion in Atabey and Alasehir.
