 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The Project Implementation Progress (IP) rating is upgraded to ‘Satisfactory’. The Team acknowledges the significant progress in the procurement
  and contracting of the passive infrastructure deployments (financed under Subcomponent 1.1), the expected completion of construction works of the
  first lots of passive infrastructure (the Racha region is expected to be completed by end December 2022), and the signature of the contract for the
  first lot of active equipment. This progress has started to translate into disbursements, with an increase in cumulative disbursements from US$1.7
  million in May 2022 to US$7.3 million in December 2022, and this uptake will continue as construction works is being completed (with an additional
  US$4.5 million planned to be disbursed by end of June 2023).
