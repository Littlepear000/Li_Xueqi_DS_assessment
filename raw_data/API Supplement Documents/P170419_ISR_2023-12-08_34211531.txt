 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  Following approval by the Board on December 15, 2020 and signing of the Loan Agreement on December 22, 2020, the loan became effective on
  February 25, 2021. The Mid-Term Review (MTR) of the PforR was carried out between June 12 and 21, 2023.

  The PMU was formally created on November 10, 2021 as well as the joint Steering Committee (with the TREEA project financed by the French
  Agency for Development). Two Steering Committees were organized (on February 14, 2022 and November 16, 2022). A third Steering Committee
  took place on November 13, 2023 to validate the proposed changes of Program after the MTR.

   Main conclusions of the MTR. The Mid-Term Review did not identify a need for profound modification of the PforR, but for: (i) target
  readjustments; (ii) redefinitions of certain indicators (PDO, DLI, DLR and IRI); (iii) addition of indicators to make important intermediate actions
  visible (previously invisible in the results chain); and (iv) simplifications in the Results Framework. Despite some delays in RA 1 and 2, at this stage,
  there is no need of changes in PDO or significant changes of PDO indicator (only adjustment of one target).

  Implementation progress. As of October 2023, 11 Disbursement Linked Results (DLR) have been fully achieved and 2 partially achieved for a
  total of 30 RLD. RA 1 (Increased Job Opportunities and income generation for rural youth) is also showing some delays in the achievement of some
  indicators but a solid operational set-up has been put in place with the CRJEAA installed in all regions of the country (the centers supporting the
  youth in the preparation of business plans and to access financing). The first two years of the Program (calendar years 2021 and 2022) have been
  dedicated to the operationalization of these centers; it is now expected that the achievement of indicator will be accelerated.

  The same conclusion applies for the RA2 (Improved efficiency of agri-food marketing systems) with the 4 wholesale markets (Marrakech, Agadir,
  Berkane and Meknes): the first part of program implementation have been dedicated to feasibility studies and the preparation of the key documents
  for the financing (agreements). Overall, RA 2 shows 7 RLDs achieved and 2 DLI fully achieved; however the DLI/DLR related to the wholesale
  markets will need a continuous attention.

  RA3 (Enhanced digitalization of agriculture and adoption of climate smart practices) is showing a satisfactory implementation with 3 RLD already
  achieved.
