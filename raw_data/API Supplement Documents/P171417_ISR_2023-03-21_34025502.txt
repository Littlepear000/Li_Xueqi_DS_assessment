 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  Significant progress has been made by the Program, including notable accomplishments such as the Cabinet's approval of the Pension Plan, Data
  Integration Plan, as well as the Provincial Assembly's enactment of the PFM Law. Additionally, the Cabinet granted its approval of an amendment to
  Public Procurement rules and notification of Standard Bidding Documents, while a MoU between the Federal Public Procurement Regulatory
  Authority and the Punjab Public Procurement Regulatory Authority has been signed. Lastly, a priority committee is now in place. These are all
  remarkable achievements, leading to an upgraded implementation rating from Moderately Satisfactory to Satisfactory for the GoPb.
