 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The overall progress toward achieving the Project Development Objective (PDO) and the overall implementation progress have remained
  satisfactory, despite the setback due to the continued restrictions associated with the COVID-19 pandemic. Some of the key achievements of the
  project thus far include:
  Component 1: PFM IT Systems Roll-Out:
  Business continuity, redundancy and performance. The establishment of a third data center recommended by the IFMIS technical QA to
  strengthen the ICT systems’ redundancies has commenced. The functional QA review of the IFMIS, a fundamental document to inform on next
  steps, is on track to be finalized by mid-February 2021. SDMS - financial module to service delivery units. As of December 2020, this module
  was rolled out to additional 299 schools against a planned target of 600 schools by June 2021. Rollout has been affected by the COVID-19
  pandemic. School-level expenditure reports of onboarded schools are available directly from the system. IFMIS as an enabler of PBB and accrual

  reporting. The new module introduced to track and report on the financing and results of the PBB pilots is complete. The module has been tested
  and is ready for deployment.
  Component 2: Accounting and Financial Reporting:
  Accrual-based IPSAS blueprint (2018–2024). Physical and virtual trainings have been carried out to cover all budget entities. In addition, staff of
  the SAI and district hospitals have been trained (1,000 staff during July–December 2020). The recruitment process of the IPSAS consultancy
  firm has commenced. Contract signing and mobilization of consultants are targeted for end-April 2021. Arrangements are under way for ICPAR to
  set up a Public Sector Unit and offer training and qualifications for the public sector. The TORs for TA have been prepared..
  Component 3: Performance-Based Budgeting and Medium-Term Budgeting:
  The National Budget Department will go for a full rollout of PBB in the FY2021/22 budget. Sector workbooks have been prepared and all staff
  have been trained. The budget preparation process based on PBB is now under way. The consultancy selection for strengthening the macro-fiscal
  forecasting, an essential foundation for an effective PBB, has been finalized and consultancy will commence by mid-February 2021. The citizen’s
  guide for FY2020/21 has been published.
  Component 4: Professionalization of PFM Staff - PFM Capacity Development
  Professional training for public sector officials. Although the component has exceeded the year 2 PDO-level result target, progress has been
  affected by the COVID-19 pandemic. Face-to-face trainings and examination sittings have been postponed (while a December 2020 sitting was held
  by ICPAR). Therefore, the relevant results targets will be reviewed and amended to recognize intermediate achievements of students (passing of
  each stage in a qualification) toward the professional qualification.
  A Mid-Term Review will be conducted February 16 - 22, 2021, to make decisions on the need for a project restructuring, including if an extension of
  the closing date and amendment of relevant results targets will be required.
