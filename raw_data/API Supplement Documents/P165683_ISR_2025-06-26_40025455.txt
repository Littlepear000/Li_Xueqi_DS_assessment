1. OBJECTIVE


 1.1 Development Objective
   Original Development Objective (Approved as part of Approval package on 28-Feb-2019)
   To (i) strengthen capacity for integrated water resources management in the State; (ii) improve reliability of water
   services in the Agreste and Borborema regions; and (iii) improve the operational efficiency of water and wastewater
   services in the Joao Pessoa Metropolitan Region.
   Has the Development Objective been changed since Board Approval of the Project Objective?

   No

4. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  A substantial increase in the Project implementation pace has been achieved along the last 12 months.
  The construction of the Cariri pipeline and water treatment plant, the implementation of the water supply automation
  system, the sectorization of the Joao Pessoa Metropolitan Region water network, and the wastewater pumping station
  and outfall are underway. These activities represent the bulk of the loan proceeds. The bidding process for the
  construction of the Joao Pessoa wastewater treatment plant was cancelled as it will be funded trough a new operation,
  approved by COFIEX in March 2025. Counterpart activities are progressing with 91% disbursement of the US$ 80 million,
  with a design build contract for the Curimatau pipeline, a NRW performance-based contract for the reduction of
  commercial losses, and a standard contract for the construction of a wastewater pumping station. Activities to
  strengthen the State's water resources management capacity continue to progress well.
  The Bank team and the client agree that the PDO is achievable with sustained efforts to maintain the current pace of
  implementation and carry out a project restructuring. Monthly monitoring and implementation support meetings are
  carried out with the client.
  Finally, dam safety activities are being followed-up by a Panel of Experts with a need of further speeding up their
  implementation.
