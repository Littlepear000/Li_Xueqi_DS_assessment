 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  A forth supervision mission was carried out on November 13 to 17 and December 4 to 8, 2023. The project is facing delays to process procurement
  packages, which are impacting the chronogram of large scale works. Efforts are being made to strengthen the Project Coordination Implementation
  Unit (PCIU) and priotize activities in the Annual Plan Operational (POA) 2024. Public consultation of safegurds instruments was carried out on
  October 2023 and disclosure is expected by April 2024. The next supervision mission is scheduled for mid-April 2024, followed by the mid-term
  review mission in early September.
