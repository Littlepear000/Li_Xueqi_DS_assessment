4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  Progress of cash transfers to poor and vulnerable households. The direct benefit transfer program to date has supported
  over 5 million households. Following the CBN circular of December 2023, mandating the use of NIN or BVN to operate an
  account after March 2024, and the recommendation from the presidential panel, all transfers since March 2024 are made
  to beneficiaries who’s NIN or BVN have been verified. About 1.4 million households, part of the total 5 million, have
  received a second and third tranche of support.
  Targeted NIN validation and registration drive: The NIN (or a BVN) is now a requirement for the registration of
  households into the direct benefit transfer program and is required to own a functional bank account or wallet. To scale-
  up cash transfers to poor and vulnerable households, the National Social Safety Net Coordinating Office (NASSCO) is
  collaborating with NIMC on a targeted NIN registration drive for households captured in the Social Register. NASSCO has
  been licensed as a front-end enrollment partner for the NIN, allowing them to register beneficiaries for the NIN. Beyond
  the registration of new NINs, NASSCO will collect and validate existing NINs belonging to household members and
  updates to information belonging to households in the Social Register. This will immediately strengthen the veracity of
  the Social Register and increase the number of households verified and registered in the direct benefit transfer program.
  Resumption of New Ministers at the Federal Ministry of Humanitarian Affairs and Poverty Reduction. A Minister and
  Minister of State for Humanitarian Affairs and Poverty Reduction were appointed in October 2024. The ministers have
  resumed oversight of the implementation of the direct benefit transfer program, taking over this role from the inter-
  ministerial panel chaired by the Minister of Finance and Coordinating Minister of the Economy.
