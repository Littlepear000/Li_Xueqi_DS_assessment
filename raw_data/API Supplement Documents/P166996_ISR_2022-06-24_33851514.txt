 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  Continuous implementation support and supervision has been provided by the World Bank, including through an implementation support mission
  conducted between March 15-17, 2022. All institutional and implementation arrangements for the project are in place, including core staffing in the
  Project Coordinating Unit and in the implementing agencies. Current implementation involves major procurement activities, with several activities

  recruitment of a management firm of the Ghana Skills Development Fund has been successfully completed in June 2022. Evidence for the
