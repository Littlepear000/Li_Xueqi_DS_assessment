   Original Development Objective (Approved as part of Approval package on 09-Jan-2020)
   The project development objective is to improve the coverage of urban cadaster services in selected municipalities to

4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  Due to the significant progress in the implementation of the 2023-2024 Action Plan, the approval of the Project
  Restructuring, which extended the project closing date from February 28, 2025 to July 31, 2026 (with the original
  closing date, as stated by the Mid-Term Review, the Project Development Objective - PDO - would not have been
  achieved), and significant advances on core Intermediate Results Indicators, the Bank Team is proposing upgrading the
  PDO from Moderately Unsatisfactory to Moderately Satisfactory in this ISR, based on the following evidence-based
  results (despite the impacts and delays derived from the COVID-19 pandemic and social-political events): (i) The signing
  of two contracts for the cadastral survey of areas 1 and 2, comprising 13 of 22 project municipalities (59% project
  coverage area), located in the provinces of Piura (four districts), Lambayeque (five districts), and Lima (four districts), with
  a target of 661,924 cadastral units, for a total amount that corresponds to 26% of the Loan. The second stage (cadastral
  survey of areas 3, 4, and 5: achievable now with the extended project closing date) covers nine districts of Lima (41%
  project coverage area), with a target of 988,507 cadastral units. The budget for the cadastral survey of areas 3, 4, and 5
  corresponds to 59% of the Loan, according to the technical specifications market studies. The cadastral survey started in
  May 2024 in the cadastral survey of areas 1 (Piura and Lambayeque: Districts of Chiclayo and La Victoria) and 2 (Lima:
  Districts of Surquillo and Los Olivos); (ii) The supervision and quality control firms for the cadastral survey of areas 1 and 2
  are already contracted; (iii) The production of modules of the urban cadastral information system, which will enable the
  collection and processing of cadastral information, are being developed in-house by a team of consultants . Phase I is
  completed, and Phase II began in May 2024; (iv) The Government's MTR report was concluded and sent to the Bank in
  January 2024; (v) Loan disbursements increased from 2% to 13.5% as of June 2024; (vi) All project municipalities included
  in the cadastral survey of areas 1 and 2 are being provided with direct technical assistance according to the Cadaster
  Implementation Plan; and (vii) Several activities that contribute to achieving each indicator target are underway (See
  Section 9 for more details). Implementation Progress is rated Moderately Satisfactory (MS): Implementation
  arrangements are working well, with the Project under the leadership of the Agency for the Formalization of Informal
  Property (Órgano de Formalización de la Propiedad Informal, COFOPRI in Spanish). Notwithstanding, to better provide
  support to the project and ensure the future sustainability of the urban cadasters, the Ministry of Housing, Construction
  and Sanitation and COFOPRI must effectively undertake the responsibilities on urban cadastral matters established in
  Legislative Decree No. 1365 and distribute them through their organizational structures. In addition, the Project is rated
  Satisfactory in compliance with social and environmental safeguards, Moderately Satisfactory in financial
  management, Satisfactory in project management, and Satisfactory in procurement.
