 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  Overview. The five-year Burkina-Faso Higher Education Support Project, approved by the World Bank Board of Executive Directors on July 10,
  2018, became effective on December 19, 2018, and it is expected to close on September 30, 2023. The Project’s development objectives are to:
  strengthen higher education institutions to increase access and deliver quality education in priority subject areas.

  Progress by Component: The project has three main components. Progress by components is summarized below.

  Component 1. Enhancing Access to Higher Education: Establishment of the Virtual University of Burkina Faso

  The February 2020 supervision mission positively appreciated the Government’s decision to allocate the former World Bank-funded distance
  education center to Virtual University of Burkina Faso VU-BF as a provisional seat. In view of the investments requested on this site and for the
  sake of sustainability, the mission strongly recommended that this decision be made final in order to reduce the risk of expropriation in the future. In
  view of the Project’s proposal for the rehabilitation of the premises, IDA hired a project infrastructure consultant to analyze and reassess the cost
  estimates sent to IDA.
  The supervision mission of June 2020 reemphasized the following recommendations: (i) ensure the effective start of training in October 2020; (ii)
  the recourse by VU-BF, in the first instance, to the acquisition of ready-made content (content that are already in use in other higher education and
  research institutions) which will certainly need to be reviewed before being deployed.
  The mission reiterated its comments during the various missions. It is essential that the UV-BF creates the conditions for its start-up. There is
  therefore a calendar imperative, which requires that it have a road map which would allow the various stages to be set for an effective start of the
  UV-BF to be set.
  Following the call for expressions of interest for the supply of computers, two suppliers were selected, agreements were signed on March 3, 2020
  and an official ceremony with the President of Burkina Faso was organized on April 30, 2020 to officially launch the program with a first discount of
  computers on May 28, 2020. In sum, to date, the “One student, one computer” program comprises: (i) 55,062 eligible students; (ii) 11,519 students
  enrolled; (iii) 9,131 expressions of interest; (iv) 3,604 grants awarded ; and (v) 133 computers delivered and 800 in the process of being delivered
  .
  In view of the enthusiasm of the students for this program, the mission authorized the Project to use the stock of the remaining seven thousand
  (7,000) computers that was supposed to be delivered in project year three.

   Component 2. Strengthen higher education institutions and support innovations.
  There is also a low implementation of programmed activities due to the reorientation of the competitive funds mechanism (sub-component 2.1)
  towards a performance contracting approach for the benefit of public education and research institutions, but also the COVID-19 pandemic which
  led to the closure of all schools. No significant progress has been made since the February 2020 mission. Nonetheless, the Bank’s task team
  continues to work closely with the Government to devise creative ways to ensure the implementation progress improves in the coming months.


  Sub-component 2.1. Competitive Funds. The implementation of the performance-based contracts (PBC) approach in the context of competitive
  funds has continued since the last supervision mission (February 2020) with : (i) training and development of activities for the PBCs of all higher
  education and research institutes; (ii) analysis of the first versions of PBCs; and (iii) ongoing exchanges with universities to stabilize versions, etc.
  The next steps can be summarized as: (i) validation of the PBCs drawn up; (ii) submission of PBCs to IDA for advice; and (iii) organization of the
  PBCs signing ceremony with universities ready to start. Due to the COVID-19 pandemic—which led to the closure of the borders (no travel in
  country possible for the consultant for example) and schools—the consultant supports the HEI remotely and this slows down the process of
  finalizing performance contracts.

  Sub-component 2.2 - Developing the Faculty in Higher Education Institutions (HEI). No activity has yet started due to the fact that other
  components (VU-BF) and sub-components (Competitive Funds), to which this faculty development activity is connected, have not yet started.
  Terms of Reference (TOR) for the training of 240 teachers on topics, such as interactive teaching and its requirements through sharing of promising
  experiences between teachers, open and distance learning, professional ethics and conduct of teachers-researchers in higher education
  institutions, are finalized and the training scheduled to take place in four sessions in January 2020 was postponed due to the closure of schools and
  will be rescheduled for the next school years and the TOR will be revised based on the national quality assurance strategy and the national
  qualification framework for higher education that is being prepared.


  Sub-component 2.3 - Strengthen Quality Assurance in HEI. The consultant in charge of the execution of the contract relating to the development
  and implementation of a national quality assurance strategy and a national qualification framework for higher education began his mission on June
  8, 2020.
  Regarding internal quality assurance unit (QAU), the establishment of quality assurance unit (QAU) in all public higher education institutes is
  noteworthy. However, the capacity building of quality assurance unit (QAU) members in quality assurance (awareness campaigns, training
  sessions) and support to QAU for training sessions and outside meetings could not take place due to the COVID-19 pandemic.
  The mission recommended to centralize the QAU training and equipment activities in sub-component 2.3, in order to avoid their inclusion in the
  performance contracts of the higher education institutes.
  .

  Sub-component 2.4 - Strengthen HEIs Management Capacity. A consultant for the development of the organizational and functional diagnosis of
  the HEIs has been identified and the next steps are the signing of the contract and the execution of the contract before validation of the study to be
  produced.
  The recruitment of technical assistance for the establishment of an integrated higher education platform on Campus Faso was carried out with a
  little delay due to the COVID-19 pandemic and the obligation to work remotely.


  Component 3. Project coordination, management and implementation activities. The following reports were received within the time frame: (i)
  financial monitoring; (ii) internal controls; and (iii) independent audit. In addition, the external audit report is expected by June 30, 2020. As of June
  15, 2020, out of a total number of fifty (50) planned activities, four (4) have been fully executed, seventeen (17) are in the process of being
  implemented and the twenty-nine (29) reminding activities have not been executed.
