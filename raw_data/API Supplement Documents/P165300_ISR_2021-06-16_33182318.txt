 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The Project was approved and Loan documents were signed in August 2018. Most project implementation efforts at this early stage focused on
  carrying out the Project's main procurement processes (consulting for Project Management Office, DFBOMT concession, and supervision contract)
  have been awarded without significant delays or any other issues. Significant efforts by EMB and the District to complete the transfer of utility
  networks are underway. EMB and IDU are advancing satisfactorily with scheduled land acquisition and resettlement processes. The concessionaire
  is advancing with project implementation, focusing at this stage on three work fronts, to be implemented in the first of this series of projects: (a)
  railyard adaptation works; (b) civil works in Avenida 68; (c) civil works in Calle 72.

 Overall Comments IR

  Component 1: indicators reflect progress in the transfer of utility networks, which are advancing well and picking up pace steadily after minor
  delays generated by COVID-19-imposed lockdowns. Grievances have been responded and addressed within time. Indicator 3 - Component 1
  is updated in this ISR, from Yes/No to number, as the indicator tracks the approval of a total of three traffic management plans, one for each of
  the civil works subprojects implemented under SOP #1: (a) railyard adaptation works; (b) civil works in Avenida 68; (c) civil works in Calle 72.
  Progress reports submitted by EMB during the April 2021 mission indicate progress in the elaboration of traffic management plans for the three
  subprojects. The concessionaire expects two of the plans to be approved by August 2021, and the three plans to be approved by January
  2022. The target is three traffic management plans approved by the Secretariat of Mobility during or before the start of construction.

  Component 2: Both indicators for component 2 (hiring of a PMO and inclusion of a target of employment for women in the O&M phase of the
  project) have been met since December 2018 and November 2019, respectively, ahead of the target date.
