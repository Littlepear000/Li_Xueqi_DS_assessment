3. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


 3.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
   Despite a low mid-term disbursement rate of 30%, the Program has made significant progress towards its Project
   Development Objectives (PDO). By the Mid-Term Review (MTR), two out of three PDO indicator targets were achieved.
   The four participating countries harmonized five statistical dimensions, meeting the end target for PDO indicator 1, with
   data available on the EAC’s open data portal. Statistical systems have improved, evidenced by increased scores on the
   Statistical Performance Indicators (SPI - PDO indicator 2). Since baseline, Kenya improved by 13 percentage points,
   Tanzania by 10, and Rwanda by 6. The SPI target of 75 is likely to be met by the project's end, already achieved by
   Rwanda. Data use in policy documents (PDO indicator 3) has improved, with the target of 37 reached at mid-term.

  Indicators show strong progress in statistical harmonization and performance, suggesting some PDO targets were
  conservatively set. Ambitions for two PDO indicators will be increased during FY26 restructuring.
  Component progress varies across countries. Kenya's implementation is moderately unsatisfactory with a 16%
  disbursement rate due to funding delays to the Kenya National Bureau of Statistics (KNBS), necessitating restructuring in
  FY26. Tanzania shows satisfactory progress with 42% completion of DLRs and a 46% disbursement rate, though some
  activities are delayed. Rwanda is on track with good progress and satisfactory ratings, while Burundi's progress is
  moderately satisfactory, requiring technical assistance for delayed activities. The EAC's statistical harmonization is highly
  satisfactory, but IT infrastructure and capacity building need acceleration. AUC's implementation is unsatisfactory due to
  delays, despite recent improvements, with restructuring planned for FY26.
