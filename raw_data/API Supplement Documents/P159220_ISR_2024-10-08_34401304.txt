4. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  The project restructuring has been completed and includes the following changes: (i) revisions to the Project indicators in
  the Results Framework, and (ii) an extension of the Project's closing date to October 31, 2026, to ensure the completion
  of all activities.
  Component 1: Due to adverse weather conditions, the Contractor resumed site activities on April 30, 2024. Despite the
  delayed start, significant progress was made on earthworks and asphalt paving. This was achieved by implementing a
  revised work program, approved by the Resident Engineer, which included mobilizing additional equipment delivered to
  the site before work began in April 2024. This action addressed a key recommendation from the previous World Bank
  Team visit.
  Component 2: The procurement of safety oversight management software and equipment for the State Civil Aviation
  Agency (SCAA) will be managed by a specialized consultant, set to begin by the end of October 2024. A
  comprehensive needs assessment for SCAA's training program, also scheduled for September, will help define staff
  training requirements. Meanwhile, renovation works in Kyrgyz Aviation Institute (KAI) are now been completed, and
  currently the WB Team reviewing a proposed equipment list for KAI's Air Transport laboratory.
  Component 3: Civil works for five Traffic Safety Centers (TSCs) are completed.
  The World Bank Team received confirmation that the "Sustainable Tourism Development Programs in the Issyk-Kul
  Region" has been approved by the Cabinet of Ministers and is already being implemented.
  Finally, the World Bank Team was pleased to confirm that the National Statistics Committee has commissioned the
  Developed Pilot Application of Tourist Satellite Accounts.
