 MPA Table
 Multiphase Programmatic Approach (MPA) Status

 MPA Table Body
 The overall FSRP Program became effective on August 16, 2022, with the meeting of all effectiveness conditions by the Republic of
 Madagascar. Financing for CCARDESA and IGAD is expected to be effective by the end of September 2022, while the effectiveness for Ethiopia will
 likely need to be extended to allow for the finalization and signing of the Third-Party Implementation Agency for the Tigray region. First
 disbursements are expected by the end of October 2022. Work on the preparation of countries for future phases for countries such as Malawi,
 Comoros and Tanzania as well as with regional organizations such as the Africa Union Commission (AUC) has been initiated.
