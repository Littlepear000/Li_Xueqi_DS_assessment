 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  Building upon previous experience, the Project looks at increasing its implementation pace. Regarding Component 1 (Physical Infrastructure), on
  June 20, 2022, the bid verification report for the design and construction of the 911 Center building indicated that the bidding process was declared
  unsuccessful. Immediately after, PRONATEL sought and received feedback from companies that had requested the SDP on the reasons and areas
  of improvement to strengthen the procurement strategy and the bidding documents (e.g., lack of soil data, complexity of the bidding documents,
  multiple areas of expertise and needs required). The Procurement Strategy was adjusted and a new bidding process for the design of the 911

  Center building upon the design.
  Similarly, for Component 2 (911 Platform), the bidding process for the Emergency Attention and Management System (SAGE) will start in June
  2023 via an initial selection process that will be advertised.
  Regarding Component 3 (Central Response Protocols), PRONATEL is working with different first response entities (National Police of Peru - NPP,
  SAMU and Firemen) and Line 100 to finalize these protocols and an action plan by end of September 2023. In this regard, PRONATEL has signed
  3 of the 4 agreed Cooperation Agreements, the last one in June 2022 with the Linea 100, only one is pending with NPP/MININTER.
  The 911 operational and technical aspects (e.g., Geolocation requirements that shall include the obligation of mobile operators to share real time
  data and transfer the geolocation data to the 911 Emergency Response System and 911 operations) are still pending the adoption of supporting
  regulations to ensure effective functioning of the 911 Emergency Response System.

                                     911 emergency response system is not yet operational. The Project has experienced significant delays
                                     primarily due to unsuccessful bidding process (for the design and construction of the 911 Center hosting
                                     the 911 Platform) back in June 2022. The procurement strategy was revised and a sequential process
                                     was undertaken separating the process into 4 activities: 2 of them have been completed (site cleaning and
                                     soil analysis) and now the design is being contracted to allow the construction bidding process to start in
                                     January 2024 with a signature expected by May 2024 and the completion in 12 months (June 2025). In
                                     parallel, there have been frequent staffing changes, exacerbated by the country context along delays in
                                     the approval of regulatory frameworks that would enable the legality of implementing some key activities.
                                     In parallel, the Project is launching the bidding process for the 911 Platform which is expected to be
                                     operational around June 2024 and allow to start receiving calls to the 911 system (at this stage no calls
                                     have been received yet and therefore no beneficiary feedback has been possible). Once operational, this
                                     will allow to monitor and track user satisfaction and allow adjustments once the system is fully operational
                                     and installed at the 911 center around June 2025.
                                     For this to happen, the Project will need to go through a restructuring process that is being requested by
                                     the counterpart and would be evaluated by the Bank once received. The main objective is to extend the
                                     Project closing date to December 2025 in order to ensure that the Project achieves the PDO, including
                                     this indicator.
                                     In summary, the Project's commitment (since design phase throughout implementation) towards citizen
                                     engagement and ensuring a proper citizen feedback system is embedded in the 911 system remains
                                     unchanged. This will be monitored through Project implementation once the 911 system is in place and
                                     will remain as a feature of the system beyond Project's closing.
