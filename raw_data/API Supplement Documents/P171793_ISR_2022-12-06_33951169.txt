 Project Development Objectives

  Project Development Objective (from Project Appraisal Document)
  The proposed Project Development Objective (PDO) is (i) to enhance connectivity and road safety along the Zinder - Agadez road section in
  Northeastern Niger through the rehabilitation of the Tanout – Tiguidit road segment and maintenance of the full Zinder - Agadez section, and (ii) to
  improve access to basic socio-economic infrastructure for selected communities, along the Zinder - Agadez road section.

  Has the Project Development Objective been changed since Board Approval of the Project Objective?
  No

 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The project was approved on March 2, 2022 and became effective on September 22, 2021.
  The Project development objective which is (i) to enhance connectivity and road safety along the Zinder - Agadez road section in Northeastern
  Niger through the rehabilitation of the Tanout – Tiguidit road segment and maintenance of the full Zinder - Agadez section, and (ii) to improve
  access to basic socio-economic infrastructure for selected communities, along the Zinder - Agadez road section is Moderately Satisfactory.
  The implementation of the OPBRC contract for Lot 1 (in the region of Agadez) has started in September 2022. The contract includes
  the rehabilitation of a road section of 116 km and maintenance services for about 254 km. The bidding documents for OPBRC Lot 2 are expected to

  The recruitment of the consultant in charge of conducting road safety audits is underway and the detailed designs for the two road sections (Lot 1
  and 2) will be audited before implementation.
  The socio-economic infrastructures to be implemented for the benefit of the population living along the Zinder-Agadez road section are being
