4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  Under Component 1, the Project finances investments to modernize DSI’s irrigation systems in Atabey, Eregli-Ivriz, Alasehir, and Uzunlu
  schemes, with a combined area of 50,764 hectares benefiting 17,312 people. Component 2 covers (i) designing and piloting a national
  program for WUA capacity building (US$1.1 million), and (ii) groundwater stewardship pilot (US$0.9 million) through US$2.0 million GEF
  grant.
  Under Component 1 and Component 2, all the contracts have been signed and the works are on-track.
