 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The Legal and Mining Sector Advisors have conducted a comprehensive review of the legal and regulatory framework for the mining bill and
  provided recommendations for improvement. Subsequently, the Minerals Resource Bill underwent a cabinet review in September 2023,
  accompanied by two amendments. Despite its progression through Parliamentary processes, the Bill has been deferred to 2024 due to its length
  and the presence of numerous Bills in the Parliament queue. Simultaneously, a Mining Sector Master Plan is being developed to guide the
  management of the Mineral Sector, and related mining sector inputs into the Solomon Island country macroeconomic model as well and an Action
  Plan and Strategy are being prepared to guide long-term Mining Sector Management initiatives, including the implementation of the new mining
  legislation. MMERE is also demonstrating a commitment to gender inclusivity by developing a Gender Equality and Social Inclusion Strategy (GESI
  Strategy), scheduled for cabinet approval once the Mineral Resource Bill is enacted in parliament in early 2024. To enhance accessibility and
  management of mining-related information, an online portal linked to the Mining Cadaster Administration System (MCAS) is now accessible at
  portal.mmere.gov.sb. additionally, an online Geodata repository, initiated in July 2023, is progressing significantly and is expected to be completed
  by mid-2024. In terms of communication and stakeholder engagement, a strategy has been formulated, gaining approval and acceptance from key
