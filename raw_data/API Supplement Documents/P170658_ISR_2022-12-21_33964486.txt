 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  Building upon previous experience, the Project looks at increasing its implementation pace. Regarding Component 1 (Physical Infrastructure), on
  March 28, 2022, the call for the selection process for the design and construction of the 911 Center building was issued. On June 20, 2022, the bid
  verification report indicated that the bidding process was declared unsuccessful. Immediately after, PRONATEL sought and received feedback
  from companies that had requested the SDP on the reasons and areas of improvement to strengthen the procurement strategy and the bidding
  documents (e.g., lack of soil data, complexity of the bidding documents, multiple areas of expertise and needs required). The Procurement Strategy
  was adjusted and a new bidding process will be launched in December 2022 for the design of the 911 Center and a separate bidding process will

  Regarding Component 3 (Central Response Protocols), PRONATEL is working closely for the implementation with different first response entities
  (National Police of Peru, SAMU and Firemen) and Line 100. In this regard, PRONATEL has signed 3 of the 4 agreed Cooperation Agreements, the

  with the FRE and Line 100 in parallel to the integrated platform component (Component 2) and has recruited consultants to support in the definition
  of the protocols by July 2023.
