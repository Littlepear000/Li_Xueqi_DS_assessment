 Project Development Objectives

  Project Development Objective (from Project Appraisal Document)
  The objective of the Project is to support the Kyrgyz Republic in mitigating the impact of the economic crisis on poor households and labor migrants,
  and strengthening effectiveness and resilience of the social assistance and labor market programs delivery systems, to be better prepared for future
  shocks.

  Has the Project Development Objective been changed since Board Approval of the Project Objective?
  No

 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The project implementation progress is satisfactory. The Ministry of Labor, Social Services, and Migration (MLSSM) has prepared and adopted the
  Sub-Manuals on Part 1.1 (for Cash Transfers subject to retroactive financing), Part 2.1 on Social Contract, and Part 2.2 on Youth Employment. The
  Project Operational Manual has also been prepared by the MLSSM and cleared by the World Bank. Thus, the retroactive financing condition set out
  in the Financing Agreement has been met, leading to the disbursement of US$ 12 million in October 2023 to cover the cash transfers to poor and
  vulnerable households of the Uy-bulogo Komok (UBK) program.
  Activities on the social contract and youth employment under Components 1 and 2 are planned to start in early 2024. Under Component 3, a Project
  Implementation Unit was established and fully operational.
