 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The Financing Agreement was signed on July 3, 2019, and the project was declared effective on January 30, 2020 and formally launched in a
  workshop in Bishkek Mar 3-4, 2020. Following the disruptions in progress due to outfall of the COVID-19 global pandemic (starting in March 2020),
  a joint projects portfolio review (JPPR) was organized with government in late August to discuss progress. The MOTR completed selection of all
  core staff for the PIU, and the procurement of the road design review study and preparation of bidding documents is expected by December 2020.
  The situation in the country was further disrupted by the political unrest over disputed elections on October 4, 2020. The new Presidential election is
  now scheduled to take place on January 10, 2021 with an interim government in place at present.

 Overall Comments PDO

  Project is in problem status and is being closely monitored by management. Following the disruptions in progress due to outfall of the COVID-
  19 global pandemic (starting in March 2020), and the post-election turmoil have resulted in delays in organizing procurement and evaluation of
  WB-IPIG staffing selection and road design review study. Key impacts included; IPIG inability to perform duties due to staff personal or family
  sickness; and to remotely manage and coordinate inputs from the Tender Committee members for evaluation reports and comments on
  various procurements. Key positions at IPIG for Director, Aviation and Tourism Coordinators have now been completed.
