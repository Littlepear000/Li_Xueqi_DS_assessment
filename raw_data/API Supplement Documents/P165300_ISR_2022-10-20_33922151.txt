 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The Project was approved and Loan documents were signed in August 2018. Project implementation efforts at this early stage focus on preliminary
  works on the Calle 72 road interchange (intercambiador vial, progress as of September 30, 2022: 5%) and the Railyards (patiotaller, progress as of
  September 30, 222: 55%). The preliminary works complete a 16.78 percent advance as of September 2022, in line with the implementation
  schedule. Resettlement activities are advancing well (89% of the 1,421 properties have been purchased), despite delays in EMB delivering
  properties to the concessionaire (only 18% have been delivered to the concessionaire). Anticipated utility transfer is advancing well, with progress at
  88 percent. The Project Supervision consultant (interventoria) is performing well and submitting the supervision reports to EMB.
