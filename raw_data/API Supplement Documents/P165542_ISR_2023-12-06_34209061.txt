 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The 'Strengthening Institutions for Refugee Administration Project' has made progress since the Mid-Term Review mission. This includes; i)
  notification of additional charge for the Project Director, Procurement Specialist and Financial Management Specialist, ii) an agreement has been
  reached with the Office of the Auditor General to perform the function of Third Party Verification Agent and a report on DLI achievement had been
  shared, ii) approval for the opening of the designated account has been received.
  The need to restructure the Project was confirmed as well as the need for continued attention and efforts to ensure progress to achieve results
  under both components. The mission also agreed on the need for a clear work plan to guide procurements and cash forecasting.
