 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  Disbursements: a small percentage (5.6%) of project funds has disbursed since project effectiveness (Aug 31, 2020). The Bank has
  worked closely with the Government to intensify procurement activities, and make them available on the Procurement Plan, available on
  the Bank's website. This work is now done. Disbursements in the coming months are expected, therefore, to significantly increase.
  Under the interim arrangement to verify achieved DLIs, three implementing agencies will soon be eligible to submit their documentation
  for review and approval by MOF. The three IAs include MOTI, which have an outstanding meeting to complete the DLI on the formal
  establishment of a Doing Business Inter-Ministerial Coordinating Committee (DBIMC); GEA for the establishment of the Ghana
  Enterprises Agency (GEA) according to best practices; and VCTF for the governance, operational, legal, and regulatory reforms,
  including approval of a sustainable funding model, and rebranding completed. This is expected by June 2021.
  The remaining dated legal covenants are nearly completed: The PCU has finalized the contract for consultancy services to develop an
  Independent Verification Manual to guide the implementation of the Disbursement Linked Indicators. The recruitment of an Independent
