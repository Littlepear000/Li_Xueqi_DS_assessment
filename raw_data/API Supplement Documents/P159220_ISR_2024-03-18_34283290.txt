 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The project team is glad to confirm that Project performance has been improved since last reporting period. Currently, construction works are
  underway and institutional components are making progress.
  Component 1: The Contractor performed well in the first construction season, completing approximately 40% of the work. While there is a need of
  additional equipment to be delivered on site the Contractor managed to purchase and stock on site bulk of materials required for construction
  season 2025.
  Component 2: Currently, State Civil Aviation Agency (SCAA) together with Project Implementation Unit is preparing Terms of References to start
  bidding procedures for: (i) equipment for safety auditors, (ii) training for inspectors and data storage facility and (iii) software with equipment for
  oversight record management for SCAA.

 Overall Comments PDO

  It is proposed to add New PDO level Indicator –(reduction in time required for travel by motor vehicle from km 39 to 76 of Tyup-Kegen road
  and from km 0 to km 13 of Karkyra-Turuk-Sary-Zhaz road section) – estimated baseline for this component is 94 minutes. This travel time
  through both road sections. The average time required to travel through 50 km of a fully constructed road with an average permitted speed limit
  of 60 km/h is about 50 minutes. Following such estimations, the end target for this indicator would be 44 minutes.

 Overall Comments IR

  Durign the last mission the status of the remaining 13 intermediate indicators was discussed. Additionally, below changes to intermediate
  indicators were discussed:

        a.   for Component 2 Aviation: following consultation with CAA and the need for adjustment of priorities for enhancement of aviation
             safety indicator “Organizational Review of the Civil Aviation Agency completed” will be changed to “The State Safety Program on the
             aviation of the Kyrgyz Republic developed”. It is proposed to change the formulation of indicator “Software and hardware is in use by
             CAA for record-keeping” to "Software and hardware is in use by SCAA for regulatory oversight management". It is proposed to
             change the formulation of indicator “Percentage implementation of CAA training plan as measured by ICAO USOAP” to "Consulting
             services for training of SCAA inspectors and personnel provided". It is proposed to change the formulation of indicator “Percentage
             of women participating in CAA training plan” to "Percentage of women-inspectors participating in SCAA training plan". It is proposed
             to divide indicator “Facilities and curriculum improvements are made to the Kyrgyz Aviation Institute” into two separate indicators:
             “Renovation of KAI buildings and facilities” and “Training equipment, teaching aids for KAI”. The last intermediate indicator for
             aviation component “Flight training device operational” will be adjusted towards “Training software and equipment for KAI”.
       b.    for Component 3 Tourism: It is proposed to change indicator “Sustainable Tourism Strategy for Issyk-Kul Oblast developed” to
             “Sustainable Tourism Program for Issyk-Kul Oblast developed”. Four indicators under this component: “Tourism Satellite Accounts
             established for Issyk-Kul Oblast”; “Number of Tourist Service Centers constructed”; “Number of beneficiaries participating in
             community-led tourism development in Tyup and Ak-Suu district”; and “Number of women beneficiaries participating in community-led
             tourism development in Tyup and Ak-Suu districts” will remain unchanged; however, due to previously received data on quantity of
             people-beneficiaries from Tyup and Aksuu districts, which was more than 150 times higher than end target, new further methodology
             to collect more accurate data was discussed. It has been specified that population who is attending public hearings are considered to
             be interested in subject and therefore considered to be direct beneficiaries of the project. And therefore it was decided to monitor
