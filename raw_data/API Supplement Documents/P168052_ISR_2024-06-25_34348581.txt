 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  Since the October 2023 mission, the project has made progress in implementing most subcomponents, meeting intermediate results indicators, and
  accelerating disbursements. Over the past six months, over 9,000 participants, more than 50% women, engaged in mobilization, focus group,
  planning and prioritization activities in the two new districts added at restructuring (Shamsidiin Shohiin, Darvoz). This led to over 100 new Village
  and Jamoat development plans, as well as village-led prioritization of 20 new subprojects (SPs), in addition to the 193 SPs prioritized in the original
  seven districts. Implementation of the SPs has accelerated, with an additional 50 SPs from the original districts completed (for a total of 103
  completed SPs out of 193). The remaining 90 SPs in the original districts are under implementation. Other donors have supported 131 SPs that
  communities identified in village and jamoat development plans, but were not selected for SERSP funding, illustrating recognition of SERSP’s
  approach to communities identifying their own development needs. Action is needed to address construction issues identified during previous
  missions, and to ensure all supported facilities include adequate water, heat, and sanitation to meet minimum standards.

  Progress continued on capacity building and livelihoods activities under Component 2. NSIFT agrees to accelerate training for youth under
  Subcomponent 2a in GBAO, and for specific modules. The Implementing Partner for Subcomponent 2b delivered livelihoods training, tools and
  equipment to almost 1,000 youth in GBAO and Khatlon since the past mission – to date a total of 1,255 youth (47% women) have received tools
  and equipment, covering 30 livelihood streams. For the second cohort of 2b beneficiaries, the mission requested NSIFT’s proposal of which
  livelihood streams will be supported, and how the additional youth will be trained, selected to receive tools and equipment, and mentored, reflecting
  lessons from the first cohort.
