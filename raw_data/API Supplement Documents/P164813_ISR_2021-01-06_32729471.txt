 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  This ISR is based on the recent third implementation support mission (August 17-25, 2020) as well as the monthly follow-up virtual meetings the
  Task Team conducts with the Project's National Implementation Unit (NIU).

  Nine months into the crisis, mobility and travel restrictions imposed amid Covid-19 continue to pose challenges to the implementation
  performance and achievements of the project. Progress have been made under all components in delivering agreed outputs but at a slower
  pace. The technical coordination mechanisms, slowly put in place, need to be strengthened, and close attention and supervision by Senior
  Management at the Ministry of Industry and Commerce (MOIC) but also the Ministry of Planning and Investment (MPI), other line Ministries, and the
  Prime Minister's Office is needed to ensure effective translation of technical outputs and recommendations into the needed policy changes.

  The project, especially the matching grant facility, continues to be an important vehicle in the government's response to the COVID-19
  pandemic. The negative consequences of the crisis for firms, their workers and dependent households stresses the urgency of accelerating policy
  changes supported by the LCTP and efficiently facilitating firms’ ability to do business on the ground, access international markets for inputs and
  outputs, thus contributing to creating the formal jobs Lao PDR needs and driving the economic recovery.
