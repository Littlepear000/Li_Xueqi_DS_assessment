 Project Development Objectives

  Project Development Objective (from Project Appraisal Document)
  To (i) strengthen capacity for integrated water resources management in the State; (ii) improve reliability of water services in the Agreste and
  Borborema regions; and (iii) improve the operational efficiency of water and wastewater services in the Joao Pessoa Metropolitan Region.

 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  At the closure of the Project's Mid Term Review (MTR) in October 2023, the client and the Bank concluded that a substantial increase in the Project
  implementation pace had been achieved, with most of the objectives of the Action Plan agreed in the November 2022 and May 2023 missions
  already attained. By November 2023, the contract for the construction of the Cariri pipeline and water treatment plant, worth US$85 million, and the
  contract for the automation system of the water distribution system of the Joao Pessoa Metropolitan Region, worth US$5 million, had been signed,
  and the preparation for contracts signing for two other major works -wastewater pumping station and outfall, sectorization of the water network-
   were well underway. Institutional support activities are progressing well and are helping the State institutions, notably CAGEPA and AESA, to
  advance their respective agendas.

  The client and the Bank team agree the PDO remains achievable within the closing date (June 30, 2026) as major contracts related to the
  Transparaíba System, the rehabilitation and expansion of the sewerage system in João Pessoa, and NRW activities to reduce physical losses for
  the metropolitan area start their implementation.
  Monthly monitoring and implementation support meetings are carried out with the client, the Bank Team will continue to support the Borrower with
  various experts. . The counterpart activities are progressing very well: works at the Curimatau pipeline and a wastewater pumping station in Joao
  Pessoa (Usina 1) are underway. Counterpart activities to reduce commercial losses are also yielding promising results with 24% of the target
  already met. Dam safety activities are being followed-up by a Panel of Experts with a need of further speeding up their implementation.
