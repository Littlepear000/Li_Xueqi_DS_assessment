 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  Overall, project implementation has slowed , resulting in a downgrade in the Implementation Progress rating. However, there has been some
  progress under the components including finalization of intervention design in selected target schools, preparatory work for school rehabilitation and
  recruitment of firm to develop structured lesson plans. The final report on the results from the participation in Program to Assess Education Systems
  of the CONFEMEN (Programme d'analyse des systemes educatifs de la CONFEMEN, PASEC) in 2022 is currently being finalized. In addition,
  preparation for the 2024 application has also started. PASEC is a regional learning assessment that tracks students in grades 2 and grades 6 in
  Portuguese and math.
