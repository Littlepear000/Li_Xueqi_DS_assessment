 Project Development Objectives

  Project Development Objective (from Project Appraisal Document)
  To (i) strengthen capacity for integrated water resources management in the State; (ii) improve reliability of water services in the Agreste and
  Borborema regions; and (iii) improve the operational efficiency of water and wastewater services in the Joao Pessoa Metropolitan Region.

  Has the Project Development Objective been changed since Board Approval of the Project Objective?

 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The loan agreement was signed in December 1, 2020; and was declared effective in January 2021 . The Project Management Unit at SEIRHMA,
  the Project Implementation Unit at CAGEPA, and a coordination team at AESA are progressing to advance technical and procurement
  documentation. Procurement of several activities with CAGEPA were already launched in the second quarter of 2021 and are also expected to be
  launched between June and December 2021, notably the DB contract for the Cariri pipeline system. Despite the initial delay, the client with the
  Bank are working closely to speed up the implementation and allow for the achievement of the PDO in the original project time; one PDO indicator
  has already been attained (annual operation water management plan for the Paraiba river, issued for years 2020, 2021 and 2022). The Bank Team
  will continue to support the Borrower with various experts, including procurement, wastewater treatment, water resources management, and non-
  revenue water specialists. Fortnightly monitoring and implementation support meetings have been scheduled for the most critical activities, including
  the Cariri water system, the wastewater collection and treatment works, and the non-revenue water program.
