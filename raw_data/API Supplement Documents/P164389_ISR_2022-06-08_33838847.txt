 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The Project was signed on February 28, 2022 and was declared effective as of June 1, 2022. To kickstart project implementation, the National
  Water Commission (CONAGUA) and the Valley of Mexico's Water Basin Agency (OCAVM) are preparing a number of works, supervision and
  goods procurement packages to be financed under the Project.
