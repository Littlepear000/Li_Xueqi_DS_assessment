 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The project has been effective since November 12, 2021, but disbursements have not started due to a delay by the Government in
  meeting a dated covenant. According to the Sections I.A.1. (a) and I.A.2. (a) of Schedule 2 of the Financing Agreement, it has been agreed that
  the Recipient should have established a Project Steering Committee and a Technical Committee, within one month of the effectiveness date. In
  addition, Section I.A.3. (a) of Schedule 2 of this same Financing Agreement also provides that the Recipient should have extended the mandate of
  the Project Implementation Unit (PIU) under the Ministry in charge of Economy and Finance, within one month of the entry into force of the
  Financing Agreement, the said PIU having to be maintained in place, during the whole implementation of the Project, with the mandate, composition
