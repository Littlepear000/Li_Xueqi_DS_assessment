 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The Project was declared effective by the International Development Association (IDA) on September 3, 2020 and is now under implementation.
  The operationalization of the project implementation unit is advanced. Word Food Program (WFP) was recruited in September 2021 to supply
  canteen stoves for 400 schools. The bidding process for the electrification of health centers and mini-grids is ongoing. Technical and environmental
  impact studies for 33 mini grid sites have started. The recruitment of a Transactions Advisor for the mini-grids is also ongoing . The grant
  administrator for the Solar and Cooking Energy Fund has been recruited since October 2022, while the recruitment of the technical assistant for the
  private sector facility for the provision of standalone solar systems and clean cookstoves to end beneficiaries is still ongoing.
