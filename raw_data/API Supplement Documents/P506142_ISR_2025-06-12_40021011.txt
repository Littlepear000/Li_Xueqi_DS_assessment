4.3 Multiphase Programmatic Approach (MPA) Status (Public Disclosure)
  The World Bank, in collaboration with Brazilian counterparts, has developed the Multiphase Programmatic Approach
  (MPA) to address challenges in family farming and improve coordination between federal and state policies in Brazil. This
  10-year program seeks to enhance the resilience of family farmers in targeted states and gradually expand agrifood
  public goods and services. It aims to transform agrifood systems and inform public policies, fostering collaboration across
  government levels to unify Brazil's agrifood policies into a cohesive national strategy.

  The MPA, concluding in 2034, comprises twelve phases aimed at benefiting 404,000 family farmer households (1.1 million
  people), including indigenous and traditional communities, with half of the beneficiaries expected to be women. It also
  supports farmers' organizations, impacting a broad range of agrifood sector stakeholders. The first two phases have been
  approved in Bahia and Santa Catarina. The program is implemented through a three-pillar strategy:
  1. Pillar 1: Direct support to family farmers and their organizations to reduce financial barriers to productive investments.
  2. Pillar 2: Enhancement of last-mile delivery of agrifood public goods and services to improve agrifood system
  development.
  3. Pillar 3: Creation of a feedback loop for governments to adopt green, resilient, and inclusive agrifood policies.
