1. OBJECTIVE


 1.1 Development Objective
   Original Development Objective (Approved as part of Approval package on 30-Jul-2020)
   The Project development objectives of the Fourth Phase of the Central Asia Regional Links Program are to enhance the
   efficiency of cross-border trade for participants of the regional economy and to improve the resilience and safety of
   regional connectivity infrastructure in Sughd region and Gorno-Badakhshan Autonomous region.

   The project contributes to the Central Asia Regional Links Programs higher-level development objectives of increasing
   cross-border connectivity and enhancing integrated regional development to revitalize historically active economic
   exchanges in Central Asia and beyond along the Silk Route.
   Has the Development Objective been changed since Board Approval of the Project Objective?

   No

4. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  Component 1: Improve Regional Connections in Sughd and GBAO Regions
  Civil works are progressing in both Sughd and GBAO regions. In Sughd, road rehabilitation across four sections has
  reached nearly 49% completion, closely tracking the planned schedule, with quality and safety measures improving
  significantly since the previous mission. In GBAO, the construction of key infrastructure, including the Khorog bridge,
  avalanche gallery, and related road works, is progressing well, surpassing the planned implementation target. Under this
  component, feasibility studies and designs for future road improvements in Sughd, GBAO, and Khatlon have been
  completed and approved.
  Component 2: Improve Road Asset Preservation and Road Safety
  Procurement process for installing Weigh-in-Motion systems is underway, with installation planned around Dushanbe.
  Road Asset Management System (RAMS) development is advancing. RAMS unit has been established, trainings were
  conducted, and procurement of necessary software and licenses were completed. On road safety, multiple initiatives are
  ongoing: efforts to improve seatbelt usage through legal reforms and public awareness are progressing, with draft
  legislation under review. Safety barrier installation for hazardous roads sections is under procurement process. A Road
  Safety Strategy is being developed, and procurement for establishing a Road Safety Observatory is ongoing after revisions
  to technical requirements.
  Component 3: Facilitate Cross-Border Movement of Goods
  Progress continues on enhancing customs infrastructure through the rollout of the ASYCUDA World platform. While key
  ICT infrastructure upgrades and server installations are complete, the ASYCUDA system remains in pilot phase at limited
  locations. Expansion is delayed, and further acceleration is required to ensure full implementation nationwide. A
  simplified version of the system is being considered for remote sites, and relevant legislative approvals are anticipated by
  May 2025 to support scale-up.
