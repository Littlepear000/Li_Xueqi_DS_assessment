 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  - Implementation support continues to be remote due to the COVID-19 pandemic, and its associated restrictions. Despite challenges, the project
  disbursed 47% of the total loan amount. Component 1 has been progressing very well. Delays are affecting the implementation of Components 2
  and 3 due to their innovative nature. Implementation is expected to accelerate in the coming months.
  - MSMEDA has been conducting a number of consultations over the past period, including a roundtable with key ecosystem players, which was
  well-attended and well-received. The official launch of the fund of funds program is expected in the upcoming FY.
