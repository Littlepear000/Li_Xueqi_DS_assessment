 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The project became effective on October 5, 2020, in during covid-19. As such the project was off to a slow start as many Energy Utilities’ ability to
  enter into power purchasing agreements with private investors was severely weakened. As a result, several infrastructure sub-projects in the
  pipeline have not materialized. Since then, TDB has managed to add several new deals to the pipeline as evidence of its strong business
  development efforts. Component #2 which finances off grid solar energy projects which had a total amount of funds of $75M, is now fully
  disbursed.
  COMESA Secretariat deliverables. The COMESA implementation progress is rated as satisfactory. Overall progress in the delivery of the
  workplan has improved with several deliverables in advance stages of completion. Two key deliverables have been approved by the Ministers of
  Infrastructure: (i) the development of COMESA model solar standards and (ii) the updating of the 2008 COMESA Model Energy Policy. Having
  received ministerial approval, COMESA is now providing technical assistance to various member states on the domestication and implementation of
