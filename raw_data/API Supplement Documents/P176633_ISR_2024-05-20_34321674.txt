1. OBJECTIVE


 1.1 Development Objective
   Original Development Objective (Approved as part of Approval package on 22-Jun-2023)
   The Project Development Objectives (PDO) are to (i) improve power system reliability and efficiency, (ii) increase access
   to electricity in selected areas, and (iii) facilitate the integration of renewable energy.
   Has the Development Objective been changed since Board Approval of the Project Objective?

4. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  The effectiveness deadline was moved to February 29, 2024, following an extension request from the government, and
  the project is now effective since that date. EDM-PIU, AMADER-PIU and the Central Coordination Team at the Ministry of
  Energy are all in place, and all remaining effectiveness conditions are met.
  Due to an acute power supply crisis faced by the country, a restructuring of the project is being explored to support the
  government in the response to the crisis. While discussions are ongoing on this potential restructuring, PIUs have been
  making progress on procurement processes for (i) hiring their additional staff needed to implement the project and (ii)
  critical project activities.
