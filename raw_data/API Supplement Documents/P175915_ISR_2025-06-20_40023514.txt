4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  The mission noted key advances in the SNRM project including signing of contracts to start the following key activities:
      1. Construction of the fishing wharfs of Ouakam, Fass Boye and Cap Skirring;
      2. Purchase of air quality stations;
      3. Technical Assistance for Capacity Building in Environmental Monitoring, Environmental Assessments.
  The project’s action plan continues to yield good results, however the ability to accelerate on implementation has been
  hindered by disbursement delays portfolio-wide in Senegal.
