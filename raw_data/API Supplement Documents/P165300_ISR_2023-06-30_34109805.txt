 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The Project was approved, and Loan documents were signed in August 2018. Project implementation efforts at this early-stage focus on preliminary
  works on the Calle 72 road interchange (intercambiador vial, progress as of March, 2023: 20.67% %) and the Railyards (patiotaller, progress as of
  March, 2023: 66.68%). The overall progress of the Project, including the concession, TAR, and resettlement is 19.29% vs 19.80 programmed.
  Resettlement activities are advancing well (91.7% of the 1,421 properties have been purchased), despite delays in EMB delivering properties to the
  concessionaire (80.2% have been delivered to the concessionaire). Anticipated utility network transfer (TAR) is almost finishing with 98.17%
  progress. The Project Supervision consultant (interventoria) is performing well and submitting the supervision reports to EMB.
