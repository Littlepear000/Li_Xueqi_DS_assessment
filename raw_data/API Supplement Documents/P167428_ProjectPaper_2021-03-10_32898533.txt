I. PROJECT STATUS AND RATIONALE FOR RESTRUCTURING

                A. The Regional Economic Development Project (RED-P) became effective on 7 December 2020. Following
                   effectiveness, the Government of the Kyrgyz Republic (GoKR) was required to meet three (3) legal
                   covenants. Two of these have been met, and one requires an extension.
                B. The GoKR has requested an extension to the dated covenant relating to the finalization of the Small
                   Grants Program Committee to be formed for the implementation of Component 4. The fulfilment of
                   this dated covenant has been delayed due to uncertainty regarding the implementation of this
                   Component. A revolving fund methodology was initially proposed to be used by the Government to
                   implement Component 4. Following inter-ministerial discussions and consultations with the World Bank,
                   it was agreed to revert to the original implementation mechanism – the use of a Small Grants Program
                   Committee. This uncertainty has impacted the Government’s ability to establish the Small Grants
                   Program Committee, which was required to have been completed 60 days after the effectiveness of the
                   project (i.e. 5 February 2021). As a result, the Government has requested additional time to establish
                   this committee.

II. DESCRIPTION OF PROPOSED CHANGES

                C. The Government of the Kyrgyz Republic has requested to extend the deadline for meeting the dated covenant
                   from February 5, 2020 to April 1, 2021. Given the types of changes to be made to the government structure,
                   the extension of the deadline for the covenant is proposed as follows:
                   (a) To facilitate the carrying out of Part 4 of the Project, the Recipient shall establish through a Ministry of
