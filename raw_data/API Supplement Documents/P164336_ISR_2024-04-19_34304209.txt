 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  This ISR is issued following the 7th ISM held on March 8-29, 2024 and reflects the progress made and results attained since the 6th ISM in
  September, 2023.
  The project continues to be rated Satisfactory for PDO as progress has continued to be made on two key PDO-level indicators while a third PDO
  indicator target has already been met (# of people with improved access to economic and social services which now surpasses 1.4 million people).

  Implementation Progress continued to be Moderately Satisfactory. Improvement in the implementation of component 1 has allowed the team to
  upgrade the component rating to Satisfactory, the steering and technical committees, along with PCUs, have effectively supported the
  implementation. The Project M&E system, aided by MIS and Geo-Enables Monitoring System (GEMS) is properly tracking, documenting, and
  reporting on project activities and results. Financial Management provides reasonable assurance that the project funds are used for intended
