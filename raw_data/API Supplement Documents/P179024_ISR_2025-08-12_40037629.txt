1. OBJECTIVE


 1.1 Development Objective
   Original Development Objective (Approved as part of Decision package on 06-Sep-2022)
   The objective of the Project is to support the Kyrgyz Republic in mitigating the impact of the economic crisis on poor
   households and labor migrants, and strengthening effectiveness and resilience of the social assistance and labor market
   programs delivery systems, to be better prepared for future shocks.
   Has the Development Objective been changed since Board Approval of the Project Objective?

   No

 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The Overall Implementation Progress is maintained at the Satisfactory level, despite further delays under Subcomponent
  1.2 “Improving the Effectiveness of the UBK Program”. Implementation of the subcomponent activities are expected to
  be on track once the new methodology is finalized. The project has disbursed US$19.4 million (65 percent of the total
  commitment amount). Out of 6 PDO indicators, 4 have reached or exceeded the targets, which are: PDO Indicator (1):
  Number of children receiving UBK top-up payments (Actual: 346,334, Target: 340,000), PDO Indicator (2): Number of
  families receiving increased UBK benefits on time (Actual: 105,626, Target: 105,000), PDO Indicator (5): Number of
  beneficiaries of the Social Contract program with productive inclusion initiatives operating after 6 months (Actual: 3,000,
  Target: 2,000), and PDO Indicator (6): Share of MLSDM (MLSSM) district offices that have access to central level
  Information Technology (IT) resources (Actual: 100 percent, Target: 100 percent).
  The first project restructuring was done in June 2025 to reallocate the savings of US$5.5 million from Subcomponent 1.1
  to Components 2 & 3 and extend the closing date till April 30, 2027, to ensure a full completion of the planned activities.
  The Ministry of Labor, Social Security and Migration (MLSSM) and the Project implementation Unit have completed the
  activities on Social Contract projects under Subcomponent 2.1 “Supporting Graduation from Social Assistance through
  Economic Inclusion Opportunities” by making payouts to 3,000 beneficiaries financed through the Project. The
  implementation of Social Contract projects by beneficiaries is ongoing. Some training activities under Subcomponent 2.2
  “Enhancing Employability and Job Opportunities among Youth Population” activities have been launched. Activities under
  Subcomponent 2.3 on the development of the Migrant Registry are expected to progress in the next several months.
