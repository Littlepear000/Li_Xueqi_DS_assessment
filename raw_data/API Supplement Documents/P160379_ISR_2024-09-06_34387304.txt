1. OBJECTIVE


 1.1 Development Objective
   Original Development Objective (Approved as part of Approval package on 28-Mar-2019)
   The Project Development Objectives (PDO) are to demonstrate the operational and economic feasibility of utility-scale
   innovative renewable energy technologies and battery energy storage solutions, and to strengthen institutional capacity
   to facilitate scale-up of such technologies on a commercial basis in India.
   Has the Development Objective been changed since Board Approval of the Project Objective?

   No

4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  a) First of the two subprojects supported by the Project - 100 MW(AC) Solar PV Project with 40 MW/120 MWh Battery
  Energy Storage System - at Rajnandgaon, Chhattisgarh was commissioned on February 1, 2024. This is India's largest
  Battery Energy Storage Project.
  The project was formally inaugurated by the Hon'ble Prime Minister of India on 24th February 2024
  (https://pmindiawebcast.nic.in/2024/24feb24.html ).
  Further details on this subproject are available at the Government of India’s Public Information Bureau’s website (
  https://pib.gov.in/PressReleaseIframePage.aspx?PRID=2008681#:~:text=The%2040%20megawatts%20(MW)%20%2F,is%
  20located%20in%20Rajnandgaon%2C%20Chhattisgarh. )
  Climate Investment Fund (CIF), which is the co-funder alongwith IBRD for this subproject, also covered this achievement
  under their Project Spotlight' series. It can be accessed here: https://www.cif.org/news/project-spotlight-indias-largest-
  solar-battery-project-delivered
  b) The second project is a 100 MW Floating Solar PV Subproject in Getalsud, Jharkhand. The contract was awarded to M/s
  L&T ahead of schedule on May 7, 2024, and site surveys have been initiated.
