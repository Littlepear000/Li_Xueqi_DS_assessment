3. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


 3.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
   Implementation Status
   Overall, satisfactory progress is noted in achieving the program's development objectives (PDOs) with four out of six of
   the annual targets of the PDO indicators achieved. This progress is reflected in:
       1. a groundnut yield of 1.27 tons/hectare in 2019-2020; 1.47 tons/hectare in 2020-2021; 1.22 tons/hectare in 2021-
            2022; 1.38 tons/hectare in 2022/2023; 1.34 tons/hectare in 2023/2024 and provisionally 1.02 tons/hectare in
            2024/2025, which is below the annual target of 1.3 tons per hectare. Irregular rainfall marked by a dry spell
            during the groundnut flowering stage led to this drop in yield despite the Government's efforts in terms of seed
            and fertilizer subsidy program;
       2. a good progress in the yields of diversification crops with: (i) a yield for maize increasing from 2.65 tons/hectare
            in 2021/2022 to 2.64 tons/hectare in 2022/2023, 3.07 tons/hectare in 2023/2024 and 3.00 tons/hectare in
            2024/2025 reaching the annual and final target of the project; and (ii) a yield for millet increasing from 1.12

      3. an increase in groundnut exports with 319,786 tons exported in 2021/2022 (139% achieved) out of a target of
           230,000 tons and 105,409 tons exported in 2022/2023 and 105,458 tons in 2023/2024 out of an annual target of

      4. farmers benefiting from the project's assets and services are estimated at 350,263 producers, including 158,862

           have not yet been measured; and
      5. a decrease in small ruminant mortality estimated at 13 percent in 2024, exceeding the annual target of 8 percent
           and close to the final target of 15 percent.
  Regarding the disbursement linked indicators (DLIs), progress is noted with the annual targets of 2024 achieved for seven
  out of the ten DLIs of the Program. In particular, significant further progress is made in the vaccination against the small
  ruminant plague (DLI4) and in the production of certified forage seeds (DLI5). Regarding the overdue institutional
  reforms, progress has been noted in the restructuring process of the Interprofessional Groundnut Committee (CNIA - ILD
  6) and in the preparation of the M&E manual and digital platform for agricultural subsector (ILD 10) taking into account
  the new agricultural sectoral policy letter.
  Key Decision
  The Government needs to disburse the achieved DLIs to improve the Program disbursement status. The important
  reforms related to CNIA and M&E are taking off and need to be speeded up to get them completed by end June 2025. A
  verification of the DLIs (3, 4,5,10) should be conducted by May to increase disbursement. Except DLI 9 related to
  operational milk processing centers with so far 42 rehabilitated/built against a target of 72, all the others could
  be met by project closing date (December 31, 2025). A partial cancellation of the remaining resource allocated
  to this DLI9 could be envisaged by end June 2025 if no significant progress as promised by the MASAE is
  observed.
