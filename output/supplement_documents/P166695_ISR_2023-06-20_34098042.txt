 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The Gambia Fiscal Management Development Project (FMDP – P166695) is a US$35 million International Development Association (IDA) Grant,
  which was approved by the Board of Executive Directors on June 4, 2020, became effective on July 28, 2020. In order to respond to a number of
  fiscal management challenges in the Gambia at that time, the Project Development Objective (PDO) sought “to increase fiscal space and improve
  the effectiveness of public resources management.” To achieve this objective, the project is organized around three components, including: (i)
  strengthening revenue collection and public procurement processes; (ii) supporting fiscal management of State-owned Enterprises (SOEs) and
  restructuring plans of telecoms’ SOEs; and (iii) project implementation support and reform coordination. At the time of appraisal, the PDO and
  project components were aligned with the strategic objectives of the Government’s National Development Plan (NDP) and the World Bank Group
  Country Engagement Note for the Republic of The Gambia for FY18-FY21 (CEN n° IDA/R 2018-0139).

  After nearly three years since effectiveness in 2020, progress toward achieving the Project Development Objective (PDO) is Moderately
  Unsatisfactory, with a cumulative disbursement rate of only 25 percent. Despite the initial delay pertaining to key activities, Project Implementation is
  currently rated Moderately Satisfactory, as improved progress has been recorded in the year. All three components have recorded Moderately
  Satisfactory performance, and key milestones have been reached over the period, including: (i) the completion of the Gambia Revenue Authority
  (GRA) business re-engineering and ITAS tendering, which as at an advanced stage; (ii) the passage of the GPPA Bill by the National Assembly; (iii)
  passage of the SOE Bill by the National Assembly; (iv) the acquisition of the Gambia Public Utilities Regulatory Authority (PURA) Quality of Service
  monitoring tool for 4G, acquisition of a Billing Verification System for PURA, and the completion of the Spectrum assessment report; (v) the
  development of an electronic government procurement (e-GP) readiness report; (vi) the organization of the market test for Gamtel/Gamcel; as well
  as (vii) the public disclosure of three SOE performance audit reports by the National Audit Office. Nevertheless, other key project milestones are
  delayed, including government decisions on Gamtel/Gamcel privatization options. All of these issues are noted in detail below. Despite this recent
  progress, annual targets have not been met on any of the three PDO indicators. Likewise, seven out of eleven intermediate results indicators have
  not met their annual targets.
