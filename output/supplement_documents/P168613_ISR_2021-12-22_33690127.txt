 Project Development Objectives

  Project Development Objective (from Project Appraisal Document)
  The Development Objective is to strengthen institutional capacities for integrated management of mineral and natural resources in Guinea and
  enhanced benefits from the mining and environment sectors.

  Has the Project Development Objective been changed since Board Approval of the Project Objective?
  No

 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  Following the events of early September 2021, OP7.30 was triggered and as a result project implementation slowed down, including procurement
  for several key activities. An annual budgeted work plan for the year 2022 is in preparation as well as the procurement plan and the terms of
  reference for the main contracts and activities.
  A project kick-off workshop and steering committee meeting are planned for early 2022.
