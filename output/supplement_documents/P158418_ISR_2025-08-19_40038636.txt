1. OBJECTIVE


 1.1 Development Objective
   Original Development Objective (Approved as part of Approval package on 22-Jan-2019)
   The objective of the project is to improve irrigation service delivery, through rehabilitation and modernization of
   distribution infrastructure in selected schemes, and through strengthening of capacity of WUAs.
   Has the Development Objective been changed since Board Approval of the Project Objective?

   No

4. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

            Türkiye Irrigation Modernization (P158418)


  Under Component 1, the Project finances investments to modernize DSI’s irrigation systems in Atabey, Eregli-Ivriz, Alasehir,
  and Uzunlu schemes, with a combined area of 50,764 hectares benefiting 17,312 people. Component 2 covers (i) designing and
  piloting a national program for Water Users Association (WUA) capacity building (US$1.1 million), and (ii) groundwater
  stewardship pilot (US$0.9 million) through US$2.0 million GEF grant.
  Under Component 1 and Component 2, all the contracts have been signed and the works are on-track.
