 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  The project was approved by the Board on March 23, 2019, and the financial agreement was signed on April 9, 2019. The project was declared
  effective on September 9, 2019 and was officially launched in Conakry during a workshop on September 27, 2019. The second session of ANAFIC
  Board of Directors took place on September 10-11, 2019.
