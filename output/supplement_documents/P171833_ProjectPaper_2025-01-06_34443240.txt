    I. PROJECT STATUS AND RATIONALE FOR RESTRUCTURING

      1. This restructuring paper seeks the World Bank Country Director’s approval for a Level II Restructuring of the
         Unleashing the Blue Economy of the Caribbean (UBEC) Project (P171833) to include and reflect the Ministry of
         Mobilization, Implementation and Transformation (MIT) as the Implementing Agency for the project with the
         responsibility for overall project management including monitoring and evaluation of project activities. If
         approved, this would be the Project’s second restructuring1.

      2. The UBEC Series of Project (SOP) is designed to be implemented in three five-year phases over a fifteen-year
         period involving multi-sectoral blue economy investments to support Caribbean countries to capitalize on their
         untapped coastal and marine natural resources, strengthen their climate resilience and accelerate economic
         recovery from the impacts of the global polycrisis. The SOP provides a framework for integrating activities in the
         tourism, fisheries, and waste management sectors around a shared goal of regional development. The SOP
         includes synchronized interventions at national and regional levels to leverage economies of scale and ensure a
         harmonized approach. The first Phase of the SOP is financed with National International Development Association
         (IDA) credits and grants, supplemented with PROBLUE Recipient Executed Trust Fund (RETF) grants. Specifically,
         Phase I of the SOP involves Grenada, Saint Lucia, Saint Vincent and the Grenadines, and the Organization of
         Eastern Caribbean States (OECS) Commission and is financed with a US$86 million package of national and regional
         IDA credits and grants, including the US$30 million Additional Financing from the Crisis Response Window-Early
         Response Financing (CRW-ERF) supplemented with US$4 million of PROBLUE RETF grants. Phase I of the UBEC SOP
         was approved by the Board on May 2, 2022. The OECS Commission declared effectiveness (Grant E020-60) on
         October 11, 2022, Grenada (Credit 7087) on November 11, 2022, Saint Lucia (Credit 7088) on January 11, 2023,
         and Saint Vincent and the Grenadines (Credit 7089) on March 31, 2023. The PROBLUE RETF Grant Agreements of
         US$1 million each for the four Borrowers have been signed for OECS Commission (Grant TF0C1218) on August 25,
         2024, Grenada (Grant TF0C1219) on August 30, 2024, Saint Lucia (Grant TF0C1220) on July 5, 2024, and Saint
         Vincent and the Grenadines (Grant TF0C1217) on June 30, 2024.

      3. Project Status and Performance: Based on the June 25, 2024, Implementation Status and Result Report, UBEC
         SOP Implementation Status Report, progress towards achieving the PDO and overall implementation
         performance of the SOP is deemed Satisfactory, given timely advancement made with implementation of the
         food insecurity CRW-ERF activities, through activation of the Contingency Emergency Response component
         (CERC), and early onset implementation of the original set of Blue Economy activities. The Project is disbursing
         well in the three countries and through the regional entity with upwards of US$18.9 million or 22 percent of the
         now US$86 m IDA financing. For Grenada, the disbursement is of US$4.0 million or 16 percent of the now US$25
         million. Disbursements to date have focused on implementation of the food insecurity activities and project
         management. Participating countries have also commenced procurement of the agreed Blue Economy activities
         in the fisheries, tourism and waste sectors. CERC activities are well advanced, and it is expected that around 95
         percent of these activities will be completed by December 31, 2024. Implementation of activities under
         Components 2 and 3 have been delayed due to a strong focus on the CERC activities, but nevertheless
         procurement of some of the key activities have been initiated to allow for project activities to be implemented in
         2025. The Central Procurement Unit (CPU) has proved efficient to procure goods, works and services for the CERC,

      4. The Ministry of Finance, Government of Grenada through its communications of May 13, 2024, and July 26, 2024,
         advised the World Bank that MIT has been authorized by the Cabinet of Grenada to be the ‘Implementing Agency’
         for this project with the responsibility for overall project management including the monitoring and evaluation of
         project activities. The MIT will work collaboratively with the relevant coordinating ministries and agencies,
         including the Ministry of Finance, the Ministry in charge of Fisheries (MoFA - Ministry of Economic Development,
         Planning, Tourism, Creative Economy, Culture, Agriculture and Lands, Forestry, Marine Resources and
         Cooperatives2), the Ministry in charge of Tourism (MoT - Ministry of Economic Development, Planning, Tourism,
         Creative Economy, Culture, Agriculture and Lands, Forestry, Marine Resources and Cooperatives) and Grenada
         Solid Waste Management Authority (GSWMA). The project is being restructured to reflect this revised
         implementation arrangements advised by the Government of Grenada.

    II. DESCRIPTION OF PROPOSED CHANGES

      5.    The Implementation Arrangements for the Project is being revised to include MIT as the Implementing Agency,
           instead of the Ministry of Economic Development, Planning, Tourism, Creative Economy, Culture, Agriculture and
           Lands, Forestry, Marine Resources and Cooperatives (MoED) for the project with responsibility for overall project
           management including monitoring and evaluation of project activities.

      6. In addition to the Financing Agreement, the Project Operations Manual and the Environmental and Social
         Commitment Plan for the project are being revised to reflect these changes in Implementation Arrangements for
         the project.
