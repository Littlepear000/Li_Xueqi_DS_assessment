 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The Ghana Jobs and Skills Project (GJSP) became effective on January 27, 2021 (the original deadline for effectiveness was November 24, 2020),
  with the establishment of the Project Coordinating Unit under the Ministry of Finance and the preparation of the Project Implementation Manual.
  Toward project implementation, the project coordinating, implementing, and partnering agencies fare are currently finalizing their workplans and
  procurement plans for GJSP covering the first 18-month period of project implementation. Consistent with the institutional and implementation
