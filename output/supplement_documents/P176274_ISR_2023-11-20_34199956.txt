 Project Development Objectives

  Project Development Objective (from Project Appraisal Document)

 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  An Implementation Support Mission (ISM) took place from October 23, 2023, to November 4, 2023. The Project Implementation Progress and
  Development Objectives are rated moderately satisfactory following steady implementation progress as follows

  Following the restructuring of the Project in December 2022, all the effectiveness conditions were met on June 22, 2023. The members of the
  Project Implementation Unit (PIU) took office on 1 July 2023. The Steering Committee and the Technical Committee of the Project have been
  created by decree and will convene in November 2023.

  A workshop was held to present the results of the SME studies undertaken by the World Bank with the support of the European Union.
