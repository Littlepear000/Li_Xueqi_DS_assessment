 Project Development Objectives

  Project Development Objective (from Project Appraisal Document)
  The Project Development Objectives (PDO) are to improve access to, and the quality of early childhood and basic education in the Project Areas
  and to strengthen the capacity of the education system to deliver better results.

 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The Project was approved by the World Bank Executive Directors on July 15, 2019. It became effective on July 15, 2020. The military coup of
  September 5,2021 led to suspension of the constitution and public institutions. As a result, the World Bank’s Operation Policy OP. 7.30 “Dealing
  with de Facto government” was triggered on September 9,2021 and all the World Bank’s operations in Guinea were put on hold.

  A new Government was fully formed on November 2021, with a new Ministry of Pre-University Education and Literacy (Ministère de l'Enseignement
  Pre-Universitaire et de l'Alphabétisation - MEPU-A). The World Bank approved the resumption of the disbursement on March 9, 2022. The Project
  Coordination Unit (PCU) is fully staffed and operational, i.e., the project coordinator and all key specialists are in place. The Mid-Term-Review took
  place on June 1-7, 2023

  To date most Project key activities are under implementation.

  Component 1: Improving equitable access to pre-school education. The bids for the construction of one pre-primary classroom in 765 public
  primary schools have been launched in March 2023 and 361 contracts have been signed. With the technical assistance of UNICEF, a draft pre-
  school policy has been developed and validated by the MEPU-A. The 1,000 pre-primary teachers in charge of the new pre-primary classes have
  been selected and recruited, and the training program for July 2023 has been validated. The four recruited NGOs have completed the sensitization
  and reinforcement of the beneficiary communities' capacity for the supervision of all the activities at the local level.

  Component 2: Improving pre-school and basic education quality using technological innovation. The development of the policy for in-
  service teachers' training using new technologies is underway with the support of a consulting firm. The contract for the purchase of 19,620 tablets
  and 4,000 chargers has been signed.

  Component 3: Strengthening education sector management capacity. The transfer of responsibility for teacher recruitment from the central to
  the prefectural and regional levels is completed and about 18,000 teachers have been recruited and assigned through the mechanism. Hence the
  DLI 3 related to the decentralized recruitment of teachers to the prefectural and regional levels has been successfully achieved. The development
  of the model for the operationalization of a non-salary recurrent budget to public primary schools is underway and the allocation of budget to all
  primary schools is planned for the 2023/2024 school year. The first activity to enhance the MPEU-A capacity on student learning assessment has
  been completed, with the training of the education staff and the piloting of the leaning assessment for pre-school (MELQO). The contract
  with UNESCO is being implemented with the first phase on the assessment of the MEPU-A information system management.

  The Independent Verification Agency (IVA) for the verification of the Disbursement-Linked Indicators (DLIs) has been recruited and the IVA has
  verified the achievement of the DLI 3 on "Recruitment of teachers is decentralized at prefectural and regional levels" and the associated four DLRs.
