 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The project made a significant improvement in performance over the last ISR cycle. The first wave of national face-to face training for more than
  33,000 village governments and institutions was successfully completed in November 2023. The second wave for the remaining 33,000 villages will
  be implemented in 2024, taking the election schedule into account. The Learning Management System is on track for development to support
  further scale in future years.

  A second project restructuring was also completed, which will enable the project to fully achieve its PDO, with a revised Results Framework that
  enables timely measurement of updated indicators and targets, using existing government data.

 Overall Comments PDO

  The first original PDO indicator (i) “Village institutional capacity to execute core functions strengthened” has been replaced by “Increase in
  village governments submitting village budget realization report (APBDes). The second PDO indicator (ii) “Beneficiaries feel that village
  investments meet their needs” has been replaced by “Increase in villages with an increase in "village development status (IDM)".

  The project exceeded its targets for 2023 on both PDO indicators: (i) The submission rate of the APBD budget realization report from village to
  regional governments, targeted at 60 percent in 2023, has now reached 79 percent, and the project’s end target has also already been
  exceeded; (ii) the number of villages with an increase in village development status has reached 28.36% percent, over the 2023 target of 25
  percent.

  These targets are derived from GoI’s own performance targets and were developed in anticipation of the impact of the P3PD (as the project is
  known locally) interventions and would not have been achieved without project outputs. Prior to the revision of the RF, the progress
  measurement towards achievement of the PDO was hindered by multiple factors: 1. Significant implementation delays (also due to the COVID
  19 pandemic), 2. Poor alignment of activities with the project’s results framework, and 3. Absence of PDO indicator data to measure progress,
  as the data monitoring systems were largely intended to be produced by the project. The delays resulted in the absence of the necessary data
  to measure the RF indicators, especially the PDO indicators. The P3PD Steering Committee (SC) agreed in March 2023 to overhaul the results
  framework based on the recommendation of the mid-term review. The indicators were agreed with the WB and were developed based on the
  following criteria: close alignment with the PDO; adequate capture of P3PD activities already implemented or under implementation, and use of
  existing GoI village level data.
