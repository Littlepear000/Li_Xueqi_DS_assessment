 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The Gambia Fiscal Management Development Project became effective on July 28, 2020. The five-year project is financed through a US$35 million
  International Development Association (IDA) Grant.

  The Project Development Objective is to increase fiscal space and improve the effectiveness of public resources management. This will be
  achieved by: (i) enhancing the Gambia Revenue Authority’s (GRA) compliance management, revenue risk analysis, and enforcement capacity, for
  broadening the tax base and improving voluntary compliance; (ii) introducing electronic Government Procurement (e-GP); and (iii)
  improving financial and operational performance of State-Owned Enterprises (SOEs) and reducing the telecom-related fiscal burden.

  The project responds to a context in which SOEs; fiscal burden is unsustainable for public finance, and in which a significant tax potential gap (5-7
  percent of GDP) constrains revenue collection. The Gambia Fiscal Management Development Project (GFMDP) is aligned with the strategic
  objectives of the Government’s National Development Plan (NDP) and the World Bank Group Country Engagement Note for the Republic of The
  Gambia for FY18-FY21 (CEN n° IDA/R 2018-0139).

  Progress towards the achievement of the PDO and overall Implementation Progress are respectively Moderately Unsatisfactory and Moderately
  Satisfactory. After the declaration of the Project's effectiveness on July 28, 2020, the procurement processes of key capacity building and technical
  assistance contracts have been launched. All Project Implementing Unit staffing recruitment is completed. The GRA business review and the
  technical assistance for Electronic Government Procurement (e-GP) readiness review contracts have commenced. The disbursement rate stands at
  9.78 percent as of November 2021. Nonetheless, critical reforms embedded in the SOE Bill and the restructuring of the Telecom SOE sector have
  been delayed.
