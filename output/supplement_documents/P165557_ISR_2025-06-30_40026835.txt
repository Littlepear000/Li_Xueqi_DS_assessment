     4. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


     4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
1. Overall progress towards the PDO and Implementation Progress (IP) are both rated Satisfactory Three out of five PDO
   indicators (PDO 2; PDO 3a; 3b; and PDO 4) are on track to be achieved.
1. PDO Indicator 2: Students benefiting from direct interventions to enhance learning (CRI) disaggregated by gender – the
   number of student beneficiaries has exceeded the target, reaching 2,982,824 students in 2023 (target = 2,199,375
   students), and 2,982,824 students in 2024 (target = 2,328,750 students). Under the GPE GALOP second additional
   financing under preparation, the end of project (EOP) target is proposed to be 3.1 million learners. The target
   proportion of girls benefiting under the project is 50 percent. Girls' beneficiaries has increased from 47.0 percent in
   2022 to 48.3 percent in 2024. The mission team advised the Ministry of Education to implement more activities that
   encourage girls’ enrollment.

1. PDO Indicator 3a: Increased percentage of kindergarten (KG) schools with pupil trained teacher ratio below 50:1, and
   PDO 3b: Increased percentage of targeted primary schools with pupil trained teacher ratio below 50:1 have increased
   from 63.0 percent in 2022 to 66.1 percent in 2024 for kindergarten, and from 81.0 percent to 84.7 percent for primary
   schools during the same period. These indicators are on track to meet the target of 70.0 percent of KG schools and 90
   percent of primary schools with PTTR below 50:1.

1. PDO Indicator 4: Increased number of schools using accountability dashboard data during cluster level meetings. This
   PDO indicator reflects that 9,856 schools are using the accountability dashboard, slightly short of the target of 10,000
   schools using accountability. With the restructuring and extension of GALOP AF2, a new target of 15,000 schools by
   end-of-project has been agreed. This new target is likely to be achieved with the scale up of interventions to 6,000
   additional primary schools under the GALOP AF2.

1. PDO Indicator 1: Increased percentage of targeted schools with teaching practices meeting inspection standards. The
   percentage of targeted schools with teaching practices meeting inspection standards, defined as schools scoring “good”
   and “outstanding” (ratings 1 and 2) is 18.98 percent in 2024 against a target of 25 percent by the extended project
   closing of December 31, 2025. To achieve this indicator, the following steps were recommended: (i) NaSIA to provide
   granular data to identify specific areas where schools are falling below “good” rating; and (ii) GES to develop a guide for
   SISOs to address the deficient areas through specific coaching and mentoring.

1. The Implementation Progress is rated satisfactory based on (i) activities implemented under key components, (ii) the
   execution rates of the 2024 implementation plan and budget (including commitments), and (iii) the status of PBCs
   achieved. The implementation status of component 1, component 2, component 3 and component 4 are all rated
   satisfactory. Overall, 60 percent of the funds committed under the 2024 annual budget for the project have been
   disbursed. An Independent Verification Agency (IVA) was contracted September 30, 2024, to complete the verification
   of PBCs under the parent GALOP. Annex 2 presents the status on PBCs under the parent GALOP. Procurement, financial
   management and safeguards are also rated satisfactory. However, monitoring and evaluation and safeguards are both
