1. OBJECTIVE


 1.1 Development Objective
   Original Development Objective (Approved as part of Approval package on 12-Dec-2019)
   The project’s development objective is to improve (i) the relevance and excellence of scientific research, and (ii)
   innovative entrepreneurship and access to finance for enterprise growth, as a way of contributing to Serbia’s growth and
   competitiveness.

   Relevance of research refers to the potential of research results to be commercialized on the market, thus contributing to
   the economy, while excellence refers to international recognition of the quality of the research.

   Has the Development Objective been changed since Board Approval of the Project Objective?

   No

4. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  Project implementation is advancing well, with disbursement on track. On September 26, 2024, the Bank approved the
  SAIGE Additional Financing of €25 million.
  Update by Component:
  1.1 Science Fund. Ongoing SF activities and programs, expected to provide high-level research, competitiveness at the
  international level, innovative results, and relevance to society, are progressing well. SF continues to support
  fundamental and applied scientific research and their potential for commercial applications, project evaluations are
  based on competitiveness, transparency, objectivity and independence, and procedures are aligned with the procedures
  followed by other funding agencies within the EU.
  1.2 RDI Transformation. RDI reforms are progressing very well. Two new Institutes have joined the reforms, bringing the
  total to 20. RDIs are continuing with capacity building activities, including on communications, human resources,
  governance, project management, grant proposal writing, and commercialization. Many RDIs are pursuing new
  collaborative projects with partner companies and research institutions. Mentorship for further advancing RDI
  development will also be explored and provided in the coming period.
  2. Innovation Fund. The Katapult Acceleration program, providing support to growth-oriented Serbian startups, through
  better access to external private sector sources of financing, continues to attract private equity co-investment into
  innovative firms, and to increase the number of innovative products and services on the market.
  3. Project Management. The PIU continues to perform very well.
