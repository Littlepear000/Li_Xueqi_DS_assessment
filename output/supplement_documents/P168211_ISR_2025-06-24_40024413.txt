1. PROJECT DEVELOPMENT OBJECTIVE


 1.1 Development Objective
   Original Development Objective (Approved as part of Approval package on 25-Feb-2020)
   The Program development objectives are to improve the financial viability, increase the reliability of electricity supply,
   and strengthen the governance of Barqi Tojik, Shabakahoi Intiqoli Barq, and Shabakahoi Taqsimoti Barq.

   Current Development Objective (Approved as part of Additional Financing package Seq. 1 on 30-Jun-2022)
   The Program development objectives are to improve the financial viability, increase the reliability of electricity supply,
   and strengthen the governance of Barqi Tojik, Shabakahoi Intiqoli Barq, and Shabakahoi Taqsimoti Barq.

3. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


 3.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
    Following the Government request (dated March 18, 2024), the PforR has been restructured to ensure accelerated
   transition towards energy sector’s cost recovery by 2027 (reflecting agreements made with the Government in the
   context of the Bank's engagement on Rogun HPP).
   As of April 30, 2025, US$100.2 million have been disbursed, with a total disbursement ratio of 47 percent. During the
   reporting period, some of the DLRs have been achieved and disbursement shall be made for those DLRs after the
   Program Restructuring becomes effective and achievement of the DLRs is assessed and verified by an independent
   verification audit. It is expected that in total about US$ 35million of the Program will be disbursed by August 30, 2025.
