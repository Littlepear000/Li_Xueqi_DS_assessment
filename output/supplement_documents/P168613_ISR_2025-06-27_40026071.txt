 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

               Guinea Natural Resources, Mining and Environmental Management Project (P168613)



  The implementation of ongoing project-supported reforms in the mining and environmental sectors underscore the
  Natural Resources, Mining and Environmental Management Project’s relevance. The Project is advancing toward
  achievement of its development objectives, by improving protected area management, strengthening
  environmental and social (E&S) monitoring for investment projects, enhancing transparency in the mining sector,
  and increasing local content and boosting local content in mining supply chains, from international mining
  companies subcontracts.
  While key activities are underway, project implementation should be accelerated to ensure the sustainability of
  project achievements.
