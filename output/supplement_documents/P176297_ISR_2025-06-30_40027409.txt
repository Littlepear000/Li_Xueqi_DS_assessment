4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  Since January 2023, PROCIENCIA and CONCYTEC have been busy preparing and implementing project activities. They

  have designed and launched grant instruments and developed Terms of Reference and technical specifications for services,

  most of which are now awarded and underway. Key progress includes:


 •Ph.D. Program Grants: Grants have been awarded, and eight alliances are being developed, with seven focusing on

 climate research.

 •Ph.D. Scholarships: 304 students have been selected, including 266 Ph.D. students with project-funded scholarships in

 climate-related programs. Among them, 137 are female, making up 45% of the group.

 •Lab Strengthening Grants: 36 research labs have been equipped and strengthened.

 •Lab Accreditation Grants: Nine labs have been chosen and are in the implementation phase.

 •Academic Entrepreneurship Grants: 16 of 23 positions were filled in the first phase. A second call is underway, with

 results expected by August 5, 2025, to fill the remaining seven positions.

 •Industry-Academia Contract Research Grants: Nine grants were issued in the first round, with a goal of reaching

 ninety. A second round will be launched to complete the remaining 81 grants, with an 18-month execution period.

 •National STI Evaluation System: The first national survey on public perception of STI was completed and presented on

 December 12, 2024. A survey on scientific equipment in research centers is currently being conducted.

 •Technology Transfer Capacity Building: Services have been launched and are under evaluation, with firm selection

 expected by November. Five Offices of Technology Transfer will be selected by December, and consultancy is set to start in

 January 2026.
