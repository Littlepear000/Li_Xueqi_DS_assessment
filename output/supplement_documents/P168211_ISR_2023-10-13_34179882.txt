 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The Program made good progress over the reporting period. Key areas of the progress include: (i) effectiveness of the Additional Financing (US$
  80 million) to Power Utility Financial Recovery Program (on May 4th, 2023); (ii) restructuring of the Group 2 Subsidiary Agreements between MOF
  and BT to convert a total debt of around TJS 8.5 billion (approximately US$800million) of BT into its equity; (iii) strengthening of the sector
  governance including establishment of an escrow account mechanism to manage cash flows in the sector, establishment of supervisory boards
  (SBs) and specialized committees (audit and compensation committees) for each entity and disclosure of the audited financial statements for 2021
  (consolidated covering BT, SIB and STB). Moreover, the revised Electricity Tariff Paper, which provide the trajectory in average tariffs increase to
  ensure cost recovery in the sector, has been prepared and is at the final stage of development and approval.
