 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The project was approved by the World Bank Board of Directors on June 29, 2020 and by Ghana's Parliament on July 30, 2020. The
  Financing Agreement was signed by the World Bank on August 17, 2020 and countersigned by the Government of Ghana on August 26, 2020.
  Effectiveness conditions comprise the preparation of the Project Implementation Manual and the establishment of the project's Coordinating Unit in
  the Ministry of Finance.
