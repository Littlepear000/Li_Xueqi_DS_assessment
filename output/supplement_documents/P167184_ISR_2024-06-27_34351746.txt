4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  The Project became effective in December 2020. Despite challenges over the last few years, the Government of Papua
  New Guinea remains committed to achieving the Project Development Objectives and the National Department of Health
  (NDOH) sees the Project as essential towards achieving the national health plan. The Restructuring of the Project was
  completed on December 12, 2023 to implement changes required because of these delays and to streamline
  implementation, particularly for the verification of results.

  Since that time, positive progress has been made with three new Expansion provinces being selected including Central,
  East Sepik and Chimbu in addition to the Early Adopter Province, East New Britain. A total of US$4.14 million (14 percent)
  has been disbursed year to date (US$3.23 million disbursed this financial year) showing progress in the pace of
  implementation. A large part of the disbursements (US$1.975 million equivalent) is from the Year 1 and Year 2
  disbursement linked indicators (DLIs), which are reimbursement to government against the achievement of key targets.
  Year 3 DLI concludes on June 30, 2024 and it is expected that 9 out of the 11 DLIs will be achieved once verified. In
  addition, 9 key priority activities were identified to support capacity development of health facilities and Provincial Health
  Authorities, equivalent to 50% of the project envelop (US$15.39 million) and is expected to expedite progress towards
  the Project Development Objective. This includes planned health facility audits in East Sepik, Central and Chimbu and
  investments to support the equipment delivery to all 159 health facilities (community health posts (level two), health
  centers (level 3) and district hospitals (level four)) in the four project provinces and rehabilitation of up to 16 health
  facilities brought up to national health service standards. Given the positive progress, the Project is now back on track
  and is being upgraded to Moderately Satisfactory.

  The next technical mission is scheduled for August/September and the Mid-Term Review expected in November this year.
