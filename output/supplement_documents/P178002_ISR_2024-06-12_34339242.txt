1. OBJECTIVE


 1.1 Development Objective
   Original Development Objective (Approved as part of Approval package on 15-Dec-2022)
   The PDO is to improve the capacity of the Lao PDR Statistical System to produce and disseminate good quality statistics in
   a timely manner and to enhance use of key statistics, and in case of an Eligible Crisis or Emergency, respond promptly and
   effectively to it.

   Has the Development Objective been changed since Board Approval of the Project Objective?

   No

4. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  The second implementation support mission took place from March 28 to April 11, 2024, with the participation of
  officials from the Lao Statistics Bureau (LSB), Ministry of Planning and Investment, and 11 relevant ministries and
  agencies. The mission noted good progress in project management and implementation. The implementation of the Lao
  Expenditure and Consumption Survey, as well as the Population and Housing Census, has advanced. LSB has committed
  to expediting activities related to GDP rebasing and data dissemination to ensure that the project remains on track to
  achieve its Development Objectives. LSB shall complete the recruitment of a national procurement specialist and an
  accountant to strengthen project management by September 2024. A technical mission was conducted during May 27-
  29, 2024, to further advance the data integration agenda, with participation from the Ministries of Finance, Industry and
  Commerce, and Planning and Investment.
