 Project Development Objectives

  Project Development Objective (from Project Appraisal Document)
  The development objectives of this project are to strengthen participatory local governance, improve the quality of local infrastructure, both in
  targeted communities, and increase extracurricular or livelihood opportunities for youth.

 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  Since the last mission in October 2022, SERSP has advanced the implementation of all subcomponents. The project's Training and Facilitating
  Partners (TFPs) have completed the social mobilization process for all 44 participating jamoats. Local communities have expressed high levels of
  satisfaction with the project’s decision-making processes and outcomes. The social mobilization process resulted in the prioritization of 193 SPs. Of
  the 93 SPs from Batch 1 jamoats, NSIFT has completed 17 and will finish most of the remaining SPs by December 31, 2023. NSIFT will start the
  construction or rehabilitation for the 100 SPs in Batch 2 jamoats by September 15, 2023.
  Since October 2022, the IP for Subcomponent 2a (youth-inclusive services) has delivered trainings on: (i) Upshift (social entrepreneurship) and
  Mind Masters (critical thinking) to 3,299 youth (target of 10,000); (ii) healthy lifestyles to 1,081 youth (target of 2,800); and (iii) digital literacy to 1,671
  youth (target of 2,800 for each module). The project is preparing to launch the delivery of psychosocial support services. Since December 2022, the
  IP for Subcomponent 2b (youth livelihoods) has launched the delivery of outreach and entrepreneurship and business proposal development
