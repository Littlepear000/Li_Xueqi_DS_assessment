 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The ISR summarizes the discussions from a virtual mission that took place from the 21st to 25th of June 2021 in order to review and update the
  status of implementation of component activities and challenges, lessons learned and pending actions. Since the last virtual mission held from
  December 16 to 18 (and the previous ISR in January 2021), the project has achieved the second disbursement-linked indicator under the
  component 1 linked to the FNDL transfer (and was associated to US$ 4 million), and made progress under the component 2 (various technical
  assistance activities including citizen engagement and capacity building of local and national stakeholders), despite challenges especially with field-
  based activities due to COVID-19. The team followed-up on action plans to recuperate any delays in progress and implementation caused by the
  COVID-19.
