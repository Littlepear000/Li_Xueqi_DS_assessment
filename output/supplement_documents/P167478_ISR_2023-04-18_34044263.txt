 Project Development Objectives

  Project Development Objective (from Project Appraisal Document)
  The Project Development Objectives (PDO) are to improve access to, and the quality of early childhood and basic education in the Project Areas
  and to strengthen the capacity of the education system to deliver better results.

 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The Project was approved by the World Bank Executive Directors on July 15, 2019. It became effective on July 15, 2020. The military coup of
  September 5, 2021 led to suspension of the constitution and public institutions. As a result, the World Bank’s Operation Policy OP. 7.30 “Dealing
  with de Facto government” was triggered on September 9, 2021 and all of the World Bank’s operations in Guinea were put on hold.

  A new Government was fully formed on November 2021, with a new Ministry of Pre-University Education and Literacy (Ministère de l'Enseignement
  Pre-Universitaire et de l'Alphabétisation - MEPU-A). The World Bank approved the resumption of the disbursement on March 9, 2022. The Project
  Coordination Unit (PCU) is fully staffed and operational, i.e. the project coordinator and all key specialists are in place.

  To date most Project activities have been prepared and key activities have been launched.

  Component 1: Improving equitable access to pre-school education. Given the increase in construction costs, the number of pre-school
  classrooms to be built has been revised downwards to 765 public primary schools form the original target of 1,000. For the development of the pre-
  primary school: (i) The convention with UNICEF signed to provide technical assistance to the MEPU-A for the elaboration of the pre-school
  policy, the development of costed and time-bound strategic and implementation plans; (ii) the bids for the construction of one pre-school classroom
  in 765 public primary schools have been advertised; and (iii) four NGOs have been recruited to sensitize and reinforce the communities' capacity for
  the supervision of all the activities at the local level.

  Component 2: Improving pre-school and basic education quality using technological innovation. A consulting firm has been recruited to
  provide technical assistance to the MEPU-A for the development and implementation of a policy for in-service teachers' training using new
  technologies. Also, the bid to purchase about 19,560 tablets was advertised on January 30, 2023.

  Component 3: Strengthening education sector management capacity. A consultant has been recruited to support the MEPU-A on the
  recruitment reform, which transfers responsibility for teacher recruitment from the central to the prefectural and regional levels. The DLR 3.1
  regarding the dissemination of the decree for the recruitment of teachers at the decentralized level (Decree No A/2019/MFPREMA/MENA.MB dated
  May 03, 2019) at all educational levels has been achieved on October 2022. A consultant has been recruited to develop the model for the
  operationalization of a non-salary recurrent budget to public primary schools. A consulting firm has been recruited to provide technical assistance to
  reinforce the MPEU-A capacity in designing and implementing early student learning assessment. The contract with UNESCO is signed to provide
  technical assistance to the MEPU-A to strengthen its EMIS.

  The Independent Verification Agency (IVA) for the verification of the Disbursement-Linked Indicators (DLIs) has been recruited and the first
  verification has been realized for the DLI 3 on the Recruitment of teachers is decentralized at prefectural and regional levels and the associated
  DLR 3.1 achieved in 2022 (i.e. dissemination of the decree for the teacher's recruitment at the decentralized level at all educational levels). The
  external auditor has also been recruited.
