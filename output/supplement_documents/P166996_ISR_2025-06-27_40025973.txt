4. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  The project has good implementation progress across all components and it is beginning to report on PDO level
  indicators, which are on-track. The progress includes successfully completing two sets of Disbursement Linked
  Indicators/Disbursement Linked Results related to the training and certification of apprentices and two sets of
  accreditation of providers in the provision of competency-based training, the development and approval of competency-
  based training packages in priority trades. The project is in the process of certifying the achievements of these DLIs.
  The CTVET through Ghana Skills Development Fund (GSDF) has administered a total of 528 competitive grants across two
  rounds of call for proposals. Across both rounds, 60 percent of funds have been disbursed. CTVET has successfully
  achieved DLIs for Year 3 and Year 4 related to the Skills Development Fund operationalization.
  All 16 PECs are now fully completed and have the GLMIS operationalized there.
  The project team is working to maintain the overall quality of project outputs and results, notwithstanding, the financial
  cap applied to all projects in the country, which have affected project disbursements and expected flow of project
  activities. A full implementation support mission is expected in August 2025, during which decisions of financial
  allocations for the various components will be clarified.
