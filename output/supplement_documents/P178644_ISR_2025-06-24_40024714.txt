4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  Implementation status is as follows:
  Component 1: Resilient Urban and Community Infrastructure (IDA US$12.0 million equivalent). This component
  finances urban upgrading investments, revitalization of historic centers, and revitalization of waterfronts in selected
  municipalities, incorporating climate-resilient features and community participation. The first three projects (Achada
  Igreja, Mosteiros, and Tarrafal de Sao Nicolau) will be tendered in the second semester of 2025.
  Component 2: Enhancing Transport Connectivity and Resilience (US$21.0 million equivalent). Six road upgrade works
  packages for approximately 51 kms are under implementation with one additional contract for 6 km signed in April.
  Component 3: Technical Assistance (US$5.3 million equivalent). Several activities are on-going, either at procurement
  stage or in implementation: Housing Information System (SIH), Territorial plans “Planes Detalhados" (PD), Civil Aviation
  legal and regulatory review, Aeronautical and Maritime occurrence platform, Parpublica Institutional Framework.
