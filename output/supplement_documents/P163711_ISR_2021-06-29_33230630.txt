            Tax Administration and Statistical System Modernization Project (P163711)



  After the ratification of the Project Financing Agreement by the country’s Parliament on June 30, 2020, the Project was declared effective on
  December 9, 2020. The State Tax Service (STS) under the Ministry of Economy and Finance and the National Statistical Committee (NSC) have
  made good progress in project implementation despite the Project being impacted by COVID-19 and changes in the Government. The STS and
  NSC have advanced the preparation of activities that will lay the foundations for modernization reforms in tax administration and the national
  statistical system. These include STS strategic planning and feedback from stakeholders on tax administration areas that are in need of
  improvements and the development of a legal and regulatory framework by the NSC to support the implementation of the Law on Official Statistics.
