4. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

               Sudan Health Assistance and Response to Emergencies (P504629)



  The Sudan Health Assistance and Response to Emergencies (SHARE) project aims to restore access to essential health and
  nutrition services and preserve core public health functions for vulnerable populations across Sudan. As of June 2025,
  over 3 million beneficiaries have been reached, including 1.2 million through primary health care (PHC) interventions and
  548,673 children vaccinated through mass campaigns and integrated outreach. Ten referral hospitals have received
  operational support, rehabilitation, and climate resilience upgrades. Progress has also been made in rolling out digital
  health tools to support service delivery and workforce management.
  Key achievements since the last ISR include the selection and onboarding of implementing partners, delivery of essential
  supplies to targeted facilities, and the launch of the national mental health and psychosocial support hotline. The project
  has adapted facility targeting and supply chain approaches in response to access constraints and shifting frontlines. While
  disease surveillance and supervision mechanisms have expanded, data quality and reporting remain areas for
  improvement.
