 Project Development Objectives
  Project Development Objective (from Project Appraisal Document)
  The Project Development Objective is to increase access to an essential package of health services in the Republic of South Sudan, with a
  particular focus on the former states of Upper Nile and Jonglei.

  Has the Project Development Objective been changed since Board Approval of the Project Objective?
  No

 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  The project was approved by the Board of Directors on February 27, 2019 and became effective for the IDA grant to UNICEF on March 11, 2019
  and for the IDA grant to ICRC on March 19, 2019. Activities of both organizations to support health service delivery in areas targeted by the project
  have begun and been scaled-up rapidly in the two months since the project was approved.
