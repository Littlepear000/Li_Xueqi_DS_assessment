 4. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
From mid-June 2024, the pace of implementation has significantly slowed down, which raises serious concerns. Further,
the Government project implementation team lost three out of four procurement specialists, which is also alarming.
These pose a considerable risk of delays to implementation of and decision making on ongoing contracts, processing of
new procurements, and overall project management. This is particularly relevant for the procurement of ICT goods and
services, which represent approximately 75 percent of upcoming contracts. Considering these challenges, both the
Progress toward achievement of the Project Development Objective (PDO) and Overall Implementation Progress
(IP) ratings have been downgraded from Moderately Satisfactory to Moderately Unsatisfactory.


Note: In the past the Progress toward achievement of the PDO was already rated MU between November 2023 and
June 2024, however, following the recent extension of the closing date until March 31, 2027, the rating was upgraded to
MS shortly before the above-mentioned slowdown in June 2024.
