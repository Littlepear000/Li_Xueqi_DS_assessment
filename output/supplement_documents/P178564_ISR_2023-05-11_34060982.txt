 Project Development Objectives

  Project Development Objective (from Project Appraisal Document)
  The Proposed Project Development Objective is to improve the national statistical capacity of Ecuador in the production and dissemination of timely
  and high-quality economic and sociodemographic statistics for evidence-based policymaking.

 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  This is the second ISR being filed for the Ecuador Strengthening Statistical Capacity Project. The Loan was approved by the World Bank’s Board
  on June 30, 2022, and declared effective on December 15, 2022. The project has been effective for four months. A supervision mission took place
  on March 2023, and based on the supervision, the Overall Implementation Progress is considered Satisfactory (S).
  The World Bank Team received an update from the INEC team on implementation progress. The team reported that on that date, the amount of the
  disbursement was $36.23 M (45%). The following developments have taken place:
  Component 1: activities are on track. There is a questionnaire agreed upon with the special statistical commissions. The mapping information was
  completed in June 2022 which allowed the preparation of the census fieldwork. In addition, the Data collection was completed in march 2023.

  The following activities are pending: data processing, final results and information on specialized topics and analyses, and completing the analysis
  on the operationalization of the new Personal Data law (2021)
  Component 2: The household income and expenditure survey (ENIGHUR), the new labor force survey, called the National Survey of Employment,
  Unemployment and Underemployment, the change of the base year of the producer price index (IPP) have advanced in the preparatory activities,
  such as the definition of questionnaires, sample design, training schedule, personnel selection, etc. However, the new agricultural survey has not
  been able to advance due to a lack of budget.
  Component 3: There are significant advances in the confirmation of the population registration base, missing the records of migration and
  education, which are close to being incorporated. INEC has established an efficient process for quality assessment of administrative records,
  establishing action plans for their improvement, and exception processes with DINARP, to access microdata.
