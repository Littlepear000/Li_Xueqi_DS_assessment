 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The project was declared effective on August 15, 2022. Funding was assigned to the project by the government starting in January 2023, as part of
  the 2023 budget law. The implementation agency (PROCIENCIA, in collaboration with CONCYTEC) has made substantial progress in preparing the
  documentation required to launch project activities. Procurement processes for many key activities planned for 2023 are expected to be launched
  within the next 3 months.
