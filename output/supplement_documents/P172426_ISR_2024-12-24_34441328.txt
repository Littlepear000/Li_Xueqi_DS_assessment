4. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  The development objectives of the project are to (i) increase the coverage of Egypt’s UHIS in Phase I Governorates, (ii)
  strengthen UHIS-related governance and institutions, and (iii) provide temporary financial protection against high out of
  pocket health expenditures for vulnerable populations outside Phase I Governorates. This ISR captures the progress made
  from the last ISR in June 2024 to date, reflecting some of the updates that happened immediately following the mission
  of November 10 - 17, 2024.

  Since the last ISR, an extension of the project closing date by 23 months to September 30, 2026, has been processed and
  approved. In addition, the Government in September 2024, formally requested a technical restructuring of the project to
  better enable it to achieve its objectives. The restructuring has been processed and approved by the Bank management,
  and an amendment letter to the Project Loan Agreement has been submitted to the Government for countersignature.
