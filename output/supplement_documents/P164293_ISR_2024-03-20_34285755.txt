 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  Overview. The 5-year Burkina-Faso Higher Education Support Project (US$70 million) was approved by the World Bank’s (WB) Board of Executive
  Directors on July 10, 2018, and became effective on December 19, 2018. It constituted the first support from the WB to the higher education
  subsector in Burkina Faso, and sought to address key constraints pertaining to access, quality, and relevance of higher education programs. The
  Project Development Objective (PDO) is to strengthen higher education institutions to increase access and deliver quality education in priority
  subject areas. Based on extensive consultations with relevant stakeholders involved in project implementation, both in central government and in
  the higher education institutions (HEIs) themselves, along with a close monitoring and review of the implementation status of the planned activities,
  the project’s overall performance is assessed as satisfactory.

  Below is a summary of the status of each component:

  Component 1. Enhancing Access to Higher Education: Establishment of the Virtual University of Burkina Faso. Component 1 has
  successfully supported key activities that have shown promising results. An integrated virtual platform (CampusFaso) has been established and is
  fully functional. The platform supports improved information and data management and carries out many administrative functions (student
  enrolment, filling administrative forms, archiving of pedagogical materials among others). The project also supports an innovative program (One
  student / One computer) which provided one computer to each of the 25,517 students via grants as of January 31, 2024. Based on its continued
  success, the program is currently underway and expected to benefit additional students with subsidized computers based on demand and
  availability of resources allocated. In addition, digital equipment and resources, including more than 4,000 e-books, were acquired for selected
  university libraries. The component has also supported the establishment of the Virtual University of Burkina Faso (VUBF) in 2018 which, to date,
  offers virtual training courses to about 7,518 students, among whom 42 percent are female students. Moreover, following the university's strategic
  plan and validated business model, coupled with the planned increased enrollment of higher education students in the VUBF, the government is
  proceeding with the construction of two open digital spaces expected to be completed by August 31, 2024. Lastly, the component has supported the
  provision of connectivity to the selected Universities. Despite significant delays encountered for the past three years, the planned connectivity of
  HEIs is presently on track with the contract awarded and signed, expected to benefit 24 HEIs with internet connection.

  Component 2: Strengthen higher education institutions and support innovations. Component 2 has supported the award of funds on a
  competitive basis to selected universities. Along the course of project implementation, the process was transformed from a competitive basis to a
  performance-based contract (PBC) modality. To date, a total of ten PBCs (amounting to US$23 million) are being implemented. The implementation
  of the performance-based contracts is strong and is expected to be completed by the project’s closing date of September 30, 2024. Moreover, this
  component supports research activities by providing scholarships to doctoral students. Presently, 300 doctoral students benefit from this
  intervention. Finally, eight internal quality assurance units (above the target of 7) are well established, and the development of the national quality
  assurance strategy and the establishment of the national higher education qualifications framework have been finalized.

  Component 3: Project coordination, management, and implementation activities. Component 3 has supported the coordination and
  management of project activities, including those related to Monitoring and Evaluation, procurement, and Financial Management, as well as
  monitoring of activities at the decentralized level. The Project Implementation Unit is composed of qualified staff who provided the necessary
  support to carry out these activities. The PIU has been proactive in building its capacity and actively engaging expert consultants to support project
  activities and evaluations as needed. The component supported the organization of the National Conference on Higher Education and Research in
  Burkina to contribute to the strengthening of higher education.
