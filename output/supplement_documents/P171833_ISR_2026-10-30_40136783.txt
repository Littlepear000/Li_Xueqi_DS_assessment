4. KEY ISSUES & STATUS
4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
The project implementation across the three participating countries and the OECS has progressed on the agreed Blue
Economy (BE) activities under components 1, 2 and 4 in the fisheries, tourism and waste sectors, noting the revision of activities through the project restructuring, now completed. Overall the component level activities are advancing at different but steady pace, except in SVG where Component 1 financed work on policies is on track but the component 2 activities need to further accelerate to particularly move infrastructure and rehabilitation works towards full execution to enable completion within the remaining project period. In Grenada, Saint Lucia and the OECS Commission, progress on key activities is being enabled by strong and functional project implementation units. For SVG there is a need to expedite the remaining pending recruitment and complete the onboarding of recent recruitments. The tourism and waste management sector activities advanced the most in this reporting period particularly in Grenada and Saint Lucia with design and supervision consultancy procurements underway for selected intervention sites, and procurements for key equipment advancing.
Overall, the regional activities under the OECS Commission are progressing well, particularly under the Matching Grants
Program targeting Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs) in the three countries with 35 blue MSMEs (focusing on fisheries, tourism and solid waste sectors) accessing financing against a final target of 75, and 165 firms have benefited from business development services against a target of 120. Women-owned or managed enterprises represent
42% of grantees against a revised target of 50 percent. Revenue gains among supported MSMEs have not yet been measured and will be reported after grant implementation.
