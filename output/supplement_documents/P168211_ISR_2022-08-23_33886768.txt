 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The Program has made some good progress with implementation of some policy actions and other operational and technical measures to improve
  the financial viability, increase the reliability of electricity supply, and strengthen corporate governance resulting in : (i) successful completion of
  unbundling process of BT into separate generation (BT), transmission (SIB) and distribution (STB) companies and establishment of Supervisory
  Boards and Audit Committees at SIB and STB; (ii) adequate purchases of electricity from and payments to Sangtuda-1 HPP; and (iii) reduction
  of power outages supported by timely repair and maintenance of the T&D assets.
  Considering the complex process of BT’s unbundling, the macroeconomic impacts of COVID-19, the government program was restructured.
  Subsequently, the World Bank Program was restructured through an Additional Financing of US$80 million (IDA grant), approved by the World
  Bank's Board of Executive Directors on June 30, 2022. The main aspects of the restructuring include (i) extension of the closing date of the project
  from August 30, 2026, to August 31, 2032 and (ii) changes in the results framework, target dates of indicators and the disbursement linked results
  (DLR) to allow sufficient time for their completion.
