 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The Crisis Resilient Social Protection program became effective on March 29, 2021. Its a 4 year, $600 million Program for Results operation with a
  $ 5 million Technical Assistance component. The program has the 3 result areas focusing on; a) Building a crisis resilient delivery system; b)
  Establishing a Hybrid Social Protection system for resilience of poor against future crisis; c) Protecting human capital through demand side
  education and health initiatives for children. The recent mission feels confident that the institution of BISP is well placed and progressing towards
  achievement of the first year results. The management also assured that some of the results might be achieved before the specified time frame
  under the PforR.
  The immediate need is for BISP to expedite the engagement of the Independent Verification Agency.
