 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The WB team and the Government are working closely to make the project effective so that implementation of activities can begin. Documents
  needed for effectiveness (effectiveness law and decree, and a legal opinion) are currently being processed by the Government and should be
  formally submitted to the World Bank once completed. The effectiveness deadline is November 18, 2021. In terms of preparation for
  implementation, the World Bank team is engaging with the Government and working closely with the Project Implementation Unit (PIU) to prepare
  operational documents needed for the launch of the project, including detailed implementation and annual work plans for 2022. A preparatory or
  launch mission (if effectiveness is reached) is expected to take place in November, 2021.
