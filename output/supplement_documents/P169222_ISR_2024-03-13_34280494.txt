 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  Overall, project implementation continues to accelerate after its restructuring. The rollout of the girls' and boys' clubs under the contract with the
  United Nations Population Fund (UNFPA) has exceeded expectations, the training of preschool teachers is on track to meet its targets, and the
  rehabilitation of water, sanitation, and hygiene (WASH) facilities in schools has now started. The national learning assessments have been
  conducted, and the results from the participation in the Program to Assess Education Systems of the CONFEMEN (Programme d'analyse des
  systemes educatifs de la CONFEMEN, PASEC) in 2022 have been disseminated throughout the country and have been made available on the
  Ministry's website. Preparations are now well underway for the next round of PASEC in 2025.
