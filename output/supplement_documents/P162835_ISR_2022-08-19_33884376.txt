 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  Despite the impact of the COVID-19 pandemic, and the recent adverse global conditions, the Catalyzing Entrepreneurship for Job Creation Project
  continues to perform well to meet its development objective. The project continues to support key priorities of the Egyptian authorities through
  offering of a comprehensive package of financial and non-financial support to MSMEs and start-ups. Despite challenges over the past two years,
  the project disbursed 77% of the total loan amount.
  The results of the project have been driven for the most part by the rapid implementation of Component 1. The Fund-of-Funds, under Component
  2, have made investments in four funds to-date and has a strong pipeline. MSMEDA has also initiated new partnerships with early-stage finance
  ecosystem stakeholders in the country, and strengthened its own internal institutional capacity for the fund-of-funds model, under Component 3.
