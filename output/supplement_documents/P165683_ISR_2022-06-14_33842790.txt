 Project Development Objectives

  Project Development Objective (from Project Appraisal Document)
  To (i) strengthen capacity for integrated water resources management in the State; (ii) improve reliability of water services in the Agreste and
  Borborema regions; and (iii) improve the operational efficiency of water and wastewater services in the Joao Pessoa Metropolitan Region.

 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  After 16 months of implementation the Borrower, through the Project Management Unit at the State Secretariat for Infrastructure, Water Resources
  and Environment (SEIRHMA), the Project Implementation Unit at the State Company of Water and Sanitation (Companhia de Água e Esgotos da
  Paraíba, CAGEPA), and a coordination team at the State Water Resources Management Executive Agency (AESA), is already implementing 12 out
  of 39 Project's activities. Nine activities currently undergoing procurement represent the bulk of loan proceeds (76%), which means that upon
  successful completion of the procurement processes most of the Project resources should be committed.
  The client and the Bank team agree the PDO remains achievable within the closing date (June 30, 2026) as long as major contracts related to the
  Transparaíba System, the rehabilitation and expansion of the sewerage system in João Pessoa, and the NRW project to reduce physical losses for
  the metropolitan area start their implementation in the forthcoming months..

  The Bank Team will continue to support the Borrower with various experts, including procurement, wastewater treatment, water resources
  management, and non-revenue water specialists. Fortnightly monitoring and implementation support meetings have been scheduled for the most
  critical activities. The counterpart activities are progressing well: works at the Curimatau Branch and one pumping station of the sewerage system in
  Joao Pessoa (Usina 1) are underway. Dam safety activities will be followed-up by the now operational Panel of Experts.

 Overall Comments PDO

  The client (CAGEPA) is already reporting progress in the indicator "Non-revenue water reduced in the João Pessoa Metropolitan Region" as a
  result of counterpart activities related to the control of commercial losses. For year 2021 it reported a reduction of 416,433 m3 as a result of
  installation of new household water meters. It has also signed a performance based contract with the aim to attain a recovery of 4.186.109
  m3/year.

  Hydromet stations: as of May 30, 2022 the end target date has been revised to be reached by June 2023.
