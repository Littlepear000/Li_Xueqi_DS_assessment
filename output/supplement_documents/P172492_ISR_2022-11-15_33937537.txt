 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The project became effective on January 25, 2022 and is still in too early a stage for meaningful progress. A recent Bank implementation support
  mission took place between October 3-11, 2022 which provided the teams with an opportunity to assess the progress of key activities and discuss
  potential implementation challenges. In addition, key activities for AWP 2023 were identified. The following action items have been identified and
  agreed upon during the mission:
            The AWP 2023 should be finalized and approved by the Project’s Steering Committee by November 30, 2022. This will require the
             organization of several technical sessions and follow up meetings between the Bank team and the GOSL to ensure that activities remain
             on track.
            A monitoring and evaluation plan will be developed in order to support close monitoring of the project results across the different project
             components.
