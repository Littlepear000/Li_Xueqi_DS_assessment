    I. PROJECT STATUS AND RATIONALE FOR RESTRUCTURING

       1. The Kyrgyz Republic Tax Administration and Statistical System Modernization Project (TASSMP) was approved by the World
       Bank Board of Executive Directors on February 6, 2020, for an International Development Association (IDA) grant of SDR12.7
       million (US$17.5 million equivalent) and an IDA credit of SDR12.7 million (US$17.5 million equivalent). TASSMP became effective
       on December 9, 2020. Its objective is to improve effectiveness of tax collection and the national statistical system. TASSMP has
       five components: (i) State Tax Service (STS) institutional development; (ii) STS operational development; (iii) National Statistical
       Committee (NSC) institutional development; (iv) data development; and (v) project management. The project closing date is
       September 30, 2025.

A. Project Status

       2. Overall, project implementation and progress towards achievement of the project development objective (PDO) are
       moderately satisfactory. The project is generally on track to achieve its development objective by the current closing date,
       especially in the tax area. However, there is a need to revise the results framework to better present the results chain and
       recalibrate end-targets under the statistics component. As of May 28, 2023, the total disbursement is US$6.05 million (17.18
       percent of IDA financing) and contracted amounts of US$12.53 million (35.8 percent of IDA financing).

       3. The project remains highly relevant for the realization of the Kyrgyz Republic National Development Strategy for 2018-2040
       (Vision 2040), which identifies reforms of the tax administration and national statistical system as priority areas and emphasizes
       the need for timely and reliable data in advancing the country’s economic wellbeing, social welfare, governance and sustainability.
       To achieve the objective of Vision 2040 of reducing the share of the shadow economy and improving domestic resource
       mobilization to finance social and economic priorities, the Medium-Term Development Program “Unity, Trust, Creation” for 2018-
       2022 (MTDP) of the government of the Kyrgyz Republic (GOKR) and, more recently, the National Development Program 20261
       seek to comprehensively reform the country’s tax administration. In line with the Vision 2040 and the MTDP, the GOKR also
       endorsed the Strategy for STS Modernization for 2023-2025. The strategy implements reforms that improve STS effectiveness and
       efficiency and reduce the compliance burden for taxpayers. At the same time, reliable statistical data continue being key for
       evidence-based policymaking and digitalization, which remain at the core of Vision 2040 and the NSC’s Development Strategy
       2020-2024. The TASSMP remains aligned with the World Bank Group’s Country Partnership Framework (CPF) for 2019-2022 which
       seeks to support the Kyrgyz Republic in strengthening foundations for inclusive, private sector-led growth.2

       4. Implementation of the tax components 1 and 2 of the project has progressed at a steady pace. There has been good progress
       on procurement of information and communication technology (ICT) equipment (computers, printers, and scanners delivered) as
       well as consultancies. The strategic performance management consultancy is nearing completion, the change management
       consultancy is ready to be launched, and the business process reengineering and human resource management consultancies
       have started. The results of these activities will inform the future Information System for Tax Administration of the Kyrgyz Republic
       (ISNAK-2). The procurement for ISNAK-2 is planned to be launched towards the end of 2023.

       5. Implementation of the statistical components 3 and 4 has advanced at a moderate pace. The Twinning Partnership (TP)
       Consortium was contracted to provide consulting services across a broad spectrum of tasks under these components. The
       inception phase for the TP activity was completed and expert missions assumed their work according to the work plan in April
       2023. A consultancy to develop tender documentation for the hardware and software was contracted in March 2023.
       Procurement of other activities that are key for implementation of the statistical components is underway, including procurement
       of a distance learning system for the NSC’s Institute of Statistical Research and Professional Development, office, and computer
       equipment for the NSC staff at the local level. Review of the legal framework for the national statistical system (NSS) was
       completed by a local legal expert and the follow up work is ongoing to advance the improvement of legal and institutional

    6. The implementing agencies (STS and NSC), with support of the respective Project Management Units, ensure compliance with

B. Rationale for Restructuring

    7. This restructuring seeks to revise the results framework to (i) replace a PDO-level indicator and an intermediate results
    indicator (IRI) that were measured using data of the discontinued Doing Business (DB) report; (ii) avoid duplication and streamline
    the measurement of project results through cancelation, addition, and modification of some indicators under components 1 and
    2; and (iii) reduce end targets to more realistic expected levels of achievement by the project within the remaining implementation
    period, for two IRIs related to the statistics components 3 and 4. There will be no revisions to the project development objective,
    closing date, component activities and costs. The request for restructuring was issued on May 11, 2023, by the Ministry of Finance
    of the Kyrgyz Republic. This is the first restructuring for the project.

 II. DESCRIPTION OF PROPOSED CHANGES

    8. The discontinuation of DB requires the revision of a PDO-level indicator and an IRI that were measured by data previously
    generated by DB. The PDO-level indicator “time required to comply with taxes” will be replaced by “cost of tax compliance,” which
    will be measured by a regular tax compliance cost survey. The DB IRI “payments required” will be cancelled and not replaced, as
    several remaining IRIs will continue measuring STS operational development.

    9. The further cancelation of one PDO-level indicator and seven IRIs, the addition of two IRIs, and the modification of two IRIs
    is warranted to avoid duplication and streamline the measurement of project results under components 1 and 2:

    (a)      PDO-level indicator “increased additional net taxes assessed per field audit” will be deleted due to an irrelevant basis of
             measurement. The dramatic increase of the indicator’s value by 6.5 times in 2022 compared to 2021 and by 7.3 times
             compared to the end of project target is attributed to limitations on field audits during the coronavirus disease (COVID)
             pandemic and double-digit inflation over the last several years. The remaining PDO-level indicators related to tax
             adequately measure the PDO dimension of improving effectiveness of tax collection.

    (b)      Seven IRIs will be canceled:

             (i)      The IRI “staff trained in strategic management, planning techniques, accountability system, quality assurance
                      and control, and internal control systems” will be replaced by a new one to streamline the results chain (see
                      paragraph 10).

             (ii)     Similarly, the IRI “increased hours in training on field audit techniques on average per auditor per year” will be
                      replaced.

             (iii)    IRI “taxpayers reporting that the experience of taxpayer services: i) STS website; ii) STS call center, has
                      improved” will be deleted, because its aspects are sufficiently covered by the modified IRI “improved
                      satisfaction of taxpayers with the State Tax Service.”

             (iv)     The IRI “payments required” will be dropped because it relied on data generated by DB (see paragraph 8).

             (v)      The data for the IRI “Improved on-time filing rate for value added tax” (VAT) is already collected for the IRI “risk-
                      based VAT refund claims automation,” which makes this indicator unnecessary.

         (vi)     The IRI “improved case management process for enforcement and tax collections” measures aspects outside of
                  the scope of TASSMP since the automated case management system will be deployed as part of ISNAK-2.

         (vii)    The IRI “taxpayer segment-focused measures in the STS Annual Business Plan and Plan of Measures to Increase
                  State Tax Revenue” will be deleted, because these plans do not have measures by taxpayer segment.

(c)      Two IRIs will be added:

         (i)      The IRI “number of staff trained under the project (disaggregated by gender)” will be added to capture the
                  training aspects previously measured by two separate IRIs (points i and ii above).

         (ii)     The IRI “strategy for STS is developed based on the new methodology, approved, and under implementation”
                  will be added to reflect policy level developments and respective government commitments.

(d)      Three IRIs will be revised:

         (i)      The wording of IRI “taxpayers reporting satisfaction with their interaction with the tax administration” will be
                  revised to “improved satisfaction of taxpayers with the STS” and be measured by the NSC satisfaction survey.

         (ii)     The wording of IRI “net taxes assessed per desk audit” will be clarified by adding “in constant 2023 prices” to
                  address inflation.

         (iii)    The IRI “improved process for compliance risk management” will be revised to “improved process for
                  compliance risk management (compliance management strategy approved and under implementation and
                  compliance management board established)” to streamline the results chain by adding the strategy here instead
                  of measuring it through a separate IRI.

10. The revision of the end- targets for two IRIs under the statistics components is required to increase realism:

(a)      Given the remaining timeline for implementation of the project and the average timeframe for review and approval of
         legal and regulatory acts, the end target of the IRI measuring the number of “decrees issued by the government to
         enforce legal and regulatory reforms on statistics” will be revised from “at least 15” to “at least 10 decrees” issued by
         the Kyrgyz government. The list of decrees will cover, among other legislative acts, the amendments to the following
         four laws critical for efficient and sustainable implementation of reforms supported under the project: (i) Law on Local
         Municipal Administration and Local Self-Government (LSG) Bodies; (ii) Law on Access to Information under Jurisdiction
         of State Bodies and LSG Bodies; (iii) Law on Personal Information; and (iv) Law on Official Statistics.

(b)      Based on a detailed analysis of the dimensions of the World Bank’s Statistical Performance Indicator (SPI), the end target
         will be reduced from “improvement by 10 points” to “improvement by 5 points.” Since activities under the statistics
         components were delayed, most of the outcomes and SPI improvements will be captured in the SPI after the closing
         date. The reduction therefore accounts for outcomes that the project will be able to impact during its lifespan.
