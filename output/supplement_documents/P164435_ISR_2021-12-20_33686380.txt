 Project Development Objectives

  Project Development Objective (from Project Appraisal Document)

  Burundi.

 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The Project was declared effective by the International Development Association (IDA) on September 3, 2020 and is now under
  implementation. Recruitment of key staff of the project implementation units is ongoing. Word Food Program (WFP) was recruited in September
  2021 to supply canteen stoves for 400 schools. The preparation of the other activities are ongoing.
