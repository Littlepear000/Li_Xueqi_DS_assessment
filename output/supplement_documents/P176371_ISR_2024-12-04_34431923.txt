1. PROJECT DEVELOPMENT OBJECTIVE


 1.1 Development Objective
   Original Development Objective (Approved as part of Approval package on 13-May-2022)
   To strengthen the regional harmonization, dissemination and use of core economic and social statistics for Kenya,
   Rwanda and Tanzania.

   Current Development Objective (Approved as part of Additional Financing package Seq. 2 on 02-Dec-2024)
   To strengthen the regional harmonization, dissemination, and use of core economic and social statistics in participating
   countries.

3. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


 3.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

   Despite facing significant implementation challenges, progress towards the DPO remains satisfactory. During the last
   mission (Sep-Oct 2024), the team observed that three common statistical dimensions (end target of five) have already
   been harmonized across participating countries and made publicly available, with strong progress on the other
   dimensions. The Statistical Performance Indicator, another PDO indicator, has increased for all three countries that have
   been in the Program since the beginning (Burundi was added as a fourth participating country in July 2024). Solid

  progress towards attaining the DPO is being made despite implementation challenges, particularly in the Kenya and
  African Union Commission components Progress by component is as follows:
      • The Kenya component progressing slowly. The low rate of achievement is due to the significant budget challenges
          faced by KNBS, which precludes implementing the activities necessary to meet the DLRs.
      • The Tanzania component remains behind schedule due to the start-up delays related to the conduct of the 2022
          Population and Housing Census, but has nevertheless made up ground over the past year.
      • The Rwanda component's implementation is on track. Only three actions from the Program Action Plan (PAP) were
          marked as delayed, among them the cybersecurity audit which was due in the Program’s first year and is being
          prioritized for implementation.
      • The Burundi component was recently added as an IPF (Board approval in July 2024) and hence is at the beginning
          stages of implementation.
      • The EAC component has made progress in developing regional harmonization guidelines and compliance
          frameworks and conducting capacity-building initiatives tailored to partner states' needs. However, challenges
          remain, mainly in the following thee areas: (i) the Bill establishing the East African Statistics Bureau (EASB) has not
          yet been approved by EALA and, hence, not yet been assented to by the Heads of State.
      • The AUC component continues to experience significant delays, with a low disbursement rate of 14% and a budget
          utilization rate of 8%, two years into the project.
