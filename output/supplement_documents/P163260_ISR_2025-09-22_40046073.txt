4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  The Project became effective on January 11, 2022, and adopts a landscape approach to promote sustainable agriculture
  and integrated water resources management in the Yaque del Norte and Ozama–Isabela river basins. Its main activities
  include: (i) support for agricultural productive subprojects (Component 1); (ii) rehabilitation of dams and large-scale
  irrigation infrastructure (Component 2); (iii) improvements in sanitation plants (Component 3); and (iv) water basin
  strengthening measures (Component 4).
  A World Bank mission conducted in April 2025 confirmed progress in procurement processes and the execution of
  activities. A first project restructuring was approved to refine the scope and results framework, and a second
  restructuring—potentially including an extension of the closing date—is currently under consideration.
