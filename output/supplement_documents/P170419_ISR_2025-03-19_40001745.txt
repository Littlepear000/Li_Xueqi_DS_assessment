1. PROJECT DEVELOPMENT OBJECTIVE


 1.1 Development Objective
   Original Development Objective (Approved as part of Approval package on 15-Dec-2020)
   The PDO is to increase the economic inclusion of youth in rural areas and the marketing efficiency and environmental
   sustainability of agri-food value chains in the Program area.

3. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


 3.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
   Access to financing is the main bottleneck for rural youth entrepreneurship (RA#1). The mission noted significant
   progress in RA#1 on the support system for rural youth with the rise of the Regional Centers for Young Agricultural and
   Agri-Food Entrepreneurs (CRJEAA) and improved coordination between all institutional stakeholders. A growing number
   of young entrepreneurs have been supported from awareness raising on entrepreneurship to the preparation of business
   plans. However, access to financing is a constraint for the realization of these business plans in a large number of cases.
   There is here an opportunity for the WBG to contribute to reduce this constraint with new intervention.

   Despite some progress in the preparation of technical and environmental/social studies, the achievement of the
   objectives relating to the four wholesale markets is uncertain if the works do not started in 2025 (RA#2). The field
   mission following the filing of this ISR will specify the timetable of activities and priority actions.
