 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The MTR has been completed in March 2023 acknowledging that following a series of delays due to institutional and implementation challenges
  over the last 2.5 years and a project restructuring in June 2022, the project has shown consistent progress across all components. The MTR also
  identified that the extended delays have contributed to a misalignment between the PDO, project-financed activities and results indicators and
  therefore confirmed the need for a second restructuring. The restructuring will enable the project to fully fulfill its PDO.
  The MOHA’s minister directive on accelerating project implementation and establishment of an informal oversight mechanism reflect Government
  ownership and commitment to the project. Changes in the project implementation structure under MoHA with the introduction of the CPIU to
  coordinate all PIU’s under MoHA has led to a more effective project management.
