4. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  The project is moving in accordance with the time-bound action plan. The first component (Deposit Insurance Fund) was
  declared effective in March 2025. The amount of US$13.2 million was disbursed to capitalize the DIF. This became
  possible after the Client provided evidence for compliance with five disbursement conditions, satisfactory to the
  Association. The second component, the Portfolio Risk Sharing Facility (PRSF), has fulfilled all three withdrawal
  conditions, and the process to lift the disbursement conditions has been completed. The disbursement conditions include
  the opening of PRSF account by the MoF, the development and adoption of the PRSF Procedural Manual, satisfactory to
  the Association, signing of the Agency Agreement with one of the Guarantee Funds in the country (the Agency
  Agreement has been signed with Zamonat, that acts as a Guarantee Fund for microfinancial organizations). The
  establishment of the New Guarantee Fund (which is not a withdrawal condition) is also underway and expected to be
  formally declared shortly. The third component is also close to the implementation stage. The PIU provided evidence for
  the fulfillment of the following disbursement conditions: The Grants Manual has been developed and agreed with the
  Association, the Agency Agreement with the Export Agency has been signed, and the hiring of an international company
  experienced in the implementation of Supplier Development Programs is well underway. Once the MoF PIU signs a
  contract with the selected company, the SEDP Component will be declared effective and will start the implementation.
  The PIU is fully staffed with competent staff. The project benefits from the staff who have been involved first-hand in the
  implementation of the REDP and who will ensure the sustainability of the WB-funded projects' implementation.
