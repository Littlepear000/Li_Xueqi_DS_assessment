 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The project is making progress toward achieving the set objectives. Key activities for design services for infrastructure and Tourism Development in
  Osh and Uzgen are ongoing. Construction works to rehabilitate city infrastructure in Osh and Uzgen are progressing well and will be completed
  gradually until June 2024. 11 productive partnership proposals have already been approved by the Productive Partnership Committee, another 3
  productive partnerships are expected to be approved by October 10, 2023.
  The Small Grants Program implementation is ongoing, 206 applicants developed their business plan and submitted to the Small Grant
  Committee(SGC), and expected that the SGC will review the business proposals by October 1, 2023

    Value                            0.00                         0.00                           350.00                     5,000.00

    Date                             01-Jul-2020                  31-Mar-2023                    25-Sep-2023                31-Dec-2026
                                     This indicator measures the number of farmers (of agricultural products) who have adopted an improved
                                     agricultural technology promoted by operations supported within the productive partnership initiatives. To
                                     date, 11 productive partnerships approved, and 62 business plans from the farmers developed and
                                     submitted. The 3 PPs that are expected to complete the tendering process in September/October, are
                                     expected to adopt several improved technologies for their respective value chains. The milk VC will
                                     include improved cattle breeds that are resilient to climate and can achieve higher productivity. The apple
                                     VC will include improved seedlings, drip irrigation, and water tanks, and commercial processing of apples.
                                     The fruits and vegetables processing VC will introduce shock-freeze technology and include equipment
                                     such as refrigeration units, small tractors, etc. The VC participants will also receive improved seedlings of
                                     fruit trees and berries, drip irrigation and a water tank system.
   Comments:
                                     NB: "Agriculture" or "Agricultural" inc ludes: crops, livestock, capture fisheries, aquaculture, agroforestry,
                                     timber and non-timber forest products. Adoption refers to a change of practice or change in use of a
                                     technology that was introduced or promoted by the project. Technology includes a change in practices
                                     compared to currently used practices or technologies (seed preparation, planting time, feeding schedule,
                                     feeding ingredients, postharvest storage/ processing, etc.). If the project introduces or promotes a
                                     technology package in which the benefit depends on the application of the entire package (e.g., a
                                     combination of inputs such as a new variety and advice on agronomic practices such as soil preparation,
                                     changes in seeding time, fertilizer schedule, plant protection, etc.), this counts as one technology.
                                     Farmers are people engaged in farming of agricultural products or members of an agriculture-related
                                     business (disaggregated by men and women) targeted by the project.
