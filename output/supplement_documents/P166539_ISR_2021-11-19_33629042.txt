 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The mission noted that implementation progress continues to be moderately satisfactory. The current disbursement rate is at 8.34 percent of
  the total project budget (3 percent comes from the achievement of Disbursement-Linked Indicators (DLIs)). While this is in line with projected
  disbursements by end-FY21 at the time of project preparation, actual expenditure at end-FY21 represented less than one percent of the total project
  budget and 10.5 percent of actual disbursement at end-FY21. Nearly half of all procurable activities have been approved in the project procurement
  plan. Out of 90 procurement activities, 27 are pending implementation. The remaining 63 contracts are either completely executed (7); ongoing at
  various stages (25); or at the pre-contract procurements stages (31). The 2022 Annual Work Plan and Budget, expected by the end November
  2021, should help expand the procurement plan and increase the number of activities under implementation.
  Slow appointment of governing boards of GoG institutions. Outside of the project scope, the delayed appointment of Chief Executive Officers
  (CEOs), especially for the National Entrepreneurship and Innovation Programme (NEIP) and Boards of some TIAs, in particular the Venture Capital
  Trust Fund (VCTF), affected project activities that require Board approval. Despite the constitution of the Investment Committee, the absence of a
