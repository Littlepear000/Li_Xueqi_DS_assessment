 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
Key reforms for NRM, mining, environment and climate are firmly and consistently underway in Guinea as part of
the new national Simandou 2040 development program priority including (i) strengthen and expand the national
protected area network developing green jobs, (ii) regular and structured technical, environmental and social (E&S)
monitoring of mining operations, (iii) increasing local content in the mining sector as well as (iv) improving
transparency and stakeholder engagement in the mining sector.
The Project has to confirm implementation acceleration (currently 25% disbursement rate) supported by technical
assistance recruited to support the ongoing reforms and to address key challenges including the development of
the Simandou Iron ore mine.
Key priority actions to accelerate Project implementation has been identified as part of the mid-term review of the
project and has to be implemented in the short term especially the adjustments regarding the result framework, the
budget and implementation arrangements.
