1. OBJECTIVE


 1.1 Development Objective
   Original Development Objective (Approved as part of Approval package on 28-Jun-2023)
   The Project Development Objective (PDO) is to improve the resilience of farmers and Small and Medium Enterprises
   (SMEs) in the Lebanese agri-food sector.
   Has the Development Objective been changed since Board Approval of the Project Objective?
