 Project Development Objectives

  Project Development Objective (from Project Appraisal Document)
  The development objectives of this project are to strengthen participatory local governance, improve the quality of local infrastructure, both in
  targeted communities, and increase extracurricular or livelihood opportunities for youth.

 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  Since the last mission in June to July 2022, the project has made good progress towards implementing community-level mobilization activities and
  capacity building activities for participatory development (subcomponent 1b), beginning the implementation of subprojects (SPs) (subcomponent
  1a), and launching the delivery of youth inclusive services (subcomponent 2a).
  The implementing agency, National Social Investment Fund of Tajikistan (NSIFT), together with the Training and Facilitating Partners (TFPs),
  National Association of Dekhan Farmers and Agro-Services Consulting, completed two cycles of community mobilization activities in the first batch
  of 22 jamoats in Khatlon and Gorno-Badakhshan Autonomous Region (GBAO). The community mobilization activities resulted in the prioritization of
  93 SPs. The most commonly prioritized SPs are related to the construction or expansion of schools, the restoration of power supply systems, and
  the construction of medical centers. To date, NSIFT has completed five SPs that involved rehabilitating rural power supply systems. NSIFT plans

  In preparation for the delivery of training for youth under subcomponent 2a, NSIFT completed the construction or renovation of 17 youth facilities

  feedback and grievances received (https://sersp.nsift.tj/bfm-grm).
