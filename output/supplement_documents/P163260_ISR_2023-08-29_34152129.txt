 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The project is on track to achieve its objectives and overall implementation progress is moderately satisfactory. A third supervision mission was
  carried out on May 22-26, 2023. Since last mission, efforts were made to strengthen the Project Coordination Implementation Unit (PCIU) capacity
  through the recruitment of procurement, FM, safeguards and technical personal. Prioritized activities in the Annual Plan Operational (POA) 2023
  are in advanced stages of procurement. Revision of the safeguard instruments are expected to be finalized by October 2023. The next supervision
  mission is scheduled for mid-November 2023.
