4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  Project implementation has advanced, with the successful delivery of key activities - establishing of boys' and girls' clubs
  in secondary schools; training of preschool teachers, including the development of pedagogical guides and a new
  supervision tool; conducting 2025 PASEC round with the data being currently decoded; completion of WASH facilities
  rehabilitation in the first batch of target schools. The additional financing to the Project was approved in March 2025,
  with the extension of the closing date to December 31, 2026. The next mission will focus on fast-tracking the
  implementation of the agreed activities. Finally, the process to develop structured lesson plans and review the special
  education strategy will be relaunched, requiring closer attention and support for timely completion.
