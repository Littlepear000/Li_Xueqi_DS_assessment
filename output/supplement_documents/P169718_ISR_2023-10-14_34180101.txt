 Project Development Objectives

  Project Development Objective (from Project Appraisal Document)
  The Project Development Objective (PDO) is to increase flood resilience and access to urban infrastructure in selected neighborhoods and to
  improve urban management in Liberia.

  Has the Project Development Objective been changed since Board Approval of the Project Objective?
  No

 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The project was declared effective on February 28, 2023. Several procurement processes under Component 1 (Climate Resilient Infrastructure and
  Urban Upgrading) and Component 2 (Strengthening Integrated Resilient Urban Development Capacity) are reaching completion or are ongoing.
  The preparation of the relevant Environmental and Social instruments is commencing in parallel.

  Design Study as well as the final stage of recruiting three advisors to support urban management, GIS, and municipal financial management.
