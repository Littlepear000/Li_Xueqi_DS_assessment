 Project Development Objectives

  Project Development Objective (from Project Appraisal Document)
  The project’s development objective is to improve (i) the relevance and excellence of scientific research, and (ii) innovative entrepreneurship and
  access to finance for enterprise growth, as a way of contributing to Serbia’s growth and competitiveness.

  Relevance of research refers to the potential of research results to be commercialized on the market, thus contributing to the economy, while
  excellence refers to international recognition of the quality of the research.

  Has the Project Development Objective been changed since Board Approval of the Project Objective?
  No

 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  Implementation and disbursement are on track. The PIU continues to be effective in coordinating tasks across the responsible agencies and
  ministries, including related to procurement, financial management, technical implementation, and overall project management. Project
  stakeholders will benefit from additional technical assistance, focused on institutional capacity building of the Innovation Fund (IF), Science Fund
  (SF), Ministry of Education, Science and Technological Development (MoESTD), and select Research and Development Institutes (RDIs)
  undergoing transformation, financed by the European Union's Instrument for Pre-accession Assistance (EU IPA).
  IDEAS, the largest program of the Science Fund program was delayed, mainly due to the COVID-19 pandemic and staffing issues. Two other new
  SF programs were expected to be announced in late 2021 at the time of reporting.

  KATAPULT, the enterprise acceleration program of the Innovation Fund, was opened and applications from innovative startup companies are
  currently being accepted. Selection decisions made by a professional investment committee are expected before March 2022.
  Following external assessments done by a consulting company, detailed transformation plans (TPs) have been developed for six selected R&D
  Institutes in July 2021. Memoranda of Understanding between each individual RDI and MoESTD are expected to be signed by December 2021,
  followed by implementation. Additional 10-12 RDIs have been identified for later rounds of assessment and TP development.
  The Bank team recently commented on a new draft Law on Innovation Activity. The Law paves the way for the Innovation Fund to continue growing
  its portfolio of instruments, however, the pace of changes must allow for careful instrument design, institutional sustainability and capacity building
  to match the significant new responsibilities.
