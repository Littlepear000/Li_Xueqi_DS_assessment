4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  The project is making progress across its four components, though slower than anticipated. Consequently, the
  Implementation Progress rating was downgraded from Moderately Satisfactory to Moderately Unsatisfactory.
  Under Component 1, the Osh Region and City of Osh Development Strategies have been officially adopted, providing a
  clear framework to guide regional growth. A digital platform for managing donor-financed projects is in testing and is
  gradually improving transparency and efficiency. Laboratory and educational facility upgrades have been completed, with
  training provided to staff, while additional equipment and technical support are underway for regional agricultural
  departments and the pilot Farmer Service Center.
  Component 2 has established all 25 targeted Productive Partnerships (PPs) across diverse agricultural value chains.
  Eleven PPs are fully operational, with additional partnerships launched in apples and poultry. Trainings have reached over
  8,200 farmers, supporting capacity development and inclusion in value chains. Efforts continue to expand participation
  and accelerate procurement, addressing challenges such as approval processes, procurement delays, and external
  constraints.
  In Component 3, key tourism and urban development projects are advancing. The Sulaiman-Too Complex and Uzgen
  Historical and Archaeological Complex are progressing, with exhibition concepts being finalized and construction of
  bridges, stadiums, and riverbank improvements nearing completion. Public-private partnerships have already mobilized
  over US$23 million in private investment, creating nearly 300 new jobs.
  Finally, Component 4 continues to support rural enterprises and tourism through small grants. To date, 172 businesses
  have received support, generating over 1,200 new jobs. A second round of grants has added 18 more businesses,
  expected to create nearly 150 additional jobs. Most recipients are effectively using funds to grow their enterprises, while
  ongoing monitoring addresses sector-specific challenges and ensures accountability.
