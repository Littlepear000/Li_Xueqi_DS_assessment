4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  The project activities under Component 1 (Climate Resilient Infrastructure and Urban Upgrading) are delayed due to the
  underperformance of the feasibility and detailed design contract. This consultancy has been terminated and re-scoped for new
  procurement. The procurement process is well advanced, and a new firm is expected by February 2025. New investments, such
  as stormwater drainage cleaning, household water connections, stormwater drainage cleaning equipment, solar streetlighting, and
  pothole patching, have been identified by the PMU and could be procured immediately to accelerate the impact for the benefited
  population. The project is progressing with Component 2 (Strengthening Integrated Resilient Urban Development Capacity)
  through the recruitment of urban and revenue advisors for capacity building. Additionally, a major contract has been signed for
  the preparation of spatial development plans, and the consultants are now on board. Discussions are ongoing regarding support
  for Solid Waste Management (SWM) under Component 2.2. This component may support (i) the procurement of goods and
  equipment, and (ii) consultant services. However, it should be noted that civil works related to SWM will not be supported.
  There are still some implementation challenges to be addressed under Component 3 (Project Management) that require
  strengthening the capacity of the staff, particularly by filling the Social Development Specialist position.
