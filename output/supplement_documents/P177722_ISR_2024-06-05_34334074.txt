   Original Development Objective (Approved as part of Approval package on 09-Jul-2023)

   MSME integration in value chains.

4. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  The Project was declared effective on February 15, 2024. The Project Implementation Unit (PIU) at the Ministry of
  Finance is now developing Procedure Manuals for two technical components, the Partial Risk Sharing Facility (PRSF) and
  the Supplier and Export Development Program (SEDP). The PIU is also working with the Deposit Insurance Fund (DIF) to
  fulfill and lift the disbursement conditions for the Deposit Insurance Fund Component. Once the World Bank confirms the
  fulfillment of the disbursement conditions and completes the procedure of their lifting, the PIU will start implementing
  the DIF, PRSF, and SEDP components expected by the end of 2024.
