4. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The implementation of initially agreed key activities went off track in February 2025, as project priorities shifted. Changes
  in the census implementation modality led the PIU to suspend the procurement of tablets. Additionally, the PIU had to
  revisit the needs assessment and technical specifications for the data center in light of Presidential Decree No. 162 (April
  19, 2024), which mandates the relocation of all government functions to New Toshkent City within two years. As a result,
  project disbursements remained low—standing at just 3% as of May 2025—and the project has remained in problem
  status since August 23, 2023.

  During this period, the government restructured the Statistics Agency under Presidential Decree No. 75 (February 24,
  2025) and Presidential Resolution No. 27, transforming it from an agency under the President’s Office into the
  autonomous National Statistics Committee (NSC) of Uzbekistan. While this reorganization delayed key procurement
  decisions in February and March 2025, it also brought renewed focus and momentum for reform. A clear agenda for
  statistical modernization and transformation was laid out in the aforementioned decree and resolution.

  These developments necessitated a project restructuring in June 2025, which has been agreed upon with the
  government, and take it out of problem status. The restructuring reflects a shift in the census implementation
  approach—now an integrated population, housing, and agriculture census—and redirects project support from tablet
  procurement to operational expenditures. However, both the timing (expected between late 2025 and early 2026) and
  the implementation modality must still be formally codified in a Presidential Decree, which remains pending.

  Furthermore, the recent restructuring was limited to adjustments within sub-components. A second restructuring is
  required to reallocate the budget across components in order to fully align the project with the government’s statistical
  modernization and institutional transformation agenda. This will require (i) an update to Presidential Resolution No. 113
  to reflect revised component budget allocations, and (ii) submission of a second restructuring proposal and formal
  request from the government to the World Bank.

  Key actions required from the government are:
     1. Finalize and approve the Presidential Decree on the implementation of the census.
     2. Present a comprehensive restructuring proposal, including reallocation of funds across components.
     3. Update Presidential Resolution No. 113 to reflect revised component budgets in line with the restructuring
          proposal.
