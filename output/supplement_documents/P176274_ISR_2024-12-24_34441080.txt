4. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  The overall implementation progress is rated as moderately unsatisfactory. Although progress has been made since the
  last mission, particularly in the launch and execution of certain key activities and attainment of first intermediary results
  indicators, this progress has not yet translated into a significant increase in the effective disbursement rate. Initial delays
  have been mitigated, and the project is now delivering tangible results:
       • Reform plans: Three reform plans have been adopted, supported by partnership agreements and initial validated
          activities. These include:
               1. CMCAA: Relaunch of Public-Private Dialogue (DPP), including validation of ToRs, technical and statutory
                    meetings, and a formal relaunch ceremony led by the Prime Minister.
               2. GUFE: Governance and legal framework reform through the drafting and review of an organic decree
                    revision.
               3. CNI: Development of an Investment Charter manual, validated through workshops, and initial
                    stakeholder training.
       • Support for MPMEs: A total of 184 MPMEs have received direct support through:
               1. Information and awareness sessions for women entrepreneurs (30), business leaders (89), and economic
                    operators (67).
               2. Needs assessments and identification of quick-win training opportunities to enhance capacity.
       • Disbursement: The disbursement rate stands at 10.7%, with a budget commitment of $1.8 million USD.
          Projections indicate an increase to 15% by Q1 2025 and 23% by mid-2025 following the first $2.5 million
          disbursement for the National Guarantee and Investment Fund (FNGI).

  Key Decisions:
     1. Accelerating implementation:
             1. Continue prioritizing hands-on support for the PIU and leveraging technical assistance to expedite
                 ongoing activities.
     2. Streamlining disbursement:
             1. Expedite administrative and financial processes to sustain the rapid implementation of activities.
     3. Strategic focus on results:
             1. Maintain a results-oriented approach, prioritizing quick-win activities to meet intermediary indicators.
     4. Guarantee Fund recruitment:
             1. Finalize the selection process for the firm managing the Partial Credit Guarantee Fund, enabling the
                 operationalization of the fund to enhance SME access to credit.
