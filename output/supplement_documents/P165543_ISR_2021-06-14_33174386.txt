 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The project became effective on February 19, 2020, after presidential elections at the end of 2019. The first disbursement made on June 2020, and
  as of today, $11.17 million or 3.7% of the loan has been disbursed.
  MoHA, as the Central Project Management Unit (CPMU), leads the activities related to overall project implementation, as the project’s Executing
  Agency, as well as to strengthening the capacity of the village government under Component 1. Currently, the CPMU under MoHA has established
  a Secretariat to support the project’s implementation and initiated the procurement for National Management Consultant (NMC). CPMU MoHA also
  had processed the Annual Work Plan and Budget (AWPB) 2021 which approved by the Bank on March 2021 and activated in May 2021.
  MoV’s activities under Component 2 focus on strengthening the community participation, social inclusion and accountability systems, or the demand
  side of village governance, through different instruments. The Coordinating Project Implementation Unit (CPIU) MoV has started the procurement

  Bappenas and Kemenko PMK are responsible to implement the activities under Component 4 of the project. Bappenas has established a

  (InfoDesa). Kemenko PMK is responsible for the establishment of a National Coordination Platform (NCP). The establishment of NCP is still in

  A recent Implementation Supervision Mission noted the need for a project restructuring, given several institutional changes in Component 2. It was
  discussed and agreed that this restructuring could also critically review and adjust activities where progress is lagging, and review the project scope
  and activities. A restructuring request is expected to be submitted once a review of the project activities is completed in June 2021.
