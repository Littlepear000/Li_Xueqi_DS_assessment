 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The Program has made significant progress, including: increased citizens’ access to government services through digital platforms; rollout of
  revenue and expenditure recording system to 19 urban local areas; Public Financial Management Reform Unit was notified; Medium Term Fiscal
  Framework and risk statement was published on the website of the Finance Department; . Program Operations Manual is finalized, etc. Progress is
  also noted on hiring of staff for the Public Financial Management Unit (PFMU) and Program Support Unit (PSU). Based on the implementation
  progress the previous rating of Satisfactory for achievement of PDO and Moderately Satisfactory for implementation progress remain adequate.
