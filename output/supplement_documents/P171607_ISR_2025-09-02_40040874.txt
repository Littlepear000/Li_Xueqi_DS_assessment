1. OBJECTIVE


 1.1 Development Objective
   Original Development Objective (Approved as part of Decision package on 20-Dec-2021)
   The objectives of the Project are to mitigate the effect of COVID-19 on private sector investment and employment and to
   support new economic opportunities including in refugee and hosting communities.
   Has the Development Objective been changed since Board Approval of the Project Objective?

   No

 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
The PDOs remain relevant and can still be achieved. The project has met the disbursement conditions
and has disbursed US$ 46.9 million. INVITE Trust has been duly established. The Investment Committee
and the Trust Manager have both been recruited.
The project needs to move faster on Export Firm Support (EFS) and RHD (Refugee Hosting District)
components.
