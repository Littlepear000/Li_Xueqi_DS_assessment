 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  Progress in implementation of project components
  Component 1: Empowering Girls through the Acquisition of Life Skills and the Promotion of a Safe School Environment (IDA US$5.15
  million, GPE US$0.50 million equivalent). The subcomponent 1.1 will support the acquisition of life skills and strengthening SRH education
  through girls’ and boys’ clubs and community outreach strategies. The government is preparing the TOR to select an institution to design and
  support the implementation of the key activities. The pilot of the intervention will be conducted in February 2022 and the implementation of activities
  are planned to start in September 2022. The design of the instrument and the survey to measure the improvement in life skills will be conducted by
  another firm. The subcomponent 1.2 will support the provision of safe and sanitary spaces, water supply, and MHM in schools through school-

  based WASH interventions. The government team is working with the Bank WASH group to conduct a survey to assess the conditions of
  infrastructure of schools, select an engineer to strengthen the government management capacity, and start the preparation of construction
  guidelines.
  Component 2: Tackling Learning Poverty (IDA US$7.27 million, GPE US$0.70 million equivalent). This component will tackle learning poverty
  in STP by: (i) increasing school readiness in preschool; (ii) promoting effective teaching of literacy and numeracy in grades 1–6; (iii) improving the
  country’s teacher management system; and (iv) promoting school leadership and accountability. The government has already contracted the
  Programme d’analyse des systèmes éducatifs de la CONFEMEN (PASEC) that will be conducted in 2021 and 2024 in grades 2 and 6 and the
  National Student Learning Assessments that will be carried out annually in grades 2, 4, 6, 9 and 12 with the support of the Centro de Políticas
  Públicas e Avaliação da Educação (CAED) from Brazil. CAED will also build the capacity of MEES to conduct formative assessments in all
  grades. The activities will begin implemented in February 2022.
  Component 3: Reaching the Most Vulnerable (IDA US$0.39 million, GPE US$0.75 million equivalent). The component will support targeted
  interventions to prevent students from dropping out and to promote their success in school. The government is preparing the TOR to select an
  institution to design the intervention to promote the provision of targeted support to low-performing students who are at risk of failing and dropping
  out—promoting their learning, reducing the likelihood that they will drop out, and increasing the likelihood that they will succeed in school.
  Component 4: Project Coordination, Monitoring and Evaluation, and Capacity Building (US$2.19 million, US$0.25 million equivalent). The
  MEES has recruited a Project Coordinator, a Monitoring and Evaluation specialist, a Safeguards specialist and an international Project Management
  specialist by March 2021.
