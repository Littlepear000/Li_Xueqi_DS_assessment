 3. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


 3.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
During the early part of FY 2025, the Program has helped improve digital pedagogy of teachers with training
carried out for 1,652 teachers in ICT competencies in line with the Bhutan Professional Standards for
Teachers (BPST). Additionally, there has been a reduction in the digital divide in rural areas with 93
additional schools having access to remote learning facilities with the supply of ICT equipment and internet
connectivity. These achievements have been supported under disbursement-linked indicator (DLI) 3).
