4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  The project was approved by the Board of Directors on February 11, 2020. The Financing Agreement was signed on
  March 19, 2020, and the project became effective on June 9, 2020. The first disbursements were made on April 27, 2021.
  The Mid-Term Review Mission held between May
  2 and 4, 2023, confirmed implementation progress across all components. Overall project progress remains Moderately
  Satisfactory.
