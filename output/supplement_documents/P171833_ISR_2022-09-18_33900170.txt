 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The Unleashing the Blue Economy of the Caribbean (UBEC) Project was approved by the World Bank Group's Board of Executive Directors on May
  2, 2022. The four clients, namely, Grenada, Saint Lucia, Saint Vincent and the Grenadines and the Organization of Eastern Caribbean States
  Commission are working steadily to establish the individual Project Implementation Units and commence procurement of the agreed Year 1
  activities. The five-year multi-sectoral Project is on track to begin formal implementation by December 2022 once the Effectiveness Conditions have
  been met at the end of November 2022.
  .
