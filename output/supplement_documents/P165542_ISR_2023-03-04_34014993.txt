 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The Mission is encouraged by the implementing agency’s ownership and leadership in implementation of Project activities despite the lack of
  availability of funds from the Project’s technical assistance (TA) component. Key achievements shared by the Chief Commissionerate of Afghan
  Refugees (CCAR) include:
      i.     Preparation of the Disbursement Linked Indicators (DLIs) delivery plan for December 2022 to December 2023, actively involving all
             Commissionerates: Balochistan, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Punjab and Sindh;
     ii.     Identification of DLI focal teams within ministries departments and agencies, including NADRA, Passport and Immigration, and Federal
             Investigation Agency;
    iii.     Initiation of monthly review meetings chaired by the Chief Commissioner to monitor and manage implementation of the DLI delivery plan.
             Two review meetings had been already held by the time of the Mission;
    iv.      Preparation of procurement plan;
