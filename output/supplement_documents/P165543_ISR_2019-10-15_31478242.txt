 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  The Institutional Strengthening for Improved Village Service Delivery supports the Government of Indonesia in developing and rolling out new
  technology-based systems focused on strengthening village institutional capacity and accountability to improve the quality of village spending,
  through its four components: (1) Strengthening Village Government Institutions, by developing a technology-based tools to improve institutional
  strengthening system for village government; (2) Promoting Participatory Village Development, to strengthen the participation and social
  accountability in village government system by digitalizing the oversight and management system of village facilitation, development of digital
  technology-based community learning system and knowledge exchange platform, and strengthening community capacity and accountability system;
  (3) Village Performance Awards, by developing a village performance system to rewards village government based on key performance metrics of
