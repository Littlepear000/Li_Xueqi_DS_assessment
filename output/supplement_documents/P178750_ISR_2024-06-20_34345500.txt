4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  The Senegal Higher Education Project – ESPOIR-Jeunes was approved by the World Bank’s Board (WB) of Executive
  Directors on June 29, 2023, signed on October 6, 2023, and became effective on December 21, 2023. The project
  development objective (PDO) is to increase equitable access to market-relevant short-term vocational tertiary education
  and strengthen governance and research in higher education and the project is expected to benefit over 400,000 young
  people in Senegal. It consists of a credit in the amount of US$150 million from the International Development Association
  (IDA), a US$1.9 million grant from the Early Learning Partnership multi-donor Trust Fund, and co-financing from the
  Agence Française de Développement (AFD) in the amount of US$55 million, for a total project financing envelope of
  US$206.9 million.

  The project consists of three components:

  Component 1. Diversifying and increasing access to tertiary technical and vocational education for improved
  employability of Youth. Component one seeks to improve equitable access to higher education programs by building,
  equipping, and supporting eight additional ISEPs throughout Senegal which offer market-relevant programs, as well as
  improving pedagogical approaches in each of the centers. Component 1 supports key reforms and capacity building of the
  3FPT to ensure an adaptive model based on ISEP needs, as well as facilitation of 3FPT support to the labor market
  insertion of ISEP students. As part of the activities managed by 3FPT, the training of for the Higher Diploma of
  Professional Education began with 100 vouchers was awarded. This will be followed by the launch of training for the
  professional skills certification with the enrollment of 600 learners and certificates for 148 beneficiaries.

  Component 2. Improving the Management and Governance of the Higher Education System. Component two seeks
  to promote tertiary education, entrepreneurship and research that respond to development challenges and improve the
  governance and efficiency of the higher education system through performance-based contracts with public universities.

  Component 3. Strengthening Project Management and Monitoring and Evaluation. Component three supports the
  necessary project management and monitoring and evaluation costs, as well as technical assistance.
