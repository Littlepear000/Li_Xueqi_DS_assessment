 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The project became effective on Jan 15, 2020, and although the implementation support mission post-effectiveness was canceled due to the
  COVID-19 crisis, the team scheduled all necessary meetings virtually to ensure smooth implementation. Worth noting that 16% of the project
  amount was disbursed in March 2020, after which MSMEDA, the Project Implementing Entity: (i) made significant progress in the debt component
  (component 1); (ii) is in the process of investment committee members nomination to be able to start implementing the equity component
  (component 2); (iii) finalized the procurement plan for the first 18 months of implementation relevant to the non-financial support component
  (component 3).
