 Project Development Objectives

  Project Development Objective (from Project Appraisal Document)
  The development objective of this project is to support the production of harmonized and quality statistics in the EAC, in support of the regional
  integration in the region, through capacity building of the National Statistical Offices of the East African Community (EAC) Partner States and the
  EAC Secretariat.

 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The project was approved on July 27, 2018, signed on October 10, 2018, and declared effective on December 20, 2018. The pace of project 's
  activities implementation was very low in 2019 due to long time it took to complete the recruitment of the PIU and technical staff. The recruitment of
  all the PIU and technical staff was completed in February 2020.
  Despite these delays as well as those due to the Covid-19 pandemic, the achievements made are encouraging. The following draft guidelines for
  compilation of better quality and harmonized statistics in the region are available: Consumer Price Index; External Sector Statistics; Poverty
  statistics; Monetary and Financial Statistics; Communication & Advocacy; and Government Finance Statistics. The zero draft Communication and
  Advocacy Strategy has been developed. In addition, the drafting of National accounts and Agriculture, Food and Nutrition guidelines are underway.
  The guideline for the HCPI was completed and approved by the HCPI Technical Working Group (TWG). The rest are being discussed by the
  respective TWGs. In developing the guidelines, the experts have collaborated with various stakeholders: CPI (IMF consultant peer reviewed the
