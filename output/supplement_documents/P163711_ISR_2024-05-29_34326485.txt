4. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  Both Implementing Agencies, the State Tax Service (STS) and the National Statistics Committee (NSC), have made good
  progress in implementation of all project components, including in preparation of major remaining procurements. STS
  and NSC have launched and successfully implemented several important procurement packages, and prepared other
  activities that have laid the foundations for modernization reforms in tax administration and the national statistical
  system. These refroms include STS strategic planning, change management, business process redesign, ICT equipment
  procurement, and the improvement and development of a legal and regulatory framework to support the
  implementation of the Law on Official Statistics, Twinning Partnership consulting services and procurement of software
  and hardware for the NSC. Following the recommendations of the mid-term review mission, an extension of the project
  closing date is needed to complete implementation and achieve development objectives.
