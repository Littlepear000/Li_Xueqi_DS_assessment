I. PROJECT STATUS AND RATIONALE FOR RESTRUCTURING

Summary


    1. In response to the request from the Government of Benin (GoB) dated June 27, 2024, this Restructuring Paper
       seeks a partial cancelation of IDA Credit 6453-BJ (the “Credit”), granted to the Republic of Benin under the Digital
       Rural Transformation Project (the Project, P162599).
    2. The proposed partial cancellation in the amount of EUR 4,573,472 (equivalent of XOF 3.000 billion, around US$
       5 million) of the total current Credit amount of EUR 42,903,700.
    3. This results mainly from economic gains realized from activities where actual cost were below the forecasted
       values, including the result of the competitive bidding process for rural connectivity (Component 1) with the lower
       than projected subsidies requested.
    4. This would be the fourth Level-2 Restructuring of the Project:
          i.  The First restructuring (May 18, 2020, CD Decision) was to: (i) cancel EUR 46,296,300 (equivalent to
              US$ 49.95 million, or 52 percent of the original financing amount of EUR 89,200,000) from Loan No.
              6453-BJ; (ii) adjust the disbursement categories as a result of the cancellation.
         ii.  The Second restructuring (January 12, 2021, RVP Decision due to change in PDO) was to: (i) adjust, reduce
              the scope and streamline the existing components of the project following the cancellation of 52 percent of
              the original IDA funding from the First restructuring; (ii) adjust the Project Development Objective (PDO)
              following the restructuring of the components; and (iii) adjust the Project Results Framework (RF) as a
              result of the reduced scope of the existing components.
        iii.  The Third restructuring (April 19, 2024, CD Decision) was to: (i) extend the closing date by eleven months
              (from July 31, 2024, to June 30, 2025); and (ii) adjust the Project RF to: (a) reflect current and expected
              level of achievement by the new Project's closing date, and (b) retrofit it by including the two new digital-
              related indicators from the Corporate Scorecard.

Project status

    5. Significant recent progress has been achieved by the Project. On Component 1, work to extend connectivity in
       rural areas is well underway with 112 sites out of 119 already commissioned (94.1% completion rate) and will be
       completed by July 31, 2024. The two mobile operators will provide data on the number of mobile broadband users
       and number of mobile money users by July 2024, and will launch awareness-raising campaign. On Component 2,
       the three digital e-Agriculture platforms are launched (platform to provide agriculture advisory services,
       agriculture equipment management platform, farm management platform) and are currently being scaled-up. The
       PIU needs to accelerate the launch of the contracts for digital financial inclusion.
    6. Based on the progress recorded, there is a high likelihood that the PDO will be achieved by the closing date of the
       project. In the latest Implementation Status & Results Report (ISR) of June 20, 2024 (Seq. No. 10), the rating of
       the “Progress towards achievement of PDO” was upgraded from Moderately Satisfactory (MS) to Satisfactory (S),
       whereas the rating of the “Overall Implementation Progress (IP)” was maintained at Moderately Satisfactory (MS).

  II. DESCRIPTION OF PROPOSED CHANGES

                  7. The proposed change includes the partial cancellation of EUR 4,573,472 (equivalent to XOF 3.000 billion –
                     US$ 5 million). To reflect the new amount of the Credit, the allocation between disbursement categories and
                     disbursement estimates are also adjusted (adjustments in disbursement estimates are displayed in dollars and take
                     into account the evolution of the exchange rate).
                  8. The proposed restructuring does not include any other changes. The PDO, the Results Framework, the project
                     components and activities, the implementation arrangements, and allocations across categories of expenditures
                     (other than the above-mentioned cancellation) all remain unchanged. The Project’s safeguards category also
                     remains unchanged, and the restructuring will not trigger any new safeguards policies. The overall risk rating also
                     remains unchanged.
                  9. The proposed partial cancellation is in line with the World Bank Policy and Directive on Investment Project
                     Financing as: (i) the PDO remains achievable; (ii) the Borrower's performance remains satisfactory; (iii) the
                     project is not subject to suspension of disbursements; and (v) there are no outstanding audit reports and the audits
                     reports are satisfactory to the World Bank.
