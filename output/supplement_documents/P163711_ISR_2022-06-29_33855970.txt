            Tax Administration and Statistical System Modernization Project (P163711)



  The State Tax Service (STS) under the Ministry of Finance and the National Statistical Committee (NSC) have made good progress in project
  implementation despite the Project being impacted by COVID-19 and changes in the Government. The STS and NSC have launched several
  important procurements and advanced the preparation of other activities that will lay the foundations for modernization reforms in tax administration
  and the national statistical system. These include STS strategic planning, change management, business process redesign, ICT equipment
  procurement, and feedback from stakeholders on tax administration areas that are in need of improvements as well as the improvement and
  development of a legal and regulatory framework to support the implementation of the Law on Official Statistics, Twinning Partnership consulting
  services and procurement of software and hardware for the NSC.

                                        overall efficiency gain. The new workflow management system would also programmatically compute
                                        such elapsed times for business process activities of other indicators or data products.
                                        At least 20 indicators have been identified to compute a broader or overall efficiency gain.
