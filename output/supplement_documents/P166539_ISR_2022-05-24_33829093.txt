 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  Implementation progressed since the last update. The Ghana Enterprise Agency (GEA) disbursed US$5 million in COVID19 recovery grants to
  nearly 400 Small and Medium Enterprises (SMEs), surpassing the final target for the number of SMEs receiving financial support under GETP. GEA
  is now getting ready to implement the SME Growth program, and a Technical Assistance consulting firm was recently selected as part of the
  program.
  Gaps in leadership at various implementing agencies have now been filled: a new Chief Executive Officer (CEO) was appointed at the National
  Enterprise and Innovation Program (NEIP); a new Board of Directors was inaugurated at the Venture Capital Trust Fund (VCTF); and a new Board
  was also inaugurated for the Office of Registrar of Companies (fulfilling one of the performance-based conditions for disbursement under GETP).
  Thanks to these developments, the VCTF has identified several child funds to make investments in under the Startup Catalyst Fund and Strategic
  Industries Fund set up through GETP.
