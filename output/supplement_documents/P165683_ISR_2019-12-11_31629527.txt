 Project Development Objectives
  Project Development Objective (from Project Appraisal Document)
  To (i) strengthen capacity for integrated water resources management in the State; (ii) improve reliability of water services in the Agreste and
  Borborema regions; and (iii) improve the operational efficiency of water and wastewater services in the Joao Pessoa Metropolitan Region.

  Has the Project Development Objective been changed since Board Approval of the Project Objective?
  No

 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  The Loan Agreement (LA) has not been signed yet; therefore, the Project is not yet effective. The State of Paraiba is working to comply with the
  Federal Government requirements for the continuity of the process for Senate approval and LA signing. In the meantime, the interim Project
  Management Unit is making some progress to advance technical and procurement documentation, so procurement of several Project activities can
  start immediately after LA signing. The Bank Team will continue to support the Borrower with various experts (such as procurement experts, water
  supply and sanitation expert, water resources management expert, review of designs) so that activities can be launched as soon as the LA is
  signed.
