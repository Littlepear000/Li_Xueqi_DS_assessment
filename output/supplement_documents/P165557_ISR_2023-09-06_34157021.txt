 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  Overall, GALOP is progressing well, both in terms of progress towards achieving its development objective and in terms of implementation
  progress. Key achievements of the project thus far include:
  Strengthening teaching practices in schools by:
           Developing and utilizing lesson observation tools that allow School Improvement Support Officers (SISOs) to provide coaching and
            mentoring support to teachers.
           Developing a national in-service training framework to guide all training for school heads, teachers and SISOs.
           Training teachers on the use of differentiated learning (DL) methods to support teachers to tailor lessons to the needs, strengths and
            weaknesses of students

  Strengthening school support, management and resourcing
          Disbursing learning grants to over 10,500 GALOP schools
          Training district educational officials to strengthen their capacity
          Reconstituting SMCs to ensure greater community oversight
          Initiating of activities by service providers to provide education services to out-of-school and at-risk youth through the Ghana Education
           Outcomes Fund.
  Strengthening accountability systems for learning by:
          Developing and implementing a national assessment framework (over 400,000 P4 pupils were assessed in the first round of assessment)
          Developing an accountability dashboard to support evidence-based decision making
          Developing a teacher deployment and transfer strategy which has led to an increase in the Percentage of targeted primary schools with
           pupil-trained-teacher-ratio (PTTR) below 50:1 from 75 percent in 2019 to 81 percent in 2022.
  Key areas focus moving forward
          There is a need to ensure capitation grants are released in a timely manner to allow schools to better plan and effectively utilize funds.
          There is a need to prioritize the placement of trained teachers to GALOP schools to ensure the KG level pupil-to-trained-teacher ratio is
           reduced
          The accountability dashboard needs to be operationalized, school management committees need to be trained, so that schools and
           education officials can begin using data for planning and accountability purposes
          Under the Ghana Education Outcomes Fund Project, lot 7 (which is targeted to urban areas) will be re-advertised by end August, 2023.

 Overall Comments PDO

  2 end of project PDO indicator targets have been exceeded while one is on track to being met (increased percentage of primary schools with
  pupil to trained teacher ratio (PTTR) below 50:1.) The end of-project target for the PDO indicator related to PTTRs at the KG level needs to be
  revised downwards as part of the restructuring due to the revised baseline which was significantly lower than what was stated in the project
  appraisal document. The increase in number of schools using the accountability dashboard is off-track however, this target can be achieved
  once training on the use of the dashboard is completed and schools begin utilizing the dashboard.
