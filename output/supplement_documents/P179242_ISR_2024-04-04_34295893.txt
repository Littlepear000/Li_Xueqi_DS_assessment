 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The project was approved on May 16, 2023. Financing Agreements were signed with the Republic of Maldives on June 18, 2023, and with the
  Indian Ocean Commission on June 22, 2023. Implementation activities are being initiated by the two implementing agencies - the Indian Ocean
  Commission (IOC) for Component 1 of the Project, and the Ministry of Fisheries and Ocean Resources and the Ministry of Agriculture and Animal
  Welfare (former Ministry of Fisheries, Marine Resources and Agriculture (MoFMRA)) for components 2 and 3 of the Project.
  The Project’s objectives and design remain highly relevant and aligns with the strategic direction and priorities of the new government of the
  Maldives for the fisheries sector, which include modernizing fisheries sector management, promoting processing (eg. canning) to reduce raw

  Main progress includes:
           Key staff have been mobilized for project management, fiduciary, Environmental and Social Framework (ESF), and Monitoring and
            Evaluation (M&E). Recruiting a Communication Officer and Science Coordination Consultants is in process.
           Component 1 (implemented by the Indian Ocean Committee, IOC): The main focus has been on finalizing a contract for $5.2 million
            (about half of the entire component budget). This component has progressed. It is expected that this contract could be signed by the end
            ofMarch 2024.
           Component 2: significant progress has been made in: (i) digitizing historical catch reports, conducting stakeholder consultations, redrafting
            Fisheries Management Plans, designing awareness materials, and enhancing regional engagements; ii) digital registry of fishermen and
            awareness activities for the Fisheries Information System (FIS); (iii) some refurbishing of quarantine and laboratory facilities; and (iv)
            ongoing capacity building, and preparatory works for initiation of a degree program and preparations for a mariculture training program.
           Component 3: with respect to (i) decarbonization of the fisheries sector, progress has been made in installing echo sounders and ongoing
            efforts to finalize TORs for an energy auditor; and (ii) hiring a consultant for a matching grant manual.
