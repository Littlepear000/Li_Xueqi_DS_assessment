    I. PROJECT STATUS AND RATIONALE FOR RESTRUCTURING

1. This restructuring paper seeks management approval to restructure the Strengthening Social Assistance and Labor
   Market Programs Project to (i) reallocate available savings of about US$5.5 million1 from Category 1 (Component 1) to
   Categories 6 and 8 (Component 2 and 3, accordingly); (ii) revise Subcomponent 2.3 and add new Subcomponent 2.4; (iii)
   modify the results framework by adding three new intermediate results indicators; and (iv) extend the project closing date
   by 20 months, from September 1, 2025, to April 30, 2027 (no-cost extension). This will be the first Level 2 restructuring and
   is based on formal request by the Ministry of Finance (MoF) dated February 20, 2025.
Project Status
2. The overall implementation progress is Satisfactory and the progress towards achievement of the PDO was downgraded
   to Moderately Satisfactory in January 2025 due to some delays under Subcomponent 1.2 “Improving the Effectiveness of
   the UBK Program” which implementation requires additional time beyond the current closing date (PDO Indicator (3):
   Number of families receiving UBK support through the new piloted eligibility criteria (Actual: 0, Target: 1,500). Out of 6 PDO
   indicators, 3 have reached or exceeded the targets, which are: PDO Indicator (1): Number of children receiving UBK top-up
   payments (Actual: 346,334, Target: 340,000), PDO Indicator (2): Number of families receiving increased UBK benefits on
   time (Actual: 105,626, Target: 105,000), and PDO Indicator (6): Share of MLSDM district offices that have access to central
   level Information Technology (IT) resources (Actual: 100 percent, Target: 100 percent). PDO Indicator (4): Number of
   beneficiaries of the Social Contract program with productive inclusion initiatives operating after 6 months is on track to be
   achieved by the current closing date. However, PDO Indicator (5): Number of beneficiaries of the Youth Employment
   program with a job after completing the in-class training is at risk of non-achievement by the current closing date since the
   completion of related activities under Subcomponent 2.2 “Enhancing Employability and Job Opportunities among Youth
   Population” need additional time beyond the project closure. As of March 2025, total disbursements stood at US$19,4
   million or about 65%. Of the total undisbursed balance US$5.1 million (excluding the savings of US$5.5 million), about US1.4
   million is expected for disbursement under subcomponent 1.2 (Improving the Effectiveness of the UBK Program) as the
   monthly social benefits for families participating in the UBK pilot, about US$2,56 million is expected for disbursement
   towards the Youth Employment component (training and on-the job allowances) under subcomponent 2.2 (Youth
   Employment), and around US$0.35 million under subcomponents 3.1 and 3.2.

Rationale for restructuring
11. Enhancing the project’s outcomes while efficiently and fully utilizing its budget is the priority for both the implementing
    agency and the World Bank. During the World Bank implementation support mission in June 2024 and mid-term review
    mission in December 2024, the teams discussed potential activities to support with available savings under Component 1.
    The MLSSM has identified a number of priority activities in supporting the migration agenda, advancing the employment
    agenda for people with disabilities, and facilitating digitalization of social services. A careful review of the project’s
    remaining activities, including the proposed new ones, confirms that additional 20 months would be needed to allow for
    their completion and full achievement of the project targets and development objective, while contributing to the reform
    actions under the Development Policy Operation (DPO)3 for the Kyrgyz Republic currently under preparation.
12. While the Government is committed to achieving all stated development objectives, additional time is required for the
    work related to improving the targeting methodology under Subcomponent 1.2 “Improving the Effectiveness of the UBK
    Program”. The approach used under this subcomponent, which envisages shifting from the income and asset test-based
    methodology to a more complex proxy-means test based methodology, is new in the Kyrgyz Republic. The implementation
    schedule shifted due to delays in concluding the analysis of the current methodology, with substantial field work carried
    out by a consultant company and subsequent challenges on the government counterparts’ side on the specifics of the
    proposed changes in the methodology. The World Bank stepped in to provide additional expertise and guidance to update
    the methodology, which is expected to be ready for piloting around April-May 2025. With the scheduled eight-month pilot,
    post-pilot adjustment of the methodology, and endorsement of the updated methodology through changes in the
    legislative documents, the subsequent rollout of the new methodology is expected to take place substantially beyond the
    current project closing date of September 1, 2025.4.
13. The ongoing activities under the Youth Employment Program in Subcomponent 2.2 are also expected to go slightly over
    the current closing date. The analysis of jobs in demand across all regions of the country has been completed and target
    beneficiaries for this subcomponent have been identified. To date, around 800 youth with vocational training certificates
    has already been identified for job placement. The contracting arrangements with training providers across the country are
    being finalized to launch the training activities with further subsidized employment. With the estimated employment to last
    6 months, the completion of main activities under this subcomponent is expected to happen around end-October 2025.
14. The migration agenda continues being one of the priority areas that needs enhanced support under Subcomponent 2.3.
    Current activities under the subcomponent focus on creating the Migrant Registry and carrying out communication activities
    around it. However, with a significant share of the Kyrgyz population (in 2022 about 0.9 million out of about 7 million
    population5 were abroad with most of them as labor migrants) choosing external labor migration as a source of income,
    and with a majority of migrants choosing the Russian Federation as destination, the risks associated with a single migration
    destination and the recent migration rule changes in the Russian Federation warrant an urgent need of diversifying
    migration destinations and preparing labor migrants for the changes. The MLSSM will provide pre-departure services to

15. Rehabilitation services for persons with disabilities in the Kyrgyz Republic are limited and not available to everyone in
    need. The existing Rehabilitation Center for People with Disabilities under MLSSM is the only facility in the country, which

    disabilities into society and family. The center is a residential facility designed to provide rehabilitation services (medical,
    and technical, psychological and social) during a full-board period for around 15-20 days. The government is planning to
    construct two additional rehabilitation centers in major regional centers (Osh and Issyk-Kul), to make such services
    accessible to populations in the regions. The Government has requested the reallocation of part of the project savings
    towards supporting two rehabilitation centers with necessary equipment, developing training materials, and delivering
    trainings to persons with disabilities, as well as minor civil works to fit the facilities for the needs of people with disabilities
    (installing ramps, disabled-friendly doorways, rooms, restrooms, wiring, switches and similar). The training will focus on
    transferring skills depending on the disability type to equip them with basic set of skills to facilitate employment

    and awareness raising among various groups, targeting the decision-makers, social workers, education sector staff,
    employers, and general population to overcome prejudice, social stigma and discrimination against persons with disabilities
    and contribute to their integration in the labor market and society. Supplementary activities focusing on strengthening
    employment opportunities for people with disabilities are proposed under a new Subcomponent 2.4. The new activities are
    in line with the objectives of Component 2 (Increasing the Employability of Vulnerable Groups Including Labor Migrants)
    and the World Bank’s recommendations based on its recent analytical work on Supporting Disability-Inclusive Employment
    and Education in the Kyrgyz Republic6. Public consultations with people with disabilities, public organizations, and relevant
    government stakeholders working on the disability agenda will be conducted prior to commencing the activities to get their
    feedback and adjust the activities as necessary.
16. Given the importance of digitalization in improving service delivery quality, the MLSSM is seeking to further strengthen
    the digitalization of services and functions of the Ministry by establishing a unified state information system of labor
    resources. This work will complement the efforts of the Ministry in transferring the employment record books and
    employment contracts to an electronic format, thereby ensuring better quality of data records, transparency of records and
    eliminating the need for retiring citizens to collect paper-based certificates to confirm the employment records at the time
    of assignment of pensions. This activity will be included in Component 3 of the project focusing on strengthening the
    digitalization of social protection.
17. The PDO will remain unchanged. All of the proposed changes are in line with the current PDO and do not alter the nature
    of the project.

IV. DETAILED CHANGE(S)

Summary Description
Operation Abstract: Summary Description of Proposed Operation
The project will be structured around three components:


Component 1: Providing Emergency Support through, and Enhancing the Effectiveness of the UBK Program
This component supports the first part of the PDO by (i) providing emergency support and ensuring that UBK social
benefits are paid on time for the households whose incomes are particularly affected by the negative economic
impacts caused by the economic crisis in Russia; and (b) strengthening the effectiveness of the UBK program to support
the poor and vulnerable during the shock and assist them to be better prepared for future shocks. To achieve these
goals, this component will use the country’s existing country systems to deliver the increased benefit of the main anti-
poverty program for families with children (UBK). The project will also support the Government in improving the
effectiveness of the UBK program by developing, piloting, and evaluating the improved eligibility criteria and
beneficiary selection methodology for the UBK program to reduce exclusion and inclusion errors.


Component 2: Increasing the Employability of Vulnerable Groups Including Labor Migrants.
The objectives of this component are to enhance the employability and provide income generating opportunities to
poor and vulnerable and difficult-to-employ individuals, as well as prospective and return labor migrants through the
provision of more effective labor and employment services and strengthening of selected activation programs.
Specifically, this component will achieve these objectives by supporting the following groups: (i) UBK beneficiaries to
transition from social assistance to labor and employment opportunities, (ii) vulnerable youth and women, including



                                                                                                                  Page 7
        The World Bank
        Strengthening Social Assistance and Labor Market Programs Project (P179024)


people with disabilities to (re) enter the labor market, and (iii) returning and prospective labor migrants to better
identify their needs, capacities, and aspirations and facilitate tailored support. In this framework, this component will
be implemented through four subcomponents to improve the skills and employability of these group of workers and
strengthen links with the private sector to engage them in jobs and income generating activities (IGA). The
implementation of this component will also support the Ministry of Labor, Social Security, and Migration (MLSSM /
MLSDM used interchangeably) and the employment services at the district level to enhance their capacity, operational
tools, and optimize business processes to support specific groups of jobseekers at the local level. Additionally, this
component will finance small works and procurement of goods to strengthen the infrastructure of specialized facilities
of the MLSSM through upgrading the rehabilitation facilities for people with disabilities and establishing pre-departure
and reintegration services for migrants.


Component 3: Strengthening the Delivery Systems of Social Protection
The proposed component has the medium- to long-term objective of improving the efficiency and effectiveness of the
social protection delivery systems in the Kyrgyz Republic. To achieve this objective, this component will complement
the activities included in the Electricity Sector Modernization and Sustainability Project (P177871). The social
protection activities under the electricity project seek to integrate the social passport module (local level household
data system) of social assistance information system into the systems that the energy companies use, and (ii) enhance
capacity and efficiency of the IT system for social assistance with new server and equipment; additionally, the activities
will contribute towards establishing a foundation for the digitalization of employment record books and electronic
employment contracts. In order to complement these efforts, the proposed project through this component will
finance activities to ensure IT sustainability, additional data security, and expanded functionality of the social
protection IT systems. This component will also finance the project implementation unit for the duration of the
operation (Subcomponent 3.2). Overall, by enhancing IT systems in social protection and improving data exchanges,
this Component will help the Government of Kyrgyz Republic be better prepared for future economic slowdown or
