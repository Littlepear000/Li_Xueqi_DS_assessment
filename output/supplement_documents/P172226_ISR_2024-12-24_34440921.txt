3. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


 3.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
   A Mid-Term Review (MTR) Mission for the Raising and Accelerating MSME Performance Program (RAMP) was held from
   November 6 to December 12, 2024. The mission assessed progress towards Program Development Objectives (PDOs),
   progress against Disbursement Linked Indicators/Results (DLIs/DLRs), fiduciary arrangements, environmental safeguards,
   and stakeholder alignment. Discussions were held with the Ministry of MSME, the National PMU, and state counterparts
   during field visits to Andhra Pradesh and Madhya Pradesh. Virtual workshops were conducted with other states, and key
   stakeholders such as SIDBI and QCI were consulted.
   The review highlighted substantial progress toward achieving the program objectives and delivering measurable results.
   Key achievements include:
       • Strategic Implementation Plans (SIPs): Gap financing enabled SIPs in 31 states/UTs, facilitating bespoke and
            targeted MSME interventions.

       •       MSME Competitiveness: Redesigned schemes have benefited 432,679 MSMEs across participating States/UTs,
               enhancing their competitiveness.
       •       Financial access through TReDS: Expanded TReDS operations routed ₹2.1 trillion (INR) in financing to over
               65,000 firms, strengthening cash flow and credit access to MSMEs.
       •       Adoption of Green Technologies: Two new schemes—GIFT and SPICE—have been launched to drive green
               innovation and sustainable practices in the MSME sector.

  As of November 2024, the program disbursed $231 million (46% of the loan), with an additional $24 million by March
  2025.
  The program’s trajectory underscores tangible outcomes in MSME growth and credit facilitation. Efforts now focus on
  maximizing these gains through improved monitoring, environmental and social safeguards, and ensuring expenditure
  alignment with program disbursements.
