1. OBJECTIVE


 1.1 Development Objective
   Original Development Objective (Approved as part of Decision package on 06-Sep-2022)
   The objective of the Project is to support the Kyrgyz Republic in mitigating the impact of the economic crisis on poor
   households and labor migrants, and strengthening effectiveness and resilience of the social assistance and labor market
   programs delivery systems, to be better prepared for future shocks.
   Has the Development Objective been changed since Board Approval of the Project Objective?

   No

4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  The Overall Implementation Progress is maintained at Satisfactory level, despite some delays under Subcomponent 1.2
  "Improving the Effectiveness of the UBK Program" which are expected to be on track. Total disbursements stand at
  US$19,4 million or about 65%.

  The Ministry of Labor, Social Security and Migration (MLSSM) and the PIU have launched the activities focusing on the
  Social Contract under the Subcomponent 2.1 “Supporting Graduation from Social Assistance through Economic Inclusion
  Opportunities.” At the time of ISR, the disbursements of payouts for all 3,000 target beneficiaries of the Social Contract
  were completed and the implementation of projects is ongoing. Supply of computer equipment as part of the upgrade of
  IT infrastructure of the MLSSM offices and regional branches has been successfully completed. The training activities
  under the Subcomponent 2.2 “Enhancing Employability and Job Opportunities among Youth Population” activities are
  ready to be launched. With this, the Overall Implementation Progress continues to be rated Satisfactory, while the PDO
  rating is downgraded to Moderately Satisfactory due to delays under Subcomponent 1.2.
