4. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  This Implementation Status and Results Report (ISR) is based on fields (face-to-face) missions in all participating countries
  (except Ghana) and regional organizations, and virtual technical mission in Ghana that were conducted from October 20 –
  December 13, 2024.

  A level 2 restructuring was approved in December 20, 2024. This restructuring introduced two changes on the project’s
  results framework. These are: (i) the removal of a PDO indicator 2, and (ii) one PDO-level and two intermediate level
  indicators will correct their baseline and end target value at the regional level.

  African Union (AU):
  The progress toward the achievement of the Project Development Objective (PDO) and the overall implementation
  progress (IP) for the AU are maintained as Moderately Satisfactory. The implementation progress report of the Strategy
  for Harmonization of Statistics in Africa (SHaSA2) for 2024 has been prepared and is under review.

  Economic Community of West African States (ECOWAS):
  There is an overall improvement in the implementation of the project activities. The progress toward achieving the PDO
  and the overall IP for ECOWAS remain however Moderately Satisfactory. Significant progress has been achieved with
  regard to two key products of the project: the regional guidelines on national accounts and the Consumer Price Index
  with their preparation and adoption by the Ministers in charge of statistics.

  Burkina Faso:
  The progress toward the achievement of the PDO and overall IP for Burkina Faso are maintained as Satisfactory. All PDO
  indicators have been successfully achieved, and the planning and implementation of project activities have significantly
  improved. This progress is marked by the commencement of the Enterprise Census and the completion of preparatory
  activities for rebasing the national accounts.

  Cabo Verde :
  The progress toward the achievement of the PDO and overall IP for Cabo Verde are maintained as Moderately
  Satisfactory. All the PDO indicators remains unchanged compared to their value of the first semester of 2024, the
  intermediary indicators also remain unchanged except the number of analytical reports produced and disseminated using
  survey macro or administrative data per country which increase from one to three. The user satisfaction survey is
  expected to be conducted in 2025.

  Cote d'Ivoire:
  The progress toward the achievement of the PDO and overall IP for Cote d’Ivoire remain Satisfactory. The PDO is on track:
  most of the other PDO indicators and intermediate level indicators are on track. The African Statistics Day was celebrated
  in 2024 marking a dissemination and communication event for a better but continued efforts are needed to reduce the
  time between data collection and results publication. Emphasis was placed on the need to carry out dissemination and
  communication activities around the project for a better visibility of the Cote d’Ivoire National Statistics Institute (Agence
  Nationale de la Statistique, ANStat) and the entire NSS, as was the case during the celebration of this year's African

  Ghana:
  There is good implementation progress of the project in Ghana. The project is well oriented in achieving its development
  objectives in Ghana, as evidenced by PDO indicators well beyond the target values. For instance, while the objective set
  for Ghana with regard to the statistical capacity index, one key PDO indicator, is 75 per cent, the value achieved as of end
  of November 2024 has reached 99 per cent, thanks to various activities implemented that strengthened the country
  statistical infrastructure like the population census, the agriculture census and the economic census. The project has also
  contributed to improving human resources capacity as well as the statistical environment with support provided to
  enhance the statistical modernization of the system.
  Liberia:
  The progress toward the achievement of the PDO for Liberia remains satisfactory. To achieve the PDO level indicator, the
  share of economic and social statistics datasets produced and available for open access, data are available but need to be
  anonymized and put on the LISGIS website , to achieve the increase in the user satisfaction index: the user satisfaction
  survey is under implementation and the data collection is expected to be completed by December 31, 2024.

  Sierra Leone:
  The progress toward the achievement of the PDO and IP ratings for Sierra Leone remain Satisfactory. Remaining ongoing
  project activities include the Integrated Household Survey (2024 SLIHS), the Multiple Indicator Cluster Survey (MICS), the
  construction and expansion of the Stats SL HQ, and the user satisfaction survey.

   Togo:
  The progress toward the achievement of the PDO and the overall IP for Togo remain Satisfactory. Project has made
  progress in the implementation of the Environmental and Social Safeguards Action Plan agreed in November 2023 and
  implementation of activities have resumed.

  Project Monitoring and Evaluation (M&E):
  The projects M&E rating is upgraded from Moderately Satisfactory to Satisfactory. There is significant progress towards
  achieving PDO indicators: Compared to the baselines, out of the six PDO indicators, at the aggregate level four PDO
  indicators are overachieved. These include: PDO Indicator 1: Modified Statistical capacity index (SCI 2.0) (74.34 percent
  actual value compared to end target value of 71.43 percent); PDO Indicator 2: Share of comparable core economic and
  social statistics indicators produced and available for open access (79.43 percent actual value compared to the end target
  value of 50 percent); PDO Indicator 3: Share of comparable core economic and social statistics datasets produced and
  available for open access (acutal value 55.14 percent compared to end target value of 50 percent); and (PDO Indicator 4
  : Share of statistical products produced and published by the National Statistics Office (NSO) following international
  standards and a predetermined release calendar (73.86 percent actual value compared to the end target value of 62.29
  percent). The remaining two PDO level indicators are on track to be achieved before the project closing date. These
  include: PDO Indictor 5: Share of countries that produce and disseminate at least two analytical reports using survey,
  macro or administrative data (85.71 percent actual value compared to the end target value of 100 percent); and PDO
  Indicator 6: Increase in user satisfaction index (3.8 percent actual value compared to the end target value of 10
  percent). In addition, six out of nine intermediate level indicatgors are achieved and the remaining three IP indciators are
  on track to be achieved.
