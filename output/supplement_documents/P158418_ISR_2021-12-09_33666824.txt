 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The project is in its third year of implementation and is expected to last for an additional four years until the official closing date of February 27,
  2026. The implementation progress to-date is Moderately Satisfactory. Construction work at Eregli-Ivriz irrigation scheme is progressing well.
  However, actual construction works are yet to begin at the three remaining schemes (i.e., Uzunlu, Atabey, and Alasehir).
  The activities under Component 2 “Innovation and Institutional Support” are satisfactorily progressing. The contracts for the SUTEM
  software development and for Bolvadin, Afyon solar-powered groundwater pilot have been signed and implementation is in progress.
  100 percent of the IBRD loan and the GEF grant is already committed.There was some budget saving due to the lower actual
  contract values than planned for the four contracted irrigation schemes. The overall disbursement level is about 12.59%, which is expected to pick-
