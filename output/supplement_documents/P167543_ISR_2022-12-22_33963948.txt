 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The required preliminary studies for project implementation have been carried out. There is also significant progress on all Components of the
  Project and key activities such as the opening of digital centers, deployment of mobile connectivity sites in rural areas, as well as the design of
  customer centric digital financial services platform are underway.
  The project is steadily moving towards achieving its PDO on the back of a thriving digital economy reinforced by improved access to mobile internet
  (31% as of October 2022 compared to 16% in May 2022) and a more conducive regulatory framework (finalization of the Digital Policy 2021– 2030
  in April 2022 and implementation of related recommendations, training of 63 senior government and parliament officials). The project has started
  deployment of rural connectivity sites in 21 villages (250 sites expected by January 2023 and 2175 by project end) and opened an additional 25
