4. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  PROCIENCIA and CONCYTEC have been highly active in preparing and implementing project activities since receiving
  funding in January 2023. This includes designing and launching grant instruments, as well as Terms of Reference and/or
  technical specifications for consulting and non-consulting services, most of which have already been awarded and are
  under implementation.

  Examples of key advancements include:
  - Institutional alliances for Ph.D. program grants. Grants have been awarded and the alliances are currently being
  developed.
  - PhD scholarships. The first round of students has been selected.
  - Lab strengthening and lab accreditation grants. Grants have been awarded and are being implemented.
  - Academic entrepreneurship grants and grants for certification of lab tests. Grant proposals are currently being
  evaluated.
  - Industry-academia contract research grants for the development of technologies to support value chains: The grant
  instrument is currently being designed.
  - Technology transfer program: The instruments are currently being designed.
  - Access to full-text international scientific literature as part of CONCYTEC’s Digitization. The contract is under
  implementation, thus allowing the Peruvian scientific community to access the literature.
