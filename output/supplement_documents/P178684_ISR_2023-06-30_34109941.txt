1. OBJECTIVES


 1.1 Development Objectives
   Original Development Objective (Approved as part of Approval package on 28-Mar-2023)
   To improve safe and equitable access to education, particularly for girls, and teaching-learning conditions in secondary
   schools in selected provinces.
   Has the Development Objective been changed since Board Approval of the Project Objective?

   No

4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  The project was approved on March 28, 2023. The project was approved by the Parliament and the Senate. It is now
  awaiting presidency ordinance. The project did not have a Project Preparation Advance (PPA); thus no activities are
  currently being implemented while awaiting effectiveness.
