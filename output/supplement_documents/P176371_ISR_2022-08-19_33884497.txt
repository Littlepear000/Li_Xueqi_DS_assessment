 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  This regional operation was approved by the World Bank's Board on May 13, 2022. In the three months since then, all five financing agreements
  (FA) have been signed by both the Bank and the clients (Rwanda, Kenya and Tanzania for the main PforR component of the operation and EAC
  and AU for the IPF component). Work on meeting the additional effectiveness conditions is ongoing and currently the expectation is that the
  effectiveness deadlines (4 months after signing of the FA) will be met.
