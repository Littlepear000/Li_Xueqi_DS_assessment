4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  Overall project implementation has improved due to the completion of procurements for the electronic procurement
  system (eGP), the PFM-Smart accounting system for Local Councils, and the audit management system (AMIS) for the
  Audit Service Sierra Leone. The 1st tranche of round 2 of the Local Development Grants has been disbursed to 15 Local
  Councils to finance 33 sub-projects.
  The project has been successfully restructured to concentrate on priorities that can be completed within the remaining
  time before project closure.
