I. PROJECT STATUS AND RATIONALE FOR RESTRUCTURING

1.      The Project Implementing Agencies, the State Tax Service (STS) and the National Statistics Committee (NSC),
have made good progress in implementation of all project components and the preparation of the major remaining
procurements, following the recommendations of the mid-term review mission (October 16-27, 2023). STS has
established a monitoring and analytical department to work on compliance monitoring, expanded electronic services to
taxpayers, and rolled out cash registers through incentives for small enterprises and mandatory use of electronic invoices.
Risk-based compliance management is pending completion of business process reengineering and the IT upgrade. NSC is
making progress on the transition to SNA-2008 standards, has modernized its IT and statistical infrastructure by procuring
IT hardware for both central and regional offices, and has strengthened the legal framework by issuing the Medium-term
Program for the Development of Official Statistics of the Kyrgyz Republic for 2022-2026 and other legal acts to ensure
alignment with the Law on Official Statistics. The Overall Implementation Progress is rated Moderately Satisfactory. Total
disbursement to date is US$11.3 million or 32%. Out of undisbursed balance of US$23.9 million around USD$6.82 million
or 28.5% is committed under the signed contracts. Remaining balance will mainly finance support to ISNAK-2
implementation and IT equipment for NSC and STS information systems.
2.       An 18-month extension of the project closing date to March 31, 2027, is necessary to successfully complete the
implementation of new IT systems and change underlying business processes in both STC and NSC and thereby achieve
the project development objective. Progress toward the PDO is currently rated Moderately Unsatisfactory and can only
be upgraded to Moderately Satisfactory when the project closing date extension is approved allowing the project to
complete the activities foreseen in the original project design. The initial delay in implementation is attributed to a 11-
month effectiveness delay due to Parliamentary ratification of the project, delays related to COVID-19 disruptions, and,
for NSC, delays in the engagement of the NSC Twinning Partner. Both STS and NSC have successfully implemented project
activities according to the original plan following the resumption of normal working practices after COVID. The Bank team
is confident that both STS and NSC can complete procurement and business process reforms following the proposed
extended implementation schedule which has been discussed and agreed between the Bank team and STS and NSC. The
project has complied with legal covenants, procurement, and financial management arrangements. To date, there are no
outstanding audit reports.

3.      STS will develop a new custom-built Information System for Tax Administration of Kyrgyz Republic (ISNAK-2)
based on the IT roadmap developed by the STS and discussed with the World Bank team. STS is developing the technical
documentation and preparing for procurement. STS will launch the development of ISNAK-2 based on the new business
processes in July 2024. The Bank will closely monitor implementation based on benchmarks identified in the roadmap:
completion of ISNAK-2 development and implementation phase I by January 2025; phase II by June 2025; and phase III
and full acceptance of the system by June 2026. The project will finance procurement of the supplemental IT capacity
necessary for timely ISNAK-2 development using a prior review QCBS procurement process. International experience
demonstrates that most tax information system installations require at least 18 months to successfully convert all existing
revenue instruments to the new software and maintain operating quality and availability standards.
4.       NSC initiated procurement of the data centers needed to support expanded requirements for official statistics in
April 2024. NSC expects to sign this contract in September 2024. Experience suggests that delivery and installation of the data
centers requires at least 14 months, with installation scheduled by November 2025. Further time will be needed for post-
implementation testing and troubleshooting. The time currently remaining in the project is insufficient to complete these and
several other (smaller) activities.

Summary of Performance of E&S Risk Compliance

STS and NSC, with the support of the respective Project Management Units (PMUs), ensure the project’s compliance
with the World Bank’s Environmental and Social Framework (ESF) requirements. The overall project's E&S risk rating is
Low. ESS1, ESS2, and ESS10 are relevant to the project activities. The project’s current environmental and social
performance rating is Satisfactory. The Project is implemented in accordance with the project’s Environmental and Social
Commitment Plan (ESCP), Stakeholder Engagement Plan (SEP), and Labor Management Procedures (LMP). ESCP
implementation status is shared with the Bank as part of the project progress reports. All project workers are familiar
with the labor-management procedures, signed the Code of Conduct, and both PMUs monitor the project’s compliance
with LMP requirements. The project-level Grievance Redress Mechanism (GRM) and Project workers GRM for both tax
and statistics components are functioning. No grievances were registered during the reporting period. The mission
reminded the PMUs of the need to ensure: (i) project implementation in accordance with the project’s ESCP, SEP, and
LMP; (ii) effective engagement with stakeholders, including interaction with final users and consideration of their
feedback in developing new business processes; (iii) register and address any project related requests/complaints in a
timely and effective manner; (iv) to procure certified formaldehyde-free furniture; and (v) to properly handle, collect,
and dispose of any equipment installation rehabilitation debris, IT residues, E-waste, and/or if confronted with asbestos
material as per national regulations and international best practices.
