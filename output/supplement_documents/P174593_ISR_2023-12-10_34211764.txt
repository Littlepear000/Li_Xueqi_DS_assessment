 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The MPA was approved the World Bank in March 2023, and the Loan Agreement for Phase 1 was signed on June 11, 2023 by the Government of
  India. In early 2022 the Assam Water Resources Department has initiated anti-erosion and embankment upgrading works on the Beki and
  Buridehing Rivers. During the first construction season in 2022-2023, protection works on the river side were largely completed. During the second
  construction season in 2023-2024 it is expected that most of the anti-erosion works will be completed. Work on strengthening the critical flood
  embankments is expected to be completed in the 2024-2025 construction season.
  WRD, with assistance from the Flood and River Erosion Management Agency of Assam (FREMAA), will contract consultants in early 2024 to
  upgrade the Assam flood forecasting system and undertake Integrated Flood Risk Management Plans (IFRMPs) in the Beki and Buridehing basins.
  This assignment will take approximately three years to completed.
