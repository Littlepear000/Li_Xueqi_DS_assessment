 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The project was approved on July 28, 2020 and significant efforts are being made towards achievement of project effectiveness and implementation
  readiness. The National Project Coordinating Unit and the State Project Implementation Units (SPIU) have all been established across the
  participating states (Borno, Ekiti, Kaduna, Katsina, Kano, Kebbi and Plateau) and Project Implementation Manual (PIM) has been drafted. All the
  states have made progress in the development of their workplans. Some important actions are mentioned below:
            Federal Executive Council (FEC) approval: The task team has long submitted the required copies of the project documents to IERD for
             FEC presentation, the FEC approval is still pending.
            The National Project Implementation Unit (NPCU) has been established. The states Project Implementation Units (SPIUs) are
             confirmed and already working for each state. In addition, they have opened project Bank accounts.
            Project Implementation Manual (PIM): the PIM is under review and will be finalized by December 30, 2020.

             Multiple technical workshops have been conducted for each subcomponent of the project. The workshops summarized what is
              expected for each subcomponent, established next steps, and gave the states an opportunity to present their workplans and receive
              feedback.
             Trainings on fiduciary management, environment and social safeguards, procurement and financial management have been completed
              with core SPIU team members onboarded on relevant project management platforms.
             The governments have made progress in developing terms of reference for multiple consultants that will support project
              implementation. The task team has been providing handholding support to ensure implementation readiness.
             The states have completed mapping of schools for expansion (construction) and renovation.
             The NPCU created virtual community of practice for each component to ensure continued guidance, knowledge sharing and
              communication with plans to commence in-person State Peer Learning events to strengthen implementation.
