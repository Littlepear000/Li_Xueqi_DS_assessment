4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  The Project’s Implementation Progress (IP) and Achievement of PDO ratings have been retained as ‘Moderately
  Satisfactory’ due to delays in implementation of key passive infrastructure development contracts under Component 1.
  Mitigation steps to avoid such delays in the future include launching parallel construction of multiple lots and enhancing
  procurement capacity.
  The Project has disbursed US$24.5 million (64% of committed amount) and expected to disburse an additional US$1.5
  million by June 2025. Co-financing with European Investment Bank (EIB) is effective and contracts under implementation
  are co-financed. Procurement of active infrastructure under Subcomponent 1.1 is ongoing and expected to drive
  disbursement rate higher.
