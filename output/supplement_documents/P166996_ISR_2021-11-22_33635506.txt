 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The Ghana Jobs and Skills Project (GJSP) became effective on January 27, 2021, with the establishment of the Project Coordinating Unit (PCU)
  under the Ministry of Finance and the preparation of the Project Implementation Manual. Workplans and procurement plans for GJSP covering the
  first 18-month period of project implementation were completed by June 2021, followed by a first disbursement by the Bank in June 2021. All key

  committees under the project's overall institutional and implementation arrangements have been established. The Project Technical Committee met
  in August 2021, and another meeting of the committee is expected in December/January 2021. Key procurements across all project components
  are currently underway. The Bank, the PCU, and the implementing and partnering agencies have been holding regular technical meetings to make
  progress on procurement and the implementation of the interventions under the results-based and standard IPF components of the project.
  The government is planning an official project launch by the end of this year.
