3. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


 3.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
   Overall, progress towards the PDO remains strong even though implementation progress continues to lag for several
   components. Reflecting agreements reached during the MTR (May 2025), participant countries have agreed to a
   substantial increase in the harmonization ambition, and it is likely that the end target of the PDO indicator on "number of
   common statistical dimensions harmonized across countries" will be doubled in the forthcoming restructuring. At the
   time of the most recent round of missions (October-November 2025), 7 common statistical dimensions had already been
   harmonized across the 4 participating countries, exceeding the original end target of 5. Implementation progress remains

  slower, witnessed by a disbursement rate of 43% with approximately 20 months of implementation remaining, but has
  nevertheless picked up substantially since the previous mission (when the disbursement rate was 30%).
  Progress varies across countries. Kenya has made progress since the Mid Term Review but implementation remains
  moderately unsatisfactory due to funding delays to the Kenya National Bureau of Statistics (KNBS), necessitating
  restructuring in FY26. Tanzania shows satisfactory progress with a disbursement rate of 57%, though some activities are
  delayed. Rwanda is on track with good progress and satisfactory ratings, while Burundi's progress remains moderately
  satisfactory, requiring technical assistance for delayed activities. Progress on the EAC's statistical harmonization is highly
  satisfactory, but infrastructure development and capacity building need acceleration. AUC's implementation has
  accelerated though remains behind schedule due to the initial delays. Program restructuring is planned for FY26.
