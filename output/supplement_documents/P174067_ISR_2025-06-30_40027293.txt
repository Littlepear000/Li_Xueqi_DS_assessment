  There has been steady progress in toward achieving project results. Under Component 1, CBC has developed Annual
  Capacity Building Plans (ACBPs) for all ministries, departments and agencies (MDAs) of the Government of India. Under
  Component 2, as on June, 2025, more than 10 million users and 2627 online courses have been onboarded onto the iGOT
  Karmayogi platform. The World Bank has shared 18 courses from its Open Learning Campus repository which are now
  integrated onto iGOT covering diverse topics such climate change, PPP, urban development, sustainable cities, health
  inequalities and open data.
