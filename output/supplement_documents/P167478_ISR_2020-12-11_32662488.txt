 Project Development Objectives

  Project Development Objective (from Project Appraisal Document)
  The Project Development Objectives (PDO) are to improve access to, and the quality of early childhood and basic education in the Project Areas
  and to strengthen the capacity of the education system to deliver better results.

 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The Project became effective on July 15, 2020 once the two conditions of effectiveness were met, namely: (i) the finalization and adoption of the
  Project Implementation Manual (PIM) satisfactory to the World Bank; and (ii) the recruitment of a project coordinator, a financial management
  specialist, a procurement specialist, and an monitoring and evaluation (M&E) specialist for the Project Coordination Unit (PCU) with terms of
  reference, qualifications and experience satisfactory to the Bank.

  The Project Coordination Unit (PCU) is now fully staffed and operational. A number of critical terms of reference (TORs) have been drafted and
  submitted to the World Bank for review and approval. These TORs are linked to critical activities such as the recruitment of an independent
  verification agency to verify disbursement-linked indicators, the recruitment of technical assistance for school construction activities, the recruitment
  of an external and internal auditors and other supporting staff for the PCU. The first Project’s first Annual Work Plan was approved by the Project’s
