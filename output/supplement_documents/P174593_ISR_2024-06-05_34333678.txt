4. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  The overall status of the project is considered satisfactory. The status of each component is as follows:

  Component 1: Institutional Development and Capacity Building for ASDMA and WRD: This component is proceeding
  well, with Project Management and Technical Consultants providing support and capacity building services, and
  consultancies for key studies under procurement (Sate Water Resources Assessment, Post-Disaster Needs Assessment,
  Multi-Hazard Risk Financing, WRD IPMS, etc). Key challenge in the next two years is adoption of a new State Water Policy.

  Component 2: WRD Water Resources Management: This component is proceeding well with river works well under way,
  and consultants mobilized for flood forecasting/flood planning. The next major activity is preparation of the Flood
  Embankment Asset Management Program, with consultants expected to be mobilized in late 2024.

  Component 3: ASDM Disaster Risk Management: This component is now proceeding well, as ASDMA has accelerated its
  procurement actions and enhanced staffing. The second half of 2024 will focus on mobilization and inception reports for
  consultant firm contracts related to flood shelters, emergency warning, and climate resilient villages. The years 2025 and
  2026 are expected to see larger investments in civil works and goods.
