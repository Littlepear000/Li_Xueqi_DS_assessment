 Project Development Objectives

  Project Development Objective (from Project Appraisal Document)
  To strengthen the capacity of the National Institute of Statistics of Djibouti (INSD) to produce and disseminate timely and reliable statistics in a
  sustainable manner and modernize the Recipient's selected economic and fiscal management tools and processes in Djibouti.

 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  Activities for both components have started, notably with the preparation of TORs for the hiring of technical assistance. For both components, a
  kick-off workshop to review international experience and establish a clear action plan is scheduled. In compliance with the program of work for the
  year, activities per component for calendar 21 have been discussed and agreed during the first supervision mission.
       o     Component 1 (stats): several significant actions for Component 1 have already been implemented, including the drafting of an agreement
             with ANSD (Senegal) and the signing of a contract with ENSAE(Senegal's School of Statistics)for the training of two cohorts of 20 INSD
             statisticians; and the preparation of the TDRs of short-term consultant to accompany the institutional transition. INSTAD's activities for
             2021 will focus on institutional transition and population census preparation.

      o       Component 1 (building): The Mission has noted considerable progress in the preparatory work for the construction of the future
              INSD/MEFI building. A consulting engineer responsible for assisting the Djiboutian authorities for the realization of sub-component
              construction of the Project was recruited, as well as an architectural firm that has already prepared the projects based, among other
              things, on the results of a geotechnical study of the soil (the last two actions, financed by the government ). A final budget is being
              prepared and will be shared in the next few days.
      o       Component 2 (Modernization of economic and management tools and processes): Substantial progress is underway: the MOU for the
              acquisition of the public debt management software from UNCTAD is being finalized and will be sent to the WB for no objection in April
              2021 with the ToRs for the development of the medium-term debt management strategy (MTDS) and training activities in the appraisal of
              PPPs and support to the technical secretariat of the CNEP. Regarding the development of financial programming and debt sustainability
              analysis frameworks, the IMF is examining the possibility of providing training in these two areas in calendar year 2021 prior to the
              modeling works.
             Financial management: The project has launched the tender for the recruitment of the consultant for the preparation of the operations
              manual, containing a part of financial and accounting management. The 2021 work plan and annual budget was sent to the Bank in
              February. The work plan and annual budget must be finalized normally in December of the current year. The recruitment of an external
              auditor for the 2021, 2022 and 2023 fiscal years is underway. However, the mission recommended that October, November and
              December of 2020 be included in the 2021 audit.
             Contracting: The Mission noted that the priority activities of the procurement plan have already entered the World Bank's (STEP)
              procurement computer system (STEP), following the various training sessions of World Bank UGP staff ' to use it. Some activities,
              including technical assistance to INSD, are initiated by the preparation of the terms of reference and the choice/identification of
              consultants.
