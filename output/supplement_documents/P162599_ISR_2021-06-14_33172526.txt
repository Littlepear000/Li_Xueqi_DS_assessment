 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The project was approved by the World Bank Board on July 1, 2019, and became effective on December 3, 2019. The implementation of the project
  has encountered significant delays mainly due to project implementation arrangements not yet in place and the Covid-19 crisis.
  Following the Government's request to release funds for the COVID-19 response, the project was restructured a first time on May 18, 2020 to
  urgently reduce the credit amount from EUR 89.2 million to EUR 42.9 million (equivalent to USD 47.2 million), and then a second time on January

  12, 2021 to revise the Project Development Objective (PDO) and project components. The PDO was adjusted as: "To improve access to broadband
  services in targeted rural communities and promote the use of digital solutions to improve efficiency of selected value-chains and financial
  inclusion." The project is now structured around three components: Component 1 aims to expand digital connectivity in targeted rural areas;
  Component 2 focuses on digital financial inclusion and digital solutions for rural development; and Component 3 supports project implementation.
  The project closing date remains July 31, 2024.
  The Project has made significant progress recently with: (i) the operationalization of the National Steering Committee for the project; (ii) the
  recruitments for the PIU staff team; (iii) the launch of several recruitments of consultants/firms in charge of technical studies for Components 1 and
  2; and (iv) the finalization of the manual for Grievance Redress Mechanism.
