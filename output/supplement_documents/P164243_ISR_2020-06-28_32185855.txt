 Project Development Objectives

  Project Development Objective (from Project Appraisal Document)
  The development objective of this project is to support the production of harmonized and quality statistics in the EAC, in support of the regional
  integration in the region, through capacity building of the National Statistical Offices of the East African Community (EAC) Partner States and the
  EAC Secretariat.

 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The project was approved on July 27, 2018, signed on October 10, 2018, and declared effective on December 20, 2018. The pace of project 's
  activities implementation was very low in 2019 due to long time it took to complete the recruitment of the PIU and technical staff. Eventually, the
  recruitment of all the 13 PIU and technical staff was completed in February 2020.
  An action plan aiming to speed up the implementation of the project’s activities was prepared when unfortunately the covid-19 outbreak has caused
  the project’s activities implementation to slow down, because by their nature and design they require meeting/workshops which involve travel within
  the region by the project staff and/or national delegates.
  However, the project team is continuing to work on areas which can be done remotely or on-the-desk. This is primarily on development of tools for
  use during the scoping missions of experts in partner states; and development of technical guidelines on statistical harmonization. In addition, the
  project will also explore options of remote technical discussions with partner states' experts in some areas.

  Furthermore, before the pandemic, the project has already supported the production of the "EAC Facts and Figures 2019", a statistical compilation
  that gives comprehensive economic and social information on all the EAC member states. A national training on the EAC Guidelines for the
  Compilation of Government Finance Statistics (GFS) and Public Sector Debt Statistics (PSDS) has also been provided in Rwanda. This training will
  also be done in other partner states.
  The work on harmonization of the Consumer Prices Index (CPI) in the region had also started. Indeed, as part of the activities supported by the
  Project, a workshop on regional regulations and guidelines took place in February 2020. The meeting, attended by CPI experts from each Partner
  State (one from the NSO and the other from the Central Bank), reviewed the draft EAC regulations for the compilation of harmonized CPI (HCPI) in
  the region as well as the proposal for adoption by the region of the new revised goods and services’ classification for CPI compilation referred to as
  COICOP-2018 adopted by the United Nations Statistical Commission at its 49th Session in March 2018.
