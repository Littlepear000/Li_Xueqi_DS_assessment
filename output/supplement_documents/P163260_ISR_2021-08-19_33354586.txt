 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The new Administration, which took an office on August 16, 2020 expressed interest in re-instating the Project, confirming that the objectives and
  interventions of the project are in line with their development policies and priorities. The Project was successfully re-instated and amended Loan
  Agreement was signed on July 8, 2021. The Government and the Bank teams are currently engaged proactively to ensure that the specific
  effectiveness conditions of the Loan Agreement are met by September 30, 2021.
