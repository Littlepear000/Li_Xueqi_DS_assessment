4. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  Key Issues
  The purpose of this first ISR is to provide updates on the project status since the Board approval on March 22, 2024.

  The project has made satisfactory progress. The major milestones includes:

  -    Approval of the Project by the Council of Ministers and the House of People's Representatives (Parliament) of
  Ethiopia on May 15 and May 30, 2024, respectively.

  -    Establishment of a Regional Project Coordination Unit (RPCU) in the newly added region of Dire-Dawa, including the
  deployment of a Regional Project Coordinator, allocation of a project office, and the ongoing recruitment process for
  remaining regional and woreda project staff.

  -   Commencement and ongoing recruitment of additional staff at the Federal Project Coordination Unit (FPCU) and
  RPCUs.

  -    Preparation of the Project Implementation Manual (PIM), which includes detailed Financial Management (FM) and
  Procurement manuals and guidelines for Pastoral Savings and Credit Cooperatives (PASACCOs) and micro and small
  enterprises as well as draft Memorandum of Understandings (MoUs) to be signed with all implementing partners. These
  documents, reviewed and approved by the Bank, were the conditions for the project's effectiveness.

  -       Declaration of the project's effectiveness on June 21, 2024.

  -     Advanced preparation of detailed designs, specifications, and Bill of Quantities (BoQ), including environmental and
  social safeguard documents, for 71 Public Economic Infrastructure (PEI) subprojects to be financed under component 1.2.
  These subprojects, initiated under phase one of the project but were not implemented due to the financing gaps, are
  expected to be uploaded to STEP by mid-July 2024.

  -    Ongoing preparations to familiarize the Project at all levels including a national launching workshop by mid-July
  2024, followed by the preparation of the Annual Work Plan and Budget (AWPB) for the Ethiopian Fiscal Year (EFY) 2017,
  planned for August-September 2024.

  Overall, the project preparatory phase is making satisfactory progress as planned. Going forward, the client is advised to
  further expedite and finalize the phase-in process, submit the AWPB for EFY 2017, and commence actual implementation
  of project activities. In addition, the client agreed to ensure timely fulfillment of the disbursement condition, of
