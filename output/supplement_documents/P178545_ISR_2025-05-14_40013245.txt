4. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  The project has made significant progress since the last mission, with all 232 (batch 1) target villages completing village
  orientation and development planning, engaging over 25,000 people. Village Development Plans (VDPs) have been
  finalized for these 232 villages, and district-level endorsement is expected by the end of April 2025. This will allow
  infrastructure subj-project construction activities to begin. The project has successfully mobilized 855 Self-Help Groups
  (SHGs), with strong participation from women, and 323 Community Nutrition Groups (CNGs) have been established.
  Additionally, 670 model farmers have been trained in good agricultural practices.
