4. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  Around one year after effectiveness, project implementation is ongoing in all 6 countries and SADC. Implementation is
  most advanced in Comoros (90% disbursed), Malawi (70% disbursed), and SADC (32% disbursed). STP is also progressing
  as expected, where the lower disbursement rate is due to the lack of data collection scheduled for 2024. Implementation
  in Zambia, Mozambique and Madagascar is slower than anticipated.
  The most significant activities implemented in 2024 include: 15 workshops and training sessions implemented by SADC;
  field implementation of a Demographic Health Survey in Zambia and Malawi; advanced preparations of Agricultural
  surveys/census in Mozambique, Malawi and Zambia; updating of National Accounts in Comoros; implementation of
  Household Budget Survey in Comoros and advanced preparations towards this survey in Mozambique; Labor Force
  Survey in Malawi; support to Consumer Price Index and Producer Price Index in Malawi and Zambia; advanced
  preparations on Economic Census of Establishments in Malawi and Zambia.
