4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  Key reforms for NRM, mining, environment and climate are firmly and consistently underway in Guinea including (i)
  strengthen and expand the national protected area network, (ii) regular and structured technical, environmental and
  social (E&S) monitoring of mining operations, (iii) increasing local content in the mining sector as well as (iv) improving
  transparency and stakeholder engagement in the mining sector.
  The Project has to accelerate implementation (currently 16,13% disbursement rate and several fundamental contracts
  still in procurement phase) to support the ongoing reforms and to address key challenges including the development of
  the Simandou iron ore mine.
  Key priority actions to accelerate Project implementation has been identified and has to be implemented in the short
  term especially the recruitment of technical assistance for implementing agencies.
