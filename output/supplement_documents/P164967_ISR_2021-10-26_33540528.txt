 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The Program implementation is on track. The fundamental principle of the PforR approach, which consists of using country resources to finance
  activities and then being reimbursed from Program funds, whenever results are achieved with related disbursement linked indicators (DLIs), is well
  respected with the effective implementation of the activities planned under this Program. The prospect is good for achieving quickly some
  disbursement linked results (DLR) of the DLIs. There is a need to finalize quickly the recruitment of the AVI.
