4. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  The development objectives of the project are to (i) increase the coverage of Egypt’s UHIS in Phase I Governorates, (ii)
  strengthen UHIS-related governance and institutions, and (iii) provide temporary financial protection against high out of
  pocket health expenditures for vulnerable populations outside Phase I Governorates. This ISR provides an update on the
  progress and challenges on the UHIS project since the last mission in November 2024.
  Since the last ISR, UHIS has been launched in Suez Governorate, enrolling over 500,000 beneficiaries, with plans to launch
  in Aswan Governorate (also launched immediately after the mission on July 1). This completes Phase I rollout of the UHIS
  in six Governorates.
