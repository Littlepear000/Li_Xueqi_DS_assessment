4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  The Second Tanzania Intermodal and Rail Development Project (TIRP-2) aims at improving safety, climate resilience and
  operational efficiency of railway operation along the Dar es Salaam to Isaka section of the existing meter gauge railway.
  The project has four following components: (i) strengthening of railway infrastructure and support to transport studies;
  (ii) strengthening of climate resilience of Kilosa-Gulwe-Igandu railway section; (iii) operational and institutional support;
  and (iv) Contingent Emergency Response component (CERC). TIRP-2 was approved by the Board of Executive Directors of
  the World Bank on March 28, 2024 with total allocation of US$200 million from IDA. Project implementation will start
  after the financing agreement is signed and effectiveness conditions are fulfilled by the Government.
