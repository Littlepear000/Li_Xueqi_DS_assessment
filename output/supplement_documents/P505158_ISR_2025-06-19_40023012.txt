  The Angola Youth Employment Opportunities Project (P505158) was approved by the World Bank’s Board
  of Executive Directors on March 20, 2025. The Government of Angola is currently undertaking the procedural
  steps necessary to authorize signing of the Loan Agreement. Preparatory activities for the start of active
  implementation are underway, namely the adoption of the Project Operational Manual (POM) and
  designation of the key staff to support project implementation.
