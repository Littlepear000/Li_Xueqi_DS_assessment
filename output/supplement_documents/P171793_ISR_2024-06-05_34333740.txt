1. OBJECTIVE


 1.1 Development Objective
   Original Development Objective (Approved as part of Approval package on 01-Mar-2021)
   The proposed Project Development Objective (PDO) is (i) to enhance connectivity and road safety along the Zinder -
   Agadez road section in Northeastern Niger through the rehabilitation of the Tanout – Tiguidit road segment and
   maintenance of the full Zinder - Agadez section, and (ii) to improve access to basic socio-economic infrastructure for
   selected communities, along the Zinder - Agadez road section.
   Has the Development Objective been changed since Board Approval of the Project Objective?

   No

4. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  The Enhancing Niger Northeastern Connectivity project (PACNEN) was approved on March 2, 2022, and became effective
  on September 22, 2021, with a total financing of US$ 175 million.
  The Project development objective which is (i) to enhance connectivity and road safety along the Zinder-Agadez Road
  section in Northeastern Niger through the rehabilitation of the Tanout – Tiguidit road segment and maintenance of the
  full Zinder - Agadez section, and (ii) to improve access to basic socio-economic infrastructure for selected communities,
  along the Zinder-Agadez Road section is Moderately Satisfactory.
  The implementation of the OPBRC contract for Lot 1 (in the region of Zinder) had started in September 2022. To-date, the
  maintenance works were carried out on the entire section 254.3 km (lot 1) and the rehabilitation of the first 25 km
  section was completed and provisionally received on October 27, 2023.
  The implementation of the OPBRC contract for Lot 2 (in the region of Agadez) started on May 15, 2023, aiming at the
  rehabilitation works (109 km) and multi-year maintenance: Agadez – Agadez/Zinder Regions limit 171 km (lot 2).
  The socio-economic infrastructures to be implemented for the benefit of the population living along the Zinder-Agadez
  Road section are being identified through a consultation process.
