 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  Component 1: PforR
  Results Area 1:Revenue Mobilization. There is satisfactory progress in implementation of activities in this Results Area. Collection of the
  province’s taxes has improved significantly from a baseline of Rs.14.30 billion in FY2018/19 to Rs. 25.14 billion in FY2019/20 (revised estimates),
  exceeding the project target for Year 1. As of June 2020, the registered Services Sales Taxpayers who filed returns stood at 75 percent against the
  assigned target for the year. There is a delay in the integration of databases of the provincial tax authorities, and simplification and harmonization of
  their tax administration processes. However, a draft integration plan has been submitted for review by the three authorities before approval. The
  targeted Urban Immovable Property Tax survey of selected cities has not been completed largely due to delay in selection of a firm to undertake
  GIS mapping. This work is now expected to be completed for Havelian and Nowshera cities by December 2020.
  Results Area 2: Public Resource Management. Overall, the Government is making satisfactory efforts to assess the volume of cash deposits in
  commercial banks, as a first step to reduce the amount of Cash deposits held by Government in commercial banks. The stock take of
  existing number of accounts and deposits has been initiated with the State Bank Pakistan. The Cash Management Policy has been approved by
  Finance Department and templates for consolidation of cash balances outside the Treasury Single Account (TSA) and cash forecasts have been
  circulated with departments. Similarly, satisfactory progress has been made in reducing the share of Annual Development Program (ADP) funds
  allocated to unapproved projects (excluding Foreign Project Assistance). The 2019/20 budget allocates 33.8 percent of the budget to unapproved
  projects compared to the 49 percent for FY 18/19, the baseline year. Similarly, the Government has prepared Education and Health sector plans to
  improve the National Financial Management Information System (NFMIS) coverage. A draft Integrated Financial Management Information and
  System (IFMIS) implementation plan has been approved. Testing and configuration will start with piloting at Haripur and Abbottabad during the first
  phase. Finally, the Output-Based Budgeting departments now include measurable gender key performance indicators (KPIs) and report on number
  of females reached by their services. At least 12 departments have KPI disaggregated by gender. This exceeds the targeted 6 departments for Year
  1. Satisfactory progress has also been made on strengthening government capacity for management of dedicated funds.
  Component 2: Technical Assistance and Capacity Building (IPF)
  Component 2.1: Providing Foundational Support for e-Government Functionality
  Progress in this component is satisfactory. The KP team has completed all activities required and held several consultations with relevant
  stakeholders including Minister Finance, KBITB, PMRU and KPRMP. Documentation of “as is” for 86 sub processes including summary processes
  and “to be” for 40 processes has been completed.
  Component 2.2: Program Coordination: Program Coordination is satisfactory. The Coordination Unit is appropriately staffed, with the exception
  of the Monitoring and Evaluation Specialist which is currently under recruitment.
