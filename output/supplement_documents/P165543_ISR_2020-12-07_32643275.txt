 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The Project will be coordinated by a Coordinating Project Management Unit (CPMU) with representation from the Ministry of Home Affairs (MoHA),
  Ministry of Villages (MoV), Bappenas and Kemenko PMK. The CPMU has been established with representation from all ministries. After the loan
  became effective on February 19, 2020, the first disbursement amounted USD 5.17million (1.7% of the loan) has been made on June 2020 as an
  initial advance. MoHA and MoV have started the procurement of the key consultancy contracts, though it experienced delay in the process due to
  the pandemic (COVID-19) which required a significant diversion of state budget resources to align with the priority programs for addressing the
  COVID-19.

  MoHA, as the Executing Agency (EA), is focused on strengthening the capacity of the village government for improved quality of spending
  (Component 1). This component will focus on developing an improved learning management system (LMS) for the Ministry to improve the
  effectiveness of capacity building of village governments as well as supporting institutions. The learning management system will (i) offer expanded
  content to village governments; (ii) deliver content in a variety of learning methods (e.g. face to face learning, facilitated learning, mentoring, on the
  job training, self-paced learning, etc.); and (iii) track the progress and capacity of village government officials. The learning management system will
  include training to village government institutions (village apparat, village council), as well as supporting institutions (sub-district, district).

  MoV is supporting activities related to improving community empowerment, improving bottom-up participation in the village governance cycle, and
  strengthening systems of social accountability (Component 2). This component supports three key transformative agendas for MoV, namely: (i)
  strengthening inclusion and empowerment of communities by improving the capacity and capabilities of the facilitators (pendamping desa and
  pendamping local desa) and introducing and replicating models for empowerment; (ii) supporting MoV in the rollout of digital service delivery
  modalities; and (iii) enabling village development through data systems and evidence based policy making.

  Bappenas and Kemenko PMK will be implementing activities under Component 4 of the project. Bappenas will implement activities related to (i)
  setting up an Integrated Data and Information System on Villages (InfoDesa), and (ii) improving the evidence base for village development and
  capacity building interventions, including through an Impact Evaluation. Kemenko PMK will implement activities related to the establishment of a
  National Coordination Platform.
