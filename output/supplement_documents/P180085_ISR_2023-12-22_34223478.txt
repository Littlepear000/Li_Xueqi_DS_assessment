1. OBJECTIVE


 1.1 Development Objective
   Original Development Objective (Approved as part of Approval package on 28-Sep-2023)
   The Project Development Objective is to improve country statistical performance, regional harmonization, data access
   and use, and to enhance modernization of the statistical system in participating countries.

   Has the Development Objective been changed since Board Approval of the Project Objective?

   No

4. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  The Project was approved on September 28, 2023. The Financing Agreements are signed for Central African Republic on
  October 17, 2023 and declaration of effectivnes date is on January 16, 2024; Chad on October 19, 2023 and declaraton of
  effectivnes date is on January 18, 2024, Central African Economic and Monetary Community (Communauté Économique et
  Monétaire de l'Afrique Centrale, CEMAC) signed on November 11, 2023 and declaration of effectinvess deadline is on March
  18, 2024. The Financing Agreement for Cameroon has not been signed yet due to government's internal processes.
  Progress is made in the process of completing the actions for effectiveness, namely: recruiting key staff for the project
  implementation Unit (PIU), preparing the project operations manual, subsidiary agreements, and the legal opinions for
  the financing agreements. Chad and the Central African Republic are very close to declare project effectiveness.
