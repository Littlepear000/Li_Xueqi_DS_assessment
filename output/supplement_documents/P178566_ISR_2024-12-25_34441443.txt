4. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  This ISR covers phase 1 of the FSRP MPA, which was approved in June 2022, including Ethiopia ($600m), Madagascar
  ($158m), IGAD ($25m) and CCARDESA ($5m). Ethiopia also received commitment of US65.5 million from the following
  donors: Germany (KfW), Netherlands, European Union and USAID. Madagascar received Additional Financing of US$70
  million in June 2024, bringing its total financing to US$228 million.
  ETHIOPIA:
       • Project implementation progress continues to be rated Moderately Satisfactory. Implementation progress has
           accelerated since the last mission due to the client executing the key actions agreed, including where possible
           decentralizing selected activities from the regions to the woredas; conducting security risk assessments to guide
           implementation; cultivating ownership by local communities, local authorities, elders and grassroots leaders
           through trust-building initiatives; and recruitment of 71 procurement specialists to strengthen capacity.
       • Despite these measures, conflict and insecurity remains a challenge in some project woreda’s, especially in the
           two largest regions – Oromia and Amhara. This has affected the overall ability of the coordination teams to
           supervise and monitor activities.
       • The project has achieved important results since effectiveness. Of the targeted 569,978 direct beneficiaries to be
           reached by the second year, the project has reached 520,155 individuals with extension and advisory services,
           crop and water management techniques, enterprise development support, and access to new technologies
           (climate-smart, nutrition sensitive, and gender sensitive), etc. Key results during the report period include: (i)
           139,387 farmers have adopted climate resilience-enhancing technologies and practices; (ii) 965 tons of breeder
           and pre-basic seed has been generated for selected crops; (iii) 73 technologies have been developed and
           transferred to extension services; (iv) completion of SSI works in 131 sub-projects covering 1,075.93 hectares (ha)
           and benefiting 3,045 households, including 704 female-headed households; (v) 159 irrigation water user
           associations have been legally registered with 80% of members paying fees; and (vi) about 298 SMEs supported
           by the project are actively running their businesses.

  MADAGASCAR:
    • Project implementation in Madagascar is rated Satisfactory. On June 6, 2024, the Bank's Board of Directors
       approved an additional financing (AF) of US$70 million, which will finance: (i) response to locust crisis; (ii) animal
       restocking; and (iii) the general agricultural census, the first in 20 year.
    • The project has achieved important results since effectiveness. Under the Project’s home-grown school feeding
       activity, financed in part by GAFSP, about 530 schools covering more than 160,000 students are receiving locally
       sourced school meals, which is good progress towards the goal of reaching 800 schools and 240,000 students
       over three years. In addition, 84 producer organizations have to date received inputs, equipment, and training,

  IGAD:
     • Project implementation under IGAD is rated Satisfactory. IGAD has ably demonstrated the capacity to identify
        and support the policy processes that are likely to have a significant and strategic impact on the food systems’
        policy environment.
     • Key results achieved include support for the Africa Soil Summit and the review of the Comprehensive Africa
        Agriculture Development Program (CAADP), both contributing towards the objective of harmonized policy
        frameworks and legislation. However, greater efforts are needed to deliver on the remaining two objectives
        (strengthening or establishing regional centers of leadership (RCoLs) in participating countries and establishing
        regional knowledge sharing networks). The networks cover themes such as trade, climate-smart technology,
        research, livestock, and groundwater management.

  CCARDESA:
     • Project implementation under CCARDESA is rated Satisfactory.
     • Key results achieved since effectiveness include establishment of a knowledge network focused on maximizing
        the use of innovative climate-smart technologies and applying a policy designed to harmonize seed policies
        across the region. CCARDESA has demonstrated adequate capacity to adapt and respond to the emerging policy
        processes, for example, establishing a regional hub focused on soil health and fertilizer.

4.3 Multiphase Programmatic Approach (MPA) Status (Public Disclosure)
  In June 2022, the Board approved IDA commitment of US$1.691 billion to support the MPA. The approval was together
  with Phase 1 (US$788 million), which included Ethiopia (US$600 million), Madagascar (US$158 million), IGAD (US$25
  million) and CCARDESA (US$5 million). Ethiopia also received a commitment of S$65.5 million from the following
  development partners: Germany (KfW), the Netherlands, the European Union, and USAID. Madagascar received
  Additional Financing of US$70 million in June 2024, bringing its total financing to US$228 million.

  Phase 2 was approved on May 31, 2023 with a financial commitment of US$300 million to Tanzania. Additional funding of
  US$8.3 million from the Food Systems 2030 Multi-Donor Trust Fund (FS 2030 MDTF) was approved for Tanzania in
  September 2024, bringing its total financing to US$308.3 million.

  Phase 3 ($603 million) was also approved on May 31, 2023, including Kenya ($150 million), Comoros ($40 million),
  Somalia ($150 million), Malawi ($250 million), and the African Union Commission ($13 million). Malawi also received a
  pledge of US$15 million from the Global Agriculture Food Security Program (GAFSP) Trust Fund, while Comoros received
  a grant of US$3 million from the Problue Trust Fund.

  At the same time (May 31, 2023), the Board approved an increase in the overall MPA envelope to US$2.75 billion.

  The Government of Japan has announced a contribution of US$13 million through the FS 2030 MDTF to support MPA in
  the areas of nutrition, gender and remote sensing/early warning systems.
