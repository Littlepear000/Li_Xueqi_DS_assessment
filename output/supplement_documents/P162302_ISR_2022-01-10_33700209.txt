 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  Component 1: Program for Results (PforR) Results Area 1:Revenue Mobilization.
  Disbursement Linked Indicator (DLI) #1: Registered Taxpayers who filed General Sales Tax on Services (GSTS) taxes in previous year
  : Collection of the province’s taxes has improved significantly from a baseline of Rs.14.30 billion in FY2018/19 exceeding targets in year 1 and 2.
  Year 3 is on track to exceed targets as well, given that Rs. 18.75 billion has been collected as of November 2021. As of November 2021, the
  registered Services Sales taxpayers who filed returns stood at 90 percent, exceeding the target (65%) for Year 3.
  DLI#2: Database integration in tax administration: The Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Revenue Authority (KPRA) has signed a Memorandum of
  Understanding (MoU) with four withholding agents, exceeding the target of three withholding agents for Year 3. out of a target of three withholding
  agents. The agents include the KP Information Technology Board , Water and Sanitation Services Peshawar, KP Magyar OLaj (MoL), and KP AG
  office. Work is underway to assess the readiness of Board of Revenue (BOR) and Excise, Taxation and Narcotics Control Department (ENTCD) for
  database linkage with KPRA (target for Year 3).
  DLI#3: Cities with updated UIPT surveys, valuations and digitized records: Progress in this DLI has been severely affected by the COVID 19
  pandemic, given the requirement for extensive field work before digitization. The KP ETNCD has now initiated the work in two districts of
  Abbottabad and Nowshera (which includes five cities), and is on target to achieve the DLR targets within Q1 of 2022.
  Results Area 2: Public Resource Management.
  DLI#4:Cash management based on regular consolidation of cash balances and regular cash plans: There is some progress in this area
  now following a slow start. Overall, the Government is making satisfactory efforts to assess the volume of cash deposits in commercial banks, as a
  first step to reduce the amount of cash deposits held by Government in commercial banks. The stock take of existing number of accounts and
  deposits has been initiated with the State Bank Pakistan. The Cash Management Policy was approved by the Provincial Cabinet, and therefore DLR
  #4.1 has been achieved. However, monthly consolidation of cash balances takes place in only three (Provincial Account 1 and 2 and District
  Account 4) out of nine government accounts/departments (DLR#2) The GoKP has initiated measures to fast-track implementation of the Cash
  Management Policy, and both DLR #4.2 and DLR #4.3 are likely be achieved in the first quarter of Calendar Year 2022.
  DLI#5: Annual Development Plan (ADP) funds allocated to Unapproved Projects (excluding Foreign Project Assistance): This DLI has
  sustained the same momentum as in previous years. There is satisfactory progress in reducing the share of Annual Development Program funds
  allocated to unapproved projects (excluding Foreign Project Assistance). The FY20/21 budget allocates 30 percent of the budget to unapproved
  projects compared to the 49 percent for FY 18/19, the baseline year. In FY 21/22 preliminary reports from the Planning and Development
  Department (P&DD) has only 16 percent of the budget allocated for unapproved projects against a target of 35 percent. Therefore, DLR #5.3 is
  likely to be achieved.
  DLI#6: Tehsil Minicipal Administrations (TMAs) using NFMIS to record transactions
  There is satisfactory progress in this DLI. The Government has prepared education and health sector plans to improve the National Financial
  Management Information System (NFMIS) coverage. A NFMIS decentralization plan has been approved by the provincial Cabinet. An NFMIS has
  been developed, tested and fully functional (DLR#6.1). About 20 Tehsil Municipal Administrations have started using NFMIS to record payment,
  therefore meeting and exceeding the target for DLR #6.2 (10 Tehsil) and DLR #6.3 (20 Tehsil).
