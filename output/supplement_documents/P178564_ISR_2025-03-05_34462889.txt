4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  The project has achieved a disbursement rate of 63.41%, equivalent to $50.73 million12. The mid-term evaluation report
  was discussed with INEC and the PIU . The report recommends a restructure of the project to reassign unused funds from
  Component 1 to reinforce INEC’s statistical capacity and new conjunctural data production. Below is the component-wise
  progress as of the mission date:

  Component 1-Population and Housing Census: The data collection and processing for the Population and Housing Census
  have concluded, with a pending administrative closure. Census results have been public since September 2023, accessible
  via the INEC website and a dedicated platform, presenting demographic profiles, historical series, and specific themes. The
  REDATAM platform was published in October, allowing users to build custom tables. The census is being documented
  through the DDI standard and will be published in INEC's ANDA catalog by March 2025. Population projections are
  complete. Research on the LGBT+ population is in its final stage, and a population with disability study is planned. An
  independent evaluation coordinated by UNFPA and CEPAL was delivered on November 5, 2024. An additional in-deep
  evaluation focused on data processing and imputation will be delivered to INEC in early 2025. The Unsatisfied Basic Needs
  Index (NBI) new estimation was updated through a methodological review.

  Component 2- Strengthening Statistical Production from Surveys: This component includes household, economic, producer
  and consumer price indices, and agricultural surveys.

  Household Income and Expenditure Survey (ENIGHUR): Cartography was done in November, and data collection began in
  December 2024, and is expected to conclude in November 2025

  Employment Survey (ENCIET): The hiring and training of personnel for the ENCIET happened in October 2024, and the
  national rollout began in November 2024 and is expected to end in October 2025. The publication of ENCIET results is
  expected by the first semester of 2026

  Agricultural Survey (ESPAC): Data collection of the economic module ends, and the new module on machinery and
  equipment will start this September.

  Component 3- Strengthening Statistical Production from Administrative Records: Most of the intermediate-level indicators
  for administrative records have progressed in reporting and are close to reaching the target.

  Eight improvement plans for various registries have been finalized. The quality of eight administrative records has
  improved, including those for people with disabilities, marital status, births, deaths, and student records. There has been
  a 55% progress in making administrative data available through visualization.
