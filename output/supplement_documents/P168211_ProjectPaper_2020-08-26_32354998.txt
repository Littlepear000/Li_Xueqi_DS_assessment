I. PROGRAM STATUS AND RATIONALE FOR RESTRUCTURING

1. The Program became effective on July 1, 2020. The implementation of the Program, including the Program Action
Plan, is overall on track. The target for Disbursement Linked Result (DLR) 5.1 under the Disbursement Linked Indicator 5
(Adequate electricity supply is received by BT from Sangtuda-1) was met. Specifically, by June 30, 2020, Barqi Tojik Open
Joint Stock Holding Company (BT) received 1,090 GWh of electricity from Sangtuda-1 hydropower plant compared to the
target of 1,000 GWh. All DLR targets for December 31, 2020 are also expected to be met.

2. The restructuring is required for revision of the deadline for achievement of the following DLRs due to impact of
COVID-19 on the speed of implementation of the required measures.

      DLR 2.1: By June 30, 2020, the Recipient converts into equity ownership of BT the outstanding principal amounts
       and interest payable by BT for ten (10) loans under Group 1 Subsidiary Agreements).
      DLR 2.2: By June 30, 2020, the terms of any new financing received by BT from the Ministry of Finance (MOF) are
       aligned with the terms in the respective legal agreements between the Recipient and the financiers).
      DLR 8.1: By December 31, 2020, supervisory boards are functional and specialized committees (audit and
       compensation) are formed at BT generation, transmission and distribution companies, comprising of members of
       supervisory boards and chaired by independent supervisory board members.

3. The revision of the deadline is justified and was requested by the MOF in its letter No. 515-19/519, dated June 29,
2020. No other changes are proposed as part of this restructuring.


II. DESCRIPTION OF PROPOSED CHANGES

4. It is proposed to revise the deadline for achievement of DLR 2.1 and DLR 2.2 till October 30, 2020 compared to MOF
request of August 30, 2020 to allow sufficient time for appropriate reflection of the revisions in the relevant financial
statements of BT considering longer than anticipated disruption of the work processes due to COVID-19. The deadline
for DLR 8.1 is revised to March 31, 2021 to allow sufficient time to introduce the specified elements of corporate
governance.

   DLR 2.1: By June 30, 2020, the Recipient converts into equity ownership of BT the outstanding principal amounts
    and interest payable by BT for ten (10) loans under Group 1 Subsidiary Agreements. The deadline is proposed to be
    revised from June 30, 2020 to October 30, 2020.
   DLR 2.2: By June 30, 2020, the terms of any new financing received by BT from MOF are aligned with the terms in
    the respective legal agreements between the Recipient and the financiers. The deadline is proposed to be revised
    from June 30, 2020 to October 30, 2020.
   DLR 8.1: By December 31, 2020, supervisory boards are functional and specialized committees (audit and
    compensation) are formed at BT generation, transmission and distribution companies, comprising of members of
    supervisory boards and chaired by independent supervisory board members. The deadline is proposed to be revised
    from December 31, 2020 to October 30, 2020 to March 31, 2021.

5. The Government Decree authorizing conversion of all Group 1 Subsidiary Agreements into equity ownership of BT
has been prepared and is currently pending inclusion into the agenda of the Government session of September 2020.
After approval of the Decree, BT will proceed with conversion and appropriate reflection of those transactions in BT’s
chart of accounts. Thereafter, BT will hire an independent verification agent (IVA), which will prepare a report to be
                               The World Bank
                               Tajikistan Power Utility Financial Recovery (P168211)


        submitted to BT and the Bank validating whether the conversion was completed consistent with the target and in
        accordance with the applicable legal framework and accounting rules.

        6. The Supervisory Boards for the newly-created electricity transmission and distribution companies have been
        established. The Government is currently in the process of appointing the management of those companies and then the
        establishment of specialized committees will commence.
