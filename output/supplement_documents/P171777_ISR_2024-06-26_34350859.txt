4. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  Some important progress has been achieved in this project in recent months:
  Component 1:
     •       the population and housing census's fieldwork was completed in May 2024 and the results should be released
         within the next 6 to 12 months.
     •       The Institute of Statistics (INSTAD) now has a board of directors appointed by the ministerial decree and has
         held 4 meetings in recent months. Some bylaws have been adopted by the board of directors and are now
         following administrative procedure for ministerial adoption.
     •       INSTAD is planning for the next household budget survey to be carried out in Fall 2024 and the Economic
         Census to be carried out over the next 9-12 months.
     •       The production of Economic Statistics has seen the release of a number of new indicators in the recent
         months including the new and updated consumer price index. The current plan involves the production and
         release of the index of construction costs and index of production costs of services, and the index of industrial
         production to be released in 2024, and the index of prices of industrial production in 2025
  Component 2:
     •       The new quasi-accounting macroeconomic forecasting model, supported by Afristat, is completed with the
         official launch planned for September 2024, and macroeconomic forecasts using this model are expected to start
         in Q4 2024. The preparation of the Social Accounting Matrix for the CGE model for 2018 should be finished by
         September 2024, with the CGE model delivery scheduled for Q1 2025. The debt sustainability analysis model
         development will be assisted by the World Bank's Debt Department, with training sessions planned for
         September 2024.
     •       Technical assistance for the development of sectoral MTEFs began with an exploratory mission in September
         2023, followed by additional missions, revealing that key prerequisites for credible medium-term expenditure
         frameworks are not yet in place. A proposed roadmap for implementing program budgets, detailing necessary
         preliminary actions from July 2024 to December 2025, has been welcomed and is consistent with the
         government's overall public finance reform strategy.
     •       The modernization of the public debt management system is progressing well, with advanced training on
         SYGADE 6 features and over 70% of the database validated. A workshop on debt data validation is scheduled for
         June 2024, and technical assistance for the prepapartion of a Debt Management Strategy is planned for
         September 2024, pending confirmation.
     •       The capacity building for project design, monitoring, and supervision is progressing satisfactorily. Training and
         supervision have continued since 2021, with additional training on GEMS and Power BI planned for 2024, and the
         deployment of IT solutions and studies on bilateral agreements scheduled for 2025. The cost-benefit analysis
         training is pending implementation despite hiring a local consultant.
