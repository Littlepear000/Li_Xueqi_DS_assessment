 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The second supervision mission was carried out on January 16-20, 2023. In 2022, efforts were made to update management documents and

  Action Plan was agreed upon for the next six months, and the Operational Plan for 2023. The next supervision mission is scheduled for mid-
  May 2023.
