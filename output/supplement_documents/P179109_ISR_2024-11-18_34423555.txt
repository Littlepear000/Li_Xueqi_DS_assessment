4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  Overall. The project implementation has been overall on track since the last implementation support mission of SOP1 in
  June 2024. All required cofinancings were approved by other donors. The SOP1 aims at financing the first phase of Port
  Boingoma investment, which is cofinanced by the AFD. The SOP2 supports the remaining part of Port Boingoma
  investment and secondary ports. The SOP2 was approved by the Bank in March 2024, followed by the other two
  cofinanciers: AfDB in July 2024 and IsDB in October 2024.
   Component 1. Port Boingoma. The procurement for Port Boingoma works (including two lots) was completed. The first
  phase of the port works (i.e., mobilization and construction of breakwater) was formally launched on July 29, 2024. The
  whole port works are expected to be completed by August 2027.
  Component 2. Secondary ports and new passenger boat program. The preparation of the detailed design, ESIA and RAP
  for the secondary ports is ongoing.
  Component 3. Implementation support/Technical assistance. Under SOP2, TA for maritime safety is supported. Draft
  TOR were prepared. Local community activities at coastal areas will also be supported. For women empowerment, a
  women centered maritime safety committee will be created.
