I.       PROJECT STATUS AND RATIONALE FOR RESTRUCTURING

     1. This Restructuring Paper seeks Management approval for a Level 2 restructuring which proposes a change
     to the project’s results framework as detailed in Section II below.

     A. PROJECT STATUS:

     2. The Harmonizing and Improving Statistics in West Africa (HISWA) project was approved by the World Bank
     Executive Directors on March 26, 2020, with an amount of US$379 million equivalent to strengthen the statistical
     systems of participating countries and regional bodies in Africa to harmonize, produce, disseminate and enhance
     the use of core economic and social statistics. The Project provides IDA Grants and IDA Credits to the African
     Union (AU), the Economic Community of West African States (ECOWAS); and to seven project participating
     countries in West Africa: Burkina Faso, Cabo Verde, Cote d’Ivoire, Ghana, Liberia, Sierra Leone, and Togo.

     3. Effectiveness was declared in all project’s participating countries and regional organizations. Both progress
     toward the project development objective (PDO) and implementation progress are rated Satisfactory (S) in the
     last Implementation Status and Results Report, dated June 16, 2024. The PDO continues to be relevant and
     achievable, despite the slow start of project implementation noted during the project mid-term review (MTR),
     dated on May 31, 2024, due to the occurrence of the Coronavirus Disease (COVID-19) pandemic. Activity
     implementation has since then accelerated and currently, the project is on track to achieving its PDO by the
     current project closing date of December 31, 2025.

     4. Disbursements: Overall disbursements currently stand at US$277.82 million, or 74 percent, with some
     variations among the participating countries and regional organizations. (i) 69.0 percent in African Union; (ii)
     60.5 percent in ECOWAS; (iii) 71.4 percent in Burkina Faso; (iv)53.4 percent in Cabo Verde; (v) 70.7 percent in
     Cote d’Ivoire; (vi) 81.8 percent in Ghana; (vii) 79.0 percent in Liberia; (viii) 76.5 percent in Sierra Leone; (ix) 49.1
     percent in Togo. Disbursement in Togo was low because activities were frozen for almost one year to resolve
     environmental and social (E&S) safeguard issues that arose during project implementation, the low
     disbursement in the regional organizations (ECOWAS and AU) were mainly due to administrative burdens that
     were caused in implementing the projects, and finally, Cabo Verde had low disbursement due to late start of
     project activities that were caused due to the COVID-19 pandemic restrictions and Project Implementation Unit
     (PIU) staffing.

     5. Financial Management performance was rated Satisfactory on July 29, 2024. The project bookkeeping is up
     to date and transactions are adequately documented. The interim financial reports' quality is satisfactory; the
     external auditors have issued unqualified opinions on the 2023 financial statements for all participating entities
     and the annual work programs have been submitted on time. The project’s staff and accounting systems are
     adequate. However, the project’s assets, mainly for those assigned to the implementing agencies have not yet
     fully codified in some participating countries. This has been addressed through the implementation of an action
     plans including organizing monthly technical discussion to address challenges, monitoring of contracts and
     procurement activities to avoid delay in activities implementation.

     6. The procurement performance has been upgraded from Moderately Satisfactory to Satisfactory on June 9,
     2024. There has been significant progress from the last assessment. Also, the Procurement policies (regulations)

8. Monitoring and Evaluation (M&E): The Project Results Framework was prepared using a core set of PDO-level
   indicators and intermediate level indicators with baselines and end target values at the country and regional
   levels to assess whether implementation of the project is on track to achieve its objectives and expected results.
   Therefore, the PDO and project outcome and output indicators are standardized across the seven project
   participating countries. Therefore, progress in project implementation statuses is documented in progress
   reports that the PIUs prepared at biannual basis both at the country and regional levels. Monthly technical
   discussions also allow the task teams to closely monitor the progress toward achieving the PDO.

9. Progress towards PDO indicators: Compared to the baselines, out of the seven PDO indicators, at the aggregate
   level two PDO indicators are overachieved. These include: (i) PDO Indicator 3: Share of comparable core
   economic and social statistics indicators produced and available for open access (69.29 percent actual value
   compared to the end target value of 50 percent) and (ii) PDO Indicator 5 : Share of statistical products produced
   and published by the National Statistics Office (NSO) following international standards and a predetermined
   release calendar (73.86 percent actual value compared to the end target value of 62.29 percent). The remaining
   four PDO level indicators are on track to be achieved before the project closing date. These include: (i) PDO
   Indicator 1: Modified Statistical capacity index1 (SCI 2.0) (disaggregated by participating countries) (70.8 percent
   actual value compared to the end target value of 71.43 percent); (ii) PDO Indicator 4: Share of comparable core
   economic and social statistics datasets produced and available for open access (43 percent actual value
   compared to the end target value of 50 percent); (iii)PDO Indictor 6: Share of countries that produce and
   disseminate at least two analytical reports using survey, macro or administrative data (85.71 percent actual
   value compared to the end target value of 100 percent); (iv) and PDO Indicator 7: Increase in user satisfaction
   index (3.9 percent actual value compared to the end target value of 10 percent).

      B. Rationale for Restructuring

      11. This level 2 project restructuring is proposed based on the project mid-term review recommendations of the
      project regional coordination committee (RCC). The meeting was held from March 25 to 29, 2024 in Cotonou,
      Benin. The RCC was established by ECOWAS to coordinate the project implementation. It is comprised of
      representatives of all projects participating ECOWAS countries, and additionally included Mauritania, regional
      bodies, as well as representatives of the World Bank and other regional and international institutions involved
      in the development of statistics in the region. The RCC reviewed the performance of the project activities and
      identified the data entry error of the Results Framework and proposed to be adjusted with the correct baseline
      and end target values. Additionally, it was proposed to remove the ICI indicator since this activity’s improvement
      was not implemented by all project participating countries and therefore difficult to monitor the progress at the
      regional level.

      12. The proposed restructuring aims to introduce the two changes of the project’s results framework:

         -     Removing one PDO level indicator: The PDO level Indicator 2, share of beneficiary countries (National
               Statistical Office) that score at least 30 out of 50 for the ICI. The indicator was included in the project to
               assess the institutional capacity regarding human resources, management structure, and knowledge and
               learning of project participating countries.
         -     Correcting data entry errors that occurred in the aggregate baseline and end target values of three PDO
               level indicators (PDO Indictor 1 - Modified Statistical Capacity Index (SCI 2.0) (disaggregated by
               participating countries) (percentage); PDO Indicator 5 - Share of statistical products produced and
               published by the NSO following international standards and a predetermined release calendar; PDO
               Indictor 6 - Share of countries that produce and disseminate at least two analytical reports using survey,
               macro or administrative data- ) and one intermediate level indicator (indicator 5 - National Statistical
               System (NSS) staff trained to produce welfare and macroeconomic statistics ).

13. The proposed changes are detailed in the Results Framework section of the Restructuring Paper.

      14. This paper seeks management approval for a level 2 restructuring with the following proposed changes:

15. PDO 1: Strengthening statistical systems. Two composite indices, the modified statistical capacity index
(SCI 2.0) and the ICI, were designed to measure the project's contribution to strengthening the participating
countries' statistical systems. The first assesses the methodologies for data production and their quality and
availability, and the second assesses institutional capacity regarding human resources, management structure,
and knowledge and learning.

       PDO Indicator 1: Modified Statistical capacity index (SCI 2.0). Modified Statistical Capacity Index (SCI
       2.0) (disaggregated by participating countries) (percentage). Proposed changes – Due to data entry error
       at the aggregate level, the baseline and end target values should be recalculated as the average values
       of the seven project participating countries. Therefore, the aggregate baseline value should be 59.29
       instead of 30, and the end target value should be 71.43 instead of 50.

       PDO Indicator 2: Share of beneficiary countries (National Statistical Office) that score at least 30 out of
       50 for the ICI. This indicator is proposed to be removed from the results framework as explained in
       paragraph 10.

16. PDO 2: Production and dissemination of harmonized statistics. Three indicators will also be used to monitor
production and dissemination activities:

       PDO Indicator 3: Share of comparable core economic and social statistics indicators produced and
       available for open access. The core economic and social statistics to be considered are the following: (i)
       Population size by region and areas of residence, (ii) Poverty indices, (iii) Inflation rates, (iv) Production
       volume of main staple crops, (v) Value added of Small and Medium Enterprises and Micro Small and
       Medium Enterprises, (vi) Gross domestic product (GDP) growth and (vii) underemployment rate. Open
       access is available to the users on a website. Statistics are comparable when produced using similar
       methodologies. For each participating country, the indicator's value is the number of comparable core
       economic and social statistics indicators produced and available for open access divided by the total
       number of comparable core economic and social statistics indicators considered for the measure of the
       indicator (7). Thus, no change is proposed for this indicator.

       PDO Indicator 4: Share of comparable core economic and social statistics datasets produced and
       available for open access. Each project-participating country is expected to produce seven datasets,
       including (i) An anonymized cartography dataset, (ii) An anonymized household poverty survey, (iii) A
       Consumer Price Index (CPI) dataset, (iv) An anonymized agricultural census or annual survey dataset, (v)
       Anonymized updated business registry, (vi) Input-output tables, and (vii) Anonymized labor force surveys
       dataset. Open access is available to the users on a website or upon request. Statistics are comparable
       when produced using similar methodologies. For each participating country, the indicator's value is the
       number of comparable core economic and social statistics datasets produced and available for open
       access divided by the total number of comparable core economic and social statistics datasets considered
       for the measure of the indicator (7). Thus, no change is proposed for this indicator.

       PDO Indicator 5: Share of statistical products produced and published by the NSO following
       international standards and a predetermined release calendar – Core statistics products include (i)
       household poverty survey (Poverty indices), (ii) CPI (Inflation rate), (iii) agricultural census and annual
       survey data (agricultural production), (iv) economic census and annual survey data, (v) national accounts
                                                        4
      The World Bank

      Harmonizing and Improving Statistics in West Africa (P169265)
       (GDP growth rate), and (vi) labor force surveys (underemployment rate). For each participating country,
       the indicator's value is the number of statistical products produced and published by the NSO following
       international standards and a predetermined release calendar divided by the total number of statistical
       products considered for the measure of the indicator. Therefore, the baseline and end target value at
       regional levels should be adjusted from 0 to 24 for baseline value and end target value should be
       adjusted from 50 to 62.29 for the end target.

       PDO Indictor 6: Share of countries that produce and disseminate at least two analytical reports using
       survey, macro or administrative data- The analytical reports are those produced using population data,
       household surveys, labor force surveys, enterprise or agricultural surveys data, macroeconomic data
       including national accounts, and price data. The reports include but are not limited to economic updates,
       economic projections, and shock policy impact analyses. Participating countries have been supported in
       the dissemination strategies of censuses and surveys and using data generated by the project. As a result,
       each country is expected to produce at least two reports under the project. Therefore, the end target
       value should be adjusted from 80 to 100 percent at the aggregate level.

       PDO Indicator 7: Increase in user satisfaction index (Percentage): This indicator is computed from user
       surveys implemented in each participating country. The team has developed a harmonized
       questionnaire, which is being used for all participants countries. A baseline survey will be conducted in
       the project's first year, and follow-up surveys will be conducted in years three and five. The indicator
       measures the change in percentage points from baseline to follow-up surveys. In each participating
       country, the baseline value of the indicator is 0, and the end target value is 10. Therefore, no change is
       required for this indicator.

17. Proposed changes under the Intermediate Level Indicators: The progress toward achieving intermediate
level indicators is satisfactory at the countries and regional level. However, a revision of end target value of
intermediate level indicator 5 is proposed as there was an error with data entry during project preparation.
Therefore, IP indicator 5 - National Statistical System (NSS) staff trained to produce welfare and
macroeconomic statistics - the end target values at the aggregate level should 69 instead of 60.
