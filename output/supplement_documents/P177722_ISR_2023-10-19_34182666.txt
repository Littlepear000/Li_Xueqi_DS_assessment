 Project Development Objectives

  Project Development Objective (from Project Appraisal Document)
  The Project Development Objective is to strengthen deposit insurance, promote bank lending to MSMEs, and boost MSME integration in value
  chains.

  No

 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The project is expected to be declared effective in the fall of 2023. The MoF PIU confirmed that the Financing Agreement had been reviewed and
  agreed upon with all the relevant ministries and agencies of the Republic of Tajikistan and is awaiting the President's written authorization for the
  Minister of Finance to sign the Financing Agreement.
