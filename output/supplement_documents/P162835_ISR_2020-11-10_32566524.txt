 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  - The project became effective in Jan 2020. A physical mission never took place due to the Covid-19 pandemic and the associated travel
  restrictions, however, the task team has been ensuring that implementation is conducted smoothly through meeting remotely with the implementing
  entity and other stakeholders.
  - Although implementation is very challenging due to the pandemic, the project disbursed 41% of the total amount (from Component 1). Delays are
  affecting the implementation of Components 2 and 3 due to their innovative nature and the Covid-19 pandemic.
