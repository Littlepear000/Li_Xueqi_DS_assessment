 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The progress towards achievement of the Project Development Objective (PDO) is rated as Satisfactory given that there are no material risks
  observed currently that would undermine achievement of the PDO. The Project Implementation Progress (IP) rating is maintained at ‘Moderately
  Satisfactory’. This primarily reflects the delay in initiation of the passive infrastructure deployments financed under Subcomponent 1.1 and the low
  disbursement levels. Project activities are expected to pick up pace with the effective launch of the first passive infrastructure deployments and the
  procurement of the active equipment.
