 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  Although the project became effective in September, 2022, it is delayed pending an authorizing decree from the Government. Delays due to
  processing (especially during the preparation and approval of the feasibility study) and the authorizing decree have pushed NSDS objectives off-
  track (including the agriculture monitoring system, modernizing the calculation of GDP, preparation of complete business registers, and preparations
  for the population census).
  In June 2022, the client published the POM acceptable to the World Bank, and conducted the competitive process for selecting PMU team
  members with ToR's acceptable to the World Bank. All project-related documents are available here:
  https://stat.uz/en/legal-acts/strengthening-the-statistical-system-of-uzbekistan-project
  and the announcements for PMU members are available here:
  https://stat.uz/en/press-center/news-of-committee/22997-e-lon64567457474-3
