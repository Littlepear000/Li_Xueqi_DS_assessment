 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  This ISR update was issued to record the upgrade of the safeguard rating from Moderately Unsatisfactory (MU) to Moderately Satisfactory (MS)
  following the completion of several safeguard-related milestones agreed to with the government. The overall Implementation Progress (IP) of the
  project itself is on the right track and progressing well towards achieving its PDO. The progress towards attaining the PDO and the IP continued to
  be Satisfactory and Moderately Satisfactory respectively. Despite the continued challenges of COVID-19 and some localized conflicts, the project
  has continued to make progress since May 2021. A full ISR will be prepared upon conclusion of the next Implementation Support Mission currently
  scheduled for December 2021.
