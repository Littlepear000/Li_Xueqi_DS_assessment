4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  Recommencement of cash transfers. In January 2024, the government temporarily suspended the cash transfer program
  and other social investment programs, to conduct a review. An inter-ministerial review panel chaired by the Honorable
  Minister, Federal Ministry of Finance, was convened, to review the delivery of the social investment programmes and
  recommend measures to strengthen their delivery and build trust in the system. The panel announced the resumption of
  the cash transfers in March 2024. Cash transfers have commenced with increased focus on using National Identity
  Number (NIN), to validate each recipient of the cash transfers.
  Oversight function of inter-ministerial review panel. Following the resumption of the cash transfer program, the review
  panel continues to provide oversight and support to the implementation of the cash transfer program.
  Extending the project closing date. A restructuring to extend the project has been agreed to and is being processed.
