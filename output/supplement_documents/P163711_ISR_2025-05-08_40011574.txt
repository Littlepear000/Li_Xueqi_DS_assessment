4. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  The overall implementation progress rating has been upgraded from Moderately Unsatisfactory to Moderately
  Satisfactory following improvements in project execution and resolution of key bottlenecks. The recent cancellation of
  major activities under Component 2 has allowed the implementing agencies to concentrate on the remaining project
  activities.
  The State Tax Service is preparing to start implementation of the remaining two major consultancies: technical assistance
  to strengthen human resource management, internal controls, and corruption risk detection (expected start of
  implementation - Q3, 2025) and consulting services to support implementation of ISNAK-2 (expected start of
  implementation - Q4, 2025).
  The National Statistical Committee has advanced toward installation of the new data center and is working with the
  Twinning Partner to finalize business processes reengineering. Both activities are crucial to achievement of relevant PDO
  indicators.
  These developments mark a positive shift in project momentum. Continued attention to timely procurement, adherence
  to implementation schedule, and recruitment of the new STS PIU manager are critical to sustaining progress.
