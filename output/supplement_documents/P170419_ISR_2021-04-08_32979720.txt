 Program Development Objectives

  PDO Table
  Program Development Objective (from Program Appraisal Document)
  The PDO is to increase the economic inclusion of youth in rural areas and the marketing efficiency and environmental sustainability of agri-food
  value chains in the Program area.

 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  Following approval by the Board on December 15, 2020 and signing of the Loan Agreement on December 22, 2020, the loan became effective on
  February 25, 2021 following the signing of the inter-institutional framework agreement on wholesale markets between the four ministries involved
  (Ministries of Agriculture, Interior, Industry and Finance)-- an effectiveness condition. The disbursement of the advance (EUR53.55 millions)
  has been completed. Lastly, the co-financing agreement between the World Bank and the French Development Agency (Agence Francaise de
  Developpement-- AFD) has been signed.
