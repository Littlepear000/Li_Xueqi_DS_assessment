4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  Implementation Status:
  The Niger: Smart Villages for Rural Growth and Digital Inclusion project continues to make satisfactory
  progress toward its development objectives. As of August 8, 2025, the project has reached a 60% disbursement
  rate, with substantial progress in the deployment of digital infrastructure, operationalization of digital centers,
  and expansion of mobile financial services. However, some activities remain behind schedule due to security
  challenges in certain regions and delays in equipment delivery.

  Key Decisions:

 •The Government of Niger is encouraged to expedite the formal adoption of key policy documents and legislation
 developed under the project to strengthen the enabling environment for digital development and financial
 inclusion.
 •Amendments to the contracts of telecom operators Zamani and Niger Telecoms are recommended to extend the
 implementation period until July 30, 2026, without financial impact, to ensure full coverage of targeted localities.
 •Niger Telecoms is advised to accelerate deployment in secure areas and consider replacing localities in the
 Tillabéry region affected by insecurity.
 •The inauguration of the IXP Niger and the completion of connectivity for health centers, schools, and
 municipalities are prioritized for rapid operationalization.
 •The Government of Niger is encouraged to expand the use of digital payments for government services,
 including student grants, civil servant salaries, and health service payments.
 •Continued close monitoring and engagement by the Country Management Unit (CMU) and the line ministry are
 essential to address implementation challenges and ensure timely completion of project activities by the closing
 date of October 23, 2026.

  These decisions aim to accelerate project implementation, address outstanding challenges, and maximize the
  impact of digital inclusion and rural growth initiatives in Niger.
