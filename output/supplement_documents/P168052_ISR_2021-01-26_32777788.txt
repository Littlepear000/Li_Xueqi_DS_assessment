 Project Development Objectives

  Project Development Objective (from Project Appraisal Document)
  The development objectives of this project are to strengthen participatory local governance, improve the quality of local infrastructure, both in
  targeted communities, and increase extracurricular or livelihood opportunities for youth.

  Has the Project Development Objective been changed since Board Approval of the Project Objective?
  No

 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  Following the virtual project launch workshop held on August 10, 2020, the implementing agency has been carrying out project start-up activities,
  including assembling its project management team at the national and regional levels and preparing the project website and management
  information system for public launch in January 2021. The implementing agency is also procuring key consultant services, including Training and
  Facilitating Partners to support community outreach and mobilization activities in Khatlon and Gorno-Badakhshan Autonomous Oblast (GBAO),
  which are expected to be selected by late-January. Subsequent to this, project orientation workshops are expected to commence at the regional
  and district levels by late-February 2021 followed by village-level community mobilization activities.
