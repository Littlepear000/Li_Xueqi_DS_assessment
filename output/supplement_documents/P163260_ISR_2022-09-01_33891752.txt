 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  First supervision mission was carried out on June 6-10, 2022. In June 2022, the Project's Steering Committee was established, chaired by the
  Minister of Economy, Planning, and Development (MEPyD) and comprised of representatives of the Ministry of Agriculture (MA), Ministry of
  Environment and Natural Resources (MARENA), National Institute of Hydraulic Resources (INDRHI), National Institute of Drinking Water and
  Sewerage (INAPA), and the Water Sector Board.
  The key activities and budget of components were revised, and an Action Plan was agreed to initiate project implementation. The next supervision
  mission is scheduled for mid-November 2022.
