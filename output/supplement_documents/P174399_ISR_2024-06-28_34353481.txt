 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  In FY 2024, several results have been achieved, attesting to improvements in early childhood and cognitive development (ECCD) quality, and the
  inclusivity and climate-responsiveness of the Skills Development Program (SDP) (supported under disbursement-linked indicators (DLIs) 1, 5 and
  9).

  A national action plan for ECCD services has been issued, and capacity building scaled up for ECCD trainers. Under the SDP, a strategy for
  enhanced inclusion of women in technical and vocational education and training (TVET) programs has also been issued. In parallel, there has been
  an increase in training delivered outside of Thimphu, Paro, and Phuentsholing, as well as an increase in training on climate-responsive occupations.
