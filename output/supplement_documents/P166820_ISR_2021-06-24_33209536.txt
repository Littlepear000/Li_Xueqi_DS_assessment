 Project Development Objectives

  Project Development Objective (from Project Appraisal Document)
  The Project development objectives of the Fourth Phase of the Central Asia Regional Links Program are to enhance the efficiency of cross-border
  trade for participants of the regional economy and to improve the resilience and safety of regional connectivity infrastructure in Sughd region and
  Gorno-Badakhshan Autonomous region.

  The project contributes to the Central Asia Regional Links Programs higher-level development objectives of increasing cross-border connectivity
  and enhancing integrated regional development to revitalize historically active economic exchanges in Central Asia and beyond along the Silk
  Route.

  Has the Project Development Objective been changed since Board Approval of the Project Objective?
  No
