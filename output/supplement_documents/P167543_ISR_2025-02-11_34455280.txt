1. OBJECTIVE


 1.1 Development Objective
   Original Development Objective (Approved as part of Approval package on 08-Jul-2020)
   The project aims to increase access to cellphone and broadband services in rural areas and to bring digital financial
   services to selected underserved areas.
   Has the Development Objective been changed since Board Approval of the Project Objective?

   No

4. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  The ISR (Sequence #9) reports on the weekly follow-up meetings held with the counterpart's project teams and the
  findings from the Mid-term review mission held from October 7 to 11, 2024.

  There is significant progress under all Components of the Project and key activities such as the deployment of mobile
  broadband connectivity in 670 rural locations, the opening of digital centers (130 operational to date), the Project
  enabled more than 153 532 persons (out of a total of 150 000 planned) to complete mobile financial transactions to this
  day, out of which 75 782 women (i.e. 49% of users).
