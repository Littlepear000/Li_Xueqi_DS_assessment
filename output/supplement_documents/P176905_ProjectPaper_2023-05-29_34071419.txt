 I. PROJECT STATUS AND RATIONALE FOR RESTRUCTURING


A. Introduction
1.      This is a proposed Level-2 restructuring of the Climate Intelligent and Inclusive Agri-food Systems (CIAF)
Project. The World Bank received a request on January 6, 2023, and an additional note on March 23, 2023 from
the Government to changes the implementation arrangements, including procurement and financial management
(FM) arrangements. These changes need to be reflected in: (i) the Loan Agreement, (ii) the Project Operations
Manual, (iii) the Subsidiary Agreement between the Directorate General of Sectorial and Special Programs and
Projects (DIPROSE) and the National Institute of Agricultural Technology (INTA), and (iv) the new Subsidiary
Agreement signed between DIPROSE and the Fundación ArgenINTA.

B. Project Background and Status
2.      The US$400 million Project (Loan 8867-AR) was approved on December 9, 2021 and became effective
on March 21, 2022. The Project Development Objective (PDO) is to (i) support economic recovery and promote
climate smart practices among Project beneficiaries in Argentina's agri-food system; and (ii) respond effectively in
case of an eligible crisis or emergency. The Project is divided into the following components: (i) Component 1 -
Public Infrastructure for Agro-industrial Development; (ii) Component 2 - Agro-industrial Development and
Strengthening of Rural Livelihoods; (iii) Component 3 - Innovation for a Green and Inclusive Agri-food System
Transformation (under the implementation of INTA); and (iv) Component 4 - Project Management and Evaluation.
The current closing date is June 15, 2027.
3.       Progress toward achieving the PDO is rated Satisfactory (S), whereas the Overall Implementation
Progress (IP) is rated Moderately Satisfactory (MS). Currently, 22 provinces have joined the Project, and 17 have
updated and approved their Provincial Agrofood Sector Strategies. Under Component 1 (Public infrastructure for agro-
industrial development) 26 rural infrastructure subprojects were requested for an amount of USD 170 million. Its
formulations are in progress and their completion is planned between June and October 2023. Under component 2
(Agribusiness development and strengthening of rural livelihoods), for Windows 1 and 2 (Rural Livelihood for Economic
and Productive Inclusion ), 27 Subprojects have already been approved for an amount of USD 3.5 million, while 109
Profile Ideas are in the process of being formulated; for Window 2 (Water Plan) a contract was signed for the
construction of 2,281 cisterns for a value of USD 18 million, and for Windows 3 and 4 (Agroindustrial Subprojects) 85
Profile Ideas were received in its first Call for Proposal for USD16 million, which are in the evaluation phase. For
Component 3 led by INTA, the necessary procurement processes needed for its institutional strengthening are
advancing well and some of them have been completed, as well as the Scholarship Program and the strengthening of
two existing incubators and the introduction of innovation nodes. The environmental and social instruments have
been reviewed and cleared, and the overall Environmental and Social Standards performance of the Project is
Satisfactory.
4.      Despite low execution rates, the Project is on track to achieve its PDO by its closing date. As of mid-May,
2023, the Project has disbursed US$67.9 million (17 percent of the loan), of which US$20.1 are committed and
US$8.9 million are executed. As planned, the first year of implementation was used to for the provinces to
formulate their rural infrastructure subprojects and for the opening of call for proposals under Component 2,
while Component 3 could already undertake multiple procurement processes. Further, DIPROSE's team will be
strengthened through the hiring of personnel to complement both the core technical and territorial teams, which
is expected to impact the Project's execution positively.

C. Rationale for Restructuring
5.       Changes to address implementation challenges. In July 2022, Decree 426/2022 was signed, which
restricts the appointment of personnel. This decree has hindered DIPROSE to hire the needed personnel to
manage the project under Component 4 of the project. In order to solve this hiring problem and to strengthen
the implementation capacity of DIPROSE, the proposed addition of Fundación ArgenINTA as a co-implementing
agency will allow the project to avail the necessary technical expertise quickly and effectively given the difficult
conditions faced by federal institutions to hire new staff and consultants. ArgenINTA has the mandate to provide
support and technical assistance in agriculture innovation. The proposed restructuring will allow ArgenINTA to
contract consultants under Component 4 and implement activities related to project management and monitoring
and evaluation functions. In addition, the proposed restructuring will also allow INTA to undertake procurement
processes under Component 3, for which it is already an implementing agency.

 II. DESCRIPTION OF PROPOSED CHANGES


6.       Summary. The proposed restructuring will make changes in the overall implementation arrangements,
specific procurement and financial management arrangements, including the addition of Fundación ArgenINTA as
co-implementing agency.
7.      Implementation arrangements. The proposed restructuring will expand the role of INTA that is already
involved in the implementation of Component 3. INTA will now also be responsible for procuring goods,
equipment, and non-consulting services to enhance its capacity and develop the activities under this component.
Besides, Fundación ArgenINTA will be included as a co-implementation agency of the Project. Fundación
ArgenINTA will support DIPROSE with the hiring of individual consultants to strengthen implementation capacity,
focusing on project management and supervision of Components 1 and 2. To reflect these changes, DIPROSE will
adjust the Project Operations Manual (POM), and a Subsidiary Agreement will be signed with Fundación
ArgenINTA once the restructuring is approved. Since INTA is already part of the current implementation
arrangements, the procurement capacity assessment of INTA was only focused on the new procurement
responsibilities (see below). In contrast, the assessment of Fundación ArgenINTA covered all fiduciary aspects.
8.       Procurement of goods and non-consultancy services by INTA under Component 3. A procurement
capacity assessment of INTA was conducted, and concluded that INTA has adequate staffing, capacity, and
experience to implement the required procurement processes. INTA is expected to procure goods, civil works and
non-consulting services related to training activities under Component 3. All the procurement processes will
follow the original provisions and the Bank’s ‘Procurement Regulations for IPF Borrowers, issued in July 2016 and
updated in November 2020. The World Bank’s Standard Procurement Documents will govern the procurement of
international competitive processes. For procurement involving national competitive processes, the Borrower
shall use documents agreed with the World Bank and the national electronic procurement system, COMPR.AR, to
manage the procurement of goods and non-consulting services under a national market approach. Each
procurement process will be reflected in the Procurement Plan and agreed between the Borrower and the Bank
and registered in the Systematic Tracking of Exchanges in Procurement (STEP).
9.       Procurement of individual consultancy activities by Fundación ArgenINTA under Component 4. DIPROSE
will be responsible for the selection of consultancies, and Fundación ArgenINTA for signing the contracts with the
selected consultants and managing payments upon the request and approval of DIPROSE. Contract amendments
or early termination will be prepared by DIPROSE and formalized by Fundación ArgenINTA. This arrangement does
not represent a substantial modification of current procurement arrangements as DIPROSE will still have final
responsibility over the scope and approval of (partial) deliverables. Nevertheless, close coordination and

communication is necessary between the parties to avoid delays and ensure proper filing. DIPROSE will continue
to be responsible for updating the Procurement Strategy for Development as necessary and shall upload the
revised versions in STEP once accepted by the Bank. The individual consultant activities will be included in the
Procurement Plan and uploaded in STEP by DIPROSE. The contracts to be signed will follow the model agreed
between DIPROSE and the Bank, which contains the Bank’s Annex on Fraud and Corruption. These arrangements
will be further detailed in the POM.
10.      Financial Management (FM) Assessment of Fundación ArgenINTA. The Bank performed a FM assessment
of Fundación ArgenINTA in April 2023 to evaluate its capacity and FM arrangement to implement the proposed
activities under Component 4. The conclusion is that Fundación ArgenINTA has acceptable FM arrangements to
receive and manage Bank funds to execute activities under Component 4. The FM activities under Component 3
will be managed by INTA, which also has adequate capacities to carry out these functions, which were assessed
during preparation. The Project will continue relying on the overall coordination of DIPROSE.
11.     Organization and Staffing of Fundación ArgenINTA. After the restructuring, DIPROSE will advance funds
to Fundación ArgenINTA to execute activities under Component 4, mostly consisting of consulting services and
minor operating costs reflected in the Subsidiary Agreement between DIPROSE and the Fundación ArgenINTA.
Fundación ArgenINTA will be responsible for: (i) making payments to consultants under Component 4; (ii)
maintaining internal control procedures and periodic reconciliations; (iii) preparing and submitting necessary
information to DIPROSE for the Interim Financial Reports (IFRs) and documentation of expenditures to the Bank;
and (iv) preparing financial documentation and relevant reports/information requested by DIPROSE for
monitoring purposes, external auditors, and the Bank. Fundación ArgenINTA will also provide monthly
reconciliations and quarterly financial reports, detailing the actual use of loan proceeds. Roles and responsibilities
and administrative procedures of ArgenINTA will be clearly detailed in the interinstitutional agreement and the
POM.
12.     Annual budgeting process of Fundación ArgenINTA. Fundación ArgenINTA will prepare an Annual Budget
Plan for activities financed by the loan under the Component 4 and included in the overall budget of Secretariat
of Development and Federal Competitiveness Planning. INTA will provide DIPROSE with all relevant information,
so that DIPROSE can prepare and monitor the detailed budget of the Project. The Project will continue relying on
MAGyP’s own procedures for budget formulation and execution purposes, the government's Integrated Financial
Management Information System (Sistema Integrado de Información Financiera, or e-SIDIF) will continue to be
used for budget accounting needs.
13.      Accounting and financial reporting system of Fundación ArgenINTA. Fundación ArgenINTA has an in-
house accounting and information system named e-SIGA to prepare the budget, manage funds and perform
audits. However, financial information related to this Project will use a format compatible with the Integrated
Financial Management System, the UEPEX System1 used by DIPROSE. DIPROSE will continue being responsible for
the preparation of the semi-annual unaudited IFRs reflecting the sources and use of funds for each semester and
cumulative use by category. Funds will be transferred from DIPROSE to ArgenINTA institutional bank account
opened at the Banco de la Nación Argentina and it will be identified using a subledger dedicated for Project funds
in local currency in this bank account.
14.      Internal controls and audits of Fundación ArgenINTA. There are written policies and procedures covering
all routine accounting and related administrative activities. Fundación ArgenINTA’s will use its internal approval
processes and systems, that provide adequate segregation of duties, supervision, quality control reviews and
reconciliation. Fundación ArgenINTA has an internal audit unit and is subject to internal audits performed by
