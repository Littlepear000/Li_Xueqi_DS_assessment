 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The project has been declared effective. Legal covenants have been met by September 30, 2020 as requested. The project implementation unit is
  fully staffed. Several large procurement packages have been launched including the hiring of the Grant Management Company to administer the
  grant and matching grant program. In addition, the company to design the landscape beautification and general improvement for the three selected
  Khatlon sites have been identified and the contract is pending signing. The shortlisting of the companies who expressed their interest to hold a
  diagnostic study for the Yamchun Fortress rehabilitation is under way. Several agribusiness experts are being hired to support rehabilitation of food
  safety labs and market infrastructure.
