4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  Overall. The overall project implementation has been on track since the last implementation
  support mission of SOP1. The SOP2 was also approved by the Bank on March 20, 2024.

  Component 1. Port Boingoma. The procurement process for Port Boingoma was completed.
  The works contractor was awarded and the contract was made in January 2024. The supervision
  consultant was also recruited and is on site. The first phase of the first lot of the port works will be
  formally commenced in July 2024, while the required environmental and social safeguard
  instruments are being reviewed and revised. The first lot will take two years, followed by the
  second lot work (quays and superstructure) under this SOP2 for another one year.

  Component 2. Secondary ports development and passenger boat pilot program. The
  detail design and bidding documents for the secondary ports at Ouroveni and Vassy remain under
  preparation.
