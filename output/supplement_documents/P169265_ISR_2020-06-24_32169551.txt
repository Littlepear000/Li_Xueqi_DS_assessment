 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The project is not yet effective and progress is made in the process of completing the actions for effectiveness, namely: recruiting key staff for the
  project implementation Unit (PIU), preparing project operations manual, subsidiary agreements and the legal opinions for the financing agreements.
  Even though there are no ongoing implementation in relation to the main project, three Project Preparatory Advance (PPAs) are under
  implementation, namely; Togo, Liberia and Ghana.

  PPA Implementation in Togo: the implementation of the activities programmed in the PPA was signed on June 18, 2018, and became effective
  on September 21, 2018. Two activities to be carried out by INSEED have been identified in order to be funded on the basis of performance (PBF).
  These are the processing and dissemination of the 2018 Enterprises/businesses Census data and the Survey of non-registered cross-border flows.
  Both activities have been completed. The survey on non-registered cross-border flows have been conducted and details of the products subject to
  transaction and the annual values of exports and imports not recorded in the official registers has been produced. The enterprise census has also
  been concluded, with the completion of activities ranging from processing to dissemination census results, through the three products identified: i)
  the cleared and processed database, ii) the census report, iii) the dissemination of the report and the archiving of the database for free access.
  PPA also supported a twelve (12 ) months maintenance service delivery to clean the INSAEED building and its environment cleaned on a regular
  base and is supporting key product under contracts following World Bank procurement procedures in STEP, including; (a) preparation of the manual
  of administrative, financial and accounting procedures; (b) study on the statistics training center; (c) study for human resources reforms: Human
  resource management practices; (d) development of a material and equipment management strategy; (e) update of the building renovation
  technical study; and (f) financial and accounting audit of the PPA. The PPA also funded the monthly evaluation of INSEED staff performance to
  provide incentives to staff members based on their performance. To allow on time implementation of the remaining PPA activities, as well as the
  effectiveness of the HISWA (August 17, 2020 for Togo), the project team is seeking an extension for the PPA refinancing date, currently set at
  June, 30th 2020.
  PPA Implementation in Liberia: Due to Covid-19 measures taken by the government some of the PPA activities were put on hold, particularly
  those that involve GIS mapping and catorgraphy for the population census. However, LISGIS is able to put process together in order to undertake
  some of the activities, including:Project management and operation activities; workshop on administrative data, review of draft curricula for the
  Statistics Degree and Certificate - CEST - Programmes at the UL. Through the STEP LISGIS also recruited procurement Specialist and Finance
  Officer for Project Management, and engaged services of a consultant to review curricula for the Statistics Degree and Certificate - CEST -
  Programmes at the UL. LISGIS has also undertaken procurement of stationery and office equipment for Project Management and procurement of 2
  No. Double Cabin Pickup Trucks for PMT.
  To allow on time implementation of the remaining PPA activities, as well as the effectiveness of the HISWA (July 28, 2020 for Liberia), the project
  team is seeking an extension for the PPA refinancing date, currently set at July, 7th 2020.
  PPA Implementation in Ghana: The PPA support for Ghana was mainly planned for the preparatory activities for the Population census.
  However, due to Covid-19 pendemic the activities did not takeoff. GSS has however started engaging various vendors to provide services and
  equipment such as tablets. To allow on time implementation of the remaining PPA activities, as well as the effectiveness of the HISWA (July 23,
  2020 for Liberia), the project team is seeking an extension for the PPA refinancing, currently set at July, 7th 2020.
