4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  The Project’s Implementation Progress (IP) and Achievement of PDO ratings have been downgraded from ‘ Satisfactory’
  to ‘Moderately Satisfactory’ due to delays in implementation of key passive infrastructure development contracts under
  Component 1. These delays have been on account of tender reannouncement, contractor performance in difficult terrain,
  and weather-related delays. Furthermore, construction costs continue to remain high.
  The Project has disbursed US$23.4 million (60% of committed amount) and expected to disburse an additional US$2.5
  million in the FY25. Co-financing with European Investment Bank (EIB) is effective and contracts under implementation
  are co-financed. Procurement of active infrastructure under Subcomponent 1.1 is expected to drive disbursement rate
  higher.
