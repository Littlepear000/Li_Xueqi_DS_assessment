4. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  The development objectives of the project are to (i) increase the coverage of Egypt’s UHIS in Phase I Governorates, (ii)
  strengthen UHIS-related governance and institutions, and (iii) provide temporary financial protection against high out of
  pocket health expenditures for vulnerable populations outside Phase I Governorates. This an interim ISR to record the
  latest progress since the last technical review with the PMU in February 2024.

  Overall, there has been some implementation progress in enrollment of the population into UHIS, the UHIS Steering
  Committee has been created, the Prime Minister has issued a decree to clarify and expand the definition of vulnerable
  groups entitled to subsidized enrollment and the UHIS IT project is expected to be finalized by March 2025, with the
  Supply Chain Procurement module already completed and in use.
