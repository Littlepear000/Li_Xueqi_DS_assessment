 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  Following approval of the project on November 6, 2020, the Financing Agreement was co-signed by on December 17, 2020. Steps are now being
  taken to satisfy the conditions agreed during project negotiations for the project to become legally effective. All three conditions have been
  satisfied and the process of reviewing the legal documents and issuing a legal opinion is underway. Efforts to satisfy conditions required to be met
  for the disbursement of funds, namely the preparation and adoption of Project Operations Manuals, are also underway. All effectiveness and
  disbursement conditions are expected to be satisfied by end March 2021. With the establishment of a Project Implementation Unit in the Ministry of
  Reintegration and Temporarily Occupied Territories, the project is expected to begin implementation activities by end April 2021.
