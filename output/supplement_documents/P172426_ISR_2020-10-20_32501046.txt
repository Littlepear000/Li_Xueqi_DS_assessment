 Project Development Objectives

  Project Development Objective (from Project Appraisal Document)
  To (i) increase the coverage of Egypt’s Universal Health Insurance System in Phase I Governorates, (ii) strengthen UHIS-related governance and
  institutions, and (iii) provide temporary financial protection against high out of pocket health expenditures for vulnerable populations outside Phase I
  Governorates.

  Has the Project Development Objective been changed since Board Approval of the Project Objective?
  No

 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The Egypt UHIS project was approved by the World Bank Board of Executive Directors on June 16, 2020 and is not yet effective. The project was
  approved by the parliament and is expected to become effective in October 2020. The implementing agency is the Ministry of Finance and a Project
  Management Unit (PMU) consisting of MOF staff and consultants will be responsible for day-to-day project implementation. The overall PDO and
  Implementation Progress ratings are rated Satisfactory based on expected project effectiveness and PMU readiness. As the project is not yet
  effective, the disbursement rate is 0%.
