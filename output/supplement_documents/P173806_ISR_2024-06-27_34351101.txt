4. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  The project continues to support the Ministry of Health (MoH) in responding to the COVID-19 pandemic, strengthening
  the health systems for emergency preparedness, and responding to the Cholera outbreak. The progress towards
  achievement of the Project Development Objective (PDO) remains Satisfactory and overall implementation progress is
  Moderately Satisfactory. In the reporting period (November 2023 - May 2024), the project has made some progress on
  preparatory works for constructing an infectious disease centre in Blantyre and renovating the central hospitals. It has
  also supported expanding planning for integrated care platforms to four additional districts, providing facility planning
  training at all care levels, disseminating Health Sector Strategic Plan III and Health Financing Strategy, and piloting
  integrated care in 10 facilities in Rumphi and Nkhotakota. Notwithstanding, overall implementation progress has been
  slow since the previous mission in November 2023. The mission identified and discussed factors contributing to slow
  implementation progress and agreed on remedial actions that will be closely monitored by both the Ministry of Health
  and the World Bank.
