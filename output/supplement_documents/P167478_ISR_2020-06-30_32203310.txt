 Project Development Objectives

  Project Development Objective (from Project Appraisal Document)
  The Project Development Objectives (PDO) are to improve access to, and the quality of early childhood and basic education in the Project Areas

 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The project was approved by the Board on July 9, 2019 and the grant agreement was signed on July 29, 2019.

  The MEN-A has started the work to fulfill the two conditions for project effectiveness. These are: (i) the finalization and adoption of the Project
  Implementation Manual (PIM) satisfactory to the Bank; and (ii) the recruitment of a project coordinator, a financial management specialist, a
  procurement specialist, and an monitoring and evaluation (M&E) specialist for the Project Coordination Unit (PCU) with terms of reference,
  qualifications and experience satisfactory to the Bank.

  The adoption and validation of the PIM has been met; the Bank team has facilitated the recruitment of a consultant to work with the MEN-A in the
  elaboration and finalization of the PIM. The recruitment of the key PCU staff encountered some delays, in spite of the task team’s support in helping
