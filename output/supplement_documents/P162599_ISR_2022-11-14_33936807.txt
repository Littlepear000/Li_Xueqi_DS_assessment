 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The project was approved by the World Bank Board on July 1, 2019, and became effective on December 3, 2019. The implementation of the project
  has encountered significant delays in part due to the Covid-19 crisis. On Component 1, significant progress has been achieved with: (i) the
  successful launch of the Request for Bids (RFB) for the extension of digital coverage in the targeted areas (the deployment of the rural digital
  connectivity sites is expected to start before the summer 2023); and (ii) the extension of the connectivity of decentralized agencies of the Ministry of

  Agriculture, with works expected to start early 2023. On Component 2, for digital financial inclusion, the RFB to provide sub-grants to eligible
  merchants for the acquisition of digital payment equipment is expected to be launched before end of 2022. Still on Component 2, for the digital
  solutions for rural development, two digital platforms are now 'live' and in pilot phase (the digital platform for agricultural e-Advisory services, and
  the digital platform for the management of agricultural materials and equipment). The third platform on farm management and monitoring should be
  'live' and in pilot phase by end of 2022.
