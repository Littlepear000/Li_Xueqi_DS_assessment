 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  Since last reporting period the Project is making good progress. This is driven by completion of mobilization of Contractor under Component 1 and
  progress on assignments under Component 2 and 3.
  Component 1: Following preparatory and mobilization activities, civil works began on May 2, 2023. While Contractor is working on full speed it is
  expected that around 30% of works will be completed by the end of construction season 2023.
  Component 2: The Consultant, CAA International Limited (CAAi UK), has submitted final report on Review the State Safety Program of Kyrgyz
  Republic is now been drafted and under review by Ministry of Transport and Communications. Meanwhile, support to Kyrgyz Aviation Institute (KAI)
  is progressing well and repairs to the KAI facilities are substantially completed.
  Component 3: Construction of five Tourist Service Centers (TSCs) is underway. Currently both Contractors are fully mobilized and performing
  works on sites.
