4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  The implementation of the project is ramping up across its various components. Under subcomponent 1.1, contracts
  have been signed with microfinance institutions to initiate the sale of solar systems to households. To date, 30,319 units
  have been sold while another 46,500 household solar systems are en route and expected to arrive in Yemen by end of
  2024. Under sub-component 1.2, as of May 2024, 230 Facilities (51 schools, 119 health centers, and 60 solar pumping
  systems) have been energized. Additionally, several assessments and studies are being launched to provide support for
  the recovery of Yemen's power sector and support capacity building to government entities and microfinance
  institutions.
  The challenging conditions on the ground are being closely monitored, as they have caused minor delays in shipment of
  solar systems and equipment into Yemen. However, the project is progressing smoothly overall without encountering any
  significant obstacles.
