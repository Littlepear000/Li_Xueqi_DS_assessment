 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The Project continues to be rated Satisfactory on Implementation Progress (IP) and Moderately Satisfactory on Project Development Objective
  (PDO). Cumulative disbursement to date has reached US$118.21 million (.53 percent of the revised IDA allocation), reflecting important
  achievements across project components.

  The key achievements under component 1 (Integrated Rangeland Development and Management) include completion of the following economic
  infrastructure subprojects: 16 vaccination pens, 2 deep-water wells, 1 emergency livestock feed store, and the rehabilitation of 60 shallow wells.
  Construction is ongoing and progressing well for another 53 subprojects. In addition, about 320 hectares of land was developed for irrigation
  (achieving 56 percent of the plan) to produce 6,392 tons of forage biomass, thus enabling the project to respond to the current drought emergency
  needs.

  The key achievements under component 2 ((Livelihood Improvement and Diversification) include: (i) completion of preparation and validation of
  socio-economic profiles for the six project regions; (ii) 95 new Rural Savings and Credit Cooperatives (RuSACCOs) were established and
  supported, thereby improving access to rural financial services; (iii) 148 Common Interest Groups (CIG) were supported through business plans and
  provided with grants to start business activities of their choice; (iv) 208 new Community Animal Health Workers (CAHWs) were trained and
  equipped; (v) construction of 45 community prioritized subprojects, bringing the cumulative achievement of completed subprojects to 563 (80
  percent of the total 702 subprojects).

  The key achievements on project management, monitoring and evaluation include (i) adoption of the remote monitoring and supervision through
  deploying the Geo-Enabled Monitoring and Supervision System (GEMS), and (ii) the GRM system was made accessible in 95 percent of the project
  woredas. The project’s financial management system and procurement management continue to provide reasonable assurances that the project
  resources are used for the intended purposes and as per the agreed procedures. However, several planned activities are lagging under these
  components. The government and World bank agreed on a number of actions to accelerate implementation progress and ensure all planned
  activities are completed on time.

  The Additional Financing (AF) (US$125M) for the project that started earlier was scheduled for decision meeting and board approval on March 11,
  and July 8, 2022, respectively. However, the process was stalled following the institutional reform that resulted in the transfer of the federal level
  project management responsibility from the Ministry of Peace to the newly established Ministry of Irrigation and Lowlands as of November 22, 2021.
  Three months since the change, the institutional set up of the new ministry, including the fiduciary system, is not yet finalized. Hence, the Bank team
  was not able to conduct the fiduciary capacity assessment, which is a precondition for processing the AF and the associated restructuring. It was
  agreed to speed up the reform process and allow the fiduciary capacity assessment.

 Overall Comments PDO

  The project so far implemented 702 community subprojects benefiting a total of 1.6 million people of which 832,000 (52 percent) are female
  beneficiaries.

  The subprojects include; water supply, schools, human health post, animal health posts, community road, small-scale irrigation schemes,
  livestock markets, flood protection, and small bridge
