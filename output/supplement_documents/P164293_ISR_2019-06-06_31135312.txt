 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  Overview. This 5-year Higher Education Sector Support Project was declared effective on December 19, 2019, and it is expected to close on
  September 30, 2023; it is focused on strengthening higher education institutions to increase access to, and deliver quality education in priority
  subject areas. . The finalization of the annual budgeted work plan and the procurement plan for year 1 of the project allows the project to advance
  on the activities of recruiting consultants to work on the Virtual University strategic and business plans as well as the setting up of the virtual
  university in temporary spaces pending the construction of its headquarters. The project team is also working to finalize the Competitive Funds
  Handbook (Component 2 of the project) so that this important activity can be launched at the beginning of the 2019-2020 academic year. The
  procurement and annual work plan was approved and the activities started. The key project staff is in place and the staff activities and reports are

  Progress by Component:
  Component 1. Strengthen access to higher education. Activities of this component are lagging behind in particular: (i) preparation of a strategic
  business plan for the UV-BF; (ii) creation of a technological infrastructure and a learning platform, and the equipment of three (3) learning centers;
  (iii) renovation of existing infrastructure (amphitheaters, classrooms, laboratories, libraries and media centers); (iv)establishment of a broadband
  Internet connection to all public educational institutions; (v) development of a learning management system; (vi) development of necessary basic
  studies such as capacity building mainly for tutoring, coaching, management needs for distance learning, and pedagogy and environmental and
  social safeguard studies; (vii) implementation of a climate assessment exercise to identify risks and implement mitigation measures; (viii)
  development, adaptation, adoption and dissemination of programs and academic content in French, Arabic and English; (ix) implementation of
  academic upgrading programs and new courses; (x) development of a functional website; (xi) adoption of a management model for UV-BF; (xii)
  creation of a digital library; (xiii) recruitment of national and international contract teachers; (xiv) facilitation of public-private partnerships (PPPs) and
  university-industry links and the implementation of the UV-BF management system, including preparation of timetables, student management,
  reservation of facilities.
  Component 2. Strengthen higher education institutions and support innovations
  Subcomponent 2.1. Competitive Funds.
  In this sub-component, the grant-making mechanism to support the development and implementation of quality improvement sub-projects in the
  priority areas is not yet effective; the project competitive fund manual having not yet been finalized, the beneficiary higher education institutions
  have still not been not selected.
  Subcomponent 2.2 - Developing the Faculty in Higher Education Institutions (HEI)
  No activity in this sub-component is yet in the process of being implemented, including: (i) ensuring the payment of professional development
  opportunities for academic staff; (ii) promoting an exchange program for visiting professors; (iii) encouraging teacher exchanges at the national,
  intra-regional and international levels; and (iv) training academic staff in new teaching methods in mathematics and science.
  Subcomponent 2.3 - Strengthen Quality Assurance in HEI
  The four (4) major activities of this sub-component are still pending implementation, namely: (i) implementation of relevant aspects of higher
  education governance in the HEI to meet the requirements of the Bachelor-Master-Doctorate (LMD) program and the African and Malagasy Council
  for Higher Education (CAMES) programs; (ii) establishment of quality assurance units in the project-selected HEIs and a National Quality Assurance
  Framework including evaluation criteria related to regional quality assurance practices; (iii) strengthening the capacity of universities to implement
  projects (regardless of the source of funding); and (iv) enhancing the management and communication capacities of the HEIs to adopt and adapt to
  diverse delivery models.
  Subcomponent 2.4 - Strengthen HEIs Management Capacity
  No capacity-building activities of all HEIs to improve their academic, technical, governance, financial and project management have yet been
  completed, as the project-selected HEIs have not yet been identified.
  Component 3. Project coordination, management and implementation activities
  Project coordination, management and implementation activities are ongoing; however, a vital activity such as the implementation of the information
  and management system is still not launched.
