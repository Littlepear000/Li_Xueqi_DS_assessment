4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  The Project was approved by the World Bank on May 15, 2022, for US$25 million (composed of an IBRD loan and concessional
  financing) and a US$4 million grant. The project is being implemented by the MoPH. The Project falls under the COVID-19
  Strategic Preparedness and Response Program (SPRP), using a Multiphase Programmatic Approach (MPA) that was approved
  by the World Bank’s Board of Executive Directors in April 2020, as well as additional financing approved by the World Bank in
  October 2020. The Project has been declared effective on December 20, 2022. The MoPH launched the procurement of the first
  batch of medical equipment for public hospitals, which was cleared by the Bank on July 11, 2024.



4.2 MPA Program Development Objective
