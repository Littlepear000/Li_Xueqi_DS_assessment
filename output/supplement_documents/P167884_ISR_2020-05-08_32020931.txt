 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  Since the project official launch in September 2019, the project had some delays in the key components 1 (disbursement-linked indicators) and 2
  (various technical assistance activities), and the mission focused on agreeing on action plans to accelerate the progress and implementation. The
  ANAFIC also took an initiative to strengthen their communication activities and collaboration with other agencies in the country and is working on
  contributing to the COVID-19 crisis response in the country.
