4. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  Since its effectiveness in June 2024, the project has made good progress on major activities. The project was approved by
  the Council of Ministers and the House of People's Representatives (Parliament) of Ethiopia on May 15, 2024, and May 30,
  2024, respectively, and declared effective on June 21, 2024. All the Project Coordination Units, including in the newly
  added region of Dire-Dawa, are staffed, except for a few positions. The Annual Work Plan and Budget for EFY 2017 has
  been prepared and approved, and the implementation of activities across components is ongoing. Overall, the
  implementation progress is encouraging. However, more needs to be done to speed up the implementation pace. Key
  actions have been identified and agreed upon to further enhance the implementation pace and budget utilization to ensure
  all the planned activities for the FY are completed on time.
