 Project Development Objectives

  Project Development Objective (from Project Appraisal Document)
  To (i) strengthen capacity for integrated water resources management in the State; (ii) improve reliability of water services in the Agreste and
  Borborema regions; and (iii) improve the operational efficiency of water and wastewater services in the Joao Pessoa Metropolitan Region.

 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  After 22 months of implementation the Borrower, through the Project Management Unit at the State Secretariat for Infrastructure, Water Resources
  and Environment (SEIRHMA), the Project Implementation Unit at the State Company of Water and Sanitation (Companhia de Água e Esgotos da
  Paraíba, CAGEPA), and a coordination team at the State Water Resources Management Executive Agency (AESA), is implementing 17 out of
  41 Project's activities. Twelve activities currently undergoing procurement represent the bulk of loan proceeds (87%), which means that upon
  successful completion of procurement and signature of contracts most of the Project resources should be committed.
  The client and the Bank team agree the PDO remains achievable within the closing date (June 30, 2026) as long as major contracts related to the
  Transparaíba System, the rehabilitation and expansion of the sewerage system in João Pessoa, and the NRW project to reduce physical losses for
  the metropolitan area start their implementation within the next six months.

  The Bank Team will continue to support the Borrower with various experts, including procurement, wastewater treatment, water resources
  management, and non-revenue water specialists. Monthly monitoring and implementation support meetings have been scheduled with the client.
  The counterpart activities are progressing well, although at a slower pace than scheduled: works at the Curimatau Branch and a wastewater
  pumping station in Joao Pessoa (Usina 1) are underway. Dam safety activities are being followed-up by the Panel of Experts.
