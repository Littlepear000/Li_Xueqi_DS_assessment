 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The Niger Smart Village project which became effective on October 21, 2020 was officially launched in Niger by local authorities on December 3,
  2020. The project implementation unit (PIU) has migrated from the transitional PIU, Public Sector Capacity and Performance for Services (PCDS) to
  the newly recruited team within the National Information Society Agency (ANSI) since December 31, 2020.

  There is good progress in carrying out the required preliminary studies for project implementation. There is also significant progress on all
  Components of the Project and key activities such as the opening of digital centers, deployment of mobile connectivity sites in rural areas as well as
  the design of customer centric digital financial services platform which are being launched in the calendar year 2022.
