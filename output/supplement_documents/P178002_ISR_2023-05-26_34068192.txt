 Project Development Objectives

  Project Development Objective (from Project Appraisal Document)
  The PDO is to improve the capacity of the Lao PDR Statistical System to produce and disseminate good quality statistics in a timely manner and to
  enhance use of key statistics, and in case of an Eligible Crisis or Emergency, respond promptly and effectively to it.

  Has the Project Development Objective been changed since Board Approval of the Project Objective?
  No

 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The project became effective on May 12. The Project Implementation Unit (Project Implementation and Management Team, PIMT) and the Project
  Steering Committee (PSC, including 5 LSB management positions and 11 DG- or DDG- level positions from 8 ministries and agencies) were
  appointed by the Minister on April 11. However, the PSC is not currently operational as two committee members have not been designated. The
  first Annual Work Plan for June - Dec 2023 is yet to be submitted to the World Bank as it requires an approval from the PSC.
