1. OBJECTIVE


 1.1 Development Objective
   Original Development Objective (Approved as part of Approval package on 27-Sept-2023)
   The Project Development Objective is to improve country statistical performance, regional harmonization, data access
   and use, and to enhance modernization of the statistical system in participating countries.

   Has the Development Objective been changed since Board Approval of the Project Objective?

   No

4. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  Central African Republic: The project was approved on September 28, 2023. The Financing Agreement was signed on
  October 17, 2023. The project was declared effective on January 17, 2024. The annual work plan and budget were
  approved by Central African Institute of Statistics and Socio-Economic Studies (Institut Centrafricain des Statistiques, des
  Etudes Economiques et Sociales, ICASEES's) board three months after the project was declared effective. The key staff of
  the project implementation unit have been recruited, and the project's implementation started in the last two months.
  The disbursement rate of 8.2% as of June 14, 2024, is driven by the initial advance of $4.9 million.




  Chad: The project was approved on September 28, 2023. The Financing Agreement was signed on October 19, 2023. The
  project was declared effective on January 4, 2024. A first disbursement of $12.35 million has been made corresponding to
  a disbursement rate of 11.8% as of June 17, 2024. However, the implementation of activities is at start-up stage.


  Central African Economic and Monetary Community (Communauté Économique et Monétaire de l'Afrique Centrale,
  CEMAC) :

  The project was approved on September 28, 2023. The Financing Agreement was signed on November 11, 2023 and the
  project was effective on June 11, 2024. In addition, the entire PIU team will be in place with July 1, 2024 as the start date
  of the contracts for financial management specialist, procurement specialist, and an accountant.

  Cameroon: The project was approved on September 28, 2023, but the Financing Agreement has not been signed yet. The
  team is closely collaborating with the client regarding the effectiveness conditions.


  Republic of Congo: Additional financing for the Republic of Congo was approved on March 22, 2024. The Financing
  Agreement was signed on April 17, 2024 but the FA is not yet effective. Progress towards effectiveness of the Financing
  Agreement is on track.

  Gabonese Republic: Additional financing for Gabon was approved on March 22, 2024. The Financing Agreement was
  signed on April 19, 2024. Progress towards effectiveness under the loan agreement is on track.
