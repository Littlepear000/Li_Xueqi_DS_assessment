4. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  The TransFORM project was approved on May 16, 2023 with the objective to strengthen regional, evidence-based
  fisheries management in the South-West Indian Ocean Region and to improve competitiveness in the fisheries sector in
  the Maldives. The project’s regional activities are implemented by the Indian Ocean Commission while the national
  activities are implemented by the Maldives Ministry of Fisheries and Ocean Resources. The project’s objectives and
  design remain highly relevant and aligned with the strategic direction of the new government of the Maldives for the
  fisheries sector, including modernizing the fisheries sector’s management, promoting processing to reduce raw material
  exports, decarbonizing the sector through adoption of green practices, investing in critical infrastructure, and leveraging
  private sector financing. The World Bank and the implementing agencies have been discussing a proposal to make some
  adjustments to the design and targets to further align the project with these priorities.
  While implementation has been delayed compared to plans, several important steps have been made in: (i) digitizing
  historical catch reports, conducting stakeholder consultations and redrafting Fisheries Management Plans in the
  Maldives; (ii) creating a digital registry of fishers and conducting awareness activities for the Fisheries Information System
  of the Maldives; (iii) refurbishing some quarantine and laboratory facilities; and (iv) ongoing capacity building and
  preparatory works for the initiation of a degree program and preparations for a mariculture training program in the
  Maldives. In terms of efforts to decarbonize the fisheries sector in the Maldives, progress has been made in installing
  echo sounders and tracking devices on anchored Fish Aggregating Devices, aiming to enhance the efficiency and
  sustainability of fishing practices by providing better data and monitoring capabilities, ultimately contributing to
  improved fisheries management and reduced environmental impact. In addition, a consultant for an energy audit was
  mobilized to assess the fish processing sector and fishing vessels to develop bankable business plans under a matching
  grants program supported by the project. This will help improve energy efficiency and reduce energy consumption,
  leading to cost savings and environmental benefits.
