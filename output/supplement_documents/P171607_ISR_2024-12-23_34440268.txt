1. OBJECTIVE


 1.1 Development Objective
   Original Development Objective (Approved as part of Decision package on 20-Dec-2021)
   The objectives of the Project are to mitigate the effect of COVID-19 on private sector investment and employment and to
   support new economic opportunities including in refugee and hosting communities.
   Has the Development Objective been changed since Board Approval of the Project Objective?

   No

4. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  The PDOs remain relevant and can still be achieved. The project became effective 13 months ago and still has not met
  one of the disbursement conditions, i.e. public disclosure of Environmental and Social Safeguards Plan (ESMF) for the
  INVITE Trust.
  The Export Support Facility (ESF) supported by the recruited firm needs to expedite the review of already received
  applications from the first round and allocation of grants, and launch the second call for applications to be done
  by February 2025.
  INVITE Trust: The recruitment of the Investment Committee for the INVITE Trust, whose contracts are yet to be finalized
  is appreciated. GoU was advised on speedy resolution of the issues related to the allocation of the office space, furniture,
  and required IT infrastructure. The Implementing Agencies should accelerate hiring the INVITE Trust Manager.
  Implementation Support and M&E. The following staff have now fully joined the implantation unit: Project coordinator,
  financial management specialist, procurement specialist, M&E specialist, communication officer, manager export firm
  support and the office administrator. The mission advised PSFU to expedite the recruitment of the remaining positions,
  most importantly Environmental and Social Specialists.
  The next mission will be held in March 2025.
