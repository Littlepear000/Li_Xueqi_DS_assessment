 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  Overview. The five-year Burkina-Faso Higher Education Support Project, approved by the World Bank Board of Executive Directors on 10-July-
  2018, became effective on 19-Dec-2018, and it is expected to close on 30-September-2023. The project is focused on strengthening higher
  education institutions to increase access to, and delivery of quality education in priority subject areas.

  Progress by Component: The project has three main components. Progress by components is summarized below.

  Component 1. Strengthen access to higher education. Under this component 1, the implementation rate of the twenty (20) planned activities is
  about 30 percent. This relatively low rate is partly explained by the World Bank's recommendation to await the realization of the strategic plan and

  the Virtual University of Burkina-Faso’s business plan (in progress). These plans are seen as prerequisites for the operationalization of the
  university. Six (06) planned activities are in progress and four (04) have not been carried out.
  Materials for the effective launch of the Virtual University of Burkina Faso (VU-BF) teaching programmes, for the 2020-2021 school year, are in
  place. Activities programmed and underway to achieve this are the rehabilitation and upgrading of the building and equipment of the headquarters
  assigned to it by the Ministry of Higher Education; the launching, in collaboration with the Virtual University of Chad, of the Masters Degree in
  Cybersecurity, the first VU-BF training programme; and the launching, at the beginning of the academic year 2020-2021, of teaching programmes
  by enrolling students on a large scale.
  The platform and Procedures Manual that support the one-student-one-computer process, with the implementation of the one-stop shop, are ready.
  The training of the staff of various institutions involved in this operation (higher education institutions, FONER, banks, scholarships directorate,
  Ministry of Higher Education) is under way. The call for Expression of Interest has been launched, two firms have been selected for the first phase
  of the programme, and the programme has been officially launched in March 2020.

  Component 2. Strengthen higher education institutions and support innovations.
  There is also a low implementation rate of about 26 per cent) of programmed activities due to the reorientation of the competitive funds mechanism
  (sub-component 2.1) towards a performance contracting approach for the benefit of public education and research institutions (HERI).
  Out of fifteen (15) planned activities, four (04) have been fully implemented, six (06) are in progress, and four (04) have not been carried out.
  Sub-component 2.1. Competitive Funds. Training in the preparation of performance contracts has been very popular with higher education
  institutions, actively preparing their performance contracts, which they will submit to the Ministry of Higher Education, Scientific Research and
  Innovation for evaluation, approval, contracting and then access to the first disbursement, which constitutes an advance. Several action-training
  sessions are underway with the Universities in order to define the challenges of the universities and the actions to be undertaken.
  The supervision mission of February 2020 recommended the signature of performance contracts and the start of activities as they are finalized.
  The financing of the doctoral programmes was deferred, so that the project would finance the necessary equipment and pedagogical inputs instead
  of just providing scholarships, the amount and use of which did not guarantee the establishment of an adequate infrastructure for a doctoral
  programme.

  Sub-component 2.2 - Developing the Faculty in Higher Education Institutions (HEI). No activity has yet commenced due to the fact that other
  components (VU-BF) and sub-components (Competitive Funds), to which this faculty development activity is connected, have not yet started.
  Terms of Reference for the training of 240 teachers on topics, such as interactive teaching and its requirements through sharing of promising
  experiences between teachers, open and distance learning, professional ethics and conduct of teachers-researchers in higher education
  institutions, are finalized and the training is scheduled took place in four sessions in January 2020.

  Sub-component 2.3 - Strengthen Quality Assurance in HEI. The various higher education institutions have set up teams that are actively
  working on the development of their programmes. A consulting firm is currently being recruited to support the Ministry of Higher Education,
  Scientific Research and Innovation in implementing its quality assurance policy. The firm is expected to start its mission by April 2020 at the latest.
  The next steps are the elaboration of the strategy and further implementation of quality assurance unit (QAU) at the level of all higher education and
  research institutions.
  .

  Sub-component 2.4 - Strengthen HEIs Management Capacity. Out of five (05) activities included in the 2019 Annual Work Plan for
  strengthening the management capacities of higher education and research institutions, four (04) were under implementation, i.e. 80 percent
  implementation rate, and one (01), i.e. 20 percent implementation rate, had not been implemented at the date of the supervision mission in
  February 3-14, 2020. The mission decided to:
          support the development of the strategic plan of the University of Fada N'Gourma. For the other two universities that have committed their
           resources to this activity, IDA funding will not be committed to their process. However, other types of activities at these two universities
           could benefit from support under the current project for the operationalization of their strategic plan;
          provide technical assistance to the Ministry of Higher Education, Scientific Research and Innovation for the implementation of its
           information and management system.

  Component 3. Project coordination, management and implementation activities
  Project management and coordination is improving but remains moderately satisfactory. The internal control is in place and functioning and the
  external auditor has been recruited and will soon be working on his report, due on 30 June 2020. The independent verification agent has been
  recruited. He has produced his first report on indicators linked to disbursement 1 to 6, that was satisfactory to the World bank, and he is expected to
  produce a second report in May 2020. However, the recruitment of the project manager has not yet been finalized.
