 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
The project was approved by the Board on June 18, 2024, and the legal agreements were signed on September 17,
2024, and became effective on December 04, 2024.
The Project aims to address some of the most critical challenges to improve access to quality basic education and
strengthen capacity for sector management in CAR. It focuses on activities that support and strengthen teacher-
learner interaction in the classroom and improve the overall teaching and learning environment in classrooms.
Finally, in alignment with the Ministry of Education's priorities, the Project aims to be inclusive across several key
dimensions, including in the design and construction of climate-resilient and environmentally sustainable school
infrastructure, taking into account the needs of students with disabilities. It also focuses on promoting girl’s retention
and completion of the basic education cycle. The Project is expected to achieve its development objective through
four components: Component 1: Increase access to improved learning environments; Component 2: Improve
teaching quality; Component 3: Strengthen data system and sector management; and Component 4: Contingency
Emergency Response Component (CERC).
