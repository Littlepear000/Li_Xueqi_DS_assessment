 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  Overall, project implementation has accelerated. In particular, there has been good progress with regard to the establishment of the boys’ and girls’
  clubs (together with UNFPA), the training of preschool teachers (together with UNICEF), and the rehabilitation of WASH facilities in schools. The
  pilot of the national assessments for primary and secondary was carried out in April, as planned, and the assessments are on track to be completed
  in June. The preliminary report on the results from the participation in the Program to Assess Education Systems of the CONFEMEN (Programme
  d'analyse des systemes educatifs de la CONFEMEN, PASEC) in 2022 has been received, with preparatory activities underway (e.g., sampling,
  testing of items) for the next round.
