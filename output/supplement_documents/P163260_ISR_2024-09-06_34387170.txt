4. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  The Project was declared effective on January 11, 2022. The Project has a landscape approach to improve sustainable
  agriculture and integrated water resources management in Yaque del Norte and Ozama-Izabela basins. Project main
  activities are ag. productive subprojects (component 1), rehabilitation dams and large-scale irrigation infrastructure
  (component 2), works on sanitation plants (component 3) and water basin strengthening activities (component 4). World
  Bank supervision mission held on April 2024 verified delays in the bidding processes, which impact the estimated
  chronogram of investments. Project evaluation review mission will be conduct in September 2024 to discuss the
  restructuring of the project to be able to achieve the project's objectives.
