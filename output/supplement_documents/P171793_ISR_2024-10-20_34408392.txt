1. OBJECTIVE


 1.1 Development Objective
   Original Development Objective (Approved as part of Approval package on 01-Mar-2021)
   The proposed Project Development Objective (PDO) is (i) to enhance connectivity and road safety along the Zinder -
   Agadez road section in Northeastern Niger through the rehabilitation of the Tanout – Tiguidit road segment and
   maintenance of the full Zinder - Agadez section, and (ii) to improve access to basic socio-economic infrastructure for
   selected communities, along the Zinder - Agadez road section.
   Has the Development Objective been changed since Board Approval of the Project Objective?

 4. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
The Enhancing Niger Northeastern Connectivity project (PACNEN) was approved on March 2, 2022, and became
effective on September 22, 2021, with a total financing of US$ 175 million.
The Project development objective which is (i) to enhance connectivity and road safety along the Zinder-Agadez Road
section in Northeastern Niger through the rehabilitation of the Tanout – Tiguidit road segment and maintenance of the full
Zinder - Agadez section, and (ii) to improve access to basic socio-economic infrastructure for selected communities,
along the Zinder-Agadez Road section is Moderately Satisfactory.
The project is on-track to achieve its PDO, but most probably with some delays in the project activities implementation
and disbursements. To-date, the project has disbursed 23% (USD35.76%) of its total amount.
The implementation of the two Output- and Performance-based Road Contracts (OPBRC) for Lot 1 (in the region of Zinder)
had started in September 2022, paused for about 10 months, and resumed on 15 July 2024 and two addendums to the
OPBRC were signed, to strengthen the works service level but without financial impacts. To-date, the contractor has
resumed rehabilitation works under lot 1 (21% is completed) and mobilization for lot2, with expected rehabilitation works
for lot 2 to start by october end 2024.
The socio-economic infrastructures to be implemented for the benefit of the population living along the Zinder-Agadez
Road section have been identified and will be prioritized by the PCU with updated planning to be submitted for approval
to the Bank over the upcoming weeks.
Monitoring & Evaluation : Since december 2023, the PCU had succesfully implmented digital monitoing of project
activities (through GEMS and Mapillary).
