4. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  The overall implementation progress is now rated Moderately Satisfactory, reflecting a positive shift in execution since
  the last mission. Key activities have advanced significantly, early results are being delivered, and disbursement is picking
  up, although some institutional bottlenecks persist. Tangible results to date include:
      • Reform Plans: Three key reform agendas have been adopted and are under implementation through formalized
           partnership agreements:
               1. CMCAA: Public-Private Dialogue (PPD) was relaunched with a Prime Minister–led ceremony, technical
                    and statutory meetings, and a reform matrix in place.
               2. GUFE: Legal and institutional reforms are progressing with a revised organic decree finalized and
                    undergoing national validation.
               3. CNI: The Investment Charter manual was validated via a national workshop, and outreach activities have
                    been initiated.
      • Support to MPMEs: Over 3,000 MSMEs and entrepreneurs have been reached through structured interventions,
           including:
               o Awareness and identification campaigns nationwide;
               o Delivery of “quick-win” training ;
               o Business development services and coaching prepared for deployment by five key implementing
                    institutions (CAPMEA, CCIMA, PLAMFE, CPMEC, UNPC);
               o Training catalogs and support programs co-developed and now submitted for Bank quality review.
      • Guarantee Fund: The technical firm has finalized the strategic, operational, and governance framework for the
           FNGI. Preparations with partner banks are underway (due diligence, training). Disbursement of the $4 million
           capitalization is expected once remaining institutional and governance steps are completed.
      • Disbursement: The disbursement rate has increased to 16.04% (9.02% this FY), and is expected to reach 18.4%
           once the current request is processed. The rate is projected to reach 32% by early July, pending finalization of the
           FNGI disbursement.

  Key Decisions
     1. Accelerate implementation and maintain delivery momentum through continued support to the PIU and
         agencies, with targeted resolution of bottlenecks.
     2. Enable disbursement acceleration by finalizing institutional/governance steps for the FNGI and aligning
         administrative workflows with project timelines.
     3. Operationalize SME support activities by completing Bank reviews and launching training and BDS programs in
         coordination with partner institutions.
     4. Resolve apprenticeship coordination issues to allow rollout of youth employment programs and clarify roles
         across ACFPE, PIU, and training providers.
