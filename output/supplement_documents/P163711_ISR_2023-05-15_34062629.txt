            Tax Administration and Statistical System Modernization Project (P163711)



  The State Tax Service (STS) under the Ministry of Finance and the National Statistical Committee (NSC) have made progress in project
  implementation despite the Project being impacted by COVID-19 and changes in the Government. The STS and NSC have launched several
  important procurement packages and advanced the preparation of other activities that will lay the foundations for modernization reforms in tax
  administration and the national statistical system, which when implemented will lead to improved tax services, quality of statistics and reliability of
  electronic services provided by STS and NSC. While implementation progress picked up in the recent 12 months, significant acceleration is requires
  to make up for the delays.
