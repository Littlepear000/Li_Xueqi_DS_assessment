  Project Development Objective (from Project Appraisal Document)
  The Proposed Project Development Objective is to improve the national statistical capacity of Ecuador in the production and dissemination of timely

 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  This is the third ISR being filed for the Ecuador Strengthening Statistical Capacity Project. The Loan was approved by the World Bank’s Board on
  June 30, 2022, and declared effective on December 15, 2022. The project has been effective for nine months. A supervision mission took place on
  September 2023, and based on the supervision, the overall Implementation Progress is considered Satisfactory (S).
  The World Bank Team received an update from the INEC and the PIU teams on the implementation progress of the project. The team reported that
  on that date, the amount of the disbursement was $36.55 M (46%). The following developments have taken place:
  Component 1: The results of the population and housing census were presented to the general public on September 2023, and this activity should
  be completed by December 2023. The ongoing activities are as follows: the final stage of data processing, final results on specialized topics and
  analyses, and data publication.

  Component 2: Activities under this component have progressed satisfactory. Preparatory activities such as the definition of questionnaires, sample
  design, and pilots, training schedule have advanced for the household income and expenditure survey (ENIGHUR), the new labor force survey, the
  change of the base year of the producer price index (IPP), and the agricultural survey.
  Component 3: There have been important advances in the integration of demographic administrative records from different sources. Indicators and
  specific interaction tools for users of this data will be developed in the short term. Additionally, INEC has established an efficient process for
  assessing the quality of administrative records and establishing action plans for their improvement.
