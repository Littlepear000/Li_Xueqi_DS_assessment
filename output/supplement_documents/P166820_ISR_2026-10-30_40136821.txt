1. OBJECTIVE
1.1 Development Objective
Original Development Objective (Approved as part of Approval package on 30-Jul-2020)
The Project development objectives of the Fourth Phase of the Central Asia Regional Links Program are to enhance the efficiency of cross-border trade for participants of the regional economy and to improve the resilience and safety of regional connectivity infrastructure in Sughd region and Gorno-Badakhshan Autonomous region.
The project contributes to the Central Asia Regional Links Programs higher-level development objectives of increasing cross-
border connectivity and enhancing integrated regional development to revitalize historically active economic exchanges in
Central Asia and beyond along the Silk Route.

4. KEY ISSUES & STATUS
4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
Implementation Status and Key Decisions
The Bank team carried out the final implementation support mission from October 14 to 30, 2026, ahead of the project's closing date of October 31, 2026. The mission reviewed the results achieved under each component, the closing arrangements and the actions that will continue after closing. Progress towards the Project Development Objective and overall implementation progress are both rated Satisfactory. Disbursement stands at 96.07%, and the mission identified no issues with the closing arrangements.
Component 1: Improve regional connections in Sughd and GBAO. All civil works are complete and open to traffic. In
Sughd, 61.3 km of road were rehabilitated against a target of 50.5 km, including 14 bridges totaling 468.85 m. In GBAO, the new Khorog Bridge, 4.16 km road, the Barsem avalanche protection gallery and associated climate-resilient structures were completed on schedule.
Component 2: Improve road asset preservation and road safety. Two Weigh-in-Motion systems are operational, and the
Road Asset Management System (RAMS) has been developed, accepted and is in use. The 2027-2046 Strategic Road
Investment Plan and the related works program are prepared. On road safety, 62.35 km of safety barriers were installed against a target of 40 km. Seat belt use is now mandatory under national law. The Road Safety Observatory is established.
The National Road Safety Strategy 2025-2040 and its Action Plan have been prepared with project support and pending the Government approval.
Component 3: Facilitate cross-border movement of goods. ASYCUDA World is operating nationwide and is integrated with the National Single Window. Paperless declarations, electronic payments and risk-based clearance are in place.
Component 4: Support project implementation, coordination and management. The implementing agencies, the
Ministry of Transport and the Customs Service, delivered all components by the closing date. Monitoring and evaluation arrangements were in place throughout the project life, and results will be documented in a Learning and Results Note after closing.
Component 5: Contingent Emergency Response. The component was not activated, and no funds were disbursed.
Agreed with the Government
• The Ministry of Transport will pursue formal Government approval of the National Road Safety Strategy 2025-
2040 and its Action Plan, to sustain the project's road safety results beyond closing.
• The implementing agencies will complete the remaining closing actions.
