 Project Development Objectives

  Project Development Objective (from Project Appraisal Document)
  The Project Development Objectives (PDO) are to improve access to, and the quality of early childhood and basic education in the Project Areas
  and to strengthen the capacity of the education system to deliver better results.

 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The Project became effective on July 15, 2020. The military coup of September 5, 2021 led to suspension of the constitution and public institutions.
  As a result, the World Bank’s Operation Policy OP. 7.30 “Dealing with de Facto government” was triggered on September 9, 2021 and all of the
  World Bank’s operations in Guinea were put on hold.

  A new Government was fully formed on November 2021, with a new Ministry of Pre-University Education and Literacy (Ministère de l'Enseignement
  Pre-Universitaire et de l'Alhabétisation - MEPU-A). The World Bank approved the resumption of the disbursement on March 9, 2022. The Project
  Coordination Unit (PCU) is fully staffed, the project coordinator and all key specialists are in place and the PCU is fully operational.

  To date most Project activities has been prepared and key activities have been launched.

  For Component 1: Improving equitable access to pre-school education. For the creation of a one year of pre-school in 1,000 public primary
  schools: (i) UNICEF is being recruited to provide technical assistance to the MEPU-A for the elaboration of the pre-school policy, the development
  of a costed and time-bound strategic and implementation plans, (ii) for school construction, the cabinet for the technical studies and supervision has
  been recruited, the sites screenings have been carried out, the bidding documents are ready and the first bids will be advertised at decentralized
  level in early January 2023; and (iii) four NGOs have been pre-selected and are being recruited to sensitize and reinforce the communities'
  capacity for the supervision of all the activities at the local level.

  For Component 2: Improving pre-school and basic education quality using technological innovation. The cabinet to provide technical
  assistance to the MEPU-A for the development and implementation of a policy for in-service training using new technologies has been pre-selected
  and is currently being recruited. The bidding documents to purchase about 19,560 tablets are ready and the bid will be advertised by the end of
  January 2023.

  For Component 3: Strengthening education sector management capacity. A consultant has been recruited to support the MEPU-A on the
  recruitment reform, which transfers responsibility for teacher recruitment from the central to the prefectural and regional levels. The Government has
  adopted the decree for the recruitment at the decentralized level and the DLI on its dissemination at all educational levels has been achieved. A
  consultant has been recruited to develop the model for the allocation of a non-salary recurrent budget to public primary schools. A cabinet to
  provide technical assistance to reinforce the MPEU-A capacity in designing and implementing early student learning assessment has been pre-
  selected and is being recruited. The recruitment of an agency to provide technical assistance to the MEPU-A to strengthen its EMIS is underway
  and expected to be finalized by the end of February 2023.

  The Independent Verification Agency (IVA) for the verification of the Disbursement-Linked Indicators (DLIs) has been recruited. The external auditor
  has also been recruited.
