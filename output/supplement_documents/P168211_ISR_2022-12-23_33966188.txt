 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  Throughout the reporting period, the project made some progress including (i) signing of the Financing Agreement for the Additional Financing (AF);
  (ii) finalization of the implementation agreements between the three sector entities (BT, SIB, and STB) which has to be signed by end-December
  2022 as part of the AF effectiveness conditions. In addition, six disbursement linked results (DLRs) have been achieved, enabling disbursement of
  approximately US$15 million to BT.
  In addition, good progress has been made with (i) preparation of the Electricity Tariff Paper by the Anti-Monopoly Service to provide the trajectory in
  average tariff increases to ensure the energy sector cost recovery; (ii) preparation of the Generation Expansion Plan; (iii) Establishment of the
  supervisory boards (SBs) and specialized committees under the Supervisory Board of BT.
  Other activities under the Program Action Plan (PAP) have also been successfully completed including: (i) hiring of key staff for the Tariff Unit at
  AMS and capacity building activities; (ii) introducing elements of good-practice corporate governance including internal audit functioning at BT, SIB,
  and STB and enclosing of the audited reports for CY 2021 and unaudited financial statements for 2022; (iii) hiring of environmental experts and
  consultants and development of corporate environmental policies and standards for effective environmental management performance at the three
  entities.
