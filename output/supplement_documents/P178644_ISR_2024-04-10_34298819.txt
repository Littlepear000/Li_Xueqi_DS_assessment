4. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  This is the first implementation status report since the project has been approved by the World Bank Board. The
  Financing Agreement was signed on November 29, 2023 and project's effectiveness was notified on February 26, 2024.
  Thanks to a preparation advance (PPA) and the work from ICV, ECV, and UGPE, preliminary studies, as well as technical
  assistance to UASE and INGT, started in advance of the project's financing agreement signature. The teams are now
  mobilized to fulfill the remaining contractual obligations and to implement the project's components: component 1
  (urban and seafront interventions), component 2 (roads), and component 3 (technical assistance).
