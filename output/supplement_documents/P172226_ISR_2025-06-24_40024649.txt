3. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


 3.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
   The RAMP project continues to make meaningful progress toward achieving its development objective of enhancing the
   competitiveness and resilience of MSMEs across India. The technical discussion with the Ministry of Micro, Small and
   Medium Enterprises have helped address key implementation bottlenecks and align reform priorities under critical
   program areas.
   While progress on some results indicators remains uneven—particularly with regard to Annual Report, ZED/LEAN
   Graduation strategy, and implementation of the Environmental and Social (E&S) inclusion strategy and online dispute
   resolution platform—these are being actively addressed through targeted support and capacity-building efforts.
