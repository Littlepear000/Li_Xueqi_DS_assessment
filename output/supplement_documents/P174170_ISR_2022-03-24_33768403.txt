 Project Development Objectives

  Project Development Objective (from Project Appraisal Document)
  The Project Development Objectives are to control the desert locust outbreak, support livelihoods in locust-affected areas and strengthen Yemen’s
  preparedness for future locust infestations.

 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  Overall good progress in project implementation has been recorded. Desert locust activity has been minimal during 2020/2021 season. Some
  desert locust activity over the summer season was contained by project-supported surveillance, spraying, and control operations in breeding
  grounds (around 422 ha). Active surveillance is underway. As of March 2022, the project's surveyed area reached 954,214 hectares (against
  400,000 target for the entire project ) in 7,481 survey stops. The livelihood support activities in Component 2 will be taking off based on partnership
  agreements between FAO and SFD signed in March 2022.

 Overall Comments PDO

  Surveys were conducted mainly in the inner breeding areas and partly on the Red Sea coastal areas of Hajjah, Almahwit, Sana’a
  and AlHodidah governorates.10 survey teams were mobilized monthly using 20 rented vehicles and 5 Elocust3 devices and printed survey
  forms. Total surveyed area reached 954,214 hectares (against 400,000 target for the entire project period), by visiting 7481 survey stops.
