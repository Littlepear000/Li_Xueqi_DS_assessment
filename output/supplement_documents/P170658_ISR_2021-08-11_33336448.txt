 Project Development Objectives

  Project Development Objective (from Project Appraisal Document)
  The proposed project development objective is to provide a more efficient response to emergencies, urgencies, and associated help line requests
  from the population of Lima Metropolitan area and Callao.

 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  Under Component 6 (Project Supervision and Management, and Technical Studies), the process for hiring a consulting firm to prepare the technical
  specifications for the Integrated Platform (to be embedded in the Terms of Reference for the Integrated Platform) was awarded on May 20, 2021,
  and the contract was signed with the provider on June 11, 2021. Based on the calendar provided by the consulting firm, the final version of the
  technical specifications will be delivered in September 2021.
  Under Component 3 (Central Response Protocols), the process for hiring a consulting firm to prepare the 911 Emergency Response Protocols was
  launched through a Request for Expressions of Interest with an extended deadline of August 5, 2021, and 3 expression of interests were received

  emergency response staff.
  In order to accelerate project implementation, the Bank team and PRONATEL agreed that the technical study and the works for the 911 Center
  would be combined under one procurement process. PRONATEL has already drafted TOR and minimal technical requirements for this process and
  sent them for World Bank no objection on July 3, 2021, and comments were sent by the Bank end of July 2021.
  A dedicated team of 9 staff members under the Project Implementation Unit (PIU) was hired between February and March 2021, including, i) a
  project coordinator, (ii) a telecommunications engineer, (iii) a civil engineer, (iv) a social specialist, (v) an environmental specialist, (vi) a
  procurement specialist, (vii) an accountant, (viii) a financial management specialist and (ix) a project assistant.
  Finally, regarding the Steering Committee, PRONATEL already sent the agreements to be signed by the Emergency Response Units and the GBV
  hotline describing responsibilities of each entity. Each responsible entity agreed on sending it back to PRONATEL by end of August 2021. Since the
  establishment of the PIU, the Steering Committee has met at least once a month, allowing PRONATEL to collect inputs needed from the response
  entities for the elaboration of the 911 Protocols and the technical specifications and minimal requirements for the technical study and works for the
  911 Center.
