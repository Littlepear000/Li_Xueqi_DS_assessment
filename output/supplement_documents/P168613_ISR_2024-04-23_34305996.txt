 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  Key reforms in NRM, mining, environment and climate are underway in Guinea including (i) to strengthen and expand the national protected area
  network, (ii) regular technical, environmental and social (E&S) monitoring of mining operations as well as (iii) increasing local content in the mining
  sector.
  The Project has to accelerate implementation (currently 13.3% disbursement rate and several fundamental contracts still being procured) to help the
  government address the NRM and the mining sectors challenges.
  Key priority actions to accelerate Project implementation identified during previous missions have been implemented with delays including (i) the
  recruitment of the Deputy Coordinator for MEDD activities and (ii) the recruitment of a senior procurement consultant.
  Several fundamental procurement process have to be concluded to boost Project implementation including (i) technical assistance for Ministry of
  Mines, for the National Parks agency (OGPNRF), for Environmental Protection Agency (AGEE), to establish the Conservation Trust Fund, to
