4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  Project implementation is progressing steadily. Especially Component 2 on Community Livelihoods Enhancement and
  Component 3 on Community Nutrition Interventions are fully on track with high levels of community participation and
  ownership, particularly among women. For example, over 850 Self-Help Groups of about 12,000 members have been
  formed, of which over 90% are women and are from non Lao-Tai ethnic groups, receiving seed grants to implement their
  income generating activities. Over 800 model farmers (out of which 83% women) were trained to provide good practices
  to peer villagers on nutritious food preparation at home gardens. Component 1 on construction of small-scale climate-
  resilient infrastructure (CRI) subprojects has been delayed, however, and the project’s Monitoring and Evaluation (M&E)
  system requires further strengthening.

  The project's Mid-Term Review (MTR) is scheduled for January 2026.

  The project's Mid-Term Review (MTR) is scheduled for January 2026.
