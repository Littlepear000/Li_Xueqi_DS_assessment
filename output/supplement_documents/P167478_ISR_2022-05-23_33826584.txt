 Project Development Objectives

  Project Development Objective (from Project Appraisal Document)
  The Project Development Objectives (PDO) are to improve access to, and the quality of early childhood and basic education in the Project Areas
  and to strengthen the capacity of the education system to deliver better results.

 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The Project became effective on July 15, 2020. The military coup of September 5, 2021 led to suspension of the constitution and public institutions.
  As a result, the World Bank’s Operation Policy OP. 7.30 “Dealing with de Facto government” was triggered on September 9, 2021 and all of the
  World Bank’s operations in Guinea were put on hold.

  A new Government was fully formed on November 2021, with a new Ministry of Pre-University Education and Literacy (Ministère de l'Enseignement
  Pre-Universitaire et de l'Alhabétisation - MEPU-A). The World Bank approved the resumption of the disbursement on March 9, 2022.

  So far most of the achievements are focused on the preparation of the activities. The Project Coordination Unit (PCU) is fully staffed, the project
  coordinator and all key specialists are in place and the PCU is fully operational. The Independent Verification Agency (IVA) for the verification of the

  international consultant in charge of review and development of technical recommendations for the scholar canteens initiative was recruited and a
  draft report issued. The 1,000 primary schools benefitting from the construction of one preschool classroom per school have been selected.
  The firm for the technical studies, monitoring and supervision of the construction of the 1,000 preschool classrooms has also been identified and
  recruited. The contract for the recruitment of the consultant to revise the standards of construction is currently being finalized and is expected to be
  signed in the coming days. The recruitment of consultants to support the activities aiming at achieving key DLRs on teacher in-service training and
  budget transfer to school is underway.

  With regards to the Disbursement-Linked Indicators (DLI), the Government issued the joint ministerial decision for decentralized recruitment of
  teachers. Once the IVA verifies in June 2022 its dissemination at regional and sub-regional levels, the first disbursement is expected to be approved
  by the World Bank by July 2022.
