4. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  A renewed engagement between the WB team and the PIU has led to an increased implementation pace of project
  activities. Communication has been improved and there is close supervision of activities on the part of the WB. This has
  led to concrete steps to solve the staffing issues that were mentioned in the previous ISR. All PIU contracts have now
  been regularized, a new RAF is in place and several recruitments for key positions are ongoing. The recruitment of key
  E&S positions is also ongoing and the team will focus on ensuring that E&S documents and requirements are in place
  especially for infrastructure projects.
  Disbursement performance continues to be on track. Total rate of disbursement stands at 37% (48 mil $) and rate of
  engagement is at 76% (98 mil $).
  A second project restructuring was processed during this period to include the re-direction of 25 $mil towards activities
  that support Madagascar Airlines, directly supporting the tourism sector. The WB team and the PIU have been working
  closely with MA teams to ensure timely support, on the most urgent procurements. Progress on these priority
  activities/contracts is followed on a weekly basis. The rate of disbursement on MA activities is 25% (6.2 mil$). The specific
  technical nature of the MA activities has placed a burden on the PIU and steps have been taken to strengthen PIU
  capacity on the procurement side.
  Planning for future project activities has suffered from a decreased budget due to the cancelation of funds of FY24 and
  allocation of funds towards Madagascar Airlines activities. This has particularly affected activities in component 3 – SME
  and entrepreneurship support, where the majority of the budget has been cut. Under the current allocation, one of the
  windows of SME support, related to acceleration, will not be able to be launched. The indicators and targets will also
  need to be adjusted to take these changes into account.
  A Mid-term Review is being planned for November 2025. The WB and PIU have been actively preparing for the MTR. It is
  expected that a restructuring of the project will follow based on the lessons learned from the MTR, including a thorough
  review of indicators and targets and alignment with the new WB scorecard.
  The government intends to ask for additional financing for PIC3 to be able to implement all the activities as envisaged
  in the PAD. This will be discussed during the MTR and the next project restructuring will be designed accordingly.
