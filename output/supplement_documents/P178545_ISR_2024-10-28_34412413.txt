4. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  Implementation is progressing since the official launch of the project in March 2024: the entire CLEAR team has been
  recruited in Vientiane and in all districts, and the first batch of villages has been selected across the 14 districts in the 7
  provinces. Recruitment of all Young Graduates is on track for completion by October 2024 to begin the Village
  Development Planning (VDP) process in these villages.

  To ensure timely implementation across all components and regions, a six-month workplan through March 2025 has
  been prepared, along with detailed district-level plans. Climate-resilient measures will be integrated throughout the
  project.

  Additionally, the CLEAR team has actively engaged with the Ministry of Planning and Investment (MPI) and other
  ministries to align the project's investments with the government's National Convergence (NC) agenda.
