 Project Development Objectives

  Project Development Objective (from Project Appraisal Document)
  The proposed project development objective is to provide a more efficient response to emergencies, urgencies, and associated help line requests
  from the population of Lima Metropolitan area and Callao.

  Has the Project Development Objective been changed since Board Approval of the Project Objective?

 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The Government of Peru approved the Supreme Decree (Decreto Supremo nº160-2020-EF) on June 27, 2020.
  The Legal agreement is planned to be signed in the beginning of July between the Government of Peru and the World Bank.
  These are key steps towards declaring the project's effectiveness.
  In parallel, the World Bank team and PRONATEL (Project Implementation Unit in charge of the project within the Ministry of Transport and
  Communications - MTC) have already started technical review of key TORs to allow an early start of implementation once project is declared
  effective.
