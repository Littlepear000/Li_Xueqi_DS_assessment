1. OBJECTIVE


 1.1 Development Objective
   Original Development Objective (Approved as part of Approval package on 29-Jun-2021)
   The Project Development Objective is to improve national land and mining administration services, register land rights in
   selected municipalities and foster inclusive benefits sharing from the mining sector
   Has the Development Objective been changed since Board Approval of the Project Objective?

   No

 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
   The implementation of the land component is progressing positively and is expected to result in key land management
   activities starting field deployment this year. Progress on the mining component has been hindered by delays in
   contracting and in procurement as well as alterations to the implementation of ongoing activities.
   Bi-weekly meetings are being organized between the Bank and project teams to address existing implementation
   challenges.
The mid-term review is anticipated for September 2025.
