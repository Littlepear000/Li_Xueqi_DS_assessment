 4. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
The overall Implementation Progress is rated satisfactory based on the summary of progress on key components and the
execution rate of the 2024 budget, as well as progress made on the achievement of Performance Based Conditions
(PBCs). Overall, 60 percent of committed funds have been disbursed and at least five (5) PBCs have been achieved and
ready for verification. Independent verification contract has also been renewed, and contract has been signed
September 30, 2024. The five (5) PBCs are:
       PBC 1.4: Percentage point increase from baseline in percent of targeted schools with teaching practices meeting
       inspection standards of teaching practices
       PBC 2.2: Number of targeted schools utilizing at least 80 percent of the learning grants (Yr.3)
       PBC 3.2: Number of schools using Accountability Dashboard data during cluster level meetings
       PBC 4.3: Increase in baseline established in DLR 4.2 in percentage of P4 pupils in targeted schools with
       proficiency in mathematics and English
       Percentage of Targeted Primary schools with PTTR below 50:1
Confirmation of these PBCs will bring total disbursement to about 70 percent.

Component 1: Strengthen teaching and learning through support and resources for teachers - 12,000 TLRs have been
distributed to schools and refresher training has been completed for teachers. (rating is maintained as satisfactory)

Component 2: Strengthen school support, management, and resourcing - Learning Grants to schools have been
transferred (base grants) together with district grants. (rating is maintained as satisfactory)

Component 3: Strengthen accountability systems for learning - Cluster level meetings have been held with
Accountability Dashboard data in 9,900 basic schools. Ghana Education Outcomes Fund (EOF) endline assessment for
Primary 2 has been completed in 2024, and the 2024 National Standardized Test (NST) assessment for Primary 4 &
Primary 6 have been completed. (rating is upgraded from moderately satisfactory to satisfactory)

Component 4: Technical assistance, institutional strengthening, monitoring and research - Mid-Term Review report,
Beneficiary Assessment, Impact Evaluation, SA, Procurement Assessment, Financial Managment Assessment, and
Communication Strategy have all been completed.
