 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The Public Sector Capability Enhancement Project (PSCEP) was approved by the Board on April 27, 2022 and project loan was signed by
  Government of India on July 22, 2022. The declaration of loan effectiveness was issued on Aug 25, 2022.
  Under Component 1, the Framework for Roles, Activities and Competencies (FRAC) Centre of Excellence (CoE) has been established in
  the Institute of Secretariat Training and Management (ISTM). Consultants were hired in Feb 2022 to develop the FRACing toolkit which is likely to
  be finalized by end of November 2022. The Capacity Building Commission (CBC) is now the nodal agency responsible for roll-out of the FRACing
  exercise cross the ministries, departments and agencies of Government of India. Since its constitution in April 2021, the CBC has piloted the
  development of annual capacity building plans for select central ministries and departments and will build on the learnings to implement the
  FRACs.
  Under Component 2, the chief executive officer of the SPV Karmayogi Bharat was appointed in early July 2022. The Bank project team is currently
  conducting the fiduciary and safeguard assessments of the SPV to be able to designate it as an implementing agency under the Project.
