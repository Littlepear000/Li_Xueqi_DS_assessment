 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  Cooperation between the sectoral ministries in charge of mining and the environment has increased significantly, including with the establishment of
  a commission in charge of addressing joint issues headed by the two Secretaries General. The project should support this dynamic of cooperation
  by accelerating the implementation of activities (the disbursement rate is only 6.4%).
  The reinforcement of the PIU personnel decided during the mission, as well as the effective recruitment of the key technical personnel for the
  executing agencies, should enable the Project to accelerate implementation.
