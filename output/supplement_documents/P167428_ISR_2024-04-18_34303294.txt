 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The project is making progress toward achieving the set objectives. The construction works on the Ak-Bura River Embankment Enhancements,
  Central Square Revitalization, Summer Theater Rehabilitation and Rehabilitation of Navoy Park are ongoing, while the renovation work around the
  Drama theater have been completed. The city-level investments in Uzgen are also at the advanced stage. The construction of the pedestrian
  bridge is currently under implementation. The rehabilitation of the stadium and the park around it is at the contractor selection stage. The works are
  expected to start in June 2024. 18 productive partnership proposals have already been approved by the Productive Partnership Committee.
  The Small Grants Program implementation is ongoing, the program has selected 181 participants and grant agreements for a total amount of USD
  1.7 million were signed in January 2024.
