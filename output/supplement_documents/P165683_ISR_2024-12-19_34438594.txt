4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  A substantial increase in the Project implementation pace has been achieved during the last six months.
  The construction of the Cariri pipeline and water treatment plant, the implementation of the water supply automation
  system, the sectorization of the Joao Pessoa Metropolitan Region water network, and the wastewater pumping station
  and outfall are underway. These activities represent the bulk of the loan proceeds (92%). The contract signature for the
  last major work, the construction of the Joao Pessoa wastewater treatment plant, is expected by March 2025 subject to
  availability of funds. Counterpart activities are progressing with 88% disbursement of the US$ 80 million, with a design-
  build contract for the Curimatau pipeline, a NRW performance-based contract for the reduction of commercial losses,
  and a standard contract for the construction of a wastewater pumping station. Activities to strengthen the State's water
  resources management capacity continue to progress well, with four contracts in progress: preparation of
  hydrogeological studies; improvement of water rights standards; revision of the Paraiba River Water Resources Plan; and
  a water pricing study, the latter being implemented with counterpart funds.
  The Bank team and the client agree the PDO is achievable within the closing date, with a sustained effort to keep up the
  current implementation pace. Monthly monitoring and implementation support meetings are carried out with the client.
  Finally, dam safety activities are being followed-up by a Panel of Experts with a need of further speeding up their
  implementation.
