I. PROJECT STATUS AND RATIONALE FOR RESTRUCTURING

A. PROJECT STATUS AND RATIONALE

Summary

   1.   This is the second restructuring of the Peru Centralized Emergency Response Project, requested by the
        Government of Peru (GoP) on November 20, 2024. The proposed restructuring seeks to extend the Project closing
        date from January 10, 2025 to December 31, 2026. The WB agrees on initially a 12-month second-phase extension
        that would allow the Project to reach critical milestones for the achievement of the Project Development
        Objective (PDO). There are no changes to the PDO, project risk ratings, or Project Implementation Unit (PIU).

   2.   This restructuring follows a successful implementation period which entailed the advance of some key
        procurement processes. After several delays compounded by the COVID-19 pandemic and staff changes in the
        GoP and an unsuccessful bidding process for the construction of the 911 Center, the Project was taken out of
        problem status and restructured in November 2023. In the last 12 months, the implementing agency awarded the
        largest contract for the 911 Platform (Emergency Response and Attention System (SAGE), the main element of
        the Integrated 911 platform (Component 2, est. $US 14 million), completed the technical design for the 911 Center
        and is about to award the contracts for the supervision and construction of the 911 Center, positively affecting
        implementation and disbursement pace.

   3.   The Project, in the amount of US$36 million, was approved by the WB Board of Executive Directors on April 7,
        2020, and declared effective on August 17, 2020. The PDO is to provide a more efficient response to emergencies,
        urgencies, and associated helpline requests from the population of Lima Metropolitan area and Callao. The Project
        includes the following components: Component 1, Physical infrastructure; Component 2, Integrated platform;
        Component 3, 911 Central response protocols; Component 4, Interconnection of the video cameras of National
        Police to the 911 Platform; Component 5, Use and appropriation of 911 platform; and, Component 6, Project
        supervision and management, and technical studies.

Project Status

   4.   The Project is implemented by the National Telecommunications Program (PRONATEL), under the Ministry of
        Transport and Communications (MTC). The Project is currently rated Moderately Satisfactory for overall progress
        towards achievement of the PDO and Implementation Performance (IP), with financial management and
        safeguards rated Satisfactory in the last Implementation Status and Results Report archived on June 26, 2024. As
        of November 19, 2024, the Project has disbursed US$2.78 million (8 percent) and has around 80 percent
        committed in ongoing procurement processes.

   5.   Shortcomings in achieving the PDO are due mainly because of implementation challenges faced between 2020
        and mid-2023, which are reflected in the Project's limited disbursement performance. However, in the last 12
        months implementation pace significantly improved reaching some critical milestones.

     6.   During the November 2024 mission, the GoP and the WB reviewed the status of Project implementation. Below
          is a summary of the implementation progress for key activities:

          a. Component 1: Physical Infrastructure. The contract for the design of the 911 Center was awarded and the
             design was finalized and approved by PRONATEL (Table 1, Action 2). The bidding processes for: (i) the
             supervision of the construction; and (ii) the works for the 911 Center, are under final evaluation with a view
             to contract signature and start of the works by February 2025 (Table 2, Action 3 and 4).
          b. Component 2: Integrated 911 platform. The contract for the central element of the unified 911 technology
             platform (SAGE - Emergency Response and Attention System) was awarded in September 2024 and
             implementation started in October 2024. This is the specialized Computer-aided dispatch (CAD) solution that
             will allow assisted, fluid and coordinated management of the complete emergency cycle, from the entry of
             calls at the 911 Center to their resolution by the various First Response Units (FRU) and Line 100 (i.e., Gender-
             based violence hotline). The SAGE will be able to replace, improve or integrate with the existing systems of
             the FRU and Line 100, as appropriate.
          c. Component 3: 911 Central Response Protocols. This activity of defining response protocols and catalogue of
             incidents for the 911 FRU and Line 100 was completed in October 2023.
          d. Component 4: Interconnection of the video cameras of National Police to the 911 Platform. This component
             will not be fully initiated until the physical infrastructure of the 911 Center is under way. However, the video
             management requirement has been considered for the development of the SAGE, and the interconnection
             with the National Police video system will be tested during the integration phase of the SAGE.
          e. Component 5: Use and appropriation of 911 platform. This component will begin in April 2025 now that SAGE
             is under implementation and prior to the completion of the 911 Center. It relates to the training of the FRU
             and Line 100 for the appropriation and use of SAGE.
          f. Component 6: Project supervision and management. After numerous changes in the PIU team, with the
             problems and delays that this has caused, a team of professionals was consolidated and strengthened and is
             expected to continue until the end of the Project (Table 1, Action 2). Similarly, coordination and supervision
             mechanisms have been consolidated within PRONATEL and also with the FRU and Line 100.

7.   Environmental and Social (E&S) Standards. As of November 2024, the Project is compliant with the WB E&S
     standards. The commitments included in the Environmental and Social Commitment Plan are being adequately and
     timely met. The PIU has updated and re-disclosed the project's E&S Assessment, Environmental and Social
     Management Plan, Stakeholder Engagement Plan and Labor Management Plan between June and July 2024. There
     are no changes to project risk ratings, or E&S performance ratings. The E&S risk rating remains Moderate and the E&S
     performance rating is Satisfactory. The Project has a functional Grievance Redress Mechanism capable of receiving
     and addressing project-related complaints.

Rationale for Restructuring

8.   The Ministry of Economy and Finance (MEF) sent the Bank an official letter on November 20, 2024 requesting the
     proposed a two-year extension of the loan closing date from January 10, 2025 to December 31, 2026. The WB will
     proceed with initially a second phase 12-month extension, until January 7, 2026, which would allow the Borrower to
     advance most of the outstanding activities and contracts, reaching critical milestones for achieving the PDO. This
     second-phase extension would also ensure that the support required for the GoP to implement the Centralized
     Emergency Response System (911) can be maintained, and the action plan highlighted in Table 2 can be achieved. A
     third-phase extension will be evaluated by the WB in mid-2025 to determine whether an additional – and final –
     extension merits consideration.

9.   This Action Plan detailed in Table 2 was prepared and agreed with PRONATEL and MTC, to ensure timely completion
     of the remaining Project activities by the extended closing date.

         Table 2: Action Plan

             Actions                                                                                     Due Date
          1. Sign contract for supervision of 911 Center works                                           January 2025
          2. Initiate works for the construction of the 911 Center (Component 1, est. 12 months)         March 2025
          3. Launch Use and appropriation of 911 platform (Component 5)                                  May 2025
          4. Launch SAGE operations (Component 2)                                                        July 2025
          5. Train EPR and Line 100 on use of SAGE                                                       September 2025

10. Client commitment and capacity. The WB and the client have agreed on the below additional measures to achieve
     the estimated completion dates of the remaining activities:

         a) Government commitment and relevance of PDO. The client, including MTC and MEF, have expressed strong
            interest in completing the Project. The authorities at MTC and PRONATEL have demonstrated a high level of
            commitment towards implementing the Project and have already made notable improvements in Project
            implementation and management, with the full PIU dedicated team in place. The Project has been highlighted
            as a priority for the GoP and was explicitly mentioned during the President’s speech to the Congress in July
            2023.1 The Ministry of Interior and PRONATEL signed the cooperation agreement for 911 operations.2 In
            addition, the 911 Platform and the design for the 911 Center were launched publicly in September and
            November 2024 respectively.3 In that regard, the PDO remains highly relevant under the Country Partnership
            Framework (CPF) for Peru (FY23-27), Objective 3 “Enhance Delivery of Public Services”.
         b) Gender-Based Violence (GBV) prevention. The Project is also unique for the country as it is building a
            comprehensive, multi-agency 911 response center with different FRU but also unique from a GBV prevention
            perspective as it aims to integrate 911 Platform benefits for Line 100 (GBV hotline), including accelerating
