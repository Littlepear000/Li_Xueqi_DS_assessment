I. PROJECT STATUS AND RATIONALE FOR RESTRUCTURING

A. Project Status

1. The Project was approved by the Board on May 5, 2022 and the financing agreement was signed by the Government
   of Central Africa Republic on May 19, 2022.

2. Unfortunately, progress on the activities which were supposed to take place to support implementation of the project and
   reaching effectiveness was slow, due to capacity constraints in the project’s supervising ministry (Ministry of SMEs,
   Artisans and Private Sector Development).

3. The capacity constraints translated in what had been designated in the Financing Agreement as the interim Project
   Implementation Unit (PIU) (the PIU for the Digital Governance project, nicknamed AGIR by the name of a former project
   it used to run) not being able to take the necessary steps to deploy the Project Preparation Advance (PPA) and hire
   new staff for forming a new PIU. A decree was issued by the supervising ministry to set up a new commission to oversee
   the recruiting of the Project PIU staff and obtaining direct oversight of financial allocations, in contradiction with the
   project’s financing agreement. The request for direct procurement decision making outside the World Bank-required
   process, resulted in blocking procurement of goods and services for the project.

4. This hindered implementation progress as the AGIR PIU, which was mandated by the Financial Agreement to handle
   the project and the PPA process did not commence the processes required to implement key activities. The Project
   Preparation Advance thus could not get deployed and has resulted in delayed effectiveness.

5.   While the project is currently behind schedule, with initial delays due to the COVID-19 pandemic but primarily due to
     procurement delays cause by capacity constraints in the supervising Ministry, the project had started some PPA-related
     activities before being stopped by the Ministry of SMEs, Artisans and Private Sector Development. As a result, the
     project’s overall rating turned to Moderately Satisfactory. However, after these initial steps, all activities stalled, and
     the recruitment of various international technical experts did not take place, particularly those required to be hired to
     support the Project Implementation Unit.

6. This has also resulted in delayed effectiveness of the project and as such none of the project components are yet
   operational. The effectiveness conditions were not met, and a first extension of effectiveness date took place to push
   effectiveness to December 31st 2022. But that date with the current institutional setup was unattainable as well by the
   government.

7. The situation led to a request signed by the Prime Minister of CAR, who is also Minister of Planning and Governor for
   the World Bank, for restructuring, asking as primary change for the project to be put under the purview of the Ministry
   in charge of Finance and Budget, instead of the Ministry of SMEs, Artisans and Private Sector Development. The
   Ministry in charge of Finance and Budget has indeed stronger capacity and is already hosting the AGIR PIU.

B. Rationale for Restructuring

        CAR Investment and Business Competitiveness for Employment (P176274)




8. General. The Bank and the Borrower jointly undertook a mission in October 2022 and conducted a detailed review of
   the status of the project to agree on potential changes. It was agreed that the PDO remains very relevant and is fully
   aligned with both the Private Sector and skills development sector priorities. However, effectiveness conditions cannot
   be met under the current institutional arrangements.

9. COVID-19 Global Pandemic. COVID-19 has also contributed to the slow implementation of the project. It is expected
   that the proposed changes with this Level 2 restructuring will improve project performance, ensure all project
   activities are closely aligned with the Government’s current priorities, and address the situation in the private sector
   and Education sector to enable the Government to continue to implement the activities.

10. Government management efficiency. While the government initially wanted to distribute PIUs for several projects
    outside the Ministry of Finance and Budget, with a view to slowly build the capacity of recipient ministries (such as
    the Ministry of SMEs, Artisans and Private Sector Development), it eventually realized that the capacity of such
    ministries was not yet sufficiently developed to reach the effectiveness conditions. The government eventually
    decided to increase efficiency by changing the implementing agency from the Ministry of SMEs, Artisans and Private
    Sector Development to the Ministry of Finance and Budget. This with the aim to increase speed of reaching
    effectiveness and delivery and ensure a stronger adherence to World Bank rules and procedures. It is also of the
    opinion of the Government, and agreed by the World Bank, that the project being hosted in the Ministry of Budget
    and Finance will facilitate the cross-institutional work which is required by the project activities.

11. Centrality of Guarantee fund component warrants centrality of Ministry of Finance and Budget. The SME partial
    loan guarantee fund planned under the project is anyhow under the purview of the Ministry of Finance and Budget,
    so the Government eventually decided for this reason to ensure oversight of the entire project, as other components
    articulate around the ability for SMEs to borrow for growth and jobs.

II. DESCRIPTION OF PROPOSED CHANGES

12. Proposed Changes. The proposed restructuring includes: (a) change in the implementing agency from the Ministry
    of SMEs, Artisans and Private Sector Development to the Ministry in charge of Finance and Budget, (b) change in
    related institutional arrangements and (c) a minor change in one of the project activities to increase direct support
    to SMEs.

13. (a) Change in the Implementing Agency The anchoring point of the project, initially planned under the Ministry of
    SMEs, Artisans and Private Sector Development, should be henceforth placed under the Ministry in charge of Finance
    and Budget.

        a. This change is required because of the transversality of the project objectives and the centrality for the project of
           the guarantee fund which will be under the supervision of the Minister of Finance and Budget, but also to create
           synergies with existing project management units, gain efficiency, and productivity vis-à-vis the legal and
           administrative procedures of the World Bank.
        b. The technical capacity of the departments of the Ministry of Finance and Budget to host a dedicated project
           management unit has already been assessed satisfactorily by the World Bank (most recently in the context of
           Public Sector Digital Governance project).
        c. The current mechanism indicated in the project and in the funding agreement, whereas the PIU of the Public
           Sector Digital Governance (known as the "AGIR" unit) plays an interim role related to the Project Preparation
           Advance, remains the same and unchanged. This unit will therefore continue, under the supervision of the Minister
           in charge of Finance and Budget, to implement the work required to reach effectiveness conditions and the initial
           implementation of activities related to the PPA. This includes:
                    The recruitment of a new project management unit, whose selection process and selection of personnel,
                    should be deemed satisfactory by the World Bank.
                    Finalization of the Project Operations Manual.
                    The implementation of preliminary studies planned under the project preparation advance fund.
                    Finalization of the preliminary arrangements necessary for the physical location of the new management
                    unit (including studies and rehabilitation works of the project coordination building – unless the
                    Government decides on alternative arrangements related to the new supervision of the Ministry in charge
                    of Finance and Budget).

14. (b) Change in the steering committee of the project mentioned in the financing agreement, which should henceforth be
    chaired by the Ministry responsible for Finance and Budget.

        a. Schedule 2, Section I. A. 1 of the Financing Agreement should indicate that the steering committee will be chaired
           by the Minister in charge of Finance and Budget.
        b. In the same paragraph, the Ministry of SMEs, Artisans and Private Sector Development should henceforth be
           named as one of the participants of the said committee instead of Chair.
        c. Schedule 2, Section I. A. 3. (a) should also indicate that the project management unit should be established within
           the Ministry of Finance and Budget.

15. (c) A minor amendment to the funding agreement should be introduced on Schedule I, component 3.1 of the project on
    business support. This is requested by the government, and agreed by the World Bank Project team, to complement the
    technical support to eligible beneficiary companies with the inclusion of assistance in terms of the provision of goods and
    services, so as to further support the customized growth plans developed with SMEs through the provision of technical
    assistance to the said enterprises.
         a. This would translate in the Financing Agreement adding an additional paragraph (g) under Schedule I, Part 3:
             Supporting Business and Workers - Part 3.1: Build Linkages and Support Firms, with the following text:
             “(g) Providing technical assistance and acquiring equipment to selected SMEs to support the implementation of
             the selected development plans”
         b. This change does not affect the PDO neither the indicators of the project, nor the Withdrawal of the Proceeds of
             the Financing table, as the point 3.1. (g) would be defacto included in row (1) of the table, thus without any change
             to said table.

16. Effectiveness deadline. The government request also includes a demand to extend the project effectiveness deadline from
    December 31, 2022 to June 30, 2023, with an understanding that the effectiveness conditions could be reach earlier,
    around February or March 2023. There is no change to the effectiveness conditions which remain that:
       1. The Recipient has established the PIU with composition and mandate acceptable to the Association in accordance
            with Section I.A.3 of Schedule 2 to the Financing Agreement;
       2. the Recipient has hired and appointed on the basis of terms of reference, qualifications, integrity and experience
            acceptable to the Association a Project Coordinator, a procurement specialist, financial management specialist, and
            an environmental and social specialist
       3. The Recipient has prepared, adopted, and submitted to the Association, the Project Implementation Manual in
            accordance with Section I.B.1 (a) in form and substance satisfactory to the Association.
       4. The LMP, GBV/SEA/SH Action Plans each in form and substance acceptable to the Association, shall have been duly
            adopted by the Recipient in accordance with the ESCP.

                Condition 3 and 4 have been met and are in the process of being approved and the extension of the effectiveness
                deadline will allow conditions 1 and 2 to be met. Regarding condition 4. all the required E&S instruments (ESMF -
                LMP - SEA/SH Plan - SMP Plan - IPP - SEP and ESCP) have been developed, approved by World Bank and disclosed
                both in country and on the World Bank website. Currently the recruitment of E&S specialists (environmental
                specialist, a social development specialist and a GBV specialist) by PIU is ongoing.
