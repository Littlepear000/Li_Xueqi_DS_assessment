  Program Development Objective (from Program Appraisal Document)

 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  Following approval by the Board on December 15, 2020 and signing of the Loan Agreement on December 22, 2020, the loan became effective on
  February 25, 2021. The Mid-Term Review (MTR) of the PforR was carried out between June 12 and 21, 2023.

  The PMU was formally created on November 10, 2021 as well as the joint Steering Committee (with the TREEA project financed by the French
  Agency for Development). Three Steering Committees took place on February 14, 2022, November 16, 2022 and November 13, 2023. The fourth is
  scheduled for mid-June 2024.

  Ratings. Based on the findings of the supervision mission carried out in May 2024, the rating measuring the likelihood of achieving the Program
  Development Objective (PDO) is downgraded from Moderately Satisfactory (MS) to Moderately Unsatisfactory (MU), reflecting delays in several key
  activities, while the rating measuring progress in implementation (IP) is maintained at MS. It is expected that the PDO rating can be upgraded back
  to MS by the next ISR following the approval of the extension.


  Construction of 4 wholesale markets in Berkane, Meknes, Agadir and Meknes (Result Area 2). The Ministry of the Interior has decided in May
  2024, to replace the Order (Arrete) with a law, due to the need to make all the necessary adjustments to the regulation of modernized wholesale
  markets (WMs). Indeed, an Order does not allow the tax system governing the WMs to be repealed, nor the status of agents, nor the enactment of
  sanctions. This new element as well as the delays in the preparatory studies for the construction of the 4 wholesale markets and some initial delays
  in the setting up of the Centers supporting youths for RA#1 will generate a need of an extension of the closing date estimated at this stage, at 22
  months (from the end of December 2025 to the end of October 2027).


  Progress on other two Result Areas. RA#1 (support to youth) and RA#3 (climate-smart and digital) are performing well now. For example, for the
  RA#1, 18 Centers supporting the Youth entrepreneurs are now operational and 2,374 young people have their business plans approved at the end
  of March 2024 compared to a revised annual target of 5,000 for the end of December 2024.

  For RA#3, the ARDNA platform, managed by the ONCA for delivering digitally advisory services, has been updated and more than 4,141 users are
  registered in total (including 530 registered in 2022 and 1,356 registered in 2023.

  The next implementation support mission is scheduled in November 2024.

                                 The revision of the regulatory framework is key to implementing the agricultural wholesale market program and
                                 needs to be completed upfront before any development of market facilities would take place. There are two
                                 activities:
                                 The first one is related to the promulgation of amendments to Article 83 of Organic Law No. 113-14 clarifying
                                 in particular the relationship between the private sector and municipalities in terms of the creation and choice
                                 of wholesale market management method modernized. The Ministry of the Interior is in the process of
                                 overhauling the 3 texts of laws (on the regions, municipalities and prefectures and provinces) to merge them
                                 into a single text. However, as it is an organic law, this process will be slow and will take time. Given the time
                                 required for the amendment of this law and its low impact on the fact that the private sector alone does not
                                 manage a wholesale market, the risk of not achieving this indicator within the time allowed for the program is
                                 significant. It was therefore proposed, during the restitution meeting of the World Bank mission held on July
    Comments
                                 18, 2022, to replace this indicator with other indicators linked to the results, in particular the signing of
                                 partnership agreements for the implementation of the four MDGs.
                                 This change will be carried out during the restructuring of the program after the MTR.

                                 The second one is related to the publication of the Order (arrete) of the Ministry of the Interior setting the terms
                                 relating to the creation and operationalization of modernized wholesale markets: In May 2024, the Ministry of
                                 the Interior has decided to replace the Order with a law, due to the need to make all the necessary
                                 adjustments to the regulation of modernized wholesale markets (MdGs). Indeed, an Order does not allow the
                                 tax system governing the MdGs to be repealed, nor the status of agents, nor the enactment of sanctions. The
                                 draft law is currently being developed, based on the content of the latest version of the Order.
