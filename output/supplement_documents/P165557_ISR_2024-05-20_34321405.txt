 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  Key achievements by component are presented below:
  Strengthening teaching practices in schools (component 1 achievements)
         A national in-service training framework has been developed to guide all training for school heads, teachers, and School Improvement
          Support Officers (SISOs).
         Over 70,000 teachers in 10,500 schools have been trained in the use of differentiated learning (DL) methods to support teachers in
          tailoring lessons to the needs, strengths, and weaknesses of learners. Teacher guides (with structured lessons) on DL have been
          developed and provided to teachers in lower and upper primary in the intervention schools.

         The National Teaching Council (NTC) has developed and provided lesson observation tools for SISOs to coach and mentor teachers. To
          date, 7,220 schools and 70,714 teachers have been supported with coaching and mentoring on various topics. As of December 2023,
          1,608 SISOs have been trained on coaching and mentoring strategies to improve DL implementation and data collection using digital
          platforms.
  Strengthening school support, management and resourcing (component 2 achievements)
         Learning grants. The project has disbursed two full cycles of learning grants to over 10,500 GALOP schools in 2021 and 2022 for a total
          of GHS 84.95 million. In 2023, a base grant of GHS 25.7 million was disbursed followed by a per capita grant on December 31, 2023. In
          terms of learning grants utilization rates, GALOP schools have utilized 95 and 88 percent of the learning grants in 2022 and 2021,
          respectively meeting the benchmark of at least 80 percent utilization required for disbursement against the relevant PBC.
         Training. The project has funded the training of 1,104 key officials in the management of learning grants, including district, budget,
          internal audit, and supervision directors. District education officials were also trained to strengthen their capacity.
         Engagement. Under the project, School Management Committees (SMCs) have been reconstituted to ensure greater community
          oversight. The project is supporting the strengthening of the SMCs and improving monitoring and collaboration between SMCs and PTAs
          to ensure their strong participation in School Performance Appraisal Meetings (SPAMs) which will help improve basic learning outcomes in
          schools.
         Education Outcomes Fund (EOF). Through the Ghana Education Outcomes Fund, service providers have initiated activities to provide
          educational services to out-of-school children (OOSC) and at-risk youth. In March 2023, 17,332 OOSC in Cohort 1 commenced their
          Accelerated Learning Program (ALP) and completed in September 2023 before transitioning into the mainstream program. Cohort 1 was
          assessed during the ALP for the baseline and endline in May and August 2023, respectively. The Cohort 2 beneficiaries started the ALP in
          October 2023.
  Strengthening accountability systems for learning (component 3 achievements)
         Assessment. The National Council on Curriculum and Assessment (NaCCA) has (i) developed a National Assessment Framework; (ii)
          organized two rounds of the National Standardized Test (NST) in mathematics and English for over 400,000 P4 learners in the first round;
          and for 456,481 P2 learners and 603,428 P4 learners in 15,716 public basic schools and some selected private schools in Ghana; (iii)
          developed and analyzed the NST assessment data disaggregated by domain, gender, and public/private schools, analyzed results at the
          school level, and shared this information with schools and district officials to improve learning outcomes; and (iv) scheduled the next NST
          for July 2024 targeting 600,000 P4 learners, and 570,000 P6 learners with capacity strengthening and technical asupport from the
          Australia Council for Education Research (ACER).
         Accountability dashboard. MoE has developed a functional accountability dashboard to support evidence-based decision making. The
          dashboard which includes a feedback mechanism for users to provide feedback on the visualizations, can be accessed at
          <<https://moedatadashboard.com/msrc_dashboard>>.
         Teacher deployment and transfer. The Ghana Education Service (GES) has developed a teacher deployment and transfer strategy
          which has led to an increase in the percentage of targeted primary schools with pupil-trained-teacher-ratio (PTTR) below 50:1 from 75
          percent in 2019 to 81 percent in 2022.
  Key areas of focus going forward
         There is a need to ensure that capitation grants are released in a timely manner to enable schools to better plan and effectively utilize
          funds for learning gains.
         There is a need to prioritize the placement of trained teachers to GALOP schools to ensure that the KG level PTTR continues to decline.
         The accountability dashboard has been operationalized and, school management committees have been trained. The team
          expects schools and education officials to start using the data for decision making, planning and accountability purposes. The verification
          of the relevant PBC is expected in the next couple of months.
         Under the GEOP, lot 7 (targeting urban areas) is being re-tendered. The procurement package has been submitted to the Public
          Procurement Authority (PPA) for approval, following which Lot 7 will be advertised.
