 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The program has made significant progress since the previous mission in June 2023, with key actions undertaken across several areas and
  disbursements of USD47.5 million during July - December 2023.
  The Ministry of Micro Small and Medium Enterprises (MoMSME) has approved an action plan for Digital Technology, Monitoring & Evaluation, and
  Policy Capacity-Building (USD7.5 million). Additionally, the first phase of the Integrated National MSME Digital Portal (INMDP) has been completed,
  focusing on operationalization of dashboards and data analytics functions (USD5 million). MSME Strategic Investment Plans (SIP) from participating
  states have been approved, specifically addressing cross-cutting themes like 'Gender, Greening, Technology, and Private Sector (GGTP)' (USD30
  million).
  Further, schemes for CLCS-TU improvement and scale-up implementation have been approved (USD5 million). The redesigned ZED Scheme has
  witnessed substantial participation, with over 80,716 MSMEs certified with bronze tier certification, 313 MSMEs under Silver tier and 305 under the
  gold tier. The MSME Council, established to coordinate interventions between the Center and State Governments, has been meeting biannually.
  Implementation plans for Digital Technology, Policy, and Monitoring & Evaluation functions have been submitted and approved. The number of
  NBFCs and MSMEs registering on the TReDS bill discounting platform has increased, and the MSME Green Investment and Financing for
  Transformation (GIFT) scheme and the MSME Scheme for Promotion and Investment in Circular Economy (SPICE) have been launched to
  enhance adoption of sustainable practices by MSMEs.
  However, the following implementation issues need to be addressed to ensure that project development objectives are met. Firstly, MoMSME needs
  to ensure adequate financing to SIPs approved to ensure that key SIP actions are achieved. Secondly, the MoMSME needs to urgently ensure that
  the Environmental and Social Assessment framework and recommendations are fully implemented across all program components. Lastly,
  MoMSME needs to expedite operationalization of the Online Dispute Resolution (ODR) platform.
