 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The Multiphase Programmatic Approach (MPA) became effective on August 16, 2022, with the declaration of effectiveness for Madagascar on
  August 16, 2022, CCARDESA on September 16, 2022, IGAD on September 22, 2022, and Ethiopia on November 28, 2022. As of February 28,
  2023, US$61.57 million (or 7.8 percent of total commitment) had been disbursed.

  In Ethiopia, disbursements since effectiveness have reached US$ 55.92 million, which amounts to about 14 percent of the financing to Ethiopia. A
  portion of this disbursement has already been paid to UNOPS to support third-party implementation in the Tigray region. UNOPS has already
  completed the various upstream assessments that are required to inform efforts to rebuild the capacity for agricultural service delivery in Tigray and
  will begin reaching the beneficiaries within weeks.
  Madagascar is working on the preparation of a Contingency Emergency Response Component (CERC) to address the impact of food and fuel
  inflation by supporting 500,000 poor and vulnerable households. It has also initiated its support to the first phase of the Agriculture Census and is
  working on hiring additional staff to strengthen the capacity of its Project Implementation Units.
  IGAD and CCARDESA have received a first tranche of financing and are in the process of recruiting specialists (procurement, M&E and value
  chain) and procuring equipment to make its teams operational. Both institutions have started cooperation on the establishment of a Regional
  Center of Leadership and CCARDESA has initiated a review of the early warning systems in the region.
  An implementation support mission is expected with all four borrowers/recipients before the end of the fiscal year.

 Multiphase Programmatic Approach (MPA) Status

 MPA Table Body
 The MPA became effective on August 16, 2022, with the declaration of effectiveness for Madagascar, CCARDESA on September 16, 2022, IGAD on
 September 22, 2022, and Ethiopia on November 28, 2022. As of February 28, 2023, US$61.57 million (or 7.8 percent of total commitment) had been
 disbursed. The borrowers/recipients have been working on staffing their PIUs, and finalizing their annual work plans and operational manuals so that
 activities can pick up over the next few months. In the meantime, Comoros, Kenya, Malawi, Mozambique, Somalia, Tanzania and the African Union
 Commission are progressing with the preparation of operations to join the MPA in subsequent phases.
