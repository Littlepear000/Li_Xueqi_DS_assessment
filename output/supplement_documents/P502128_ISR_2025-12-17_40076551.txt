 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The project was approved by the Board on June 18, 2024, and the legal agreements were signed on September 17, 2024,
  and became effective on December 04, 2024. The Project aims to address some of the most critical challenges to
  improve access to quality basic education and strengthen capacity for sector management in CAR.
  The implementation status of the PARE project shows progress in several areas. The school infrastructure construction
  and rehabilitation program, which typically experiences delays, has recently achieved notable progress. The
  procurement process for selecting contractors for the first phase of civil works in six prefectures: Bangui, Ombella,
  Mpoko, Kemo, Lobaye, et Nana-Gribizi—has either been completed or reached the contract finalization stage. This
  phase covers the construction of 564 classrooms, 7 lower secondary schools (collèges de proximité), and 87 latrine
  blocks. To date, 5,232 qualified contract teachers have been recruited and assigned to teaching positions nationwide,
  out of the 6,350 planned under the Project. The development of textbooks and teachers’ guides for the first three grades
  of primary education, including materials for the introduction of Sango language instruction, has been finalized.
  Additionally, procurement for printing more than 1,200,000 copies of these materials is well advanced. Furthermore,
  the bilingual education (Sango-French), accelerated learning (PEA), and remediation programs are being implemented
  as scheduled. Recruitment of key personnel for the Project Implementation Unit is almost complete, except for the
  social safeguard specialist, who is expected to be hired in January 2026.
