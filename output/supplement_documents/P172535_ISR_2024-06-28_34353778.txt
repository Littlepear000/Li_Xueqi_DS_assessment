1. OBJECTIVE


 1.1 Development Objective
   Original Development Objective (Approved as part of Approval package on 26-May-2021)
   To improve the quality of housing and public space for vulnerable households, including migrants from Venezuela, in
   selected municipalities.
   Has the Development Objective been changed since Board Approval of the Project Objective?

   No

4. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
   The project implementation continues to increase pace reflecting the efforts to overcome obstacles in implementation;
   all of which can be evidenced in the acceleration of procurement processes, the implementation of key activities and,
   most importantly, in the concrete benefits for vulnerable households.
