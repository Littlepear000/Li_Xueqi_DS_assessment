 Project Development Objectives

  Project Development Objective (from Project Appraisal Document)
  To strengthen the capacity of the National Institute of Statistics of Djibouti (INSD) to produce and disseminate timely and reliable statistics in a
  sustainable manner and modernize the Recipient's selected economic and fiscal management tools and processes in Djibouti.

 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

             Component 1 (Upgrading the physical infrastructure and equipment of the Statistics Office):The Bidding document (DAO) was finally
             completed in mid-June and is expected to be published by early September.
            Component 1 (Strengthening capacity of the National Statistics Office): Since October 2021, INSTAD and the authorities have made
             progress on a number of activities in relation to strengthening the capacity of their statistical system. INSTAD has organized a consultative
             workshop with key actors to discuss the technical instruments of the population census and held a financing roundtable to close the
             budget gap. The census budget itself had been reviewed and cost-reducing measures had been taken into consideration. The government
             has also committed 1 million USD to support the census within the state budget. The GEDES project is financing the pilot cartography and
             pilot census as well as the primary cartography phase. The World Bank team is providing regular technical assistance to the Institute of

              Statistics on the population census operation, advising them on the choice of technology, staffing, and logistics of the operation. The pilot
              cartography is planned to start as soon as possible after a procurement plan is agreed and approved. INSTAD is also working on
              improving the production, analysis and dissemination of key economic statistics with technical assistance from AFRISTAT. Long term
              experts have also been engaged under GEDES to support the institutional transition of INSTAD. In particular, an advisor to the director
              general has been onboarded as well as a long term expert in the geographic information systems department who is also supporting the
              census cartography.
             Component 2 ( Subcomponent Debt management) :The convention with UNCTAD for the upgrading and maintenance of the IT system of
              the debt management department has finally been signed and the technical assistance is planned to start during the second half of 2022 .
              The permanent secretary of the National debt committee was hired and is fully operational.
             Component 2 ( Subcomponent macroeconomic modeling): The Directorate of Economy and Planning has finally opted for a reimbursable
              technical assistance with AFRISTAT for the development of a macroeconomic forecasting model and the timely production and publication
              of regular economic statistics and dashboard. The contract is pending signatures o of the empowered government.
             Component 2 (Subcomponent budget preparation and investment planning): the hiring of long-term consultant to support the development
              of a new Budget framework and the preparation of a medium-term framework has been delayed due to the withdrawal of the selected
              consultant. Since he was the only qualified candidate among those who applied to the position, the project unit has decided to relaunch
              the call for applications.
             Component 2 : Three websites from the Department of Economy and Planning, the Public Debt Department and the Budget Department
              are now fully operational.
