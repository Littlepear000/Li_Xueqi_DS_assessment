 Project Development Objectives

  Project Development Objective (from Project Appraisal Document)
  To strengthen regional, evidence-based fisheries management in the South-West Indian Ocean Region and to improve competitiveness in the
  fisheries sector in the Maldives

  Has the Project Development Objective been changed since Board Approval of the Project Objective?
  No

 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The project was approved on May 16, 2023. Financing Agreements were signed with the Republic of Maldives on June 18, 2023, and with the
  Indian Ocean Commission on June 22, 2023. Implementation activities are being initiated by the two implementing agencies - the Indian Ocean
  Commission (IOC) for Component 1 of the Project, and the Ministry of Fisheries, Marine Resources and Agriculture (MoFMRA) for components 2
  and 3 of the Project.
