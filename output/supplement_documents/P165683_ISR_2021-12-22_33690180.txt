 Project Development Objectives

  Project Development Objective (from Project Appraisal Document)
  To (i) strengthen capacity for integrated water resources management in the State; (ii) improve reliability of water services in the Agreste and
  Borborema regions; and (iii) improve the operational efficiency of water and wastewater services in the Joao Pessoa Metropolitan Region.

 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The loan agreement was signed in December 1, 2020; and was declared effective in January 2021. The Project Management Unit at the State
  Secretariat for Infrastructure, Water Resources and Environment (SEIRHMA), the Project Implementation Unit at the State Company of Water and
  Sanitation (Companhia de Água e Esgotos da Paraíba, CAGEPA), and a coordination team at the State Water Resources Management Executive
  Agency (AESA) are progressing to advance technical and procurement documentation, with several processes to be launched in the first six months
  of 2022. Despite the initial delay, the client with the Bank are working closely to speed up the implementation and allow for the achievement of the
  Project Development Objective (PDO) in the original project time. The Bank Team will continue to support the Borrower with various experts,
  including procurement, wastewater treatment, water resources management, and non-revenue water specialists. Fortnightly monitoring and
  implementation support meetings have been scheduled for the most critical activities, including the Cariri water system, the wastewater collection

 Overall Comments IR

  The beneficiary feedback chosen for this project refers to the “level of beneficiary satisfaction with project activities in the Transparaíba Water
  Supply System” indicator. It was agreed that the indicator will be measured along three rounds of beneficiary surveys. The first round was
  envisaged to be carried out during the first year of project implementation; the second, after the completion of the Curimataú Branch
  pipeline system; the third, after the completion of the Cariri Branch pipeline system. Since project effectiveness was declared on
  January 2021 the implementation of works of the Cariri Branch is delayed; the bidding documents (turn-key) of the Cariri Branch include the
  beneficiary surveys as part of the contractor’s obligations. The initial survey was also postponed because of the social distancing measures
  followed since the Covid-19 outbreak. Regarding the Curimataú Branch, works financed by counterpart funds are scheduled to initiate in the
  first quarter of 2022. The client has locally disseminated channels for raising concerns and complaints and requiring information on these
  works. The indicator will be updated in the next ISR.
