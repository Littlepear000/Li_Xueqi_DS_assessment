I. PROJECT STATUS AND RATIONALE FOR RESTRUCTURING

A. Project Status
1.       The original financing for the Tax Administration and Statistical System Modernization Project (P163711) was
approved by the IDA’s Board of Executive Directors on February 28, 2020. The total financing for the project was US$35
million: a US$17.5 million equivalent IDA credit (Credit No. IDA-65460-KG) and a US$17.5 million equivalent IDA grant
(Grant No. IDA-D566-KG). The project was declared effective on December 9, 2020. The original closing date was
September 30, 2025.
2.    The PDO is to improve effectiveness of tax collection and the national statistical system. The Project
Implementing Agencies are the State Tax Service and the National Statistics Committee. The project comprises five
components:
        Component 1. STS Institutional Development:(US$2,070,000)
        Component 2. STS Operational Development:(US$14,490,000)
        Component 3. NSC Institutional Development:(US$4,800,000)
        Component 4. NSC Data Development:(US$11,820,000)
        Component 5. Project Management:(US$ 1,820,000).
3.      The project was restructured in June 2024 to allow for an 18-month extension of the project closing date to
March 31, 2027. The purpose of the extension was to complete the implementation of new Information and
Communication Technology (ICT) systems and business processes in both State Tax Service (STS) and the National Statistics
Committee (NSC) and thereby achieve the PDO. The extension of the closing date was necessary because of an eleven-
month delay in Parliamentary ratification of the financing agreements and resulting effectiveness delay, delays related to
COVID-19 disruptions, and, for NSC, delays in the engagement of the NSC Twinning Partner. Following the closing date
extension, the rating of Progress toward achievement of the PDO was upgraded from Moderately Unsatisfactory to
Moderately Satisfactory. Overall Implementation Progress continued to be rated Moderately Satisfactory
4.       However, after strong progress in implementation and procurement preparation, project execution slowed
significantly from mid-June 2024. The procurement of key packages for the STS and NSC has experienced significant delays
due to frequent staff turnovers in the Project Management Units (PMUs) and STS and NSC specialists. An investigation of
project activities by law enforcement agencies has also caused significant disruption. The PMUs lost three out of four
procurement specialists. These staff were only recently replaced. The STS National Project Coordinators changed twice
between June 2024 and February 2025. Staff turnover has caused disruptions to and delays in decision-making on ongoing
contracts, procurement processing, and project management. This has particularly affected procurement of ICT goods and
services, which represent approximately 75 percent of upcoming contract amounts. Considering these challenges, the
Progress toward achievement of the PDO and Overall Implementation Progress (IP) ratings have been downgraded from
Moderately Satisfactory to Moderately Unsatisfactory in October 2024.
Status of STS-implemented Components 1 and 2 (implemented by STS).
5.      Since June 2024 STS has not undertaken any significant new procurement due to the disruptions highlighted
above. Of 34 planned procurement packages under Components 1 and 2, 24 contracts with a value of US$5,605,189 have
been completed, two with a value of about US$1.5 million are at initial stages of preparation, and the remaining nine
contracts with a value of US$11,609,891 have not been initiated.
6.     Implementation of the major technical assistance contract for business process re-engineering (BPR) for the
operational areas of STS and the development of the Second STS Tax Administration Information Management System
(ISNAK-2) specifications is delayed. STS management has decided to cancel the contract for BPR and develop ISNAK-2 in-
house using recourses provided by the State Budget.

7.       The end-of-project targets for the two PDO indicators related to components 1 and 2 still can be achieved,
although the risk of non-achieving them will increase if STS modernization is further delayed. The first indicator
measures the percent reduction of VAT gap which tracks improvements tax compliance through higher effectiveness of
tax administration as a result of modernization of the STS. As of October 2024, the percent reduction was at 13.20 percent
against the end-of-project target of 12 percent. STS decision to cancel this TA and use own specialists for tax gap
calculation could make it more difficult to calculate the VAT gap, but will not jeopardize achievement of target values, if
STS is successful in modernizing tax administration. The second indicator, the cost of tax compliance, measures time spent
on all procedures related to time compliance. As of October 2024, the value was 76.30 days against the end-of-project
target of 40 days. The reduction of time needed for tax compliance serves as an indicator of improved tax administration
procedures achieved because of technical and procedural modernization of STS. The current high value is a result of
changes in reporting arrangements and expected to go down when taxpayers internalize new requirements and STS
strengthen their IT systems. There is a risk that the end-project value of the cost of tax compliance indicator may not be
achieved if STS modernization is further delayed.
Status of NSC-implemented Components 3 and 4 (implemented by NSC)
8.      The procurement of the primary and secondary data centers (estimated cost US$4 million) is significantly
delayed. As an effect and due to the NSC not making a selection, the validity and security of the bids originally submitted
had expired. The retender has been now initiated by the NSC with the draft evaluation report to be submitted by March
31, 2025.
9.      NSC coordination with the Twinning Partner (TP) has improved but TP activities are delayed. The draft
description of future business processes was scheduled for November 2024. This activity is now scheduled for completion
by November 30, 2025.
10.     The procurement process for modernization of the NSC website is ongoing. NSC is in the final stages of
completing the evaluation report and NSC expects to award the contract in April 2025. The contract will be completed
before the current project closing date of March 31, 2027.
11.     Implementation of the E-reporting system is on track for completion by the end of CY2025 with full integration
of all 146 forms. NSC has established 24/7 helpdesks to support staff and conducts weekly training sessions via
videoconference.
12.     Other TA activities are progressing and expected to be completed on time.
13.     The achievement of two PDO indicators related to Components 3 and 4, specifically, reduction of time between
completion of data collection and release of key statistical indicators to the public and openness of official statistics:
measures coverage and openness of data across 21 data categories is on track. The proposed restructuring will not affect
the probability of these indicators’ achievement.
B.      Rationale for Restructuring
14.     The Ministry of Finance (MoF) has requested restructuring of TASSMP and cancellation of US$8.75 million of
the IDA credit by letter dated February 25, 2025. Through the same letter, MoF has transmitted the STS letter dated
January 29, 2025 laying out the rationale for the project restructuring, and notified about the decision to procure below
two ICT and two consulting service packages (under components implemented by STS) with total value of US$8.77 million
using resources allocated from the State Budget:
        Development of ISNAK-2 (budget US$5,365,920)
        Procurement of data center and other IT equipment (budget US$2,931,279)
        Procurement of consulting services for the development and implementation of a risk management and tax gap
        calculation mechanism (budget US$ 432,560)

                  Procurement of services to develop regulatory documents (budget of US$ 48,000).
15.      Through an order of the Chairman of the Cabinet of Ministers of the Kyrgyz Republic dated January 29, 2025,
No. 18-17/2 "On the tasks of tax administration" the State-Owned Enterprise “Salyk Service” was mandated to develop
of ISNAK-2 with financing from the State Budget. Further, in the meeting of the Situational Control Center for
Monitoring the Implementation of Decisions of the President and the Cabinet of Ministers of the Kyrgyz Republic on
January 23, 2025, it was decided to purchase of equipment for the STS data center using State Budget funds. Funding
for these activities was allocated in the 2025 State Budget. According to STS, these procurement activities can be
completed more expeditiously using State Budget and national procurement procedures. It was also decided not to
procure consulting services for the development and implementation of a risk management mechanism and tax gap
calculation and for development of regulatory documents using the project funds in order to avoid duplication with other
activities and due to changed priorities of STS. This may complicate the calculation of VAT gap, however, at the same time
it will not jeopardize the achievement of target values for reduction of VAT gap (PDO indicator 1), provided that STS
successfully modernizes tax administration. STS has the capacity to progress on modernization processes; the main risk is
the risk of delays in ICT modernization. The project will continue providing TA support to implementation of ISNAK-2,
which procurement is expected to launch in April 2025. Additionally, STS may be supported through a Bank-executed
Global Tax Program (GTP) grant (GTP decision is pending).
16.     Having reviewed the restructuring request, the Bank team confirms that remaining activities can be completed
by the current closing date of March 31, 2027, and the project remains on track to achieve PDO indicators. The Bank
team will continue active implementation support and coordination with implementing agencies.

related financing are presented section IV. Detailed changes are below.
18.   Revision of Disbursement Estimates. The disbursement estimates will be revised to reflect the reduced cost of
