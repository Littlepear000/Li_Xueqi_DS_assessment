4. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  Additional Financing Grant in the amount of US 35 million was approved by the Board on February 25, 2025 and became
  effective on March 7, 2025. The AF will finance a 36-month extension of the project closing date of August 31, 2025, to
  August 31, 2028, for the completion of the following activities: (i) scaling-up of Component 1 to support data collection to
  strengthen fundamental data needs and fill sex-disaggregated data gaps on climate change, human capital, and private
  sector development; (ii) scaling-up support under Component 2 to expand the investment in Information and
  Communications Technology (ICT) Development and Equipment to support statistical activities in Somaliland and Federal
  Member States (FMS); and (iii) scaling-up support under Component 3 to support the national development plan and
  strategies, and build the capacity of the public sector to use data to address policy challenges, including on climate,
  human capital, and private sector development.
  Progress towards achieving the PDO and implementation progress are rated Satisfactory. By March 2025, the cumulative
  disbursement is US$22.70 million, about 38% of the combined financing of US$60 million. The parent project is on track
  to complete all remaining planned activities, including the Multi-Indicator Cluster Survey and the Somali Integrated
  Business Establishment Survey, the second user satisfaction report, and the National Integrated M&E Framework. The
  project has achieved strong outcomes, with the World Bank's Statistical Performance Indicators (SPI) rising from 19.6 in
  2019 to 49.7 in 2023. The number of United Nations Statistics Division (UNSD) core gender indicators reported by Somalia
  has nearly doubled, from 18 in 2022 to 34 in 2023. In March 2025, the Federal Government of Somalia has completed
  and launched the National Transformation Plan for 2025-2029.
  To provide additional implementation support, Hands-On Expanded Implementation Support (HEIS) has been introduced
  to support complex, high-value procurement activities critical to implementing the project. Under the additional
  financing HEIS may be further extended based on implementation progress and an ongoing need.
