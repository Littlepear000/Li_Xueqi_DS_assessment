 4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

               Strengthening the National Statistical System in Ecuador Project (P178564)



  The Strengthening the National Statistical System in Ecuador Project aims to enhance the quality, coverage, and timeliness
  of official statistics to support evidence-based policymaking and inclusive development. Since the last Implementation
  Status Report, the project has made notable progress toward its development objectives, particularly in expanding
  statistical coverage and improving data infrastructure.


  Key achievements include the continued rollout of the National Statistical Registers System, which now integrates
  population, activity, economic establishments , and buildings and dwellings data. These registers are strengthening the
  availability and reliability of public statistics. In addition, the national household income and expenditure survey (ENIGHUR)
  and the new labor force survey (ENCIET) have reached over 85% effective coverage in most regions, with fieldwork
  extended through December 2025 to ensure comprehensive data collection. During the August 2025 implementation
  support mission, the team agreed on targeted improvements to field operations, including enhanced supervision,
  communication outreach, and coordination with public security forces. These measures are helping to address challenges
  such as informant refusals and logistical constraints in high-risk areas. Additionally, efforts to improve data access and
  quality—such as aligning regulations and strengthening inter-agency collaboration—are contributing to more robust and
  inclusive statistical outputs. Population census results have been publicly available since September 2023 via the INEC’s
  website and a dedicated platform featuring demographic profiles, historical series, and thematic content. In October 2023,
  INEC launched REDATAM, which allows users to generate custom tables. The census was documented in DDI and published
  in INEC’s ANDA catalog in 2024. Thus, census data is widely disseminated and actively use. According to the ANDA Catalog,
  3,067 users have downloaded the population census microdata, and 24,288 users have viewed the census data online..
  Additionally, the census results have received more than 1 million views on the INEC website, demonstrating public interest
  and widespread use of the data. The project continues to demonstrate strong institutional commitment and is delivering
  tangible benefits to Ecuador’s statistical system and its users.
