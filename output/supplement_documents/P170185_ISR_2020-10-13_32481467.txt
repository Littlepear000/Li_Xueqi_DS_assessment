 Project Development Objectives

  Project Development Objective (from Project Appraisal Document)
  The project’s development objective is to improve (i) the relevance and excellence of scientific research, and (ii) innovative entrepreneurship and
  access to finance for enterprise growth, as a way of contributing to Serbia’s growth and competitiveness.

  Relevance of research refers to the potential of research results to be commercialized on the market, thus contributing to the economy, while
  excellence refers to international recognition of the quality of the research.

  Has the Project Development Objective been changed since Board Approval of the Project Objective?
  No

 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The Project was approved by the Board in December 2019, and the Loan Agreement ratified in February 2020. The Project became effective in May
  2020, after conditional Subsidiary Agreements (SA) being signed between the Ministry of Finance (MoF) and Ministry of Economy, Science, and
  Technological Development (MoESTD) on the one hand, and the Innovation Fund (IF) and Science Fund (SF) respectively, on the other.
  Designated Accounts (DAs) have been opened, and PIU establishment is proceeding well. Technical preparations are also proceeding well, with
  key Grant Manuals of the Science Fund drafted and approved, and the first grant programs of the Science Fund underway. The Innovation Fund is
  holding stakeholder consultations on the design of its acceleration program, while MoESTD is consulting with the research community on plans for
  transformation of select R&D institutes.

 Overall Comments PDO

  Although implementation has begun, notably the grants for the Science Fund COVID-19 program have been selected, they are still in
  contracting stage and have not had a notable impact on the results targets. Similarly, the Innovation Fund and the MoESTD are advancing with
  plans related to acceleration and R&D institute reforms, but these steps cannot yet be reflected in the results framework. Thus, the only result
  indicator that shows progress is the number of R&D Institute that have completed self-assessments (57, as of the time of reporting).

  In addition, it should be noted that the Additional Financing (AF) for this project is being processed. This AF will have an effect on the Results
  Framework targets (but not the indicators), which will be reflected after formal approval of the AF.
