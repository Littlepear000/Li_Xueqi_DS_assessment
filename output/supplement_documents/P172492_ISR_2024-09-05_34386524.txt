4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  To date, the project has achieved some results across the different areas of intervention, including supporting all 15
  district local councils to meet the minimum criteria for rounds 1 & 2 local development grants, establishing audit
  committees at all local councils, completing sub-projects of round 1 local development grants, and supporting the
  development of a Fiscal Decentralization Strategy and Own-Source Revenue Guides, However, implementation has been
  slower than expected. Resulting delays in the progress towards meeting the PDO. Despite the slower implementation, the
  project is still considered likely to achieve the PDO based on results achieved to date and the implementation of activities
  that are currently under procurement. One key finding from the Mid Term review mission is that the project will need to
  be restructured to reallocate project resources to priority activities that can produce further results before the closure of
  the project.
