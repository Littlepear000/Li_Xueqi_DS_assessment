 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  Under Component 1 (Physical Infrastructure) On March 18th, 2022 the World Bank approved the final bidding documents for the design of the 911
  Center and works for the 911 Center. On March 28th, the call for the selection process for the design and construction of the 911 center building
  was issued. On June 20th, the bid verification report was signed, indicating that the bidding process was declared unsuccessful. Immediately after,
  on June 21st, PRONATEL contacted by mail the 30 companies that had requested the SDP asking their reasons and causes for not submitting
  proposals. Companies were invited to a meeting to present the project and the second call for applications for the design and construction of the
  911 Center. This meeting took place on July 18th, 2022, with the participation of 10 companies. During the meeting, the lack of some essential

  central will be placed and soil studies, together with the estimated execution times.
  Under Component 3 (Central Response Protocols), PRONATEL is working to sign Cooperation Agreements with different first response agencies.
  In this regard, PRONATEL has signed 3 of the 4 agreed Cooperation Agreements, the last one in June with the Linea 100, being the agreement
