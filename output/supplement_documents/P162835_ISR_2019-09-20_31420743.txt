 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  The Catalyzing Entrepreneurship for Job Creation Project in the amount of US$200 million, was approved by the World Bank Group Board of
  Executive Directors on April 17, 2019. The Loan Agreement and Project Agreement were signed on May 5, 2019. The Project has not yet been
  made effective. (N.B. Parliament is in summer recess, reconvening in October 2019).
