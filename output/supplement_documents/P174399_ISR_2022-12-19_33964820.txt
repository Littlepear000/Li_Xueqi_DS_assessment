 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  Implementation status can be summarized as follows:
         The Program was approved on February 3rd, 2022, and became effective on March 30th, 2022. The Program has successfully disbursed
          USD 2 million under the Ministry of Labor and Human Resources (MOLHR) pertaining to prior results under DLIs 5 and 8.
         In early December, 2022, the Program has successfully confirmed the achievement of results amounting to additional USD 3 million under
          the Ministry of Education pertaining to Year 1 DLRs for DLI # 3. Disbursement is currently underway.
         The implementation Guidance Note, which details the program verification requirements and disbursement process, as well as the
          responsibilities of various stakeholders, has been prepared. The WB team delivered a virtual implementation capacity building workshop
          on November 30 and December 1, 2022 and during the workshop, all sections of the Implementation Guidance Note were covered. Very
          recently, the team also completed training on environmental and social safeguard policy measures that are required for implementing
          agencies to comply with during program implementation.
         Project restructuring was completed in September, 2022 to allow for prior result on teacher training listed under DLI # 3 to disburse under
          the regular DLR process for Year 1.
