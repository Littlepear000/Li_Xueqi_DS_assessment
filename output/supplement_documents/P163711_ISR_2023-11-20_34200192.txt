            Tax Administration and Statistical System Modernization Project (P163711)



  The State Tax Service (STS) under the Ministry of Finance and the National Statistical Committee (NSC) have made progress in project
  implementation in the past six months. The STS and NSC have launched important procurement packages and advanced activities that lay the
  foundations for modernization reforms in tax administration and the national statistical system. Key activities include: STS strategic planning;
  change management; business process redesign; ICT equipment procurement; capacity building; feedback from stakeholders on tax administration
  areas that are in need of improvements; improvement and development of a legal and regulatory framework to support the implementation of the
  Law on Official Statistics; Twinning Partnership consulting services; and procurement of software and hardware for NSC. However, due to
  significant delay at the beginning of the project, the Mid-Term Review of the project has assessed the progress toward PDO as Moderately
  Unsatisfactory owing to risks that the project cannot achieve the PDO within the current project timeline. The Bank and NSC and STS management
  have agreed timebound actions to accelerate the pace of implementation.

   IN01364869
   ►Overall efficiency gain: reduction of time between completion of data collection and release of key statistical indicators to the public (Text,
   Custom)

                                       Baseline                     Actual (Previous)             Actual (Current)           End Target

    Value                              90 days                      90 days                       90 days                    66 days

    Date                               15-Jul-2019                  14-Apr-2023                   08-Nov-2023                31-Oct-2025
                                       The project activities are still in their initial stage of implementation and thus have not had impact on
                                       Project’s results indicators. The results will materialize following business process changes and the IT
                                       system upgrade.
                                       This indicator measures elapsed time between completion of data collection and release to public of key
                                       statistical indicators (such as GDP, Financial, External Trade and Poverty and Income) to the public. To
                                       compute efficiency gains, a reference or baseline ‘elapsed time’ data would be required for a few of the
                                       business processes used in the current production cycles of NSC. A few representative indicators (e.g.
                                       GDP, Poverty & Income, etc.) can be used to compute a broader or overall efficiency gain. A weighted
   Comments:                           average (equal weights) of efficiency gains of such representative indicators can be used to compute the
                                       overall efficiency gain. The new workflow management system would also programmatically compute
                                       such elapsed times for business process activities of other indicators or data products.
                                       At least 20 indicators have been identified to compute a broader or overall efficiency gain.
                                       Currently, the indicator remains at the baseline. A significant reduction of the actual value is expected
                                       after the full-scale implementation of the e-reporting and business processes, including training. These
                                       changes should take up to 36 months (about 3 years). The indicator can be achieved only if the project is
                                       extended.
