 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The Project, now post-midterm review, is on track to achieving its Project Development Objective (PDO).
  Component 1: Financial Support to MSMEs is progressing well and is disbursing to the end beneficiary in a timely manner; exceeding all set
  targets.
  Component 2: Risk Capital for Innovative Start-ups and High-growth SMEs. Over the past period, MSMEDA has further developed the Fund of
  Fund program (under Component 2), with full mobilization and functioning of Investment Committee (IC) and proactive cultivation of fund
  managers/RCIs who may apply for MSMEDA investment as per the program parameters. 5 deals have been already completed and the
  component is currently on track on its key targets.
  Component 3: Business and Capacity Development. Under this component, MSMEDA has activated the capacity building of its staff, bringing in
  technical investment and legal advisors/experts to support Components 1 and 2 implementations, with plans for further staff training in FY23.
  MSMEDA has also successfully hosted the annual African Private Equity and Venture Capital Association conference (AVCA 2023, May 1-5, 2023)
  in Egypt with participation from over 500 private equity and venture fund managers and investors from Africa and other regions, under the auspices
