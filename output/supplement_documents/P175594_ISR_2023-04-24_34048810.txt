 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The Program became effective on December 6, 2022. Disbursement Linked Results DLR 1.1 (RSU Geographic Roll-out Strategy) and DLR 4.1 (the
  PTMP Operations Manual) were programmed for December 31, 2022. The BCPFS (Coordination Unit of the Safety Nets Programs) has transmitted
  the respective deliverables to the World Bank's Task Team on December 29, 2022. The recruitment of the Independent Verification Agent (IVA) is
  underway; submitted DLRs will be reviewed once the recruitment process is completed (expected in May 2023).
  Moreover, identification efforts using the Proxy Means Test (PMT) and community validation protocols are progressing well, and 100 000
  beneficiary households of cohort 5 of the National Productive Cash Transfer Program are expected to be identified within the first half of April 2023
  (allowing the government to make important progress on the achievements of DLIs 4, 5 and 6).
