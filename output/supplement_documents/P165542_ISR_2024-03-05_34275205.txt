 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The 'Strengthening Institutions for Refugee Administration Project' has made progress since the conclusion of the last mission early December
  2023. This includes; i) submission of the Third Party Verification report by the Office of Auditor General to the World Bank, ii) update and approval of
  Procurement Plan in STEP, iii) advertisement of OSU positions, and iv) submission of withdrawal application for an additional advance of US$6.5
  million.
